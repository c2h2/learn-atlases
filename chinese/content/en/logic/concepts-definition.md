The previous chapter took an argument apart into a ledger; this chapter is in charge of the **names of the accounts** on that ledger — **half of those who quarrel are quarrelling over "which 负责 ('responsibility') you mean by 负责"**: when the concept is unclear, however solid the logic, one is still measuring the distance between the buildings from two different buildings.

## Definition: three-way reconciliation {#def-sanjiao}

::: definition A broken line of word, mind and thing {#def-zsx}
**Concept ≠ word ≠ object**: one and the same object has countless names ("土豆／马铃薯／洋芋" — 'potato', under three regional names — **a dialect is the dialect of a name, a concept is the dialect of a concept, and the two may pretend to live in the same house**); behind one and the same name there are countless stocks (ten people say "素质" ('quality'), and ten kinds of goods are set out on the shelf — **"you agree on this word" and "you agree on the same thing" are two independent accounts: the greatest illusion of a deal struck, in a dispute, is registered here**). **Definition = reconciling the word you utter with the stock in my mind** (genus plus specific difference is the standard way of keeping the accounts: **"a human is a rational animal" = find the broad class (animal) + find the price difference (rational)** — **Aristotle's formula ruled for two thousand years, until Plato gave the version "featherless and two-footed", and Diogenes plucked a chicken bare and threw it into the Academy: "This is the definition of a human!" — the teaching value of the scene of the crash: the specific difference must be chosen at the decisive level; a specific difference chosen at the wrong level can be pierced by one bald chicken**).
:::

## Five rules and five crashes {#def-guitse}

::: definition The audit procedure {#def-shenji}
**① Commensurate** (the extension is exactly equal to the definiendum) — **"a bird is a feathered animal" is exact; "a bird is an animal that can fly" both misses (the penguin) and exceeds (the bat): too wide is opening the door and bowing the robber in, too narrow is starving one's own guards — the way policy language dies is exactly the same: "dangerous articles must not be carried onto the vehicle" (what does not count as dangerous? The security screener's stock has the say: the moment a definition transfers discretion to enforcement, it has already failed by half**); **② must not be circular** — "a cause is the phenomenon it gives rise to", "beauty is that which produces aesthetic pleasure" — **a circular definition is a loan at zero interest: printing money produces no value; but note that mutual explanation of "definiens and definiendum" inside one system is the normal case (a dictionary as a whole is circular; so long as each word, on its own, can be saved by its neighbours, the system lives — a vicious circle means "tying itself up on the spot", not "everything mutually glossing everything"**); **③ use negation with care** — "humour is not being profound" fails, but "a bachelor is an unmarried man" and "innocence is not being what the charge points towards" hold — **the true form of the rule: if the concept being defined is itself negative, the definition may of course be negative; negation can define only a class with a definite complement ("not-P" may be used as the price difference only when the boundary of P is clear**); **④ must not be vague (metaphor forbidden as a definition)** — "architecture is solidified music", "a city is an organism" — **a metaphorical definition is a heuristic, not an audit tool: it gives you a new pair of glasses, but it does not give you scales (it can light up an association; it cannot be used to reconcile accounts — in an essay it is good writing, in a court it is good poetry)**; **⑤ neutrality of order (rank the core words by importance, not by feeling)** — in "**freedom is doing, without interference, what one ought to do", deleting "ought" or "interference" yields a different freedom in each case: a dispute over a definition is often a dispute over deleted words**.
:::

::: definition Intension and extension: a seesaw, one end up and one end down {#def-neihan}
**Intension (the list of attributes) ↑, then extension (the set of objects it applies to) ↓** — "**employee → an employee who arrives on time → an employee who arrives on time and replies to messages": with each attribute added, a batch of people lose their eligibility (the physics of a job advertisement: modifiers are sieves)**. **Corollary one (the law of duality)**: **if you want to widen an exemption, reduce the intension (change "本校学生" ('students of this school') into "学员" ('trainees') — every generic designation is a side door opened for discretion); if you want to tighten admission, pile on intension (the number of characters in the application is the threshold)**; **Corollary two (failure of the concept)**: **when every item of the intension is vague ("态度" ('attitude'), "相关" ('relevant'), "适当" ('appropriate')), both ends of the seesaw are left hanging in the air: the extension is decided by the mood of the person who holds the word — this is not vagueness; it is the smuggling of power (an echo of the history of definition in Chapter 1: "nine-tenths of disputes in taxonomy are disputes in the study of resources" — the power to define is the power to allocate)**.
:::

::: definition Three kinds of definition and one sorcery {#def-sanzhong}
**Stipulative definition** — **"'Personal information', as referred to in these measures, means …": the legislator's magic: creating a ledger account out of nothing (a stipulative definition is not sorted into true and false, only into good and bad: the standard of good and bad = whether it lets later disputes in — a good definition saves lawsuits, a bad definition opens a court**); **lexical definition (the dictionary type)** — reporting the stock already there (**a dictionary gloss is a snapshot of custom, not the original copy of a contract**); **persuasive definition (persuasive definition) — using a definition to persuade, not to render the accounts**: "**Is freedom each person's power to do what he likes? Or the power to do what he ought to do?" — the same object, two stocks, each carrying an emotional label**: **persuasive definition = keeping the emotional load and covertly replacing the cognitive content (this is the craft of propaganda at the level of definition: the vocabulary unchanged, the shelves rearranged — the branch, in the study of definition, of the previous lesson's "appeal to emotion"; the formula for spotting it: take away this word's applause and boos, and what is left)**.
:::

::: example The case of "related to this course" {#ex-xiangguan}
The student-union charter provides: "**Textbook fees may be used only for books related to this course**." At the end of term, the teacher of the course used the textbook fees to buy a science-fiction novel the class was reading together, and a documentary. The student-union audit: ① the side in favour defines "related = covered by the examination syllabus"; ② the side opposed defines "related = helpful for achieving the aims of this course"; ③ the teacher maintains that "what is related is judged by the person teaching"; ④ one student proposes changing it to "**related = listed in an appendix to the course outline, and signed by a group of three**". Using the tools of this chapter, issue an **audit report**: what is each of the four positions doing (stipulative / lexical / persuasive / operationalisation)? Which definition leaves the fewest disputes, and which leaves the least learning? What is your revision?
::: solution
① **The syllabus view** = outsourcing the definition to another document (**a definition run up as a debt: the syllabus's own "covered" has not been defined — transferring a debt is not discharging it**); ② **the aims view** = a persuasive / value definition (**"helpful" has an extension wide enough to hold an entire term — too wide: the audit loses its teeth**); ③ **the view of the person teaching** = giving up the definition and handing over the discretion (**extension decided by mood = the smuggling of power; the least trouble institutionally, the most expensive for learning: it turns every intake into a precedent**); ④ **the list-plus-signature view** = an operational definition (**"only what is measurable is operational": from then on the audit can be carried out — but the price is that the kind of class the aims view can save (a novel that changes a life) is systematically excluded: operationalisation is science's tax — paying the tax gets you onto the motorway, but the motorway does not lead to every narrow path**); **conclusion of the audit report**: keep the accounts separate; do not merge them — **split the textbook fee into two columns: "inside the list in the outline's appendix" (governed by an operational definition) + "shared reading beyond the list" (governed by a discretionary clause with a cap and the joint signature of two people: discretion is kept, but priced and traced; the maturity of a definition is not the abolition of vagueness, but deciding which vagueness to leave in the budget and making it sign — precision is not a virtue; honesty of allocation is)**.
:::
:::

::: intuition Borderline cases and "the budget of precision" {#int-bianjie}
**The paradox of the heap** (one grain of rice does not make a heap, nor two … how many grains make a "heap"? — **a vague boundary is the normal state of language, not a fault: most concepts have a bright centre and a dim dusk ("bald", "rich", "young": every society plants its own boundary stake in the dusk — a boundary stake is policy, not a discovery**). **Wittgenstein's "game"** (**there is no finding an attribute common to every game, only a "network of similarities overlapping one another" — family resemblance: a concept may be held together by kinship rather than by a definition (the four thermometers of Lesson 15, "What is literature?", show their hand here: "essence" lost, "kinship" won — but note: what lost was "definition in the manner of an audit", not "definition in the manner of an operation" — a court cannot decide a case by family resemblance; a playground can**). **The teaching conclusion**: **in everyday conversation, borderline cases are settled by analogy ("Does this of yours count? Is it more like that case we all agreed on last time?" — thinking in the manner of case law is the logic of analogy: see Chapter 7); when rules are being made, the stake must be driven in hard, and the politics of the stake must be borne ("full fare from the age of twelve" is unjust, but it can be carried out: a vague definition bites no one; a clear definition must give notice in advance of whom it bites)**.
:::

::: history A few acts of legislation in the art of definition {#sec-lishi}
**5th–4th centuries BC**, the dispute over the correctness of names (**China: Confucius, "必也正名乎" — "What is necessary is to rectify names." (Our translation.) — 名不正则言不顺: "If names are not correct, then speech does not follow." (Our translation.): the inaugural year of making the problem of definition political; Greece: sophists of the sort of Critias and Antiphon put "nomos/physis" (convention/nature) on the table — a campaign to set names and actualities straight took place at both ends of Eurasia at the same time: both were, in an age when the rites had collapsed and the music had decayed, an impulse to "reset the stock"**); **4th century BC**, Aristotle's *Organon* 《工具论》 (**definition = legislation by genus plus specific difference, and it is placed at the starting point of disputation: first establish the name, then distinguish the sense**) + the Mohist "**以名举实**" ('using the name to pick out the actual') (*Xiaoqu* 《小取》: "**夫辩者，将以明是非之分，审治乱之纪……以名举实，以辞抒意，以说出故**" — "One who disputes will use it to make clear the distinction between right and wrong, and to examine the guiding threads of order and disorder … to pick out actualities by names, to express intent by statements, and to bring out the ground by explanations." (Our translation.) — the three-layer frame of name → statement → explanation = concept → judgement → inference: a three-step staircase of the same age as the *Organon***); **the late Qing and the early Republic**, the logic of Western learning comes ashore (**Yan Fu 严复 translated Mill's *A System of Logic* as *Mule mingxue* 《穆勒名学》 (1905), and himself wrote *Mingxue qianshuo* 《名学浅说》 ('A brief account of the study of names', 1909): logic was in the end given the fixed name "逻辑" — a transliteration is a purely stipulative definition of a term: the birth of a word is nothing more than a vote (the two characters "逻辑" defeated "名学" ('the study of names'), "辩学" ('the study of disputation') and "论理学" ('the study of reasoning'): winning and losing depend on people, not on reason — the history of terminology is a living textbook of the study of definition**); **1923**, Ogden and Richards, *The Meaning of Meaning* (**the pioneering text of the "revolution in semantics": definition became the daily business of philosophy, and was also written into modern civic literacy**); **the 1930s–40s**, the Vienna Circle's criterion of verifiability (**"a meaningful definition" was legislated anew: metaphysical sentences were driven out of the workshop of science — a movement, in the study of definition, for the separation of church and state**); **today**, **"what counts as overtime", "what counts as an intimate relationship", "what counts as the same sex" — platform rules, the law and the hot-search list together become the bidders for the power to define: every new word is a temporary invitation to tender ("打工人" ('one who labours for a wage'), "小镇做题家" ('the small-town doer of exam questions'): the right of the person named to refuse delivery is the new battlefield of the study of definition: naming is upgraded from rhetoric to governance)**.
:::

::: warning Four pits of definition
**Dependency on "the dictionary says"** (**a dictionary is a snapshot of the stock, not the original copy of a contract: winning the case of "ought 知识分子 ('intellectuals') to pay tax?" with the dictionary gloss of "知识分子" is to take a description as a norm (the definitional version of Lesson 1's "confusion of is and ought"**)); **the superstition of excessive precision** (**defining "happiness" down to 0.001 of a point: precision beyond the budget of measurement and of consensus is a false number — "precision that can be operated" comes before "pretty vagueness", but "operable" does not equal "worth it" (the average of item 3 on the questionnaire is not your life**)); **using a definition to stop the discussion** (**"A true X would, all of them, …" — No true Scotsman: when the definition has been raised high enough that every counter-example is automatically out, it has been immunized（）: it no longer states the world, it only states itself ("a true friend never betrays": what it defends is a stretch of self-esteem, not a fact**)); **forgetting that a definition has a shelf life** (**the previous lesson: implicit premises expire; this chapter: stipulative definitions do too — "motor vehicle" has met the electric vehicle, "wages" have met equity incentives: the world changes its stock, and the law patches the definition: every year there are words moving house (change of meaning is linguistics' earthquake; the study of definition is the earthquake-resistance code**).
:::

::: exercise An audit of definitions {level=1}
Issue an **audit report** on each of the following (which rule it violates / whether it may be exempted / a scheme for rewriting it): ① "幸福就是快乐的感觉" ('happiness is a feeling of pleasure'); ② "健康就是不生病" ('health is not being ill'); ③ "文艺就是文学和艺术" ('文艺 is literature and art'); ④ "宪法就是国家的根本大法" ('a constitution is the fundamental great law of the state').
::: solution
Marking: ① **circular, and vague** ("feeling" itself has to be explained by way of happiness — pleasure and happiness are each other's stock; exemption: permissible as persuasion, not permissible as an audit); ② **negative in form, but may be exempted** (when the boundary of "disease" is clear, "non-disease" is legitimate — but modern medicine understands health as a functional state: the extension is too narrow ("亚健康" ('sub-health') has nowhere to be placed) — **the definition moves house with medicine's stock: a case of shelf life**); ③ **circular (a definition by enumeration)** — **enumeration is a legitimate form of extensional definition, on the condition that the class is finite and can be exhausted: "文艺" cannot be exhausted, so it is downgraded to "illustration by example"**; ④ **tautology ("宪法" has "根本" in it)** — **acceptable as a lexical definition; a stipulative definition must give the criteria for "根本" ('fundamental') (who amends it, how many votes it takes to pass, whether a court may cite it: operational criteria are the remedy)**.
:::

::: exercise Class rules put into a written undertaking {level=2}
Rewrite the class rule "**during class, one must not do anything unrelated to the class**" as an **auditable clause**: give an operational definition, a discretionary clause (who decides, and how a trace is left), and an appeal clause (how it is reviewed); a self-assessment of 60 characters: **what you sacrificed, and what you held on to**.
::: solution
Marking: **the set of three is complete** (an operational definition (a list + examples), discretion (the teacher explains on the spot + it is entered in the log), review (the class meeting votes to withdraw the clause — **the separation of the legislative, executive and judicial powers of definition is the smallest model that can run, at the scale of a class rule**); the self-assessment is not allowed to write "it is all better" (**every operational definition kills "that spell of inattention which changed a life": imagination is sacrificed in exchange for auditability — an honest statement of the price is the only thing this question tests**).
:::

::: exercise Short essay: the power to define, and the dispute over deleted words {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**on the annual bill for public language, the dearest item has never been a new word; it is an old word whose definition was quietly changed — the word is still that word, and the applause and the boos have changed their delivery address**". You must call on at least five items: **genus plus specific difference, and the lesson of the bald chicken**, **the rule of being commensurate (too wide / too narrow)**, **the craft of persuasive definition**, **operationalisation is science's tax**, **the immunising clause of No true Scotsman**; and adjudicate: **who has the right to define a normative concept of the kind "school bullying" — the school, the parents, the platform, or the students (argue for your scheme of allocation, and give the conditions under which that scheme fails)**.
::: solution
Marking points: the five mechanisms (**choosing the level of the specific difference = choosing the level of value; width and narrowness = the consideration exchanged between openness and safety; persuasion = diverting the emotional load; the tax = measurability levied upon richness; immunity = a definition protecting itself until it leaves the world**); the adjudication (**marks fall in the two columns "procedure + conditions of failure": a reference scheme — the power to define is layered: the list of core acts belongs to the school (an operational definition, and it can be carried out), borderline cases belong to a hearing that includes the students (analogy and discretion, a trace left), and appeals belong to a third party (review); condition of failure: "the list has been written full, and the hearing has been abolished" = the dusk of pure operationalisation — "when a word has only one list, and that list has no exceptions, it no longer explains the world; it only registers households"**); if the conclusion is written "**the person who can quarrel changes the premises; the person who can legislate changes the definition — and what is required of a good citizen is one level higher: he can point out who, at this moment, while everyone is disputing the conclusion, is quietly revising the dictionary**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The reconciliation, filed: the triangle (**word—mind—thing: your agreeing on this word ≠ your agreeing on the same thing**), the procedure (**commensurate; do not be circular; use negation with care; do not use metaphor; watch for deleted words: the lesson of the bald chicken = the specific difference must be chosen at the right level**), the uses (**stipulative / lexical / persuasive: a persuasive definition = a change of delivery address for the applause and the boos**), the boundary stake (**borderline cases by analogy; when legislating, drive the stake in hard and bear the politics; family resemblance won the playground, and did not win the court**). The method in one sentence: **first ask "which stock is the X you speak of taken from?", then ask "who is in charge of this stock?"**. The next chapter enters propositions: the sentence is put on the scales of truth value.
:::
