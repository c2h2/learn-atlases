---
title: "Registers"
---

A word has a household registration, a sentence has an occasion, and a whole text has a "ti" (体). Register (语体) is the ledger in which language keeps the accounts of social life: **for talking a thing through, use the mouth; for a red-header document, use paper; for a comment in code, use a third tone of voice** — a grammatically correct sentence put to the wrong occasion is still a wrong sentence.

## Spoken and written language {#sec-koushu}

::: definition A paired continuum {#def-erfen}
The first cut in the splitting of register falls on the **medium**: spoken language (an acoustic signal, co-presence on the spot, short sentences stitched in a line, high redundancy — “那个，就是说，大概，可能吧” ('um, that is to say, roughly, maybe') are all face-saving buffer-pads) and written language (visual symbols, detached from context, an architecture of long sentences, high density — a sentence may run to three hundred characters and still be no breach). But the two are not two countries; they are **the two poles of one and the same spectrum**: scripted lines, read-aloud broadcasts and live streams are spoken language carrying a script; WeChat chat is spoken language tapped out on keys (a new variant, “书面化的口语”, 'speech made written' — living the life of the mouth at the speed of typing). The history of Chinese has ferried back and forth along this strip: Classical Chinese (*wenyan* 文言) and the vernacular (*baihua* 白话) kept separate households for a thousand years (“言文分离”, 'the separation of speech and writing'), and the vernacular movement of 1917 took them in under “言文一致” ('speech and writing made one') — **written Putonghua (普通话, standard Mandarin) simply is the orthographic standardisation of speech**, which counts as young in the pedigree of written languages the world over.
:::

::: definition The official document between the registers {#def-gongwen}
The registers of the national language (function style) are traditionally cut into five — **the scientific register, the political-commentary register, the news register, the official-document register and the literary register** (the spoken register is another coordinate, on the side of the medium; do not mix its axis with the five). The teaching system of the 1960s imported the scheme from Soviet functional stylistics and fixed it: the scientific register (terms are univocal, the logical connectives come as a full set, and passives and nominalisations run high — “实验表明” ('the experiment shows') forbids “我感觉” ('I feel')); the political-commentary register (the stance comes first, judgement sentences and rhetorical questions draw up in ranks, and “必须/坚决/深入贯彻” ('must / resolutely / implement in depth') come as a run of three); the news register (the inverted pyramid, direct quotation, **the disclaimer design of verbs of reporting** — “据称” ('it is alleged') and “当地时间” ('local time') are rhetoric's airbags against risk); the official-document register (the wording is turned into dictionary entries: “贵局” ('your bureau'), “收悉” ('received and noted'), “妥否，请批复” ('whether this is in order, please reply with a decision') — the whole string is a pragmatic formula, and not a character of it may be replaced; the passive and the subjectless sentence are on duty by nature); the literary register (the only register that **encourages a personal dialect (*fangyan* 方言) and collocations beyond the norm** — a mistake in the scientific register is a scoring point in the literary register, a boundary case of register discipline).
:::

::: example Five universes of one utterance {#ex-wuyu}
Rewrite “本公司决定不再生产某型号产品” 'This company has decided to stop producing a certain model of product.' (Our translation.) as one sentence in each of the five registers, and mark the "register markers" (体标) of each.
::: solution
Scientific/technical: “型号 X 于 2024Q2 停产，替代方案见表 3。” 'Model X ceased production in 2024Q2; see Table 3 for the replacement.' (Our translation.) (register markers: numbering, a timetable); political commentary: “坚决淘汰落后产能，把资源交给未来。” 'Resolutely eliminate backward production capacity, and hand the resources to the future.' (Our translation.) (register markers: “坚决” ('resolutely') plus sloganising into verb and object); news: “记者从公司获悉，该型号产品将于下季度停产。” 'A reporter has learned from the company that this model of product will cease production next quarter.' (Our translation.) (register marker: a frame for the source of the news); official document: “经研究，现就X型号产品停产有关事项通知如下。” 'Upon consideration, notice is hereby given of the matters concerning the cessation of production of model X, as follows.' (Our translation.) (register markers: the formula frame “经研究” ('upon consideration'), “现就……通知如下” ('notice is hereby given of … as follows')); literary: “流水线尽头，最后一台X没有装上一颗螺丝——像一句决定不说出口的话。” 'At the end of the line, the last unit of X had not been fitted with a single screw — like a sentence that decided not to be spoken.' (Our translation.) (register markers: a licence for metaphor and simile, the blank left by the dash). **Five suits of register-clothing for one and the same proposition; the register markers of each can be put to autopsy.**
:::
:::

::: definition Internet language: one register or a nest {#def-wangluo}
"Internet language" is a basket: bullet comments (弹幕) (instant co-presence plus **parasitism on the subtitles** — the original text is still there, and the comments are scrolling), Weibo and status updates (rhetoric condensed under the shackles of a character limit; clickbait syntax), group chat (@, [表情], “收到” ('received') repeated — **the emoticon is paralanguage made official**: intonation has acquired a spelling, a new thing in the history of writing), live-stream read-alouds (a verb of closing the sale plus a countdown: “三二一上链接” 'Three, two, one, the link goes up.' (Our translation.)). Their common genes: **platform affordance (可供性) casts syntax directly** — a limit on characters produces a condensed register, the reply tree produces nested ellipsis, and the language of reposting produces “套壳引语” ('shell quotations') (“看到最后泪目了” 'I got to the end and my eyes filled.' (Our translation.) = a monster stitched from the words of the repost and the original text; the kin of the chapter on faulty sentences of confused structure are ordinary constructions here). The blow that internet registers strike at tradition does not lie in "bad grammar" but in **the speed of mixing**: the official-document tone, the customer-service tone and the cant of the two-dimensional world flirt on the same screen (“宝子，家人们，奉告各位” 'Babes, my family, I have the honour to inform you all.' (Our translation.)).
:::

::: history The registration history of the concept 'register' {#sec-dengji}
Chinese has all along had a critical tradition of "ti" (*The Literary Mind and the Carving of Dragons*, the chapter 体性 ('Style and Nature'), distinguishes eight *ti*; what that discusses is style; modern “语体学”, 'the study of register', is another line). In the 1930s “语文体” ('the language-and-writing style') and “公文式” ('the official-document form') had already entered everyday use; from the 1940s on, the field defined “语体 = 功能变体” ('register = functional variety'); the true moment of institutionalisation was **the introduction, around 1960, of the Soviet classification of functional styles into the teaching system**, the five registers were written into the textbooks, and they became several generations' map of their feel for the language; functional stylistics in the 1980s, and the “语体交际观” ('the communicative view of register') in the 1990s, added to the map a further dimension, that of the user (a register is not a wardrobe; it is a ticket to a conversation). After the 2010s, internet linguistics exploded — and for the first time the map of the classification was pushed backwards by the volume of users: **the corpus added each day is greater than the sum of the past thousand years, and the household registration of "ti" must be night-audited.**
:::

::: warning Three cases of register illness
One, **mis-dressed for the hall and the inner room**: an obituary written “老爷子下线了，R.I.P.” 'The old man has logged off, R.I.P.' (Our translation.) (the mourning language of the internet invades the official-document register of funerals; allowable inside the circle, and an incident the moment it is made public — see [[modern-grammar/faulty-sentences]], the precedent “语域得体” ('appropriateness to the language domain')); two, **infection by the customer-service tone**: a government bulletin “宝子们注意啦！病毒来袭，亲，戴口罩哟” 'Babes, attention! The virus is coming; dear, do wear a mask.' (Our translation.) — the price of closeness is an overdraft of authority, and the history of the language of the epidemic has it in the books; three, **genre drift**: an abstract written as an essay (“在这个快节奏的时代……” 'In this fast-paced age…'), a court judgement written as a denunciatory proclamation — the standard for marking is always **what the readers of this genre expect**, not the author's comfort zone. The one name that covers errors of register: **breach of appropriateness** — a grammatical error is an illness, a register error is an embarrassment, and embarrassment is harder to forgive than illness.
:::

::: exercise Register translation officer {level=1}
Rewrite “您的快递晚了，别急” 'Your parcel is late; don't fret.' (Our translation.) as one sentence each of: ① the official customer-service register; ② the official-document register of a letter of apology; ③ the stand-up register.
::: solution
① “非常抱歉给您带来不便，您的快件当前延迟，我们将持续为您跟进物流信息。” 'We are very sorry for the inconvenience caused to you. Your shipment is currently delayed, and we shall continue to follow up the logistics information for you.' (Our translation.) (customer-service register = apology + status + action + the form of address “您”, 'you', polite); ② “关于快件延误事宜，我处深表歉意，已启动赔付流程，特此致函。” 'Concerning the delay of the shipment, this office expresses its deep regret; the compensation procedure has been started; this letter is hereby sent.' (Our translation.) (the formulae “我处” ('this office') and “特此” ('hereby')); ③ “您的快递正在完成它的西西弗斯之旅——推上去，又滚回来。” 'Your parcel is completing its journey of Sisyphus — pushed up, and rolling back again.' (Our translation.) (the literary register / the register of the comic bit: a myth with its corpse borrowed, plus a frivolous mismatch of theme, manufactures the joke — humour = a controlled breach of register). Full marks once the register markers of all three are in place.
:::

::: exercise Identifying hybrids {level=2}
Collect three sentences of real "register hybrid" data (for example “本平台郑重告知：双击666” 'This platform solemnly gives notice: double-click 666.' (Our translation.)), and judge the mechanism by which each was generated (irony / marketing / meme-play) and the income and expenditure of appropriateness.
::: solution
Reference mechanisms: solemn words plus a light scene = **comedy of demotion** (the official-document frame “郑重告知” ('solemnly gives notice') is set over the cant “666”, and the sense of solemnity, once commandeered, bursts its chamber); the marketing register feeds on cadging authority (“家人们，奉告”); judging income and expenditure depends on **whether the audience shares the code for decoding** — recognition inside the circle = income, offence taken outside the circle = expenditure. Full marks when each of the three examples is paired with a mechanism.
:::
:::

::: exercise Five sets of clothes for one notice {level=2}
On “周五停电检修” ('a power cut for maintenance on Friday'), write five notices: a property-management noticeboard, a department group, a private message to an important client, a broadcast read-aloud, and a broadcast script for primary-school pupils — and for each note three reasons for the choice of register.
::: solution
Reference points: noticeboard = the skeleton of an official document + reduction to key points; department group = short sentences + a request for “收到” + time and place put first (scan-reading on a mobile); private message = the honorific system fully on + “您” + a personal name in the signature; read-aloud = numbers put into spoken form, “五号下午两点到四点” ('from two to four on the afternoon of the fifth'), + the key information repeated (hearing cannot be scrolled back); the primary-school version = “小灯泡要休息一个下午” 'The little light bulb needs to rest for an afternoon.' (Our translation.). Full marks when the three reasons for each include the dimensions of medium, audience and purpose.
:::

Write eight hundred to twelve hundred characters: "the official-document register returns for an encore in sticker packs; the customer-service tone invades bulletins; academic cant leaves its circle and becomes a meme" — have the boundaries of register loosened, or have they been reorganised? Using this lesson's method of "register markers", analyse two items of real, disputed language data, and give three articles of your "amendment to register etiquette".
::: solution
Marking points: the register markers of the two items of data are taken apart in a way that can be checked; the core argument acknowledges that "acceleration of mixing ≠ disappearance of boundaries" (what an occasion expects is still sitting in judgement; only the jury has grown younger); the amendment is specific to scenes (for example: public-service texts forbid meme-play; a private account is labelled with a disclaimer of "personal register"); quote "embarrassment is harder to forgive than illness" and give a dosage clause of your own.

::: summary
Register is language's ledger of occasions: one cut by medium (spoken and written, and the broad tidal flats between them), five functional registers (scientific, political commentary, news, official document, literary — the literary is the only one that issues a pass for going beyond the norm), and the internet's register-clusters write platform affordance into a new syntax. A breach of appropriateness cuts closer to the heart than a grammatical error, because what it offends is **everyone present** — which is why the next lesson has to go from "occasion" to "person": style, those names on the assembly line of register that refuse to wear the uniform.
:::
