title: "Figures of form"
---

The previous hall looked at meaning moving house; this hall looks at **bodily stance**: the symmetry of antithesis, the square formation of parallelism, the incised mark of repetition, the steps of climax, the relay of anadiplosis, the spin of the circular figure, the cipher of allusion, the shock pad of euphemism. Figures of form are the childhood training of Chinese — parallel prose (*piánwén* 骈文) and regulated poems (*lǜshī* 律诗) have made 'regularity' the show flat of an architectural experiment two thousand years long.

## The symmetry family: antithesis and parallelism {#sec-duicheng}

::: definition Antithesis: the symmetrical art of the smallest frame {#def-duiou}
Two lines stand side by side, of the same structure, equal in the number of characters, and related in meaning — from the aphorism in the *Book of Changes*, 同声相应，同气相求 — 'Like sounds respond to one another; like *qi* seeks its fellow.' (Our translation.), to the orthodox form of the **parallel couplet** (*duìzhàng* 对仗) in the second and third couplets (颔联, 颈联) of a regulated poem (无边落木萧萧下，不尽长江滚滚来 — 'Boundless, the falling leaves come soughing down; unending, the Yangtze comes rolling on.' (Our translation.): word class, level and oblique tones, and meaning — all three gates are checked), and on to spring couplets, pillar couplets, and the four-six sentence pattern of official documents: Chinese has carved symmetry into its aesthetic genes. **Direct antithesis** (*zhèngduì* 正对; the two lines heap brightness in the same direction), **opposed antithesis** (*fǎnduì* 反对; 横眉冷对千夫指，俯首甘为孺子牛 — 'With brows set flat I coldly meet a thousand pointing fingers; head bowed, I am willing to be an ox for a child.' (Our translation.): one refusal and one gift, and the tension arises of itself), **sequential antithesis / the flowing couplet** (*chuànduì* / *liúshuǐduì*, 串对/流水对; 即从巴峡穿巫峡，便下襄阳向洛阳 — 'Straight from Ba Gorge through Wu Gorge, then down from Xiangyang towards Luoyang.' (Our translation.): the skeleton of antithesis is loaded with the stream of a narrative — where transformed mastery shows most clearly). The rhetorical ledger of antithesis: **trading space for time** — the two lines mirror one another, and in a single glance the reader gains a sense of order and a sense of completeness; the price is the ban on **hezhang** (*hézhǎng* 合掌; the line above and the line below say the same thing = two columns of an advertisement print the same picture, and the space is wasted).
:::

::: definition Parallelism: the square formation that stands up at three lines {#def-paibi}
Three items or more, similar in structure and uniform in tone of speech, line up and press forward — 我们不会忘记，是这片土地养活了革命，是这片土地掩护过伤员，是这片土地埋下了英名。 — 'We will not forget: it was this land that fed the revolution, it was this land that sheltered the wounded, it was this land that buried honoured names.' (Our translation.) The divide between parallelism and a continuous run of antithesis: **antithesis seeks evenness (two lines, and repeated characters are avoided); parallelism seeks momentum (from three lines upward, and the recurrence of a shared frame is allowed and encouraged)**. The energy of parallelism comes from **the recurrence of the frame, plus the content increasing by degrees**; the moment the content does not increase, 'parallelism' is exposed as 'replay' (see the warning on parallelism spinning idle).
:::

## The recurrence family and the staircase family {#sec-fuxian}

::: definition Repetition, anadiplosis, the circular figure, and climax {#def-fuxian}
**Repetition**: the same words deliberately brought out again — consecutive repetition (沉默啊，沉默，不在沉默中爆发，就在沉默中灭亡 — 'Silence, oh silence: either one breaks out in silence, or one perishes in silence.' (Our translation.)), and spaced repetition (recurrence at the head of a paragraph, that is, repetition by direct address; the principle of the refrain in a song lyric). The line between this and the 'disease of repeating': **repetition is the incised mark of emphasis; repeating is redundancy of information** — delete it, and if the feeling is lost, it is repetition; delete it, and if clarity comes instead, it is the disease (set this beside the redundancy cases in [[modern-grammar/faulty-sentences]]). **Anadiplosis** (*dǐngzhēn* 顶真): the tail of the line above becomes the head of the line below — 归来见天子，天子坐明堂 — 'Come back and see the Son of Heaven; the Son of Heaven sits in the Bright Hall.' (Our translation.) (*Mulan shi* 《木兰诗》, 'The Ballad of Mulan'), a chain-like **advance by biting the tail**, the sense strung like pearls. **The circular figure** (*huíhuán* 回环): a mirror-spin from AB to BA — 人人为我，我为人人 — 'Everyone for me, and I for everyone.' (Our translation.), the most playful piece in the symmetry family. **Climax** (*céngdì* 层递): by three steps, of magnitude, of scope or of time, moving up (or down) one step at a time — 修身、齐家、治国、平天下 — 'cultivate the self, order the family, govern the state, bring peace to the world' (Our translation.) — three steps, and not a single connective is used; the logic climbs the stairs by itself.
:::

::: definition Allusion and euphemistic veiling: two arts of the implicit {#def-yongdian}
**Allusion**: ready-made old events and established lines, compressed into a new context — 怀旧空吟闻笛赋，到乡翻似烂柯人 — 'Cherishing the past, I vainly chant the rhapsody on hearing a flute; come to my village, I seem instead the man of the rotten axe-handle.' (Our translation.) (one couplet by Liu Yuxi 刘禹锡, two allusions: Xiang Xiu 向秀 hears a flute and remembers Ji Kang 嵇康; Wang Zhi 王质, his axe-handle rotten, feels the vastness of change). The account book of allusion is built on the same pattern as metonymy: **the reader's decoding rate decides the gain and the risk** — if the decoding goes through, then 语约而义丰 ('the words are few and the meaning is rich'; what Liu Xie 刘勰, in the 'Shi lei' chapter ('Citing Events') of *The Literary Mind and the Carving of Dragons* (*Wenxin diaolong*), calls 据事以类义，援古以证今 — 'Rely on events to set meaning in its class; cite antiquity to verify the present.' (Our translation.)), and if it does not, one is only rattling a bag of books. **Euphemistic veiling** (讳饰; euphemism, 委婉): by diminution, by a detour, and by metaphor, a shock pad is set under a hard reality — '走了' ('has gone'), '百年之后' ('after a hundred years'), '特殊岗位' ('a special post'), '经济下行' ('the economy heading down'). Euphemistic veiling belongs to a family with pragmatics in front and rhetoric behind (the whole course of semantic pejoration may be looked up in [[modern-vocabulary/word-meaning]]): **shock absorption and the covering of ugliness are a single line apart** — veiling in a eulogy is politeness; veiling in a financial report is rhetorical fraud.
:::

::: example One operation in the debate between parallel and free prose {#ex-piansan}
Turn the parallel sentences into free sentences, and then comment on how the two styles divide the labour. The original text of the scheme: 山线穿城，绿带织网，碧水流金，青峦拱璧 — 'A mountain-line threads the city; a green belt weaves a net; blue water flows with gold; green ridges arch about a jade disc.' (Our translation.)
::: solution
The free-sentence version: 城市沿山修了一条景观线，配套绿地系统和两条治理后的河道，周边山体检修复了植被。 — 'Along the mountain the city has built a scenic line, with a green-space system to match and two river channels after they were put in order, and has checked the surrounding slopes and restored their vegetation.' (Our translation.) The information is not lost; the picture is lost altogether. The other way about, to stuff the free version of an official document into the parallel style is 'parallelism with old wine in a new bottle'. The verdict on the division of labour: **parallel prose paints the form and raises the momentum; free prose reasons and narrates**. Li Yu 李渔 of the early Qing, discussing the language of couplets, also gives the warning again and again: what parallel sentences must guard against before anything else is *hezhang* and spinning idle. Parallelism in fours suits the caption of a planning drawing, and does not suit the body of a feasibility argument — **genre is rhetoric's first written judgement**.
:::
:::

::: history The thousand-year experiment of parallel prose, and two reckonings {#sec-pianwen}
Of the self-awareness of formal beauty, the time the Chinese world carried it furthest was the Six Dynasties: **parallel prose** raised antithesis from a figure of speech to the default format of a composition (four characters and six characters alternating, hence also called four-six prose). The 'Li ci' chapter ('Paired Phrasing') of *The Literary Mind and the Carving of Dragons* is the case made for this 'natural form of writing' — Liu Xie says 心生而言立，言立而文明 — 'The mind arises and language stands; language stands and pattern is manifest.' (Our translation.), and even heaven, earth and the myriad things come with parallel phrasing of their own (cloud-forms bright with colour; mountains and waters holding pattern) — and human pattern models itself on nature. A thousand years later, two reckonings: the **Han–Liu *guwen* movement** (ancient-style prose), in the name of 载道 ('carrying the Way'), condemned the froth of parallel prose (the mid-Tang, from the late eighth century); the **vernacular movement** (*báihuà*), in the name of 不民主 ('undemocratic'), again condemned the barrier of Classical Chinese (Literary Chinese; *wényán*) (around 1917). But the parallel style did not die outright: it fell back into couplets, stele inscriptions and epitaphs, the four-six of official documents, and the parallelism of letters of congratulation — and whenever an occasion needs a 'sense of gravity', it returns to the stage. **The history of the rise and fall of figures of form is the history of how Chinese configures its solemn register.**
:::

::: warning Four idle spins of form
First, **parallelism spinning idle**: 我们要重视、要高度重视、必须高度重视 — 'We should treat it as important, should treat it as highly important, and must treat it as highly important.' (Our translation.) The frame is stacked three deep and the information is zero; it is a replay machine, not a square formation. The ticket of admission to parallelism is **that each sentence carry one new variable**. Second, **antithesis with *hezhang***: 红旗招展歌盛世，锣鼓喧天庆丰年 — 'Red flags flutter, singing of a flourishing age; gongs and drums fill the sky, celebrating a year of plenty.' (Our translation.) The line above and the line below mean the same; two columns, one draft. Third, **a mania for obscure allusion**: five allusions in a row are like five silent riddles one after another, and the reader has not hands enough — there is only one iron rule, **allusions go no further than three; at three they turn obscure; obscure, and they fail**. Fourth, **the slippery slope of euphemistic veiling**: '阵亡' ('killed in action') rewritten as '掉队' ('fallen out of the ranks'), '亏损' ('a loss') rewritten as '负增长' ('negative growth') — the first time is politeness, the third time is public relations, the fifth time is evidence of a crime. A euphemism is polluted by what it covers, and so devalued (an arms race of semantic pejoration; the vocabulary lesson has already filed the case).
:::

::: exercise A check-up report {level=1}
Diagnose the following. ① 我们的队伍浩浩荡荡、气势磅礴、威风凛凛地开进食堂。 — 'Our ranks, vast and surging, overwhelming in momentum, majestic in their awe, marched into the canteen.' (Our translation.) ② 胜利属于过去，胜利属于现在，胜利属于未来，胜利属于大家。 — 'Victory belongs to the past, victory belongs to the present, victory belongs to the future, victory belongs to everyone.' (Our translation.) Give each the name of its lesion.
::: solution
① All three adjectives of the parallelism are unhooked from the point of 开进食堂 ('marching into the canteen') (big words pressed onto a small scene = the solemn and the comic out of joint, and the parallelism has become a generator of jokes); ② the frame is stacked four deep and 胜利 ('victory') brings no new information, and the four steps 属于过去/现在/未来/大家 ('belongs to the past / the present / the future / everyone') do not advance one beyond another — **parallelism spinning idle + not in accord with logic**, taken as one case. The name of the judgement, plus one sentence of remedy, earns full marks.
:::

::: exercise Write a couplet for graduation season {level=2}
Compose a graduation couplet of your own (an upper line, a lower line, and a horizontal inscription), and check three things yourself: that word classes match; the level-and-oblique ending (oblique on the upper line, level on the lower); and whether there is *hezhang*.
::: solution
A reference: upper line 三年灯火，题海同舟人识味 — 'Three years of lamplight; sharing a boat on a sea of questions, one comes to know the taste.' (Our translation.); lower line 六月蒲帆，云程各渡梦知途 — 'Cattail sails in the sixth month; each crosses a road in the clouds, and the dream knows the way.' (Our translation.); horizontal inscription 后会有期 — 'There will be a time to meet again.' (Our translation.) A model self-check: the final character 味 is oblique and 途 is level ✓; 题海同舟 ('a sea of questions, one boat') and 云程各渡 ('each crossing a road in the clouds'), one a gathering and one a scattering, opposed antithesis drawn taut ✓; no *hezhang* ✓. A piece that passes, plus evidence on all three points of the self-check, earns full marks.
:::

::: exercise An audit of euphemistic veiling {level=2}
Collect three real euphemisms (an official bulletin, a company's financial report, or your own dinner table — choose any domain). List them thus: the original matter → the veiling → an assessment of the shock-absorbing dose (so many marks for politeness, so many for covering ugliness), and predict the path of their 'polluted devaluation'.
::: solution
A reference case: 裁员 ('layoffs') → 向社会输送人才 ('delivering talent to society') (shock absorption = a minus mark; covering ugliness at the full — the irony has become a famous meme). The prediction of devaluation: once it has turned into a meme, 输送 ('delivering') itself becomes another name for layoffs, an extreme specimen of the speed at which a euphemism is killed off. What earns the marks: three examples that are real, an assessment of the dose that gives reasons, and a chain of devaluation that fits the rule that 'a euphemism is polluted by what it covers' (a correct application of the theory in the lesson).
:::

::: exercise A short essay: why symmetry pleases {level=3}
Write from eight hundred to twelve hundred characters. Starting from antithesis and parallelism, argue one concrete mechanism of 'form is content' (hint: the sense of order and the reward of prediction, time made spatial, the production of a sense of solemnity — the history of parallel prose returning to the stage is the bank of evidence). You must cite at least four figures from this lesson, and match each with one piece of real language (spring couplets, official documents, song lyrics or bullet comments are all acceptable).
::: solution
What earns the marks: the mechanism set out clearly and open to a test (for example: a prediction that hits = pleasure → the recurrence of parallelism's frame manufactures predictability, and the increase of content manufactures surprise — the formula 'the familiar + the unexpected'); each of the four figures matched with real language; a generalisation about the 'occasions on which parallel prose returns' (letters of congratulation / eulogies / official documents); one is not permitted to stop at 'parallel couplets are truly beautiful'.

::: summary
The labels in the hall of form are in fact only three sentences. **Symmetry is a free prepayment of beauty** (antithesis, the circular figure); **recurrence is the carving knife of attention** (parallelism, repetition, anadiplosis — but to spin idle is to replay); **the implicit is a business in decoding rates** (allusion, euphemistic veiling — gain and risk have the same source, the reader's account book). The thousand-year experiment of parallel prose tells us that a form will go out of date, and the sense of form will not — the solemn register always returns to the stage inside parallel couplets and parallelism. The next lesson closes the hall and goes upstairs, to the hall of sound: rhyme, level and oblique tones, reduplicated sounds — the piece of luggage Chinese carries with it.
:::
