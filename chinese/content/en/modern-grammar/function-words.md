---
title: "Function words"
---

Content words put up the house; function words lay the cement — the cement is worth little, but without it the whole thing is a dangerous building. Function words are half the realm of Chinese grammar: the four departments of prepositions, conjunctions, particles and modal particles, plus the three brothers unique to Chinese, the 'aspect particles'. This lesson takes stock of them by the four actions 'frame — relating — attaching — hovering'.

## Prepositions: makers of frames {#sec-jieci}

::: definition Prepositions and prepositional frames {#def-jiekuang}
A preposition is **a contractor for location and relation, grammaticalised out of a verb** (从, 自, 向, 对, 关于 and 按照 — all of them were once verbs; see [[modern-grammar/parts-of-speech]], the four-in-a-row of 给). The weapon that belongs to the Chinese preposition alone is the **frame**: '在……上/中/下', '对……来说', '把……怎么样', '连……都', '由……来/负责'. A frame locks the slot in front and the slot behind at the same time; leave out half and the sentence is incomplete (* 关于这个问题，进行了讨论 → the subject is missing, or the frame must be recast with 就). The discipline of its post, for a prepositional phrase: **as an adverbial it stands before the verb; as an attributive it carries 的**. In '给你买的书' the preposition and its object are fronted; only a few verbs of giving, of the '买给你' type, may take a complement after the verb (a Chinese complement, 补语, follows the verb or adjective; it is not an English complement).
:::

::: example Filling in frame constructions, and the lesion {#ex-kuang}
Judge, and repair: ①通过这次学习，使我提高了认识。②对于这件事上，他要负责。③这个问题，我同意了你的看法的意见。
::: solution
① **Abusing a preposition and drowning the subject** — '通过……使' is two frames crammed into one mouth; delete 使, or delete 通过. ② Frames hybridised (a mixed platter of '对这件事' × '在这件事上'); choose one of the two. ③ '同意你的看法' and '采纳你的意见' are two patterns stacked; keep one set. The three cases share one root of the illness: **a frame is a ready-made drawer; be greedy for more and the drawers will not stack** — of the family of 'structural confusion' in the chapter on faulty sentences ([[modern-grammar/faulty-sentences]]), half are born in function words.
:::
:::

## Conjunctions, particles and modal particles: relating, attaching, hovering {#sec-lianci}

::: definition Conjunctions and groups of connectives {#def-lian}
A conjunction does only one thing: **connecting**, and, in doing it, marking the relation (和/或/但/因为/所以/虽然/即使……). The greater part of the skeleton of a Chinese complex sentence is held up by 'pairs of connectives': '因为……所以', '虽然……但是', '即使……也', '只有……才', '只要……就' — a pair out of place is a common family of faulty sentences ('虽然…但…却': three markers, two links). Conjunctions and prepositions have quite a few amphibious members ('和、跟、比、叫'). The test: **if, between what precedes and what follows, it can be replaced by 并且 or 或, and if the subject is not a single term standing alone, it is a conjunction** (the two-reading ambiguity of '我和他去了' is lodged exactly on the axis of word class; see [[modern-grammar/complex-sentences]]).
:::

::: definition The structural particles '的地得' and the aspect particles '了着过' {#def-de}
**的** marks the attributive (红的苹果, 我的, 木头**的**桌); **地** marks the adverbial (慢慢**地**说); **得** marks the complement (跑**得**快, 好**得**很) — after the May Fourth pattern the three characters divided the labour; in speech they are homophonous (de), and in writing they are signposts of grammatical constituents; write the wrong one and the constituents' lines are crossed. **了**: (1) an aspect particle (吃了 — attached to the verb, it marks realisation); (2) a modal particle (吃了 — it marks a new situation or a change). Sentence-final '了₂' often has the two readings superimposed. **着**: continuation (挂着); **过**: experience (去过). The three brothers govern '体' (aspect) and do not govern '时' (tense) — in Chinese, time depends on context and on time words. This is the structural difference between Chinese and a language that has tense (an echo of the three temperaments in [[modern-grammar/what-is-grammar]]).
:::

::: example A continuum of modal particles {#ex-yuqi}
Set '走吧', '走吧？', '走吧！', '走吧……' and '走吧。' against '走不走': how do the modal particle and the intonation divide the labour? Then, with each of 呢, 吗, 吧 and 啊, make one sentence of a minimal pair in which none can replace another.
::: solution
Here 了 is no longer the meaning of completion, but a **mood of softening, or of making a proposal** ('吃个苹果吧' ≠ 吃了苹果). A question relies chiefly on intonation ↑; 吗 is the mark of the question; 呢 suspends a follow-up question ('你呢？' = the free-standing modal package of an elliptical question); 啊 is, in connected speech, a variant for softening and for response ('好啊 → 哇', assimilated to the coda of the preceding syllable — the liaison of the phonetics chapter [[sounds-of-mandarin/speech-sounds]], projected onto the layer of grammar). Minimal pairs: 你去吗？ (a yes–no question) / 你去呢。 (a reminder of what is already known) / 你去吧。 (a suggestion) / 你去啊！ (urging, with feeling) — the semantic proposition of the four sentences is entirely the same. **The modal particle is an 'interpersonal operating lever' outside the proposition.**
:::

::: history A thousand years of 的, and the May Fourth orthography {#sec-deshi}
In the medieval vernacular, '底/地/得' were originally one family (the structural particle was grammaticalised from the character 底; recorded sayings of the Tang and the Song, of the sort 圣人之下底事, 'a matter under a sage'). 的 was originally a content word meaning 'arrow target' or 'bright and clear'; the sound being close, it took over the office of 底. Around May Fourth, in order to translate the distinction European languages make among the relative clause, the adverbial and the complement, Liu Bannong 刘半农 and others promoted the **three-way orthography of '的, 地 and 得'** (的 for the attributive, 地 for the adverbial, 得 for the complement). It was established by the *Guoyu tonghan* 《国语通函》 ('circulars on the national language') of 1920 and by the textbooks that followed. After 1955, normative documents and textbooks confirmed the three-way spelling once more. Homophonous in speech and threefold in writing, it is an **orthography invented for the study of grammar** — an experiment rare in the history of Chinese, one that deliberately assigns characters according to syntax (compare [[chinese-characters/simplification]]: that one is simplification; this one is division).
:::

::: warning Three precautions with function words
First, guard against **piling bed upon bed and roof upon roof**: '对于…来说', '通过…使', '关于…的问题上' — nest the frames past the limit and the sentence is faulty. Second, guard against **sticking 了 on at random**: 了₁ clings tight to the verb, and 了₂, at the end of the sentence, marks a change; the two cannot exchange places. '我到了家了' is grammatical; '我到家了了' is not — function words have strict positional slots. Third, guard against **regulating Chinese by English**: that a Chinese conjunction may be omitted (parataxis) is not a defect. The co-occurrence of '因为…所以…', rather, has its own constraints in Classical Chinese (Literary Chinese; *wenyan*) and in speech — do not try Chinese by the English contract of 'one conjunction to a sentence'.
:::

- [ ] Qing-dynasty Mandarin (*guanhua*)

May Fourth promoted the three-way division so as to translate and introduce European syntax; from the 1920s on, it was established. 1955 was only a further confirmation (in speech they have always been homophonous).

::: quiz
The lesion in '通过这次学习，使我提高了认识' is (　).
- [ ] Semantic repetition
- [x] Prepositional frames stacked, drowning the subject
- [ ] A mismatch of tense
- [ ] A mismatch of classifiers (measure words)
::: solution
The frame '通过…' occupies the adverbial, and the frame '使…' needs a subject — two sets of frames press at once, and the subject comes up empty at both ends. Delete the head of either frame and it is cured.
:::
:::

::: quiz
The core meaning of sentence-final '了' (了₂) is (　).
- [ ] The past tense
- [x] Affirmation of a new situation, or of a change
- [ ] Another name for the marker of perfective aspect
- [ ] The modal '的' of certainty
::: solution
'要下雨了' and '我吃饭了' (no longer hungry) both mark a change or a new situation — Chinese has no tense. 了₂ is an all-rounder on the border between mood and aspect.
:::

::: exercise Frame surgery {level=1}
Repair, and classify: ①他的这种浪费粮食的现象，是应该批评的。②这个问题上，我们要明确。③他不仅去了，而且我也去了。
::: solution
① This sentence is grammatical (the emphatic frame '是……的' is intact); it needs only a slight adjustment of style — **a trap question: on seeing a sentence, first judge whether it is faulty; if it has no fault, do not repair it**. ② The head of the frame is missing; supply 在 → '在这个问题上，我们要明确'. ③ The position of the connective: the clauses before and after have different subjects (他/我), so 不仅 should be moved to before 他: '不仅他去了，而且我也去了'. A hit on each of the three cases (including the first, 'not repairing is the right answer') is full marks.
:::
:::

::: exercise Minimising modal particles {level=2}
Delete the modal particle from each of the sentences below, and write down the semantic and pragmatic loss: '这事我知道的。' '你先走嘛。' '吃了饭再走哇。'
::: solution
Delete 的 from '我知道的': what is lost is a mood of **soothing, or of a consoling assurance** (here 的 is close to the fused sound of the modal '的呀'; delete it and what remains is a cold statement). Delete 嘛 from '你先走嘛': what is lost is the sense of **a light urging, or of something that goes without saying**. Delete 哇 from '再走哇': what is lost is an intimate softening (哇 = 啊, a variant in connected speech, assimilated to the coda of 了). The point: a modal particle has no conceptual meaning; all of it lies in interpersonal meaning — an experiment in deletion is the method that proves its identity.
:::
:::

::: exercise Short essay: the power of function words {level=3}
Write eight hundred to twelve hundred characters: from the hundred years of debate over the three-way split of '的地得' (some advocate merging and simplifying them) to the rumour that '吗呢吧' are draining out of speech (are modal particles growing fewer in short videos?), argue that 'function words are the most fragile and also the most honest part of grammar' — fragile (the first to be simplified, or to be eliminated under the influence of a foreign language) and honest (in one second they lay bare the syntax of the mother tongue, and the register of age). Requirement: collect five real samples yourself (chat, advertisements, the classroom), including one comparison of modal particles across two generations of your own family.
::: solution
Marking points: the fragility claim has a mechanism (pressure to merge homophones, the cost of the written norm, generational replacement in speech); the honesty claim has evidence (misuse of modal particles is the most stubborn error of a second-language learner — transferring the mother tongue's 'system of manner'); the data are real and can be checked; the comparison of the two generations lands on particular words (the rise and fall of '吧/嘛/呀', the new uses of '哈/嗷').
:::
:::

::: summary
The four actions of function words: prepositions build frames (a drawer has its number of compartments; be greedy for more and they will not stack); conjunctions take charge of connecting (what may be omitted, and what is obligatory, keep their accounts within the register); particles stick on by attachment (的, 地 and 得 are signposts of constituents; 了, 着 and 过 govern aspect and not tense); modal particles hover at the end of the sentence (an interpersonal operating lever outside the proposition). A thousand years of the character 的: from a content word, to a stand-in for 底, and on to the three-way split of May Fourth — a miniature history of grammaticalisation + planning. Function words are the emptiest of all, yet they are the hardest shell of Chinese grammar: set a frame wrong and the sentence is incomplete; change the mood and you change the person. The next lesson looks at how these parts are fitted into phrases — immediate-constituent analysis takes the stage.
:::
