---
title: "Bai Juyi and the mid-Tang poets"
---

After the An-Shi Rebellion (安史之乱), the empire entered a long aftermath: the three systems of the regional military governors (*fanzhen* 藩镇), the eunuchs and factional strife were all leaking current at once. Poetry made **two opposite strategic adjustments**: one road **downward-compatible** (Yuan and Bai — Yuan Zhen 元稹 and Bai Juyi 白居易: written so that women and children could understand), one road **high-end encryption** (Han and Meng — Han Yu 韩愈 and Meng Jiao 孟郊: perilous right into the bone). This chapter walks both roads to the end, then picks up a foreshadowing left from the High Tang — those materials of Du Fu 杜甫 that "do not look like poetry": who caught them?

## Voices of the aftermath {#def-dali}

::: definition The Ten Talents of Dali: a group list and individual famous lines {#def-shizicaizi}
"The Ten Talents of Dali" (大历十才子) is **a school enfeoffed after the fact** (when Tang writers formed a group, it mostly depended on one anthology and one mouth; the list in circulation never had a fixed text: ten personal names, and since the Song the accounts have differed — **the boundary of the group is still blurred, and the boundary of the current is therefore clear: after the wars, poetry retreated into exchange and social verse, and grandeur yielded to fine crafting**). What Gao Zhongwu 高仲武 gathered in the *Zhongxing jianqi ji* 中兴间气集 ('Anthology of the Outstanding Talents of the Restoration') is the main force of this period — **an anthology is a party roll**. The representative scene: **Qian Qi 钱起, 'The Xiang Spirit Playing the Se at the Departmental Examination' (《省试湘灵鼓瑟》), "曲终人不见，江上数峰青"** ('The tune ends, and no one is seen; on the river, several peaks are green.' (Our translation.)) — **the most famous examination script in Chinese literature: a full-mark essay that happens to grow exactly on a full-mark aesthetic, so ethereal that the examination hall cannot be seen**; the legend of "a ghost passing on the pure lines" (at night he heard a poem recited before the steps, and the voice called itself "幽意" ('hidden intent') — **a fine line from the examination hall needs a ghost to endorse it, which shows precisely that people do not believe a human could have written it in the departmental examination** (*shengshi* 省试)). Liu Changqing 刘长卿 credited himself with "**五言长城**" ('the Great Wall of five-character verse') — **the mid-Tang's first slogan is about bulk, not about grandeur: the Great Wall = a defensive building, and the metaphor is very honest**.
:::

::: definition Han and Meng: the cry of what cannot get its peace, and the supply chain of the strange and perilous {#def-hanmeng}
**Theory**: Han Yu's 'Preface Sending Off Meng Dongye' (《送孟东野序》; Dongye 东野 is Meng Jiao), "**大凡物不得其平则鸣**" ('In general, a thing that cannot get its peace will cry out.' (Our translation.)) — **he enters everyone from Qu Yuan 屈原 to his own dynasty in the genealogy of "crying out", and issues a licence for every sound that is hard to hear** (the same move as Chen Zi'ang's 陈子昂 coup of *fenggu* 风骨 ('wind and bone'; vigour of feeling and structure): **under the flag of restoring antiquity, the substance of rebellion**). **Product**: Meng Jiao, "**冷露滴梦破，峭风梳骨寒**" ('Cold dew drips, and the dream breaks; a sheer wind combs the bones cold.' (Our translation.)) — **what the windscreen wiper scrapes is not the glass but the dream: the "hardness" of the Han–Meng school is writing the sense of pain into the nature of things**; Jia Dao 贾岛, "**鸟宿池边树，僧敲月下门**" ('A bird roosts in the tree by the pool; a monk knocks at the door under the moon.' (Our translation.)) — **the legend of "push or knock" (he charged Han's procession, and riding abreast they settled it) is a success-studies anecdote of examination society: the value of one line of poetry has to be priced by a chance meeting with the chief of the literary world**; "**二句三年得，一吟双泪流**" ('Two lines took three years to get; one chanting, and both tears flow.' (Our translation.)) — **a parable of refining the word: the mid-Tang promoted *kuyin* 苦吟 ('bitter intoning') from a habit to an identity**. Taken together: **the Han–Meng supply chain = theory (the cry of what cannot get its peace) → craft (*kuyin*, refining the perilous) → connections (Han Yu recommending and pulling them up) → titles (Jiao and Dao, the poetry-prisoner, the strange ghost) — a complete anti-mainstream production line**.
:::

::: definition Li He: the verb's coup {#def-lihe}
"**黑云压城城欲摧，甲光向日金鳞开**" ('Black clouds press the city, the city about to fall; the gleam of armour faces the sun, and golden scales open.' (Our translation.)) — **celestial bodies used as siege engines; "石破天惊逗秋雨" ('The stone splits, heaven is startled, and teases out the autumn rain.' (Our translation.)) — from the crack where Nüwa 女娲 mended heaven, "逗" ('tease out') teases out a rain: Li He's 李贺 revolution is in the verb: not decorating the world with adjectives, but legislating it anew with verbs** ([[rhetoric/figures-of-meaning]] an examination script beyond the syllabus in the subject of metaphor: **the distance between vehicle and tenor is stretched to light-years, and a verb forces them to kiss**); "**衰兰送客咸阳道，天若有情天亦老**" ('Withered orchids see the guest off on the Xianyang 咸阳 road; if heaven had feeling, heaven too would grow old.' (Our translation.)) — **letting the abstract "heaven" attend a farewell: time is the defendant, stone is the witness**; "**玉轮轧露湿团光**" ('The jade wheel rolls the dew and wets the round light.' (Our translation.)) — **the moon's cartwheel has rolled the dew wet: mythic materials are a prop store he refits as he pleases**. The limits of his life: he did not sit the *jinshi* examination, so as to avoid his father's taboo name ("晋肃" collided with "进士" — **a homophone gag of ritual law, which cut off a genius's place on the establishment: a famous accident of the Tang name-taboo system**); all his life a minor official, the *fenglilang* 奉礼郎; he died at twenty-seven; **the posthumous title "poetry ghost" (诗鬼) forms a couplet with "immortal" — Romanticism needs a shelf with one immortal and one ghost**. Du Mu 杜牧 himself compiled the collection for him and wrote the preface — **a first-rank peer scrambling to be executor of the estate**.
:::

## The strategy of the common style {#def-tongsu}

::: definition Bai Juyi: treating poetry as a product {#def-bdy}
**Programme**: the 'Letter to Yuan the Ninth' (《与元九书》; Yuan the Ninth, 元九, is Yuan Zhen), "**文章合为时而著，歌诗合为事而作**" ('Prose should be written for the times; songs and poems should be made for events.' (Our translation.)) — **the most famous product-requirements document in the history of Chinese literary criticism**; his own note to the *New Yuefu* (*Xin yuefu* 新乐府), "**其辞质而径，欲见之者易谕也；其言直而切，欲闻之者深诫也**" ('Its phrases are plain and direct, wishing that those who see them may easily understand; its words are straight and keen, wishing that those who hear them may be deeply warned.' (Our translation.)) — **even a "readability index" is written into the rules of the form (taking up, from the lesson on *The Literary Mind and the Carving of Dragons*, "事核言直" ('the matter verified, the words straight'): the honesty of exegesis is reincarnated as the format of remonstrance — the aesthetic of the official-document system, and Bai Juyi is the chief designer)**. **Reader testing**: the legend of "老妪能解" ('an old woman could understand it') (he wanted his poems written so that an old woman could understand them, and if she could not, he revised — **whether or not it was really tested, set that aside for now; this legend sets "intelligibility" up as a myth of method: Bai Juyi did not need to test in fact; everyone was already willing to believe that he was testing**). **The structure of the poem of sympathy**: 'Watching the Wheat Reaping' (《观刈麦》), "**今我何功德，曾不事农桑。吏禄三百石，岁晏有余粮。念此私自愧，尽日不能忘**" ('What merit, what virtue, have I? I have never worked at farming or mulberries. An official's salary of three hundred *shi*, and at the year's end grain left over. Thinking of this I am privately ashamed, and all day I cannot forget.' (Our translation.)) — **an upgrade of Du Fu-style bookkeeping: Bai Juyi does not describe how wretched the suffering is; he registers the gap in the accounts between himself and the suffering — the poet takes the defendant's seat, and the reader automatically enters the jury box**.
:::

::: example The author himself downgraded his own representative works {#ex-sijia}
When Bai Juyi edited his collected poems into a fixed form, he classified them himself into four: the categories of **remonstrance (*fengyu* 讽谕), leisure (*xianshi* 闲适) and sorrowful feeling (*ganshang* 感伤)**, and the rest, and he stated plainly that what he valued lay in the remonstrant poems (works of praise and blame, and of *xingji* 兴寄, 'lodging intent by evocation'), while the various classes of leisure and of sorrowful feeling were mostly the self settling itself amid illness and gloom. The fact: what has been most widely recited is precisely what he downgraded, the *Song of Everlasting Sorrow* (《长恨歌》) and the *Song of the Pipa* (《琵琶行》) — Yuan Zhen's 'Preface to Mr Bai's *Changqing Collection*' (《白氏长庆集序》) records that at the time "**禁省、观寺、邮候墙壁之上无不书，王公、妾妇、牛童、马走之口无不道**" ('On the walls of the palace offices, of abbeys and temples, of post stations and lookouts, there was nowhere it was not written; in the mouths of princes and dukes, of concubines and wives, of ox-boys and grooms, there was no one who did not recite it.' (Our translation.)) — **the hits all sit in the cell the author scored lowest**. Please answer: ① on what variable does the author's taxonomy run, and on what variable the reader's study of circulation? ② why could the programme of "for the times and for events" not stop the *Song of Everlasting Sorrow* from becoming a hit? ③ in the *Song of the Pipa*, "**同是天涯沦落人，相逢何必曾相识**" ('We are alike, fallen people at the sky's edge; meeting, why need we have known each other before?' (Our translation.)) — he sets the Marshal of Jiangzhou (江州司马) and the merchant's wife sitting together in one boat: what does this "sitting together" inherit from the "defendant's seat" structure of 'Watching the Wheat Reaping', and where does it slip?
::: solution
① **The author keeps accounts by political utility (praise and blame = capital that can be submitted upward: poetry is his second memorial); the market keeps accounts by story (love + music + a noted gentleman: Tang "popularity" took singing and copying as its unit — only narrative and the expression of feeling are fit for a melody)**. ② **The programme constrains subject matter, not the overflow of talent: inside the *Song of Everlasting Sorrow* the frame of "for the times" is filled with an obsession that is not "for the times" — a good long poem always leaks at the edge of its programme** ("**讽乐之体，叙事之情：白居易自己也没能分清**" ('The form of satirical song, the feeling of narrative: Bai Juyi himself could not tell them apart.' (Our translation.))). ③ **Inheritance**: both seat "my surplus" at the same table as "another person's suffering"; **slippage**: in 'Watching the Wheat Reaping' Bai Juyi stands on **the guilty side** (the original words about grain left over), while in the *Song of the Pipa* he and the pipa woman are **alike fallen people at the sky's edge** — a slide from a structure of sympathy towards a structure of identification (**demotion was the way he obtained his ticket of admission to "fallenness": the beginning of mid-Tang gentlemen making an aesthetic of political disappointment — this road of "sorrowful feeling", the song lyric (*ci* 词) of the Song would go on to walk**). Extra credit: if one can point out that the "division into four classes" is itself **the collected works' self-defence (the author classifies in advance on behalf of scrutiny and of moral censure)**, add marks.
:::
:::

::: intuition Two strategies of information {#int-lianglu}
Treat the Yuanhe court as a content platform: **Yuan and Bai = the sinking market** (language plain and familiar, subjects public, with "getting through" as the KPI — the *New Yuefu* is **an open letter written to the empire**); **Han and Meng = the high-end vertical** (with "the strange" as the moat — *kuyin* is **screening readers into fellow practitioners**). The situation the two roads share: **under Emperor Xianzong 宪宗 the avenues of speech were sometimes open and sometimes blocked (Wu Yuanheng 武元衡 was stabbed in the street; Bai Juyi was suspended and dismissed on account of his mother, over "赏花" ('Admiring Flowers') and "新井" ('The New Well') — memorials could not get through, and poetry took over remonstrance and speaking in one's own defence)** — **when the public channel is congested, every genre is reincarnated as metaphor**. The "change" in mid-Tang poetry is therefore not a decline, but **a reallocation of the same people across different bandwidths**: what could be said was written as *yuefu* (乐府); what could not be said was written as strange poems; what could not be said and could not be endured was written as the *Song of the Pipa*.
:::

::: history Mid-Tang coordinates {#sec-zuobiao}
**785–805**: the Zhenyuan examinations for presented scholars valued poetry and the rhapsody; the lingering notes of Dali and the new generation shared the same ground (**Han Yu passed the Ministry of Rites only on his fourth attempt** — **a league of those who had failed in the examination hall rewrote the standard of admission in literary history**); **803–805**: Yuan and Bai began to outdo each other in the *gexing* 歌行 ('song-style verse'); **806–820**: the Yuanhe style flourished — **"the Yuanhe style" (元和体) originally carried a derogatory sense (frivolous writers imitated Yuan and Bai's thousand-word long regulated verse of "exchanging poems by matching rhymes", and paper grew so dear in Luoyang 洛阳 that the authors were embarrassed): a style is invented by those who are imitated, and priced by those who copy**; **815**: Wu Yuanheng was assassinated, and Bai Juyi was demoted to Jiangzhou (**Year One of the *Song of the Pipa*: a setback in the imperial capital, once posted out, turned a profit in literature instead**); **824**: Yuan and Bai exchanged poems to the end of their lives (**the operating mechanism of the joint name "Yuan–Bai": more than three thousand replies, matching rhymes and linked verse — poetic exchange went from social courtesy to a game, and from a game to a genre**); **846**: Bai Juyi died — **he left the most complete self-archive of the mid-Tang: a self-compiled collection + a taxonomy + the long letter to Yuan the Ninth: author, critic and editor, the three offices in one**; **later ages**: "**元轻白俗，郊寒岛瘦**" ('Yuan is light and Bai is common; Jiao is cold and Dao is thin.' (Our translation.)) (Su Shi's 苏轼 eight-character verdict, whose circulation exceeds that of any *shihua* 诗话 ('remarks on poetry') — **a critic's golden sentence is a secondary creation: to be quoted is to win the prize**); the Jiangxi school of poetry honoured Du and also learned from Han (**the lineage of the strange and perilous was taken onto the regular strength as "the raw and the new"**); **now**: "an old woman could understand it" has entered the reading of product methodology (**the end of the strategy of the common style is internet slang: Bai Juyi has become the earliest spokesperson for thinking like the user**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls in reading the mid-Tang
**Treating the "Ten Talents of Dali" as a club** (**no manifesto and no journal: a school is a joint product of anthologies and the history of criticism — check the version of the list before you talk about style**); **marking by "reflecting reality"** (**the value of the *New Yuefu* lies in "proposals about institutions", the value of Han and Meng lies in "expanding the senses": one ruler for both roads, and both ends lose**); **reading the strange and perilous as a failure of obscurity** (**the verb in "石破天惊逗秋雨" ('The stone splits, heaven is startled, and teases out the autumn rain.' (Our translation.)) is precise: first restore its physics, then complain of its difficulty**); **ignoring Bai Juyi's contradiction** (**the inversion between his self-rating and what is recited is not a scandal, but a specimen for the sociology of literature: the author's intention, the text's tendency and the reader's choice, the three parties, are forever in negotiation**).
:::

::: exercise A requirements document {level=1}
Imitating the formal rules of the *New Yuefu*, write a "poetry product description" (100 characters) for one kind of public issue you care about: **it must have the four columns "its matter, its people, its language tier, its channel of circulation"**. In peer review: which item is Bai Juyi most likely to fault as "文胜质" ('form prevailing over substance')?
::: solution
Marking: all four columns present and constraining one another (**the channel decides the language tier: a subway poster = short lines, a short video = an opening hook**); the Bai Juyi test: **if the "matter" moves only oneself (no public reference), that is "文胜质"** — his hard standard: **after reading, one can retell "who was short-changed"**.

::: exercise An evidential study of the ghost couplet {level=2}
Qian Qi's "曲终人不见，江上数峰青" ('The tune ends, and no one is seen; on the river, several peaks are green.' (Our translation.)) appears in two works: 'The Xiang Spirit Playing the Se at the Departmental Examination' and 'Returning in Late Spring to the Thatched Hall on the Old Mountain' (《暮春归故山草堂》) (the poem that begins "谷口春残黄鸟稀" ('At the valley mouth, spring is spent, and yellow birds are few.' (Our translation.)) — **the position on the materials: both titles are found in the *Qian kaogong ji* 钱考功集 ('Collected Works of Qian the Kaogong') in common circulation**). ① Which kind of problem in the study of editions is this? ② Give two schemes of adjudication (the evidence on each side, "original to the departmental examination" and "an old couplet fitted to a topic"); ③ why does the legend of "a ghost reciting" attach itself to these two lines?
::: solution
Reference: ① **a problem of which text a passage belongs to, when one collection gives it different titles or the same text appears in more than one place (of the same kind as the authorship case of 'Climbing Stork Tower' (《登鹳雀楼》) — [[classical-chinese-2/commentaries]])**; ② **Scheme A: original to the departmental examination** (the set topic and its restricted rhyme forced out something heaven-made — **the legendary quality of "a *shitie* 试帖 ('examination poem on a set rhyme') can produce a famous line" is precisely what drives its circulation**); **Scheme B: an old couplet entered under a new topic** (a man of talent kept a "bag of lines", and the examination was a shelf — **when Tang writers sent round their presentation scrolls and offered up their writings, they relied on a stock of old work**); adjudication rests on **the earliest documents in which the two appear together**, and on how well the rhyme words fit; ③ **the legend issues a certificate for what, in the human world, cannot be believed: a full-mark script + a full-mark aesthetic = a non-human author is required** (the same state of mind as "thought entering the extraordinary" and "aid from the spirits" — a famous line is always granted a supernatural origin after the fact — taking up the case of Xie Tiao's 谢朓 "池塘生春草" ('Spring grass grows by the pool.' (Our translation.))).
:::

::: exercise Short essay: the common style is a kind of ambition {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**Bai Juyi's 'commonness' is his hardest technique: to say a complex problem simply is much harder than to say it in a complex way**". You must call on at least four items: **the legend that "an old woman could understand it"**, **the embarrassment of the "Yuanhe style" being imitated**, **the sitting-together structure of the *Song of the Pipa***, **the demand side of "Yuan is light and Bai is common"**; and you must deal with one opposing case: **is plainness a beautifying of suffering (the evil of the palace market (*gongshi* 宫市) in 'The Old Charcoal Seller' (《卖炭翁》) is packaged by verse into a "good poem" — is that morally safe)?**
::: solution
Marking points: the four mechanisms (**intelligibility is design; the embarrassment of imitation = the replicability of the common style biting back — the moat is too shallow; sitting together = the aesthetic dividend of identification; "Bai is common" is elite literature's anxiety about its share — commonness is a market result, not a defect of craft**); handling the opposing case: **acknowledge the tension — poetry makes evil "able to circulate", and also makes evil "able to be appreciated"; Bai Juyi's solution is to leave the anger at the level of fact (the accounts of the palace market) and to leave the music at the level of form — the two do not cancel** (those who blur the two together lose marks: one must take a side and admit the cost); a conclusion that writes "**simplicity is a kind of courage: it gives up the protection of the guild, and accepts the judgement of every reader**" earns an A.
:::

::: summary
The two roads, accounts closed: the aftermath (**the Ten Talents = a blurred current produced by an anthology; the "Great Wall" metaphor is very honest**), the encrypted line (**the cry of what cannot get its peace issues the licence; Li He's verbs legislate anew; the "poetry ghost" and the "poetry immortal" are shelved as a pair**), the compatible line (**programme + rules of the form + the myth of reader-testing + the structure of the defendant's seat**), the inversion (**the cell the author scored lowest circulates most widely — the three parties are forever in negotiation**). Method in one sentence: **to read a school, first check who compiled its list; to read a programme, first ask what it opposes**. The next chapter returns to the ground between court and battlefield: the late Tang — Du Mu's manner of historical argument, and Li Shangyin's 李商隐 multiple entrances.
:::
