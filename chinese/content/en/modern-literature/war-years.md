---
title: "Literature of the war years"
---

The chapter that closes the course looks at the torn map once it has been pieced back together: **1937–1949 is not “one piece” of wartime literature, but four literatures, each keeping its own accounts** — that of exile and the rear, that of the base areas, that of the occupied areas, and that of the circle of theoretical polemic. The chapter’s closing sentence is given away in advance: **the war did not make literature cease publication; it made literature proliferate; and in 1949 one of these schemes obtained a national edition**.

## Definition: a map of four lines {#def-sixian}

::: definition Four account-books in parallel {#def-sizhang}
**① The line of exile and the rear** (writers from Beiping, Tianjin and Shanghai withdrew along three corridors of migration towards Chongqing, Kunming and Guilin — **the inland move of universities and of the press = packing up the facilities of literary production and moving house: the exile edition of Chapter 1’s “infrastructure locks the outcome”: Southwest Associated University (西南联大) opened a Chinese department under a sheet-iron roof, and the fog-season performances sold tickets in the gaps between bombings — the map was redrawn, and the account-book did not stop**); **② the base-area line** (Yan'an — **from “go to the front” to “for the workers, peasants and soldiers”: a distribution network endorsed by policy: cadres, literature-and-art work troupes, wall poems, *yangge* operas (秧歌剧) — the first time the “reader” was taken as an object of engineering and built**); **③ the occupied-area line** (remnant subjects of the old capital in Beiping, Shanghai’s “Solitary Island”, and life under the puppet regime — **the thickness of censorship determines the folds of the text: this line was long absent from literary history, until the archives reopened from the 1980s (the national-border case of the frontier checkpoint in Chapter 9)**); **④ the theoretical front** (the debate on “national form”, the case of “subjective fighting spirit” — **the most enduring genre of the war years is polemic: pamphlets and critical notes are the mainstream of the periodicals of this period, and poetry and fiction, under four systems of wages, each look for a unit that will pay**). **Overall verdict: the four lines share one era; they do not share one inventory of genres, one language, or one “people” — to combine them into a single chapter of “the later period of modern literature” is itself an administrative convenience of the textbook (the modern-literature final paper of the classification exercise in Chapter 1)**.
:::

## The rear: specimen, meditation and madness {#def-houfang}

::: definition The metrology of a country’s fall in *Four Generations Under One Roof* {#def-sizhang2}
Lao She 老舍 made Beiping into a **specimen box** — **Xiaoyangjuan Hutong = Beiping = China, a miniature on three levels (the patriotic edition of the schematised exterior in Chapter 4: put every detail of a city on file, because the occupier is about to turn it into something else)**; **Old Master Qi’s theology of “the rules”** — **the longevity noodles are still gone through with, the threshold is still bowed to, and even when the city gate has changed its flag it “does not get in the way of eating noodles” — for the old man, the country’s fall is not a proclamation; it is a gradual change in firewood, rice and the asking-after (“an everyday metrology of the country’s fall”: Lao She’s terror does not write massacres; it writes the pastry shop changing its signboard — occupation is completed by the week)**; **four generations = four schemes** (Old Master Qi’s endurance, Tianyou’s righteousness, Ruixuan’s dilemma, and the great-grandchildren’s casting aside the brush to join the army — **the generational axis of the family novel is rewritten by the war as “a route map for responding to the country’s fall”**); the **final limit** of humour in Lao She’s manner (**Guan Xiaohe and Da Chizao’s “study of the Han” — control of the distance from the laughable to the frightening: humour is a buffer and also a scalpel, the wartime carrying-out of the verdict in Chapter 6: “To laugh is to take a posthumous photograph of the enemy, and it still has to be taken clearly”**). **Mu Dan 穆旦 and one line out of Southwest Associated University** (1940–45 — **“the poetry of meditation”: soldiers of the Expeditionary Force made the lyric subject (‘Praise’, and the poems of soldiers and beacon-fires all along the road into Burma) — the enlightener lays himself on the nation’s dissecting table: sacrifice is no longer celebrated; it is pressed with the question “我用我的血肉换你什么” (‘What do I get from you in exchange for my flesh and blood?’ (Our translation.)) — the wartime forming of the Nine Leaves’ “dramatisation of new poetry + the intellectual” (the true entry on the settlement day of Chapter 4)**); **Lu Ling 路翎, *Children of the Rich* (《财主底儿女们》), and “subjective fighting spirit”** (Hu Feng 胡风’s theoretical common formulation of 1945: **the writer must “thrust towards the spirit and thrust towards reality” (向精神突进、向现实突进); without subjective fighting spirit there is no true realism** — **Lu Ling’s madness and embrace: a limit test-run of psychological realism — the literary-historical postscript to this case: in 1955 the “Hu Feng case” ended in a political conviction, and in the 1980s it was rehabilitated according to law — every year in which a theoretical dispute ends in an archive ought to be entered on the register of the history of genre: the way criticism fails determines the volume at which writing is heard for the twenty years that follow**).
:::

## The base areas: engineering a new person and new forms {#def-gendi}

::: definition The *Talks* as a theoretical genre {#def-jianghua}
**The 1942 Talks at the Yan'an Forum on Literature and Art** (the common name), and where they sit in this lesson (Lesson 15): **they are not a footnote to “politics intervening in literature”; they are themselves a document of literary theory, and they answer all four questions this lesson has been asking** — **redefining literature** (“我们的文学是为人民大众的，首先是为工农兵的” ‘Our literature is for the masses of the people, and first of all for the workers, peasants and soldiers.’ (Our translation.) — the question of for whom is declared the fundamental question), **reordering the genres** (the swing between popularisation and the raising of standards: “提高是应该提高的，但不是在封建阶级、资产阶级、小资产阶级知识分子的‘上’，而是在工农兵群众‘前进’的方向提高” ‘Raising ought to be raising, but not “above” the intellectuals of the feudal class, the bourgeoisie and the petty bourgeoisie; it is raising in the direction of the “advance” of the masses of workers, peasants and soldiers.’ (Our translation.) — the common formulation — **the seed has to fall into the soil of popularisation: first let a person be able to read it, then talk of reading it well**), **replanning distribution** (literature and art are “整个革命机器的一个组成部分” (‘a component part of the whole revolutionary machine’) — the institutional high point of the lamplight clause in Chapter 2: the duty roster of the firelight is drawn up by the Centre), **retraining the author** (“学习社会”, “工农兵化” — ‘studying society’, ‘becoming workers, peasants and soldiers’ — the clause on the remaking of identity (the final assembly of the three-stage advance in the interface war of Chapter 7: from whom one writes for, and with what signs, through to whom one becomes)). **The *yangge* opera *Brother and Sister Reclaim the Wasteland* (《兄妹开荒》) and the new *yangge* movement** (1943 — **the wartime loading of folk forms: the waist drum replaces the Nine Leaves, and the headscarf replaces the moon, in the stock of images — an experiment in “forms of mobilisation”: not writing the people, but letting the people write for themselves: for the first time, the establishment of the lyric subject holds an open recruitment aimed at the workers, peasants and soldiers**).
:::

::: definition Zhao Shuli: a new reading of problem fiction {#def-zhaoshuli}
**Zhao Shuli 赵树理, *Xiao Erhei's Marriage* (《小二黑结婚》, 1943) and *Li Youcai's Rhymes* (《李有才板话》)** — **“problem fiction” changes its costume in the base areas** (the common formulation: by Zhao’s own account, the writing often began from “being provoked by a problem” — **the rural edition of the Enlightenment “diagnose the illness and give the medicine” in Chapter 6: this time the medicine is the marriage-registration regulations of the district office**); **the modern re-employment of the storytelling style (*pingshu*)** (**“written to be heard by peasants who have learned to read”: the oral skeleton of linked chapters, rhymed talk (*banhua*) and clapper verses (*kuaiban*), plus a clean vernacular in the dialect — the “musical score” clause of Chapter 4: he writes a score for two audiences at once, the newspaper-reading group and the head of the kang — a thorough rewriting of the format of distribution (the terminus in Chapter 8: genre is the channel; Zhao Shuli wrote the channel into the grammar**)); **the policy science of the “grand reunion”** (**the victorious registration of Xiao Erhei and Xiao Qin inside the new regime: the grand reunion of traditional storytelling is requisitioned as proof that policy can be cashed — the verdict of the drama lesson in Chapter 7, played in reverse: here “the closing = the accounts cleared” is endorsed by political power; the boundary condition of criticism appears at the same time: whatever problem policy has not yet reached, the fiction tends not to write as far as no solution (“the way a problem is posed determines whether the ending can be solved” — not a list of defects, but the clauses of the contract for a new genre: what the reader signs is this same contract**)). **Land-reform narrative** (Ding Ling 丁玲, *The Sun Shines over the Sanggan River* (《太阳照在桑干河上》), and Zhou Libo 周立波, *The Hurricane* (《暴风骤雨》) — **policy as the engine of the plot: the table of class status = the list of characters’ entrances; the struggle meeting = the structural climax; the assigning of class status = a contest, in narratology, over “the right to name” — the state changes from an “offstage factor” into the person who holds the pen for the whole length of the novel (the institutional internalisation of zero focalisation in Chapter 5: the point of view is no longer “zero”; it has acquired Party membership**)).
:::

## The occupied areas: the study of folds in the crevice {#def-lunxian}

::: definition Silence, oblique writing and the double reader {#def-zhezhou}
**Solitary-Island Shanghai and the areas under puppet administration** (1937–45) — **Yang Jiang 杨绛 writing comedies in Shanghai in the 1940s (the line of *As the Heart Desires* 《称心如意》 and *Turning the True into the False* 《弄真成假》), and Ke Ling 柯灵 and others holding the gaps in *Zhoubao* 《周报》 and *Shiji* 《世纪》 (the list of periodicals is taken only for the mechanism): censorship is not in the text; it is in the grammar of every sentence (the making of oblique writing into ordinary life, in Chapter 3: not a choice of rhetoric, but a technique of survival)**; **the “double reader” device** (**every sentence testifies at once to the censor and to one’s compatriots: compliant on the surface, a message passed in the dark — the political edition of unreliable narration in Chapter 5: unreliability here is a virtue, and to be reliable is to go to prison**); **Eileen Chang (张爱玲; the previous chapter) is the high point of this line, and not the exception** — **the love stories of the occupied areas all carry a mortgage (the price list of the previous chapter): the city falls in the background, while the characters care only about the rent and the marriage papers — “not asking about national affairs” is read as decadence, and is also read as a kind of honesty that refuses lofty rhetoric (the two readings are entered on the same form to this day: the literature of the occupied areas is the filing cabinet on which the history of effects diverges the most)**; **the confiscation of the archive after the war** (**for a long time the writers of the occupied areas existed as a name-list of “collaboration with the enemy”, and not as an existence in literary history: from the 1980s “literature of the occupied areas” became a discipline, and in the 1990s it entered general histories — the national border in Chapter 9 and the four account-books of this chapter: not a lack of documents, but line items deleted when the account-books were consolidated into one statement**).
:::

::: example ‘The army has come’: three schemes on one topic {#ex-sanfang}
“**一支军队开进村子**” (‘A body of troops marched into the village.’ (Our translation.)) — under each of the three literary schemes, write sixty characters, and for each mark one line: **who the reader is, who pays the manuscript fee, and what may be written and what must not be written**: ① rear-area realism (the line of Lao She and Lu Ling); ② the base-area scheme of the new character (the line of Zhao Shuli); ③ the occupied-area scheme of oblique writing (the double reader). Then judge one another’s work: **on one and the same bayonet, what each of the three grammars saw, and what each was hidden from, and by what**.
::: solution
① **The rear edition** (**write the “mixed” quality of the troops entering the village: the groans of the wounded, the locals looking on, the students’ speeches — a sociological layering of suffering, the ending left unresolved: reader = readers of the newspapers and periodicals; manuscript fee = the supplement; what may not be written = a cheap guarantee that “the War of Resistance must be won” (it would damage the qualification of the real)**); ② **the base-area edition** (**write the mutual claiming of “our own troops” and “the masses”: borrowing the kang, sweeping the courtyard, the literacy class — the three lines of the conflict (fear–suspicion–closeness) are walked to the end, and the ending stops on “如今他们成了公家人” (‘Now they have become people of the public household.’ (Our translation.)): reader = peasants who can read, and the work teams; manuscript fee = the public supply system; what may not be written = a bad specimen of relations between the army and the people — the subject matter has a table of class status**); ③ **the occupied-area edition** (**write only “昨夜村口的狗没叫” (‘Last night the dog at the village entrance did not bark.’ (Our translation.)) and “王三爷家粮囤高了一截，谁也没问” (‘The grain bin at Third Master Wang’s had risen by a tier, and no one asked.’ (Our translation.)) — the event’s presence is proved by a deformation of silence: reader = people who can read between the lines; manuscript fee = the black market and conscience; what may not be written = the four characters “他们来了” (‘They have come.’ (Our translation.)) themselves — and so the whole text does not contain a single gun, and the page is full of**); **the masks read one against another** (**the rear edition can see the complexity of society, and cannot see political power’s capacity to act; the base-area edition can see action, and cannot see the grey of the transitional zone; the occupied-area edition can see neither, and can see only the shape of “not seeing” — three kinds of blindness complement one another, and only together are they that China (Lesson 15: each theory is the historiographical edition of a pair of short-sighted spectacles: the four account-books have to be read together, and none of them is the general ledger)**).
:::

::: intuition 1949: a dividing line or a starting line {#def-fenshui}
Is this lesson to be taken as **the closing statement of modern literature**, or as **the opening words of contemporary literature**? — **The answer to the dispute over periodisation was written long ago in Chapter 1: periodisation is a classification exercise, and the purpose of the exercise is to open a course (if a university needs two courses, it needs two periodisations; if it needs one course, either 1942 or 1949 can serve as the doorplate)**. **The substantive judgement**: what 1949 changed was not the subject matter but the **institutional matrix** — **the five-piece set of the system of manuscript fees (the supply system replacing the market), distribution (unified purchase by the Xinhua Bookstore), the reader (workers, peasants and soldiers as objects of policy), the author’s status (a post on the establishment of the Writers’ Association) and criticism (the power to adjudicate the line) changed track all at once: the four lines merged into one main channel, and the rest changed their trade or fell silent — this lesson has gone from “the vernacular obtaining a licence” to “the people changing the licence”, and the thickest account-book in between is called distribution**.
:::

::: history A table of the main events of the eight years and the three {#sec-dashibiao}
**1937.7** The full-scale War of Resistance breaks out (**“the literary world goes together to the national emergency”: the All-China Association of Literary and Art Circles (全国文协) is founded in Wuhan in 1938, and Guo Moruo 郭沫若 takes charge of the Third Department of the Political Department of the Military Commission — the first nationwide establishment of wartime culture (old school and new school on the same form: the literary edition of the “year of the national emergency” in Chapter 1: the national contradiction temporarily freezes the internal accounts**)); **1938** the line of migration from Wuhan → Chongqing takes shape (**the corridor along which cultural institutions and universities moved west**); **1940** the debate on “national form” (**whether or not “folk form is the central source” — the sequel to the interface war of Chapter 7: in the war years even “the ear of the nation” had to be put out to tender**); **1942** the Yan'an *Talks*, and Mu Dan enters Burma with the Expeditionary Force (**two routes at the age of twenty-three: one goes to define the guiding policy for literature and art, one goes to serve as an interpreter-officer — people of the same generation begin to keep separate accounts**); **1943** the new *yangge* movement, and *Xiao Erhei's Marriage*; **1944** *Qu Yuan* (《屈原》) runs continuously in Chongqing (**the previous chapter: the public square is located in the theatre**); **1945** the first volume of Lu Ling’s *Children of the Rich*, and the thread of Hu Feng’s *On the Road of Realism* (《论现实主义的路》) takes written form; **1948** *The Sun Shines over the Sanggan River* is published (the Stalin Prize for Literature bestowed after the event in 1951 — **a world prize for the Chinese novel of land reform: one of the four lines begins to export a standard**); **1949.7** the First All-China Congress of Literary and Art Workers (**opens in Beiping; old hands of the “Literary Association” and the “new army of literature and art” link up — literary history moves its capital: from the newspaper offices of Shanghai to the documents of Beijing — the ceremony that opens the class of contemporary literature, in Lesson 16, stands on the last line of this chapter**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of wartime literature
**Measuring four lines with one ruler** (**to rank their seats by “high or low achievement” is to simplify the war into a school report: first ask each line’s contract (reader / manuscript fee / forbidden zone), and only then talk of literature — the structure at step 3 determines the aesthetics at step 5**); **taking the *Talks* as nothing but a political event** (**they are the text of literary theory with the greatest effect in twentieth-century China: all four questions — definition, ordering, distribution, training — are answered — the class in literary theory and the class in history must share a desk here (the map of four elements in Lesson 15; this is the only version completed in the form of policy)**); **the polarisation of the occupied areas** (**“all of them collaborated” and “all of it was underground struggle” are both propaganda: the great majority of those who wrote were making a living in the crevice — understanding the “double reader” device is harder than issuing them a good-citizen certificate or a pass, and it is also more nearly right than either**); **taking the “new person” as nothing but propaganda** (**it is an aesthetic project in which the goods are genuine and the price is the form: the open recruitment of the lyric subject — the point of success (Zhao Shuli’s miracle of spoken language) and the cost (the disappearance of the grey) are entered at the same time: the audit standard for experimental literature is one and the same throughout (the double-column table in Chapter 6)**).
:::

::: exercise Four-line archive cards {level=1}
For any one work of 1937–1949, make a **four-line archive card** (if it crosses lines, mark its main line and its shadow line): writer — place — channel of publication — source of the manuscript fee or of supply — a portrait of the reader — the measure kept around the forbidden zone; and judge in one sentence: **which account-book it belongs to**.
::: solution
Marking: **all six columns filled** (the channel of publication and the “portrait of the reader” are the whole of this question: if *Thunderstorm* is publicly performed in Chongqing and discussed at the Lu Xun Academy of Arts (鲁艺) in Yan'an, it belongs to two account-books — **the channel is nationality**); a sharp eye for the shadow line (Eileen Chang’s main line is the occupied-area line, and the shadow line runs straight on to the change of name and the republication in postwar Shanghai and Hong Kong — citing either one is permitted).

::: exercise Short essay: the years of changing the licence {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing “**In 1917 writers were issued with the licence of the vernacular; in 1949 they exchanged it for the licence of the people: both times the ‘reader’ was invented all over again — the thirty-seven years of modern literature are thirty-seven years in the history of licences**”. You must draw on at least these five: **the language revolution (the theory that institutions reimburse)**, **the literary field and the unit that pays (societies / periodicals / the supply system)**, **the format of distribution determines genre (at least two cases)**, **the four questions of the *Talks***, **the merging of the four account-books**; and give a ruling: **should “the end of modern literature” be fixed at 1937, 1942 or 1949 — use the dispute over periodisation to test the view of definition you yourself took in Chapter 1**.
::: solution
Points for marking: the five mechanisms (**reimbursement = victory and defeat are settled at the administrative window; the unit that pays = the base of the field; the channel = the nationality of the genre; the four questions = a general programme in the form of policy; consolidating the statements = deleting line items**); the ruling on the period (**the 1937 account — the war cuts off the “experimental period”; the standard is the mode of literary production; the 1942 account — the standard is the coming on line of the “new scheme for literature and art”; the amount by which it is brought forward is the greatest, and it suits intellectual history; the 1949 account — the standard is the institutional matrix changing track, which agrees with the administration of the textbook; the mark does not turn on which is chosen, but on whether one admits that “the standard of periodisation = the source of funding for this course, and the way faculties and departments are set out” (the meta-level confession of the taxonomy in Chapter 1: the textbook, too, is a format of distribution**)); if the conclusion can run “**Licences expire, but the driving goes on: 1949 only laid it down that some stretches of the road are one-way — and every turn this vehicle, the Chinese language, has taken since is still being marked on the report card of that first driving test in 1917: this lesson completes its course, and the examination hall is handed over**”, that is an A.

::: summary
The four account-books, filed: the rear (**the specimen box and the poetry of meditation: the country’s fall measured by the week, and sacrifice laid on the operating table**), the base areas (**the distribution project: a general programme of four questions, from definition to training; the new *yangge* = the open recruitment of the lyric subject; Zhao Shuli wrote the channel into the grammar**), the occupied areas (**the study of folds: the double reader and the deformation of silence — the archive was forty years late**), the link-up (**1949.7: literature moves its capital, and the five-piece set changes track**). One sentence of method: **first ask which account-book this account is entered in**. The thirty-seven years have been told through: **from the licence of the vernacular to the licence of the people — the completion of the republic of modern literature is the enrolment of contemporary literature**.
:::
