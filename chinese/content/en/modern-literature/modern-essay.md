---
title: "The modern essay"
---

While poetry is fighting its lawsuit over metre and fiction is taking apart the insurance on narrative, **the essay has already, quietly, taken the first bestseller shelves of the New Literature** — this chapter examines the tenant in this genre who is best at disguise: **it looks the least difficult, and is therefore the hardest to write; it claims only to be chatting at random, and is therefore the best at arranging the scene**.

## Definition: take out the licence first, then put up the building {#def-zhiliu}

::: definition The naming event of the "beautiful essay" {#def-meiwen}
**In 1921 Zhou Zuoren 周作人 published 'Meiwen' 《美文》 ('The beautiful essay'), and applied for a business licence for "论说性的艺术文" ('argumentative writing of an artistic kind')** (the gist: the modern essay may combine **the art of argument with the art of narrative and of expressing feeling**; it is not fitted to the exposition of the classics, nor to the historian's brush, and it is fitted to be called a "beautiful essay") — **the textbook scene of the theorem in Chapter 8, "the genre precedes the single piece": first a manifesto, and afterwards the "recorded-sayings form, the informal essay, the little piece", flood and savage beast, come surging in step under the one name (one argumentative submission of two thousand characters set the business scope of the essay for the twenty years that followed)**; the other two pages of the household register: **"monologue and letter box"** (the letter form, the diary form, random jottings — **"the turning of private forms into public writing": the diary and the letter were forms "not to be read"; once printed in the newspapers and magazines, the institutionalisation of self-exposure is complete — the fulfilment, at the level of form, of the New Literature's "humane literature" (人的文学) (Chapter 1): raising privacy to the rank of literature was its most thorough revolution**); **claiming kinship** (lecture scripts of the kind of Zhou Zuoren's separate volume *Zhongguo xin wenxue yuanliu* 《中国新文学源流》 (*Sources of the Chinese New Literature*) take the Ming and Qing little pieces of *xingling* (性灵, 'native sensibility') for distant ancestors — **an outline of "the cyclical history of carrying the Way and expressing intent" (the gist): the Literary Revolution = one restoration, by the tradition of expressing intent (*yanzhi* 言志), against the orthodoxy of carrying the Way (*zaidao* 载道, writing as a vehicle of the Way) — the most contradictory move of the New Literature: using a licence for restoring the old to sell the goods of the new (Chapter 1: the power to define, and the study of resources — a genealogy is a financing document)**).
:::

## Three tones: mild plainness, humour, intellect {#def-sanqi}

::: definition The school of mild plainness {#def-chongdan}
Zhou Zuoren, 'Beijing de chashi' 《北京的茶食》 ('Tea foods of Beijing'): "**我们于日用必需的东西以外，必须还有一点无用的游戏与享乐，生活才觉得有意思。我们看夕阳，看秋河，看花，听雨，闻香……**" ('Apart from what is necessary for daily use, we must also have a little useless play and enjoyment, and only then does life feel to have any point. We look at the evening sun, look at the autumn river, look at the flowers, listen to the rain, smell the fragrance……') (Our translation.) — **"the useless" as an aesthetic programme (the lived version of Chapter 1's "the minimum wage of literature": that little above survival is the essay's only legitimate scope of business)**; the fingerprint of the style: **short sentences, plus an even temperature of classical and vernacular (taking only the mechanism: the sinew and bone of classical Chinese, the flesh and blood of the vernacular, the joints of Europeanisation, the three held at a constant temperature, the temperature of "chat" — *chongdan* (冲淡, 'mild plainness') is not an absence of flavour, it is the managing of the heat: every strong feeling is stewed down to a low temperature that melts as it enters the mouth —** a seat the study will not give, so he puts up a "Bitter Rain" studio (苦雨) of his own to sit in: the "rain" is bitter, and the "bitter" is slowly savoured: a concession in the naming is an occupation by the stance**. The shelf life of mild plainness**: court opens in the next section (the crisis of 1935) — but first record its speech for the defence: **under the White Terror and the system of censorship, "talking of the tiger" is daring to talk, and "not talking" is also a way of talking: mild plainness is a cost sheet of silence, drawn up as a style**.
:::

::: definition Humour and intellect {#def-youmo}
**Liang Shiqiu 梁实秋, 'Yashe' 《雅舍》 ('The elegant cottage')** — "**'The elegant cottage' is both mean and leaking; 'when the wind comes, it gapes like a cool pavilion'; 'on a moonlit night it is so fine there is nothing to be done' — each defect is packaged, item by item, into a style (the gist): writing secluded ease in the midst of displacement — the essay as a technique of psychological self-defence: a modern vernacular footnote to 斯是陋室 ('this is a humble room')**" — **humour = payment by instalments in a negotiation with the predicament (no revolution, no indictment, no kneeling: "concession" is exchanged for the residual right of a subject not wholly swallowed by the predicament — the earmarked item "capital from yielding" at the exchange of Chapter 2)**; **Qian Zhongshu 钱锺书, *Xiezai rensheng bianshang* 《写在人生边上》 (*Written in the Margin of Life*) ('Chuang' 《窗》, 'The window')** — the mechanism: a metaphor of the kind "the window = the eyes of the house" is pushed down to the bone, and then "the door" and "the window" are sent into court to argue public against private (the whole of 'The window' is a cross-examination conducted in metaphor) — **an arms race of metaphor: one idea comes on stage wearing three or four coats of figure — the intellectual informal essay refits the sense of distance in 'Duanzhang' 《断章》 ('Fragment') as a microscope of satire (the virtuoso version of Chapter 8's "selection function": Qian Zhongshu's stock of metaphors, and his grammar, both refuse automation — Shklovsky's 什克洛夫斯基 defamiliarisation, a full-marks answer paper in the Shanghai essay of 1940)**. **The two schools, taken together: humour is a hedge fund of feeling; intellect is the accountant who refuses even the interest — neither is "sincere to a frightening degree", but both are sincere to a safe degree — the three tones of the essay (mild plainness, humour, intellect) = three allocations of position (仓位, a holding) for "how, in a disordered age, to place the 'I'" (the finance of literary form in Chapter 6's doctrine of posts).**
:::

## 1935: the trade association of tea and the dagger {#def-wei}

::: definition The crisis of the little essay comes to court {#def-weijing}
**The debate on the *xiaopin* essay (小品文) in 1935** (the criticism on the left came from the line of Zhou Yang 周扬 and Hu Feng 胡风; Lu Xun 鲁迅 held a more finely drawn position). The gist: **the situation was critical, and the *xiaopin* essay tended to turn into a "little ornament" (小摆设) —** the evidence used is Lu Xun's own open admission in 'Xiaopinwen de shengji' 《小品文的生机》 ('The vitality of the *xiaopin* essay'): "**生存的小品文，必须是匕首，是投枪，能和读者一同杀出一条生存的血路的东西**" ('The *xiaopin* essay that is to survive must be a dagger and a javelin, a thing that can, together with the reader, fight out a bloody path of survival.') (Our translation.), and one sentence of the received account is added: what Lu Xun criticised was the direction of "**将小品文变成小摆设**" ('turning the *xiaopin* essay into a little ornament'). **Key points of the court record**: ① **the two faces of one authorship** (of the Zhou brothers, one hands over a tea bowl and one a dagger — **a controlled comparison under the "collateral" clause of Chapter 3: the two brothers placed opposite bets on "the function of the essay", while their training in the form came from the same source — style is the best proof of the selection function: the same pen can call up two tones; the choice takes place in the position, not in the wrist**); ② **the substance of the debate is not "whether leisurely ease is right or wrong", but "who the page belongs to"** (in the 1930s the market in newspapers and periodicals expanded, while the supplement editors' supply of copy and the censorship tightened at the same time — "tea and the dagger" = two strategies of distribution); ③ the after-effect of the judgement (**the "dagger *xiaopin*" became mainstream in the 1940s and ran straight through to *zawen* (杂文, the polemical essay) (Chapter 3), while the "leisurely *xiaopin*" was long out of issue after 1949, and found employment again when "the essay returned" in the 1980s — the list of which forms revive is a barometer of policy (Chapter 1's institutional account: the essay is the most sensitive, because its licence is the thinnest)**).
:::

## The soliloquy mode: lyricism with the workstation withdrawn {#def-duyu}

::: definition The echo-wall experiment {#def-huiyin}
**The line of He Qifang's 何其芳 *Huameng lu* 《画梦录》 (*A Record of Painting Dreams*)** (the 1930s): **lyrical essays with no speaker opposite — the "soliloquy" mode (独语) (the experiments of the same period by Fei Ming 废名, Li Ni 丽尼 and Lu Li 陆蠡 — taking only the mechanism)**: **the reverse operation of the apostrophe clause in Chapter 6: the "you" position is vacated completely, and the lyrical subject fits the room out against its own echo** (**giving loneliness a shape: the colour, the temperature and the plans of the building all come from correspondence that no one receives** — a paraphrase of our own making, not to be set down to the author; resources of the sentence: long modifiers, pivotal constructions, reiteration — the devices of poetry are carried into the essay: the soliloquy mode = the case of the essay's border with the poetic (the gentle version of the "breach of contract" clause in Chapter 8: it does not break the genre; it only moves the furniture from next door over, and lives there)). **Entered in the theoretical register: when the essay gives up the "letter box" (its quality of dialogue) and keeps only the "monologue", it measures how much vacuum the genre can bear — the conclusion: the essay can survive with no audience, but it must rewrite the definition of "hearing": soliloquy is hiring oneself as the reader — the earliest Chinese licence for a polyphony of the self**.
:::

::: example One topic, three writings: sheltering from the rain {#ex-biyu}
Take "**在山寺里避了一夜雨**" ('sheltered from the rain for a night in a mountain temple') and, under each of three contracts, write 100 characters: ① mild plainness (in the manner of Zhou Zuoren — a list of useless play and enjoyment; verbs of feeling forbidden); ② the dagger (in the manner of Lu Xun — an outburst, on a rainy night, of the "正人君子" ('gentlemen of rectitude'); there must be one pair of quotation marks); ③ soliloquy (in the manner of *A Record of Painting Dreams* — no audience; write only with perceptions and objects). For the mutual review: **in the same night of rain, what has each of the three forms "omitted", and in which position has each placed the "I"**.
::: solution
① **The mild-plainness version** (tea, the sound of the tiles, the fading of an old mural, a cake bought before the rain — **the rain itself is not beautiful; the fastidiousness toward the rain is what is beautiful: the aesthetic = the plating of attention; the "omission": darkness, fear, cold — the method of stewing at a low temperature forces the goods to be returned**); ② **the dagger version** (an outburst of the type "**这雨一下，倒显出许多人的‘深沉’来了**" ('Once this rain falls, it rather shows up a good many people's "profundity".') (Our translation.) (this sentence is cast by us — the marksmanship is imitated, not the target) — **the rain is a backdrop; the target is "respectability"; the omission: every physical detail of the rain — the inventory of mild plainness is, in the dagger version, entirely ammunition**); ③ **the soliloquy version** (a bell, a lamp-wick, the reflection on the stone steps, someone else's umbrella in a dream — **thing linked to thing in perception takes the place of the event; the omission: any dialogue and any conclusion — with the "you" position withdrawn, even reflection counts as a courtesy**); a summary of the positions: **mild plainness: I am a collector; the dagger: I am the plaintiff; soliloquy: I am the court itself — three rains in the same night, falling on three kinds of "instrument of self-authorisation": the essay's "writing just as one pleases" turns out to be three different contracts**.
:::
:::

::: intuition The paradox in the study of genres {#int-bunlei}
The essay is **the genre in the New Literature that matured earliest, and the hardest to define** — because it **has no licence for its form** (fiction has plot, poetry has the line break, drama has the form of dialogue; the essay has only the all-purpose signboard of "**artistic casualness**" — **Chapter 8, "a genre is an agreement": the essay's agreement is so thin that almost a single clause remains: do not be too much like an academic paper, and do not be too much like a poem**). **From this the paradox is born: the freest genre = the genre most easily written to excess = the genre most in need of an endorsement by the person** (**when metre, plot and structure are all absent, all the reader can check is "whether the person writing can be relied on" — the old saying "文如其人" ('the writing is like the person'), which Yuan Haowen 元好问 had long since shot through (Chapter 8), becomes, in the essay, the only usable (though unreliable) instrument of falsification: what the essay sells is the author's credit**). **This chapter's prescription: in reading an essay, check three things first — check the tone (to whom it speaks), check the position (where the "I" sits in a disordered age), check the concessions (what it deliberately does not write: mild plainness does not write fear, the dagger does not write the rain, soliloquy does not write people — where the omission is, there the style is).**
:::

::: history The annual inspection of the essay's licence {#sec-nianjian}
**1921**: the naming in 'The beautiful essay', and the column of random jottings (随感录) in *Xin qingnian* 《新青年》 (*New Youth*) had already opened the road (**the licence and the dagger were shipped in the same year**); **1924–28**: the age of *Yusi* 《语丝》 ('Threads of speech') (**the polemical essay and the informal essay in the same pot: the "talking at will" of the *Yusi* manner = the period when mild plainness and the dagger were packed together**); **1930–32**: *Lunyu* 《论语》, *Renjian shi* 《人间世》 ('The human world') and *Yuzhou feng* 《宇宙风》 ('Cosmic wind') (**Lin Yutang's 林语堂 matrix of magazines, "with humour and leisurely ease as weapons": humour was institutionalised into the character of a magazine — the main arena of the commercial war of tea and the dagger in Chapter 3**); **1935**: the debate on the *xiaopin* essay (**this chapter comes to court**); **1936–37**: Li Ni's *Haixing* 《海星》 ('The starfish'), Lu Li's *Qiulu ji* 《囚绿记》 ('A record of imprisoning the green'), He Qifang's *A Record of Painting Dreams* (**the high point of the soliloquy mode; *A Record of Painting Dreams* won the essay prize of the *Dagong bao* 《大公报》 Literary Award in 1936 — a prize for the "pure essay" = the year the genre stood on its own**); **1938–45**: wartime reorganisation (**"leisurely ease" cut off from supply: the reportage form and *zawen* shared an office in wartime, and "soliloquy" fell back on the academy — forms, too, divide into wartime materiel and luxuries**); **after 1949**: the Yang Shuo 杨朔 model (**the model of the "poeticised essay": the essay is taken in under the fixed recipe of "scene + feeling + principle" — the industrial application of the "two-part form" in Lesson 15: making the essay a school subject = making it a recipe**); **the 1980s**: "the return of the essay" and "the weight of the confessional form in Ba Jin's 巴金 *Suixiang lu* 《随想录》 (*Random Thoughts*)" (**letting the essay out of the recipe: the call to "speak the truth" = a maintenance notice for the system of credit**); **today**: unlimited credit extended to "the essay" on public accounts (**anything at all may be an "informal essay": the other face of the all-purpose signboard is all-purpose dilution — this chapter's prescription is still the three checks: tone, position, concession**).
:::

::: warning Four pits in reading the essay
**The master key "形散神不散" ('the form scatters, the spirit does not')** (**this is phrasing from lesson plans of 1960, used to describe and not to judge — to inspect *A Record of Painting Dreams* by it is a wrongful case: its "spirit" is precisely scattered away to loneliness; ask about the contract first, and only then talk of scattering and not scattering**); **rejecting the writing on account of the person / transmitting the person by the writing** (**the essay sells credit, and so a stain on the moral quality of the writing is marked down at once — a return visit to the Yuan Haowen clause: credit is the essay's means of marketing, not the security it pledges: checking the tone comes closer to the text than checking the file on personal character**); **reading mild plainness as weakness** (**a choice of position is not a rating of cowardice: a low temperature is the managing of the heat — and the left-wing criticism of 1935 amounts to only half a verdict: the dagger and the tea each won its own market (Chapter 2: two tax burdens, two pleasures)**); **the modern prejudice that "the little piece = lightness"** (**in the two hundred characters of 'Tea foods of Beijing' a whole charter of "the right to be useless" is hidden: a light form is often the packaging of heavy goods — Chapter 8's ban on adjectives: first count what it has ceded, and only then praise it for being mild**).
:::

::: exercise A list of concessions {level=1}
Choose a short piece by a master of the modern essay (drinking tea, watching the snow, or keeping a cat will all do), **list its "list of omissions" (at which line the unwritten fear, the unwritten rain and the unwritten person are each absent)**, and decide its position (collector / plaintiff / the court); add 60 characters on how much money this silence has saved you ("what it did not say, and how that could have destroyed it").
::: solution
Marking: **the omitted items can be pointed to by the line** (of the order of "the whole piece writes of tea, and not one character mentions the air-raid siren next door" — **the concession is the style**); the decision on position has evidence from the tone (to whom it speaks, what it says, what it does not say); a sentence that settles the account ("**mild plainness does not write fear, and only then does the fear look so mild; an omission is not a nothing, it is stock under lock — to read an essay is to read its off-book assets**").
:::

::: exercise Retuning tone and position {level=2}
Take one complaint of yours from last week (a takeaway, or a traffic jam, either will do) and rewrite it once for each of the three positions: **the collector's version (a list of useless fastidiousness), the plaintiff's version (one pair of quotation marks with a round chambered), the court's version (an impersonal record of perception)**; count the "number of adjectives that survive" in the three versions.
::: solution
Marking: **the three testimonies of the same event do not cross into one another's lines** (the semantic core of the complaint does not move; the tax bill of the style changes with each); **the method of counting adjectives** (the mild-plainness version has the most adjectives, but the mildest — of the sort "fastidiously old"; the dagger version concentrates its adjectives in a single pair of quotation marks ("delivered 'on time'"); the court version approaches zero — **the inverse curve of the density of adjectives and the intensity of tone: this is the physics of "mild plainness"**).
:::

::: exercise A short essay: credit, concession and the page {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**the essay is the balance sheet of the New Literature: it does not produce plot; it only registers what the author dares to cede and what to keep — under every cup of tea is entered a debt that has not been declared**". You must call on at least five of these: **the naming event of the beautiful essay**, **claiming kinship (the restoration of expressing intent)**, **the three tones and the three positions**, **the struggle over the page in 1935**, **the echo wall of the soliloquy mode**; and adjudicate: **"the decline of the essay" is announced once every ten years — what is declared dead is, in the end, the form, the market, or the credit**.
::: solution
Marking points: the five mechanisms (**naming = the agreement precedes the work; claiming kinship = a genealogy is a financing document; position = style is the placing of the self; the page = two strategies of distribution; the echo wall = a test of how much absence of an audience can be borne**); the adjudication of the declaration of death (**the form: not dead (it cannot even be bothered to have a definition, and so cannot be proved dead); the market: transferred (newspaper supplement → public account → short-video copy — another chapter of the channel determining the genre); credit: this is what each "theory of decline" really points to — when the "I" becomes a persona that can be produced in batches (the Xiaohongshu manner, AI writing), the essay's only instrument of falsification fails — "the history of the crises of the essay is the maintenance record of the system of the author's credit: at each major overhaul, the instrument of falsification is changed once (in 1935 it was changed for the dagger, in the 1980s for 'speaking the truth', and today it may be changed for 'a presence that can be verified' — the essay branch of the AI line in Chapter 10)**); if the conclusion is written "**fiction may lie ten thousand times; the essay can be honest only once — and so it is forever scarce: it is not the form that is scarce, it is people who will set their credit against the account that are scarce**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The licence, filed: naming (**take out the licence before putting up the building: a living specimen for the study of genres**), tone (**mild plainness is the managing of the heat, humour is payment by instalments, intellect is the refusal of interest: three positions in which to place the "I"**), crisis (**1935: the war of distribution strategies between tea and the dagger — the brothers placed opposite bets**), soliloquy (**the "you" position vacated: hiring oneself as the reader**), paradox (**the freest = the most dependent on credit: in reading an essay, check three things — tone, position, concession**). One sentence of method: **first check to whom it keeps its mouth shut**. The account still open from the chapters on fiction — the map of schools, societies and debates — is unfolded in the next chapter.
:::
