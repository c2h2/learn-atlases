---
title: "Shen Congwen and the Beijing and Shanghai schools"
---

The spoken drama of the previous chapter grew up in the city; this chapter spreads the map out and draws it again — **Chinese literature of the 1930s is not one river, it is a table of price spreads**: the desks of Beiping, the page-space of Shanghai, the ferry of western Hunan, the flats of the occupied zones, each names its own price, and none gives another change.

## Case One: the birth record of two camps {#def-lunzheng}

::: definition The label precedes the camp {#def-biaoqian}
**The Beijing–Shanghai debate (京海论争) of 1933–35**: Shen Congwen 沈从文 wrote in *Dagong bao* 大公报, in the supplement *Wenyi* 《文艺》 ('The Attitude of the Literary Man', 《文学者的态度》, and other pieces), criticising the Shanghai men of letters as **a hybrid of *mingshi* air and merchant air** (*mingshi* 名士, 'a celebrated man of letters with an unconventional pose'; the usual formulation is “用笔与用舌的生意”, 'a trade carried on with the pen and with the tongue'); Du Heng 杜衡, under the false name "Ruohua" 若华, struck back, mocking Shen Congwen as a “乡下人” ('country man') and scorning his native-soil mood — **the whole effect of one pass out and back: the two sides jointly completed two classifications which had not existed to begin with** (**a textbook case of the principle in Chapter 1: the work of classification creates those who are classified — the "Beijing school" (京派) is a product of the debate, not the premise of the debate: the “乡下人” that was stuck on him, Shen Congwen later claimed as a signature for life: again and again he styled himself a “乡下人”, and these three characters became the fixed calling card of his biography**). **The sociological negative of the camps**: the root of the Beijing school = **the university + the supplement** (Zhu Guangqian 朱光潜 and his *Literature Magazine* 《文学杂志》, the criticism of Li Jianwu 李健吾 signed "Liu Xiwei" 刘西渭, the academy fiction of Fei Ming 废名 — **a mode of production on a low hourly wage: the academy supplies "distance" (Bian Zhilin 卞之琳's "distance" in Chapter 4; the berth of style in Chapter 8: distance is the shared metre of the Beijing school)**); the root of the Shanghai school (海派) = **the magazine + the cinema + advertising + the market in fees for manuscripts** (**the latter half of the New Sensationists' course (新感觉派): the speed the city feeds determines the speed of the aesthetic — an "aesthetics of distance" against an "aesthetics of hourly speed", and behind the two, two units in which pay is issued**). **Verdict: the debate had no win and no loss, only a division of labour — it invented for literary history a two-column ledger which has been used ever since (native soil / metropolis, purity / market, academy / newspaper office): every time the "contest of the refined and the vulgar" is opened again, it is the annual settlement of this set of books**.
:::

## Case Two: the economics of *Border Town* {#def-biancheng}

::: definition Weighing "change" on the "constant" {#def-chang}
*Border Town* 《边城》 (1934). The preface states, in his own words, that what he wants to write is a kind of “优美、健康、而又不悖乎人性的人生形式” ('a form of life that is beautiful, healthy, and not contrary to human nature') — **read this sentence as a contract: what it promises is not a tourist guide to western Hunan, but a weighing experiment: with the "constant" (custom, the ferry, the Dragon Boat Festival) to weigh how heavy the age's "change" is (warlords, prices, Shanghai)** (**the most elegant version of the sensor theory in Chapter 2: do not report the accident, report the normal — once the normal is carefully described, it becomes, of itself, an indictment**). **Readings from three devices**: **① the ferry's clause of "not taking money"** (**ferrying is the old boatman's obligation and his honour, “公家设的” ('set up by the public'); the copper cash is returned, and is given on** (a ferry on the official road does not take money for use; a merchant stuffs money in; the old boatman chases him to return it, and takes the money to buy tea and rough tobacco for people on the road — the gist of the detail): **a model of a pre-modern economy of mutual aid — and the antithesis, each of the other, of Jin Ba 金八's exchange in *Sunrise* 《日出》: Chadong has no going price, only rules (the verdict of the previous chapter: rules vs the going price = the line between tradition and modernity)**); **② the accounts of the "white pagoda"** (**the white pagoda falls on a night of thunder and rain, and is rebuilt at the end of the piece: in structure it stands in for the cause and effect of the plot — a natural disaster carries out one stocktake of fixed assets; the pagoda's being new or old is the world's being new or old, and Shen Congwen wrote the commentary into the plaster**); **③ Cuicui 翠翠's waiting** — the ending “这个人也许永远不回来了，也许‘明天’回来！” ('Perhaps this person will never come back; perhaps he will come back "tomorrow"!') (Our translation.) — **narrative duration is extended without limit: the novel stops the clock inside the two-valued judgement of "perhaps / perhaps" — the tragedy is not declared; the books are simply no longer kept (a national-grade specimen of the undetermined point in Chapter 4: this hole, two generations of readers have not finished filling, and cannot finish filling: any definite way of filling it is to turn *Border Town* into a different novel)**.
:::

::: definition The pastoral's other lens {#def-jingtou}
**An audit of the list of "what is not written"**: what did *Border Town* cut out? **In *Xiangxing sanji* 《湘行散记》 (*Random Notes on a Journey through Hunan*) and *Wo de jiaoyu* 《我的教育》 (*My Education*) there are the same boatmen and prostitutes, the same beheadings and public exposures** (the public executions of western Hunan in the 1920s appear as scenery — Shen Congwen's memories of service in the army) — **pastoral and on-the-spot record are two lenses on the same camera: the wide angle is for custom, the macro lens is for blood** (**Chapter 8's "list of concessions", carried out by a master: mildness does not write fear, the dagger does not write rain, the pastoral does not write the corpse — but Shen Congwen's corpses are in the drawer: not writing ≠ not knowing: he knew more clearly than anyone; the right to choose is precisely the definition of style**). **Narrative voice**: the storyteller's kindliness + the humour of the aside (**a self-mocking aside of the sort “凡事要是太巧了也就有点近于谎话”** ('If anything is too neat, it comes a little close to a lie') (Our translation.)**: the pastoral carries its own anti-counterfeit mark — the author reminds the reader that this is a construction — the pastoral version of the theory of the mask in Chapter 6: he will yield a step even on "the true", so that "the sincere" may advance a step**). **The change of trade after 1949** (**Shen Congwen turned to the study of cultural relics (the history of dress), and the fiction stopped — a case of the history of effects carried out upon a person: the genre changed its licence, and the obsession with observation and with fine detail was moved across, seal unbroken, into the new subject: the quietest version of "distance in time is a filter" in Chapter 9: he was not silent; he changed the office of registration**).
:::

## Case Three: Eileen Chang's uneven contrast {#def-zhang}

::: definition The balance sheet of love {#def-zhangai}
*The Golden Cangue* 《金锁记》 (1943) — **the money coming back in from a pricing of emotion** (**Cao Qiqiao 曹七巧 is locked by gold for half a lifetime, and then uses her own lock to saw through the lives of her son and daughter: she uses the son as collateral with which to press the daughter-in-law, and treats the daughter's marriage as a bad debt to be written off — “三十年来她戴着黄金的枷。她用那沉重的枷角劈杀了几个人”** ('For thirty years she wore a cangue of gold. With the heavy corner of that cangue she hacked several people to death.') (Our translation.) (*The Golden Cangue*, the text in common circulation) — **narrative voice = the coldness of an accountant: no defence, no indictment, only the posting of accounts**); *Love in a Fallen City* 《倾城之恋》 — **the fall of a city stands security for a marriage** (**the flirtation of Fan Liuyuan 范柳原 and Bai Liusu 白流苏 is a bilateral negotiation** (**Fan Liuyuan recites “执子之手” from the *Book of Songs* ('I hold your hand') (Our translation.) in the tone of a man of business quoting a price; the explosions of the war sign for the two of them: the fineness of personal happiness is the collateral of the age's credit: a city signed for the pair — an ironic footnote to a romance**)). *Chenxiang xie* 《沉香屑》 (*Scraps of Aloeswood*) **makes a nested narration out of the moon of "Shanghai thirty years ago"** (the old-school tone of "the person who tells the story" in Chapter 1 — **she borrows the frame of popular fiction, and packs it with a modernist kernel**). **A technical list**: ① **“参差的对照”** ('uneven contrast') ('My Own Writing', 《自己的文章》, states the position in her own words — **she does not write violent contrast (the tragic grandeur of soul and flesh); she writes uneven contrast (a little of the true inside compromise, a little warmth inside calculation): only the term itself is kept — "uneven contrast" as an aesthetic programme: refuse the moral drama of the climax, and pass, on inspection, the grey of the ordinary day**); ② **the montage of the cinema** (**colour, mirror-images, the detail of the qipao 旗袍 as a language of shots — the methods of the New Sensationists are re-compounded by Eileen Chang 张爱玲 from "the speed of the metropolis" into "the quiet speed of the flat": the methods of the Shanghai school + the distance of the Beijing school, in one body (the illegitimate daughter of the two schools of this chapter)**); ③ **the *baimiao* bloodline of *Dream of the Red Chamber* and *The Plum in the Golden Vase*** (*baimiao* 白描, 'plain delineation without ornament') (**the anti-market case of "the inheritance of form" in Chapter 8: under the popular title of being denounced as the "Mandarin Duck and Butterfly school" (鸳鸯蝴蝶派), what she did was the most avant-garde experiment in narrative — the opposition of type and experiment is once again defaulted on by a single author**).
:::

::: example The Chadong exchange: price lists for the two places {#ex-jiage}
For **Chadong (*Border Town*)** and **Shanghai (*The Golden Cangue* / *Love in a Fallen City*)**, draw up one "price list" each: under the four heads **marriage, labour, honour, time**, mark for each the "unit of pricing" and the "means of settlement"; then answer: **are Shen Congwen and Eileen Chang opponents or accomplices** (hint: both refuse "the grand words of enlightenment"; one calls as witness a market that does not exist, the other calls as witness a market that is everywhere).
::: solution
**The Chadong table** (**marriage = the contest of the ferry and the mill (the mill given as dowry by the militia chief, the *tuanzong* 团总, vs the old boatman's ferry): the bid is made in "things", the settlement is made in "lives" (the account of the two brothers, Tianbao 天保 and Nuosong 傩送); labour = the turning of the taking of money into an obligation (the public-duty ferry, *yidu* 义渡); honour = face and rules (yielding the road in a duel, the race of the night songs); time = the cycle of the Dragon Boat Festival (compound interest on the "constant")**); **the Shanghai table** (**marriage = a long-term meal ticket (what Bai Liusu "marries" is a boarding for meals through the latter half of a life: a deposed consort, divorced, must be listed on the market again); labour = that which does not exist (the Qiqiaos do not labour; they only guard the property); honour = reputation = the going price ("reputation" is short-sold, maliciously, like a stock); time = thirty years of compound interest (Qiqiao's cangue is accrued, entry by entry, over thirty years)**); **the ruling, opponent or accomplice** (**the opponent's side: one writes the market outside the door (the public-duty ferry returns the money), one writes the market into the bedclothes (the account beside the pillow); the accomplice's side: both keep the books, in the most sparing rhetoric, on "men and women of the old kind", and neither cries enlightenment (Shen Congwen does not revile feudalism, Eileen Chang does not cry liberation — their "not writing / not crying out" is the deepest criticism of the age: the former proves that some good things die if they are marketised, the latter proves that no one takes the market for a person)**. **Verdict: accomplices in the diagnosis, opponents in the prescription: Chadong is a vaccine, Shanghai is a pathological section**).
:::

::: intuition Geography is a delayed autobiography {#int-dili}
From "Beijing school / Shanghai school" to "native soil / metropolis" to "the contest of Beijing and Shanghai", **the geography of modern Chinese literature has never been a description of setting; it is a spatial projection of the literary field** (**Beiping = academic capital + a slow hourly wage; Shanghai = the capital of print + a fast turnover; western Hunan = "the presence of the one who is absent" (all his life Shen Congwen wrote Chadong while in Beiping — “乡下人” is the receipt for distance: the pastoral is the interest on distance (a reprise of the wording in the first section of this chapter): distance is not escape, it is an interest rate)**). **A reminder from this lesson: the frame of the map is drawn by those who come later — writers of the 1930s for the most part did not walk by the map (Shi Zhecun 施蛰存 was at once modern and of the market streets; Mu Shiying 穆时英 turned to the left and inclined to the right; Eileen Chang came out of an empty sky and belonged to no school — a school is an administrative unit invented by magazines and by debates; writing is an individual's smuggling)**.
:::

::: history A map redrawn three times {#sec-ditu}
**1933–35** the Beijing–Shanghai debate (**the midwifery of the label**); **1935** the literary prize of *Dagong bao* · *Wenyi* and *Literature Quarterly* 《文学季刊》 (**the peak of the Beijing school's institution-building: prize + a journal of the academy = a declaration of independence for the right to judge (Chapter 1: the prize is one of the four modern institutions of the literary system)**); **1936** Shen Congwen takes over the editing of *Dagong bao* · *Wenyi* (**the supplement = the editorial office of the genres: in one year he finds a batch of new writers — the academic school serving as editor = "cultivation" as an aesthetic practice**); **1937–41** the war reorganises the map (**the map of the two cities shatters: Chongqing of the move inland, Shanghai of the solitary island, the cave-dwellings of Yan'an — the three lines of Chapter 10 are sited here**); **1942–45** literary production in Shanghai in the occupied zone (**the complex market of "enemy and puppet" periodicals, Eileen Chang, Su Qing 苏青, Ke Ling 柯灵, *Zazhi* 《杂志》, *Gujin* 《古今》 and the like: under the Japanese occupation, the censorship of publication and the market in manuscript fees stand side by side — a "troubled age" is, for the writer, a danger, and also a busy season for publishing (a grey ledger in the history of effects: after the war the charge of her “失节” ('loss of chastity') and her genius were heard on the same table)**); **1943** *The Golden Cangue* and *Love in a Fallen City* are published, and Fu Lei 傅雷 ("Xun Yu" 迅雨), in a review, both praises and admonishes (**the two faces of the critic: the usual account of 'On Eileen Chang's Fiction', 《论张爱玲的小说》: it calls *The Golden Cangue* “我们文坛最美的收获之一” ('one of the most beautiful harvests of our literary world'), and at the same time objects that she “偏于男女” ('leans too far towards men and women') — criticism as the notarising of the reader's contract**); **1949–70s** each of the two sides is put into cold storage (**Shen Congwen changes trade, Eileen Chang goes to Hong Kong and on to America, and the writing stands still for more than ten years — the name-list of "Beijing school and Shanghai school" is long a blank in literary history**); **1980s–90s** a double reopening (**the usual account: scholarship overseas (Xia Zhiqing 夏志清 (C. T. Hsia), *A History of Modern Chinese Fiction* 《中国现代小说史》) rediscovers Eileen Chang and Shen Congwen — the national border of "being discovered": the border checkpoint of canonisation: in the 1990s they come onto the double track of textbooks at home and the bestseller lists (the most violent case of the "shares issued on a rolling basis" in Chapter 1: two stocks that had been delisted become, one after the other, constituents of the index)**); **now** **the long tail of "Eileen Chang fever" + the turning of "Border Town" into cultural tourism** (**the ticket economy of the ancient town of Fenghuang has made a market out of "the sample that was against the market" — the ultimate irony of this chapter: the ferry at Chadong now takes a ticket: the interest on distance has at last bought the principal as well**).
:::

::: warning Four pits in reading Beijing and Shanghai
**Reading the "Beijing school" as "refined" and the "Shanghai school" as "vulgar"** (**restating an administrative classification in words of value: each school has its experiments and its failures; "rank" itself was the ammunition of the polemic of that year; do not take it over and use it as a critical remark (the geographical version of the hierarchy of genres in Chapter 8)**); **taking *Border Town* for a scenic film of folk custom** (**an audit of the list of omissions: every stroke of the scenery comes from a deletion — to read it as "beauty" is to demote it from an instrument of weighing to a postcard: first count what it did not write**); **"Eileen Chang = a writer of love"** (**her love stories are, all of them, disputes over rights of property: a way of reading that only copies a popular sentence of the kind “于千万人之中” ('among thousands upon thousands of people') steps, precisely, past the whole of her work as an accountant (the repeated naming, in Chapter 3, of the tax on the golden sentence)**); **ignoring the historical premise the two schools hold in common** (**the "autonomy of the literary field" in the 1930s had, in the colonial metropolis and in the enclave of the academy, its conditions and also its costs: inside the Shanghai school's "market" there is Japanese capital; inside the Beijing school's "academy" there are civil war and unemployment — neither purity nor commerce is a purely commercial question (Chapter 2, the autonomy of the field: the clauses always carry small print)**).
:::

::: exercise Short essay: the interest on distance and the interest on the market {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing the claim "**Shen Congwen and Eileen Chang are two tables of interest rates for Chinese literature of the 1930s–40s: one uses an old world at zero interest to prove how terrible compound interest is; the other uses the reality of usury to prove that love, too, can be dealt only at the market price**". You must bring in at least five items: **the midwifery of the label (classification precedes existence)**, **the weighing experiment (measuring change by the constant)**, **the clause by which the public-duty ferry returns the money**, **uneven contrast**, **the border checkpoint of canonisation**; and give a ruling: **do "the ancient town of Fenghuang taking tickets" and "the making of Eileen Chang into cultural products" prove that these two writers have already been wholly incorporated by the market — or prove, precisely, that their works are still resisting**?
::: solution
Points for marking: the five mechanisms (**midwifery = the fighting strength of classification; weighing = the normal standing as an indictment; the return of the money = the antithesis of rules to the going price; unevenness = grey as the standard of acceptance on inspection; the border checkpoint = the national border of canonisation, and its age**); the ruling (**two lines of reference: the thesis of incorporation — tickets and canvas bags plane heterogeneity flat into an atmosphere, and commodification buys only "the shell of the manner"** (no department store can put into practice Chadong's clause of returning the money: once the sample is made into a specimen, it loses the power to indict); **the thesis of resistance — what the ticket sells is the sign "a negation of the ticket economy": every act of consumption advertises the work on its behalf, and the community of interpretation still settles according to the original text: those who are truly incorporated are the sightseers who do not read the original**. **Marking turns on whether "the circulation of the text" is distinguished from "the meaning of the text": the two are often seized by the market and held apart**); if the conclusion can be written "**the legacy the two writers hold in common is the keeping of accounts: Shen Congwen recorded the assets written off by compound interest, Eileen Chang recorded the interest that has no assets — that the market can make cultural products of both of them is precisely that it cannot alter the ledger into a blank: so long as someone is still checking page by page, the ferry at Chadong still does not take money**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The three cases, filed: midwifery (**the label produces the camps: every contest of the refined and the vulgar is an annual settlement**), weighing (**measuring change by the constant: the public-duty ferry's return of the money = the antithesis of rules to the going price; Cuicui's waiting = an undetermined point extended without limit**), contrast (**the grey of unevenness, the balance sheet of love, the illegitimate daughter of Shanghai-school technique + Beijing-school distance**), redrawing (**two cities → many points: canonisation has a border checkpoint, and also a window for the refund of tickets**). The method in one sentence: **first check who drew the map, then check who paid the manuscript fee**. The war tears the map up completely. The next chapter: 1937–1949 — the three-line literature of exile, the rear, and the base areas.
:::
