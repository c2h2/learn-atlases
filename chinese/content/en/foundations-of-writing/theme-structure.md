---
title: "Theme and structure"
---

Material is the brick, the theme is the drawing, and structure is the load-bearing wall — a site with no drawing is called a pile of bricks, and a house with no load-bearing wall is called a stage set. Two things in this chapter: **a piece keeps only one master**; **invite that master to the most conspicuous place**.

## Theme: legislating one idea {#sec-liyi}

::: definition The central sentence is a constitution {#def-zhongxin}
**Theme** (立意) = the one-sentence answer to what this composition "is actually saying" (in the language of the classroom it is called the central idea; in rhetoric, the **purport**, 主旨; in journalism, the judgement sentence of the lead). Three procedures for making that law. ① **The one-sentence test** (the central sentence must **have its subject, predicate and object complete, and be open to rebuttal** — "母爱" ('a mother's love') is not a sentence; "母亲的唠叨里藏着她的时间焦虑" ('Hidden in a mother's nagging is her anxiety about time.' (Our translation.)) is one. **Only what can be rebutted thereby becomes a view.**) ② **The pruning method** (ask of every sentence in the draft, once: whom does this sentence serve? — **a sentence that serves a second master is called, in literature, a digressive stroke (岔笔), and on the examination paper a previous offence of leaving the topic**). ③ **Delayed judgement** (do not weld the theme shut before you take up the pen: Ye Shengtao 叶圣陶's "意在笔先" ('The idea is there before the brush.' (Our translation.)) means **having one whole mass**, not having a slogan — **the mass is a blurred fullness; once the pen moves it will break and it will reassemble, and only after it has developed do you see it clearly**. See [[foundations-of-writing/writing-process]].) The sense of rank in a theme: **writing what others have not noticed > writing what everyone knows, but which you can write fine > writing a parroted "depth"** — better one inch of the concrete than hanging up a foot of the abstract.
:::

::: example A clinic for the theme: the same photograph {#ex-zhaopian}
The composition class hands out a photograph: a repair stall at the school gate, the keeper of the stall mending a tyre and reading a book at the same time. The central sentences of three classmates are as follows. See them in clinic, and write a prescription for each:
甲: 《我们要热爱学习》 ('We must love learning.' (Our translation.))　乙: 《修车摊也精彩》 ('The repair stall is splendid too.' (Our translation.))　丙: 《他在等一个不会来的人——不，他就是来看书的》 ('He is waiting for someone who will not come — no, he simply came to read.' (Our translation.))
::: solution
**甲: a cold in the theme** — the slogan disease ("热爱学习", 'loving learning', is a slogan on the wall, not your judgement; the person in the photograph is reading a book, not looking at the concept "学习", 'learning'). Prescription: go back to the detail and ask — what book is he reading? Why this one? (Press the question and it comes out: open on his stall is a cookbook, and tucked inside the cookbook is a volume of poems — **contrast is the theme**: a hand that earns a living is also worthy of poetry — draw it in to one sentence that can be argued.)
**乙: anaemia in the theme** — "也精彩" ('splendid too') takes someone else's evaluation as your own (splendid in what way? If you cannot say = you have not established a theme). Prescription: replace "精彩" ('splendid') with **one concrete discovery** (while he is reading, no one is at the stall; when someone comes, the speed at which he shuts the book — so he is **on his guard against being seen reading**? → "体面的偏见：连旁观者都觉得修车的不该读诗" ('The prejudice of respectability: even a bystander feels that a person who repairs vehicles ought not to read poetry.' (Our translation.)) — three layers deeper than "精彩").
**丙: an allergy in the theme** — the illness of being moved by oneself ("等一个不会来的人", 'waiting for someone who will not come', is the tone of a novel forced onto a factual record; what the photograph did not give is reimbursed in full by the imagination — **fiction that has entered the wrong genre**, and the case is valued as in the previous chapter). But it **can be saved**: keep the self-correction after the dash as the theme — "我原本想替他写一段苦情，后来发现他就是来看书的" ('I had meant to write a passage of misery on his behalf, and afterwards found that he had simply come to read.' (Our translation.)) — **writing '我想多了' ('I had thought too much.' (Our translation.)) up as the piece** is precisely a vaccine against sentimental excess (the professional name of this method is **metanarration**, 元记叙, demonstrated in the spirit of "反方辩论" ('arguing the other side') in [[pre-qin-literature/legacy]]). The clinic's summary — three checks on the theme: **Is it your own wording? Does it stand on the detail? Is it worthy of the length of this piece?**
:::

## Structure: the economics of attention {#sec-jiegou}

::: definition Detail and brevity are a vote; order is a script {#def-xianglue}
**Detail and brevity** (详略) = casting the number of characters as votes, and deciding who receives them (every keyword of the central sentence must have one "heavy scene", 重场戏; **give a supporting part more space than the protagonist, and the reader changes the play**). **Order**, in four schemes: **chronological narration** (顺叙; safe, but apt to lie flat — the remedy: at the crucial node, slow down into a scene), **reverse narration** (倒叙; the result goes first: the first sentence hands over the corpse — suspense is attention on loan), **inset narration** (插叙; halfway through, borrow a stretch of the past; **there must be a door to go in and to come out**: you enter by a trigger, "那把伞让我想起……" ('That umbrella made me think of …' (Our translation.)), and you leave by a tie-back, "伞还在我手里" ('The umbrella is still in my hand.' (Our translation.)) — **in and out with no door is leaving the topic**), **parallel narration** (平叙; two strands, "花开两朵各表一枝" ('Two flowers have opened; tell each on its own branch.' (Our translation.)) — use it with caution in the examination hall; two strands = two budgets). **Transition and echo** (过渡与照应) are the synovial fluid: paragraph and paragraph are stitched by **signpost words** (然而 'yet', 更重要的是 'more important still', 那年之后 'after that year') and by **recurrent keywords** (复沓; the central image that comes back, in every paragraph, and knocks once at the door); an echo between opening and close is a handshake given to the reader.
:::

::: definition Opening, continuation, turn and close, and a typology of beginnings and endings {#def-qc}
Qiao Mengtan 乔孟谈 of the Yuan, on four methods for the *yuèfǔ* (乐府, Music Bureau poems; ballads), which later ages carried over to the essay: **起要美丽，中要饱满，转要有筋斗，结尾要响亮** ('The opening must be beautiful, the middle full, the turn must have a somersault, and the ending must ring.' (Our translation.)) (the popular version of "凤头、猪肚、豹尾", 'phoenix head, pig's belly, leopard's tail') — *qǐ-chéng-zhuǎn-hé* (起承转合) is not the eight-legged essay (八股); it is **a four-stage financing of the reader's attention**: the opening borrows money (why read on), the continuation extends the building, the turn repays the debt (the surprise, and the cost), the close pays the interest. **A shelf of openings**: straight into a scene ("他合书的速度比谁都快" 'He shut the book faster than anyone.' (Our translation.)), suspense ("我这辈子没见过我爸哭，除了那台收音机" 'Never in my life have I seen my dad cry, except for that radio.' (Our translation.)), definition and redefinition ("所谓老家，就是你要回去一次才能确认回不去的地方" 'What people call the old home is a place you have to go back to once before you can confirm that you cannot go back.' (Our translation.)), and a ban on the misuse of quoting famous lines (opening with "古人云" 'as the ancients said' = writing someone else's name on the title deed); **a shelf of endings**: close on an action (do not set resolve upon resolve, 不立志志; set down one action), close on an image (the thing is still there, the person already is not — be wary of stock lines such as "雨还在下" 'The rain is still falling.' (Our translation.)), close in a ring (the same scene at the opening and at the end, the viewpoint already changed), **a ban on "通过这件事我明自我"** ('Through this matter I have made the self clear.' (Our translation.)) (the summarising statement at the end is the cheapest part in the whole piece). **A quick index of cases**: off the topic = the constitution has failed; lying flat = there is no heavy scene; top-heavy = the opening is a rich pig's belly and the ending is a poor leopard's tail; a break = there is no synovial fluid — **each illness has a one-line operating note**.
:::

::: intuition Structure is arranging seats for the reader {#int-zuowei}
The beginner writes in **the order of time** ("我先是去了，然后看了，最后吃了" 'First I went, then I looked, and last I ate.' (Our translation.)); the practised hand writes in **the order of understanding** (what most needs to be known first is said first; the heaviest thing is the finale, or is put at the head). To judge a piece's structure you need only change places: **suppose the reader has only three chances to be patient** — the first paragraph (why read), the most tiring place in the middle (why not give up), the last paragraph (whether it is worth lingering over) — at each of the three places stake one best piece of goods; all the rest is transport. This is the modern decoding of "凤头猪肚豹尾": **not a metaphor of build, but an allocation of budget**. The contract between structure and theme is signed here as well: **structure = using length to argue the central sentence**; where you write in full is the evidence, where you write briefly is the backdrop — and so the one-sentence composition can be saved: **enlarge the central sentence into every scene**.
:::

::: history From the eight-legged essay to the outline {#sec-bagu}
**Ming and Qing**: the eight-legged essay pushed the "study of structure" to the extreme and also pushed it over into its opposite — the 起讲 ('opening the discussion'), the 入股 ('entering the legs') and the 大结 ('grand conclusion') each had its set form; **a format can secure the lower limit; it cannot produce an upper limit**. **1902–1905**: the imperial examinations were abolished, and classroom writing lost the matrix of its template. **1919–1930s**: after the National Language Movement (国语运动) came a publishing fever for "methods of composition" (作文法); *qǐ-chéng-zhuǎn-hé* gave up its place to "opening — middle — ending" — **the three-part form took the baton of the formula**. **From the 1950s**: under the influence of Soviet methods of teaching, training in **"compiling an outline"** (编写提纲) entered the syllabus (listing an outline = making the procedure of structure explicit; to this day it is a standard move in the composition class). **1980–1990s**: the "sequence of training in genres" (文体训练序列) (narration — exposition — argument, each stage cleared in turn) and "single-item training compositions" (单项训练作文) (expanding, condensing, rewriting — **structure taken apart into parts and practised**). **From the 2000s**: the topic composition (话题作文) and "genre of one's own choosing" (文体自选) returned authority over structure to the author, and what came with that was **the structural counterfeit of the "cultural-essay manner"** (文化散文腔) — imitations made of parallelism + allusion + exclamation marks ran riot. **Today**: mind maps and "story software" (beat sheet) run side by side in the writing workshop — **the tools of the outline have changed their skin; the study of structure has not gone out of date**.
:::

::: warning Four entries on structure's record
First, **top-heavy** (the first 800 characters are still on "那是一个风和日丽的早晨" 'It was a fine, bright morning.' (Our translation.), and the climax has three lines left — **the budget for the heavy scene is inverted**; the change: start writing from paragraph 2 of the draft). Second, **dependence on subheadings** (the assorted-platter form of "春/夏/秋/冬" 'spring / summer / autumn / winter' and "一/二/三" 'one / two / three' — **a subheading is an external brace on the structure, concealing the fact that there is no load-bearing wall**; if you use them, each heading must have its own centre of gravity). Third, **addiction to reversals** ("其实是梦/其实是我记错了" 'Actually it was a dream / actually I remembered it wrong.' (Our translation.) — **a reversal is a credit card; it overdraws the trust of the ending**; a reversal counts only when it calls in the foreshadowing laid down earlier). Fourth, **the cage of a gorgeous opening** (the opening lays down four sets of parallelism, and the main text cannot afford the same weather — **the rhetoric of the opening is an IOU; the whole piece has to pay it back**).
:::

::: exercise Surgery on the central sentence {level=1}
Operate on the following "central sentences", so that they can be rebutted and are worthy of the detail: ① 《诚信》 ('integrity') ② 《我的老师》 ('My teacher') ③ 《坚持》 ('perseverance').
::: solution
Directions to consult: ① "被看见的诚信不算诚信——考场橡皮递过去之前，才是真正的考验" ('Integrity that has been seen does not count as integrity — the real test is before the examination-hall eraser is passed across.' (Our translation.)); ② "他改我作文的红笔比批改更狠的是那句‘你自己再读一遍’" ('Of the red pen with which he marked my composition, fiercer than the marking was that sentence, "Read it through once more yourself."' (Our translation.)); ③ "坚持的故事只讲成功，是幸存者写给失败者的嘲讽" ('A story of perseverance that tells only of success is the mockery a survivor writes for those who failed.' (Our translation.)). Marking: the rewritten sentence must (a) have a way in to a concrete scene (b) be open to challenge by one counter-example (c) be something you yourself can go on writing — if any one of the three is missing, it goes back into the furnace.
:::
:::

::: exercise Rearranging the budget {level=2}
Take your last composition (or a model piece) and do a "length audit": the percentage each of five paragraphs takes, set against the entrances and the relative weight of the keywords in the central sentence; open an **inversion list**, and write a rearrangement plan of 100 characters (which paragraph to cut in half, which to expand into a scene, from which sentence to open).
::: solution
Marking: the audit uses figures (38%/25%/20%/10%/7%); the diagnosis of the inversion is exact to the sentence ("开头 6 行天气对中心句贡献为零" 'Six lines of weather at the opening contribute nothing to the central sentence.' (Our translation.)); the rearrangement plan contains **a new first sentence** ("从第 2 段开始写" 'Start writing from paragraph 2.' (Our translation.) is the all-purpose opening stance). A common entry already on the record of model pieces: parallelism at the opening, 15%, plus a slogan at the end, 12%, = **rhetoric fat at both ends, the body thin in the middle**.
:::
:::

::: exercise A short essay: structure and sincerity {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters: "把文章写得‘像篇文章’，是否背叛真诚？" ('Does writing a piece so that it "looks like a piece of writing" betray sincerity?' (Our translation.)) Requirements: ① give one positive example of "结构即真诚" ('structure is sincerity') (for instance the repeated appearance of "背影" in Zhu Ziqing 朱自清, 《背影》 'The Sight of His Back'); ② give one counter-example of a "结构赝品" ('a structural counterfeit') (an imitation of the cultural-essay manner / a platter of subheadings); ③ give the line you draw: **when structure is service, and when it is performance**.
::: solution
Points for the mark: the positive example can point out how recurrence **deepens what is real** (not padding a format, but feeling thought of again and again); the counter-example points out the mechanism of the counterfeit (structure before content = parallelism for the sake of parallelism); the dividing line is where skill shows — a position to recommend: **structure is a host's courtesy towards the reader; sincerity is honesty towards the material; a host's courtesy must not counterfeit the guest** (a feeling of being moved which you do not have must not be put on by means of parallelism).
:::
:::

::: summary
Two tools in this chapter: three checks on the theme (is it your own wording / does it stand on the detail / is it worthy of the length — **stop first the three cold medicines, slogan, anaemia and allergy**); four pieces of structure (detail and brevity are a vote, order is a script, the synovial fluid is signposts and recurrence, opening and close are two handshakes). The eight-legged essay secures the lower limit and does not secure the upper limit; the outline has changed its skin, and the study of structure is not out of date — because **the reader's patience has not grown in three thousand years**. The next chapter makes its particular business the "scene": the time-order of narration, a ledger of the six elements, and the study of proportion between summary narration (概叙) and scene.
:::
