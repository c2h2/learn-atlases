---
title: "The legacy of pre-Qin literature"
---

The last chapter reads no new book; it is a **stocktaking**: the complete inventory of what a thousand years of the pre-Qin shipped to the two Han dynasties and to later ages — one column each for genres, motifs, methods and templates of character — and, along the way, it answers that old question: why the childhood of Chinese literature looks like a headquarters.

## The joint case of Feng and Sao: the first page of the general ledger {#sec-fengsao}

::: definition How the joint filing was made, and what it conceals {#def-bing}
The project of opening a file on the joint name 'Feng and Sao': **Liu An 刘安, in his *Lisao zhuan* 离骚传 ('Commentary on *Encountering Sorrow*'), likens *Encountering Sorrow* (*Li Sao*) to “国风好色而不淫，小雅怨诽而不乱”** ('The Airs of the States delight in beauty but are not licentious; the Lesser Odes resent and censure but do not fall into disorder.') (Our translation.) (cited in the *Records of the Grand Historian* — the first motion to bind the files in the joint case of Feng and Sao); **Liu Xie 刘勰, in 'Bian sao' 辨骚 ('Discriminating the Sao'),** judges that “自《九怀》以下，遽蹑其迹，而屈宋逸步，莫之能追” ('From the *Nine Regrets* on, they hastily trod in those tracks, yet none could catch the unbound steps of Qu and Song.') (Our translation.) — Qu Yuan 屈原 and Song Yu 宋玉 join the ranks, and the southern volume is opened as a case; **from the Tang and Song on,** 'Feng and Sao' became another name for 'literature' — 'men of the Sao' (“骚人”) and 'the purport of the men of the Airs' (“风人之旨”) entered poems, prose and the turns of critical talk on every side. The ledger has two columns: **what it took as its authorities** — two great sources, folk song and composition by the individual; two kinds of energy, concern for the actual world and romantic imagination; and two later bloodlines, 'poetry expresses intent' (“诗言志”) and 'writing a book out of pent-up frustration' (“发愤著书”) (the genealogy of Sima Qian's 司马迁 “此人皆意有所郁结”, 'These men all had something pent up in the mind.') (Our translation.)); **what it hid** — that great item, narrative by the scribes, was shifted by the doorplate 'literature' into a side hall (in writings on literature, the household registration of the *Zuo Tradition* (*Zuozhuan*) and of the *Intrigues of the Warring States* is much thinner than that of the *Songs* and the Sao), **the lower half of myth and shamanic sacrifice** (the carnival of the *Nine Songs*, the uncanny force of the *Heavenly Questions*) was for a long time trimmed to the measure of “温柔敦厚” (*wenrou dunhou*, 'gentle and sincere'), together with numberless 'nameless poems' (“无名之诗”: *yishi* 逸诗, 'poems left out of the anthology', and *tuge* 徒歌, 'unaccompanied songs') — **the joint filing is great, and it is a choice selection, not a complete collection**.
:::

::: example Delivery list: pre-Qin → Han {#ex-jiao}
From the nine chapters of this course, make out a waybill 'consigned to the two Han dynasties' (“发往两汉”): four columns — genres, motifs, methods and templates of persons — at least two items in each, and name the recipient.
::: solution
**The genres column**: four-character verse (the Han song texts for suburban and ancestral-temple sacrifice, and Wei Meng's 韦孟 'Fengjian' 讽谏 ('Remonstrance'), all the way to Cao Cao's 曹操 encore); the sao form (Jia Yi's 贾谊 'Diao Qu Yuan fu' 吊屈原赋 ('Rhapsody lamenting Qu Yuan'), and the early Han writers of the 'sao' — the *Records of the Grand Historian* goes so far as to carry Jia Yi's rhapsodies twice, from which the heat is plain); the rhapsody (the name in Xunzi's 荀子 'Fu pian' 赋篇 ('Rhapsodies') + the expansive laying-out of the *Songs of Chu* (*Chuci*), compounded into the great Han rhapsody); memorials (the 'Jian zhuke shu' 谏逐客书 ('Memorial remonstrating against the expulsion of the guest officers') is the first finished product the pre-Qin handed over to the Han court); **the motifs column**: fragrant plants and fair ones (香草美人, the stock of *bi* and *xing*, comparison and evocation, in the Han rhapsody), drooping millet and wheat in ear (黍离 and 麦秀, two factory codes for grief at a fallen state), roaming as an immortal and the *Heavenly Questions* (inscriptions on Han mirrors, and the questions pressed about life and death in the *Nineteen Old Poems*), the *shi* who does not meet his time (士不遇, the name of a family illness running through Jia Yi, Dongfang Shuo 东方朔 and Zhao Yi 赵壹); **the methods column**: the Spring and Autumn brush (deciding matters by the *Spring and Autumn Annals*, and Han Confucians explaining the classics), the first shoots of doubting and questioning old books (people of the Han could already see that the documents were uneven — the forerunner of collating books and of a consciousness of accretion in layers), lodging intent by *bi* and *xing* (the hermeneutics of the teaching of the *Songs* took up its post at the start of the Han); **the column of templates of character**: Sanlü (三闾, the prototype of loyalty sent into exile, claimed by a queue running from Jia Yi to Sima Qian), the fisherman and Jie Yu 接舆 (reserve options for leaving the world), the jester's line of the black-headed remonstrator (not until Dongfang Shuo in the Han does it grow a whole face), and the 'teacher' type (“师”) of Confucius, Mozi 墨子, Mencius and Xunzi — **of the Han empire's spiritual equipment, half the title deeds are the pre-Qin's**.
:::

## Four items of ancestral property {#sec-zuchan}

::: definition Master table of the legacy inventory {#def-zongbiao}
**① The complete catalogue of genres**: every later genre goes back to the pre-Qin to claim an ancestor — verse (four-character / sao), the rhapsody (*fu*: Xunzi's rhapsodies + the sounds of Chu), the discourse (debate topics of the School of Names, and refutations in sets in the *nan* 难 form), the memorial (the memorial on the guest officers), the letter (Li Si's 李斯 'Shangshu' 上书 ('Writing submitted to the throne') and the 'Bao Yan Huiwang shu' 报燕惠王书 ('Letter in reply to King Hui of Yan') are its pre-Qin precedents — the Grand Historian's 'Bao Ren An shu' 报任安书 ('Letter in reply to Ren An') is already a Han hand); the short parabolic piece (from Zhuangzi to Liu Zongyuan 柳宗元 to the 'appraisal', “赞”, in *Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio*); historical-biographical narrative (the annals-and-biography form draws jointly on the classic and the commentaries of the *Spring and Autumn Annals* and on the several forms of the *Discourses of the States* and the *Intrigues* — the common account in the field takes this in its general sense); **② the narrative contract**: the scribe holds the brush, praise and blame are built in, and details give advance notice of rise and fall — from this is issued the 'licence for realism' of Chinese narrative, and for a thousand years fiction has had to cadge that credit by 'aiding trustworthy history' (“羽翼信史”); **③ the dictionary of symbols**: fragrant plants and fair ones, drooping millet, pine and cypress, the Canglang — an inheritable public store of metaphor (longer-lived than any handbook of rhetoric, because it has **two thousand years of precedents in use**); **④ character and the *qi* of writing**: Sanlü's dying for the cause, Zhuang Zhou's 庄周 roaming, the burden borne by Confucius and Mencius — **three postures of the spine for the Chinese writer**; add Mencius' doctrine of 'nourishing *qi*' (“养气”; *qi* 气, vital energy) and, from Han Yu 韩愈 on, the theory of the *qi* of writing in which 'when *qi* is abundant, the words are fitting' (“气盛言宜”), and the two join tracks — **a single-chain audit of the quality of the person and the quality of the writing, open for business from the pre-Qin**.
:::

::: intuition Why childhood looks like a headquarters {#int-zongbu}
The childhoods of humankind each have their myths; why does the Chinese pre-Qin alone look like a **headquarters still at work**? Three causes, set side by side: **the script was early to mature, and the line never broke** (from oracle bones to clerical script, the same set of characters lets later people connect straight through to the original text — Greeks reading Homer depend on a relay of transcription and translation); **the system of education welded the texts into classics** (from 'family learning — private learning — the Erudites of the Five Classics — the examinations on the Four Books', “家学—私学—五经博士—四书科举”, every age pays a fresh salary to keep this set of books); **the hermeneutic tradition allows rewriting without repeal** (accretion in layers is not afraid of coming late; commentaries and subcommentaries allow what is new — the headquarters is continually refitted, and never moves its address). The comparative view is used with restraint: this is not 'Chinese exceptionalism' (“中国特殊论”); it is **a comparison of the conditions of transmission** — the same stories, loaded into an archive system that never loses power, will go on serving as a headquarters.
:::

::: history The method of the stocktaking must itself be stocktaken {#sec-fangfa}
A check of the toolbox this course has used: **the theory of layered accretion** (Gu Jiegang 顾颉刚: first look at the year a text was born, then talk about its content); **the stratigraphy of interpretation** (Mao and Zheng — Zhu Xi 朱熹 — the Qing scholars — selections of today, each returned to its own stratum); **distinguishing types of evidence** (the logic of compilation order / the setting of names and things / the function of folk song — the lesson of the three accounts of 'Taoyao' 桃夭 ('The peach is young')); **caution about how genres arise** (one genre, one source is a fairy tale; many sources flowing together is the normal case); **excavated and transmitted texts confirming each other** (the Tsinghua slips collated against the transmitted text — this course cites them only twice, and leaves the rest to a specialised course); **memory and claiming** (Qu Yuan's two thousand years, and myth made into IP — reception history is half the life of a text). This knife-work was not designed for the pre-Qin alone — **from the next course (the Han, Wei and the Six Dynasties) on, these tools are to stay at their jobs**.
:::

::: warning Three cautions in speaking of the legacy
One: **tracing a source in the manner of ancestor worship** (every rhapsody must take Xunzi as its ancestor, and every piece of parallel prose must take the antithesis of the *Songs* as its ancestor — sources are many and the streams differ; to acknowledge an ancestor is allowed, to stage a collision is not); two: **letting an inventory stand in for reading** (to have got by heart 'ten items of the legacy' (“遗产十条”) is not to have read *Encountering Sorrow* — the lessons on the original texts in the first nine chapters of this course cannot be skipped); three: **writing 'influence' up as a 'verdict'** (to say that the *Zuo Tradition* influenced the *Records of the Grand Historian* is where research begins, not where it ends — Sima Qian also fed on the *Shiben* 世本 ('Records of generations'), the *Intrigues* and oral transmission; **influence is the science of nutrition, not the science of blood types**).
:::

::: exercise A rite of acknowledging ancestors {level=1}
Choose any one later work (a poem by Cao Cao, Li Shangyin's 李商隐 'Wuti' 无题 ('Untitled'), the opening of *Romance of the Three Kingdoms*, or any full-mark essay from the university entrance examination — the choice is yours), write down **two pieces of its pre-Qin luggage**, and name the source lesson for each — each piece of luggage must have evidence at the level of the original text.
::: solution
Example: the opening of *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* — luggage one: the historian's brush and the map of the realm (“话说天下大势，分久必合”, 'The tale says that the general trend of all under heaven is: what has long been divided must unite.' (Our translation.), is the sentence-pattern “合从连衡” ('vertical alliance and horizontal alliance') in the *Intrigues*, plus “治乱循环” ('order and disorder in a cycle') in the *Gongyang* 公羊, put into the colloquial); luggage two: the device of telling in advance (the talk of “数尽”, 'the count is exhausted', by Cui Zhouping 崔州平 and Sima Hui 司马徽 is the later incarnation of divination and dreams in the *Zuo*). It is enough that luggage one be accurate and luggage two have a basis; 'evidence at the level of the original text' (“原文级证据”) means that one can quote an original sentence from the lesson.
:::

::: exercise A debate draft for the negative {level=2}
For the motion “风骚并称遮蔽了先秦文学的多样性” ('Naming Feng and Sao as a pair has concealed the diversity of pre-Qin literature.') (Our translation.), write a debating speech of five hundred characters (the affirmative), then with the same effort write a rebuttal (the negative), and finally judge the case.
::: solution
Points for the affirmative: the joint filing = a spotlight of two thousand years; the *Zuo Tradition*, the *Intrigues* and myth have long stood in the dark of the stage; the measure of 'expressing intent' (“言志”) trimmed the carnival of the *Nine Songs* and the heterodoxy of the *Heavenly Questions*. Points for the negative: to become a classic is necessarily to pick and choose; **where something is concealed, archaeology and writings on literature of themselves keep it alive** ('Discriminating the Sao' and the *Wenxin* have already reversed the verdict on the 'strange', “怪”); the joint name also **empowers** — history and the Sao each received treatment at the rank of a 'classic' (“经”). Points for the mark: both sides use evidence from the lessons; the judgement does not sit on the fence (precise formulations such as “遮而不灭”, 'concealed but not put out', and “灯下亦有光”, 'Under the lamp there is light as well.' (Our translation.), are allowed).
:::

::: summary
Four cabinets in the stocktaking: a catalogue of genres (every genre goes back to the pre-Qin to claim an ancestor, but claiming an ancestor is not staging a collision); a narrative contract (the historian's brush issues 'credible' (“可信”), and fiction cadges the credit for a thousand years); a dictionary of symbols (public metaphors with two thousand years of precedent); a spine of character (dying for the cause, roaming, bearing the load). One box of methods: layered accretion, strata, types of evidence, claiming and memory — they stay at their jobs. The childhood of pre-Qin literature closes its gallery here; its boyhood is called the 'Han rhapsody' (“汉赋”); and the name of its adolescence the next course will call out in a single breath — **the literature of the Han, Wei and the Six Dynasties**: the empire, the great rhapsody, the Bamboo Grove, Tao Yuanming 陶渊明, and that famous old case, 'the self-consciousness of literature' (“文学的自觉”), which we shall try again when the time comes.
:::
