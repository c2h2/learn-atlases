---
title: "Historical prose"
---

The first mountain of Chinese prose is not philosophy but the court historian (史官): recording speech, recording events, lodging praise and blame — from proclamations and charges, to the method of the brush, to the state-by-state form, to the *zònghéngjiā* 纵横家 ('persuaders of the vertical and horizontal alliances'), historical prose all along the way wrote "history" as "literature", and at the last let "literature" walk carrying "history" on its back.

## The difficulty of recording speech: the *Book of Documents* {#sec-shangshu}

::: definition The *Book of Documents*: high antiquity's briefcase {#def-shangshu}
The *Book* is **an archival compilation of the oaths, charges, proclamations and speeches of high antiquity** ("尚" means high antiquity — Sima Qian 司马迁: “《书》记先王之事，故长于政” ('The *Book* records the affairs of the former kings, and so it excels in government.') (Our translation.)). The New Text in 28 chapters (Fu Sheng 伏生 transmitting it orally, and the text fixed in writing in Han clerical script) and the Old Text in 25 chapters (presented by Mei Ze 梅赜 of the Eastern Jin; **judged a forged book since the Song** — a thousand years of the *Old Text Book of Documents* is the prime specimen of a case of a forged classic, and Yan Ruoqu 阎若璩's *Evidential Analysis of the Old Text of the Book of Documents* closed the case). The genealogy of the genres is the genealogy of official documents: **Canons** (institutions), **Counsels** (strategies), **Instructions** (teaching), **Proclamations** (announcements), **Oaths** (mobilisation orders), **Charges** (letters of appointment). The style: crabbed and tongue-twisting (Han Yu 韩愈's indictment — **it is the dialect of our language in a former life**; phrases of the sort “克明俊德” ('able to make bright a lofty virtue') have to be unlocked by the study of exegesis). The literary value lies precisely in the "difficulty": the hardness of a military order in the 'Oath at Mu', “今日之事，不逾一日之间” ('Today's business will not go beyond the space of a single day.') (Our translation.); in 'No Ease', the Duke of Zhou 周公 going on and on, “先知稼穑之艰难” ('First know the hardship of sowing and reaping.') (Our translation.) — **the first living person's manner of speech inside the genre of proclamations and charges**. The posture for reading the *Book*: with the aid of modern commentaries such as those of Zeng Yunqian 曾运乾 and Liu Qiyu 刘起釪, first recognise the stele, then listen for the human voice.
:::

## The method of the *Spring and Autumn* brush: the court historian's brush and knife {#sec-bifa}

::: definition Subtle words and great meaning {#def-bifa}
The *Spring and Autumn Annals* (*Chunqiu*): the annals of the state of Lu (from Duke Yin to Duke Ai, 722–481 BC), the wording extremely terse — “陨石于宋五” ('There fell stones in Song: five.') (Our translation.); “晋赵盾弑其君夷皋” ('Zhao Dun of Jin assassinated his ruler Yigao.') (Our translation.) (the one who actually killed Duke Ling was Zhao Chuan 赵穿 — the entry writes Zhao Dun 赵盾; the blame lay on the chief minister for fleeing without leaving the territory and, on his return, not punishing the assassin — **the rules of recording** (书法) are the verdict). Three great methods of the brush: **praise and blame in a single word** (the criminology of “弑” ('a subject killing a ruler'), “杀” ('to kill') and “诛” ('to put to death as punishment'); “及” ('joining'), “次” ('encamping') and “围” ('besieging') each have a penal grade); **concealed writing** (concealing for the honoured — “天王狩于河阳” ('The Heavenly King hunted at Heyang 河阳.') (Our translation.): Duke Wen of Jin 晋文公 summoned the Zhou Son of Heaven, and under the brush it became the Son of Heaven out hunting — Mencius's “孔子成《春秋》而乱臣贼子惧” ('Confucius completed the *Spring and Autumn*, and rebellious ministers and villainous sons were afraid.') (Our translation.) shakes hands here with what people now say, “历史书写即政治行为” ('the writing of history is a political act') (Our translation.)); **joining phrases and comparing events** (setting years, months and wording side by side, so that the facts open their own mouths — the four-character proclamation of the calendar, “春，王正月” ('Spring, the royal first month.') (Our translation.)). A catch-up lesson in the study of historical sources: the three pre-Qin "*Traditions* of the *Spring and Autumn*" each have their own school method (家法) (the *Zuo* **narrates the events themselves**; the *Gongyang* and the *Guliang* **explain the principles and precedents of the classic** — the first battlefield of New Text classical learning and Old Text classical learning).
:::

::: example Forensic medicine in five characters {#ex-wuzi}
“陨石于宋五” ('There fell stones in Song: five.') (Our translation.) (the sixteenth year of Duke Xi) — the *Guliang Tradition* explains: “陨石，记闻。闻其磌然，视之则石，察之则五” ('Falling stones: a record of what was heard. One heard the thud of them, looked and they were stones, examined them and they were five.') (Our translation.) — please explain why this sentence is an early case of "language and cognition".
::: solution
The five characters restore three steps of perception: **hearing** (磌然 — first one hears the sound of the fall: seeking the thing by its sound) → **looking** (视之则石 — looked, and they were stones: going up close to tell the substance: they are stone) → **examining** (察之则五 — examined, and they were five: a clear count fixes the number: five pieces in all). The *Gongyang Tradition* is in the same key: “曷为先言陨而后言石？陨石记闻……曷为先言石而后言五？五石记见。” ('Why is "fell" said first and "stones" after? Falling stones record what was heard…… Why are "stones" said first and "five" after? Five stones record what was seen.') (Our translation.) — what the three *Traditions* contend over is not the historical fact, but a philosophy of language in which **the order of wording = the order of cognition**: every word-order of the historical brush is the written record of an investigation. Conclusion: the other face of what is called "subtle words and great meaning" is **the science of information-coding in an extremely terse genre** — the source of later ages' "sparing ink as if it were gold" is not the saving of paper, but the belief that every character is in court.
:::

## The state-by-state form and the mouths of the *zongheng* persuaders {#sec-guobie}

::: definition The *Discourses of the States* and the *Intrigues of the Warring States* {#def-guobie}
The ***Discourses of the States*** (*Guoyu*): a compilation of **recorded speech** from the eight states of Zhou, Lu, Qi, Jin, Zheng, Chu, Wu and Yue (the old ascription is to Qiu Ming 丘明 — the theory that it and the *Zuo Tradition* (*Zuozhuan*) are the "Inner and Outer Traditions" has been held in doubt since the Song), and it excels at remonstrance: Duke Shao 邵公 remonstrating with King Li 厉王 against the suppression of criticism, “**防民之口，甚于防川**” ('**Blocking the people's mouths is worse than blocking a river.**') (Our translation.) — the eternal motif of remonstrance writing. The ***Intrigues of the Warring States*** (*Zhanguoce*): a comprehensive collection of the speeches of persuasion of the Warring States (Liu Xiang 刘向 fixed the title when he collated the books; not the work of one person or one time), in which **the mouths of the *zongheng* persuaders** become the protagonists of the literature: Zou Ji 邹忌's indirect remonstrance with the king of Qi (wife, concubine and guest, three mirrors → the politics of the court — a fine stroke grafting a parable of daily life onto indirect remonstrance); Chu Long 触龙 persuading the Queen Dowager of Zhao (entering from “父母之爱子，则为之计深远” ('If parents love a child, they make far-reaching plans for that child.') (Our translation.), the psychology of negotiation turned wholly into literary art); Jing Ke 荆轲 (the scene arrangement of the farewell at the Yi River 易水, a half-finished piece from which the *Records of the Grand Historian* (*Shiji*) drew its material). Coordinates in literary history: the *Zuo Tradition* still keeps the sternness of the ritual officers; the *Intrigues* is entirely the flash of parading the book-bag (掉书袋) — **the slide from "phrasing" to "exaggeration" is the *shi* 士 ('men of service') turning from envoys on missions of courtesy into persuaders who sell their art** (Liu Xiang's 'Record of the *Intrigues of the Warring States*' has already set down the comment of assessment: “捐礼让而贵战争，弃仁义而用诈谲” ('They cast aside courtesy and yielding and prized warfare; they abandoned humaneness and rightness and employed deceit and trickery.') (Our translation.)).
:::

::: intuition The Chinese contract between history and literature {#int-hei}
The Western epic grew up first (narrative was assigned to poetry); in China **the court historian grew up first** (narrative was assigned to history) — this contract has influenced the whole of literary history: fiction is to “羽翼信史” ('be wings to reliable history'), and the chapter novel is to “据正史” ('rely on the official histories'); outside the lyric tradition, the first legitimacy of Chinese narrative comes from historical biography (史传). To read the historical prose of the pre-Qin one must wear two pairs of glasses: **the glasses of history** (to what level is this material credible — a proclamation or charge? a retrospective record? a composition imputed to someone?) and **the glasses of literature** (how this stretch of narrative is arranged: fullness and brevity, advance narration, speeches composed on another's behalf — the techniques of fiction drawing a salary inside the history books). Mencius's two principles, “以意逆志” ('using one's understanding to meet the poet's intent') and “知人论世” ('knowing the person and the times'), starting from the reading of the *Book of Songs* and the *Documents*, are the birth certificate of Chinese hermeneutics.
:::

::: history From Fu Sheng's oral transmission to the Tsinghua slips {#sec-zhongren}
**Early Han**: Fu Sheng transmitted it orally, and Fu Sheng fixed the "New Text" in writing in Han clerical script, in twenty-two chapters (the learning of Ouyang and of Xiahou was established in office); once the Old Text from the wall of the Kong house appeared, **the New Text–Old Text controversy** sounded the gong (Ma Rong 马融 and Zheng Xuan 郑玄 connected the New and Old Texts through and through); **Eastern Jin**: Mei Ze presented the Old Text *Book of Documents* containing the *Kong Commentary*, and for one thousand three hundred years thereafter it served as the model text of official learning; **the 1680s**: Yan Ruoqu's *Evidential Analysis of the Old Text of the Book of Documents* adduced, in more than a hundred items, evidence of the forgery, and the verdict on the "forged Old Text" was delivered — a milestone case of evidential scholarship; **from 1923**: Gu Jiegang 顾颉刚's *Debates on Ancient History* (*Gushi bian*) judged the 'Canon of Yao' and the 'Tribute of Yu' to be political arguments which men of the Warring States designed and passed off as ancient (the theory of layered accumulation making a frontal assault on the *Book of Documents*); **from 2008**: the Tsinghua slips entered the collection, and the chapters of the "*Documents* class" were collated against the transmitted text (many pieces can be read against the current text for what is the same and what differs); the old case of the genuine and the forged gained a new chain of evidence in the script of the Warring States — **the reception history of one *Book of Documents* is half the methodology of Chinese classical learning**.
:::

::: warning Three thunderclaps in historical prose
First, **treating the *Zuo Tradition* as a verbatim news record**: the book was completed in the early Warring States, and the speeches and charges have mostly passed through **imitative composition and the revision of wording** (on the point of the historical brush "revising the wording", Mr Qian Zhongshu 钱锺书 exposed it the most resoundingly — between recorded speech and speech composed on another's behalf, the historian's hand is present); second, **believing the *New Text* entirely and discarding the *Old Text* entirely**: the sixteen-character transmission of the mind in the forged Old Text, “人心惟危，道心惟微” ('The human mind is precarious; the mind of the Way is subtle.') (Our translation.), was enshrined in earnest by the scholars of the Learning of Principle — **the document is forged, yet the history of thought is real**; one cut for every case will cut one's own foot; third, **using the *Intrigues* to settle history**: the men who went about persuading were “面墙自是” ('facing a wall and sure they are right'); events in the *Intrigues* often diverge from the *Records of the Grand Historian* and the *Jinian* ('Annals') — it is **a sample library of the Warring States arts of persuasion, not the ledger of the Warring States**.
:::

::: exercise Practice in the method of the brush {level=1}
“夏，五月，郑伯以璧假许田” ('Summer, the fifth month: the Earl of Zheng, with a jade disc, borrowed the fields of Xu.') (Our translation.) (the event is in the eighth year of Duke Yin of Lu) — using your knowledge of exegesis and of the rules of recording, explain what is "not normal" in this sentence (one point each for names and status, for ritual institutions, and for wording).
::: solution
Three checks. (1) **The event is not normal**: the exchange of Beng 祊 and the fields of Xu; the regional lords, on their own authority, exchanged with one another the fields of the Son of Heaven (“假” is in fact “易” — the skin of borrowing wraps the flesh of exchanging). (2) **The ritual is not normal**: an exchange of fields ought to be reported to the king; they exchanged on their own authority without reporting — the text does not record what is great in the affair but records what is small in it, and the trace of the transgression of ritual appears of itself. (3) **The wording is not normal**: “以璧假” — the jade disc is a loincloth covering the handling fee; the *Gongyang*'s “假不言易，以是易也” ('It says "borrow" and does not say "exchange"; by this, it is an exchange.') (Our translation.) exposes the wording's hiding of the shame. Nine characters in one sentence, a thermometer of the collapse of rites and music — marking point: it is enough to make plain that "wording = judgement".
:::
:::

::: exercise Anatomising the rhetoric of remonstrance {level=2}
Take the full text of 'Duke Shao Remonstrates with King Li against Suppressing Criticism', and draw the chain of argument in the remonstrance (damming the river — bursting — giving vent — “善败于是乎出” ('Success and failure come forth from this.') (Our translation.)), together with the sources of its persuasive force and its risks.
::: solution
The chain: suppressing criticism = damming a river (the analogy establishes the claim) → when a river is dammed and then bursts, those it injures are sure to be many (the consequence in disaster) → the people's having mouths is like the earth's having mountains and rivers (the productivity of the mouth: the making public of success and failure = of use both as revenue and as public sentiment) → blocking the people's mouths is worse than blocking a river (the conclusion raised a step). Source of the persuasion: the shared fear of a hydraulic society is taken as the premise, and the vehicle of the comparison is the argument. Risk: **the analogy takes on the work that evidence ought to take on** — a “川” ('river') can be channelled (导), but who directs (主导) the "channelling" of the people's mouths? The vehicle gives cover to a premise about power (Duke Shao wants to “宣之” ('give it vent'), yet the power to give it vent still lies with the king). Whoever can point out this layer of risk gets an A.
:::

Write eight hundred to one thousand two hundred characters. “中国叙事文学的奶妈是史官” ('The wet-nurse of Chinese narrative literature is the court historian.') (Our translation.) Argue for it, or argue against it: (1) give one example each from the *Zuo Tradition* and the *Intrigues* of a prefiguration of a technique of fiction (choose any one among scene arrangement, the design of detail, and the arrangement of dialogue); (2) set this against the line of Zhang Xuecheng 章学诚's “六经皆史” ('the Six Classics are all history') (Our translation.) (*Comprehensive Meaning of Literature and History*, *Wenshi tongyi*), and discuss the status of "history" as lineage-head in the Chinese genealogy of knowledge; (3) discuss the long-term influence of this layer of "the orthodox line of history" on the later awkwardness, in fiction, of "fabricating on the basis of fact".
::: solution
Marking points: the technical examples are concrete (for instance the foreshadowing of advance and retreat at the battle of Chengpu 城濮, and, at the farewell on the Yi River, a cluster of details of the type “士皆垂泪涕泣” ('the gentlemen all let their tears fall and wept') (Our translation.)); "the Six Classics are all history" is used in the Chinese context rather than forced onto the Western dispute between poetry and history; the ending can point out that the idea of the "remainder of history" (史余) is both the antenatal education of fiction and its shackles (the self-abasement of the "petty officials" (稗官) and the self-respect of "being wings to reliable history" share one root).

::: summary
Four steps of historical prose: the *Book of Documents* records speech — being crabbed and tongue-twisting is a dialect fossil, and the case of the forged Old Text is the coming-of-age rite of evidential scholarship; the brush of the *Spring and Autumn* — one-word names of crimes joined in one body with a cognitive transcript; remonstrance in the *Discourses of the States* and persuasion in the *Intrigues of the Warring States* — from the sternness of the ritual officers, to the flash of parading the book-bag, to the mouths of the *zongheng* persuaders; the Chinese contract signed between history and literature let narrative literature grow up for two thousand years under the eaves of historical biography. The next chapter reads closely the protagonist of this play: the *Zuo Tradition* — how war was written as literature.
:::
