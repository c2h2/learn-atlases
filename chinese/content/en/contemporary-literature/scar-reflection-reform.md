---
title: "Scar, reflection and reform literature"
---

The literature of the previous chapter had no readers; the literature of this chapter suddenly had readers, and the readers were more impatient than the authors — **the three characters "伤痕", at the end of 1978, became the name of an age: a movement of literary naming began; what this chapter asks is not what these works said, but how they were permitted to say it: to be wounded is a kind of right, and a right must have an application form**.

## Definition: three words, three inquiries {#def-sange}

::: definition The ladder of the question {#def-jieti}
**The thread of the received account** (**"scar literature" (Literature of the Wounded), "reflection literature" and "reform literature" are all names that the world of criticism attached afterwards, and the proposing of the names and the controversy occurred almost at the same time —** please read these three words as three steps of a single inquiry (**scar asks: who suffered? Reflection asks: from which year did the suffering begin, and who decided it? Reform asks: then what is to be done now? (**each step pushes the question onto a less convenient person to hold responsible: the individual, policy, the system —** this is why the "degree of permission" of the three steps falls in turn, and the lifespan of the works shortens in turn**)); **A technical fact** (**the thread of the received account: in 1977–1978 journals resumed publication, the systems of manuscript fees and of prizes were restored one after another, and novels could be written again —** the previous chapter's "market with no one to sell to" once again had sales figures (**so criticism could, for the first time, let print runs do the talking (Chapter 1, the format of distribution:** the same novel, printed in a million copies and printed in thirty thousand, will be written into two kinds of literary history**.
:::

::: definition Why begin with the short story {#def-duanpian}
**The advantage of the short story** (**the thread of the received account: the landmark works of this period are almost all short stories and novellas —** the short story is the natural form of trauma:** it cuts only one cross-section, and need not explain how the cross-section came about (Chapter 7, "implicitness is pressure":** what is omitted is not detail, but responsibility (**the risk that a novel explaining the causes had to run at that time was exactly equal to its depth**)); **The place of 'The Class Teacher'** (**the received account: Liu Xinwu 刘心武, *Banzhuren* 《班主任》 ('The Class Teacher', 1977), asks its question out of a teacher's anxiety, and is regarded as the beginning of "scar"; Lu Xinhua 卢新华, *Shanghen* 《伤痕》 ('The Scar', 1978), makes the whole piece out of a final parting of mother and daughter and a repentance that came too late to be a self-criticism —** please note the structure the two pieces share:** the suffering must take place inside the family, and must be borne by a family member who can be forgiven (**the family is the only legitimate opening for a wound (**this is the core mechanism of this chapter:** when a public calling-to-account is impossible, private relationships take the place of the court and receive all the evidence**.
:::

## The family as a court {#def-jiating}

::: definition Reunion is the stand-in for the verdict {#def-tuangyuan}
**The plot formula** (**the plot pattern of the received account: separation—suffering—exoneration—reunion (or the reunion delayed until an instant at the end of the piece) —** this is the last reuse, in the twentieth century, of Chapter 1's "reunion = a judicial declaration" (**the difference lies in who declares: this time it is not an upright official, but "history has at last come right" (**so scar literature looks like a rebellion, yet its deep grammar is still that of a rehabilitation play (Chapter 1, the clause on Yuan variety plays (*zaju*):** the three wishes, the ghost marriage and the reunion are all there; only the judge has been renamed "time"**)); **The function of tears** (**the thread of the received account: readers' responses to the works of this period were marked by weeping, and readers' letters became a new genre of criticism —** weeping is an expression that may be shown in public:** in an age that had just ended a rule of "no weeping", to weep was itself a political statement (**this is one of the few cases, in the study of literary effect, that can be observed directly:** a newspaper's printing of readers' letters was the same as publishing an opinion poll (**a distant relative of Chapter 1's "paid room = something one can rely on":** for the first time, readers knew that they were not alone**.
:::

::: definition Reflection literature drills downward {#def-fansi}
**The lengthening of the timeline** (**the thread of the received account: in 1979–1981 a number of works pushed the cause of the suffering from the ten years to the seventeen years, and even to the first political life after the victory of the revolution —** this is the whole technical content of "reflection":** it adds no new theme; it moves the starting point of the narrative earlier (Chapter 8, "the document that assigns responsibility":** to move the starting point is to redraw who is responsible (**it is more dangerous than scar literature, because it begins to touch years in which the people who decided are still living**)); **Memoirs of the *Sitong lu* kind fill the gap** (**the thread of the received account: personal narratives that arrived late, together with diaries and letters, became the side-branch historical materials of this line —** they supply what fiction cannot get:** the linguistic habits of those years themselves (**what is most worth having, in reading them, is not the events but the wording: how a person, in 1957, described his own neighbour (Chapter 8, the file of public sentiment:** vocabulary is testimony more honest than figures**)); **An updating of the types of character** (**the gist of the received account: Rightists, returnees from abroad, and cadres who had been wrongly dealt with become the protagonists —** a nation writes "people who had received an education" once more as objects worthy of sympathy (**this was not something to be taken for granted in the literature after 1949:** it is the real ideological change in this chapter, deeper than any denunciation**.
:::

## Reform literature and its difficulty {#def-gaige}

::: definition The entrepreneur as hero {#def-qiyejia}
**The thread of the received account** (**around 1981 there appeared fiction and reports with factory reform as the main line, and works of the kind of *Qiao changzhang shangren ji* 《乔厂长上任记》 ('Factory Director Qiao Assumes Office') were regarded as representatives of "reform literature" —** the new hero's CV:** he understands the technology, dares to sign, can tell people off, and has a crowd of old cadres holding him back (**this is one lateral transfer of the grammar of the "three prominences" (三突出) in the Seventeen Years:** the composition does not change; the protagonist is changed (Chapter 1, "the shot list":** spatial grammar outlives ideology**)); **The difficulty of form** (**reform has no ending:** any novel that writes of what is "in progress" must make its own ending, and so it can only choose, by force, between victory and defeat (**this lesson calls an ending of this kind "a verdict signed under compulsion" (the counter-example, in Chapter 7, of *The Peach Blossom Fan*, "refusing to sign the verdict":** reform literature does not dare not to sign, because it has a commissioning party: it is testifying on behalf of a policy**); **Its legacy** (**the thread of the received account: this line later flowed into the "reportage boom" and into documentary writing —** the first professionalisation of Chinese non-fiction:** journalists and writers converge again (downstream of Chapter 1's "genre is a move in officialdom":** reportage is a hybrid of literature and official documents; its good point is that it is fast, and the price is that it cannot be slow (**later, in the 1990s, it was taken over by "in-depth reporting"; see the next chapter, and 'Reports and In-depth Reporting' (《报告与深度报道》) in the writing course**.
:::

::: definition The dispute over the quota {#def-xianeg}
**"Which of brightness and darkness is the mainstream"** (**the thread of the received account: in 1979–1980 the debate on "writing the dark side" entered the press on several occasions, and the critics demanded that one distinguish "nine fingers and one finger" —** this lesson reads it as one negotiation over quotas (**what the two sides contest is not the truth-value, but the quota for publication:** "mainstream" is a quota-word (Lesson 1, the right to define is the right to allocate:** whoever can fix the minimum proportion of "brightness" holds the plan of the whole of literary production (**this is also the sequel to the previous chapter's "cost structure": when the quota is raised, the writer's optimal solution is not to write less darkness, but to write the darkness as someone else's story (history, the borderlands, allegory —** and so the "root-seeking" and the "avant-garde" of the next chapter were, as a matter of quota, forced into being**.
:::

::: definition The queue: who is qualified to be wounded {#def-paidui}
**The difference in treatment among three kinds of scar** (**the thread of the received account: intellectuals and cadres who had suffered were the quickest to obtain the right to be depicted; while the narratives of other groups harmed in the same period (families of landlords and rich peasants, people under supervision, ethnic minorities, and peasants) went for a long time with no one to take them up —** this is not a conspiracy; it is the mechanism of submission:** editorial offices and readers allocate the budget of sympathy on the "misunderstanding–correction" model, and accept only the story of "a good person wrongly blamed" (**any suffering that does not fit this model is judged "complicated", and complication must queue (Chapter 3, survivors discounted to eighty per cent:** the scars we read are the scars that passed the editor's consent (**this is the most important criticism in this chapter, and it is also the point most easily overlooked by this chapter's own textbook:** every literary liberation has a gallery seat that was not invited**.
:::

::: example The three steps {#ex-jieti}
The table below is a rewriting of three plots of the same type. Please judge which level each belongs to, **where the person responsible is placed**, and for each write one line on "**what information the author must add in order to rise to the next level**".
| Plot | Person responsible | What rising requires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① The mother dies of illness during the period when the daughter is sent down; the daughter does not manage a final parting | ? | ? |
| ② The same person is disciplined because of a document from 1957; the one who wrote the directive was a classmate of that year | ? | ? |
| ③ A factory director puts an assessment system into force, and is held back jointly by the trade union, family members, and the regulations of the level above | ? | ? |
::: solution
**① Scar** — **Person responsible: an unnameable "that stretch of history" (**What rising requires: to write "who gave the order" as one specific institution and one specific document (**and once this step is taken, the work turns from the lyrical to the giving of evidence, and the risk turns from an individual into an organisation (**note: with many works it is not that they do not dare to rise; it is that once they have risen there are no tears left:** tears need a nameless enemy**.
**② Reflection** — **Person responsible: a person who has a name, and who has an old affection (**What rising requires: to explain why the one who wrote the directive had to act that way, that is, to write the structure of rewards the system offered him (**this step is the fork between "reform" and "the avant-garde":** the former writes how bad people are cleared away; the latter writes that no one is bad, and yet the thing still happens (**this lesson calls the latter the higher degree of difficulty:** it needs a set of narrative devices with which to present a fault that has no subject (**this is why the modernist techniques of the next chapter suddenly became necessary in the mid-1980s, and not because they were fashionable**.
**③ Reform** — **Person responsible: a set of rules and habits in conflict with one another (**What rising requires: to give a set of rules to replace them; but the novel has no power to legislate (**so novels of this kind can only end on "someone is appointed to a greater office":** an institutional problem is solved by a decision about personnel (Chapter 6, the problem of who is to carry out a policy proposal:** this is the structural ending of reform literature, not the weakness of some particular author**.
:::
:::

::: intuition The right to name {#int-mingming}
**A critic's power** (**the name "scar literature" was made to catch on by journalists and editors together; "reflection" and "reform" were likewise products of articles of criticism (the thread of the received account) —** this chapter treats naming as an institutional act:** naming binds a writing that is still developing into one bundle, and then decides when it is "out of date" (**literature that has been named has a shorter life, because the name dies before the works do (**the obverse and the reverse of Chapter 1's "system of peer review": naming lets works be discussed, and also lets works be settled up**); **A check that can be carried elsewhere** (**whenever you see a movement-label of the form "-literature", "-fiction", "-poetry", ask three things first:** who said it first, who is opposing it, and why the opponents' works did not enter the same label (**this is the everyday application of Lesson 6's "clearing the field":** the most silent part of literary history is usually the part that was given the wrong label**).
:::

::: history A five-year timetable {#sec-richen}
**1977** 'The Class Teacher' (**one of the first batch of texts after publication resumed:** it proves that the journals' opening of their doors was earlier than the official announcement of the policy**); **1978** 'The Scar' and the name "scar" (**the birth of a name took less than three months:** this shows that editorial offices and readers were both waiting for a word (**the mirror image of Chapter 1's "literature comes to stamp the seal": this time it was literature waiting for society to stamp a seal on it**); **1979** the Fourth Congress of Literary and Art Workers, and *Wenyi bao* 《文艺报》 ('Literary Gazette') reopened a number of discussions (**the institution co-opts again, and at the same time issues quotas again**); **1979–1980** the several discussions of "writing the dark side", of "human nature and humanism", and of "alienation" (**in the same winter, the quota, philosophy and literature were contending over one and the same thing:** who can represent the pain of the collective (Lesson 3, the four uses of "we":** this time "we" began to split**)); **1980** short stories of the "customs–lyric" kind appeared, of the sort of Wang Zengqi 汪曾祺, *Shoujie* 《受戒》 ('Ordination') (**this chapter's technical way out:** when direct statement is restricted, literature retreats into description (**it is not opposed to "reflection"; it is two product lines of one and the same unbinding (Chapter 8, the duty-free zone:** a new way of writing lives first beside the old one**)); **1981–1983** reflection and reform stood side by side, and the reportage boom arose (**the expansion of "fast" forms:** for the first time, literature scheduled its production by the calendar of the news**); **after 1983** a new rectification and a "clean-up" began to affect the staffing of journals (**the quota tightened again —** and so the main line of the next chapter is already visible: when "what to write" is restricted, "how to write" becomes everything (the third cycle of Chapter 4's *shiyu* 诗余 ('the remainder of poetry') and Chapter 11's "the programme is the equipment"**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of this lesson
**Taking "scar" as a pejorative** (**it is a descriptive label for a period:** to abuse a work with it is the same as abusing a poem with "Tang" (Lesson 1, a list is a pair of glasses**)); **Substituting subject matter for technique** (**"writing the Cultural Revolution = important" does not hold:** one must say what narrative device it used (**this chapter's criterion:** under the same subject matter, whoever writes the nameless enemy into a named structure is the one who remains**)); **Ignoring the readers' side** (**the readers' letters, the readers' votes, the print runs and the prizes of this period make up a rare "period in which readers can be observed":** to read only the texts and not the readers is to waste the historical material (Chapter 1, "the paid room one can rely on"**)); **Settling a quota negotiation as an aesthetic dispute** (**"nine fingers" is not an aesthetic judgement, but a scheme of allocation:** the correct response to it is not to write more brightness, but to ask who calculates the proportion (Lesson 2, the right to define**).
:::

::: exercise A quota table {level=1}
Find a piece of material that the institution you belong to displays to the outside, and estimate in 80 characters: **how much quota it leaves for the negative, and in what form (time, a single case, other people)**, and write down **the kind of subject it is least willing to have appear**.
::: solution
Marking: **the quota must have a denominator** (**what fraction, how many paragraphs it occupies:** a numerator without a denominator is not to be trusted (Lesson 3, the return visit**); **the form column must be specific** (**"placed three years ago", "only one person is written of", "caused from outside":** these three items are precisely the quota techniques of scar literature (Lesson 1, "doing the accounts that other people leave out".
:::

::: exercise Short essay: the application form for being wounded {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**every age has a list of who is allowed to tell of suffering, and the list is usually fixed by the readers who need these stories most: they are willing to pay for only one kind of suffering — the kind that can be explained as a misunderstanding; so a generation of writers learned to translate their own experience first into 'a good person wrongly blamed', and then to submit it; what this chapter is truly worth remembering for is not how successful these works were, but the format they accepted in order to be heard**". You must call on at least six items: **the three steps**, **the family as a court**, **reunion as the stand-in for the verdict**, **reflection's technique of moving the starting point earlier**, **the structural ending of reform literature**, **the gallery seat that was not invited**; and you must adjudicate: **is accepting a format in order to be heard a compromise or a strategy — you must give a criterion, for distinguishing "borrowing the format" from "being incorporated by the format", and give one example of each**.
::: solution
Marking points: the six mechanisms (**the steps, the court, the stand-in, the move earlier, the structural ending, the gallery**); the criterion (**for reference:** look at whether a residue is left after the format has been borrowed — **if the work, inside a shell of compliance, keeps a loss that cannot be explained (a person who does not apologise, an account that cannot be made to come out, an ending in which there is no reunion), it is borrowing; if in the end every loss becomes understandable, forgivable, and creditable to history, then it has been incorporated (the marking looks at whether two kinds of specific text are cited, and at which sentence the "residue" sits; vague talk of "sincerity" scores nothing**); if the conclusion can be written "**so the achievement of scar literature is not that it spoke the truth, but that it made the truth into a format which could be read aloud in public; its cost is equally clear: the sufferings that could not be translated into the format waited another twenty years, and while they waited no one remembered that they had once been absent**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
Filed under the steps: the three levels (**who suffered, from which year, what is to be done**), the court (**the family receives all the evidence, and reunion takes the place of the verdict**), the move earlier (**the whole technique of reflection is to move the starting point earlier**), reform (**an institutional problem is solved by a decision about personnel: a structural ending**), the quota (**"mainstream" is a word of allocation, not a word of aesthetics**), the gallery (**any liberation has a list of those who were not invited**). One sentence of method: **when you meet "the voice of an age", first check which clauses of the format it signed in order to be heard**.
