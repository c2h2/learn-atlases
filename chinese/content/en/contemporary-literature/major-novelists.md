---
title: "Major novelists since the 1980s"
---

The previous chapter said that literature had been removed from the office of speaking for others; this chapter writes of several people who demanded that office back — **their method is first to make a piece of ground: a county, a city, a clan, a river; and then to claim that everything once happened there: this is the first time Chinese fiction took "fictional geography" as the basis of sovereignty**.

## Definition: first make a county {#def-xian}

::: definition Territorial rights on loan {#def-lingtu}
**Received account** (**Faulkner's Yoknapatawpha and Márquez's Macondo entered, through translation, the kit of methods of Chinese writers in the 1980s; Northeast Gaomi Township, Shangzhou, White Deer Plain and the Gao family compound appeared one after another — **this lesson calls the move "the outsourcing of territorial rights"** (**when a writer cannot obtain legislative power inside the real institutions, he goes into fiction and applies for a piece of ground (Chapter 5, "the universe that can be authorised":** authorisation is not rhetoric; it is a title deed (**its technical consequence is extremely concrete:** once he has this piece of ground, the same writer can write for thirty years without repeating himself: the subject matter changes, the map does not (**this is the organisational form of the long novel's productive capacity, not a problem of inspiration**.
:::

## The four {#def-siwei}

::: definition Mo Yan 莫言: the mixing of testimony {#def-moyan}
**Received account** (**_Red Sorghum Family_, _Big Breasts and Wide Hips_, _The Republic of Wine_, _Sandalwood Death_, _Life and Death Are Wearing Me Out_, and others; in 2012 he received the Nobel Prize in Literature, and his prize speech took 'My Mother' and 'The Storyteller' as its titles; the author has repeatedly explained that his fictional county takes its material from what he saw and heard at home — **this lesson attends to three things (**①** the deliberate mixing of the ranks of sources** (**gist of the received view: family oral accounts, local gazetteers, the news and imagination set side by side — **this is the writer's version of Chapter 8, "the layering of historical sources":** other people layer in order to be honest; he layers in order to get the volume (**when a stretch of ghost-talk sits hard against a stretch of county gazetteer, the reader cannot downgrade any one stretch (**this is the actual craft of "the magical" in Chinese:** not believing the myth, but placing the myth among the references**)); ②** sensory inflation** (**the received view remarks on his dense metaphors and on the overload of colour and smell — **this lesson reads that as a hedge against the previous chapter:** new realism obtains credibility by "adding no adjectives"; Mo Yan obtains existence by "too many adjectives" (**the two policies of visibility are exactly the opposite:** one says "I only record", the other says "there is nothing I am willing to let pass" (**readers in the 1990s needed the latter, because they had already read enough of the former (**this is the metabolism of literary history, not a verdict of higher and lower**)); ③** the return of the professional storyteller** (**his later works borrow the structures of local opera (_xiqu_) and of speaking-and-singing (gist of the received view) (**the contemporary closing of the loop in Chapter 1, "the grammar of the vernacular (_baihua_) grew up in the _gōulán_ 勾栏 ('theatre yards')":** a novelist of the twentieth century, and the audience he goes back in the end to find, is people who listen to stories told (**this is also one link in his being amplified, when he won an international prize, by translation and by film and television (the "film as intermediary" clause of the previous chapter**.
:::

::: definition Jia Pingwa 贾平凹: the ledger of one banned book {#def-jiapingwa}
**Received account** (**_Ruined City_ was published in 1993, banned almost at once, and only in 2009 formally reissued by the original publisher; in between it circulated in duplicate copies and pirate editions, and the author's situation and income were affected (received view) — **three consequences that can be checked (**①** times banned = a count of influence** (the contemporary positive instance of the clause in Chapter 3:** one generation of readers' experience of "the first banned book" is this book (**a stock phrase in its text of the 今夕何夕 ('what evening is this evening?') kind (gist of the line as commonly cited) became, instead, a signal that could be passed on (the formula of transmissibility in Lesson 4:** a quotation mark one does not need to understand has the highest rate of transmission**)); ②** what it writes is the hollowness of the literati, not the injustice of society** (**gist of the received view: a novel of the daily life of writers, celebrities and literary retainers — **this is where it truly gives offence:** it writes intellectuals as vessels of sexual desire and vanity — **Chapter 9's return visit to Wu Jingzi 吴敬梓:** _Ruined City_ is _The Scholars_ of the 1990s, but it no longer believes that "healthy people" can be set outside the book (**Wang Mian 王冕 is dead, and the four extraordinary men are gone too; all that is left is a person writing, sitting inside his own book and staring blankly**)); ③** his later turn** (**received account: works of the _Qin Opera_ kind, taking the breaking up of the village as their subject — **this chapter reads this line as a ledger of hollowing-out:** the people have gone, and the opera is still being sung:** the contemporary way of filling in the third-party column of Chapter 6 — **what it counts is the old people who remain, the fruit rotting on the trees, and the tickets nobody buys (**this is the first time fiction in Chinese made "there being no event" the theme of a whole long novel (**new realism wrote it as one day; Jia Pingwa writes it as one village: the scale has changed, the device has not**.
:::

::: definition Chen Zhongshi 陈忠实: the cost of an epic {#def-chenzhongshi}
**Received account** (**_White Deer Plain_ was published in 1993 and received the Mao Dun Literature Prize in 1997; the author had consulted county gazetteers and put the materials of his native place in order; according to the author's own account and to related recollections, the wording was revised in the course of publication and of the prize (received account) — **this lesson takes this as a specimen of an institution (**①** the last smile of "a prize is an inspection"** (**the contemporary version of Chapter 1, "literature comes to put on the seal", and of Chapter 2, "the approved copy is the established text":** what was cut this time was not politically sensitive detail, but the body: the prize system's tolerance for "sex" is lower than its tolerance for "death" (**from this one can measure the place where a system hurts (**this is the most important piece of measurable evidence in this chapter**)); ②** the weight of national allegory** (**gist of the received view: half a century of history written through the changes of two clans — **the epic requires three generations, the land, the clan order and famine all to be in place:** the cost is that characters must become representatives (the typification in Lesson 1, "one person represents a class of people":** Chen Zhongshi is the last novelist in Chinese still carrying out this rule in earnest (**this is why, when one reads him, he is "old" and he is "heavy":** the oldness is the format, the heaviness is the quota — every single thing he wants to say has to have a legitimate heading**.
:::

::: definition Wang Xiaobo 王小波: writing freedom as humour {#def-wangxiaobo}
**Received account** (**_The Golden Age_, _The Silent Majority_, and others; little was published in his lifetime, and after his death in 1997 his reputation rose quickly, and he became a "bestselling classic" among young readers in the 2000s — **what this lesson cares about most is this lag (**①** the channels of the reputation** (**received account: friends putting the papers in order, pirate editions and the early online communities, reviews and word of mouth among literary youth (_wénqīng_ 文青) — **this is the first time literature in Chinese made a god mainly through a network of readers, rather than through journals and prizes (contemporary proof of Chapter 1, that the format of distribution decides life and death:** a kind of writing can go on waiting until the Party A best suited to it appears (_jiǎfāng_ 甲方, the party that commissions) (**②** the technique of humour** (**gist of the received view: sexual description and an absurd account in his own voice are used to see through political vocabulary — **his method is not irony (**irony still needs an orthodoxy that can be satirised**), but a dislocation of register (**Lesson 12: make official vocabulary do what it cannot do (**a sentence-form of the kind "我要用一种方法证明，我活着" ('I am going to use a method to prove that I am alive.') (Our translation.) (gist of what is commonly quoted) — **so humour is not a temperament; it is a way of giving evidence:** in an age when everyone says the same set of words, being funny is the only form in which freedom can appear in public (**the continuation, in the 1990s, of Chapter 8, "the species explosion of rhetoric"**)); ③** it becomes a mark of cultivation** (**received account: in the 2000s "Wang Xiaobo" was quoted and retold in great quantity — **this chapter's cold judgement:** the more the words "interesting" and "free" are used under his name, the thinner their meaning (Lesson 3, becoming a set phrase = the rate of circulation rises and the meaning goes to zero:** any classic that got its start in rebellion is, in its thirtieth year, a slogan in a stationery shop**.
:::

## One device {#def-zhuangzhi}

::: definition _To Live_ and _The Ancient Ship_: who has the standing to retell {#def-fushu}
**Received plot** (**_To Live_ collects Fugui 福贵's own account through a folk narrator who cleans ears, and Fugui calmly tells, to the end, the deaths of his whole life; _The Ancient Ship_, inside a family's accounts and hatred, leaves the protagonist blaming himself for a long time — **two devices of retelling (**①** the victim's own account, plus an unreliable listener outside** (**the efficiency of the former was already set out in Chapter 5:** a confession cannot be rebutted; and its ethical cost lies exactly in this device — **the reader is arranged into the position of "listening to a story", and so the sympathy is designed (**this is the technical reason it is praised as "compassion" and blamed as "cheap" at the same time (**this lesson's way of judging:** do not ask whether it is true or not; ask whom it puts in the listener's seat (**②** the descendants of those who did the harm, clearing the account themselves** (**the protagonist of _The Ancient Ship_ keeps watch over the family's crime and its poverty — **this is the end of that line of reflection literature:** responsibility is traced back from the institution to this village, this family, this person (**received account: it has been praised very highly and disputed relatively little (**this lesson's explanation:** Chinese readers are more willing to accept "a guilt that everyone has a share of", because it is safer, and more comfortable, than "someone is guilty" (**mark this sentence: it is the hardest judgement this chapter can give reception aesthetics (reception theory)**.
:::

::: definition Shi Tiesheng 史铁生: writing disability as a method {#def-shi}
**Received account** (**_My Faraway Qingpingwan_, _Life Is Like a Qin String_, _Notes on the Abstract_ and _The Temple of Earth and I_; on account of a disability he wrote for a long time seated in a wheelchair — **this lesson's handling (**①** what he solves is the hard problem the previous chapter left** (**when the narrator cannot be trusted, and history has no necessity, can one still speak of meaning? His answer is the answer to that question in Chapter 5:** the end is empty, the process is real (**the blind monk's nose, the purpose on the string, the mother in the Temple of Earth (**the three works share one structure of proof:** first give an ending that cannot be realised, then prove that a person must still walk the whole course (**②** he wrote "process" as an ethic that can be defended** (**this is the rarest sentence-form of the 1990s:** it neither accuses (scar), nor records (new realism), nor plays (Wang Shuo 王朔) (**this chapter's chart of coordinates is completed here:** four stances, four Parties A**); **③** the composition of his readers** (**received account: his works have long entered textbooks and recitation, and are heavily excerpted — **this lesson's warning:** the writer who is excerpted is the easiest to be read as "chicken soup" ('comfort-reading') (Chapter 7, to make a selection is to cut and alter:** what is carried off from _The Temple of Earth and I_ is the mother and the tree; what is cut is that long stretch of argument about sex, disability and death (**to read him one must read the whole piece, and the whole piece is not pleasant to read — **this is the fate of moral philosophy as a kind of writing**).
:::

::: example Two productive capacities {#ex-channeng}
Compare Mo Yan and Chen Zhongshi: **① how much each writes in a day, ② where the material comes from, ③ what the "map" and the "archive" of one long novel each are**; then answer: **why a high-output writer is taken to have "an excess of imagination", and a low-output writer to be "weighty" — what the evidence for each of these two judgements is, and which of them are only a prejudice about the production period**.
::: solution
**① The production period** (**received account: the former kept, over a long time, an extremely fast speed of writing, and a large number of short stories; the latter spent years preparing local materials for one book — **the difference in output is first a difference in how the body and the household schedule the work (the contemporary version of Chapter 4, "output = labour time"**); **② Material** (**one takes oral accounts from home and sensory memory (**which can be called on at once**); one takes county gazetteers, archives and interviews (**which must be collected in advance**); **③ The device** (**map against archive:** one makes the world into a stage, one makes the world into a case file (**this explains the two criticisms:** "an excess of imagination" is said of the one who does not check; "weighty" is said of the one who checks too much (**this lesson's ruling — **both are prejudices about the production period:** the evidence is that in the 1990s they exchanged directions (**the same high-output writer, once translated, was read as "abundance"; the same low-output writer, after the prize, was often read as "slow work yields fine work":** the adjectives changed, the production period did not (**this is exactly the application to literary history of Lesson 3, "a qualifier is a bumper":** the qualifiers we add are usually the comfort we ourselves need**).
:::

::: intuition The four gates of canonisation {#def-sidao}
**Gate one: journals and series** (**the place of publication decides the first layer of legitimacy;** Gate two: prizes** (**the Mao Dun Literature Prize and the like turn the long novel into an "achievement" rather than a "work";** Gate three: textbooks and anthologies** (**they decide whom one is compelled to meet at the age of twenty;** Gate four: translation abroad and international prizes** (**they decide who obtains a budget outside the mother tongue (received account: after the 1990s the relative weight of the four gates gradually shifted towards the rear — **this lesson's method is just this table (**any judgement that "this writer is a classic", first ask which of the gates it passed, and who opened them (**and then you find:** "important" is a supply-chain adjective (the final formula of Lesson 1, "literature comes to put on the seal", and of the retrospective recognition in Lesson 15:** it is not that the work entered literary history; it is that literary history's buyer stopped the car in front of it**.
:::

::: history One supply chain {#sec-gongying}
**1985—1987** the avant-garde and root-seeking drew on local places at the same time (**received account:** county and village became, for the first time, a property right one could apply for**); **1986—1993** the wave of long novels (**received account:** writers turned from the short story to the long novel, and in the same period publishing could pay advances, and film and television could buy the rights:** a novel had, for the first time, two departments of income (the taking shape of the three incomes in Chapter 6**)); **1991—1997** the first few rounds of the Mao Dun Literature Prize (**received account:** the long novel obtained a national "certificate of completeness" (**the side effect is that every long novel began to grow so as to look like a prize-winning work (**this chapter's suspicion of "weighty" comes from here**))); **1993—2009** the ban on _Ruined City_, and the lifting of the ban (**received account:** the value of one book was established jointly by two opposite acts of the same institution (**this is the complete life cycle of Chapter 3, "a banned-book list = a list of recommendations" (**it even sold better after the reissue that followed 2009**))); **2000—2012** online communities and translation abroad, pushing side by side (**received account:** a "belated bestseller" of the Wang Xiaobo kind and a "crowning from the outside in" of the Mo Yan kind happened in the same decade (**the difference between the two paths is a difference of distribution systems, not a difference of literature**))); **2012—now** the effect of prizes, and pricing at home (**received account:** in the week of the prize, the complete works go onto the shelves, the series are reset, the passages in the textbooks are rewritten:** canonisation is an emergency restocking along a supply chain (**this lesson's closing:** all four writers write better than this supply chain; remember this point, and only then have you finished this chapter**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of this lesson
**Organising one's reading by prizes** (**a prize is one year's purchasing list:** use it as a catalogue, and the other experiments of the same period get treated as if they had not happened (Chapter 3, the luck of the archive**)); **Reading the local as the national** (**Gaomi, Shangzhou and White Deer Plain are all places that have been written; they are not "China":** one writer's county is not the nature of a country (**this is the only objection this chapter retains to "越是民族的越是世界的" ('The more national, the more it is of the world.') (Our translation.)**)); **Substituting the life for the production period** (**disability, poverty and being sent to the countryside explain the material and the motive; they do not explain the structure (Lesson 2, the mediation document**)); **Taking "cuts and revisions" for the writer's stain** (**cuts and revisions are an act of the institution; one records it in order to measure the boundary of the institution:** to read it as a moral flaw in the author is exactly to let the party truly responsible leave the scene (Chapter 6, the finding of responsibility**).
:::

::: exercise Apply for a piece of ground {level=1}
In 80 characters, write a "specification for a fictional county": **a place name, three products, and one thing that is not to be written in**; the requirement: **that one thing "not to be written" must be such that the reader can infer it**.
::: solution
Marking: **the "not to be written" must be inferred back from the list** (**write three products and do not write grain, and the reader will be left hungry:** this is the principle of a layout in Mo Yan's manner (Chapter 8, the chain of evidence:** silence, too, must have a place**.

::: exercise A ruler for the cuts and revisions {level=2}
Find a work you know to have been cut and revised (either Chinese or Western; film, television and games are all allowed), list **an inventory of the items cut**, then answer: **what the person who made the cuts feared most — the picture, the word, the structure, or the conclusion**?
::: solution
Marking: **the four items must be judged separately** (**mix them together and it is the same as not having measured (**the common conclusion is "what is feared most is the conclusion":** if some work has had only the process and the words cut, that shows the inspecting side admits the conclusion can be safe — **this is one extremely useful measurement of an institution (the contemporary version of Chapter 4, the edition is the ideology**.

::: exercise Short essay: the production period and the weight {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**Inside our respect for the long novel, a great part is respect for the production period: written over a long time, revised a great deal, checked in fine detail, and so we call it weighty; written fast, and in such quantity that it cannot be stopped, and so we call it out of control. But the production period is an arrangement of the body, not a rank of value; what really ought to be asked is something else — whether this novel has a place where its author could clearly have written it more for pleasure, and more fun, and deliberately did not write it that way**". You must call on at least six items: **the outsourcing of territorial rights**, **the mixing of the sources of testimony**, **sensory inflation and the inventory of empirical things left unwritten**, **the ban on _Ruined City_ and its reissue**, **the epic's quota and the measuring of cuts and revisions**, **the four gates of canonisation**; and give a ruling: **whether "deliberately not writing for pleasure" can be the core criterion of a piece of criticism — you must state its fallibility (in what cases it would judge wrongly), and name a novelist whom you take to be one for whom "writing for pleasure is itself the discipline"**.
::: solution
Points for marking: the six mechanisms (**territory, sources, inflation, the banned book, the quota, the four gates**); the ruling (**the column for fallibility is the key:** it will misjudge two kinds of author — **① those whose style is improvisation (**the colloquial, the gag and the running account are his form itself, not a case of his "having held back and not written it"**); ② authors who work from materials (**his pleasure needs checking; slowness is his pleasure**); an argument that passes for "writing for pleasure is the discipline" must cite **the kind of syntax that looks casual and yet sets a limit at every point (such as the short-sentence restraint of the A Cheng 阿城 and Wang Zengqi 汪曾祺 kind, or carnival of the Rabelaisian kind that keeps to a rule) — **the mark depends on whether evidence is given at the level of the sentence (**if the conclusion can be written "**So this chapter, at the end, returns the word 'weighty' to the publisher: it is a line of marketing. The only thing we can really check is one thing: in the direction the author is best at, did he ever stop; the place where he stopped is his morality**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The supply chain, filed: territory (**the county is a title deed**), testimony (**place the myth among the references**), inflation (**the opposite of "adding no adjectives"; both are policies of visibility**), the ban (**a reissue is the best advertisement**), cuts and revisions (**measure out the place where the system hurts**), the four gates (**journals, prizes, textbooks, translation abroad: "important" is assembled from these**). The method in one sentence: **do not ask whether it is important; ask where it deliberately does not write for pleasure**. Next chapter: the wider Chinese-language world — Hong Kong, Taiwan and overseas.
