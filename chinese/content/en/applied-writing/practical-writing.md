---
title: "What practical writing is"
---

The first move of this course is to throw away the three characters 写得好 ('write it well') — **the success of practical writing does not lie in the text: a notice succeeds when the people have come; a request for instructions succeeds when it has been approved; a report succeeds when somebody acts on the strength of it. It is the only kind of writing that will be sent back for an error of format, and that fact exposes its identity on the spot: an institutional act carried out in language**.

## Definition: three elements and one criterion {#def-sanyao}

::: definition Purpose, reader, format {#def-yaosu}
**Purpose** (**determines the content: practical writing has no place for "saying a few words in passing";** one text has only one purpose, and two purposes ought to be split into two documents (**this is exactly the real reason that "a request for instructions must be one document, one matter"; Chapter 4 will set it out in full**). ② **Reader** (**determines the register: writing to a superior, writing to an equal, writing to the party concerned and writing to the public are four languages;** if the reader is wrong, the whole text is wrong, and florid wording cannot bring it back). ③ **Format** (**determines whether it can enter the procedure: title, main addressee, body, signature, date, document number, attachments — miss one, and one link of traceability is gone**). **This lesson's criterion, in one sentence:** a document that does not conform to the format is not "written in a non-standard way"; it is absent from the register of incoming documents — **said in the language of institutions, it does not yet exist**. **This is the source of every difference between this lesson and literary writing:** the entrance to literature is the reader's eye; the entrance to practical writing is a security check (**a small desktop version of Course 1's "the catalogue is the examination"**). **Whoever fixes the format fixes what counts as a document** (**see the item on politics below**).
:::

::: definition Four qualities, and their exchange rate {#def-sitiao}
**Accuracy** (**a figure must have a defined basis, a source and a date; a time must be given to the day; a scope must be given down to the unit;** a numeral that does not state its source is an apology written in advance (**the official-document version of Course 3's "a numerator without a denominator"**). ② **Clarity** (**one sentence, one matter;** a long sentence in an official document is not the fault — a misplaced modifier is: **a modifier is a bumper** (Course 4, argumentative writing), but a modifier that can be attached in two positions is an expenditure waiting to happen (**the typical accident is ambiguity in a numeral attribute:** “三家银行的贷款” — are the three the banks, or the loans? The two readings correspond to two sums (**the structural ambiguity taught in Course 5, modern grammar, is here a fiscal risk**). ③ **Brevity** (**delete noun phrases that carry no information:** any opening of the type “为进一步加强……” ('in order to strengthen … further'), if the quantity of information is zero once it is deleted, is purely a burden on the paying party (**this lesson's law:** length and responsibility are often inversely proportional — **a long document tends to dilute responsibility into the paragraphs; a short document must name a person**). ④ **Courtesy** (**expressions of tone are a table of powers: upward, use “恳请” ('we earnestly request') and “当否，请批示” ('whether this is acceptable, please instruct'); downward, use “特此通知” ('this is to notify') and “请遵照执行” ('please observe and carry out'); between equals, use “请予支持” ('kindly give your support') and “特此函达” ('this is conveyed by letter')** (**to use the wrong tone is not to be impolite; it is to declare a wrong administrative relationship** (**this point will be taken up separately in Chapters 3 and 5**). **A note on the exchange rate:** the four things consume one another inside the same sentence — **seek speed, and accuracy is easily lost; seek completeness, and brevity is easily lost; seek safety, and clarity is easily lost**. **This lesson does not promise all four. It promises only this:** before each piece of writing, first decide which one you are willing to concede, and a record must be left.
:::

::: definition Vagueness is a kind of authorisation {#def-shouquan}
**The received account** (**official documents contain a set of legitimate vague expressions: “原则上” ('in principle'), “视情” ('as the situation requires'), “酌情” ('at discretion'), “适时” ('at a suitable time'), “有条件的” ('where conditions allow')**); **this lesson classifies them by the direction of the document** (**①** **vagueness in an upward document is leaving room** (**a subordinate will not sign a military pledge for something outside their control, and so writes the commitment as “力争” ('will strive to')** (**its real function: to push back the criterion by which a breach is judged**). ② **Vagueness in a downward document is shifting discretionary power** (**the single sentence “结合实际贯彻执行” ('carry it out in the light of actual conditions') hands the cost of setting the standard to the person who carries it out;** **the benefit the superior obtains is that the words apply generally; the price is that practice differs from place to place, and that, when people are called to account, each side keeps its own story** (**this is the linguistic basis of the phenomenon of "documents forwarded level upon level"** (Chapter 4). ③ **Vagueness in a parallel document is leaving a way out** (**in a letter of consultation, “如蒙同意” ('should you kindly agree') and “可否见复” ('might we ask for a reply') push the other party into the seat of decision, while reserving for oneself the plea “未获回复” ('no reply was received')**). **The criterion** (**this lesson requires one question to be put to every vague word:** **who, in future, will define this word?** **If the answer is "we will see when the time comes", then this document has not taken a decision; it has only postponed the decision and changed the person who takes it** (**postponement itself is sometimes the correct administrative decision,** **but it must be written down as a postponement**). **This is the whole of this lesson's moral requirement for vague words: honest vagueness is better than disguised precision** (**“原则上同意” ('agreed in principle') is an authorisation; “原则上同意，具体范围由我处解释” ('agreed in principle; the concrete scope will be interpreted by this office') is a complete authorisation; writing only “同意” ('agreed'), and not writing the scope, is the original of the contract for a future dispute**).
:::

::: definition Predictability is its beauty {#def-mei}
**The received account** (**the language of practical writing is highly given over to set phrases: 承 ('received'), 悉 ('noted'), 拟 ('it is proposed'), 兹 ('hereby'), 特此 ('this is specially to …'), 为荷 ('for which we shall be obliged')**). **This lesson's threefold defence** (**①** **sparing the reader** (**the four characters “特此函达” are a cue of format:** **they tell a busy person that this stretch can be skipped** (**predictability is institutional altruism**). ② **Bearing a legal effect** (**certain set phrases have already been bound, by precedent and by custom, to determinate consequences** (**put "a slightly prettier way of saying it" in their place, and the effect is left hanging** (**the administrative version of Course 3's "rhyme is notarisation":** **fixed wording is a low-cost notarisation**). ③ **A guard against forgery** (**if the set phrases do not come out looking like themselves, the author is unfamiliar with this procedure;** **that crudeness is a useful signal** (**by contrast: literature takes defamiliarisation as an asset** (Course 7, "Style"), and practical writing takes familiarisation as an asset; **the directions of the two virtues are opposite, while their criterion is the same:** **each makes the text save time in its own use**). **This lesson's summary in one sentence:** **the virtue of literature is to make a person unwilling to finish reading; the virtue of practical writing is to make a person not need to read it again.**
:::

::: definition No reader, no verb, no date {#def-sanbai}
**① No reader** (**the common fault is to write to "the leadership" rather than to a specific section that has to do the work;** **the result is that the whole text names no one anywhere**). ② **No verb** (**there are only words, such as “加强” ('strengthen'), “推进” ('push forward') and “提升” ('raise'), which describe a direction and do not describe an action** (**the method of audit:** circle every verb, and press each one with the question "who does it, what do they do, and to which object"; **the verb you cannot answer for is the hollow place in this document** (**this move is the same technique as the "topic-sentence interview" in the course on argumentative writing**). ③ **No date** (**a target with no time limit cannot be assessed;** **“尽快” ('as soon as possible'), with no deadline, is the handiest word of disclaimer in Chinese**). **The minimum audit this lesson gives** (**before any piece of practical writing is sent, one must be able to answer five questions:** **who, by when, do what, on what basis, and checked by whom**). **If one of the five is missing, the document can still be sent, but that one will be answered by somebody else, and the answer is usually to your disadvantage** (**this is the whole of the ethics of practical writing:** **you are not expressing; you are allocating obligations.**
:::

::: example Dismantle the device that dilutes responsibility {#ex-xichai}
Rewrite the passage below into text that can be carried out, list every item you delete, and state whom it originally excused from what:

“为了进一步加强管理，提高认识，各单位要结合实际，认真落实，确保取得实效，力争年底前有明显改观。”

In order to strengthen management further and raise understanding, every unit is to carry this out conscientiously in the light of actual conditions, ensure that real results are obtained, and strive for a marked improvement before the end of the year. (Our translation.)

::: solution
**One version of the rewrite** — **“各处室：9 月 30 日前完成本处室在用设备清查，填写附表并由负责人签字，报后勤处；10 月 20 日前由后勤处汇总抽查，抽查比例不低于三分之一；对未按期报送的，在 11 月办公会上说明原因。**后勤处 8 月 25 日**”** ('To all sections: before 30 September complete a full check of the equipment in use in this section, fill in the attached form and have it signed by the person in charge, and report to the Logistics Office; before 20 October the Logistics Office will collate the returns and spot-check them, the proportion spot-checked being not lower than one third; those who have not submitted by the deadline will explain the reason at the November office meeting. Logistics Office, 25 August.') (Our translation.) (**Set against the original, nine substitutions can be seen** (**① “为了进一步加强管理” ('in order to strengthen management further')** (**deleted; it is a purpose, not content, and it does not specify which document it rests on** (**the responsibility excused: no need to account for the source of the authorisation;** ② **“各单位” ('every unit')** (**changed to “各处室” ('every section') and a list of names attached;** **a generic reference leaves the responsibility with no one to claim it**). ③ **“结合实际” ('in the light of actual conditions')** (**deleted;** **vagueness in a downward document is the shifting of discretionary power** (② above). ④ **“认真落实” ('carry out conscientiously')** (**changed to the checkable actions “清查、填写、签字、报送” ('check, fill in, sign, submit')** (**“落实” ('put into effect') is the type case of having no verb**). ⑤ **“确保取得实效” ('ensure that real results are obtained')** (**changed to “抽查比例不低于三分之一” ('the proportion spot-checked not lower than one third');** **a target with no proportion of checking is not a target; it is a wish**). ⑥ **“力争” ('strive to')** (**deleted;** **the vagueness of an upward document is left for use towards a superior; to use it in a downward document is to set no standard for oneself** (① above). ⑦ **“年底前” ('before the end of the year')** (**changed to two specific dates;** ⑧ **the subject and the addressee were not written** (**the main receiving organ and the signature are supplied;** ⑨ **the basis was not written** (**if there really is a document from above, its title and document number should be stated**). **This lesson's marking** (**find six of the nine, and correctly state "what responsibility was excused", and this chapter is a pass;** **if you can go on to point out that "every item I deleted makes somebody more uncomfortable, and that is exactly why it works", that is an A —** **the degree of civilisation of practical writing shows in the way it makes someone uncomfortable.**
:::

::: intuition Are you writing for the procedure, or for that person {#int-liucheng}
**A real conflict** (**practical writing has two kinds of reader: one is the procedure (the several stages of registration, proposed handling, supervision and filing; they require the essential elements to be complete and the terminology to be standard**); the other is the party concerned (a person who has come to get something done; what he wants is "what should I do, what should I bring, how many days") (**the received account** (**for a long time the first kind of reader has stood alone in power, and so there appeared that sort of notifying text of which it is said that "cadres can read it, the masses cannot";** **in recent years the requirements for the writing of public texts have expressly added one item, "plain and easy to understand"**). **This lesson's analysis** (**①** **this is not a problem of level of skill; it is a problem of incentives** (**the writer's direct responsibility is owed to the procedure (an error of format will be sent back; obscurity of meaning will not;** **so long as nobody complains of being unable to understand, vagueness is free** (**so to put this kind of writing right depends not on training in rhetoric, but on making "complained of as unreadable" into something for which someone can be held responsible** (**this is an assignment in the design of institutions, not an assignment for the Chinese class**). ② **A technique for having both** (**the same text can be divided into two layers:** **the body satisfies the procedure (essential elements complete, terminology standard, the basis cited made clear), with a checklist attached in the manner of "notes for the person who has come to get it done"** (**steps, time limit, materials, telephone number**). **This lesson calls this the "dual-track system":** **it does not lower conformity to the norm; it only shifts a little of the cognitive cost back from the party with the least bargaining power**). ③ **It applies to all eight chapters of this course** (**a letter applying for a job must face at once the human-resources system that screens for keywords and the person who will read your sentences; news must satisfy at once fact-checking and a reading of three minutes;** **every piece of practical writing has two readers, and the writer usually remembers only the one who pays**).
:::

::: history Standardised earlier than literature {#sec-biaozhun}
**Warring States to Qin and Han** (**unearthed official documents show that the forms of registration, copying, dating, signature and postal transmission had long since taken shape; administration by documents came before literary self-consciousness**). **The received account:** **this course places the point in the first lesson so as to break a prejudice once and for all:** **"practical prose is low-spec composition" — the fact is exactly the reverse; what was strictly standardised earliest is precisely this** (**an earlier footnote, here, to Course 1's "everything that is examined is a classic"**). ② **Six Dynasties to Tang** (**one work of literary criticism devotes a chapter to letters and memorials; in the Tang, the documents of commissioned posts and the genre of the judgement became technical requirements of selection for office**). **The received account:** **being able to write official documents was a qualification for holding office, not a form of cultivation**). ③ **Song to Qing** (**sub-statutes, set forms, and the letter manuals of private secretaries; forms of address for documents going up and documents going down, and line elevation (抬格), were prescribed in fine detail**). **Line elevation is format in its extreme shape:** **blank spaces on the page, and the moving of a line, directly indicate rank** (**it proves that format was never decoration, but a printable map of power**). ④ **1912/1920** (**the Republic abolished a number of Qing document types; the general order that schools change to vernacular prose eventually reached official correspondence**). **One change of language altered the whole register of official documents, while document numbers and the system of filing hardly changed:** **inertia at the institutional layer is far greater than at the linguistic layer**). ⑤ **1951/1981/2012** (**three nationwide sets of rules for the handling of official documents; the regulations now in force fix fifteen kinds of official document, and one national standard specifies layout down to the page margins and the type size**). **The received account:** **this is the first time in Chinese history that the layout of an official document was written down as measurable parameters** (**this lesson's verdict:** **measurable means searchable across organs, and searchable means moving administrative memory out of the pile of paper and into an index** (**the linguistic version of Course 8's "infrastructure"**). ⑥ **The last twenty years** (**public texts have been required to be "plain and easy to understand", while new media set every announcement directly before the party concerned**). **The reader who is the procedure, and the party concerned, sit for the first time on the same screen** (**this is the cause of the conflict spoken of in the intuition experiment above, and it is also the assignment which the eight chapters that follow are to deal with one by one**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls in this chapter
**Taking literary polish for a merit** (**adjectives are a liability in practical writing:** **they increase the time of reading and do not increase what can be carried out** (③ above)). **Taking vagueness for room to manoeuvre** (**first ask who will define this word in the future;** **vagueness that nobody defines is a bill left for somebody else** (the authorisation clause above)). **Taking format for mere form** (**to omit one essential element may keep the document from taking effect, or make it impossible to find later** (element ③ above)). **Taking "the reader is a cadre" as a reason the writing need not be smooth** (**the party concerned has a right to understand a document that concerns them, and in recent years this right has been written into the requirements** (intuition ① above): **smoothness is an obligation, not a style**.
:::

::: exercise A check-up on the three elements {level=1}
Take three real pieces of practical writing that you have to hand (a notice, a poster, an announcement or an email, any of these), and for each, in 60 characters, fill in **purpose, reader and format**, and mark the one of the five questions that is missing.

::: solution
Marking: **the missing question must be uniquely determined** (**if you write "the date is missing and the basis is missing too", you must say which question has the more serious consequence:** who, when people are called to account, will take it up and argue from it (the three failures above).
:::

::: exercise Writing in reverse {level=2}
Rewrite a piece of everyday writing you wrote last week (a course notice, a group announcement, a message left for a teacher): delete every phrase that contains no information, and supply **a time limit, the person responsible and the means of checking**, keeping the length to half that of the original.

::: solution
Marking: **more than half must really have been deleted** (**a "rewrite" that keeps twice the length of the original is the same as not having written** (the clauses above on brevity and on the dilution of responsibility).
:::

::: exercise Short essay: the style of writing is the style of government {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing the following. “**一份机关文风的质量，可以用一个可查的指标估出：把它发给一个从未接触该事务的人，看他能否照做。凡是必须打电话问才能用的文件，都在把写作成本转成执行者的时间成本，而这种转嫁不会出现在任何一张预算表上，所以它总是被低估。因此“改进文风”从来不是一场语言运动，而是一次成本重分配：它要求写的人多花二十分钟，好让一百个人每人少花二十分钟——这就是它极少真正发生的原因**”. **The quality of an organ's style of writing can be estimated by one indicator that can be checked: send it to a person who has never come into contact with the matter, and see whether he can do as it says. Every document that cannot be used without telephoning to ask is turning the cost of writing into a cost in the implementer's time, and this shifting appears on no budget sheet, so it is always underestimated. Therefore "improving the style of writing" has never been a movement in language; it is a reallocation of costs: it asks the person who writes to spend twenty minutes more, so that each of a hundred people may spend twenty minutes less — and this is why it so rarely really happens.** (Our translation.) You must draw on at least six items: **the three elements**, **the exchange rate of the four qualities**, **vagueness is authorisation**, **predictability as a virtue**, **the three failures**, **the dual-track system**; and adjudicate: **what bad thing the claim "a document that is hard to understand ought to be a matter for accountability" most easily turns into when it is put into practice** (you must give one concrete form of failure, and design one procedure to prevent it).

::: solution
Marking points: the six mechanisms (**elements, the exchange rate, vagueness, predictability, failure, the dual track**); the form of failure (**an acceptable answer must give a concrete mechanism, not a blanket name such as "formalism" —** candidates: ① putting an upper limit on the number of characters in place of quality, so that modifiers are deleted into ambiguity (**accuracy is eaten by clarity**); ② putting an evaluation that "the masses are satisfied" in place of completeness of the essential elements, so that the document can no longer be retrieved and later cannot be used to call anyone to account; ③ accountability falls on the drafter, while the drafter has no power to change the format or the procedure, with the result that the indicator of writing style becomes a punishment of the youngest (**what the marking looks for is whether this point is made: any assessment in the name of the quality of writing will slide down the gradient of power**). **An acceptable way of answering for the procedure that prevents it: define the indicator as an "executability test"** (**find a person unfamiliar with the matter and, with nobody guiding them, have them carry the business out according to the document, and record the place where they get stuck**) + at the same time keep the hard constraint that the essential elements are complete (**the two are written on the same review sheet; if either falls short, send it back**) + make public the sample of that test and the reasons for the judgement (**auditability — Course 7's "procedural rationality is auditability" is made good here**); if the conclusion can be written "**so the morality of practical writing is very simple: whatever time you could have saved other people by writing clearly, to have written it vaguely is to spend other people's time; the technique of all eight chapters of this course exists only so that this account can be calculated**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
Identity, filed: nature (**an institutional act carried out in language**), format (**a security check at the entrance, not decoration**), qualities (**the four consume one another; a concession must leave a record**), vagueness (**a kind of authorisation, and it must be written who defines it**), set phrases (**familiarisation is an asset, and it can guard against forgery**), failure (**no reader, no verb, no date**). The method, in one sentence: **before sending, read the five questions through once; the one you cannot answer will be answered for you by somebody else**. Next chapter: letters and notes — the smallest official documents between private persons.
:::
