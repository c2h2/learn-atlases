title: "Applications and personal statements"
---

The previous chapter gave an account, to ten million strangers, of what other people had done; this chapter gives an account, to one reader or to two or three, of what you yourself have done — **the whole technique of this chapter can be compressed into one sentence: write what is checkable into the CV, write what is not checkable as a commitment, and never use an adjective in the part that is checkable**.

## Definition: three texts, three strengths of evidence {#def-sanwen}

::: definition The CV, the cover letter and the personal statement {#def-fengong}
**The CV** (**a list that a third party can verify: dates, institutions, posts, quantified results, certificate numbers;** every entry of it assumes that someone can telephone and check) (② **the cover letter** (**one argument of fit: what your problem is, the one thing I have done that comes closest, and what I shall do in the first 30 days after I take up the post;** every entry of it assumes that the other party can at once verify item 3) (③ **the personal statement** (**a narrative of intention and trajectory: why you do this, what you have prepared for it, and what problem you have not yet solved**) **The core criterion of this lesson:** what the side that hires and the side that admits really read is the difference between these three texts — **if the CV says you “主导” ('led'), the cover letter says “参与” ('took part'), and the statement says “我很感兴趣” ('I am very interested'), then what is read is not your ability, but the credibility of what you have said about yourself** (**This is the honesty arithmetic of this chapter:** one fabrication destroys all that is true, because checking is a sampling inspection; to falsify one place is to falsify the whole) (**whereas one frank admission (“这段空白我在照护家人” 'During this gap I was caring for my family.' (Our translation.)) is usually discounted very little, because it raises the credibility of the rest.**
:::

::: definition Endpoints, quantity and reverse order {#def-duandian}
**An acceptable sentence of experience has four things** (**action + object + quantity + result endpoint;** “负责社团招新” ('responsible for recruiting new members to the society') has only the action and the object; “为社团招新设计报名表并把报名转化从 40 份提交提高到 26 份完成（2023 年 9 月，两届数据可查）” 'Designed a registration form for the society's recruitment of new members, and raised the conversion of registrations from 40 submissions to 26 completions (September 2023; the data for two rounds can be checked).' (Our translation.) has all four) **Note that this and the previous chapter's “变化要有两个端点” ('a change must have two endpoints') are the same sentence: writing about oneself and writing about a city use the same set of rules of evidence** (**Three precise rules** (① **Reverse order** (**the most recent thing first;** the other party's budget of attention is reckoned in tens of seconds) (**the one-page principle holds for posts in industry; an academic vita is the opposite, and must be long and complete**) (**The received account: academia uses a vita that lists every achievement; industry uses a one-page CV trimmed to the post**) ② **The verb list** (**“主持、搭建、起草、谈判、复核” ('take charge, set up, draft, negotiate, re-check') are checkable actions; “参与、协助、支持、负责” ('take part, assist, support, be responsible for') are postures that cannot be checked:** their only function is to dilute responsibility) (the previous chapter's “各相关部门” ('the various departments concerned'), recurring at the level of the person) ③ **Leave a hook that can be followed up** (**in every section leave one detail, so that the other party can ask the next sentence;** a CV is not a conclusion, it is an outline: its standard of success is to be questioned, not to be admired) (**This is the sentence this chapter requires you to remember:** a CV that is admired often cannot withstand questioning; a CV that is questioned can generally secure the second interview.
:::

::: definition Keywords, screening-out and privacy {#def-guanjianci}
**The received account** (**many employers do the first screening with searchable systems of forms, and with keywords**) **Three points of this lesson** (① **Keywords are not for pleasing a machine; they remove ambiguity** (**if the post's description writes “数据治理” ('data governance') and you write “管过数据” ('have handled data'), you are gambling on the other party's interpretation;** when the same concept does not line up in the two vocabularies, it is recorded as “不匹配” ('not a match') rather than “不了解” ('not acquainted with it')) (**This is the same thing as the previous chapter's “转载链删掉来源说明” ('the chain of republication deletes the note of the source'):** information is lost among the levels at which it is passed on, and the responsibility falls on the last level) ② **What screens a person out is often the system, not a person** (**hard conditions of the kind “必须由某类学历” ('must be held by a qualification of a certain type') and “年龄以下” ('under a given age') are written into the rules of screening;** the advice of this lesson is: if you do not meet the hard conditions, do not polish the style; go and change the conditions, or change the door) (**This sentence sounds cold; it is in fact the advice that saves the most money**) ③ **A demand for private matters is a transfer of risk** (**a photograph, an address, plans for marriage and children, height and weight: for every further item the recruiting side demands, it places the cost of information on the applicant's side, and lowers the probability that the cost of a mistaken judgement remains on its own side**) (**The received account: the law of this country on labour and employment, and the norms of recent years, clearly oppose discrimination on such grounds as sex and household registration, and the restriction of questions touching marriage and childbearing has also been stressed**) (**The criterion of judgement in this lesson cites not a clause of statute but a piece of common sense:** to demand personal information that has nothing to do with performing the post shows that what the post is selecting is not ability; you may refuse to answer, and you may also, from this, judge how the organisation will in future handle your leave, your sick leave and childbirth) (**Privacy is not a question of sincerity; it is advance notice of the terms of the contract.**
:::

::: definition How to write a gap {#def-kongbai}
**A generally noted phenomenon** (**a gap on a CV is more readily questioned than a low grade-point average**) **This lesson gives three honest ways of writing one** (① **Give it a verb** (**“备考” ('preparing for an examination'), “照护家人” ('caring for one's family'), “接零工维持收入” ('taking casual work in order to keep an income'), “自学并作出三件作品” ('studying on one's own and making three pieces of work')**) (**A gap is not the problem; the lack of a verb is the problem: without a verb, no one can follow it up**) (**The previous chapter's old matter of “把失业写进号” ('writing unemployment into the issue') is of use here:** the way people record their own unemployment decides whether, later, it can be counted as experience) ② **Give it a checkable product** (**a piece of open-source code, three notes, a certificate number;** a gap that has a product is a project; a gap that has no product needs one sentence of explanation) ③ **Do not compress the timeline** (**writing March 2023 as January 2023 in order to cover three months is the most costly operation in this chapter:** it turns one explanation into one statement that is not true, and what a background check finds most easily is precisely dates) (**A suggestion for wording in this lesson** (**write the gap into the CV rather than hide it in silence:** “2023.04—2023.09 待业期间完成××课程并通过××考试，同时面试××家单位未果，主要不足是××” 'From April to September 2023, while out of work, completed the course ×× and passed the examination ××, and at the same time interviewed at ×× organisations without success; the main shortcoming was ××.' (Our translation.)) **——** **The last sentence is the most counter-intuitive weapon in this section, and also the most effective:** it hands you the right of judgement, for you to exercise once first, and interviewers usually give one step more latitude to “知道自己在挑什么的人” ('a person who knows what he himself is picking out') (**This is that point in signalling theory: a merit that anyone can write down for nothing is not a merit;** a shortcoming that one admits oneself is, on the contrary, expensive, because it is hard to forge.
:::

::: definition The three paragraphs of a cover letter {#def-sanduan}
**The first paragraph: I understand your problem** (**use one checkable observation to show why you are applying to this one:** “你们的帮助文档里，××问题在三个版本中重复出现” 'In your help documents, the problem ×× appears again across three versions.' (Our translation.)) **——** **it is stronger than any “久仰” ('I have long admired you'), because it proves on the spot that you are already at work** (② **The second paragraph: the one thing I have done that comes closest** (**one story, containing two endpoints and one detail of a failure;** the detail of a failure is the source of credibility) (**the previous chapter's rule “反方不可省” ('the opposing side must not be omitted'), used upon oneself**) ③ **The third paragraph: what I shall do in the first 30 days after taking up the post** (**one concrete action, deliverable within two weeks, and capable of being accepted**) (**this is the job-application version of Chapter 6's “没有验收方式的计划不是计划” ('a plan with no means of acceptance is not a plan')**) **This lesson's definition of the cover letter:** its only function is to lower the other party's cost of imagining — **so that he need not imagine what you look like when you come to work; he has already seen one thing that has been finished** (**The negative list:** “我什么都能学” 'I can learn anything.' (Our translation.) is a sentence of buying on credit; “我热爱贵单位” 'I love your organisation.' (Our translation.) is an adjective with no endpoint; “期望薪资面议” ('expected salary to be discussed face to face') leaves the cost of negotiation to the other party) (**The received account: most norms of recruitment require the candidate to state his qualifications as they are; a false statement may constitute grounds for terminating the contract**) (**This point is not a threat; it is good news:** it brings the unit in which the whole trade quotes a price back onto the same line — things that can be checked.
:::

::: definition The personal statement: one unfinished problem {#def-weiwan}
**The received account** (**an application document is usually expected to answer why one chose this field, what preparation one has done, and what one wants to do in future**) **This lesson's reordering** (① **Write the "why" as a scene of turning** (**not “我从小就喜欢” ('I have liked it since I was small'), but one concrete puzzlement on a certain day:** it must be able to be followed up with a second sentence) ② **Write the "preparation" as a list that can be set out item by item** (**courses, tools, languages, links to work;** a list is cheaper than expression of feeling, and it makes the third paragraph credible) ③ **Write the "future" as one unfinished problem** (**This lesson's definition:** the core of a good statement is not an answer, but a concrete problem for which you already have some of the tools, but which you have not yet solved) (**It has three functions:** it proves that your interest has a structure; it lets a supervisor see where you can be taught; it makes your statement one that can be remembered among twenty similar documents) (**because it is an opening on which someone else can take up what has been said**) **A note on the trap of style** (**a personal statement is the easiest thing to write as expression of feeling, because expression of feeling costs the least effort:** the mark of expression of feeling is that it takes the responsibility onto oneself and yet has no checkable object; so the whole advice of this section is: write your statement as one timeline plus one unfinished problem, and do not allow an adjective with no endpoint to appear in between) (On ghostwriting and polishing: the received account: most institutions allow revision at the level of language, and do not accept another person writing the substance in one's place; generative tools are redrawing this line) (This lesson's position is a narrow one: every sentence that someone else has written for you, and that you cannot say, will become the first question in the interview on which marks are lost — this is not moralising; it is a purely empirical observation.
:::

::: example Turning “负责” into endpoints {#ex-duanzhao}
Three common sentences from CVs: **① “负责学院公众号运营” ('responsible for running the college public account'); ② “参与一项省级课题研究” ('took part in one provincial-level research project'); ③ “组织过一次大型活动” 'Once organised a large event.' (Our translation.)**. Rewrite them as result sentences that carry endpoints, and judge one by one: **does the rewriting cross the line into exaggeration? Where is the boundary?**

::: solution
**Examples of rewriting** — **① “2023.09—2024.06 独立运营学院公众号，发布 118 篇，平均阅读量由 210 增至 940；单篇最高 1.2 万；6 月起建立选题清单并沿用”** 'From September 2023 to June 2024, independently ran the college public account; published 118 pieces; average reads rose from 210 to 940; the highest single piece was 12,000; from June, a list of topics was set up and kept in use.' (Our translation.); **② “参与省级课题××（编号可查），负责 216 份问卷的发放与清洗，独立完成第 3 章文献综述初稿”** 'Took part in the provincial-level project ×× (the number can be checked); responsible for distributing and cleaning 216 questionnaires; independently completed a first draft of the literature review in Chapter 3.' (Our translation.); **③ “作为 5 人筹备组成员负责签到与动线，参会 320 人，签到耗时由每人 40 秒降至 12 秒（现场记录表）”** 'As a member of a preparatory group of five, responsible for sign-in and the route of movement; 320 people attended; the time taken to sign in fell from 40 seconds per person to 12 seconds (on-site record sheet).' (Our translation.) (**Three boundaries between what is exaggerated and what is not** — **first, the verb and the role must be honest:** “参与” ('took part') cannot be written as “主持” ('took charge'); “负责其中一部分” ('responsible for one part of it') cannot be written as “完成” ('completed') (**this is the commonest crossing of the line on a CV, because in Chinese the two differ by only a word**); second, a figure must be a figure of which you hold a record (**for “平均阅读量” ('average number of reads') you must be able to show a screenshot of the back end; do not write a figure for which you cannot produce the proof**); third, **the attribution must not be taken for oneself alone** (“由我提升了” ('it was I who raised it') is almost always an exaggeration where several people worked together; **writing “期间由 210 增至 940，我的改动是×× 与××” 'Over the period it rose from 210 to 940; my changes were ×× and ××.' (Our translation.) meets both needs: it keeps the contribution, and it also acknowledges the other causes**) (**Marking** (one who can write the third boundary scores an A for this chapter — it is at the same time that line of the third course and of the fourth course here: pressing many causes into one person is a laziness of narrative, and on a CV it is a laziness that can be interrupted.
:::
:::

::: intuition Three versions of a self-introduction, and one fact sheet {#int-shishi}
**Why “介绍一下你自己” 'Introduce yourself.' (Our translation.) is so hard** (**because it requires you, within two minutes, to play two roles at once: the person who did these things in the past, and the person whom the other party is about to use;** these two are not the same text) (**This lesson's way of doing it** (① **Prepare three spoken scripts** (**a one-minute version (for the opening of the interview**), a three-minute version (for “讲讲你的经历” 'Talk about your experience.' (Our translation.)**), and a version of “为什么你不适合这个岗位” 'Why you are not suited to this post.' (Our translation.)) (**the third is the most useful: it means that, when you are on the defensive, you need not invent on the spot**) ② The three share one fact sheet** (**ten figures, five dates, three links to work, written on a single sheet of paper;** whatever is said on any occasion is taken from this sheet) (**this is the only technique for preventing contradiction with oneself, and it also answers the criterion of difference at the opening of this chapter: if the three texts agree with one another, credibility comes from the agreement, and not from fineness of writing**) ③ **After every interview, update the fact sheet** (**a question that floors you is the row you lack;** after three or four rounds, this sheet will be longer than your CV — **and it is at the same time your best record of work: a person's ability to prove what he has done is the whole of his asset in the labour market**) (**The close of this chapter:** one writes a CV not in order to be hired, but in order that one may at any time answer accurately the question “你做过什么” 'What have you done?' (Our translation.) — **being hired is only its by-product.**
:::

::: history Who keeps the record about you {#sec-dang}
The nineteenth century (employment agencies appeared, and so did newspaper columns of recruitment; letters of recommendation were for a long time the main form of credit) (The received account: the passage from “有人替你担保” 'Someone stands guarantor for you.' (Our translation.) to “有一张你自填的表” 'There is a form you fill in yourself.' (Our translation.) is one relocation of credit: it exchanges the guarantor's responsibility for a statement of your own) (② The middle of the twentieth century (this country established the system of personnel files for cadres and for staff and workers; personal records of career, appraisals, and rewards and penalties were kept by the work unit and the organisation department) (The received account: this lesson's neutral analysis: the file is a CV kept by a third party, which the person himself may not read; it supplies both credit and constraint, and it turns “换单位” ('changing one's work unit') into one migration of documents) (to understand today's background checks, certificates of leaving a post, and records of social insurance, one must first understand this layer of institutional legacy) ③ The 1980s–1990s (talent markets, and the movement of labour, made the self-completed CV standard equipment; at the same time “招聘会上的一沓简历” ('a stack of CVs at a recruitment fair') became a sight of the city) (The received account) ④ The 2000s (platforms for recruitment on the network standardised the CV as a form plus keywords; items such as a photograph, height and weight were for a time in wide use) (The received account: this lesson's observation: when retrieval replaced conversation, style degenerated towards fields) (the version, on the person, of Chapter 9's “分发格式改变内容” ('the format of distribution changes the content')) ⑤ The 2010s (online vitae, portfolios and code made public; “可核” ('checkable') for the first time partly replaced “可信” ('credible')) (The received account) ⑥ The 2020s (generative tools entered from both directions: on one side they write CVs and statements, on the other they screen CVs and arrange the first interview) (The received account) (The conclusion of this chapter: when both sides use a model to generate and to screen, the text degenerates into a protocol of each side guessing at the other; the only two things that are still firm are detail that can be followed up, and work that can be opened — so the whole advice of this chapter is in fact only one sentence: go and do a few things that you can write clearly, and that you can also produce.
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of this chapter
**Exaggerating the role** (**the gap between “主持” ('take charge') and “参与” ('take part') is the most costly point in this chapter (example ① above**)); **listing with no endpoint** (**“负责多项工作” ('responsible for many pieces of work') is not experience; it is a way of dodging (the four things of an endpoint, above**)); **treating privacy as sincerity** (**volunteering an account of marriage and childbearing, and of a housing loan, will not be exchanged for trust; it will only be exchanged for a price fixed with certainty (privacy ③ above**)); **one CV sent to every post** (**it pushes the work of matching onto the other party's imagination, and screening-out by keywords will not imagine on your behalf (keywords ① above):** **the ten minutes spent revising the CV are the ten minutes with the highest rate of return in this chapter.**
:::

::: exercise Build a fact sheet {level=1}
For yourself, list ten figures, five dates, and three pieces of work or records that can be opened; then write a one-minute self-introduction, taking material only from this sheet.

::: solution
Marking: **no fact from outside the sheet may appear in the spoken script** (**if one appears, it shows that your self-introduction and your record do not agree, and this is precisely the source of being floored at interview (fact sheet ② above).**
:::

::: exercise Write a gap {level=2}
For an empty interval of your own, or of a fellow student's (any one of: an internship not obtained, preparation for an examination, or family reasons), write 80 characters. It must contain one verb, and one checkable product or explanation.

::: solution
Marking: **dates must not be compressed or altered** (**and it must contain the sentence “当时的不足是××” 'The shortcoming at the time was ××.' (Our translation.);** if that sentence is absent, the piece is treated as still not having admitted it (gap ③ above).

::: exercise Short essay: the information economics of honesty {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing “**求职文书是典型的信号市场：人人都能免费说“我有责任心”，于是这句话的价格是零。真正昂贵的信号只有一个特征——它难以伪造，因此通常也是痛苦的：可被第三方核实的数字、可以打开的作品、愿意写出的失败、以及主动交出的不利信息。诚实在这里不是一种美德，而是一种占优策略，因为你无法记住自己说过哪几个版本**” ('Application documents are a typical signalling market: everyone can say for nothing “我有责任心” ('I have a sense of responsibility'), and so the price of that sentence is zero. A truly expensive signal has only one feature — it is hard to forge, and for that reason it is usually also painful: figures that a third party can verify, work that can be opened, failures one is willing to write down, and unfavourable information that one hands over of one's own accord. Honesty here is not a virtue, but a dominant strategy, because you cannot remember which of the versions you have spoken.' (Our translation.)). You must draw on at least six items: **the division of work among the three texts**, **the four things of an endpoint**, **keywords and screening-out**, **privacy as a transfer of risk**, **the gap**, **the unfinished problem**; and you must rule on **whether the signalling market will fail because of generative tools (when everyone can produce the appearance of an expensive signal — a portfolio that looks real, handsome figures that can be verified, a narrative of failure polished by a model — what is left to you that is hard to forge**).

::: solution
Points for marks: the six mechanisms (**the division of work, endpoints, keywords, privacy, the gap, the unfinished problem**); the ruling (**an answer that passes cannot stop at a tautology of the kind “真本事最重要” 'Real ability matters most.' (Our translation.); it must offer one candidate that is still hard to forge, and explain in what way that candidate cannot be produced by proxy —** the candidates: ① **doing one small thing on the spot** (**at the interview itself, revise one passage of material, and on the spot ask three questions: it cannot be forged beforehand, because it happens at this moment**); ② **a public record that can be traced over a long period** (**work continuous over several years, commits of code, publication, a record of having been cited by others: the cost lies in time, and time cannot be accelerated**); ③ **a third layer of detail that can be followed up** (**a model can give an answer at the second layer, but the third layer requires one to have been there: what that person said at the time, what you changed, and how the result came to be known;** this is the technical meaning of the capacity to “被追问” ('be questioned'), which this chapter stresses again and again**); in addition one should acknowledge the other face of the inflation of signals (**when the appearance can be forged, the recruiting side will turn to new expensive signals — a written test on the spot, a probationary period, an internal recommendation; that is, an “信号军备竞赛” ('an arms race of signals'); this judgement keeps the essay from being naive); if the conclusion can write “所以诚实在这个市场里的位置是：它不是道德要求而是成本控制——说真话的人不需要维护多个版本，因而能把全部精力放在让数字变大上；而说谎的人要永远背着那张事实表的另一份副本” ('So the place of honesty in this market is this: it is not a moral requirement but a control of costs — a person who tells the truth does not need to maintain several versions, and can therefore put all his energy into making the figures larger; whereas a person who lies must for ever carry on his back another copy of that fact sheet.' (Our translation.)), that is an A.

::: summary
Writing oneself into documents: the three texts (**what is read is the difference**), the sentence of experience (**action + object + quantity + endpoint**), keywords (**removing ambiguity rather than pleasing a machine**), privacy (**one demand is one term of a contract**), the gap (**give it a verb**), the statement (**one unfinished problem costs the least effort and has the most force**), the fact sheet (**your real asset is that you can be questioned**). The method in one sentence: **first go and do a few things that you can write clearly, and that you can also produce**.
