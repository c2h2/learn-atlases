title: "News writing"
---

The documents of the previous chapter face a reader who can question you; the documents of this chapter face ten million readers who cannot question you — **news writing is the only kind of practical writing that writes "verifiability" into the genre itself: it puts the most important words in the first paragraph, marks the source inside the sentence, and treats a correction as part of the body**.

## Definition: the inverted pyramid {#def-daojinzita}

::: definition A structure written to be cut {#def-jiegou}
**The prevailing account** (通说, the view generally received). Facts are arranged in descending order of importance: the first paragraph gives the core facts, and what follows supplies background and detail layer by layer. There is more than one explanation of its origin, including the telegram charged by the character and the breaking of the line, and scholarship does not agree on the cause. **This lesson's explanation.** ① **It was a revolution in order.** Since oral narrative, every story has put the most important thing last. **The inverted pyramid was the first to put the conclusion at the beginning, and to admit in public that the rest need not be read.** What it sacrifices is suspense and pace; what it receives in exchange is that it can be cut short. ② **Its first reader is the layout editor.** Before the deadline the page is short by a third, and the editor deletes from the tail. **Only copy that can be deleted in this way is a competent news item** (消息). It is the extreme of what page 1 of this chapter calls a "two-reader style": **the first paragraph is for the reader, the tail is for the editor.** ③ **Its cost is equally concrete.** Who is to judge importance is a decision left to the writer. **And the writer's judgement of importance often comes from the interviewee's line** (口径, the wording they mean to have repeated). This is the most structural point in journalism: it has no independent source of "importance" unless it has a second set of information channels of its own. **This lesson's self-check:** if the importance of a piece of copy comes entirely from the interviewee's agenda, then it is closer to the summary of the previous chapter than to news.
:::

::: definition The lead, the five Ws and the headline {#def-yaoyu}
**The lead** (导语). The first paragraph of a hard-news story answers who, what, when and where, and the second or third paragraph answers why and who said it. **What the usual textbook calls "the five Ws and one H" is in fact a checklist of verifiability: among the six cells, the fewer that stand empty, the lower the cost of checking the copy.** ② **Two points about figures.** Wherever there is a figure, give a base or a point of reference; wherever there is a proportion, give the absolute quantity (the counterpart, in news, of the three distortions in the previous chapter). **“增长 46%” ('an increase of 46%') is worse in news than in a summary, because the reader will not go back and find the base.** ③ **The headline.** A fact, plus a verb, plus a specific object. **Do not write a judgement into the headline: the extreme words “最、首、唯一、第一” ('most', 'first', 'only', 'number one') must have two independent sources. This is a requirement shared by the working rules of most news organisations** (the prevailing account). **The prevailing account: a compound headline sets a kicker (引题) and a deck (副题) in addition, carrying background and supplementary fact respectively.** **This lesson's test:** reading the headline, one ought to be able to retell the fact; if reading the headline one can retell only an emotion, that is a topic for Chapter 9. **One note:** a news item does not write “笔者” ('the present writer'), does not write “我们感到” ('we feel'), and does not let an adjective appear in a paragraph of fact. **The adjective is not banned; it must be put inside a quotation, so that the person who says it signs it.** **This is the most important technique of anonymity in news writing: not the absence of a view, but giving the view a source.**
:::

::: definition Three functions of “某负责人表示” {#def-sanzhong}
**The prevailing account.** The working rules of the major news organisations require that the source of a report be stated, and require that a claim which cannot be verified be marked with the character of its source. **This lesson sorts the usual formulae into three grades.** ① **A named direct quotation.** **The strongest.** It hands the reader a sentence together with the person who said it. ② **Anonymity by post.** “有关负责人表示” ('a relevant person in charge said'), “相关通知明确” ('the relevant notice makes clear'): it does not say who, but it names a post that can be traced back. **Its functions are often three: an authorised release, testing a line, and leaving oneself a way out.** **Note the third:** when an organisation is preparing to change what it says, it first sends an unnamed person out to utter one sentence. **The reader reads copy of this kind as a signal, not as a fact —** and this is a legitimate way of reading, and it is what training in news really teaches. ③ **Pure anonymity.** “知情人士称” ('a person in the know said'), “有消息称” ('there are reports that'): it shifts the part that cannot be verified onto the reader's judgement, and is therefore a liability rather than an asset. **The prevailing account: the custom of the trade usually requires that anonymity meet three conditions — the information is important and touches the public interest, it cannot be obtained through open channels, and the source is credible and has passed approval.** **This lesson's requirement for how it is written:** anonymity, too, must be able to be checked: **at least state the category of identity, the nature of the knowledge (handled it directly, or heard it at second hand), and why the speaker is anonymous.** “据一位直接经手该项审批的部门工作人员说，本报因采访需要对其身份予以隐藏” ('According to a member of staff in the department who handled this approval directly, this paper has concealed that person's identity because the interview required it.') (Our translation.) is twenty characters longer than “知情人士称”, yet it upgrades the remark from hearsay to evidence. **This is the whole secret of the chapter:** news does not hold by the word “客观” ('objective'); it holds by detail that can be checked.
:::

::: definition The date test {#def-riqi}
**One criterion this lesson gives** (**change the date on the copy to yesterday: does it still hold?**). **Three answers.** ① It does not hold, because inside it there is a specific point in time and a change that has just happened. **This is news.** ② It still holds. **It is material, a summary, or publicity copy.** Its news peg is an internal calendar: a meeting, an anniversary, a milestone in a piece of work. **The prevailing account: copy of this kind is often called meeting news or a report on work, and its function is mostly to leave a record and to convey a message.** ③ It half holds. **The change is small but can be checked, and it needs an explanatory paragraph of “为什么现在” ('why now').** If that paragraph is badly written, the copy falls back into the second kind. **From this the lesson draws the line between publicity and news:** the peg of publicity copy is the organisation's calendar; the peg of news is a change the public can perceive. **This is not a judgement of value but a judgement of time —** and it can be tested directly on the copy. **This also explains why meeting news all reads the same:** its readers are the people who attended and their superiors, and the people reported on are the readers. **This kind of writing does not need new information; it needs accuracy and etiquette.** **That "two-track system" of the previous chapter appears here in reverse:** if copy of this kind is to become news, it must be rewritten — find, inside the meeting, the change that can be perceived, and put the rest in the second paragraph.
:::

::: definition The scene is the feature's figure {#def-changjing}
**The prevailing account.** A feature (特稿) or an in-depth report often opens with a specific person or scene and then enters the subject; the middle is unfolded through material, statements from several parties, and background; the last paragraph returns to the person or scene of the opening. **This lesson's four disciplines.** ① **Do not composite.** **To give one person the words of three, or to make one meeting out of two, is fabrication, not cutting.** This is the hardest rule in the norms of the trade (the prevailing account). ② **Detail carries the argument.** “他的办公桌上有 17 份待批的材料” ('On his desk there are 17 documents waiting for approval.') (Our translation.) is forceful because it can be checked; one detail that can be checked is more persuasive than a paragraph of adjectives. **This lesson's definition:** the evidence of a news item is a figure, and the evidence of a feature is detail; **both must be able to be checked, or both are only rhetoric.** ③ **Where the adjective sits.** **A feature allows adjectives, but it requires that the adjective hang on an observation that can be re-checked:** first the observation that can be checked, then one word. **Written the other way round, it is literature.** **This sentence is the border between this chapter and the whole course: the line between practical writing and literature does not lie in how much rhetoric there is, but in whether the rhetoric has a warrant.** ④ **Return to the opening.** **For the last paragraph to return to the person of the opening is an honest device of structure:** it forces the writer to say what change came over that person in the course of this month. **If the change cannot be written, the subject of the feature has nothing to do with the person.** **This is a requirement of structure, and also a requirement of choosing the material.**
:::

::: definition Corrections, apologies and retractions {#def-gengzheng}
**The prevailing account.** Codes of professional ethics and the rules of institutions require that a correction be prompt and placed where it is prominent, and that an error of fact be distinguished from a mishandling in editing. **This lesson's four points.** ① **A correction is not a stain; it is an asset.** **It is the only thing in this trade that can prove in public that one does check.** **The previous chapter says** “更正一封、留一份新版本，是文书的正道” ('Correct the one letter, and keep a new version: that is the proper way of documents.') (Our translation.) **News makes that sentence into an institution.** ② **A correction must write out the original sentence.** **If one writes only “有误，应为” ('in error; it should read') and does not quote the original sentence, the reader cannot judge what kind of error it was.** A correction that hides the original sentence is a correction written for oneself. ③ **A retraction is the most expensive act.** **It strips every chain of republication of its warrant.** **The prevailing account: most rules advise that, unless a serious falsehood or a legal risk is involved, a silent deletion is not to be put in place of a correction.** “悄悄删掉” ('quietly delete it') was criticised in Chapter 2, and here it is the same point: **rewrite history on one side only, and you will certainly be required to produce the versions.** ④ **Republication must be traced to its source.** **The prevailing account: the rules on republication require that the source be named, and that the original sense of the original headline not be distorted.** **This lesson's account of the mechanism:** the longer the chain of republication, the further the proportion of fact to feeling tips towards feeling, because each republication deletes a stretch of the note that states the source. **This curve is another demonstration of Chapter 9's "the format of distribution changes the content".**
:::

::: example Measuring a lead with three rulers {#ex-lingdu}
“为深入推进××工作，昨日下午，我市召开专题会议，会议强调要提高站位、压实责任，确保各项任务落地见效。” ('In order to carry the work of ×× further, yesterday afternoon our city held a special meeting, and the meeting stressed that standpoints were to be raised, responsibility pressed home, and every task put into effect with a result to show.') (Our translation.) Complete three tasks: **① do the date test; ② point out which of the Ws it lacks; ③ suppose the meeting really did contain one change that can be checked (the pilot's scope extended from 2 districts to 8, and one new item was added free of charge), and write a competent lead**.

::: solution
**① The date test** — **change “昨日下午” ('yesterday afternoon') to the day before yesterday, and the whole text still holds:** there is no new change that can be perceived. **Ruling: material, not news.** Its function is to leave a record of the meeting. ② What is missing — **who (**the full name of the organising body is not written**), what (**only a noun of the action, and no item of business**), where (**“我市” ('our city'), and no extent**), why (**no background, and no previous state**), who said it (**“会议强调” ('the meeting stressed') is the weakest sort inside the third grade of this chapter: the meeting is used as the quotation, and no one signs**). **③ The rewrite** — “本市 8 日将××试点范围由 2 个区扩大到 8 个区，新增的 3 类事项免收办理费用，覆盖约 42 万常住居民；此前该试点已运行 14 个月。市××局介绍，扩围依据是本月印发的××方案（×政办发〔2024〕12 号）。” ('On the 8th this city will extend the ×× pilot from 2 districts to 8; the three newly added categories of business will be handled free of charge, covering about 420,000 permanent residents; the pilot has already been running for 14 months. The municipal ×× bureau states that the extension rests on the ×× plan issued this month (×政办发〔2024〕12 号).') (Our translation.) **The six cells are filled, there is one absolute quantity and one document number that can be looked up, and the quotation lands on a named body.** Words of the “提高站位” ('raise our standpoint') kind are all deleted, because they cannot be checked. **Marking.** **If the rewrite still does not contain “从什么到什么” ('from what to what'), it is a fail for this chapter —** a change must be defined by two endpoints, and this is the only difference, at the level of syntax, between news and publicity. **This sentence can be taken away and used in every document you are able to write.**
:::
:::

::: intuition Why meeting news all reads the same {#int-huiyi}
**A structural explanation, not an explanation by ability.** The authors of copy of this kind are usually an organisation's publicity staff, and what they are assessed on is "the number of times they were reported", not "whether readers want to know". The style is therefore a product of the incentive. **Three corollaries.** ① **Its reader has already been assigned.** Superiors, those who attended, and people in the same system. What these three kinds of person need is to have been written about, not new information. ② **The system of the shared release makes homogeneity economical.** One 通稿 (a shared release: one text for many outlets to use) supplied to several outlets is the cheapest production of news. This lesson does not condemn it, and only marks its consequence: **the shared release carries the facts, and local news carries the judgement of importance; when the latter disappears, the reader is left with nothing but the shared release, and so the front pages of the whole city are the same.** This sentence held in the age of print; in the age of algorithmic distribution it is magnified into something else. **See Chapter 9.** ③ **A self-rescue that can be taught.** Someone in a publicity post can also write news, so long as in each piece they can find one of these: the two endpoints of a change, a number of people affected, a document number that can be looked up. Find one of the three, and the copy turns from material into a news item. **The only advice this chapter gives that post:** replace “领导高度重视” ('the leadership attaches great importance') with “本次会议决定了一件什么以前不能做的事” ('what this meeting decided that could not be done before'). The latter is harder to write, but someone will quote it tomorrow morning. **Being quoted is a reward the style pays, and it is much better than the volume of copy filed.**
:::

::: history From the news agency to the label {#sec-tongxunshe}
**1840s—1860s.** The modern news agency took shape on the telegraph lines of Europe and America; charging by the character, and a uniform format for supplied copy, shaped a concise style. **The prevailing account:** this lesson stresses that the direction of the cause is not “记者变懒” ('reporters grew lazy') but “渠道按字收钱” ('the channel takes payment by the character'). The previous chapter's “按什么单位卖决定文体” ('the unit you sell by decides the style') is realised for the first time in journalism. ② **The early twentieth century.** "Objectivity" gradually became a professional norm; related research often links it to the commercial interest of the mass-circulation newspaper: only by not taking a side can one sell to every reader. **The prevailing account.** **This lesson reads it as a technical invention:** moving the judgement out of the sentence and into the label of the source is journalism's engineering solution to the difficulty “我们无法裁定谁对” ('we cannot adjudicate who is right'). ③ **The 1930s—1940s.** Wartime publicity and broadcasting made the relation between “消息” ('the news item') and “动员” ('mobilisation') a central question; after the war, professional codes and bodies of self-regulation appeared in the various countries. **The prevailing account.** ④ **The 1980s.** The reform of news practice in China introduced concepts such as "information" and "readers' needs"; in-depth reporting, and writing in the manner of the investigation report, arose. **The prevailing account:** this chapter sets it beside the "investigation" tradition of the previous chapter: **both assume that the writer should obtain material that can be checked before making a judgement.** ⑤ **The 1990s—2010s.** Websites, portals and algorithmic distribution; the headline turned from a sentence of fact into a sentence of suspense; the correction moved from the ear of the front page (报眼) to the bottom of the page. **The prevailing account:** one quantitative observation of this lesson: **when republication and push distribution become the main source of traffic, the "first paragraph" is given to the machine to read, and the "headline" is given to feeling to read —** the first structural deformation of the inverted pyramid. ⑥ **The 2010s to the present.** Fact-checking bodies, labels on platforms, techniques for tracing pictures and video back to a source, and text generated by models entering the chain of supplied copy. **The prevailing account.** **The close of this chapter:** every technique in this chapter can be reduced to one sentence — let every sentence be such that it can be questioned back to a person or a document. **Copy written by a machine does not change that sentence; it only makes it more necessary that one can question back, because when there is no one to ask, the format becomes the only testimony.**
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls in this chapter
**Treating the shared release as news** (its peg is an internal calendar (the date test above)); **extreme words with no two sources** (“最、首、唯一” ('most', 'first', 'only') are the three words that cost the most to check (the headline above)); **polishing a quotation** (smoothing spoken words into order is rewriting; joining two people's words into one sentence is fabrication (① of the feature, above)); **taking “据网友爆料” ('according to a netizen's exposé') as a source** (it cuts the chain of sources off at a collective that does not exist; **the prevailing norms require that republication be traced to its source (④ on republication, above):** in this chapter that sentence equals “无来源” ('no source').
:::

::: exercise Find the two endpoints {level=1}
From today's local news, pick three reports of meetings or events. For each, in 50 characters, state: **what the change that can be checked is, and what its two endpoints, the one before and the one after, are**; if you cannot find them, write which kind of writing it is disguised as.

::: solution
Marking: **an endpoint must be a quantity or an item of business that can be looked up** (**“加强了” ('has been strengthened') is not an endpoint;** if they cannot be found, mark the piece as material (date test ②, above).
:::

::: exercise Short essay: objectivity is a technique {level=3}
Write six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**objectivity has never meant that the reporter has no position; it means a whole set of procedures by which a person can go round the reporter's position: state the source, mark the line, keep the original words, give the other side, publish the correction. All its force comes from a modest design — moving the centre of gravity of judgement from "believe me" to "you can check". And precisely because it depends so much on procedure, it is the easiest thing to counterfeit: a piece of copy whose format is complete, and every one of whose sources is the same interested party, can be entirely compliant on every single technique and entirely false as a whole**". You must draw on at least six items: **the two readers of the inverted pyramid**, **the cost of checking the five cells**, **the three grades of source**, **the date test**, **detail carries the argument**, **correction and tracing a republication to its source**; and you must decide: **whether the common requirement of "diverse sources" is enough to resist the counterfeit described above — you must give a rule stronger than "diversity", and state why it is resisted in practice**.

::: solution
Points for the mark: the six mechanisms (**two readers, the cells, the three grades, the date, detail, tracing to source**); the stronger rule (**an answer that passes must point to "the independence of sources" rather than to their number —** formulations that would qualify: ① **the same fact must be traceable to two chains of evidence, neither of which is held by the other** (**one document and one observation on the spot, rather than two outlets both quoting the same shared release**); ② **state the interest of every source** (**who gains by this claim**); ③ **material that conflicts with the main source must not be omitted in at least one place** (**that is, the rule "the other side may not be omitted"**); the reason for the resistance (**it must be interest, and not "a failure to understand":** checking independence takes time, offends the party that supplies the copy, and makes the organisation lose the space on the page that the shared release brings; **on a post assessed by the volume of copy filed, diverse sources are a negative output** (**this has the same structure as the previous chapter's clause on indicators**); if the conclusion can be written "**so objectivity is not a state of mind but a cost: whoever is willing to pay two further hours for one cross-check possesses it; otherwise what we possess is only a distortion whose format is correct, and it is harder to refute than publicity stated outright, because every cell of it has been filled in correctly**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
Procedures archived: the inverted pyramid (**a structure written to be cut**), the five cells (**the fewer the empty cells, the cheaper the check**), the source (**anonymity, too, must be able to be checked**), the date test (**a change must have two endpoints**), the feature (**detail carries the argument; to composite is to fabricate**), the correction (**the only thing that can prove in public that you do check**). The method in one sentence: **each time you write a sentence, ask whom it can be traced to; if it cannot be traced, delete it, or mark it as a claim**.
