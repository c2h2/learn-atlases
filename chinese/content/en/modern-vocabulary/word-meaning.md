title: "Word meaning"
---

In a dictionary 打 has some twenty senses, 上 a dozen or more, and 了 only two — yet each of those two is highly frequent. Word meaning is not a label stuck onto a word, but a **map with a centre, with paths of extension, with habits of collocation, and with affective colouring**. This lesson teaches how to draw that map.

## Two layers and two faces of word meaning {#sec-liangceng}

::: definition Lexical meaning and grammatical meaning {#def-clyy}
**Lexical meaning** (referential meaning plus sense) is the way a word claims a slice of the world: 春 claims a season, 跑步 claims an action. **Grammatical meaning** is the way a word takes on a role in combination: the "individual / container" sense of a classifier (一**张**纸 as against 一**打**纸), the actualisation and completion of 了 (下雨**了**), the plural of -们. 春 has grammatical meaning too (the syntactic behaviour of a noun of time); it is merely thin. Outside **conceptual meaning** (the conceptual core) there is also **colouring**: affect (成果, 后果 and 结论 are close in their cores, yet poles apart in commendation and derogation), register (父亲, 爸爸, 老爷子), image (the distinct imagistic feel and regional colouring of 土豆, 马铃薯 and 洋芋), and honorific and humble use (犬子, 令郎).
:::

::: theorem Senses are not a list; they are a map {#rule-yixiang}
The senses of a polysemous word extend around a **central sense** (the prototype) along two routes. **Radial**: the original meaning is the centre; metaphor and metonymy each draw out a branch, and a branch may fork again (深: 水深 → 夜深 → 感情深 → 颜色深; the branches lead on from one another, yet do not lie on a single line). **Chain-like**: sense A gives rise to B, and B to C, until A and C no longer have a direct motivation (头: the head → the top end → the beginning → the bleached ending 头 in 石头). A real dictionary entry mixes the two: the centre is clear, the edges join into stretches, and the outermost fringe may even cast itself off as a different morpheme of the same sound (花: 花草 / 花费). Lexicographic practice: senses are ordered by **frequency of use plus psychological reality**, not by etymology — this is the fundamental division of labour between the *Xiandai Hanyu cidian* 《现代汉语词典》 and the *Ciyuan* 《辞源》.
:::

::: example Drawing a sense-map for 老 {#ex-lao}
Common senses of 老: advanced in years, senior in standing, long-standing, deceased (a euphemistic name), a prefix (老虎, 老王), an adverb (always). Draw a radial map of the senses and label the mechanism of extension on each branch.
::: solution
Centre 【advanced in years】 — metonymy → 【senior in standing】 (老教师); — metaphor → 【long-standing】 (老房子); — euphemism → 【deceased】 (他老了). 【Prefix】 and 【adverb (always)】 are two exits into **grammaticalisation**: the former is bound to a noun (老王, 老虎 — the sense of age is already gone); the latter sinks to the position before the verb (老不理会). Each mechanism label sits where it belongs: metonymy rests on contiguity (great age and seniority often keep company), metaphor on similarity (what is old and what is aged resemble each other), bleaching on occupation by high frequency. One map strings together the three great engines of semantics; that is why a "map" beats a "list".
:::

## The engines of change in word meaning {#sec-yanbian}

::: definition Three kinds of change and two mechanisms {#def-sanbian}
Divided by **range**: **broadening** (菜 in earlier Chinese referred to vegetables; now meat, eggs, fungi and algae are all included; 江 and 河 change from proper names to common nouns), **narrowing** (金 in earlier Chinese referred to metal in general — 金就砺则利 ('Metal brought to the whetstone grows sharp.') (Our translation.) — and now refers to gold alone; 臭 in earlier Chinese was any smell at all (其臭如兰 ('Its odour is like the orchid's.') (Our translation.)), and now refers to a foul smell alone — narrowing together with pejoration), **shift** (闻 originally meant to hear, and now is specifically the sense of smell; 牺牲 was originally an animal used in sacrifice, and now is any dying for a righteous cause). Divided by **rise and fall**: **amelioration** (乖 in earlier Chinese meant to go against, and now means clever and well-behaved), **pejoration** (爪牙 in earlier Chinese was praise for a brave man, and is now an accomplice). There are only two mechanisms: **metaphor** (projection by similarity: 桌面, 硬盘) and **metonymy** (substitution by association: 白宫发表声明; 他刚读完一部《红楼梦》 — the book one buys stands in for the making of it). Bleaching (lexical meaning → grammatical meaning) is the furthest extension of metonymy in a high-frequency syntactic position.
:::

::: example The two faces of 潜水 {#ex-qianshui}
潜水, from a military and sporting sense to the sense of not speaking in a group chat: which engine is that? And what has the 水 of 水贴 further passed through (from the verb 灌水, in a describing use, to the adjective in 这帖真水)?
::: solution
潜水 (not speaking): **metaphor** — 沉在水面之下不露头 ('sunk beneath the surface, not showing one's head') (Our translation.) and 在群里不发声 ('making no sound in the group') (Our translation.) are similar in structure, and the image of 人在场 ('the person is present') is kept (the person really is on the list). It belongs to a systematic cluster of metaphors that online communities produced of themselves (冒泡, 灌水, 上岸). The 水 of 水贴: **metonymy plus bleaching** — from the action 灌水 (prolific output of the water-injecting kind) one borrows the properties of the stuff, then generalises them into a qualitative sense of "low in quality, heavily watered", and the word class slides towards the adjective (太水了). Internet language has opened this old engine up to maximum revolutions, but **every step of grammaticalisation has a prototype off the page** (the *Xianhan* 《现汉》 enters the adjectival sense of 水 only with caution; the speech community runs ahead of the dictionary).
:::
:::

::: intuition Is polysemy a defect or a superpower
The arbitrariness of sound and meaning, together with linearity and the principle of least effort, jointly sentence "one word, one meaning" to death. Polysemy, by way of **a centre plus disambiguation in context**, presses the cost of memory to the minimum (remembering the map of one 打 beats remembering twenty new words). The real accident rate of "misreading an ambiguity" is extremely low — context (打酱油, 打官司 and 打人 each differ) and collocation (classifiers, resultative verb compounds) are two filters that come free. What really calls for attention is the cross-generational misalignment of polysemy produced by **synchronic layering** (意思 has a different sense in each of 小意思, 没意思 and 意思意思 — a joke, yet humour does indeed feed on this dividend). Psycholinguistics bears this out: retrieving a sense of a high-frequency polysemous word is faster than integrating the semantics of a monosemous word — polysemy is not a debt; it is compound interest.
:::

::: history From the *Erya* to seme analysis: how the Chinese take word meaning apart {#sec-cixue}
The *Erya* 《尔雅》, grouping and stringing words under 释诂 ('explaining the glosses') and 释亲 ('explaining kinship'), is a three-thousand-year forerunner of the idea of the **semantic field**. The *Shuowen jiezi* 《说文》 (*Explaining Graphs and Analysing Characters*), by way of *zhuanzhu* (mutually glossing characters; disputed) and phonetic loan (转注／假借), establishes a mechanistic view that 义生于用 ('meaning is born of use') (Our translation.). Liu Xi's *Shiming* 《释名》 is wild in its sound-glosses, yet everywhere it tests the thought that 义存于音 ('meaning resides in the sound') (Our translation.). After the modern transformation had brought in frameworks from abroad, the seme analysis of the 1950s (a decomposition into components on the pattern of “丈夫”＝[＋人＋男＋已婚]) entered Chinese — **componential analysis** brought the opposition of elder and younger between 哥哥 and 弟弟 into a table for the first time. From the 1980s, cognitive semantics (radial categories, systems of metaphor, prototype theory) drew 深, 上, 老 and their kind as maps. Over three thousand years the study of word meaning has gone from classification to decomposition and then to networks — the methods change; the question never has: **by what right a meaning can be carried off by a single sound**.
:::

::: quiz
臭, which in earlier Chinese referred to smell in general and now refers to a foul smell alone, belongs to which change in word meaning? (　)
- [ ] broadening + amelioration
- [x] narrowing + pejoration
- [ ] shift + neutralisation
- [ ] bleaching
::: solution
The range narrows (any smell → a bad smell) and the evaluation is lowered (the 臭 of 其臭如兰 ('Its odour is like the orchid's.') (Our translation.) was not originally derogatory) — narrowing often goes together with pejoration, because negative experience seizes the vocabulary.
:::
:::

::: quiz
The mechanism of word meaning in 桌面 and 硬盘 is (　).
- [ ] metonymy
- [x] metaphor
- [ ] abbreviation
- [ ] grammaticalisation
::: solution
A similar structure (on a flat surface there are things one can set down / the physical shape of a disc) is projected into a new domain — metaphor. Metonymy depends on contiguity (a part standing for the whole, and the like); these words have no relation of contiguity.
:::
:::

::: quiz
The first basis on which a dictionary orders its senses is usually (　).
- [ ] historical order
- [x] frequency of use and psychological reality
- [ ] complexity of strokes
- [ ] rank of register
::: solution
A modern descriptive dictionary orders senses by frequency and psychological salience (the origin sits in the "origin and development" column); ordering by history is the division of labour of a historical dictionary (the way of the *Ciyuan*).
:::

::: exercise A sense-map {level=1}
Draw a radial sense-map for 重: the zhòng family — great in weight, important, solemn — and the chóng family — repetition, layering. Divide by pronunciation first, then divide the maps.
::: solution
Take them separately first: zhòng (great in weight [centre] → important [metonymy: heft → value] → solemn [metaphor: the steadiness of a heavy object → a sedate bearing]); chóng (the centre "layered" → again, repetition). Two pronunciations and two maps are the standard treatment of "one form, several pronunciations, several meanings". Whoever links the 庄重 of zhòng onto the chóng map is judged to have the structure wrong.
:::

::: exercise Diagnosing change {level=1}
Judge the type of change over time in the following words (broadening / narrowing / shift / amelioration / pejoration / bleaching): 子, 乖, 牺牲, 去, 金.
::: solution
子: a general term for an infant narrows to "son", and is further bleached into a nominal ending (the 子 of 桌子) — a chain of narrowing plus bleaching; 乖: the sense of going against turns into clever and well-behaved (amelioration); 牺牲: a sacrificial animal turns into dying for a righteous cause (shift plus amelioration); 去: "to leave a place" turns into "to go to a place" (a shift that reverses direction, a classic unjust case); 金: metal in general narrows to gold. 祥 may be set as an extra question: today's 吉祥 is a survival of the old sense "blessing"; in Middle Chinese a branch "omen" once spread out from it (不祥之兆, 'an ill omen'), the range having once broadened while the original meaning was not lost.
:::

::: exercise Observing new senses {level=2}
Collect three new senses that have arisen in online communities over the past two years (for example, 卷, 卷王 and 卷生卷死 count as three levels of grammaticalisation in one word-family). For each, label the mechanism (metaphor / metonymy / bleaching) and whether the "period of transparent motivation" has already passed.
::: solution
Sample answer: 卷 (competition turning involuted) = metonymy (rolling up → squeezing → the **metonymy of the action** of high-intensity commitment); it has already entered the productive period of 卷王 and 卷不动, and the motivation is still transparent; 破防 (a line of defence in a game → psychological collapse) = metaphor, transparent; 蚌埠住了 = homophonic **symbolisation**, the motivation already broken (a newcomer does not know that 蚌埠 is not a place name) — any example that contains a mechanism label, a judgement of transparency, and one sentence of use made up by the student meets the standard for full marks.
:::

::: exercise Short essay: should the dictionary ratify internet senses after the fact? {level=3}
The inclusion in the *Xiandai Hanyu cidian* 《现代汉语词典》 of such new senses as those of 门, 被 and 晒 has more than once set off discussion across the whole public. Write a short essay of eight hundred to twelve hundred characters. What criteria should a dictionary have for admitting a new sense? How should the roles of "descriptivism" and "gatekeeper of the language" divide the account? You must use at least three concepts from this lesson: the "map of polysemy", "colouring" and "bleaching".
::: solution
Marking points: being able to propose operable thresholds for inclusion (frequency in a corpus + spread across communities + survival over a forecast period), and to pair each with one example for and one against; understanding that "one records the sense and does not record the ill name" — labelling of colouring (derogatory / jocular) is precisely a tool of description, not a switch for censorship; being able to anticipate the misplacement in the claim that "admitting a word = endorsement" (a dictionary's authority lies in prompting standard usage, not in the praise or blame of letting a sense in); and an accurate analysis of example sentences for the bleaching of the passive with 被.
:::

::: summary
Word meaning = lexical meaning (the referential core) + grammatical meaning + colouring (affect / register / image / honorific and humble). A polysemous word is a map, not a list: a central sense plus radial or chain-like extension, the dictionary ordering by frequency and psychological reality. Three engines govern change: metaphor (similarity), metonymy (contiguity), grammaticalisation (bleaching under high frequency). The account of range divides into broadening, narrowing and shift; the account of evaluation divides into amelioration and pejoration. "Word meaning = etymologies added together" and "compositional meaning = the sum of the morphemes" are two horses that eat the seed grain. The discipline of description is to record the community, not to legislate for it. Internet language has put its foot down and brought the old engines up to speed. The dispute over admitting senses (the dictionary's right to ratify them after the fact) is in essence a dispute over the division of labour between the study of norms and the study of description — both are wanted, but the account must be divided.
:::
