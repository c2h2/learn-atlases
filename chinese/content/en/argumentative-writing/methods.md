title: "Methods of reasoning"
---

The previous chapter prepared the materials; this chapter connects the circuit — **the same evidence, joined to a different method, is a different lamp: the "methods of reasoning" of an argumentative essay are not a menu of composition terms, but five schemes for allocating risk**.

## Definition: five circuits {#def-diaolu}

::: definition Each method is a way of losing {#def-fangfa}
**① Deduction (including the elliptical form)** (**safe, but the conclusion does not go beyond the premises — the fault in the writing is often not in the minor premise, but in a major premise that does not dare to see the light ("他成绩好是因为补课" ('his marks are good because he had extra lessons'): the major premise "补课决定成绩" ('extra lessons determine the marks') dies the moment it sees the light (the writing site of the omitted syllogism in the logic course: writing your major premise out from memory and reading it through once is the first operation of a method of reasoning**))); **② Induction (enumeration)** (**from the sample to a regularity: the regime of risk-quotation sheets (Chapter 6) — the requirement in writing: declare the square in the essay itself ("调查来自本校三个重点班" ('the survey comes from three key classes in this school'), one line, buys an honesty policy on your whole essay**)); **③ Example** (**induction's live performance: for the three-character formula 选, 摆, 挖, see the next section**); **④ Analogy** (**a loan against similarity: the three-piece set (a list of relevant properties + the boundary at which it fails + a testable inference) — this chapter adds one rule of bookkeeping: every hop of an analogy has to pay interest (similar ≠ of the same kind; of the same kind ≠ the same in principle); a long-chain analogy (A is like B, B is like C) is compound-interest debt, and must default within two segments**)); **⑤ Cause** (**the most expensive policy: miss one of the three slips, and you downgrade the wording in the essay (Chapter 7's tax rate is realised in the rhetoric: if you can write 相关 ('correlated'), do not write 导致 ('causes'), unless you have paid the tax**)). **The matching table** (**a policy claim feeds on cause + costs and benefits; a definitional claim feeds on the consequences of the classification and the purpose of the naming; a value claim feeds on standards, trade-offs and counter-examples; a factual claim feeds on the data chain and the measurement scope — a method is not decoration, it is a matching of type: a mismatched essay is what gets called "complete in its methods of reasoning"**).
:::

::: definition The three-character formula for examples: choose, lay out, dig {#def-liuge}
**Choose the typical** (**typical ≠ extreme: an extreme case catches the eye, but the other side can send it back with the one phrase 个例 ('an isolated case') — unless your task is precisely to falsify a universal (a sniper's post); typical = the sample that demonstrates the mechanism most cleanly ("arguing that 'a subsidy changes behaviour', choose a county town in which, before and after the policy, the only thing that moved was the subsidy: it beats three noisy first-tier cities"**); **lay out a set, not a clan** (**the illusion that three examples of the same kind beat ten of different kinds — counting examples is primary-school arithmetic: if the three examples each carry a different mechanism, they undermine one another instead (the logic for arranging an array of examples is to decompose mechanisms, not to stack quantity: A exemplifies incentive, B exemplifies information, C exemplifies the default option — three examples, three gears**)); **dig down to the mechanism layer** (**"an example is ore, not a gold ornament" — three questions for the digging: which variable moved in this example? If the scene is changed, does it still hold? Which of my layers does it support (fact / mechanism / value)? An example dug all the way down can grow a paragraph of analysis; an example that will not be dug can only be fitted with the one sentence 可见…… ('it can be seen that…') (a count of 可见 across the whole essay is a free test of the quality of examples: "可见 appears four times, which means four examples are running naked"**).
:::

::: definition Reductio and the counter-example: two lines of defence and counter-attack {#def-guimiu}
**Reductio (以子之矛, 'with the other's own spear')** (**first work for hire for the other side's position, then let it go bankrupt: "若按此逻辑，则……" ('if one follows this logic, then…') — the writing version of the Mohist *tuī* 推 ('extending') (以其所不取之同于其所取者予之, "presenting him with that in which what he does not accept is the same as what he does accept." (Our translation.)); Mencius's clamp in the 守株 manner ('watching the stump') (*Mencius*, "Gongsun Chou I", "非徒无益，而又害之": "not merely of no benefit, but harmful besides." (Our translation.): first go along with it, and let the absurdity grow up by itself); Han Fei's display of paired propositions, a spear put up for sale and a shield to be pierced — the constitutional article of reductio: "**以子之矛，陷子之盾，何如？**" ("Take your spear and pierce your shield — what then?" (Our translation.)) — **in this sentence, Chapter 1 of the logic course puts *yuán* 援 ('adducing') and *tui* into business as one firm**); **the technical point** (**the absurdity must be an inference from the position, and not a caricature in the rhetoric: what you derive is "the other side's necessity", not "the other side's manner of drawing": a caricature reductio = a straw man in formal dress**)); **counter-example, in attack and in defence** (**a counter-example you build yourself = a vaccination in advance (in the main text, fire one shell on the opponent's behalf and deal with it on the spot — if it cannot be dealt with, write it into the "limits" paragraph and adjust the strength of the claim (thesis): a counter-example is the claim's fitness coach, training specifically the muscle you did not put a limit on**). **The two-part recipe (concession–turn)** (**"诚然……但是……" (诚然, 'granted', ……但是……, 'but') is the sentence pattern with the highest return for its cost in a Chinese argumentative essay — the precondition: the 诚然 part must be sincere (Chapter 6: a concession is a beam, not a scar), and the 但是 part must catch the ball that the 诚然 throws back (strength that has been conceded may not be quietly taken back at the turn: taking it back is a breach**).
:::

::: example One claim, three methods: side by side {#ex-sanfa}
Claim (thesis): **"Evening self-study in senior high should add a period of autonomous time."** With each of three methods write one core paragraph of 120 characters, and have them point out one another's "power loss" (what that method necessarily loses here).
::: solution
**A · the deduction paragraph** (**major premise "the formation of a learning skill requires practice time that one allocates oneself (the zone of consensus in research on self-regulated learning)" + minor premise "evening self-study is entirely teacher-led, with no room to allocate for oneself" ∴ it should be added — **power loss: the minor premise's "none whatsoever" is a universal proposition, and any one class running a trial pierces it; and the conclusion reaches only "there ought to be", and cannot reach "there ought at once to be it across the board": the strength is stuck inside deduction's credit limit, and the policy detail has to open another paragraph in order to pay**); **B · the induction paragraph** (**pilot data from three schools set side by side: sleep, the homework-completion rate, and marks, three indicators improving in the same direction — power loss: all three are pilot schools that signed up of their own accord (a self-selection square); and the window of "improvement" is only two semesters (a trend is not everything): an induction paragraph must declare its own square, or one sentence from the other side, "the pilot schools are all good schools", wipes it to zero**); **C · the analogy paragraph** (**"a coach cannot lift the weights for the athlete": learning is like training, and a displacement of who directs is a displacement of the load — power loss: analogy supplies only "a convenience to intuition", and does not supply proof that "the relevant properties hold in this scene" — you must add a paragraph of "three relevant items of the same kind between evening self-study at school and training + one key item of a different kind (the student has no right to choose)" — the different kind is precisely the opponent's main gun-position: an analogy paragraph that does not itself declare the different kind is leaving the back door for someone else to kick in**); **the side-by-side verdict** (**the three paragraphs each govern one layer: deduction governs the direction's being legitimate, induction governs the facts' being effective, analogy governs the reader's being able to understand — a good policy essay is a relay of the three, and the power losses make up for one another; an essay that runs a single method to the end loses by taking one lamp for the whole circuit**).
:::
:::

::: intuition A method is a scheme for allocating risk {#int-fenpei}
**The "safety–added value" ordering of the five circuits** (**deduction is the safest and the poorest; induction and analogy grow richer by degrees and riskier by degrees; cause is the richest and the most expensive; example sits in the middle but is extremely easy to use indiscriminately — choosing a method = choosing the strength of test you are willing to promise the reader (the method version of Chapter 3's clause on the way of dying: the way a deduction paragraph dies is hidden in the premises, the way an induction paragraph dies is in the sample frame, the way an analogy paragraph dies is in the different kind: only when you know which paragraph you die in are you fit to write that paragraph**). **The order for mixing methods** (**the order of a letter to a reader you do not know: analogy opens the door (so the lay reader understands) → induction delivers the goods (so the specialist is brought to accept) → deduction closes (the particular case is raised to a rule) → cause covers the bottom (the intervention and the boundary are stated) — this order is also the order of "the cost of persuasion": first borrow a sense of familiarity, then bring on the evidence, and talk about the mechanism last; the other way about, an argumentative essay that leads with a regression table is often not that it will not do, but that it does not want to be read to the end**).
:::

::: history Chinese and Western tracks in how method is conceived {#sec-shuanggui}
**The Chinese track** (**the *Mobian* ('Mohist dialectical chapters'), *pì* 辟 ('illustrating'), *móu* 侔 ('paralleling'), *yuan* and *tui* — Warring States names for four basic circuits (a return visit to Chapter 10 of the logic course); the two-layered voice-parts of the 或曰 form in the "Nan" chapters of the *Han Feizi* (或曰, 'or someone says') — the form, as the histories record it: first set out a position that can be cited and is then refuted; it is the earliest device of a "virtual opposing side" in the history of Chinese argument (the institutionalising of Lesson 1's "keeping a seat": letting the opponent live in your essay under the name of 或曰**); Mencius, skilled at figures and skilled at *tui* (a legion of analogies in "以杀人喻为政" ('taking killing as a figure for governing'), and the distinction of 能 ('able') from 可 ('permissible') in "是不为也，非不能也" ("It is that one does not do it, not that one cannot." (Our translation.)) — analogy and deduction trained as a pair; the making of Qing Confucian scholars' evidential research into an inductive method — the trade rule 孤证不立 ('a single piece of evidence does not stand') (a one-vote veto on the quantity of evidence and the independence of sources: the Chinese world's own standard of "multiple comparison"**)); **the Western track** (**Aristotle's *Rhetoric*, the split into example and deduction; the *Prior Analytics*, the deduction circuit formally named; the induction circuit of Bacon's *New Organon*; Mill's five methods (the circuit put into engineering form); the Toulmin layout brings the "warrant" from behind the curtain onto the table — the movement to make a hidden major premise explicit (Chapter 1's front, garrisoned in the writing course**); **where the tracks meet** (**in 1905 the *Mule mingxue* ('Mill's Logic') was brought out in translation, and induction entered the toolbox of terms for writing in Chinese; in the early twentieth century the three elements "evidence — reasoning — claim" settled as the grammar of *guówén* 国文 ('Chinese as a school subject') teaching (compressing method into items for recitation is the price of institutionalising: a generation had by heart the four nouns "the method of example, the method of quotation, the method of metaphor, the method of contrast", and then used them as four genre templates**); **the present** (**criticism of "structure reduced to a pattern" in the argumentative essay, and the reform of "critical-thinking writing" (the task-driven composition: tying method back onto the problem — after 2014, question-faces of "a disputed situation + multiple tasks" became current: the official signal of an examination in methods returning to an examination in reasoning**).
:::

::: warning Four pits in method
**The genre turned into a template** (**"general–particular–general + three examples + one celebrated saying" is a specimen of a method's corpse: the correct use of a template is as scaffolding, not as the building (the technique is when it is taken down, and in what order: Lesson 4 goes into it in full**)); **analogy taken as proof** (**analogy can save a person from not understanding; it cannot save a person from not believing: when the analogy paragraph is written, ask yourself "have I paid for its being of the same kind" (the lesson of paragraph C among this chapter's A, B and C: if the different kind is not declared, the back door will certainly be kicked in**)); **three paragraphs, one method** (**an essay that dares use only induction throughout: it reads like a compilation of materials; or only deduction: it reads like a proof exercise, and the major premise grows more and more ill at ease — "a monotone method is not a style; it is a single asset-class: one counter-example pierces one method, and variety can force the other side to open three further fronts**); **"argument by quotation" taken as an independent method** (**quotation is the hauling operation for evidence, not a type of inference: what an authority says still, in the end, reports for work through deduction (credible if the authority is inside its domain) or induction (a consensus of the many) (writing the hauling down as a line item of its own is the root of the illness of "methods complete"**).
:::

::: exercise Rotating drill in the five methods {level=1}
On the one topic "should the school uniform be standardised", write for each a paragraph of 80 characters: deduction, induction, analogy, cause, reductio — and attach to each a line, "the way this paragraph dies".
::: solution
Marking: **five paragraphs, five ways of dying, each different** (**deduction dies of the premises, induction dies of the sample, analogy dies of the different kind, cause dies of confounding, reductio dies of "that is not what the other side means" — if you cannot write the way this paragraph dies, you have not really grasped the method**); the verdict on the reductio paragraph: **the absurdity you derive must be the position's necessity, not its manner of drawing**.
:::
:::

::: exercise Change an essay's circuit {level=2}
Take the core paragraph of an old piece of your own (in the original it was most likely a heap of examples), and rewrite it as one induction paragraph and one causal paragraph, 150 characters each; state the added cost in evidence of each of the two versions.
::: solution
Marking: **changing it to induction must really supply a sample frame** (**if you do not supply one, you have only renamed three examples "the majority": new soup, the same medicine**); **changing it to cause must really list the confounding** (**a causal paragraph with no list of confounders = writing a correlation as a command**); awareness of cost (**an answer that can write "this version needs two tables and one interview; I will not pay — so I keep the original and downgrade the wording" scores full marks: honesty of method is, first, honesty of budget**).
:::
:::

::: exercise Short essay: method is morality {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**choosing a method of reasoning is choosing how you are willing to be refuted: the person who uses deduction accepts a check of the premises, the person who uses induction accepts a check of the sample, the person who uses analogy accepts a check of the different kind — one who refuses every one of these tests and professes 'you do not understand me' is not a problem of method, but does not want to pay the tax on reasoning**". You must call on at least six items: **the five ways of losing**, **the three-character formula for examples**, **the three-piece set for analogy**, **reductio's anti-caricature clause**, **the 或曰 device**, **the order (analogy opens the door — induction delivers the goods — deduction closes — cause covers the bottom)**; and give a ruling: **"write the essay for people willing to be persuaded" or "write it for people who hold the resources" — does the audience determine the method, or the method the audience**.
::: solution
Marking points: the six mechanisms (**a way of losing = a category of tax; digging the example = a quality inspection; the three-piece set = a loan contract; anti-caricature = honesty towards the opponent; 或曰 = an institutional keeping of a seat; the order = the study of the cost of persuasion**); the ruling (**two references: the view that the audience determines — the reader who decides where resources go needs the causal paragraph and the cost paragraph, and persuasion of a public needs analogy and induction: the method comes clean to the audience; the view that the method determines — training in the genre shapes the reader in return (write only template essays for long enough, and readers lose the capacity to digest extended reasoning: an audience is not a natural object, it is a product of education — what the marking looks at is a third thing: whichever side you stand on, you must declare "which way of losing this essay (this short essay of yours) has chosen, and in which paragraph it is prepared to die" — the meta-level is cashed on the spot (declaring your own way of dying: the highest honour-clause in the whole essay**); if the conclusion can write "**method is morality: the kind of inspection you are willing to accept is the kind of reader you are fit to have; the kind of refutation you run from is the kind of person you are becoming**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
Circuits filed: the five methods (**each is a way of losing, and the way of dying sits in a different place**), example (**choose the typical, lay out a set, dig for the mechanism: counting 可见 is a free test**), analogy (**the three-piece set + the law that a long chain defaults**), reductio (**the position's necessity, not a manner of drawing**), 或曰 (**letting the opponent live in the essay**), the order (**understand first, then assent, then the rule**). Method in one sentence: **before you set pen to paper, first choose a way of losing you can afford**. The next chapter runs the wires: structure.
:::
