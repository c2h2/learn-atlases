title: "The examination essay"
---

The concluding chapter returns to that ninety-minute room — **the examination essay is the ability to compress the whole course into a delivery: the prompt is the contract, time is the budget, and the marker is a commissioning party who gives you only two minutes. This chapter does not teach opportunism; what it teaches is how to make seriousness into an assembly line**.

## Definition: the prompt is the contract {#def-hetong}

::: definition Three clauses {#def-santiao}
**① The task clause** (**"write a speech", "a letter to the headteacher", "an exchange with young readers" — the obligations of genre and of voice (the reverse of the sugar-coating on the subheadings in Lesson 5: genre is not packaging; it is a second claim. The evidence in a speech must be fit to be heard, and the reasoning in a letter must be fit to be received by one person ("the three characters 演讲稿 mean: there are people present, there is your voice, there is a rhythm that applause can break in upon — to write it as a formal paper is to attend a hearing wearing a swimsuit**)); **② The materials clause** (**the materials are the foundation, not the ceiling: to open by quoting the materials is to hand over the keys; to retell the materials through the whole piece is to view the property for the landlord (the reason the "three-part form" of "200 characters extracted from the materials + 300 characters of example-cabinet + 100 characters of slogan-shouting" is a specimen of a zero score: it has fulfilled the obligation to quote, and it refuses to fulfil the obligation to reason**)); **③ The scoring clause** (**“深刻” ('profound') = the layer of mechanism (a return to Lesson 2: not that the pitch of the voice is high, but that you have stated a cause and effect which others have not seen); “丰富” ('rich') = the layers of putting one item to several uses; “有文采” ('with literary flair') = accurate positions of emphasis, and rhythm (Lesson 7); “有创意” ('with originality') = the narrowness and the depth of the angle — translate the four items back from metaphysics into a glossary, and the development grade is not luck; it is a list for selecting and fitting**.
:::

::: definition Four steps in reading the question, and three grades of going off it {#def-shenti}
**The four steps** (**① circle the task-verb (what genre to write, to whom one is speaking, what action to complete); ② distinguish the type of position (does this question want fact, definition, value, or policy? — a mismatch among the four types is the deepest channel of going off the question ("where “该不该” ('should one, or should one not') appears = policy or value; where “是什么” ('what it is') appears = a negotiation of the definition**)); ③ **find the words to be negotiated** (**what the question gives is not a claim; it is several words one must go and negotiate: “本手、妙手、俗手” ('the basic move, the brilliant move, the vulgar move'), “故事的力量” ('the power of stories') — first, on the rough paper, draw for each keyword the spectrum of its interpretations (本手 = the fundamentals? within the rules? keeping to the established way?), choose one, and declare it (Lesson 2: the right to define is the right to allocate — in the examination hall the right to allocate is in your hands; this is what the prompt concedes to you**)); ④ list the concession slots (**who is the strongest opposing side on this question — if you cannot list them, your essay will be brittle in the hall as well**). **Three grades of going off the question** (**misreading the task (it is written, and the genre is what is ruled on: “给青年读者” ('for young readers') written as a report to the leadership) < drifting (the keywords were talked about, but the dimension was changed: talking of “故事的力量” and writing it as "one ought to read more") < stock-fitting (a corpse wearing someone else's formal dress: the model essay brought in by recitation and the prompt are classmates by two words only — the three grades share the one guilt: this contract has not been performed. The mark does not look at what you meant to say; it looks at what you handed in**.
:::

## The ninety-minute sequence of operations {#def-gongxu}

::: definition Eight-twelve-three-five-five {#def-yunzhuan}
**Reading the question: 8 minutes** (**the four steps gone through to the end, plus a diagram of the spectra of the words to be negotiated: these 8 minutes buy out the risk to the direction of the whole piece — "the greatest waste in the examination hall is to spend 70 minutes writing a piece on a question you have misread. That fast is slow, and slow is fast, is here the literal sense"**)); **Driving the pile, and the outline: 12 minutes** (**the minimum runnable package: a one-sentence pile + one qualification + one opposing side + one line for the way of dying (60 characters, the specification in Lesson 2) + a six-line blueprint of sentences (the specification in Lesson 5) — "the 12 minutes of the outline are the only investment sure to gain: what they spend is what you are least short of (time); what they preserve is what you are most short of (logic"**)); **Writing the skeleton: 55 minutes** (**timed by the function of the paragraph: the pile paragraph 8, the mechanism paragraph 15, the concession-and-refutation paragraph 12, the prescription paragraph 12, the close 8 — the contingency for a paragraph that collapses as you write it: skip on and add a signpost; never turn back for a large rewrite ("perfectionism in the examination hall is a cheap luxury: the honesty of the first draft is worth more than the splendour of a paragraph. What you have to deliver is a house someone can live in, not an advertisement for a renovation"**)); **Checking: 15 minutes** (**three checks: is the pile still there (whether the closing paragraph and the opening paragraph are the same claim — changing husbands as one writes is a high-frequency accident); do the connectives pay what they promise (spot-read, through the whole piece, every “因此／可见” ('therefore / from this it can be seen'), and clear the connectives at customs); the number of characters, and the hard flaws (wrong characters, quotations, personal names: what is deducted is marks with the price posted — "checking is not courtesy; it is a second audit. To audit yourself is more decent than to be audited"**).
:::

::: definition The opening-sentence interview, and the response to a prefabricated essay {#def-mianshi}
**The opening sentences take the interview for you** (**the route of the marking is two minutes for one question: the six opening sentences, read in a chain, ought to be a summary able to survive on its own (once the writing is done, do this "summary spot-read": where one place breaks, mend that place — this is the single drill in the examination hall with the best return for its cost**)); **The boundary of the prefabricated essay** (**to recite a model essay = to pre-install fake furniture (the prompt comes and knocks, and none of the furniture fits: one can only improvise on the spot — and the holes in that improvising are the proof of the offence of stock-fitting); the lawful equipment is "the sequence of operations, and sentence patterns" — patterns for concession, a stock of qualifiers, the three questions for a counter-example, the blueprint method: what can be carried across is the scaffolding; what cannot be carried across is the building ("the person who prefabricates 'Su Shi 苏轼 was demoted' into all-purpose evidence will, on a question of 'the ethics of technology', be forced to send Su Shi up in a rocket: file the examples by mechanism (the incentive class, the supervision class, the cognition class); what one draws in the examination hall is not a story, but a functional part"**)); **Three steps for driving the pile on the spot** (**from the words to be negotiated on the prompt, find one "interpretation that everyone grants by default", and put to it the one sentence "Always? For whom? At what cost?" — the pile, on the great majority of real past questions, is buried precisely in this run of three questions ("a question on 本手: everyone grants by default that “练好本手才有妙手” ('only when the basic move is well practised is there a brilliant move') — press on with “有没有把本手练成肌肉记忆反而不敢变通的人” ('are there people who have practised the basic move into muscle memory and who, for that reason, dare not adapt?'), and one paragraph of qualification comes to life. Innovation in the examination hall does not need a new compendium of cases; it needs only half a question more, put to an old consensus**).
:::

::: example A 60-character delivery package for three questions {#ex-jiaofubao}
Using "pile + qualification + opposing side + way of dying", give each of the three questions one minimum runnable package, and for each write one line, "the point of risk at which I would be misjudged as having gone off the question": ① Lao She 老舍, 'Thinking of Beiping' (《想北平》): ‘我的雨是跟着风走的’ 'My rain follows the wind as it goes.' (Our translation.) — "the city and the person" (adapted); ② “有人称我们是‘强国一代’” 'Some people call us a "generation of a strong country".' (Our translation.) (the gist of the 2023 Beijing paper); ③ “好的故事可以引发我们联想和思考，给我们以启迪和感动” 'A good story can stir us to association and to thought, and give us inspiration and a sense of being moved.' (Our translation.) (the gist of the 2023 Shanghai paper).
::: solution
① **Package** (**"the city shapes the person, but on the premise of being 'able to exit': only towards a Beiping one is able to leave is Lao She's attachment a choice and not a cage"; qualification: discuss only the attachment of "those who have the right to exit"; opposing side: native-soil literature will object — leaving is precisely what is harder; way of dying: unable to produce a sample of the dilemma of "the right to exit and the intensity of attachment"**); **Point of risk** (**being read as "writing about one's hometown" = a breach of contract by a values essay: the pile of this piece is in "the right to exit", not in "nostalgia for home" — the first paragraph of the whole piece has to nail this phrase on**); ② **Package** (**"'generation of a strong country' is a title that has been conferred, and the account is worth checking: in a generation's book of merits a column must be left for the individual's liabilities"; qualification: discuss the act of naming itself, and do not deny the achievements; opposing side: the grand narrative does confer dignity on the individual; way of dying: unable to produce one concrete case of 'a title covering the individual's account'**); **Point of risk** (**extremely easy to slide into overturning at both ends, the stance-declaring essay or the angry-youth essay: the concession paragraph (the achievements are real) must appear before the dismantling (the examination-hall use, from Chapter 6, of putting the concession first**); ③ **Package** (**"the inspiration of a good story comes from its daring not to draw the conclusion in your place: where a blank is left, the reader alone completes the moral judgement — a story told through to the end only trains obedience"; qualification: discuss stories of "the kind that stirs thought"; lyrical stories are excluded; opposing side: folk tales are passed down from generation to generation precisely by being told through to the end; way of dying: a comparative experiment (the difference in reception between the version with a blank left and the version told through to the end) has no support either in the imagination or in the literature**); **Point of risk** (**if the word “故事” ('story') is not narrowed first (Lesson 5, "one word, one sense"), the whole piece will be pulled about by three senses: the first sentence of the pile paragraph must first declare that the sense taken is “叙事作品”, 'narrative work'**.
:::

::: intuition The examination hall is a genre {#int-wenlei}
**Study the examination hall as a real genre, rather than complain of it as a prison of scholarship** (**it has a contract that has been published (the examination specification), a stable community of scoring (the marking panel), and a two-minute attention economy (the engineering of opening sentences) — the whole methodology of Chapter 8 applies here in the same way: read the contract, assign the voices, control the budget ("the generation that complains 'the examination hall has no room for reasoned thinking', and the generation that writes reasoned thinking by means of the examination hall, are using one and the same prompt. The difference is whether the prompt is treated as a written verdict, or as a negotiating table**). **Its honesty** (**standardised marking is indeed dull towards what is unlike the rest, and sharp towards clarity — so deliver clarity first, and then, inside the clarity, hide a little of the real thing ("a set limit is not a humiliation: within 140,000 marking-hours, clarity is the greatest courtesy towards a stranger. And true reasoned thinking never fears a format; it has only learned, inside the format, to speak more softly and more truly — and this is also the common experience of every high-pressure genre through the long course of writing (the lineage of oblique writing (曲笔) in Lesson 16 draws, here, one dividend from the examination hall**).
:::

::: history The succession of examination-hall genres {#sec-huandai2}
**The 1977–80s**, the era of the assigned-topic essay (**the restoration of the gaokao (**on the common account: in the early years the long essay was mostly a single topic set for everyone, and writing about people, recording events, and the response-after-reading stood side by side; in the 1980s “一事一议” ('one matter, one argument') and the “短论” ('short commentary') became the mainstream of training — the argumentative essay, by means of the two-way supply of blood between the examination hall and the pedagogy of topical commentary (Lesson 8), ascended the throne again, and topical commentary and the examination hall have fed each other ever since**)); **1999**, the topic essay (**“答案是丰富多彩的” 'The answer is rich and colourful.' (Our translation.) — an experiment in devolving authority, the title set by oneself and the genre chosen by oneself: the price was a golden age of prefabricated essays and of stock phrases (authority was devolved without a standard of acceptance issued along with it, and the hall itself then invented the reading on which “文体不限” ('genre not restricted') = “文体不必” ('genre not required')**)); **the 2000s**, the era of the materials essay (**authority taken back: the materials are given, and the scope is limited — from then on, training in reading the question became a prominent field of learning (the four-step method is a way of reading a contract that was forced into being**)); **after 2014**, the task-driven type (**"an exchange with young people", "a letter to ××": the task clause comes in (the national version of the "obligation of voice" in Lesson 8: a revival of the study of genre in the examination hall — form has become important again, because it has become an action that can be accepted on inspection**)); **the 2020s** (**“本手、妙手、俗手” (2022) and "a good story" (2023 and others: a double upgrade, the negotiation of keywords plus the specification for reasoned thinking — **in the detailed rules for marking, the wording “辩证” ('dialectical') and “多层” ('multi-layered') grows denser. This is the latest lot in the "annual call for tenders for arguable points" (Lesson 1): the question-setters have evidently taken Lesson 2 of this course**).
:::

::: warning Four pits in the examination hall
**Start by copying the materials, and finish by shouting slogans** (**and in the middle lay out three examples more: a "fake delivery" that has not performed even one contract (the official version, on the face of the script, of Lesson 1's "to list is not to reason" — the marking table has a special name for it: base-level marks**)); **Genre drift** (**a speech at the opening, an argumentative essay in the middle, a lyrical essay at the end: three voices undermine one another — once the genre is fixed, silently write on every card for an opening sentence the one line "to whom I am speaking now, and what I am saying" (the examination-hall switch for the awareness of voice in Lesson 8**)); **Fake depth** (**the comprehensiveness of “既要……又要……还要……” ('one must …, and must also …, and must still …'), which is in fact not daring to assert a position: to be dialectical is not the egalitarianism of dealing each side fifty strokes (Lesson 6: a concession must be one you can take back; to sit on the fence is only to fear a fall — "weighing" must give the weights and the reasons, otherwise "comprehensive" = abstention**)); **The plainly priced loss in handwriting and in the number of characters** (**staking the most expensive reasoned thinking on the cheapest item of deduction: the face of the script and the number of characters are "the only marks, outside the reasoning, that are 100% under one's control" — an engineering problem is given an engineering solution (in ordinary practice, practise the close at 820 characters, and in the hall the flow will of itself not run dry**).
:::

::: exercise The summary spot-read {level=1}
Write one 800-character timed essay (any real past question). When it is finished, hand in only the six opening sentences. Self-assessment: **where is the break in the spot-read? Read in a run, do the opening sentences already constitute a summary that would pass?** (the remaining paragraphs are void on the spot, and do not count)
::: solution
Marking: **the six sentences must contain one each of: the pile, the qualification, the handling of the opposing side, the mechanism, the prescription, and the close** (**a missing part = a defect in the blueprint, and has nothing to do with literary flair**); **locating the break** (**between two sentences a “因此／然而” ('therefore / however') was needed and you did not write it = the two paragraphs do not know each other (the examination-hall re-check of the connective lie-detector in Lesson 5**).
:::
:::

::: exercise Remaking a stock-fit {level=2}
Take one paragraph of a typical prefabricated essay (an opening in parallel: “当苏轼被贬……当司马迁受刑……当海伦·凯勒……” 'When Su Shi was demoted… When Sima Qian 司马迁 was put to punishment… When Helen Keller…'), and perform two operations: ① re-file the examples by mechanism (three classes: incentive / supervision / cognition); ② using the words to be negotiated on this question, join the filed parts onto the pile — write the 150 characters after the operation, and mark "what was deleted".
::: solution
Marking: **what is deleted must be more than what is newly added** (**the illness of the prefabricated essay is overload: what the three names squeeze out is your position — "delete the names, add the mechanism" is the lead surgeon in this lesson**); **the filing labels are accurate** (**if Su Shi is used for "psychological adjustment", he belongs to the cognition class; a forced join onto "supervision by the institutions" = a part mounted in the wrong place: **the on-the-scene version of sending Su Shi up in a rocket**).
:::
:::

::: exercise Short essay: after the script is handed in {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**What the examination essay teaches a person is, not so much writing, as a minimum specification for civic writing: within a public contract under a time limit, to people who do not wholly agree with you, by a means that can be checked, say clearly one sentence you dare to answer for — after the bell rings and the script is handed in, life is only the change to a larger answer-sheet**". You must call on at least six items: **the three clauses**, **the four steps in reading the question**, **eight-two-three-five-five**, **the engineering of opening sentences**, **the fake-depth clause**, **"the examination hall is a genre, not a prison"**; and rule: **are the standardised examination and reasoned writing, in the end, each other's remedy or each other's shackle — answer with one concrete scheme on the lines of "if I were the question-setter" (including one "anti-stock-fitting clause" you would set on purpose**).
::: solution
Where the marks fall: the six mechanisms (**contract, negotiation, budget, the interview, recognition of abstention, awareness of genre**); the ruling (**for reference: the remedy view — standardisation turns reasoned thinking into an action that can be accepted on inspection, and forces it to leave the salon; the shackle view — two-minute attention rewards clarity and punishes what is faint and fine, and over the long term shapes the taste on which "to be clear is to be profound"; the scheme is marked on its detail (a specimen that would pass: give a position that can be rebutted + require the candidate to quote in the essay, and to respond to, one assigned text from the opposing side + award marks separately according to 'the quality of the concession' — an example of an anti-stock-fitting clause: "any famous person who appears in the essay and who has not been activated by the keywords of this question: that example is void". Turn the prefabricated essay from a risk into an open loss — in marking the script, look at whether the scheme also abides by this lesson's contract (your scheme is itself arguable, has an opposing side, and has a way of dying: this is the final meta-examination of the whole course**); if the conclusion can be written "**only after the script is handed in does one understand: what this course ever taught was never the essay; it was a character willing to be examined again — prompts will expire, marks will lose their value, and the only thing valid for a lifetime is that 'every sentence I say keeps a button by which it can be withdrawn'**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The delivery, filed: the contract (**three clauses — task, materials, scoring: the mark looks at what you handed in**), reading the question (**circle the verb, distinguish the four types, find the words to be negotiated, list the concession slots**), the sequence of operations (**eight-two-three-five-five: checking is a second audit**), equipment (**the scaffolding may be brought in; the building must be put up on the spot: file the examples by mechanism**), character (**clarity is courtesy towards a stranger; reasoned thinking learns, inside the format, to speak softly**). One sentence closes the ten lessons: **Say it clearly, keep the accounts distinct, leave a seat for the opponent, and leave yourself a button by which to withdraw — when the bell for handing in rings, the citizen's answer-script only begins.**
:::
