---
title: "What an argument is"
---

The logic course has handed out the scales, and this chapter breaks ground to put the house up — **an argumentative essay is reasoning written as prose that someone else is willing to read to the end. Its first definition is a negative one: an argumentative essay is not an essay of '我有观点' ('I have a view'), not an essay of feeling with the punctuation changed, and still less a chorus of three slogans**.

## Definition: the debatable point {#def-kebian}

::: definition Title, claim, assertion, slogan {#def-sige}
**Title ≠ claim** (**《谈骨气》 ('On Backbone') is a title; '骨气是穷人的不动产' ('Backbone is the real property of the poor.' (Our translation.)) is a claim: the title is the ground, the claim is the stake you drive into that ground — the test: after the title, can you add '我认为……，请你不必同意' ('I hold that …; you need not agree')? What will not take the addition is only a topic ('论', '说', '谈', '浅议': a signboard of genre is not a claim; 《我的理想》 ('My Ideal') is still less of one — it assumes that you need only give an account, and do not need to argue**); **a claim = an assertion that can be argued** (**one sentence, which can be agreed with or opposed, and for which an opposing position exists ('吸烟有害健康' ('Smoking is harmful to health.' (Our translation.)) does not count as a claim today — it is common knowledge that nobody opposes: a claim must be able to lose: a proposition that cannot lose does not deserve to open an essay (the return, in Lesson 3, to qualifiers: add common knowledge back up to a concentration of dispute, and only then does it become a claim again — write '吸烟有害健康' as '公共场所禁烟的收益大于执法成本' ('The gains from banning smoking in public places outweigh the costs of enforcement.' (Our translation.)) and there is something to argue about**)); **assertion ≠ slogan** (**a slogan is the compressed biscuit of a claim: it is for spreading, not for testing ('我命由我不由天' ('My fate depends on me, not on Heaven.' (Our translation.)): a quotable line, an emoji and a repost, and the job is done — as a claim it needs three pages; as a slogan it needs only one breath: training in the argumentative essay is turning the quotable line back into a statement of liabilities**). **An essay is allowed only one overall claim** (**points standing side by side are called a collection: '中心明确' ('a clear centre') in senior-secondary composition is not a virtue but an audit requirement — there is only one overall claim, and the sub-claims are hired to work for it; if you find two overall claims, you have written two and a half essays**).
:::

::: definition The genre contract: the argumentative essay and its three neighbours {#def-hetong}
**With expository writing** (**exposition answers '是什么' ('what it is'), and does not require you to make an enemy; an argumentative essay must have an imagined opponent standing opposite (the writing version of Lesson 5's '聚焦≠同情' ('focus ≠ sympathy'): the opponent is not an enemy, but your second reader and, at the same time, your quality inspector**); **with the essay of feeling** (**the truth of an essay of feeling rests on sincerity ('我真的难过' ('I really am sad.' (Our translation.))), the truth of an argumentative essay rests on grounds ('我说的成立' ('What I say holds.' (Our translation.))) — the two may share a flat (Lu Xun's 鲁迅 *zawen* (杂文, polemical essay) is reasoning that carries body heat, Lesson 16), but the accounts must be kept apart: being moved cannot be entered in the books as evidence (the tear-receipt of Lesson 1**); **with the refutation essay** (**refutation is a special procedure of constructive argument: refute first and then establish, or refute and establish at the same time — it is not a genre but a job of work (Lesson 6 is the chapter set aside for it**)). **The overall contract** (**the covenant between the argumentative essay and the reader: I undertake ① a position that can be stated clearly ② reasons I can produce ③ honesty towards the other side — the reader undertakes ① to read to the end ② to judge by the reasons and not by personal ties: a breach can begin with the author, because the reader can leave at any moment — in the age of new media, his leaving is reckoned in milliseconds: that is why opening paragraphs today are all rewriting the 'contract negotiation' (契约谈判): the first sentence has to declare '我要跟你辩什么' ('what I mean to argue with you about.' (Our translation.))**).
:::

::: example Four openings of the same topic {#ex-siktou}
Topic: 《手机该不该管》 ('Should mobile phones be controlled?'). Four openings. Judge which contract each one signs (exposition / expression of feeling / slogan / argument), and give the fourth a mark: ① '**手机，一种二十一世纪的通讯工具。**' ('The mobile phone: a communications tool of the twenty-first century.' (Our translation.)) ② '**夜深了，屏幕还亮着，像一扇不肯关的门。**' ('The night is deep, and the screen is still lit, like a door that refuses to shut.' (Our translation.)) ③ '**管住手机，赢回人生！**' ('Get the mobile phone under control, and win your life back!' (Our translation.)) ④ '**学校普遍禁手机，但我不认为禁令提高了成绩——它只是把课堂失败改名为纪律问题。**' ('Schools generally ban mobile phones, but I do not think the ban has raised marks — it has only renamed classroom failure as a discipline problem.' (Our translation.))
::: solution
① **An expository invoice** (**the definition is correct, the risk is zero, the claim is zero: the reader will wait for you to show the stake, and patience lasts only three lines — 'opening from a dictionary definition' (从词典释义开头) is the argumentative essay's most common breach of contract: taking exposition as the starting point of reasoning often shows that the author has not yet decided whom to oppose**); ② **a contract of feeling** (**the figure is beautiful, and sincere — but what it promises is a feeling, not a reason: to use it as an opening paragraph is to borrow the argumentative essay's money to buy the goods of feeling, and the second paragraph must repay principal and interest: going on with '我的成绩因此……' ('and so my marks …') is another breach (a personal anecdote is seasoning for the evidence, not the main dish**)); ③ **a slogan** (**compressed biscuit: the tone of voice is doing the walking in place of the reasoning — a phrase such as '赢回人生' ('win your life back') is a prepaid card of rhetoric: swiping it feels good, and honouring it depends entirely on the author writing the rest in: and writing the rest in is precisely the workload of the whole essay**); ④ **signing the contract** (**debatable (someone asserts that the ban is effective), single (the relation between the ban and marks), with a place left ready for the opposing side (the three characters '普遍禁' ('a general ban') acknowledge how things stand — the trace of a concession is there from the first sentence), and the structure of the whole essay is implied (first establish the factual layer of '没提高成绩' ('it did not raise marks'), then argue the mechanism layer of '改名为纪律问题' ('renamed as a discipline problem'): the planning of the piece in Lesson 4 begins at the fourth sentence**); **the mark: ④ full marks — note that its tone is not forceful, and even carries a concession: the sign of a good claim is not a high volume, but that the opponent can find himself on the same page**.
:::
:::

::: intuition You are not giving a speech; you are posting a registered letter {#int-guahao}
**The model of oratory is a crowd; the model of the argumentative essay is a registered letter that will be forwarded** (**a reader will read it selectively, forward it to an opponent, screenshot your weak points, and dig it out three years later — so every sentence must withstand '被最有能力误解你的人转述' ('being retold by the person most capable of misunderstanding you'): this is the writing version of the steel-man principle: first examine yourself by the reading that is at once the most malicious and the most intelligent**). **The dividend that logic pays to writing** (**said once, it is forgotten; written down, it can be audited: writing fixes a momentary emotion into accounts that can be checked — this is precisely the obsession that runs in one line from Han Fei's 韩非 *nan* form (难体, 'raising difficulties') to the Toulmin layout: turning the giving of reasons from the weather of speech into the geology of the page (Lesson 8, the rendezvous of the *nan* form and the six elements: why the arts of debate all, in the end, need stylistics: because persuasion has listeners, and reasoning has a reader**).
:::

::: history Four professions of the argumentative essay {#sec-zhiye}
**3rd–2nd centuries BC** roving persuaders and the *nan* form (**Han Fei 韩非, the *Nan* chapters (《难》): quote the other side's position paragraph by paragraph, then take it apart paragraph by paragraph — the earliest 'refutation-form' (反驳体) composition template in history: its existence shows that there were already 'standard claims' (标准论点) available to raise difficulties against (Lesson 8: every age has its own stock of 'debatable points'**); **Han** policy essays and memorials (**Jia Yi 贾谊, 'On the Faults of Qin' (《过秦论》), establishes the claim by force of momentum; Chao Cuo 晁错, the memorial 'On Valuing Grain' (《论贵粟疏》), establishes the claim by doing the accounts — two original tracks of the Chinese argumentative essay: the pitch school and the counting-house school, which to this day divide the gold and warm the wine, each holding one side**); **Tang–Qing** the examination-hall *lun* (论, 'discourse') and *ce* (策, 'policy essay') (**'On Discourse and Persuasion' in *The Literary Mind and the Carving of Dragons* (《文心雕龙·论说》) laid down the rule early: '**义贵圆通，辞忌枝碎**' ('In the meaning, what is prized is rounded completeness; in the wording, what is shunned is branch-like fragmentation.' (Our translation.)) (the discourse must be well rounded = no dead angle; the wording must shun fragmentation = do not show off technique) — **the 'small topic' (小题) training of the eight-legged essay (八股文) (on '学而时习之' ('To learn, and at the proper times to practise it.' (Our translation.)), writing three hundred passages of *poti* (破题, 'breaking the topic')) drilled one principle and many headings into a national muscle, and also tamed the debatable point into speaking on the sages' behalf (代圣立言): the two-sided legacy of the argumentative essay inside the civil-service examinations (Lesson 9, the remote ancestor of the examination hall: examinations shape the genre, and the genre moulds thought in return**)); **1902–1920s** policy essays in the newspapers (**Liang Qichao's 梁启超 'new prose' (新文体)**, '**笔锋常带情感**' ('The tip of the brush often carries feeling.' (Our translation.))? — the usual account: 其文纵笔所略不检束 ('His writing lets the brush go and is somewhat unrestrained.' (Our translation.)) — **feeling and logic went into partnership on the newspaper: circulation is the number-one reader; Hu Shi 胡适 (Hu Shih) changed the guiding principle to '作文章如说话' ('Write prose as one speaks.' (Our translation.)): the *chuyi* form (刍议体) lowers the stance to *chu* (刍, 'one who cuts grass'), and the authority of reasoning is moved from the sages to method (the twin event of Lesson 16's '白话不是被杀死是被停止报销' ('The vernacular was not killed; it was stopped from being reimbursed.' (Our translation.)): a coup in genre**); **1977–the present** the examination room (**the gaokao (高考) restored: the argumentative essay became the writing mother tongue of a hundred million people — every June the whole country answers on the same topic: this is the largest regular qualifying examination in argument in the world, and also the annual tender for 'debatable points': '本手妙手俗手' ('the proper move, the brilliant move, the vulgar move'), '故事的力量' ('the power of stories') — topics come out year after year, which is tantamount to issuing you a letter commissioning a motion**).
:::

::: warning Four pits at the start of the work
**Dictionary-opening dependency** (**'XX，字典里解释为……' ('XX, explained in the dictionary as …') — treating exposition as a safe zone: after three lines the reader knows that what you mean to argue is something nobody opposes: open from consensus, and move towards the dispute; do not open from a definition, and move towards a blank**); **examples standing duty for the claim** (**Edison at the beginning, Sima Qian 司马迁 in the middle, Touching China (感动中国) at the end: between the three stories there is no reinforcing bar of reasoning — giving examples is one procedure of reasoning, not the whole of it (Lesson 3): between the examples there must be a sentence of '因此说明' ('and this therefore shows'): without that sentence, the essay is a shelf, not a warehouse**); **pretending there is no opponent** (**one-way output from start to finish: the most dangerous reader is dismissed — real writing keeps a seat for him ('有人会说……但我还认为……' ('Someone will say …; but I still hold …') is not a show of weakness, but a structural part: the concession sentence is a beam in a load-bearing wall (Lesson 6)**); **taking 'significance' for 'proof'** (**saying '这件事很重要' ('This matter is very important.' (Our translation.)) three times — importance is not truth: value and fact are two ledgers (Lesson 1's distinction of '是/应当' ('is / ought')): what you argue is '对不对' ('right or not'), not '重不重要' ('important or not'): many essays that leave the topic have not gone far; they have only replaced proof with emphasis**).
:::

::: exercise Driving the stake {level=1}
For each of five topics, drive one **debatable stake** (a one-sentence claim, plus one sentence of 'who would oppose it, and why'): ① 《谈韧》 ('On tenacity'); ② 《 AI 与作业》 ('AI and homework'); ③ 《远方的风景》 ('The view far away'); ④ 《规则与人情》 ('Rules and human feeling'); ⑤ 《熬夜晚点名》 ('Staying up late, and the evening roll-call').
::: solution
Marking: **the stake must be able to lose** (**'韧是美德' ('Tenacity is a virtue.' (Our translation.)) does not qualify — nobody opposes it; '逆境中‘放弃训练’比坚持更能提高成功率，因为我们从未为放弃设计过课程' ('In adversity, "training in giving up" raises the success rate more than perseverance does, because we have never designed a course for giving up.' (Our translation.)) qualifies: it dares people to pick holes at once**); **the opposing side must be concrete** (**not '有人可能不同意' ('someone might disagree'), but '班主任与家长委员会都会反对，理由是课堂秩序优先于个体节律' ('Both the class teacher and the parents' committee would oppose it, on the ground that classroom order takes priority over an individual's rhythm.' (Our translation.)) — the more concrete the opposing side, the easier what follows is to write: cheating in writing often begins with a vague audience**).
:::
:::

::: exercise Surgery on the opening paragraph {level=2}
Take an opening paragraph from an old piece of your own (or write an imitation), and perform three operations: rewrite it as an expository opening, as a slogan opening, and as a **contract-style opening that qualifies** (including one concession sentence); with fifty annotations, set out what each of the three versions gains and loses.
::: solution
Marking: **gains and losses must be asymmetrical** (**the expository version gains safety and loses by having no stake; the slogan version gains traffic and loses honouring; the contract version gains the spending of two extra lines to seat the opponent — a candidate who writes that 'cost' out as two lines shows that he really understands the contract is not decoration**); in the annotations, '更好' ('better') and '更生动' ('more vivid') are forbidden (**replace the adjectives, and write the mechanism: which sentence is working for whom**).
:::
:::

::: exercise A short essay: on the inflation of the 'debatable point' {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**当一个社会把所有问题都变成‘观点之争’，可辩之点就通货膨胀了：连重力该不该存在都可以‘我觉得’——救币的办法不是禁止表态，是恢复兑付：每个‘我认为’后面要能跟出‘因为’，以及‘怎样算我说错’**" ('When a society turns every problem into a "clash of views", the debatable point inflates: even whether gravity ought to exist can be met with "I feel" — the way to save the currency is not to forbid taking a stand, but to restore honouring: after every "I hold" one must be able to follow with "because", and with "what would count as my being wrong".' (Our translation.)). You must call on at least six items: **title ≠ claim**, **the slogan's obligation to be honoured**, **a seat left for the opponent**, **'importance ≠ truth'**, **the structural preview in the fourth kind of opening**, **the 'annual tender' after 1977**; and decide: **is an age in which everyone can issue a view a golden age of reasoning, or an age of bad debts (your proposal must include one discipline that 'the author himself observes first')**.
::: solution
Marking points: the six mechanisms (**driving the stake, honouring, leaving a seat, separating the accounts, structure, the tender**); the decision (**for reference: the golden-age view — the cost of checking falls, and more than one party being present is itself progress; the bad-debt view — taking a stand at zero cost produces bad money; the marking looks at the proposal (an example of a 'discipline of honouring': attach to every assertion a condition on which it could be falsified — '我认为作业应限总量，若本校试点后睡眠时间未升而焦虑未降，我即收回' ('I hold that the total amount of homework should be capped; if, after a pilot at this school, time asleep has not risen and anxiety has not fallen, I withdraw the claim at once.' (Our translation.)): whoever cannot write this sentence is automatically downgraded to a mood diary — the freedom of '我觉得' ('I feel') has, as its reserve, the obligation of '我怎样算错' ('how I count as wrong'): Central Mum (央妈) does not issue currency; what it issues is verifiability**); if the conclusion can write "**议论文的全部尊严在于它随时可以被反驳，并且作者把这个‘随时’写在了明处：这就是开头段的工作——先告诉世界：我在这儿，我错在哪儿**" ('The whole dignity of the argumentative essay is that it can be refuted at any time, and that the author has written this "at any time" in the open: that is the work of the opening paragraph — first tell the world: I am here, and this is where I am wrong.' (Our translation.)), it is an A.
:::

::: summary
Filing the start of the work. Four items (**the title is the ground, the claim is the stake, the slogan is a compressed biscuit, one essay and one guiding principle**); the contract (**promise the reader a position, reasons and honesty, and leave a seat for the opponent**); the opening (**move from consensus towards the dispute; the concession sentence of the fourth kind of opening is a beam, not a scar**); the history of the professions (**the *nan* form — the policy essay — the examination hall — the newspaper — the examination room: examinations shape the genre, and the genre moulds thought in return**). One sentence of method: **drive a stake that can afford to lose, then start the work**. The next chapter is the further working of how a claim is established: the types of an assertion, and its strength.
:::
