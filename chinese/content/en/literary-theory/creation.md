title: "The creative process"

The previous two chapters examined the defendant (literature) and the plaintiff (society). This chapter calls the third witness: **the writer himself**. The trouble is that this witness's testimony **comes entirely from memory, and memory is the clerk most given to making up stories** — the theory of creation is therefore a peculiar discipline: **its primary materials are all depositions, and there is not a single monitor**.

## Testimony one: inspiration {#def-lingan}

::: definition The lodestone rings and the order of opening and blockage {#def-ciyi}
**Imported testimony**: Plato's *Ion* — the poet is a madman **possessed by the god of poetry**; the lodestone draws the rings, and god—poet—rhapsode—audience are one chain of iron rings: “**你并非凭技艺，而是凭神说讲这些**” ('**you do not speak these things by craft, but by a god's speaking**') (Our translation.) — **the external-source account of inspiration: the creator is a channel, not a source** (a side effect of this receipt: **the author's talent is cancelled, and the right of explanation is handed up to the god — the ancestral property, two thousand years later, of the "death of the author"**). **Domestic testimony**: Lu Ji 陆机, in the *Wen fu* 文赋 ('Rhapsody on Literature'), writes of inspiration coming and going: “**来不可遏，去不可止；藏则影去，舒则声寄**” ('**when it comes it cannot be held back; when it goes it cannot be stopped; hidden, the shadow departs; released, the sound is given a lodging**') (the gist) (Our translation.) — **the first phenomenology of creation in Chinese literary theory: it does not explain where the god comes from; it only describes the feel of opening and of blockage** (“应感之会，通塞之纪，不可常居，理不可推” ('the meeting of stimulus and response, the order of opening and blockage, cannot be dwelt in as a constant abode; the principle cannot be inferred') (Our translation.)); Liu Xie 刘勰, in 'Spirit Thought' (《神思》, *shénsī*), supplies the operating manual: “**陶钧文思，贵在虚静；疏瀹五藏，澡雪精神**” ('**in turning literary thought on the wheel, what is prized is emptiness and stillness; dredge the five viscera, and wash the spirit clean as snow**') (Our translation.) + “**思接千载**” ('**thought joins a thousand years**') (Our translation.), “**视通万里**” ('**sight runs ten thousand *li***') (Our translation.) — ***xūjìng* 虚静 ('emptiness and stillness') is not metaphysics; it is a procedure for clearing interference: the whole equipment list of the study of inspiration comes to two sentences: clear the cache (*xujing*), let association fly (*shensi*, spirit and thought)**. Read together, the two testimonies: **one says "I said it, and it was not I who said it"; the other says "I know it will leave — it cannot be kept, but one can remember what it looked like when it came": the former is theology, the latter is phenomenology — this lesson takes only the latter as its teaching text, and takes the former as a specimen**.
:::

## Testimony two: the stage chart {#def-jieduan}

::: definition Four stages: the statistics of memoirs {#def-sijieduan}
Planck, in a lecture, describes the birth of atomic physics: the anxiety of a long **preparation** → the withdrawal of **contemplation** → an **insight** beside a hotel window ("counter-arguments proved useless, a guiding idea came to me in a flash", the gist) → the ordering of **verification** — the history of science later handed this chart (preparation—incubation—insight—verification) on to the whole psychology of creativity; **its lifespan and its weakness are of the same rank: all four stages come from the memoirs of those who succeeded — those who failed supply no sample, and a rejected thought does not keep a diary** (**survivorship bias + reconstruction after the fact: the narrative of insight is sexy precisely because it compresses three months of blur into a single frame: the film version of inspiration**). **The literary version of the four stages has an honest variant of its own**: Wordsworth, “**诗起源于平静中回忆起来的情感**” ('**poetry originates in emotion recollected in tranquillity**') (Our translation.) (the Preface to *Lyrical Ballads* — **the four stages are rewritten as "emotion—settling—recollection—combination": insight changes its name, and is called recollection; and improvisation on the spot is explicitly refused — the Romantics, with their own hands, set a seal of calm on the Dionysian licence they had given themselves**).
:::

::: definition Automatic writing and its insomnia {#def-zidong}
In 1924 Breton issued Dionysus a uniform: the Surrealist manifesto defines pure **psychic automatism** — “**思维的笔录，不受任何理性控制、外在于一切美学与道德**” ('**a written record of thought, under no control by any reason, and outside all aesthetics and morality**') (Our translation.) — and the experiments of the *Magnetic Fields* kind (《磁场》) had two people write by turns without sleeping, trying to **let the hand outrun the censor**. **The result of the experiment**: a considerable yield of novelty, and an absurdity that could not be kept up — **the censor is more loyal than was imagined: the moment the hand stops, the censor wakes; and the sense of novelty expires, all together, on the third page (automatic writing in the end lived on as a "device": random juxtaposition, images from dreams — a rhetoric that can be imitated, not a channel that can be run)**. **The judgement of the theory**: automatic writing falsified "cancel the censorship and you connect straight through to the rich vein", but it confirmed the other half — **the censor (the inner editor) does exist, and its volume can be adjusted**: **the real question for a theory of creation has never been 'whether there is inspiration', it is 'where the knob for the censor's volume is' — Jia Dao 贾岛 weighing one word against another (*tuīqiāo* 推敲) and Breton's exemption from sleep are the extreme marks on one and the same knob**.
:::

## Testimony three: craft {#def-shouyi}

::: definition Cut, chase, revise {#def-shanzhui}
**Cutting (the right word)**: the gospel of Flaubert's letters — “**一个名词只有一个正确的词，一个动词只有一个正确的词，一个形容词只有一个独特的词：找不到它，你就得一直找**” ('**for a noun there is only one right word, for a verb only one right word, for an adjective only one distinctive word: if you cannot find it, you have to keep looking**') (Our translation.) (the received account of *le mot juste*) + “**才华就是漫长的耐心**” ('**talent is a long patience**') (Our translation.) — **the natural enemy of the study of inspiration: genius is rewritten as the bodily labour of looking for words**. Cao Xueqin 曹雪芹, “**批阅十载，增删五次**” ('**gone over for ten years, added to and cut five times**') (Our translation.) (chapter 1 of *Dream of the Red Chamber*) — **the Chinese record of carrying out the same clause: ten years and five times are the accounting period of cutting**. **Chasing (the time lag of *chéngzhú* 成竹, 'the bamboo already formed')**: Su Shi 苏轼 records Wen Tong 文同 painting bamboo — “**故画竹必先得成竹于胸中**，执笔熟视，乃见其所欲画者，**急起从之，振笔直遂，以追其所见，如兔起鹘落，少纵则逝矣**” ('**Thus, to paint bamboo, one must first have the completed bamboo in the breast**; one takes up the brush and looks long, and then sees what one wishes to paint; **one starts up at once and follows it, lifts the brush and drives straight on, so as to chase what one has seen, like a hare breaking cover and a falcon dropping — give it the least slack and it is gone**') (Our translation.) — and **in the same piece Su Shi confesses his own failure**: “**心识其所以然，而不能至者，内外不一，心手不相应**” ('**the mind knows why it is so, and yet cannot arrive: inside and outside are not one, and heart and hand do not answer**') (Our translation.) — **the theory of the completed bamboo comes with its own failure clause: the gestalt in the breast cannot catch up with the speed of the hand; the five characters 心手不相应 are the original wording of the contract for all the anxiety of creation**. **Revising (the fossil of a choice)**: the case of the drafts revised over the character 绿 (春风又**绿**江南岸 ('the spring wind **greens** the south bank of the river again') (Our translation.) — 到 ('arrive'), 过 ('pass'), 入 ('enter'), 满 ('fill'), trial and error the whole way along) — ***liànzì* 炼字 ('refining the word') = running the method of substitution at the site of creation: the traces of revision are the only fossil record of the literary process** (Jia Dao, “**二句三年得，一吟双泪流**” ('**two lines took three years to get; one chant, and the tears fall in two streams**') (Our translation.), and Du Fu 杜甫, “**为人性僻耽佳句，语不惊人死不休**” ('**by nature perverse, I am addicted to the fine line; if the words do not startle, I will not rest even in death**') (Our translation.) — **the shop signs of the school of bitter chanting (*kǔyín* 苦吟), bound in one volume: inspiration is a membership, not compulsory education**).
:::

::: example The pond grows spring grass: a retrial of a case of inspiration {#ex-chuncao}
Xie Lingyun 谢灵运, “**池塘生春草**，园柳变鸣禽” ('**spring grass grows by the pond**; the willows in the garden change their singing birds') (Our translation.) — tradition has it that he himself said of this line, “**此语有神助，非我语也**” ('**these words had divine aid; they are not my words**') (Our translation.) (the legend of divine aid is quoted at second hand through the *Yanshi jiaxun* 《颜氏家训》 ('Family Instructions of the Yan Clan') and other books; the line of transmission is rather complicated — **the position on the materials: this question examines only the "attribution of inspiration" itself**); another case: Jiang Yan 江淹, “**才尽**” ('**talent spent**') — the legend that he dreamed of someone demanding the five-coloured brush back — **the mythology of 江郎才尽 ('Jianglang's talent is exhausted')**. Please: ① which **structure of attribution** the two cases share (and whether Wordsworth's structure of "recollection" is the same model); ② use the four-stage chart to write in, for "the pond and the spring grass", the first two stages it omits (of which kind of preparation period it is the product); ③ if you were Xie Lingyun's agent, how you would publicise "divine aid" to the outside, and how you would train on the inside.
::: solution
① **Attribution to an external warehouse**: inspiration = goods drawn from "outside me" (god / dream / brush) — **its function is double insurance: a waiver of liability when one succeeds (it was not I who said it, it was the god who said it = the goods are raised in price and the competitors are pressed down, at the same time), and a waiver of liability when one fails (the warehouse is out of stock; it is no fault of the fighting)**; Wordsworth's "recollection" is a **secular version** of the same structure: the warehouse is moved from Olympus into the unconscious, and the key is changed from a divine grant to time. ② Filling in preparation—incubation: Xie Lingyun is a **production-line worker of landscape poetry** (a great quantity of viewing—keeping accounts—finishing the piece, on the same subjects and in the same form — "**the incubation period is built on preparation in excess: divine aid favours only the highly productive**" — a Romanticism of statistics: “非用心所至” ('not reached by taking pains'), translated, comes out as “不像是用心所至” ('it does not look as if it was reached by taking pains') = **the degree of automation is too high**). ③ The double ledger of the agent and the coach: **outward, sell the miracle (the narrative of inspiration = the brand); inward, drill the attendance (the four stages = the rota)** — **both pages of the real business licence of the creation industry are required: cancel either page and it closes down (selling only divine aid = a fraudulent insurance claim; speaking only of attendance = a daybook)**.
:::

::: intuition The black box and the interface {#int-heixiang}
The honest self-placement of the psychology of creation: **the black box cannot be opened (introspective reports contradict one another; experiment can only give averages), but the interface panel is standardised**: *xujing* (settings for attention), *shensi* (the mode of association), *míkuáng* 迷狂 ('frenzy') (the level of arousal), *kuyin* (the threshold of editing), insight (an accident of retrieval), the completed bamboo (inner modelling and the hand's chase) — **the six sets of concepts are nameplates of one and the same machine, facing different ways: Chinese criticism chose a vocabulary of atmosphere, Western psychology a vocabulary of cybernetics, and both sides are speaking of "the censor's volume", "the capacity of the cache", "the timing of retrieval"** (Cook Ding's cutting-up of the ox supplies a description of the end state: “**以神遇而不以目视，官知止而神欲行**” ('**I meet it by the spirit and do not look with the eyes; the knowing of the senses stops, and the spirit's desire goes on**') (Our translation.) — **the reverse of heart and hand not answering: skill advancing into the Way = the feel of it running once every interface has been levelled — note that this is a report from the body after the fact, not an operating manual that can be carried out beforehand: this is the common fault of every description of a *jìngjiè* 境界 ('realm'), and also the reason such a description cannot be replaced: the black box cannot be opened, but the lamp that shows it running is lit**).
:::

::: history The scheduling of the testimonies {#sec-paiqi}
**4th century BC**, the lodestone rings — **a theological licence**; **late in the 3rd century**, Lu Ji's *Wen fu* — **the phenomenology of opening and blockage powers on**; **501**, the chapter 'Spirit Thought' — **the interface terms are fixed in their final form (*xujing* / *shensi* / *qíngmào* 情貌, 'the look of feeling' / *cíling* 辞令, 'phrasing')**; **11th century**, Ouyang Xiu 欧阳修 transmits “**诗穷而后工**” ('**poetry grows skilful only after the poet is straitened**') (Our translation.) (the words of Mei Yaochen 梅尧臣 — **suffering as a compulsory financing of the preparation period: an important page in the history of attribution: one's lot is written up as fuel, and the institution is let off the bill**) + the theory of the completed bamboo, and the regret of heart and hand; **16th century**, the theory of the "**child mind**" (Li Zhi 李贽, 'On the Child Mind', 《童心说》 — **false persons and false words: a social name for the obstacle to creation: the censor is recognised as wearing the uniform of *lǐjiào* 礼教, 'the teaching of the rites'**); **17th century**, Pu Songling 蒲松龄, in the *Liaozhai zizhi* 《聊斋自志》 ('The Author's Own Account'), says of himself “孤愤” ('solitary indignation', *gufen*) — **a joint signature of talent and indignation**; **18th–19th centuries**, genius—imagination—inspiration are raised to a national religion of the Romantics (Kant, “天才给艺术立法” ('genius legislates for art') (Our translation.) — **in the 18th century the concept of "genius" becomes the spiritual twin of copyright law — is it a coincidence that the theory of genius and the law of copyright were born at the same time?**); **1924**, automatic writing — **the coup against the censor does not succeed**; **1945/1960s**, the four-stage chart enters the textbooks, and the psychology of creativity enters the laboratory (Guilford, "divergent thinking" — **insight is rewritten as items that can be measured: inspiration turned into an IQ test, large in sales and small in explanation**); **now**: **the writing workshop and the AI co-pilot institutionalise "revision": the creative-writing degree + the age of human–machine collaboration — "inspiration" retires to the brand department, and "process" takes over the production department: the job description of the theory of creation and the object it studies come to a settlement — both admit that what they are is process management**.
:::

::: warning Four pits in the theory of creation
**Taking the narrative of inspiration for a production process** (**"divine aid", "talent spent", "a line got in a single dream" are all copy for release: the way this lesson reads them — ask for whom the copy waives liability**); **taking "genius" as an explanans** (**the theory of genius cannot explain a single poem; it only explains why there are people who do not learn to write poetry: it is an honorific for laziness in attribution — Kant is its most respectable speech for the defence, not evidence**); **the cult of bitter chanting** (**"two lines took three years to get" is both a fact and marketing: slow is not the same as good; Jia Dao and Li Bai 李白 were of the same age, and neither apologised to the other — the rate at which hours of work exchange for quality differs by author, and cannot be transferred from one bank to another**); **closing the case in a single sentence, "method can be taught, talent cannot"** (**the very reason a degree in creative writing exists is this: what can be taught is not, it is true, the source, but the discipline of cutting, the tuning of the interface, and the ear of the one who reads the draft — to take what cannot be taught as a wall is to open a museum as a gymnasium**).
:::

::: exercise A method for keeping inspiration's accounts {level=1}
Record three “灵光” ('flashes') of yours within one week (any scene of creation), and each time mark four items: **the preceding task / the length of the interval / the trigger / the degree to which it survived in the end**; at the end of the month, add them up: behind which operation your "divine aid" is most likely hiding.
::: solution
Marking: **all four items present**; **an honest sample** (beautifying the trigger after the fact is not allowed — **"it came to me in the bath" is, more often than not, a disguise for the seventh rereading of the draft**); a conclusion that passes has a form such as "my insights cluster when the interval is 2–3 days and the preceding task is definite — **my divine aid has a record of attendance**".
:::

::: solution

::: exercise Short essay: is creativity a resource or a process? {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**the word 'creativity' is the greatest placebo the twentieth century invented: it renames a process that can be practised as a gift that cannot be divided, so that the mediocre may look inborn**". You must draw on at least five items: **the lodestone rings (attribution to an external source)**, **the survivorship bias of the four stages**, **the censor's knob**, **the financing narrative of “诗穷而后工” ('poetry grows skilful only after the poet is straitened')**, **the birth, in the same age, of genius and of copyright**; and you must deal with one opposing argument: **if creativity is only a process, why does the same training produce only one batch of people — a theory of process can explain the distribution, and cannot explain the peak**.
::: solution
Marking points: the five mechanisms (**external source = a waiver of liability and a raising of the price; the stage chart = the statistics of memoirs; the knob = the name of the part that can be taught; straitened, and only then skilful = writing one's lot as fuel and letting the institution off the bill; the theory of genius = naturalising the scarcity of the market**); the handling of the opposition (**two reference routes: ① the peak = the product of process × uncontrollable variables — a theory of process does not promise that everyone has the same value; it only promises that uncontrollable items and teachable items are kept on separate accounts: to credit the peak to "gift" and then stop the research is precisely the substance of the placebo effect; ② "distribution" is itself a problem of field theory: the same training is exchanged, at different bureaux of exchange (a position in the literary world, the market in a language), for a different output — half of "only one batch of people comes out" is a product of the way the accounts are kept**); if the conclusion can be written "**genius is the name of the column that was never written down on a rota: to study it is not as good as going and writing the rota — but to deny that the column exists is also nothing but arrogance**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The three testimonies, filed: inspiration (**the lodestone rings versus the order of opening and blockage: one says "it was not I"; the other says "I know it will leave" — this lesson takes the latter as its teaching text, and the former as a specimen**), the stages (**preparation—incubation—insight—verification: the memoirs of survivors + the Romantics' operation of changing the name**), craft (**cutting: ten years and five times; chasing: heart and hand do not answer; revising: the fossil of the character 绿 — the volume of the censor is the only knob that really exists**), the black box (**six nameplates, one machine; the lamp that shows it running is lit, and the lid cannot be opened**). Method, in one sentence: **when you hear "divine aid", ask for whom it waives liability**. The previous chapter was the place of society; the next chapter is the levels of the work — see you on the dissecting table.
