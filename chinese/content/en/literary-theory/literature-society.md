---
title: "Literature and society"
---

The previous chapter put "literature" in the dock; this chapter changes the plaintiff: **society**. For two thousand years the job application society handed to literature listed only one post: **mirror** — this chapter follows that form through its four revisions (mirror, searchlight, lamp, bureau of exchange), and closes the case on a more respectable post: **sensor**.

## The four revisions of the mirror {#def-jingzi}

::: definition The first generation: a mirror of universality {#def-jing1}
The birth certificate of the mirror theory is not "depicting reality", but Aristotle's **edict of elevation**: "**诗比历史更哲学、更严肃：历史说他者说了什么，诗说它可能说什么**" ("**Poetry is more philosophical and more serious than history: history says what the other said; poetry says what it might say.**" (Our translation.)) (the gist of chapter 9 of the *Poetics*) — **poetry does not copy out the individual case; it sets possibilities in order: the first version of the mirror theory is precisely opposed to "photography"** (this layer of the original sense is often forgotten: **Aristotle is the ancestor, inside reflection theory, who least resembles reflection theory**). The contemporaneous document on the Chinese line is **observing the airs to know government**: the poem-collecting institution of the *Book of Songs*, and "**诗可以观**" ("poetry can be used to observe") (the *Analects* — **poetry was appointed an observation station for the popular will: from this was approved the civil-service establishment, across two thousand years, of "文变染乎世情" ("literary change is dyed by the conditions of the age" (Our translation.)) (*The Literary Mind and the Carving of Dragons*, 'Sequence of the Times')**) — **the Western mirror reflects possibility; the Chinese mirror reflects the condition of the people: two generations of the product share one pane of glass (imitation), and the uses for which they leave the factory differ**.
:::

::: definition The second generation: the typical and the searchlight {#def-jing2}
**The theory of the typical** (Engels on realism: "**除细节的真实外，还要真实地再现典型环境中的典型人物**" ("**Besides truth of detail, one must also truthfully reproduce typical characters in typical circumstances.**" (Our translation.))) — **the mirror is upgraded to a palette: reality is to be "reproduced", not "copied"; characters are to be "typical", not "specimens"**; the famous case that goes with it is the **Balzac case**: a royalist writer wrote a materialist history of the French economy — **Engels himself said that he had learned more economic detail from the novels than from all the professional statistics put together: realism here signed that letter of pardon, "political views and creation can be separated"** (it later became the **evidence for reversing the verdict** most often cited in the theory of realism: the author's standpoint gave away the truth he opposed — **the text knows the road better than its driver**). **The searchlight version** (Zola and naturalism: the novelist = the operator of a social laboratory, **recording the facts and demonstrating their causes** — **the mirror changed costume and took on a light source: it does not shine things out passively, but actively** — and the question moves forward with it: **in whose hand the switch is, what is lit, and which darknesses are left**).
:::

::: definition The third generation: the lamp and engagement {#def-jing3}
Lu Xun 鲁迅's comparison splits the post with precision: "**文艺是国民精神所发的火光，同时也是引导国民精神的前途的灯火**" ("**Literature and art are the flame given off by the spirit of the nation, and at the same time the lamp that guides the spirit of the nation on the road ahead.**" (Our translation.)) ('On Looking with Open Eyes') — **one sentence stuffs in the household registrations of two families, reflection theory (the flame) and functional theory (the lamp): this is precisely the draft constitution of the twentieth-century Chinese view of literature**. **The theory of engagement** (Sartre: writing is an engagement with the age, **prose is a kind of action** — the writer "**把自己的语言选为武器；写作因此是“选择被谁读”**" ("**chooses one's own language as a weapon; writing is therefore 'choosing by whom one is read'**" (Our translation.))) — **the mirror is formally transferred to the post of a tool**; the other side of the book is the **school of aestheticism**: "**生活模仿艺术远甚于艺术模仿生活**" ("**Life imitates art far more than art imitates life.**" (Our translation.)) (Wilde, 'The Decay of Lying' — **the famous line of anti-mirror theory: it is not that poetry reflects life, but that life gets through its days on poetry's mould, because people first learn to cry in postures the poets invented**). What the two sides are really quarrelling about is not "whether literature resembles society", but **"who imitates whom" = who holds the power to define: the mirror theory was always an epistemology; the school of aestheticism rewrote it as a struggle for control**.
:::

::: definition The fourth generation: the bureau of exchange (the literary field) {#def-jing4}
Bourdieu's sociology does not look in a mirror; **it overturns the dressing table**: the "**literary field**" = a relatively autonomous small world running by its own laws: **economic capital and symbolic capital are inversely exchanged here** (**selling well = the risk of being devalued inside the circle; poor, yet authoritative = a kind of deposit**) — "art for art's sake" is no longer an aestheticist slogan, but **the historical fruit of the field's battle for autonomy** ("**纯粹艺术信仰与纯粹艺术生产场是同一场斗争的两个产物**" ("**Faith in pure art, and the field of production of pure art, are two products of one and the same struggle.**" (Our translation.)) — a stable paraphrase); thus "society reflects literature" is rewritten as "**what literature reflects is the literary world**: the subject matter, the reputation and the orthodoxy over which writers compete are themselves a function of position inside the field" (**being avant-garde = a strategy of distinction directed against the orthodoxy of the previous generation**). **This lesson's ruling**: field theory does not fight the first three generations — **it explains "why the mirror changes its plating every twenty years": a dispute over plating has always been a dispute over positions inside the field** (in the China of the 1930s the theory of the typical overwhelmed expressionism; victory and defeat in the debate between Lukács and Brecht were received and dispatched with the camps: **it is not which mirror is truer, but which side holds the museum**).
:::

::: example A new hearing of the Balzac letter of pardon {#ex-balzac}
Take Engels's Balzac precedent and try it again as a dossier in court: ① "**the text knows the road better than the driver**" — what relation does this **exemption clause for the author's confession** bear to the theory of form in the previous chapter (literariness = a linguistic device) and to the theory of creation in the next (the intentional fallacy): an alliance, or a division of the spoils? ② If the mechanism by which a standpoint gives away the truth holds, **does "literature reflects society" in fact explain only half of a masterpiece, the other half (language, form, genre) being stocked entirely by other theories**? ③ Give a definition of "sensor" to replace "reflection": **genre literature = a sensor of social anxiety — which anxiety zombies, detective fiction and martial-arts fiction each pick up; deformation and distortion are themselves the reading**. Each of the three questions in no more than 100 characters.
::: solution
① **A three-way split of the spoils**: Engels and the formalists share one **anti-intentionalism** (the text ≠ the author's political views), but Engels books the source of the "textual surplus" to the **reality** account, the formalists book it to the **device** account, and the theorists of the intentional fallacy book it to the **readers' agreement** account — **one phenomenon, three title deeds; this chapter admits that all three houses hold a key** ("**the text's excess of knowledge**": the novel knows more than the author; to whom the extra ought to belong is the boundary stone of each school). ② Admitted: **the explanatory radius of reflection theory = subject matter, characters and relations; about "why this syntax and this genre" it is basically speechless** — **this is precisely the way in for formalism's counter-strike: society explains content, form explains form, and the peace treaty of the two shifts is the foundation of the twentieth-century textbook (the sequence of lessons in this textbook defaults to this treaty: sociology first, narratology after — see the contract in the next chapter)**. ③ A sample of sensor readings: **martial-arts fiction = the demand for justice in a period when the rule of law is a vacuum + an imagined outlet for identity on the move; detective fiction = the modern faith that "procedural justice can restore the truth"; zombies = indiscriminate collective risk (epidemics, consumerism, population)** — **the crux is that distortion is information: the shape one comes to fear shows what, then and there, was taken as the shape of "evil" — "reflection" says likeness; the sensor says a ringing; a likeness can deceive, a ringing cannot** ("**Literature is not society's mirror; it is its tinnitus: it restores no sound, but it records with precision which nerve is degenerating**" — the closing argument).
:::
:::

::: intuition Imbalance is the norm {#def-bupei}
Marx, in passing, left the most useful back door: **the unbalanced relation between artistic production and material production** — "**希腊艺术仍然是高不可及的范本**：某种艺术繁荣与社会的‘发达’并不同步" ("**Greek art remains a model too high to reach**: the flourishing of a certain art does not keep step with a society's 'development'." (Our translation.)) (the gist of the 'Introduction' to *A Contribution to the Critique of Political Economy*) — **this exception clause later fed half the world of theory: why do ages of war produce epics, and ages of modest prosperity lyric? Why is an economic lowland often a literary plateau (nineteenth-century Russia; China in the early years of reform and opening)?** **A model for the answer**: society supplies **material and anxiety**, the literary field supplies **a stock of forms**, and the mix of the two changes with fashion, institutions and the turn of generations — **"imbalance" is not a hole in reflection theory; it is its adaptive cruise control: the real cause of death of the mirror theory has never been that it cannot be talked through, but that anything at all can be said with it (it is unfalsifiable)**.
:::

::: history How the job description changed {#sec-shuoming}
**The 4th century BC** observing the airs / poetry can be used to observe — **the observation station's establishment**; **Han–Tang**: "**文章合为时而著，歌诗合为事而作**" ("**Writings should be composed for the times; songs and poems should be made for events.**" (Our translation.)) (Bai Juyi 白居易 — **a ninth-century precedent for reflection theory, with its own KPI (补察时政, 'remedying and inspecting the government of the day')**, [[tang-literature/mid-tang]]); **around 1800** mirror and lamp formally divide the household (the Romantics moved the light source into the author — **before this, no one had separately called himself a "reflection theorist": the category was made afterwards; people did the work first**); **1830s–1880s** the period of the debates on realism (Stendhal's "沿路行走的镜子" ('a mirror walking along the road'), Engels's typical, Zola's experimental novel — **three generations of mirror bidding on the same stage**); **the 1880s** the aesthetes give up the lease ("art for art's sake" — **the autonomy clause begins to be written into the constitution of the field**); **the 1930s** the debates on realism (Lukács vs Brecht; the dispute in China's League of Left-Wing Writers over the "typical" and the "subjective" — **a civil war of mirrors: the searchlight faction vs the collage faction**); **1942** *Talks at the Yan'an Forum on Literature and Art* — **"for whom" becomes an institutionalised question: direction, popularisation and the raising of standards, the united front — literature's social post is for the first time fixed in a policy document (this lesson treats it as a landmark in the history of concepts: the gravitational field of twentieth-century writing in Chinese)**; **the 1960s** Althusser, "意识形态与文艺" ("ideology and literature and art") (**literature and art make ideology perceptible by way of a "separation" (隔) — relative autonomy**); **the 1990s–** Bourdieu's "field" enters Chinese departments; **now**: **the "sociological turn" is complete — cultural studies rewrites "literature and society" as "literature in society": one no longer asks whether the likeness in the mirror holds, but asks about the whole process of production, circulation, consumption and institutions (all four versions of the mirror in this chapter retire as museum exhibits — exhibits are useful too: one learns how the mirror once ruled the arrangement of the room)**.
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of the social reading
**Determinism by subject matter** (**to have written about those at the bottom = progressive literature? — "what is written" is only half a coordinate: "how it is written" decides the fineness of the same subject matter inside the bureau of exchange (a conflict between labour and capital turned into a detective story, into martial-arts fiction, or into sweet romance gives entirely different readings)**); **matching the person to the seat** (**treating characters as parties to a case and checking their class status ("宋江是投降派", "Song Jiang is a capitulationist") — the typical is a function, not a suspect: to sentence an image by biography and class origin is to smash the letter of pardon which says "the text knows the road better than the driver"**); **reading "engagement" as "a set essay"** (**Sartre's action means literature's call to freedom, not a catalogue of topics — "intervention" and "service" are two contracts: to confuse them is to cancel the aesthetic dimension**); **pleading "imbalance" in defence of everything** (**the non-synchrony clause only explains why one cannot convert directly; it does not exempt the analysis — "the flourishing of art has nothing to do with the economy" is not "art has nothing to do with society"**).
:::

::: exercise Sensor readings {level=1}
Choose one contemporary genre craze (transmigration, sweet romance, suspense short drama, or survival at the end of the world — any one of them) and write 150 characters of a "**report on social tinnitus**": **do not discuss whether the works are good or bad; answer only which nerve it rings for, where the point of deformation is, and what anxiety the shape of the distortion corresponds to**.
::: solution
Marking: **locate one particular nerve** (of the order of "transmigration = anxiety about irreversible inflation — going back to the start and buying once more"; writing "reflects social reality" is an empty form and scores nothing); **the point of deformation may be indicated** (the golden finger of wish-fulfilment fiction = the instant cashing-in of a return on ability — **one pulling of the tube on the narrative of "十年寒窗" ("ten years at a cold window")**); in the form of a report (a reading plus an inference, and no crying of injustice) — **this chapter's position: a likeness can deceive; a ringing cannot**.
:::

::: exercise Two mirrors set side by side {level=2}
On the same historical material (for example, "the implementation of the Two-Tax system in the Tang"), **write two drafts**: A on the theory of the typical (one family's fate under the tax law; truth of detail + typical circumstances); B on the logic by which field theory chooses a subject (say what this subject matter can be exchanged for in the literary field today). Review each other: **how much of A's "depth" is B's positional dividend**.
::: solution
Marking: draft A, in at least one place, **synthesises possibility** (not an individual case out for the curious — Aristotle's line for acceptance); draft B honestly exposes the logic of exchange (**the going price of a "sense of historical weight" in prizes, publishing and the classroom**); if the conclusion of the mutual review writes "**'profundity' is symbolic capital granted by consensus inside the field — but the granting does not manufacture the feeling; the feeling has a reality of its own**" (neither swallowing the other), full marks — **this is precisely the boundary between field theory and reflection theory: one explains price, the other explains use value**.
:::

::: exercise Short essay: is literature society's voice, or society's tinnitus {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**To call literature a mirror is flattery; to call it tinnitus is respect: it refuses translation, and insists only on resonance**". You must draw on at least five items: **Aristotle's universality**, **the Balzac letter of pardon**, **Lu Xun's flame and lamp**, **the exchange mechanism of the literary field**, and **the imbalance clause**; and you must deal with one opposing case: **the tinnitus account is too passive — literature can plainly make laws, forge a nation and incite revolution (the legend that *Uncle Tom's Cabin* "started a great war")**.
::: solution
Marking points: the five items in place (**universality = the first version of the mirror, against photography; the letter of pardon = the text's excess of knowledge; the lamp = functional theory's household registration; the bureau of exchange = the study of positions in the dispute over plating; imbalance = the non-convertibility clause**); the opposing case handled (**for reference: the legend itself was made by literature (whether that stone-inscription sentence of Mrs Stowe's is genuine is in doubt) — "being able to start a great war" is precisely the social efficacy of narrative as tinnitus: it does not reflect the war; it changes the ear;** **efficacy belongs to literary history (this chapter), the reading belongs to sociology (this chapter), and translation belongs to politics (the boundary of the next chapter)**); if the conclusion can write "**the mirror waits for society to come and stand before it; tinnitus heeds no speech — this asymmetry is precisely literature's special contribution to society: a stock of perception that does not join the consensus**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The four revisions, filed: the first generation (**the mirror of possibility + observing the airs to know government: glass in common, and different uses on leaving the factory**), the second (**the typical and the searchlight: the question of the switch comes on stage**), the third (**flame and lamp: a draft constitution; the aesthetes' anti-mirror = a struggle over the power to define**), the fourth (**the bureau of exchange: it explains the plating, not whether the likeness holds**), and the closing of the case (**sensor / tinnitus: a likeness can deceive, a ringing cannot**). Method, in one sentence: **first ask for whom it rings, then ask whether it is a likeness**. The previous chapter said that a definition is a receipt; this chapter says that a receipt can also be voided — the next chapter enters the factory: the creative process — the dissecting room of the lamp (the artist).
:::
