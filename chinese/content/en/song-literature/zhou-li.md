---
title: "Zhou Bangyan and Li Qingzhao"
---

In the previous chapter, Su Shi 苏轼 wrote 我 ('I') into the song lyric (_ci_) and stirred up a lawsuit over jurisdiction. This chapter deals with the two judgements in that lawsuit — **Zhou Bangyan 周邦彦 writes a constitution for the song lyric (a statute of prosody), and Li Qingzhao 李清照 writes a copyright for it ("词别是一家", 'the song lyric is a household of its own'): one makes the song lyric teachable, the other makes it provable**.

## Definition: two ways of fixing the score {#def-dingpu}

::: definition The Dasheng Bureau's grammar book {#def-dasheng}
**Zhou Bangyan 周邦彦 (1056–1121)** (**advanced in office by presenting a rhapsody (_fu_ 赋) (the usual account: he rose from post to post because he had presented a rhapsody); in his later years he served as intendant of the Dasheng Bureau 大晟府 (提举大晟府; records by people of the Song differ, and the usual account is followed) — **a music official kept by the bureaucratic system: his post determines that his question is not "what to write", but "how to fix the score"**); **three projects** (**① a statute of prosody** (**the four tones fix the "pattern", character by character: formerly the song lyric was filled in to the musical phrase; thereafter it was filled in to the characters of the score — **the perfect tense of the shift from "following the melody" (依声) to "following the score" (依谱) (the Dasheng Bureau entry in Lesson 1 is settled here: the tune title (_cipai_) gained a way of surviving that does not depend on music, at the price that its melodies began to be lost (the rules are completed and the music perishes: all standardisation pays its bill in this way**))); ② **extended narration, with turns and pauses** (**the folding of time in the long lyric (_manci_): present — recollection — present — advance (drawing ahead of time on what has not yet come) (_Lanling Wang_ 《兰陵王·柳》, 'Willow', in three stanzas: the farewell — remembering old times — the boat on its way, while the "this moment" of the three sections seeps through one another — **Liu Yong 柳永's extended narration is a straight line; Zhou Bangyan's is a loop: he changes the narrative of the song lyric from a train to the underground (the song-lyric version of the compression of time and space in Lesson 13**))); ③ **making the old into the new** (**melting lines by Tang poets into the song lyric without showing the seam (the usual account: earlier critics said of him "善于融化诗句", 'skilled at melting lines of verse') — **allusion is upgraded from "carrying bricks" to "dissolving salt into water": the rate at which words are reused becomes an explicit index for measuring skill (this is the formal division of households between the literati song lyric and the song-artisan song lyric: the song-artisans want new words; the literati run a renovation project on familiar phrases**.
:::

::: definition The song lyric is a household of its own {#def-bieyijia}
**Li Qingzhao 李清照 (1084–c. 1151)** (**her father, Li Gefei 李格非, was a disciple of Su Shi; at eighteen she married Zhao Mingcheng 赵明诚: the merger of two book-collecting families (the usual account) — **a critic who grew up at Su Shi's dinner table: to name Su Shi is a patricide she is qualified to commit**); **_Cilun_ 《词论》 ('A Discourse on the Song Lyric')** (**a short essay that runs through the famous writers of her own dynasty: Liu Yong, "词语尘下" ('the wording is dusty and low'); Zhang Xian 张先, Song Qi 宋祁 and Yang Yi 杨亿, "句读不葺" ('the pauses are left unrepaired'); Yan Shu 晏殊 and Ouyang Xiu 欧阳修, "学际天人而作为小歌词" ('their learning reaches heaven and man, yet they compose little song lyrics'), as if to say that the work is not finely done; and so on to the criticism of Su Shi, "往往不韵" ('often lacking in musical accord') (the gist of the quotations in the usual account; the wording is omitted) — at last the hammer falls: "**the song lyric is a household of its own**" — **three techniques of the audit: ① scoring person by person (no favour is kept back, including none for the benefactors of her father's generation); ② a single standard (musical rules); ③ the standard comes before the work: it is not that she dislikes Su's lyrics; she holds that the song lyric has an acceptance standard independent of _shi_ poetry**); **the two faces of taking prosody as the basis** (**this one standard locks, at the same time, Su Shi's expansion and Liu Yong's popular manner: **constitution and copyright divide the work here: Zhou Bangyan lays down how one must write in order to count as qualified; Li Qingzhao lays down why a separate subject for the song lyric must still be set up (to classify is to exercise sovereignty (Lesson 2, the right to define: what she is contending for is not a literary style, but the song lyric's household registration**.
:::

## A paired reading of the writing {#def-kongdui}

::: definition Zhou: the teachable song lyric {#def-zhou}
**The title incident of _Liuchou_ 《六丑》 ('Six Uglinesses')** (**Song notebooks (_biji_) transmit the legend that he named the tune "六丑" and claimed clumsiness and crudeness for himself (the story layer; it need not have happened) — **the title becomes advertising space for the work: people of the Song were already running "topic operations" (the Song scene, in Chapter 9, of a title being a prospectus**); **the acceptance sheet for the three stanzas of _Lanling Wang_ 《兰陵王》** (**the first section, the farewell feast under the willows; the second, remembering words shared; the third, "沉思前事" ('sinking into thought of what went before') while the person is in the boat — **the three sections are not three times, but three tenses of one time: "parting" is written as a live broadcast of memory (the Song-_ci_ version of narrative point of view in Chapter 4**); **"叶上初阳乾宿雨，水面清圆，一一风荷举" (_Sumuzhe_ 《苏幕遮》)** — On the leaves the early sun dries the rain that stayed the night; on the water, clear and round, one by one the wind-lotuses lift. (Our translation.) — **Wang Guowei 王国维 remarked "真能得荷之神理" ('it truly catches the lotus's essential principle') (the gist of the comment as the usual account cites it) — **in the three lines there is physics (乾, 'dries'), geometry (圆, 'round') and dynamics (举, 'lift'): the highest technique of scene-writing in Song _ci_ is to write observation up as the report of an experiment (the counter-example to the "substitution method" of Lesson 12: this character 举 cannot be replaced**.
:::

::: definition Li: the provable song lyric {#def-li}
**_Rumeng ling_ 《如梦令》 ('Like a Dream')**, "**知否，知否？应是绿肥红瘦**" — Do you know, do you know? It must be that the green is fat and the red is thin. (Our translation.) — **a doubled question, and a comparison: a questionnaire a maid cannot answer (**the cognitive content of the song lyric: she makes "the crab apple after rain" into a test about attention and pronouns: green = leaf, red = flower, fat and thin = a measurement by bodily feeling (the legitimate use of "synaesthesia" in Lesson 12: she is not showing off a skill; she really does see in this way**); **_Zuihuayin_ 《醉花阴》 ('Drunk in the Shade of Flowers')**, "**莫道不销魂，帘卷西风，人比黄花瘦**" — Do not say the soul is not undone; the west wind rolls the curtain, and the person is thinner than the yellow flower. (Our translation.) — **the comparative degree plus a bodily measurement (Song notebooks record that Zhao Mingcheng was ashamed he could not equal her, wrote fifty pieces more, mixed them in, and asked a friend to judge, and that "只三句" ('only three lines') stood unsurpassed — the legend layer; the usual account keeps it): **the force of a metaphor is not in how many points of likeness there are, but in making an invisible quantity of depletion visible: thinness is a heading in the accounts of this marriage**); **_Shengsheng man_ 《声声慢》 ('Sound upon Sound, the Slow Tune')** — "**寻寻觅觅，冷冷清清，凄凄惨惨戚戚**" — Seeking, seeking, searching, searching; cold, cold, still, still; bleak, bleak, miserable, miserable, grieving, grieving. (Our translation.) (**fourteen reduplicated characters: people of the Song both sighed "创获自妇人，大奇" ('the invention comes from a woman; how extraordinary') and raised objections (the usual account: the dispute itself shows that she had stepped out of bounds) — **reduplication here is not a musical ornament; it is a recording of behaviour: seeking is the action, cold stillness is the setting, wretched grief is the report sheet: **she writes a song lyric as the record of the scene of an accident, and the recorder is the scene**); **_Yongyu le_ 《永遇乐》 ('Ever Meeting with Joy') (the Lantern Festival) and _Fenghuangtai shang yi chuixiao_ 《凤凰台上忆吹箫》 ('Remembering the Flute on Phoenix Terrace')**, "**从今更憔悴，非干病酒，不是悲秋**" — From now on, haggard still more; it has nothing to do with sickening on wine, and it is not grieving over autumn. (Our translation.) — **in the later years the viewpoint moves downward:** "**不如向、帘儿底下，听人笑语**" (_Yongyu le_) — Better to turn to the foot of the curtain, and listen to other people laughing and talking. (Our translation.): **the position of feeling is moved from the inner quarters to the street corner — she is no longer the beauty who is looked at, but a person who watches others keep the festival (Lesson 15: the highest use of an indeterminate point is to turn the camera towards what is outside the window**.
:::

::: definition The _Jinshi lu_: the fall of the state written as an inventory checklist {#def-jinshi}
**The Postscript to the _Jinshi lu_ 《金石录后序》 ('Records on Metal and Stone')** (**it records the book collecting of the two families as "collect — collate — lose — collect again", and at the end resolves it for herself with "有有必有无，有无必有有"** — If there is having, there must be not-having; if there is not-having, there must be having. (Our translation.) — **an autobiography in the form of an account book: the fall of the state is written as one stocktaking (**the politics of the literature of lists: numbers (so many fascicles, so many scrolls) are the hard currency of suffering: **she does not say "everything is lost"; she lists a catalogue — because a catalogue can be checked, and grief cannot**); **a genre exceeds its authority** (**a preface-postscript is written into one of the best pieces of autobiographical prose of the Song: Li Qingzhao outside the song lyric is likewise "a household of her own" — **her critical claims and her practice in writing stand surety for each other (the person who writes song lyrics comes to fix the song lyric's constitution; the person who writes prose comes to furnish the song lyric's evidence: this is the highest qualification a critic can have (the specimen of "practitioner criticism" in Chapter 9**.
:::

::: example Technical dismantling: the yellow flower and the wind-lotus {#ex-chaijie}
Read "人比黄花瘦" ('the person is thinner than the yellow flower') and "一一风荷举" ('one by one the wind-lotuses lift') side by side: ① what operation each has completed (comparison / depiction of a state); ② whether the acceptance standard of each can be exchanged for the other; ③ if 举 ('lift') is replaced with 舞 ('dance'), and 瘦 ('thin') with 愁 ('sorrow'), what the loss is.
::: solution
① **The operation** — **the yellow-flower line = metaphor (a mapping across domains + quantification); the wind-lotus line = direct depiction (a physical description inside the same domain); **what the two lines share is that both refuse the stock plate of the adjective ("dance" and "sorrow" are the stock plates**); ② **the acceptance standards cannot be exchanged** — **"人比黄花瘦" can be tested by "does it truly look like that?" (the rate at which the metaphor is honoured: the yellow flower has a thin shape, and the person has an outline grown thin — **the metaphor holds because both sides are in the season of growing thin: the chrysanthemum's fading and the haggardness share their seasonal signs (this is where she is more accomplished than the type "愁如春水", 'sorrow like the waters of spring': she does not borrow an abstract quantity; she borrows the temperature of the same season**); "风荷举" can be tested by "was it seen?" — **举 is a verb with no life-force, used for a living movement: it writes the lotus's self-possession at the moment the wind bears it up (what Wang Guowei calls the "essential principle" is the irreplaceability of this verb**); ③ **the loss** — **"dance" gives personification; what "lift" gives is mechanics: **dance is the spectator's point of view, and lift is the lotus's point of view (the theory of point of view in Chapter 4); "sorrow" is a conclusion, and "thin" is evidence: **half the power of the song lyric to persuade lies in its insisting on producing evidence (the Song-_ci_ branch of the study of evidence in Chapter 3**.
:::

::: intuition Teachable and provable {#int-jiao}
**Zhou Bangyan's legacy is a curriculum** (**in the Southern Song, Fang Qianli 方千里, Chen Yunping 陈允平 and that line matched Zhou's lyrics character by character (the lineage as the usual account gives it) — **the hardest evidence of canonisation is not praise, but that someone hands in homework character by character: once the metrical pattern can be taught, the reproduction of the song lyric stands independent of music (the extreme case of "the procedure can be copied" in Lesson 2: what those who matched Zhou copied was precisely the procedure, and so they lost the mood (the lesson of Lesson 2 is played out again in them**)); **Li Qingzhao's legacy is a precedent** (**"the song lyric is a household of its own" thereafter becomes a standing court for disputes in the study of the song lyric: the school of expressing intent and the metrical school both have to plead before her judgement — **how long a precedent lives depends on whether those who come later still need this piece of ground: the revival of the song lyric in the Qing still uses this title deed (Lesson 15, "a classic = being continually cited"**.
:::

::: history Two acts of legislation for fixing the score {#sec-lifa}
**1056–1121** Zhou Bangyan (**Bianjing 汴京 — Luzhou 庐州 — Lishui 溧水 — the Dasheng Bureau — Mingzhou 明州: **a three-stage career, official — staff officer — music official, corresponding exactly to three contributions to the song lyric (experience of the city + local administration + the ordering of prosody**)); **1084–c. 1151** Li Qingzhao (**Bianjing — Qingzhou 青州 — Jiankang 建康 — Lin'an 临安 — Jinhua 金华: **her route of migration is the map of loss from the Northern Song to the Southern Song**); **1127** the Jingkang catastrophe (**the book collections and the _yuefu_ of both houses were scattered and lost at the same time: **Zhou Bangyan's scores and Li Qingzhao's books became, in the same year, supplies carried into exile: rules and evidence both need a warehouse; when the warehouse is burnt, literature has only recitation left (the Song version of "archives and fire" in Lesson 8**)); **the 1130s**, the Postscript to the _Jinshi lu_ is completed; **the middle of the Southern Song**, the vogue of matching Zhou, and anthologies of elegant song lyrics (**the victory of the metrical school is really the victory of the anthology: **textbooks live longer than genius**); **the 1160s–1190s**, _Cilun_ is cited as the source of "the theory of proper character" (_bense_ 本色, 'the true colour of the form') (**a recognition after the fact: a short essay by a woman in exile became the first stele in eight hundred years of dispute in the study of the song lyric (Lesson 15, "criticism takes effect after a delay"**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of Zhou and Li
**The verdict "the metrical school = formalism"** (**reading the four tones, character by character, as a cutting-off from content: the density of feeling in Zhou's lyrics is inside the structure (the loop of tenses across the three stanzas is itself the shape of longing) — **the metrical pattern is not a shackle on feeling; it is the scaffolding of feeling: take the frame away, and the building collapses too (the speech for the defence in the study of prosody, Lesson 14**)); **the three-layered label "Li Qingzhao is a woman lyricist of the graceful school"** (**first there is the binary "graceful / bold" (_wanyue_ 婉约 / _haofang_ 豪放) (modern textbooks); then she is stuffed into "graceful"; then the character 女 ('woman') is added as a limit on subject matter: **all three labels are taxonomy being lazy (Lesson 15, to classify is to exercise sovereignty) — her poem on history, _Yongshi_ 《咏史》 ('On History'), "生当作人杰"** — In life one ought to be an outstanding person. (Our translation.) — **is not on this table at all**)); **taking legend as history** (**the mixed judging of the fifty pieces around "thinner than the yellow flower", and Minghuang's 明皇 one question about "Six Uglinesses": the layer of Song notebooks — **these stories are the first batch of reader reports on the works: they prove that the sentences had been remembered; they do not prove that the events happened (the layering of sources in Lesson 1**)); **ignoring her critical writing** (**to read only _Shuyu ci_ 《漱玉词》 ('Lyrics of Rinsing Jade'), and not _Cilun_ or the Postscript, is to press the triple identity of critic + prose writer + lyricist into one: **Li Qingzhao's reliability comes from her three identities testifying for one another (the author's version of the principle of independent sources in Chapter 8**).
:::

::: exercise The scaffolding of the metrical pattern {level=1}
Choose a passage from _Lanling Wang_ or _Liuchou_, and mark the points where its tense shifts (present / recollection / advance); then, on the same three-part pattern of tense, rewrite a modern farewell of 100 characters (舍不得, 'cannot bear to part', must not appear).
::: solution
Marking: **the points of shift must be identifiable** (**one must be able to say which character sends the reader into recollection — a switch-word of the "沉思前事" ('sinking into thought of what went before') type**); **the rewriting must keep the structure of "three tenses of one time"** (**straight-line narration = a retreat to Liu Yong: **what this question tests is the loop, not length**).

::: exercise Short essay: rules and evidence {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**the song lyric's two legislators happen to stand, one in the deepest part of the bureaucratic system, and one in the refugee column of a dynasty's collapse: Zhou Bangyan is qualified to fix the rules because the warehouse is his; Li Qingzhao is qualified to tell the truth because she watched, with her own eyes, the warehouse burn — the institutional history of literature often assigns the right to speak in just this way: in stable times it produces codes; in turbulent times it produces precedents**". You must call on at least six items: **from following the melody to following the score**, **allusion as salt dissolved into water**, **the tenses of the three stanzas**, **the song lyric is a household of its own**, **autobiography in the form of an account book**, **character-by-character homework matching Zhou**; and give a ruling: **is the metrical pattern (formal constraint) a net cost or a net gain for writing that expresses feeling — answer once from Zhou's situation and once from Li's, and then give your "conditional sentence"**.
::: solution
Marking points: the six mechanisms (**statute, melting-in, tense, copyright, the list, emulative matching**); the ruling (**for reference: the conditional sentence — when the community must still produce by cooperation (the music artisans, the singers and the colleagues are all present), the metrical pattern is a net gain: it is the common currency of collaboration; when the community breaks apart (the warehouse is gone, the listeners have scattered), the metrical pattern quickly becomes a luxury, and the only thing that still has the power to persuade is experience that can be checked — so the growth of Li Qingzhao's reputation in the Southern Song is institutional: the return on formal constraint fluctuates with the stability of society (this is not a moral judgement; it is a table of interest rates: **Zhou Bangyan is the central bank of a peaceful year; Li Qingzhao is the humanitarian accountant after the war**); if the conclusion can be written "**rules live in the homes of people who have a roof; the truth lives in the mouths of people who have no roof: one dynasty, two throats**", that is an A.

::: summary
Filed under the two chambers: the constitution (**fixing the score by the four tones: the rules are completed and the music perishes; the metrical pattern is the scaffolding of feeling**), the copyright (**the song lyric is a household of its own: to classify is to exercise sovereignty**), Zhou (**a loop in the manner of the underground; salt dissolved into water; 举, 'lift', cannot be replaced**), Li (**if the evidence does not appear, the case is not closed: thinness is a heading in the accounts; reduplication is a recording made on the scene; a list can be checked, and grief cannot**), posterity (**handing in homework character by character = canonisation; a precedent depends on later people needing it**). The method in one sentence: **rules lay down how writing counts as qualified; situation lays down why it is worth writing**.
