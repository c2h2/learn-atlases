---
title: "Early Northern Song *ci*"
---

The Tang literature course closed after the An Lushan Rebellion; this lesson opens the feast again, at a banquet in a flourishing age of great peace — **the song lyric (词, *ci*; also the *quzi ci* 曲子词, 'words set to a tune') was in origin a thing of the song-hall: banquet music (*yànyuè* 燕乐) was fitted to lines of unequal length (*chángduǎnjù* 长短句), and drinking-commands (*jiǔlìng* 酒令) pressed out the first group of authors; what the early Song has to tell is how this "banquet form" was taken over by the study, and how those who took it over were seized back by the wharf**.

## Definition: the production line of a genre {#def-shengchan}

::: definition Banquet, singers, text {#def-yanxi}
**Three pieces of equipment for the song lyric of the early Song** (**① the site of the performance** (**wine banquets, singing parties, gatherings for the seasons and the festivals: the length of a song lyric is fixed by how long the banquet lasts, and the structure in which the first stanza (上片) waits to be sung and the second stanza (下片) urges the wine still keeps the remains of a programme sheet** (**"宴酣之乐" ('the joy of a banquet in full flow'), in 'Record of the Old Drunkard's Pavilion' (*Zuiweng tingji* 醉翁亭记), is the prose version; the song lyric's "怅望立苍苔" ('In melancholy I gaze, standing on the green moss.') (Our translation.) is the version for when the company breaks up**)); **② the mechanism of circulation** (by mouth, and by manuscript copy: of the song lyrics of Liu Yong 柳永 it was said, "凡有井水饮处，即能歌柳词" ('Wherever there is well-water to drink, Liu's song lyrics can be sung.') (Our translation.) (the common account recorded in *Bishu luhua* 避暑录话, 'Recorded Talks while Avoiding the Heat', by Ye Mengde 叶梦得) — one remark of the time writes out every element of communication studies: the coverage network (well-water = the path along which daily life moves), the user (the one who drinks), the carrier (the singer's mouth))); ③ the author's situation (the chief minister Yan Shu 晏殊 and the minor clerk Liu Yong wrote one and the same literary form, and their situations stood as far apart as heaven and earth — and so, inside the form, two aesthetic governments grew (the scene of the "field" (*chǎng* 场) in Lesson 15: the same currency, two kinds of credit)). The zero-mark of the institution (the early Song, following the Five Dynasties, established the *jiàofāng* 教坊 (the Court Entertainment Bureau) and set up the Dasheng Bureau (*Dàshèngfǔ* 大晟府; the Chongning era of Emperor Huizong 徽宗) to take charge of music: the state kept the stock of sounds of ritual music (*yǎyuè* 雅乐) and of popular music (*súyuè* 俗乐) alike — the "pattern" (格) of the tune title (*cipai*) had from then on an official copy: filling in a song lyric (*tiáncí* 填词) slowly changed from "following the melody" (依声) to "following the score" (依谱) (the Song semicolon in the study of prosody, Lesson 14)).
:::

::: definition The grammar of the short lyric {#def-xiaoling}
**The heritage of the Tang and the Five Dynasties** (**the vocabulary of the boudoir in *Huajian* 花间 ('Among the Flowers'), plus the deep feeling of the Southern Tang scholar: Yan Shu's *Zhuyu ci* 珠玉词 ('Lyrics of Pearls and Jade') takes up the line that comes down from Feng Yansi 冯延巳 (**the rationality inside soft phrases of the "无可奈何" sort ('there is nothing to be done') is his accent); the genre-contract of the short lyric (short, concealed, left blank: inside forty characters a stretch of argument is not allowed — its kin among genres is the quatrain (*jueju* 绝句), not the rhapsody (*fu* 赋); and so the "deep beauty" (*shēnměi* 深美) of the early-Song song lyric is a matter of quotas: feeling must pass through a schedule (调度) before it may take the stage: in one piece to the tune *Huanxisha* 浣溪沙, the first stanza sets out the scene and the second stanza releases the feeling, like a chief minister endorsing a state paper: the precedent once fixed, it is approved).
:::

## The chief minister in the study, and the lyricist by imperial command {#def-liangfu}

::: definition Yan Shu: writing movement by not moving {#def-yanshu}
**The ease of *Zhuyu ci*** ("无可奈何花落去，似曾相识燕归来" ('The flowers fall, and there is nothing to be done; the swallows return, as if one had known them before.') (Our translation.) — parallelism compounded with function words: one layer regrets the spring, one layer is a coming back, and the drop between the two layers is smoothed flat by the art of balance (Song Qi 宋祁 admired its "match" (对); the anecdote of this couplet is recorded in the talk of the time — only the common account is taken); the chief minister's way of writing is "not supplying an incident" (in his song lyrics there is no place of demotion, no cause of a parting, no personal name: pain is processed into weather — this is the true body of the "air of wealth and rank" (*fùguì qì* 富贵气): not that one has money, but that one has the assurance not to finish saying the thing (the class character of the "place of indeterminacy", *wèidìngdiǎn* 未定点, in Lesson 15: leaving a blank is a luxury good; Liu Yong just cannot get a blank to stand up)); the economics of the study (disciples and old subordinates fill the empire, and the song lyric is a matter for spare time ("不为无益之事，何以遣有涯之生" ('If one does not do things of no benefit, with what is one to pass a life that has a limit?') (Our translation.) — a saying handed on among people of the Song (the notebook system) — this sentence may serve as a disclaimer for the whole dynasty's study of the song lyric: the "lack of benefit" (无益) of the song lyric is precisely the precondition of its freedom: official status and the private literary form keep separate accounts, and both ledgers are clean).
:::

::: definition Liu Yong: the algorithm of filling in lyrics by imperial command {#def-liuyong}
**An incident of identity** (he sat the *jinshi* examination (进士); Emperor Renzong 仁宗 wrote on the paper "且去填词" ('Be off, and fill in song lyrics.') (Our translation.), and so he styled himself "奉旨填词柳三变" ('Liu Sanbian, filling in song lyrics by imperial command') (the common account: see the notebooks of the Song) — one letter of rejection rewritten as a pen-name: the system's veto was turned, by himself, into a brand (the early-Song version of the "licence" story in Lesson 16: if no licence is issued, print your own); the technical revolution of the long lyric (the long tune (*chángdiào* 长调), plus extended narration (*pūxù* 铺叙), plus the speech of the marketplace: *Wanghaichao* 望海潮 writes of Hangzhou 杭州, "有三秋桂子，十里荷花" ('There is the osmanthus of three autumns, and lotus flowers for ten *li*.') (Our translation.) — a *li* was a unit of distance, and its length was not the same in every age — the legend that the Jin ruler Wanyan Liang 完颜亮, on hearing the song, conceived the will to cast his whip (the common account in *Helin yulu* 鹤林玉露, 'Jade Dew of the Crane Grove', by Luo Dajing 罗大经), which shows exactly the power of a song lyric of this kind to travel: it is not for matching poems in reply; it is for a city to hear; "杨柳岸，晓风残月" ('A willow bank, a dawn wind, a waning moon.') (Our translation.), in *Yulinling* 雨霖铃 (*màncí* 慢词, the long lyric, is the form under discussion), set beside Yan Shu's "小园香径" ('the scented path in a little garden'): one is the wharf's timetable ("今宵酒醒何处", 'Where, tonight, shall I wake from the wine?') (Our translation.), the other is the study's schedule for the day ("夕阳西下", 'The evening sun goes down in the west.') (Our translation.); the former set the hours for a parting, the latter set the years for an idle sorrow)); the audit of the "vulgar" (the fact, in the history of criticism, that "the lyric world took it as a fault" (词坛以为病) (the common account: Chao Buzhi 晁补之, Li Qingzhao 李清照 and their line mocked his "词语尘下", 'the wording down in the dust') — yet "down in the dust" (尘下) is precisely his strategy of position: when the scholar-official writes "feeling" (情), he has to protect his status; when Liu Yong writes "feeling", he protects only the market (Lesson 15, "genre = an agreement": Liu Yong altered the agreement from his side alone, and the readers came in a body); a man of two ledgers (sung on every side, and rated by scholars at the very bottom: a normal case of the literary field (two medals at the exchange of Bourdieu (布迪厄): a bestseller in the market = the guarantee of a devaluation inside the circle: Liu Yong took both, and so literary history has had to reverse the verdict for him twice, once by the circle of Su Shi 苏轼, once by Wang Guowei 王国维).
:::

::: definition Ouyang Xiu 欧阳修: the specialist in splitting the accounts {#def-ouyang}
**One person, two faces** (leader of the *guwen* movement (古文, ancient-style prose), and of *Liuyi shihua* 六一诗话 ('Remarks on Poetry of Six-One') (the first form of criticism in China to take *shīhuà* 诗话, 'remarks on poetry', as its name — it freed the discussion of poetry from prefaces and colophons and turned it into the occasional essay: an invention of the literary form of criticism) + the amorous (*yàn* 艳) of the song-lyric collection *Zuiweng qinqu waipian* 醉翁琴趣外篇 ('Outer Chapters of the Old Drunkard's Delight in the Zither') and the refined (*yǎ* 雅) of *Liuyi shihua*, standing side by side (people of the Song were early in doubt, as between genuine and false, about his amorous song lyrics: the case of other writers' pieces mixed in among the titles — a literary collection is a filter; a collection of song lyrics is the run-off: two sets of ledgers for one and the same author, and the ancients understood the splitting of accounts better than we do); the licence of "the remainder of poetry" (the received idea that the song lyric is "the remainder of poetry" (*shīyú* 诗余) took shape in the language of people of the Song — this is not a devaluation; it is a duty-free zone: poetry (*shi* 诗) has charge of expressing intent (the official ledger), the song lyric has charge of expressing feeling (the private ledger): a division of labour among literary forms = a compliance scheme for the person (the moral version of the genre-contract in Lesson 15); the company he kept, and the ecology of the lyric world (the network of verses matched in reply among the staffs of great offices, same-year graduates (*tóngnián* 同年) and juniors: Zhang Xian 张先 gained the designation "Zhang of the Three Shadows" (张三影) by "云破月来花弄影" ('Clouds break, the moon comes, and the flowers toy with their shadows.') (Our translation.) (the common account: this line, and "娇柔懒起，帘压卷花影" ('Delicate and soft, she rises in idleness; the curtain weighs down and rolls the flowers' shadows.') (Our translation.) and "柳径无人，堕絮飞无影" ('On the willow path there is no one; falling floss flies, and there is no shadow.') (Our translation.), were lines he himself put forward — a nickname given you by your readers is marketing at the lowest cost of circulation: people of the Song already understood labelling)).
:::

::: example The allotment of literary forms at one banquet {#ex-yanxi2}
Suppose that in the Zhihe era, in Bianjing 汴京, you are "programme director" for Ouyang Xiu's birthday banquet, and that you may use one piece by each of three authors: Yan Shu's *Huanxisha* ("一曲新词酒一杯", 'One tune of a new song lyric, one cup of wine.') (Our translation.), Liu Yong's *Wanghaichao*, and one *Chaozhongcuo* 朝中措 of Ouyang Xiu's own (a send-off). Please set the order of performance, and write the linking words (40 characters each). State **which item of the banquet's programme each piece occupies, which group of listeners it addresses, and which identity it triggers**.
::: solution
**A reference arrangement** — **① the opening, *Wanghaichao*** (**the item: receiving the guests; the listeners: the whole company; the identity: pride in this city — take Liu Yong's hit from the wharf as public background music, because a laying-out in the manner of the rhapsody (赋体) can hold the room, but keep away from his long lyrics of love, so as to avoid the risk of "down in the dust" (尘下) during the feast (**which piece of Liu Yong you choose is which face of his you choose: a writer's management of his image lies in the selected anthology (*xuǎnběn* 选本), not in the account he gives of himself)); ② the middle, Yan Shu's *Huanxisha* (the item: the passing of the wine; the listeners: the guests of honour; the identity: the cultivation which the scholar-officials hold in common — the couplet "无可奈何" turns a feeling for the season into a password by which identities make contact: whoever can hear the parallelism is enrolled automatically (the class gate of the "implied reader" in Lesson 15)); ③ the close, Ouyang Xiu's *Chaozhongxian* 朝中羡 (the item: the farewell; the listeners: the protagonist himself; the identity: the political homesickness of those who remained from the Qingli era — the language of a government hall in "平山栏槛倚晴空" ('The balustrades of Pingshan lean on the clear sky.') (Our translation.) nudges the birthday banquet gently onto the topic of "merit and fame" (功名), and gives the host a step that neither takes a bribe nor fails in courtesy); the principle of the links (the public before the private, the city before the study, the bustle before the aftertaste — to study the structure of the banquet is to study the structure of literary form (here the song lyric's nature, that it is "written to the song" (*yìnggē* 应歌), shows itself: it is not that we choose song lyrics to fit the programme; the programme is born already wearing the shape of a song lyric).
:::
:::

::: intuition Continued peace is a kind of aesthetic {#int-chengping}
**The "air of wealth and rank" of the early-Song song lyric is not a subject; it is the order of the age** (the long great peace of the governance of Emperor Zhenzong 真宗 and Emperor Renzong, plus the rise of commerce in the cities: a language with no incident in it grows up at its ease (Yan Shu); where there is an incident, still no one panics, because the institution will underwrite it (Ouyang Xiu) — both kinds of ease are interest paid by continued peace (*chéngpíng* 承平) (compare Lesson 16: the moment war comes, the interest on the song lyric is cleared to zero at once; the second half of Li Qingzhao's life is this curve); the double identity of "the remainder of poetry" (precisely because it was not taken for serious literature, the song lyric dared to say what serious literature was not permitted to say: a low-voltage line can carry alternating current (Lesson 15, "the age-grades of genre": a new genre gains the freedom to experiment by not being trusted)).
:::

::: history From the song-hall to the selected anthology {#sec-xuanben}
960s—1000s The early Song carried on the *jiaofang* of the Five Dynasties and the stock of songs (the *Huajian ji* 花间集 ('The Collection Among the Flowers') had already been compiled in the Guangzheng era of Later Shu: the first comprehensive collection of song lyrics in China (the common account) — the selected anthology comes before criticism: first a stock of "what counts as a song lyric", and afterwards the debate over "what ought to count as a song lyric" (the theory of institutions in Lesson 15: canonisation begins in bibliography); 1000s—1050s Liu Yong put his whole strength into the long lyric (*manci*) and into being sung through the marketplace (well-water is circulation; and at the same time there began to appear the scholar-officials' audit, that "the lyric world took it as a fault"); 1040s—1060s the reform of poetry and prose, and the division of labour by which the song lyric was "the remainder of poetry", settled into a fixed shape (the reformers won in prose, and at the same time gave up authority in the song lyric — one movement, two fronts, and neither snatched the other's grain (the other half of the "coup in literary form" in Lesson 2); 1057 Ouyang Xiu presided over the examinations (*zhī gòngjǔ* 知贡举) (the standard of marking changed the literary style: ancient-style prose was fixed as the single orthodoxy by means of the examinations (the first Song round of "the examination shapes literary form", in Lesson 9); 1102 the Dasheng Bureau (the directing officials revised the old system, and searched out what was missing so as to supply it — **the state made a library of versions for popular songs: the "pattern" of the tune title had its official copy**); **1140s—1170s** the matching of poems, and selected anthologies (selected anthologies of the *Yuefu yaci* kind were put in order (the common account: *Yuefu yaci* 乐府雅词, 'Elegant Yuefu Lyrics', by Zeng Zao 曾慥) — **the standard of refined correctness (*yǎzhèng* 雅正) was written into the list of what was chosen: no comment, only deletion: the highest form of censorship is the catalogue (the selected-anthology version of the power to define, in Lesson 2**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of the early-Song song lyric
**Using "progress" to account for style** (**Liu Yong "developed" the song lyric, Yan Shu was "conservative" — an ordering by value, passed off as a description of the mechanism: the two men are the positioning of two product lines, not a fast train and a slow train (the long lyric does not equal "a more advanced stage": after Zhou Bangyan 周邦彦 the short lyric was still being written**); **taking what a writer says of himself as a curriculum vitae** ("filling in song lyrics by imperial command" is a layer of legend (the notebook system); the official histories do not record it: this lesson marks it as the common account — the notebooks of the Song are the historical sources of the study of the song lyric, and they are also its street stall: before you cite one, first check on which layer of the accounts it is entered); ignoring the institution of "splitting the accounts" (reading one person's *shi* and song lyrics as the history of one heart: people of the Song themselves would object: a literary collection and a collection of song lyrics are two sets of ledgers; to read them with the accounts mixed is to read false accounts (the case of the genuineness of Ouyang Xiu's amorous song lyrics is precisely the product of later people mixing the accounts); reading the "air of wealth and rank" as content (Yan Shu's ease is a compound of the order of the age and of status: read it as "character", and you have read history as psychology (Lesson 15, "style = a selection function": that he does not write incidents is his quota, not his inborn nature).
:::

Choose one *shi* poem and one song lyric by Ouyang Xiu (or by Yan Shu) from the same period, and make a **table for reading the two against each other**: subject / self-designation / addressee / what may be said and what may not. From the table, answer: **why a certain matter can appear only in the song lyric**.
::: solution
Marking: **the column "what may be said and what may not" must record the institution, not the temperament** (**poetry has an addressee for poems of presentation and reply, and a duty to express intent; the person who sings the song lyric bears a share of the author's responsibility for you (the Song version of "speaking for another" (代言) in the *zawen* 杂文, the polemical essay, of Lesson 16**); the conclusion is forbidden to say that "the song lyric is more sincere" (**the technical conditions of that sincerity have to be stated: who is called to account in your place**).

::: exercise Short essay: the interest on great peace {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**the ease of the early-Song song lyric is interest collected by the day: continued peace pays the principal, status pays the insurance, and 'the remainder of poetry' pays for exemption from inspection — let any one of the three stop, and this sound changes at once. To read Yan Shu's ease, therefore, one must read it as reading a table of interest rates, and not as reading a heart**". You must call on at least six items: **the three pieces of equipment — banquet, circulation, situation**; **the quota system of the short lyric**; **the way of writing that "does not supply an incident"**; **Liu Yong's two ledgers, and his position in the market**; **the duty-free zone of the remainder of poetry**; **the layering of notebook sources**. And give a verdict: **whether "national prosperity raises the quality of literature" holds in the case of the early-Song song lyric, or is a counter-proof (you must give, as a control experiment, a working-out of "what would happen to the same set of equipment, were it after the crossing to the south" (*nándù* 南渡))**.
::: solution
Points for marking: the six mechanisms (**equipment, the quota, the blank, the market, duty-free status, the layer of the sources**); the verdict (**for reference: the side on which it holds — the coverage network (well-water), the economy of performance, and the quota of idle sorrow are all gifts of continued peace; the side of the counter-proof — continued peace also nourished the self-censorship of "not supplying an incident", and the real enlargement of the language's capacity was carried out by the one who was excluded (Liu Yong): prosperity does not raise quality; prosperity raises circulation; quality often occurs in the accounts where circulation is poor (the control experiment: after the crossing to the south, the same equipment — the banquet is changed into exile, circulation is changed into writing on walls (*tíbì* 题壁) and the damaged remaining copies of verses matched in reply, and identity is changed into that of a remnant subject (*yímín* 遗民), so that "leaving a blank" fails in an instant; Li Qingzhao's *Cilun* 词论 ('On the Song Lyric') and 'Postface to *Records on Metal and Stone*' (*Jinshi lu houxu* 金石录后序) strike the quota system back into a list of incidents: the interest has stopped, the principal is still there, and the interest was only rhetoric**); if the conclusion can be written, "**to read a song lyric of continued peace is to read its bill: every sentence that does not move its voice or its face has one loss that was never paid for; the aesthetic of the early-Song song lyric is the part one refuses to pay**", that is an A.

::: summary
The production line, filed: equipment (**banquet, singer, situation — two aesthetic governments writing one and the same currency**), the short lyric (**the quota system, and the class character of leaving a blank**), the study (**Yan Shu does not supply an incident**), the wharf (**Liu Yong: one letter of rejection changed into a pen-name; the hours were set for a parting**), the splitting of accounts (**the duty-free zone of the remainder of poetry, and two sets of ledgers**). Method, in one sentence: **first ask which seat this song lyric has at the banquet, then ask which heading it comes under in the author's ledger**. The next chapter: the person who changed the duty-free zone into a free-trade zone — Su Shi.
