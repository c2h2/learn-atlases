---
title: "Jiang Kui and the late Southern Song"
---

The song lyrics (*ci* 词) of the previous chapter were still asking the empire for a budget; the song lyrics of this chapter have already begun to inventory the inheritance — **the poetry societies of Hangzhou 杭州, the patron households of Suzhou 苏州, the loyalist elders (*yílǎo* 遗老) of Kuaiji 会稽: when large sentences are no longer permitted, small objects become the last archive: cicadas, the new moon, the yellow wasp on the ropes of the swing. They remembered an age that was not allowed to speak**.

## Definition: the reckoning of the elegant song lyric {#def-yaci}

::: definition The trinity: poetry societies, anthologies, and lyric metre {#def-yinshe}
**Form of organisation** (**the three supports of the song lyric in the late Southern Song: the regular matched compositions of the West Lake poetry society (assigned rhymes, a shared topic, matching the previous rhyme words in order), the settling of anthologies (the line of *Juemiao haoci* 绝妙好辞, 'Supremely Fine Lyrics'), and the making of lyric metre into a technique (a character-by-character fastidiousness about the four tones, the departing-then-rising sequence, and the clear and the muddy (*qīngzhuó* 清浊); the usual account of the line:** the elegant song lyric (*yǎcí* 雅词) was not a movement; it was a system of peer review (Lesson 1's 'the highest form of examination is the catalogue', and Chapter 3's 'a textbook outlives genius', join shut here**)); **Structure of personnel** (**Jiang Kui 姜夔 was a commoner pure guest (*qīngkè* 清客; he depended on the households of Xiao Dezao 萧德藻, Zhang Jian 张鉴 and others); Wu Wenying 吴文英 for a long time entered the private secretariats of the various offices as a guest clerk; Zhou Mi 周密 and Zhang Yan 张炎 were sons of established families who, after they passed into the Yuan, became loyalist elders —** three sources of income determine three styles of writing: the courtesy of the patron household, the close intricacy of the private secretariat, and the inventory of the former state (the late-Song song-lyric cohort of Lesson 15's 'the economics of genres'**)); **Aesthetic programme** (**Zhang Yan's *Ciyuan* 词源 ('Origins of the Song Lyric') has "**词要清空，不要质实**" ('The song lyric should be clear and empty; it should not be solid and substantial.' (Our translation.)) (a quotation in the usual account) + "**清空则灵，质实能滞**" ('Clear and empty, and it is quick; solid and substantial, and it can clog.' (Our translation.)) —** the use of a slogan: to fit a statute to a taste that had already formed: first the palate of the poetry society, and only afterwards the trademark 'clear and empty' (*qīngkōng* 清空) (the reverse case of Lesson 15's 'criticism takes effect belatedly':** the late Southern Song was the generation in which the critics at last beat the authors**.
:::

## Jiang Kui: the music of the pure guest {#def-jiangkui}

::: definition The fineness of a wild cloud flying alone {#def-qingke}
**'Yangzhou man' 扬州慢 ('Slow Tune of Yangzhou') (1176)** — "**二十四桥仍在，波心荡、冷月无声**" ('The twenty-four bridges are still there; at the heart of the waves a cold moon rocks, soundless.' (Our translation.)) "**念桥边红药，年年知为谁生**" ('I think of the red peonies beside the bridge; year after year, do they know for whom they grow?' (Our translation.)) — **twenty-four bridges become twenty-four columns of accounts not yet written off: the ruined city is written as a ledger that no one will claim (**the two characters "仍在" ('still there') are where all the violence lies: the things remain, the people are lost; the concession dance-step of Chapter 6 is written into the scenic phrasing: I admit that the bridges are still there precisely in order to deny that the order you brought is still there**); **the preface to 'Yangzhou man', "予过维扬……予怀怆然，感慨今昔"** ('I passed through Weiyang … my heart was stricken, stirred by present and past.' (Our translation.)) (**a tune he composed himself (*zìdùqǔ* 自度曲, a self-composed tune) and a tune title he wrote himself (the *gongche* notation, 工尺谱, has come down to us: material of the rank of a unique case in the history of music and literature) —** he does not fill in other people's tunes; he makes the tune: a man with no degree from the examinations and no office holds the legislative power of the song lyric (on the musical side): this is the finest breach of grade in the cultural market of the Song (a distant cousin of the transmission by well water in Lesson 1: the works of Baishi daoren 白石道人, 'the Daoist of the White Stone', were issued on two tracks, the patron household and the notated copy**).
:::

::: definition 'Hidden Fragrance' and 'Sparse Shadows': contract processing of the poem on an object {#def-anxiang}
**'Anxiang' 暗香 ('Hidden Fragrance') and 'Shuying' 疏影 ('Sparse Shadows'), self-composed tunes on the plum** (**the tune titles are taken from Lin Bu 林逋, "疏影横斜水清浅，暗香浮动月黄昏"** ('Sparse shadows lie aslant; the water is clear and shallow. A hidden fragrance drifts and floats; the moon is at dusk.' (Our translation.)) **(the early-Song precedent, in Lesson 13, of the plum as a wife is recovered here) —** borrowing another man's lines to serve as one's own tune title: in his hands, quotation is a ceremony of transferring property rights**); **the gist of 'Shuying' in the usual account: the whole piece writes the plum by stacking stories such as those of Zhaojun 昭君 and Ajiao 阿娇, so that person, flower and history reflect one another on three tracks (readers in the Song already had a line of comment running "前新句、后用工迹" ('fresh lines in front, traces of applied craft behind'); we take the usual account) —** the substance of the technique: to write the poem on an object (*yǒngwù* 咏物) as a jigsaw:** the reader has to keep converting between the object and the allusion, and the object thereby acquires the weight of 'what cannot be said outright' (the aesthetic dividend of the oblique brush (*qūbǐ* 曲笔) in Lesson 16: what cannot be said is the dearest, and metaphor therefore becomes the default rate of exchange of the song lyric in the late Southern Song**.
:::

::: definition The seven-jewelled tower: Wu Wenying's density {#def-wuwenying}
**Zhang Yan's verdict, "**吴梦窗词如七宝楼台，炫人眼目，碎拆下来，不成片段**"** ('The song lyrics of Wu Mengchuang 吴梦窗 are like a tower of seven treasures: they dazzle the eye; taken to pieces, they do not make passages.' (Our translation.)) (a quotation in the usual account) — **the most famous dismantling of a tower in the history of Chinese criticism (enter it first in the history of unfavourable verdicts; the threefold duty of an unfavourable verdict, Chapter 9:** it gave a conclusion and it gave a metaphor, but it did not give a location that can be checked again — so the line of Zhou Ji 周济 and Chen Xun 陈洵 in the Qing was able to reverse the verdict (the usual account of the line)**); **'Feng ru song' 风入松 ('Wind Entering the Pines')** — "**黄蜂频扑秋千索，有当时、纤手香凝**" ('Yellow wasps keep striking the ropes of the swing; there remains, from that time, a fragrance condensed from slender hands.' (Our translation.)) — **a complete chain of evidence: the wasp is striking → because a remainder of fragrance is there → the fragrance comes from slender hands → the person is no longer there:** Wu Wenying compresses causal reasoning (Mill's five methods, in Lesson 5) into a description of physical evidence: for the first time the song lyric possessed a detective's imagination (and the conclusion of the inference is grief: the bees do not know that she is already dead: this is the cruellest "无人会" ('no one understands') in all the song lyrics of the Song**); **the Lingyan Yu preface to 'Basheng Ganzhou' 八声甘州 ('Eight Sounds of Ganzhou'), and "**时靸双鸳响，廊叶秋声**"** ('At times the paired mandarin-duck slippers drag and sound; leaves in the corridor are the sound of autumn.' (Our translation.)) — **a collage of time and space: the sound of wooden clogs in the corridor of resounding clogs and the sound of falling leaves are stacked on the same beat —** Wu Wenying's syntax does not mark time (the tense-switch of the three stanzas, from Chapter 4, is dismantled in him: the reader must change tracks, unaided, among dream, memory and the present:** the density is not a density of diction; it is a density of omitted connectives (the contrary case of the customs house for connectives in Lesson 7: he withdrew the customs house entirely, and so there came the poetic quality of what is smuggled, and a seasick experience of reading**).
:::

## Remnant loyalists: the poem on an object as a will {#def-yimin}

::: definition Cicadas, the new moon, and the catalogue {#def-yizhu}
***Yuefu buti* 乐府补题 ('Further Topics for the Music Bureau')** (**Wang Yijian 王易简, Wang Yisun 王沂孙, Zhou Mi, Zhang Yan and others wrote poems on objects under separate topics (dragon-spittle incense, the white lotus, the cicada, water shield, the crab, the new moon; the usual account: the compiled text was completed at the turn of the Song and the Yuan) —** one topic, one offering table: after the fall of the Song, the lyricists used the poem on an object as a collective summoning of souls (**the topics are an inventory of sacrificial offerings, and the rhyme words are the order-sheet of the rite: after the state was lost, metre acquired a new function — it was the only device that could still maintain order (the highest respect paid by the study of prosody in Lesson 14:** when reality loses its metre, poetry must keep its metre**)); **Wang Yisun's 'Qitian yue: the cicada' 齐天乐·蝉 ('Music Level with Heaven') and 'Meiwu: the new moon' 眉妩·新月 ('Charm of the Brows')** (the usual account: longing for the former state is lodged in the poem on an object) — **the strength of metaphor is in direct proportion to the degree to which politics cannot be spoken (the closing of the genealogy of the oblique brush in Lesson 16:** the most reticent style of writing appears in the most dangerous age; reticence (*hánxù* 含蓄) is not a trait of character, it is pressure**); **Zhou Mi compiled *Juemiao haoci*, and wrote *Wulin jiushi* 武林旧事 ('Former Events of Wulin') and *Qidong yeyu* 齐东野语 ('Rustic Words from East of Qi'); Zhang Yan's *Ciyuan*, and his song lyrics after he entered the Yuan** ("**无风也自垂**" ('Even with no wind, it hangs of itself.' (Our translation.)) and forlorn, scattered phrases of that kind; the old reading of the text in circulation takes them as lodging a longing for the former state (we take the usual account) — **the second profession of the remnant loyalist (*yímín* 遗民) is that of the scholar of documents: compiling anthologies of song lyrics, writing memories of the old capital, laying down the law for the study of the song lyric:** when memory cannot be carried on, one changes over to making a record (the late-Song version of Li Qingzhao's 李清照 inventory in Chapter 3:** an inventory is a state that refuses to fall apart**).
:::

::: example Appeal and memorial rite {#ex-shensu}
Xin Qiji's 辛弃疾 'Yongyu le: Jingkou' 永遇乐·京口 ('Joy of an Eternal Meeting') ("凭谁问，廉颇老矣，尚能饭否" 'On whom can one rely to ask: Lian Po 廉颇 is old — can he still eat?' (Our translation.)) and Jiang Kui's 'Yangzhou man' ("念桥边红药，年年知为谁生" 'I think of the red peonies beside the bridge; year after year, do they know for whom they grow?' (Our translation.)) both write of the 'former state' (故国). Fill in a table of three columns: **① the addressee, ② the direction in time, ③ the action demanded**; and answer: **why one is asking a person, and the other is asking flowers**.
::: solution
**① Addressee** — **Xin is asking the court (an application form in the shape of a question; the enclosure is a historical precedent); Jiang is asking the flowers (the red peonies know nothing; to ask them is to talk to oneself:** one asks the flowers because there is no one to ask, and there is no one to ask because the court does not want to listen**); ② **Time** — **Xin points forward (the northern expedition has not yet taken place; policy options still remain); Jiang points backward (1176 is half a century from Jingkang; "淮左名郡" ('the famed commandery of Huai-left') has already become the past:** the former is still at the negotiating table, the latter is already in the museum**); ③ **The action demanded** — **Xin demands an allocation of funds, a levy of troops, and authorisation; Jiang demands only "记得" ('remember') (**no one answers "知为谁生" ('know for whom they grow'); the reader is forced to answer in their place: Jiang Kui's technique is to rule the reader's absence an accomplice**); **Overall verdict** — **the division of labour between appeal and memorial rite:** an appeal assumes that the institution can still be negotiated with; a memorial rite assumes that the institution can no longer reply; the former is the literature of policy, the latter is the literature of memory (this chapter is therefore the closing lesson of Southern Song literature: when appeal stops, literature moves into the memorial hall, and from then on keeps a community's accounts in balance by elegance, by metre, and by metaphor**).
:::
:::

::: intuition 'Clear' is the fineness of the currency after a contraction of credit {#int-qing}
**The "清" ('clear'), "冷" ('cold') and "空" ('empty') of Jiang's song lyrics are not a preference in rhetoric; they are a balance sheet** (**he does not write wealth and rank (he is not qualified), does not write anger (there is a risk), does not write intense feeling (it would injure his standing) — once the three items have been deducted, what remains is 'clear and empty' (the reverse proposition of Lesson 1's 'leaving a blank is a luxury':** even a pure guest's blank space has been compressed: his wild cloud flying alone is an aeroplane with no airport (a late-Song near relation of the double readership in occupied territory, in Lesson 16: the absence of anyone to write for shaped the style**));
**The lifespan of 'clear and empty' in the history of criticism** (**the study of the song lyric thereafter (the Zhexi school of the Qing took Jiang and Zhang as its ancestors (the usual account of the line)) called this standard into use again and again —** an aesthetic of a fallen state was honoured by later ages as the highest virtue: this is the least decent, and the most true, phenomenon in the history of literature:** elegance is often the dividend of those who lost (the extreme case of Lesson 2's 'the classic = recognition after the fact' (经典=追认): the person who recognises after the fact does not have to answer for the content that is recognised**.
:::

::: history The last few decades {#sec-houshi}
**About 1155–1221** Jiang Kui (**a commoner, a pure guest, self-composed tunes, *gongche* notation transmitted —** there existed in the Song a kind of man of letters with no office and yet with the power to legislate: music was his only property right**); **about 1200–1260** Wu Wenying (**he entered the private secretariats of Suzhou and Hangzhou as a guest clerk:** the appearance of the professional lyricist: the writing of song lyrics became a professional skill for which one was hired (the economy of the patron household = the social security of literature in the late Southern Song**)); **1259–1260** two deaths while Wu Wenying was in the secretariat at Hangzhou (the usual account: a secondary wife and a serving concubine died one after the other, and many of his later song lyrics are works of mourning — **the biographical layer must be handled in separate layers: his mourning lyrics are literary evidence, not evidence of the household register (the layering clause of Lesson 1**); **1276** Lin'an falls; **the 1280s–1290s** the matched compositions of *Yuefu buti*, the compiling of *Juemiao haoci*, and the completion of *Ciyuan* (**the usual account of the line:** the twenty years after the loss of the state were the harvest season of the Southern Song study of the song lyric — not a peak of creation, a peak of putting in order (the inertia of a civilisation turns creation into cataloguing**); **the 1290s–1300s** Zhang Yan returns from his journey to the north, drifts, and dies (**Shanzhong baiyun* 山中白云 ('White Clouds in the Mountains'): a nobleman of the former dynasty got by selling divination —** the textbook he wrote for the Song song lyric was written in an age when there was no song lyric to write**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls in this chapter
**The laziness of the 'decline' thesis** (**closing the case with the single sentence "南宋词不如北宋" ('Southern Song song lyrics are not as good as Northern Song song lyrics.' (Our translation.)):** a turn in style is the result of a turn in institutions (poetry societies, patron households, the loss of the state), not an ebb of genius (the clause from evolutionary theory in Lesson 15: a genre does not grow old; there is only a further division of labour**)); **Treating "清空／质实" ('clear and empty / solid and substantial') as a pair of scales** (**Zhang Yan's metaphor is rhetoric, not an instrument: the verdict on the seven-jewelled tower lacks a comparison and lacks a location (Chapter 9's duty of an unfavourable verdict):** an unfavourable verdict of the rank of a famous line may also be an assignment that does not pass, and reversing a verdict is the ordinary metabolism of the history of criticism**)); **Reading the poem on an object by searching out a hidden meaning** (**finding an emperor for every cicada:** the boundary of a reading for what is lodged in the poem (*jìtuō* 寄托, 'lodged meaning') is the stable posture of the work as a system (Lesson 4) + the shared rite of this set of topics: the 'collectivity' of *Yuefu buti* does indeed raise the probability of a lodged meaning, but the specific referent of a single piece is in most cases not ascertainable (the usual account: the occasion (*běnshì* 本事) is not known**)); **Ignoring the presence of music** (**the exacting fineness of prosody in the song lyric of the late Southern Song is not pedantry:** the song lyric could still be sung, or at least chanted; the care taken over the four tones is a legacy of the ear, not a cleanliness obsession of the page (the last lesson of Lesson 14 within the Song**).
:::

::: exercise Writing a chain of evidence {level=1}
On the model of "蜂扑—余香—纤手" ('wasp striking — a remainder of fragrance — slender hands'), write a passage of 60 characters: from an observable detail of the present moment, infer backwards to a person who is not present. Psychological verbs such as "想念" ('to long for') and "记得" ('to remember') are not to appear.
::: solution
Marking: **the chain of inference must be separable into three stages** (**observation → a hypothesis about the cause → the person is already absent**); **the ban on psychological verbs** (**break the ban and the whole difficulty is lost:** letting the object grieve in your place is the proper work of the poem on an object (the working version of the transfer of feeling (*yíqíng* 移情) in Lesson 12**).
:::

::: exercise Short essay: the dividend of elegance {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**clear and empty is the fineness of the one who has lost: when a person has no office, no army, and no future that can be negotiated, the only thing he can still control is the cleanness of sound — the song lyric of the late Southern Song called this cleanness a virtue, and later ages honoured it as the highest taste; a history of the study of the song lyric is a history of people continually translating powerlessness into grace**". You must call on at least six items: **the three-piece set of poetry society, anthology and lyric metre**, **music with no airport**, **the chain of evidence**, **the order-sheet of the sacrificial rite in *Yuefu buti***, **the remnant loyalist's second profession, as a scholar of documents**, **the reversal of the verdict on the seven-jewelled tower**; and adjudicate: **is 'political oppression gives birth to an aesthetic of reticence' a law that can be generalised, or a narrative after the fact — for each, one example must be given of material outside the Song song lyric (any one of calligraphy, drama, or modern and contemporary literature), as a comparison experiment**.
::: solution
Points for marking: the six mechanisms (**institution, property right, the detective, the sacrificial offering, making a record, reversal of the verdict**); the adjudication (**for reference: on the side of a law — high pressure → a rise in the density of metaphor appears repeatedly at the end of the Song, the end of the Yuan, and the beginning of the Qing (the usual account of the line: literary inquisition, and the waxing and waning of *bi* and *xing* (comparison and evocation), are a topic historians often discuss; the present text only records that this is a commonplace); on the side of narrative — 'translating powerlessness into grace' is a crowning by people who come later; most of those who were there only wanted to go on living and to keep their works:** that oppression gives birth to reticence is a statement of a mechanism; calling reticence a virtue is a narrative; the two must be kept in separate columns (an example of the comparison experiment: the calligraphy, in the line of Fu Shan 傅山 and Bada Shanren 八大山人, of "宁拙毋巧" ('rather clumsy than clever') (the usual account of the line: loyalist calligraphic style bound to the narrative of moral integrity) — the mechanism can be copied; the person who sets the value changes: marking looks at whether one can tell 'what happened' apart from 'who is giving the marks'**).

::: summary
The reckoning, filed: institutions (**poetry society + anthology + lyric metre = peer review**), Jiang Kui (**asking the flowers, making the tune, an aeroplane with no airport**), Wu Wenying (**a detective's imagination, and a poetic quality that has dismantled the customs house**), the remnant loyalists (**cicadas, the new moon, the catalogue: an inventory is a state that refuses to fall apart**), criticism (**'clear and empty' is a trademark; the seven-jewelled tower is an assignment; reversing the verdict is metabolism**). The method in one sentence: **when appeal stops, inspect their ledgers: small objects are more honest than large sentences.** The next chapter turns back to the Northern Song: on what grounds Song poetry opened another road.
:::
