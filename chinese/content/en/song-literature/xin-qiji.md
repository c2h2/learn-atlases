title: "Xin Qiji and the bold style"

The end of the previous chapter said that, after the warehouse burned, literature had nothing left but recitation; this chapter writes of a man who had not even an army, and had only the song lyric (*ci*) left — **the whole of Xin Qiji's 辛弃疾 art of the song lyric can be read as the interest generated by those two policy essays of his that were not adopted: the empire froze the account of armed force, and he made a substitute withdrawal in lines of unequal length** (*changduanju*, another name for the song lyric).

## Definition: the policy essays that were refused {#def-celun}

::: definition One person's two documents {#def-liangfenshu}
**《美芹十论》 (*Meiqin shilun*, 'Ten Essays Offering Celery') (1170) and 《九议》 (*Jiu yi*, 'Nine Proposals') (the year of presentation differs slightly in the usual account)** (**a complete strategic document: take Qi, hold Lu, pacify the Jin, establish military-agricultural colonies, spread rumours, mobilise labour —** the chain of reasoning is tight enough to be set straight into the template of a “政策主张” ('policy claim') in Course 4 of this curriculum (matching against the four types: this is a textbook-grade policy claim, with an executing agent and a budget)**); **the court's reply** (**it commended his “壮声” ('a vigorous voice') and shelved his scheme for an indefinite period —** note this way of handling the matter: it does not negate your claim; it only arranges your term of office (Lesson 6, “驳不掉的就不采用” ('what cannot be refuted is not adopted'): the most economical veto is praise**); **and so there occurred the most profitable currency exchange in the history of Chinese literature** (**the whole technique of the policy essay (a check of the materials, a calculation of advantage and harm, tables of the enemy's situation, the language of mobilisation) was transferred into lines of unequal length:** Xin Qiji's song lyrics are not a poet's by-product; they are a policy analyst's reusable software (Lesson 2, “论点要有输的可能” ('a claim must have the possibility of losing') — the win he wanted most in his whole life could not be realised on paper, so on paper he wrote it so that it looked as much as possible like the real thing**.
:::

::: definition The *guizhengren*: the clause of his status {#def-guizheng}
**“归正人”** (*guìzhèngrén*, 'a person who has returned to the rightful dynasty') (**Xin was classed as a “归正人” (the official designation for those who came south from the north; the usual account: the Song court both used this group and guarded against it) —** this one status determined the performance assessment of his whole life: what others were assessed on was administrative achievement; what he was assessed on was the sustainability of his loyalty**); **his own note to the events of his youth** (**《鹧鸪天》 (*Zhegutian*, 'Partridge Sky'), “**壮岁旌旗拥万夫，锦襜突骑渡江初**” — In the years of my strength, banners and flags crowded ten thousand men; in brocade jackets, with charging cavalry, I first crossed the river. (Our translation.) — the anti-Jin incident in Geng Jing's 耿京 army in 1162, and the binding of Zhang Anguo 张安国 and the return south (historical fact in the usual account):** his song lyrics often carry their own footnotes to a curriculum vitae: when other people use an allusion, they use the ancients; he uses his own file (Chapter 3: Li Qingzhao 李清照 testifies with a catalogue; Xin Qiji testifies with a battle report: both of them are finding, for the expression of feeling, proof that can be checked**); **《水龙吟·登建康赏心亭》 (*Shuilong yin*, 'Water Dragon's Chant: Climbing Shangxin Pavilion at Jiankang')** — “**把吴钩看了，栏杆拍遍，无人会、登临意**” — I have looked the Wu hook over, and slapped the railing all along; no one understands the intent of this ascent. (Our translation.) — **a weapon inspected over and over, and never drawn from the scabbard:** the use of the weapon has degenerated into a rhetorical prop (this is the core sentence of the whole lesson: the technical starting point of the bold style (*hàofàng* 豪放) is the inflation of armed force — if the blade does not cut anything, one can only look, and must slap, must slap the railing). 吴钩 ('a curved blade of Wu').
:::

## The perfect of using prose as the song lyric {#def-yiwen}

::: definition Four techniques {#def-sixiang}
**① Using argument as the song lyric** (“**凭谁问：廉颇老矣，尚能饭否**” — On whom can I rely to ask: Lian Po is old now; can he still eat? (Our translation.) (1205, 《永遇乐·京口北固亭怀古》 (*Yongyu le*, 'Joy of Eternal Union: Recalling Antiquity at Beigu Pavilion in Jingkou')) — **the ending is an application form in the shape of a question: density of allusion = the number of attachments to a written appeal (the whole lyric fires off five or six allusions in a row, and each one proves “我有先例、请对号入座” ('I have a precedent; please find your number and sit down'))**); **② Bringing the manner of ancient-style prose into the song lyric** (**turns on function words, sentence-endings in 之 and 矣, and the lead-in characters of loose sentences —** the breathing of the song lyric was changed into the breathing of a memorial: people of the Song already suspected that it was “非本色” ('not the proper colour'; *bense*) (the usual line of account):** “不本色” ('not in the proper colour') is precisely the category under which he files: he changed the tax category of the song lyric from words-for-singing to the prose composition (the two pieces of legislation in Chapter 3; he transacts his business at a third window: he heeds neither the musical rules, nor the house rules, but practical achievement (事功)**); **③ Using the Classics and the Histories as material** (**the military use of shaking the book-bag —** an allusion is not decoration; it is ammunition: each allusion is a usable precedent (the soldier's version of Lesson 7, “引经据典是借债要还” ('to cite the Classics and deploy allusions is to borrow, and the debt must be repaid'): he can afford to repay, because his creditor is history**); **④ Using structure to manufacture a reversal** (**《破阵子》 (*Pozhenzi*, 'Breaking the Battle Array'): eight lines of a war film + one line after waking — “**了却君王天下事，赢得生前身后名。可怜白发生**” — I would finish the ruler's business of all under Heaven, and win a name in life and after death. Pitiable, white hair grows. (Our translation.) —** the more complete the narrative of the first stanza, the crueller the cancellation in the line that follows: this is the song-lyric grammar of “计划失败” ('the plan has failed') (the heroic version of the death-method test in Lesson 2: his claim itself wrote its own refutation**.
:::

::: definition The rule for reading *jituo* {#def-jituo}
**《青玉案·元夕》 (*Qingyu an*, 'Green Jade Table: The Night of the Lantern Festival')** — “**众里寻他千百度，蓦然回首，那人却在，灯火阑珊处**” — A thousand times, a hundred times, I sought that one in the crowd; suddenly I turned my head, and that person was there, where the lantern-light was dying away. (Our translation.) — **Liang Qichao 梁启超 commented “自怜幽独，伤心人别有怀抱” — pitying himself in seclusion; the heartbroken keeps a different thing in the breast. (Our translation.) (the general sense of the comment as usually cited);** the rule for reading “寄托” (*jìtuō*, 'an intent entrusted beyond what is said'): it is not that every lyric has a veiled pointing (that is guessing riddles), but that in the system of his works there is a stable posture of “不与同席” ('not sharing the mat') (the place where the light thins = the position of refusing to enter: **the posture of non-cooperation was written as an aesthetic in the song lyric, because at court it was written as a charge**); **《摸鱼儿》 (*Moyu er*, 'Groping for Fish')** (1179) — “**君莫舞，君不见、玉环飞燕皆尘土**” — Do not dance, sir; do you not see — Yuhuan and Feiyan are both dust. (Our translation.) “**闲愁最苦……受人妒**” — Idle sorrow is the bitterest … suffering another's jealousy. (Our translation.) — **using the fragrant-plant and beautiful-person system of 《离骚》 (*Encountering Sorrow*) as an outer shell, and inside it a draft of an impeachment (the Song-dynasty renewal fee for the lineage of Quzi 屈子 (Qu Yuan) in Lesson 9:** when the cost of direct remonstrance is higher than the cost of allusion, literature receives a transfer payment (the main Song-lyric account of the survival technique of the indirect brush (*qǔbǐ* 曲笔) in Lesson 16**)**); **《菩萨蛮·书江西造口壁》 (*Pusa man*, 'Bodhisattva Barbarian: Written on the Wall at Zaokou in Jiangxi')** — “**青山遮不住，毕竟东流去**” — Green hills cannot block it; in the end it flows away to the east. (Our translation.) — **geography written as a historical judgement:** water is his colleague: it can advance without needing approval (contrast the policy essay: a document needs approval, so it stops in his drawer**.
:::

## Forty years of living in retirement {#def-xianju}

::: definition The pastoral volume of the bold school {#def-tianyuan}
**《清平乐·村居》 (*Qingping yue*, 'Pure Serene Music: Living in the Village') and 《西江月·夜行黄沙道中》 (*Xijiang yue*, 'Moon over the West River: Walking the Huangsha Road at Night')** — “**稻花香里说丰年，听取蛙声一片**” — In the fragrance of the rice-flowers they talk of a bumper year; listen, and take in a whole expanse of frogs' voices. (Our translation.) “**茅檐低小，溪上青青草**” — Thatched eaves, low and small; by the stream, grass green and green. (Our translation.) — **written in the period of living in retirement at Daihu 带湖 and Piaoquan 瓢泉 (the usual account):** one of the best rural song lyrics in Chinese literature, from the hand of a man who had wanted to lead troops (an extreme case of Lesson 15, “风格＝选择函数” ('style = a choice function'): the independent variable of the function is not inclination, but the letter of appointment**); **a technical note** (**《清平乐》 unifies the point of view by “醉” ('drunkenness') (the old man and the old woman in dialogue, and the little boy scooping something up —** wine is a licence to narrate: what a drunkard can say, and can boast of, a sober person cannot bring off: the true protagonist of Xin Qiji's song lyrics of living in retirement is “被允许无事可做” ('being permitted to have nothing to do')**)**); **《贺新郎》 (*He xinlang*, 'Congratulating the Bridegroom'), “我见青山多妩媚，料青山见我应如是”** — I find the green hills full of charm; I reckon the green hills, seeing me, ought to feel just so. (Our translation.) — **extending the relation of colleagues as far as geology:** when the human audience has all left, he applies for a witness from nature for the work (the dialogic quality of the “抒情我” ('the lyric I') in Lesson 15: the interlocutor of Xin's song lyrics moves from ruler and friend, at once demoted and promoted, to the mountain**.
:::

::: example After-action review and drill {#ex-fupan}
Compare 《破阵子》 (醉里挑灯看剑 — Drunk, I trim the lamp and look at the sword. (Our translation.)) with Su Shi's 苏轼 《江城子·密州出猎》 (*Jiangchengzi*, 'Song of the River Town: Hunting at Mizhou') (老夫聊发少年狂 — This old man, for the moment, lets loose a young man's wildness. (Our translation.)). Both are “武事词” ('song lyrics of military affairs'). Make a three-column table: **① narrative position (who is acting), ② direction in time (which way it is going), ③ the account at the ending**; then answer: **why, both being “想打仗” ('wanting to fight'), one is a drill and one is an after-action review**.
::: solution
**① Narrative position** — **Su Shi: the prefect, inside the drill, plays a general (“亲射虎，看孙郎” — In person I shoot the tiger; behold Sun Lang. (Our translation.) “持节云中，何日遣冯唐” — Holding a tally for Yunzhong, on what day will they send Feng Tang? (Our translation.) —** role-play plus waiting for an appointment: the action lies in imagination and in requesting a command; in reality he stands securely off the field**); Xin Qiji: **he really did, at night, lead men and seize someone, so his narrative position is the command post of memory (“八百里分麾下炙，五十弦翻塞外声，沙场秋点兵” — Roast meat is shared out under the banners across eight hundred *li*; fifty strings play through the sounds from beyond the frontier; on the autumn battlefield the troops are mustered. (Our translation.) —** the detail of military procedure is precise enough to resemble a duty roster: the degree to which the detail is grasped exposes the grade of experience**); ② **direction in time** — **Su's song lyric faces forward (hoping to be sent, looking towards the northwest, “会挽雕弓如满月” — I shall draw the carved bow till it is like a full moon. (Our translation.))**: the expectant mode**; Xin's song lyric steps back into the dream and then falls one step further (“了却君王天下事” is already completed in the dream, **and on waking only the cash-flow entry “可怜白发生” remains**); ③ **the account at the ending** — **the ending of Su's song lyric is an uncashed cheque (heroic spirit is a credit limit)**; the ending of Xin's song lyric is a letter confirming a bad debt** (**victory in the dream produces no depreciation; the ageing of reality produces the whole cost**); **overall verdict** — **the boundary between drill and after-action review:** Su Shi writes “我想去” ('I want to go'); Xin Qiji writes “我去过” ('I have been'): the former's bold sweep comes from possibility, the latter's bold sweep comes from what cannot be pursued — the reason the five characters “可怜白发生” are the heaviest words of closing an account in the song lyric of the Song is that they are the only kind that cannot be cashed by means of rhetoric**.
:::

::: intuition The bold style is a substitute withdrawal of frozen assets {#int-tixian}
**Profit and cost of the combined “苏辛” ('Su and Xin') volume** (**binding Su Shi and Xin Qiji into the “豪放派” ('the bold school') (a layered accumulation from Ming and Qing criticism of the song lyric down to modern textbooks; Lesson 2 has already broken one point) —** the profit of the combined volume goes to the textbook, and the cost goes to understanding: Su Shi is an experiment in expanding the capacity of the scholar-official; Xin Qiji is the practical-achievement manner of writing of one who was refused: one holds a licence, one is lodging an appeal (the “豪” ('bold') of the two men is separated by a distance of status**); **his output and his situation** (**more than six hundred song lyrics survive, the first place among writers of the Song (the order of magnitude in the usual account) —** high output and being left idle are two sides of the same thing: what the Song gave a disappointed civil official as compensation was time (salary + a *cilu* stipend (祠禄, a stipend for a nominal shrine office) + not being permitted to countersign documents: the period of political disablement is the period of literary production — “贬官是隐形主编” ('demotion is an invisible editor-in-chief') is upgraded in this chapter to “闲居是隐形生产线” ('living in retirement is an invisible production line') (a renewal fee on the Huangzhou entry in Lesson 2**); **a two-sided audit of “掉书袋”** (*diàoshūdài*, 'shaking the book-bag'; a display of one's reading) (**people of the Song already had the complaint of “好用事” ('a fondness for using allusions') (the usual line of account) — the defence:** density is not an illness; density is anxiety: in an age when only a precedent could move those in power, the expression of feeling had to carry documents (the three grades of language in Lesson 7: he pulled the grade of the song lyric up to the grade of the briefcase; the price was losing a part of his readers, and the gain was leaving the whole of the evidence**.
:::

::: history The life of an appellant {#sec-shengping}
**1140** born in Licheng, Jinan, under Jin occupation (**he studied under Jin rule (the usual account: the line of influence from the literary style of northern scholars in the succession of Cai Songnian 蔡松年 and Dang Huaiying 党怀英) —** there is the north in his cultural accent: later writers of the Southern Song found him “硬” ('hard'); that was his accent, not his fault**); **1161–62** Geng Jing raised an army; he bound Zhang Anguo and returned south (**twenty-one years old:** the peak of the curriculum vitae is at the starting point — for the sixty years after that he was trying throughout to catch up with the prehistory of his own seventeenth year**); **1170** 《美芹十论》; **1179** 《摸鱼儿》 (**“君莫舞” ('Do not dance'): the avenues of speech do business by means of metaphor**); **from 1181** dismissed from his post and living in retirement at Daihu (**about ten years or more:** the character 稼 from this point becomes a pen name: turning retirement into a profession (the self-mockery of “稼轩” (Jiaxuan, 'the Studio of Planting') is a pre-emptive registration in rhetoric: say the self-mockery out to the end first, and ridicule loses its effect (the reverse operation of Lesson 16, “把渠道写进语法” ('write the channel into the grammar'): **write unemployment into the literary name**))); **1192–1203** residence at Piaoquan (**the peak of song-lyric output:** the best village song lyrics of the Song, and a frozen strategist, under the same roof**); **1203–1205** he took office and then left (**recalled to office for Han Tuozhou 韩侂胄:** the war party needed to use him as an endorsement; he needed to borrow a road by which to send troops — both sides played only half the match (this case later became one of the stains on his reputation after his death, the usual line of account)**); **1205** the Jingkou song lyrics (《永遇乐》, 《南乡子》 (*Nanxiangzi*, 'Song of the Southern Country')); **1207** died (**the usual account: on his deathbed he cried “杀贼” ('Kill the thieves') —** the layer of legend appears again (the stratification clause of Lesson 1): this layer does not prove the event; it proves that everyone refused to believe he would die quietly**.
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of Xin's song lyrics
**The postage-stamp reading of the “爱国词人” ('patriotic writer of song lyrics')** (**stick the theme on first and only then read the sentences, so that even 《西江月·夜行黄沙道中》 must be searched for an anti-Jin metaphor:** the boundary for reading *jituo* is whether a stable posture exists in the system of the works, not that the critic needs a theme (the red version of “过度诠释” ('over-interpretation') in Lesson 15**)**); **bold style = loudness** (**reading style as decibels: his one piece 《祝英台近·晚春》 (*Zhu Yingtai jin*, 'Slow Song of Zhu Yingtai: Late Spring') has all along been taken as of a piece with “昵狎温柔，魂销意尽” — unduly familiar and tender, the soul melted and the feeling spent. (Our translation.) (a comment by a writer of the Yuan; the usual account preserves it in the layer of legend and commentary) —** two levels of volume within one collection: to unify his volume under “豪放” is to treat his depression as accompaniment**)**); **taking allusion for a display of learning** (**Xin's use of allusion is often read as a book-bag, or read as scholarship:** ask the function first: is this material ammunition, a precedent, or a talisman of exemption from guilt (the same allusion has different functions in different passages)**); **ignoring his identity as a northerner** (**to treat him only as a writer of song lyrics of the Southern Song is to delete half his file:** he was an official of the Southern Song who grew up under the Jin; his “恢复” ('restoration') is not a slogan; it is an accent, his kin, and a homesickness that cannot be translated (his experience of the northern lands was a scarce asset in the song-lyric world of Jiangnan, and also a lifelong political risk (the *guizhengren* clause)**).
:::

::: summary
The account book, filed: two documents (**policy essay refused → the song lyric bears interest**), status (**the *guizhengren*: what the performance assessment measures is the sustainability of loyalty**), technique (**an application form in the shape of a question, the breathing of a memorial, allusions as ammunition, completeness — cancellation**), *jituo* (**only a stable posture may be read; do not go looking for a metaphor in the frogs' voices**), living in retirement (**write unemployment into the literary name: demotion is an invisible editor-in-chief, and living in retirement is an invisible production line**). Method, in one sentence: **first find the document he was unable to have sealed, and only then read the sentences on which he did set a seal**. The next chapter: Jiang Kui 姜夔 and the late Southern Song — the settling of accounts of the elegant song lyric (*yǎcí* 雅词).
