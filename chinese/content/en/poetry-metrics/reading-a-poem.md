title: "Reading a poem"
---

This is the concluding chapter of the metre course, and the chapter in which the course negates itself: **the first nine chapters taught the instruments; this chapter teaches when to put the instruments away**. First the procedure, then the autopsy knife (the substitution method), and last a rule for self-defence: **every reading must go back to the poem itself and be weighed again**.

## The five-step procedure {#def-wubu}

::: definition Close reading: from genre to the balance {#def-xidu}
**① Identifying the genre** (by which code was this poem written: ancient-style verse, regulated verse, the song lyric (*ci*), or *qu*? In which system does it rhyme? — **settle the genre first, or the charge has no ground: to judge an ancient-style quatrain by the ruler of regulated verse is an error of enforcement (Chapters 3 and 4 handed down two such judgements in succession)**); **② Name and occasion** (the title, the original composition, to whom it was given, in what year — **the text's birth certificate: half the information in 《自京赴奉先县咏怀五百字》 ('Going from the Capital to Fengxian County, Chanting My Feelings, in Five Hundred Characters') is in the title (iron evidence from the lesson on poetry as history)**); **③ The structural map** (in a regulated poem, look at opening, continuation, turn and closure, and at the positions of the parallelism; in a *gexing* (a long song-style poem), count the changes of rhyme and divide the scenes; in a song lyric, look at the stanzas and the lead-in words; in *qu*, look at the beat (*ban*) — **draw the wiring diagram before you judge the lights**); **④ Points where the language is anomalous** (which character in the whole poem "does not look as if it ought to appear": the eye of the poem, function words, displacements, compressions that break the grammar — **the inventory method: find three places where one asks "what would be lost if this were changed for a plain way of saying it"**); **⑤ Checking the ledger** (does the stock of images square with the occasion: is "雁" ('wild goose') used to carry a letter, or as a figure for the writer's own state? Is the allusion used straight, or as a reversal of the received case? — **the acceptance sheet of Chapter 9**). Only when the five steps have been walked through do you open your mouth to judge — **the whole spirit of this procedure is one sentence: describe first, judge afterwards; measure first, express feeling afterwards**.
:::

::: definition The substitution method and the blacklist {#def-tihuan}
**The substitution method** = a test of the poem's translatability: **replace one word with its "equivalent", and measure the cave-in**. In the legend of Wang Anshi's 王安石 line "春风又绿江南岸" ("Again the spring wind greens the southern shore." (Our translation.)), "绿" had once been "到、过、入、满" — **a story of revision is experimental data the ancients left us: the colour-verb makes spring undergo a physical change; the other candidates are only displacement**; "红杏枝头闹春意" (Song Qi 宋祁) ("Red apricot branches riot the feeling of spring." (Our translation.)) — change it for "浓、深、满" ('thick', 'deep', 'full') and it turns at once from a documentary into a statistical report — **Wang Guowei 王国维 said "着一‘闹’字而境界全出" ("Set down the one character 闹, and the whole realm comes forth." (Our translation.); *jìngjiè* 境界, 'world; realm'); in fact the substitution method ran that conclusion out by itself**. **A blacklist of stock phrases**: ① "语言优美流畅" ("the language is beautiful and fluent") → which character? Changed for another, why would it not be beautiful? ② "意境深远" ("the realm is profound"; *yìjìng* 意境, 'the realm of meaning; artistic conception') → profound at which layer: the age of the images' accounts, or a blank in the structure? ③ "表达了作者强烈的思想感情" ("it expresses the author's intense thoughts and feelings") → what **metrical evidence** in the text separates the intense from the slight (an entering tone? reduplicated words? a reach across the line?) — **every stock phrase on the blacklist brings a counter-question that must be answered; fail to answer, and the phrase is withdrawn**. The value of the blacklist is not the satire. It is **forcing the adjectives back into syntax that can be checked**: this course's "沉郁" (*chényù*, 'sombre, pent depth') = layers and folds (a working drawing has already been fitted to it); in the same way, "the language is beautiful" must be translatable into some concrete choice of level and oblique tones, of the place of the rhyme, of a lead-in word, or of the position of a verb, **or else it is the same as not having spoken**.
:::

::: example End-of-term practical: a full five-step scan of 'Climbing High' {#ex-denggao}
"**风急天高猿啸哀，渚清沙白鸟飞回。无边落木萧萧下，不尽长江滚滚来。万里悲秋常作客，百年多病独登台。艰难苦恨繁霜鬓，潦倒新停浊酒杯。**"

The wind is keen, the sky is high, the gibbons' whistle is mournful.
The islet is clear, the sand white, birds fly and wheel.
Boundless, the falling wood comes soughing down;
unending, the Yangtze comes rolling on.
Ten thousand li of autumn grief, and ever a sojourner;
a hundred years of many illnesses, climbing the terrace alone.
Hardship, bitter regret, the frost at the temples thick;
cast down, I have newly stopped the cup of cloudy wine. (Our translation.)

Set the report out by the five steps: ① genre; ② name and occasion; ③ structure; ④ three points of anomaly; ⑤ the ledger. Then an extra question: **this poem is often cited as "metrically perfect" — but why might "perfect" be a misreading?** (Hint: how is the contradiction between Chapter 3's "two machines, irregular fury and crafted steadiness" (拗怒 / 工稳) and step ④ here to be reconciled?)
::: solution
① **A seven-character regulated poem**: the form with an oblique onset that rhymes in the first line ("哀、回、来、台、杯" — **the tenth of the Pingshui rhymes, *hui* (十灰)**: 哀回来台 are all of the *hui* rhyme, and "杯" is of the *hui* rhyme ✓ **one rhyme throughout**; opposition and adhesion both fully met — a re-check of the old scan on file in Chapter 3). ② **Occasion**: climbing high at Kuizhou on the Double Ninth in the second year of Dali — **the season is a clause of the contract: on the Double Ninth one must climb high, and to climb high is to grieve (the memory stored in the genre of poems of ascent: the "long pavilion" logic of Chapter 9)**. ③ Structure: **all eight lines are parallel, and yet each of the four couplets has its own office** — the opening couplet shoots six pieces of scenery in one burst (急, 高, 哀, 清, 白, 回, 'keen, high, mournful, clear, white, wheeling': **density is volume**); the second couplet is a long take on a cosmic scale (无边 / 不尽, 'boundless / unending' = **a double exposure of space and time**); the third couplet moves from the scene into the self (万里 = space, 百年 = time; "常作客", "独登台", 'ever a sojourner', 'climbing the terrace alone' = **one grain of a person set inside the double exposure**); the closing couplet comes down onto the cup (**the law by which the whole poem closes: of all the accounts under heaven, in the end only one cup of cloudy wine is left, and even that cup has been "新停" ('newly stopped') — Du Fu 杜甫 writes a small matter such as "giving up wine" as the last straw of the tragedy: the ending of the epic is an empty cup**). ④ Points of anomaly: **the "独" of "独登台"** (in the parallel position it is set against "常" — frequency against a count of one: **sojourning is the normal state, climbing the terrace is a single instance — two adverbs carry the weight of a life**); **the meanings piled in "苦恨"** (苦 = to the extreme, 恨 = regret: **a modern reader takes it as "pain and resentment", and loses the coldness of degree — to call regret bitter is to regret to the point of reckoning the cost**); **the division of labour between the reduplicated words "萧萧" and "滚滚"** (the **sound** of the falling leaves and the **force** of the river: two words, one imitative of sound and one of shape, each in charge of one sense — **a field test of the substitution method: change 萧萧 for "纷纷" and only the picture is left; the sound is gone**). ⑤ The ledger: **the gibbons' whistle (the stock of allusions to the Three Gorges: "巴东三峡巫峡长，猿鸣三声泪沾裳" — "East of Ba, the Three Gorges; the Wu Gorge is long; the gibbons cry three times, and tears wet the clothes." (Our translation.) — the character "哀" has a prior entry in the accounts)**; falling wood (**"木" = the literary household register of falling leaves, entered since "洞庭波兮木叶下" in the *Nine Songs* — "The waves of Dongting, ah, and the tree-leaves fall." (Our translation.)**) — **"落木" is not written "落叶": 木叶 carries of itself the old account of autumn and of water**. Answer to the extra question: **"perfect" is a misreading of the metrical count — that all eight lines of this poem are parallel is a limit, not a model: its neatness and its violence are of the same magnitude (to use parallelism until no air can pass is itself a cry of what is not at peace: crafted steadiness to the point of suffocation = the suffocation of the content); the "two machines, irregular fury and crafted steadiness" converge here — the highest realm is not keeping the rules or breaking the rules, but letting the rules become the shape of the pressure (the final hearing of the verdict in Chapter 3: the topping-out judgement that "metre is the vessel of grief")**.
:::

::: intuition The operation that removes the method {#int-zhuchu}
The five steps are spectacles, not a filter. **Bao Zhao 鲍照, on the poetry of Xie Lingyun 谢灵运, said "如初发芙蓉，自然可爱" ("Like the lotus just opening, naturally lovely." (Our translation.)) (*Song shu*, the *Book of Song* — an early specimen of impressionistic criticism: no measuring, only smelling the scent)**; Zhong Rong 钟嵘, in *Shipin* (*Grades of Poetry*), and the doctrine of "**直寻**" (*zhíxún*, 'seeking it directly'): good poetry comes from "观今所见" ('looking at what one sees in the present'), and does not rely on "补假" ('patching it out with borrowings') — **the history of criticism itself keeps a strain that is against method**; Yan Yu 严羽: "**羚羊挂角，无迹可求**" ("The antelope hangs by its horns, and no trace can be sought." (Our translation.)) … "**不涉理路，不落言筌**" ("It does not enter the path of reason, nor fall into the fish-trap of words." (Our translation.)) — **a writer of the Song says it outright: the highest place of poetry is not in the readings of the instruments**. This course's settlement: **readings are for self-defence (against stock phrases, against misjudgement, against reading an old poem as a summary in the vernacular); the aesthetic is for handing the paper in** — "**大漠孤烟直**" ("In the vast desert a lone column of smoke stands straight." (Our translation.)). You may analyse its parallelism, its tones and its geometric composition, but what makes a child remember it is always that one look at the desert. Method is scaffolding: **once the building is topped out, the frame has to come down — what is not taken down clean is not a building but a site** (the course page of this volume says "describe first, judge afterwards", and the reverse of that sentence must be remembered too: **when the judging is done, throw the description away, and return to the liking of that one second**).
:::

::: history How this course came to be arranged {#sec-paike}
**Tang**: poetry was **practical business** (the examinations, visits to seek patronage, banquets — nobody wrote a "guide to how to read poetry"; the rules grew in the muscles); **the mid and late Tang to the Five Dynasties**: manuals of *shige* and *shishi* appear (Wang Changling 王昌龄, *Shige*; Jiaoran 皎然, *Shishi* — **the rules degenerate into teaching materials: where muscle memory fails, the notebook form comes on stage**, and the anthology [[tang-literature/high-tang]] does the same work); **Song**: ***Canglang shihua* (*Canglang's Remarks on Poetry*) figures the reading of poetry as "参禅" ('investigating Chan') — the first civil war of methodology (the rules of the Jiangxi school against Yan Yu's wondrous awakening, *miaowu*: this course stands at both ends); commentaries on Du, annotations on Li — the turn towards scholarship and the turn against it set off together**; **Jin and Yuan**: Yuan Haowen 元好问 uses quatrains on poetry in place of prefaces and colophons — poems on poetry, plus the anthologising of criticism of poetry and prose (ways of reading begin to be compiled as rhymed mnemonics); **Ming**: the *gediao* school ('style and tone') legislates for the way of reading ("poetry must be the High Tang" = the judge stands in for the reader); **Qing**: **Wang Shizhen's 王士禛 *shenyun* ('spirit and resonance'), Weng Fanggang's 翁方纲 *jili* ('texture'), Yuan Mei's 袁枚 *xingling* ('native sensibility'), the Tongcheng school's *yifa* ('principles and methods') — four great ways of reading open for business at once: the Warring States era of the history of criticism, which is exactly the proof that there is no "standard way of reading"**; **the 1920s to today**: **writers of the new literature used the Western "细读" ("close reading", on its travels into Chinese) to rewrite the syllabus; textbooks split the poem into four columns, "theme — image — technique — feeling" — the unofficial forerunner of this course's five-step method, and a simplified version of it (its defect is precisely the blacklist: fill the four columns in, and the poem is gone)**; **today**: an AI can, within three seconds, generate "意境深远" ("the *yijing* is profound") — **stock phrases have for the first time reached mass production at zero cost: the blacklist of this course is therefore raised to a survival skill — when praise can be auto-completed, description that can be checked has become the last post left to a human being**.
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of appreciation
**"Bombardment with background" in place of close reading** (**recount the life and the age three times over, and of the poem only one sentence is left: the author's affairs are not the poem's affairs — step 2 only supplies ammunition for step 4; it is not a refuge for the essay**); **closing with a "standard answer"** (**"it expresses … feeling" is the format of a fill-in-the-blank, not a conclusion: any judgement of feeling must point back to evidence in the text (this course's chain of metrical evidence) — otherwise you are working for a dictionary of appreciation**); **taking the instrument for the object by mistake** (**level and oblique tones and rhyme groups from beginning to end, and not one look at the poem: this is reading a blood-test report as a last letter**); **the all-purpose "I feel"** (**feeling is free; argument is not: if reading 《登高》 ('Climbing High') puts me in mind of moving house, I may write an informal essay; I may not write it up as "this couplet of Du Fu's is about moving house" — imagination must apply for a visa; the destination is stamped by the customs of the text**).
:::

::: exercise End-of-term independent paper {level=3}
Choose for yourself one seven-character regulated poem or one long lyric (*manci*). Hand in a **complete five-step report** (≤600 characters) + **the substitution method at two places** (at each, state "what word is changed, and what caves in") + a **self-audit of stock phrases**: from your own previous assignment haul out two sentences of blacklist language and rewrite them as formulations that can be checked. Peer marking by the standard of this lesson: genre, occasion, structure, points of anomaly and the ledger, 20 marks each.
::: solution
Marking: the five steps are all there, and **there is a relation of reference between the steps** (the points of anomaly in step 4 must fall on the map of step 3, and the ledger of step 5 must serve the occasion of step 2 — **the procedure is not five questions; it is one assembly line**); the "cave-in" at the two points of substitution is described concretely (of the order of a loss of sound / a loss of the sense of time / a loss of class colouring); **the self-audit is a test of character for the whole course**: only the person who can strike a killing blow at their own stock phrases counts as having understood the spirit of the blacklist — **what this course opposes is not praise, but praise exempt from inspection**.
:::

::: exercise Short essay: is appreciation a science, or is it appreciation? {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing the claim "**what the metre course teaches is not 'how to praise a poem', but 'how not to let the praise be wrong' — the whole use of method is to issue intuition a receipt that can be audited**". You must call on at least four of: **the mutual checks of the five-step procedure**, **the substitution method**, **"隔 and 不隔 ('veiled' and 'not veiled') = the difference in stock" (Chapter 9)**, **the anti-method case of "羚羊挂角" ("the antelope hangs by its horns")**, **the age in which AI mass-produces stock phrases**; and answer: **if the aesthetic is private in the end, on what ground does this course's "marking" stand** (hint: what is marked is an audit of the process, not taste itself — write that out clearly).
::: solution
Points for marking: the logic of the checks (**the five steps are a system for keeping error out: genre guards against misjudgement, occasion against excess, structure against the plucking of lines, points of anomaly against a reading that slips, the ledger against a forced reading**); the substitution method = **the smallest unit of falsifiability**; the difference in stock (**the verdict "隔" ('veiled') floats with the market of readers — the method measures even the conditions of its own failure: this is the source of its claim to be a science**); the anti-method case (**"羚羊挂角" ("the antelope hangs by its horns") does not negate method; it marks the bounds of method's jurisdiction: the instruments can govern the letter, and cannot govern "no trace" — this course's position: the place with no trace is precisely a boundary condition of the method's design**); the question of marking (**a sample answer: we never mark "that you like it"; we mark "whether your liking can be traced" — the receipt is not the enemy of feeling; it is the accountant of feeling; AI can issue praise, and cannot issue the accounts of "how I checked it in the text" — that is the moat of a humanities course in the age of auto-complete**); if the conclusion can be written "**when you have read to the end of this course, may it be that, at the moment a line of poetry knocks you down, besides crying 'good', you can also say which brick it was that shook the world**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
Closing the ledger: the five steps (**describe first, judge afterwards: one assembly line, not five questions**); the knife (**the substitution method: experimental data the ancients kept themselves; the blacklist forces adjectives back into syntax**); the paper of final judgement (**'Climbing High': crafted steadiness to the point of suffocation = the suffocation of the content — let the rules become the shape of the pressure**); the taking-down (**scaffolding not taken down clean is a site; the moment a line of poetry knocks you down is the certificate that the course is complete**); the accounts (**veiled and not veiled = the difference in stock; what the marking marks is what can be traced, not taste**). One sentence to close the ledger of the ten lessons: **level and oblique tones are the ruler, rhyme is the scale, genre is the code of laws, the image is the currency, method is the spectacles — and poetry is that little something which the scale cannot weigh, the ruler cannot measure, and against which the code of laws sets no guard**. This course ends here; the next volume is the literature of the Song.
:::
