---
title: "Reading a poem"
---

This is the concluding chapter of the metre course, and the chapter in which the course negates itself: **the first nine chapters taught the instruments; this chapter teaches when to put the instruments away**. First the procedure is given, then the autopsy knife (the substitution method), and last a rule of self-defence is laid down: **every reading must go back to the poem itself and be weighed again**.

## The five-step procedure {#def-wubu}

::: definition Close reading: from genre to the balance {#def-xidu}
**① Identifying the genre** (which legal code this poem is written under: ancient-style verse / regulated verse (recent-style poetry) / the song lyric (*ci*) / *qu*? Which system is the rhyme in? — **the genre is settled first, and only then does the charge have grounds: to judge an ancient-style quatrain by the ruler of regulated verse is a fault in enforcing the law (Chapters 3 and 4 decided two cases in succession)**); **② Name and occasion** (the title, the original poem being answered, to whom it was given, in which year — **the birth certificate of the text: half the information in 《自京赴奉先县咏怀五百字》 ('Going from the Capital to Fengxian County, Expressing My Feelings in Five Hundred Characters') is in the title (the iron evidence of the poetry-and-history course)**); **③ A map of the structure** (in a regulated poem, look at opening, continuation, turn and close, and at the positions of the parallelism; in a *gexing*, count the changes of rhyme and divide the scenes; in a song lyric, look at the stanzas and the lead-in characters (*lingzi*); in *qu*, look at the beats (*ban*) — **draw the circuit diagram first, then judge the lighting**); **④ Points of linguistic anomaly** (which character in the whole poem "does not look as if it ought to appear": the eye of the poem, function words, displacement, compression that violates grammar — **the checklist method: find three places of the kind "what would be lost if this were changed for a plain way of saying it"**); **⑤ Checking the ledger** (whether the stock of images (*yixiang*) can be matched to the occasion: is the "雁" ('wild goose') carrying a letter, or a figure for oneself? Is the allusion used straight, or used to overturn the case? **— the acceptance sheet of Chapter 9**). Only when the five steps have been walked through do you open your mouth to evaluate — **the whole spirit of this procedure is one sentence: describe first, judge afterwards; measure first, express feeling afterwards**.
:::

::: definition The substitution method and the blacklist {#def-tihuan}
**The substitution method** = a test of the poem's translatability: **replace one word with its "equivalent", and measure the collapse**. In the legend of Wang Anshi 王安石 and "春风又**绿**江南岸" ('The spring wind again **greens** the bank south of the river.' (Our translation.)), "绿" had once been written "到、过、入、满" — **the story of the revisions is experimental data the ancients left us: the colour-verb makes spring undergo a physical change; the other candidates are only displacement**; "红杏枝头**闹**春意" (Song Qi 宋祁) ('On the red-apricot branch the feeling of spring **clamours**.' (Our translation.)) — change it for "浓、深、满" and at once it turns from a documentary into a statistical report — **Wang Guowei 王国维 said "着一‘闹’字而境界全出" ('Set down the single character "闹", and the whole *jingjie* 境界 ('world; realm') emerges.' (Our translation.)); in fact it is the substitution method that has run by itself and come out with the conclusion**. **Blacklist of stock phrases**: ① "**语言优美流畅**" ('the language is beautiful and fluent') → which character? Changed for another, why would it not be beautiful? ② "**意境深远**" ('the *yijing* 意境 ('the realm of meaning; artistic conception') is deep and far') → deep at which layer: the age of the images' accounts, or a blank in the structure? ③ "**表达了作者强烈的思想感情**" ('it expresses the author's intense thoughts and feelings') → what, in the text, is the **metrical evidence** for the intense as against the slight (an entering tone? reduplicated characters? a crossing of the line?) — **every stock phrase on the blacklist carries a counter-question that must be answered; if it cannot be answered, withdraw the phrase**. The value of the blacklist is not in satire. It is in **forcing the adjectives back into syntax that can be checked**: this course's "沉郁" = layers and turns (a working drawing has been supplied for it); by the same token, "the language is beautiful" must be translatable into some concrete choice among the level and oblique tones, the place of the rhyme, the lead-in character and the position of the verb, **or else it amounts to having said nothing**.
:::

::: example End-of-term practical: a full five-step scan of 《登高》 {#ex-denggao}
"**风急天高猿啸哀，渚清沙白鸟飞回。无边落木萧萧下，不尽长江滚滚来。万里悲秋常作客，百年多病独登台。艰难苦恨繁霜鬓，潦倒新停浊酒杯。**" ('The wind is keen, the sky high, the gibbons' cry mournful; the islet clear, the sand white, birds wheel and return. Boundless, the falling wood comes soughing down; unending, the Yangtze comes rolling on. For ten thousand *li*, grieving at autumn, ever a sojourner; for a hundred years of much illness, climbing the terrace alone. Hardship and bitter regret, the frosted hair at the temples grown thick; cast down, and the cup of cloudy wine newly stopped.' (Our translation.)) Produce the report by the five steps: ① genre; ② name and occasion; ③ structure; ④ three points of anomaly; ⑤ the ledger. Finally an added question: **this poem is often cited as "metrically perfect" — but why might "perfect" be a misreading?** (Hint: how is the contradiction between Chapter 3's "two machines, 拗怒 and 工稳" ('irregular fury' and 'crafted steadiness') and step ④ of this procedure to be reconciled.)
::: solution
① **Seven-character regulated verse**: the pattern that starts on an oblique tone and rhymes from the first line ("哀、回、来、台、杯" — **Pingshui rhyme 10, *hui* (十灰)**: 哀, 回, 来 and 台 are all in the *hui* rhyme, and "杯" is in the *hui* rhyme ✓ **one rhyme from start to finish**; opposition and adhesion both fully met — a re-check of the old scansion case in Chapter 3). ② **Occasion**: climbing high at the Chongyang festival in Kuizhou in the second year of Dali — **the season is a clause of the contract: at Chongyang one must climb high, and in climbing high one must grieve (the generic memory of poems of ascent and prospect: the "long pavilion" logic of Chapter 9)**. ③ Structure: **all eight lines are parallel, and yet the four couplets each have their office** — the opening couplet is six kinds of scene shot in a run (keen, high, mournful, clear, white, returning: **density is volume**); the second couplet is a long take on a cosmic scale (boundless / unending = **a double exposure of space and of time**); the third couplet moves from the scene into the self (ten thousand *li* = space, a hundred years = time; "常作客" ('ever a sojourner') and "独登台" ('climbing the terrace alone') = **one grain of a person set inside the double exposure**); the closing couplet comes down to the cup (**the law by which the whole poem closes: of the accounts under heaven, in the end only one cup of cloudy wine remains, and even this cup has been "newly stopped" — Du Fu 杜甫 writes so small a thing as "giving up wine" as the last straw of the tragedy: the ending of the epic is an empty cup**). ④ Points of anomaly: **the "独" of "独登台"** (in the position of parallelism it is set against "常" — frequency against a solitary number: **sojourning is the normal state, climbing the terrace is a solitary case — two adverbs bear the weight of a life**); **the semantic stacking of "苦恨"** (苦 = extreme, 恨 = regret: **a modern reader reads it as "痛苦与怨恨" ('pain and resentment'), and loses the coldness of degree — to call the regret bitter is to regret to the point of reckoning the cost**); **the division of labour between the reduplications "萧萧" and "滚滚"** (the **sound** of the falling leaves and the **force** of the river: an onomatopoeic word and a pictographic word, each in charge of one sense — **an empirical test by the substitution method: change 萧萧 for "纷纷" and only the picture remains, the sound lost**). ⑤ The ledger: **the gibbons' cry (the stock of Three Gorges allusions: "巴东三峡巫峡长，猿鸣三声泪沾裳" ('At Badong, of the Three Gorges the Wu Gorge is long; the gibbons cry three times, and tears soak the clothes.' (Our translation.)) — the character "哀" has a prior account)**; falling wood (**"木" = the literary household register of falling leaves, entered from "洞庭波兮木叶下" in the 《九歌》 ('On Dongting the waves rise, ah, and the tree-leaves fall.' (Our translation.))**) — **"落木" is not written as "落叶": 木叶 carries of itself the old account of autumn and of water**. Answer to the added question: **"Perfect" is a misreading of the metrical statistics — that all eight lines of this poem are parallel is a limit, not a model: its neatness and its violence are of the same magnitude (to take parallelism until it is airtight is itself a cry of disquiet: crafted steadiness to the point of suffocation = the suffocation of the content); "the two machines, of irregular fury and of crafted steadiness," converge here — the highest realm is not keeping the rules or breaking the rules, but letting the rules become the shape of the pressure (the final review of the verdict in Chapter 3: the capping judgement that "metre is the vessel of grief")**.
:::

::: intuition The operation that excises method {#int-zhuchu}
The five steps are glasses, not a filter. **Bao Zhao 鲍照, judging the poetry of Xie Lingyun 谢灵运, said "如初发芙蓉，自然可爱" ('like a lotus just opening, naturally lovely' (Our translation.)) (the 《宋书》 — **an early specimen of impressionistic criticism: it does not measure, it only smells the scent**)**; Zhong Rong's 钟嵘 《诗品》 and the theory of "**直寻**" (*zhixun*, 'seeking directly'): good poetry comes from "观今所见" ('looking at what one sees now'), and does not rely on "补假" ('patching with the borrowed') — **the history of criticism itself keeps a vein that is against method**; Yan Yu 严羽, "**羚羊挂角，无迹可求**" ('the antelope hangs by its horns, and no trace can be sought' (Our translation.)) … "**不涉理路，不落言筌**" ('it does not tread the path of reason, and does not fall into the fish-trap of words' (Our translation.)) — **a man of the Song says it plainly: the highest place of poetry is not in the readings of the instruments**. This course's terms of reconciliation: **the readings are for self-defence (against stock phrases, against misjudgement, against reading an old poem as a summary in the vernacular), and the aesthetic is for handing in** — "大漠孤烟直" ('In the vast desert a lone column of smoke stands straight.' (Our translation.)) you may analyse for its parallelism, its tones and its geometric composition, but what makes a child remember it is always that one look at the desert. Methodology is scaffolding: **once the building is topped out, the frame is to be taken down — what is not taken down clean is not a building, but a construction site** (the course page of this volume says "describe first, judge afterwards", and the reverse of that sentence is also to be remembered: **when the judging is finished, throw the description away, and return to the liking of that one second**).
:::

::: history How this course came to be arranged {#sec-paike}
**Tang**: poetry was **practical business** (examinations, visits to seek favour, banquets — nobody wrote a "guide to how to read poems"; the rules lived in the muscles); **the mid and late Tang to the Five Dynasties**: the *shige* and the *shishi* appear (Wang Changling's 王昌龄 《诗格》, Jiaoran's 皎然 《诗式》 — **the rules degenerate into teaching material: where muscle memory fails, the notebook form comes on stage**, and the anthology [[tang-literature/high-tang]] does the same work); **Song**: ***Canglang shihua* 《沧浪诗话》 (*Canglang's Remarks on Poetry*) uses "参禅" ('entering Chan') as a figure for reading poetry — the first civil war of methodology (the rules of the Jiangxi school vs Yan Yu's wondrous awakening (*miaowu*): this course stands at both ends); commentaries on Du Fu, annotations on Li Bai — the turn to scholarship and the turn against scholarship set off together**; **Jin and Yuan**: Yuan Haowen 元好问 uses quatrains on poetry in place of prefaces and colophons — poems on poetry + the anthologising of criticism of poetry and prose (ways of reading begin to be compiled into rhymed mnemonics); **Ming**: the school of style and tone (*gediao*) legislates for the way of reading ("诗必盛唐" ('poetry must be of the High Tang') = putting the judge in place of the reader); **Qing**: **Wang Shizhen's 王士禛 *shenyun* 神韵 (spirit and resonance), Weng Fanggang's 翁方纲 *jili* 肌理 ('texture'), Yuan Mei's 袁枚 *xingling* 性灵 (native sensibility), the Tongcheng *yifa* 义法 ('principles and methods') — four great ways of reading open for business at once: the Warring States age of the history of criticism, which proves exactly that there is no "standard reading"**; **the 1920s to the present**: **people of the new literature rewrite the syllabus with the Western "细读" (the travels of close reading into Chinese); textbooks split the poem into four columns, "theme — image — technique — feeling" — the popular forerunner of this course's five-step method, and its simplified version (its fault is exactly the blacklist: fill in the four columns, and the poem is gone)**; **now**: an AI can generate "意境深远" ('the *yijing* is deep and far') within three seconds — **stock phrases have for the first time achieved mass production at zero cost: this course's blacklist is thereby upgraded to a survival skill — when praise can be auto-completed, description that can be checked has become the last post left to a person**.
:::

::: warning Four pits of appreciation
**"Bombardment with background" in place of close reading** (**the life and the age recited three times over, and of the poem only a sentence left: the author's affairs are not the poem's affairs — step 2 supplies ammunition only for step 4; it is not a refuge for the essay**); **sealing it off with a "standard answer"** (**"表达了……感情" ('it expresses … feeling') is the format of a fill-in-the-blank question, not a conclusion: any judgement of feeling must point back to evidence in the text (this course's chain of metrical evidence) — otherwise one is hired labour for a dictionary of appreciation**); **mistaking the instrument for the object** (**level and oblique tones and rhyme groups the whole way through, and not one look at the poem: this is reading a blood-test report as a last letter**); **the all-purpose "我觉得"** (**feeling is free; argument is not: if reading 《登高》 makes me think of moving house, I may write an informal essay; I may not write it as "杜甫此联是写搬家" ('this couplet of Du Fu's is about moving house') — **imagination must apply for a visa; the destination is stamped by the customs of the text**).
:::

::: exercise An independent paper at the end of term {level=3}
Choose for yourself one seven-character regulated poem or one long lyric, and hand in a **complete five-step report** (≤600 characters) + **the substitution method at two places** (at each place state "what word is changed, and what collapses") + **a self-audit of stock phrases**: from your own previous piece of work pull out two sentences of blacklist language and rewrite them as formulations that can be checked. Peer marking by the standard of this course: genre, occasion, structure, points of anomaly and the ledger, 20 marks each.
::: solution
Marking: the five steps are all present, and **there are relations of citation between the steps** (the points of anomaly in step 4 must fall on the map of step 3, and the ledger of step 5 must serve the occasion of step 2 — **the procedure is not five questions; it is one assembly line**); the description of the "collapse" at the two places is concrete (on the scale of losing the sound / losing the sense of time / losing the colouring of class); **the self-audit is a personality test for the whole course**: only a person who can deal a killing blow to their own stock phrases counts as having understood the spirit of the blacklist — **what this course opposes is not praise, but praise exempt from inspection**.
:::

::: exercise Short essay: is appreciation a science, or is it appreciation {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**what the metre course teaches is not 'how to praise a poem', but 'how not to let the praise go wrong' — the whole use of method is to issue intuition with a receipt that can be audited**". You must call on at least four of: **the mutual checks of the five-step procedure**, **the substitution method**, **"隔与不隔 = the difference in stock" ('veiled and not veiled') (Chapter 9)**, **the anti-method case of "羚羊挂角" ('the antelope hangs by its horns')**, **the age of the mass production of AI stock phrases**; and answer: **if the aesthetic is private in the end, on what ground does this course's "marking" stand** (hint: what is marked is an audit of the process, not taste itself — write that out clearly).
::: solution
Points for marking: the logic of the checks (**the five steps are a system for preventing error: genre prevents misjudgement, occasion prevents excess, structure prevents the lifting of lines, points of anomaly prevent a skidding reading, the ledger prevents a forced decoding**); the substitution method = **the smallest unit of falsifiability**; the difference in stock (**the verdict of "隔" ('veiled') floats with the market of readers — the method measures even the conditions of its own failure: this is the source of its being a science**); the anti-method case (**"羚羊挂角" ('the antelope hangs by its horns') does not negate method; it marks the jurisdiction of method: the instruments can manage the letter, and cannot manage "无迹" ('no trace') — **this course's position: the place with no trace is precisely the boundary condition of the method's design**); the question of marking (**a reference answer: we never mark "that you like it"; we mark "whether your liking can be traced" — the receipt is not the enemy of feeling; it is the accountant of feeling; an AI can issue praise, and cannot issue the account of "how I checked it in the text" — this is the moat of a humanities course in the age of auto-completion**); if the conclusion can be written "**having finished this course, may you, when a line of poetry knocks you down, besides crying 'good', also say which brick it was that shook the world**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The account at completion: the five steps (**describe first, judge afterwards: one assembly line, not five questions**), the knife (**the substitution method: experimental data the ancients kept themselves; the blacklist forces adjectives back into syntax**), the final-review paper (**《登高》: crafted steadiness to the point of suffocation = the suffocation of the content — let the rules become the shape of the pressure**), the taking-down (**scaffolding not taken down clean is a construction site; the moment of being knocked down by a line of poetry is the course's certificate of completion**), the accounts (**veiled and not veiled = the difference in stock; what the mark records is what can be traced, not taste**). One sentence for the general account of the ten lessons: **level and oblique tones are the ruler, rhyme is the scales, genre is the legal code, the image is the currency, method is the glasses — and the poem is that little something which the scales cannot weigh, the ruler cannot measure, and for which the legal code sets no guard**. This course ends here; the next volume is Song literature.
:::
