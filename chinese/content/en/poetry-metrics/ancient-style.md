---
title: "Ancient-style verse"
---

The first three chapters built the court of regulated verse; this chapter lets out its control group: **ancient-style verse**. First, one rule for marking the paper: **ancient-style verse is not "a regulated poem (*lüshi*) that was not written up to standard"; it is another paper, running parallel to regulated verse, with a specification of its own** — it keeps the accent of the Han and the Wei, the lineage of the *yuefu* (Music Bureau poems; ballads), and an ambition that only people of the Tang had: **to use the capacity of verse to hold a whole life of prose**.

## Five clauses of clearance {#def-fangxing}

::: definition The identity card of ancient-style verse {#def-shenfen}
After parting from the regulated poem, ancient-style verse moves freely under five clauses: **① Freedom in the number of lines** (anything from four lines to a hundred rhymes will do: the long five-character run of *Journey North* 《北征》, around seven hundred characters, is ancient-style verse); **② Freedom of rhyme** (an oblique-tone rhyme is allowed, an entering-tone rhyme (checked tone) is allowed, and one may **change rhyme part-way through** — carrying one rhyme through to the end is the shackle of regulated verse); **③ Freedom of level and oblique tones** (also called level and deflected; not bound by the four-line pattern, and so **three level tones at the end of a line are no longer a crime, but the signature of ancient-style verse** — the household registration of the genre of 'Quiet Night Thoughts' 《静夜思》 is settled here); **④ Parallelism (the parallel couplet) is not required** (where there is parallelism it is loose; where there is none, the lines run free — non-parallel lines are the formal dress of ancient-style verse); **⑤ Line-forms may be mixed** (three-character, four-character, seven-character and nine-character lines may be loaded together as "mixed-length lines" (*zayan*), and **for the first time the length of a line takes its orders from the breath rather than from a table**). Taken together: **the regulated poem welds all five dimensions shut, and ancient-style verse leaves every one of them as a live opening — the two are not the new and the old, but a division of labour between "the container ship and the bulk carrier"**.
:::

::: definition The *gexing*: a long-haul distribution format {#def-gexing}
The *gexing* form (song and ballad) = **the singable long-distance coach** within ancient-style verse: titles often carry *ge* 歌, *xing* 行, *yin* 吟 or *qu* 曲 (*Song of Everlasting Regret* 《长恨歌》, *The Pipa Ballad* 《琵琶行》, 'Song of the Wandering Son' 《游子吟》), and the technical features are three: **① A change of rhyme = a change of scene** (a change every four lines or every six, level-tone and oblique-tone rhymes alternating — **the moment the rhyme word changes, the lights, the set and the mood all change: the division into chapters in a *gexing* is not a division into lines, but a division into acts**; Cao Pi 曹丕's 'Ballad of Yan' 《燕歌行》, as the first complete seven-character poem now extant, was in the first place a song text of the *yuefu*, [[han-to-nanbeichao/jianan]]); **② It opens on a vocative** (“**君不见**黄河之水天上来” '**Have you not seen** the waters of the Yellow River come down from the sky' (Our translation.); “**听妇**前致词” '**Hear the woman** come forward and speak her piece' (Our translation.) — **the *gexing* keeps the live scene of the art of speaking-and-singing: a listener is named, and the poem is spoken to him**); **③ Mixed line-length = the length of a breath** (*The Road to Shu Is Hard* 《蜀道难》 runs now long, now short, from four characters to nine: **the three characters of exclamation “噫吁嚱” are a free volume outside the table — the saw-tooth of feeling has to be recorded with the saw-tooth of line-width**). **The registry of "taking prose as poetry"**: Han Yu 韩愈 pours the narrative procedure of prose, and function words, into the poem (“**横空盘硬语，妥帖力排奡**” '**Hard phrases coil across the void; set true, their force heaves and shoves.**' (Our translation.) is the placard he set up for other people's poems; *Song of the Stone Drums* 《石鼓歌》 writes a **report of a visit** to one artefact as a long rhymed piece — **conjunctions, the sentence order of prose, and the tone of the study of metal and stone enter the poem: from then on the writers of the Song were connected to the head office's water supply**).
:::

::: example The emotional ledger of a change of rhyme: 'Bring in the Wine' {#ex-jhuanun}
The whole poem is something over a hundred characters, and the rhyme words change through several rounds (the rough contour: it **opens the rhyme on the level tone** (来, 回) → **turns oblique** (发, 雪, 宴, 欢) → **turns again** (真, 有人, 尘, 身) → **closes on the level tone** (雷, 酣, 岸, 换, 愁) — **a great loop of level, oblique, oblique, level, a textbook change of rhyme in the *gexing***). Please: ① divide the whole poem into acts by rhyme section, and mark the "plot" of each act; ② explain **why “与尔同销万古愁” ('With you I will melt away the sorrow of ten thousand ages.' (Our translation.)) must land on a level-tone rhyme to which the poem has already changed** (for comparison: if the whole poem rhymed on one entering-tone rhyme throughout, what would the effect be); ③ put "changing rhyme" together with the clause on the "turn" (*zhuan*) in the previous chapter, and sum up **why the unit of rhythm in ancient-style verse is the "rhyme section" and not the "line"**.
::: solution
① A reference division into acts: **the opening act** (the two “君不见” 'Have you not seen' (Our translation.): the Yellow River entering the sea, black silk turning to snow — the level tone opens the rhyme and opens a large question) → **the act of pressing wine** (“人生得意须尽欢” 'When life goes to one's liking, one must drink joy to the full.' (Our translation.); “会须一饮三百杯” 'We must drink three hundred cups at a single sitting.' (Our translation.) — it turns into the oblique tone; the passage that pounds the table uses short sounds) → **the act of resentment** (“钟鼓馔玉不足贵，但愿长醉不用醒” 'Bells, drums, and fare like jade are not worth prizing; I would rather stay long drunk and have no need to wake.' (Our translation.); “圣贤寂寞” 'Sages and worthies are left in silence.' (Our translation.); “饮者留名” 'The drinker leaves a name.' (Our translation.) — the oblique tone continues, and the voice sinks) → **the act of settling the account** (from “主人何为言少钱” 'Why should the host speak of having too little money?' (Our translation.) to “**与尔同销万古愁**” '**With you I will melt away the sorrow of ten thousand ages.**' (Our translation.) — it changes back to the level tone; the long melody of the You rhyme (尤) is drawn out far). ② **One entering-tone rhyme all the way through = a banquet that will not break up, stuck in the throat**: urgent, clipped, angry; falling at the end on the level-tone You rhyme (愁) **exchanges anger for a long note of melancholy** — the sense of time in “万古” ('ten thousand ages') is dragged out precisely by the way "ou" opens and then gradually closes: **a change of rhyme packs emotion separately into several pages one can turn; choosing the level tone for the last rhyme is deciding "with what bearing one finishes speaking"**. ③ The unit of the regulated poem is the line (every line complies); the unit of ancient-style verse is the rhyme section: **within a single rhyme one may freely lose adhesion (the tonal link between lines), write non-parallel lines, and mix long and short — the constraint has been moved onto "what sound this section uses": ancient-style verse gives up dictatorship at the micro level and institutes a federation at the macro level**.
:::
:::

::: definition The double industry of ancient-style verse {#def-chanye}
**Industry one: carrying history.** The official vehicle of "poetry as history" (*shishi*) is five-character ancient-style verse, not the regulated poem: *Journey North* and *Going from the Capital to Fengxian County, Chanting My Feelings: Five Hundred Characters* 《自京赴奉先县咏怀五百字》 need **titles in the manner of an account of conduct (*xingzhuang*), narration in the manner of a running account, and a verbatim record of dialogue** (the Three Officers and the Three Partings, 三吏三别) — **the real estate of a regulated poem's eight lines cannot hold an An-Shi Rebellion; freedom in the number of lines in ancient-style verse is an extension of the archive** (Du Fu 杜甫's new-title *yuefu*, which "names the piece from the event"; the New Yuefu of Yuan Zhen 元稹 and Bai Juyi 白居易, “**文章合为时而著，歌诗合为事而作**” '**Writings ought to be composed for the times; songs and poems ought to be made for events.**' (Our translation.), institutionalised this production chain: ancient-style verse + the old bottle of the *yuefu* + the new wine of current affairs, [[tang-literature/mid-tang]]). **Industry two: the return to the ancient.** Chen Zi'ang 陈子昂's 'Moved by Encounters' 《感遇》 and Li Bai 李白's fifty-nine 'Ancient Airs' 《古风》 — **using ancient-style verse to "restore the Airs and the Odes, comparison and evocation" (*feng*, *ya*, *bi*, *xing*): ancient-style verse naturally carries the copyright term of the Han and the Wei, and to write it is to claim a critical tradition** (the armoury for "contending for the world with *fenggu*" (*fenggu*: 'wind and bone'; vigour of feeling and structure) — [[tang-literature/early-tang]]); **one and the same set of ancient-style verse, Du Fu takes and loads with news, Li Bai takes and loads with a banner: genre is a platform, and the loading policy is set by the author**.
:::

::: intuition The cost of freedom {#int-chengben}
Each of the five freedoms of ancient-style verse is marked with a price: **freedom in the number of lines → there is no fair ring in which "twenty-eight characters decide who wins and who loses", and long and short must plead their own case**; **freedom to change rhyme → the sense of unity in the sounds is outsourced to a sense of structure, and a writer with no structure falls to pieces** (the inferior *gexing* is precisely "the rhyme is on the move, the poem is not"); **freedom of level and oblique tones → one loses the automatic beautifying that regulated verse does, and whether a line is any good depends entirely on the syntax itself — so "the craft of people of the Tang outside the tonal rules" is precisely the bare examination that tests mastery**; **freedom of mixed line-length → lose the music, and it becomes prose broken into lines**. Hence an interesting phenomenon in literary history: **the masters of ancient-style verse are all people who also score full marks on regulated lines (Li Bai and Du Fu alike) — freedom is a qualification one first earns, and only then a privilege one exercises; the hardest reach is "writing ancient-style verse with the ear of regulated verse": where the three level tones at the end of a line fall is a letting-go chosen with care** (“**宽于言者，严于法**” '**Ample in words, strict in method.**' (Our translation.) — the companion assignment, in poetry, of “唯陈言之务去” 'Let one's only task be to get rid of stale phrasing.' (Our translation.) in Han Yu's prose).
:::

::: history The ageing of the account {#sec-zhangling}
**Han**: five-character ancient-style verse comes to maturity (the *Nineteen Old Poems* 《古诗十九首》 — **the first batch of originals of literati five-character verse, with no tune one could harmonise them to**, [[han-to-nanbeichao/nineteen-old-poems]]); **Wei to Chen**: Cao Pi's 'Ballad of Yan' is the first complete seven-character poem, and Cao Zhi 曹植's bone and spirit flourish together — **the two founding shops of ancient-style verse**; **Jin 晋, Song and Qi**: verse of arcane discourse (*xuanyan*), landscape, and the new departures of Yongming — **the sprout of regulated verse grows out from a side branch of ancient-style verse** ([[han-to-nanbeichao/landscape-yongming]]); **Tang**: after regulated verse (recent-style poetry) had taken its fixed form, **ancient-style verse was for the first time recognised as "another institution" — a genre acquires self-consciousness only when it is being replaced**; Chen Zi'ang and Li Bai took the return to the ancient as their banner, Du Fu enlarged its use by narration, and Han Yu, by taking prose as poetry, exhausted its variations — **the Tang ran, at the same time, through all three ways of living open to ancient-style verse**; **Song**: Su Shi 苏轼, Lu You 陆游 and Yang Wanli 杨万里 were prolific in ancient-style verse — **the main force of Song poetry is in fact ancient-style verse and the half-ancient, half-regulated: a dynasty's choice of examination paper is just like an author's choice of tense**; **Jin 金, Yuan, Ming and Qing**: ancient-style verse turns into **an "impression show of the accents of an age", imitating the Han and imitating the ancient** (the turning of *yuefu* titles into a museum) — **the last stage of a genre's lifespan is an exhibition of rhetoric**; **Today**: **"*gufeng* lyrics" have caught the shell of the *gexing* (lines of unequal length + a change of rhyme + the vocative), while regulated lines have withdrawn to the place of inscribed dedications — ancient-style verse is brought back to life by music once again: the last upgrade of its distribution format**.
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls in reading ancient-style verse
**Using the ruler of regulated verse to judge ancient-style verse "in breach of the metre"** (**identify the paper before you mark it — every place in Chen Zi'ang's 'Song on Climbing Youzhou Terrace' 《登幽州台歌》 where adhesion is lost or the rhyme runs outside its group is a "lawful freedom": marking Li Bai with the examination paper, the warning of the previous chapter carries double force in this one**); **thinking that "freedom = anything goes"** (**a *gexing* has no tonal rules, but it has a method of composition: where the rhyme changes, the saw-tooth of line length, the placing of the vocative — to give up constraints at the micro level is to add to responsibility at the macro level**); **taking "taking prose as poetry" as pejorative** (**it is an increment: prose syntax entering the poem brings speed of narration and an air of metal and stone; its failures (sliding into rhymed writing broken into lines) are counted separately — what is judged is the execution, not the programme**); **ignoring the layers of allusion in ancient-style verse** (**the "ancient" in ancient-style verse lies half in the lexicon: it is fonder than the regulated poem of Han and Wei precedents and of function words — a genre brings its own accent of an age, and to come upon “焉哉乎也” is not to pad out the characters; it is the patina of imitating the ancient**).
:::

::: exercise Changing rhyme for the mood {level=1}
Write a free poem in the ancient manner of about twelve lines: **you must change rhyme twice, and each change of rhyme must correspond to one "turning of the page in the plot"**; mark, for each act, whether its rhyme group is level or oblique, and the name of the plot. In peer comment: try deleting one of the changes of rhyme — does a corner of the plot collapse?
::: solution
Marking: **the place where the rhyme changes = a plot point that can be named** (a switch of viewpoint, a jump in time, a change of temperature — a change of rhyme with no plot = changing the tyre without moving the car); **the choice of a level-tone or an oblique-tone rhyme serves the plot** (the entering tone for what is urgent, the level tone for what is far — use the "temperature chart" of the previous chapter); whoever passes the "collapse test of deleting a rhyme change" gets full marks — **proof that your structure grows on the rhyme, and is not pasted onto the rhyme**.
:::
:::

::: exercise Two papers for the same journey {level=2}
Choose one real journey (a commute, a climb up a mountain, a night shift), and **write two versions**: A, a seven-character regulated poem (compliant in every respect: four couplets, parallelism in the middle two couplets, one rhyme throughout); B, a *gexing* (two or more changes of rhyme, mixed line-lengths allowed, a vocative of the order of “君不见” 'Have you not seen' allowed). Append a note of fifty characters: **which materials can enter only version B, and why**.
::: solution
Marking: version A **passes the medical for regulated verse** (all four layers are checked: the lone level tone, three level tones at the end of a line, adhesion and opposition, parallelism); version B **has a method of composition** (the changes of rhyme have a plot, the lengths of the lines have a breath); the note hits the essence: **a sequence of events in the manner of a running account, a tone of speaking to someone, a cry of surprise that comes too fast to be made symmetrical — these are lawful residents of ancient-style verse, and structures put up in breach of the rules in a regulated poem** (the hands-on judgement on "the container ship and the bulk carrier").
:::

::: exercise Short essay: genre is an institution {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing "**the regulated poem is the poetry of the imperial examination system; ancient-style verse is the poetry of the scholar's private archive — two genres, two regimes of governance**". You must draw on at least five items: **the five clauses of clearance**, **division into acts by a change of rhyme**, **the vehicle of "poetry as history" (the account of the archive)**, **the archaising banner of 'Ancient Airs' and 'Moved by Encounters'**, and **"a genre acquires self-consciousness only when it is being replaced"**; and deal with one counter-argument: **the ancients did not write according to our "institution of genre" (famous pieces are for the most part "breaks with the form", *poti*) — does the institutional theory overestimate the rules?**
::: solution
Points for the mark: the correspondence of institutions (**regulated verse = a form that can be marked in batches → examinations and the selection of officials; ancient-style verse = impossible to standardise → pleading one's own case, recording events, imitating the ancient: a division of genres is a division of governance**); the counter-argument from breaking the form (**Answer one: the real work of a rule is that there exists "the possibility of being broken" — the shock of breaking the form presupposes that the institution exists; Du Fu's "irregular indignation" (*aonu*) is an achievement because the tonal rules are standing beside it; Answer two: grant that the institutional theory is a retrospective putting-in-order — classification is criticism's ledger after the event, and creation is the cash flow of the moment: both accounts must be kept**); if the conclusion is written "**genre is neither a cage nor a suggestion; it is a pricing mechanism: it decides which kind of freedom is worth how much money**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The bulk carrier settles its account: the identity card (**five live openings — freedom is a specification, not a mistake**), the *gexing* (**division into acts by a change of rhyme, the vocative there on the scene, line-width recording feeling**), taking prose as poetry (**Han Yu starts the work, the people of the Song take over the firm: the increment of narration and of function words**), the industries (**Du Fu loads news, Li Bai loads a banner — genre is a platform, and the loading policy is set by the author**), the cost (**giving up constraints at the micro level = adding to responsibility at the macro level: ample in words, strict in method**). The method, in one sentence: **first ask which country's flag this ship is flying (the genre), then judge whether it runs well**. The next chapter leaves the country of poetry: the song lyric (*ci*) — a genre that hands in its homework according to the tune title (*cipai*).
:::
