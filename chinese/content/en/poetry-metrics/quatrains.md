title: "Quatrains"
---

The quatrain (*jueju*) is **half a machine** of the regulated poem (*lüshi*): four lines, twenty-eight characters, and parallelism (the parallel couplet) not required — **it looks as though the work has been lightened, when in fact the whole difficulty has been compressed into the single question of "how to stop"**. This chapter takes two things apart: **the factory settings of the regulated quatrain (*lüjue*), and the other universe of the ancient-style quatrain (*gujue*)**.

## Half a machine {#def-bantai}

::: definition The regulated quatrain: a complete argument in four lines {#def-lvjue}
Regulated quatrain = regulated lines × four lines + opening, continuation, turn and closure (*qi cheng zhuan he*): **a four-line skeleton of the sort ①④／③② or ②④／③②, derived from opposition and adhesion, is exactly the first half of a regulated poem, the second half, or a cut taken at any point** — **and so an old note on the character 绝 offers the saying "截（律诗之句）而止" ('cut [the lines of a regulated poem] and stop'): the quatrain is a sliced specimen of the regulated poem**. The difficulty of the slice: **there are no middle two couplets in which to "lay it out", and within four lines opening, continuation, turn and closure each have only one step** — **the "turn" of the third line therefore becomes the quatrain's vital point**: "**忽见陌头杨柳色**" ('Suddenly one sees the colour of the willows at the head of the lane.' (Our translation.)) — the first two lines travel at a steady speed inside the closed loop of the inner chamber, hair being dressed and shoes embroidered; the third line lifts the head, and the colours of spring crash in: **the turn is not a change of topic; it is prising up one corner of the frame of the first three lines itself**; the fourth line, "**悔教夫婿觅封侯**" ('She regrets having sent her husband to seek a title of nobility.' (Our translation.)), follows that force and releases the flood — **twenty-eight characters complete one coup in the mind**. Writers of the Ming exalted the seven-character quatrain of the High Tang, and placed Wang Changling 王昌龄 and Li Bai 李白 together at the top (the order of seats in Wang Shizhen 王世贞's *Yiyuan zhiyan* 艺苑卮言, 'Goblet Words from the Garden of the Arts') — **the title of "sage hand" was issued to the person who best knew how to turn the corner**.
:::

::: definition The ancient-style quatrain: another line, parallel to the regulated {#def-gujue}
The ancient-style quatrain does not observe adhesion or opposition, **may rhyme on an oblique tone (including an entering-tone rhyme)**, and is not held to a limit of eight lines — it is not "a regulated quatrain written badly"; it is **the ancient form, in its own person, of the five-character short poem**: Wang Wei 王维's 'Seeing Off' (送别), "**山中相送罢，日暮掩柴扉。春草年年绿，王孙归不归**" ('The seeing-off in the mountains is done; at dusk I close the brushwood door. Year by year the spring grass grows green; O prince, will you return, or not?' (Our translation.)) ("绿", an entering tone, serves as a brake inside the line; "归不归" closes on a question — **the archaic, elegant way of writing a regulated quatrain**); Liu Zongyuan 柳宗元's 'River Snow' (江雪), "**孤舟蓑笠翁，独钓寒江雪**" ('In a lone boat, an old man in straw cape and hat fishes alone the snow on the cold river.' (Our translation.)) — the rhyme words "**曰、灭、雪**": a continuous run of rhyme in the entering-tone 屑 group — **a poem at a temperature below zero, rhyming on a batch of door-closing sounds: every time you read it through, the throat shoots the bolt for that face of the river** (a full-marks case of the previous chapter's "study of tone-colour": **rhyme can rhyme a temperature out**). The way to tell the two lines apart hooks back to the previous chapter: **check for three level tones at the end of a line, and for adhesion and opposition, then check the tone of the rhyme: level-tone rhyme + a conforming pattern = regulated quatrain; oblique rhyme, or a failure of adhesion (*shinian* 失粘), = ancient-style quatrain — when you mark a script, look first at which paper is in front of you**.
:::

::: example Releasing a single: painting the walls at the Flag Pavilion {#ex-qiting}
Xue Yongruo 薛用弱 of the Tang, in his *Jiyi ji* 集异记 ('Records of Collected Marvels'), records: on a snowy day Wang Changling, Gao Shi 高适 and Wang Zhihuan 王之涣 gathered at a wineshop in Bianzhou 汴州; musicians played together, and several singing-girls were in attendance — the three **made a private wager**: each of us has a name for poetry; **we look only at whose poems the singing-girls sing, and by that we settle who stands higher**. Changling and Gao Shi each had several pieces sung; Zhihuan, sure of himself, pointed to "**the foremost of the famous singers**" among those seated and said: if she sings my poem, the two of you ought to bow to it; in a moment they did indeed hear her sing 'Song of Liangzhou' (凉州词), "**黄河远上白云间**" ('The Yellow River rises, far off, among the white clouds.' (Our translation.)) — Zhihuan teased his two friends: **"田舍翁，我句固合此辈歌。"** ('Old men of the farmyard — my lines were always the ones for this company to sing.' (Our translation.)) (Details differ slightly from text to text; the structure of the wager is the same in all of them.) Reread the story through communication studies: ① what the wager is set to show about the quatrain's **functional position** at the time; ② "what the performers sang" as the **sampling bias** of a chart (who is it that asks for a song?); ③ joined to this chapter's "turn in the third line": **why what suits being sung and passed on is precisely the quatrain, and not extended regulated verse (*pailü*)**.
::: solution
① **Quatrain = single**: the length fits one performance at a banquet, one memorising, one copying-out (**twenty-eight characters are the standard capacity of a popular song in the Tang** — five- and seven-character recent-style verse is built on the same beat as *yànyuè* 燕乐, banquet music); a poet's "name" **is confirmed by the singer's set list: the forerunner of chart, copyright and concert-tipping rolled into one**. ② **Sampling bias**: what the performers sing is **the aesthetic of the wine table** (a bold phrase to open with, a clever line to close on; the "黄河远上" version's "**opening line in the manner of a public square + closing line for private hearing**" takes both kinds of ear) — **today's chart of hit songs works in the same way: what can get onto the list is "the median of popular preference", not the median of the history of poetry** (chart and anthology, anthology and textbook, one and the same chain of bias — the mirror of [[tang-literature/high-tang]]). ③ **To be sung and passed on, a poem needs one turn**: an "opening–turn" structure inside four lines lets the listener pass through a complete arc of feeling within two minutes; the symmetrical long dragon of extended regulated verse suits reading and the examination, and **does not suit beating time with chopsticks** — **genre is a distribution format: the quatrain is the short video of Tang poetry, the regulated poem is the paper, and the *gexing* 歌行 (the song-ballad) is the documentary**.
:::
:::

::: intuition The resolution of the blank left open {#int-liubai}
The regulated poem is like **a paper**: eight lines, two couplets offered as evidence (parallelism), and opening, continuation, turn and closure carrying one complete argument through. The quatrain is like **a photograph**: **only one exposure is allowed, and a place must be left outside the frame for the reader to step into**. "**却话巴山夜雨时**" ('Then we shall talk of the time of night rain in the Ba mountains.' (Our translation.)) leaves that place in the future; "**低头思故乡**" ('I lower my head and think of home.' (Our translation.)) demotes the conclusion to an action; "**白云千载空悠悠**" ('For a thousand years the white clouds drift on, empty and unhurried.' (Our translation.)) deletes the evidence entire and leaves only the weather — **the quatrain's "not exhausting it" is not that the saying cannot be finished; it is a blank left by design: the author hands resolution over, and takes in exchange bandwidth in the reader's imagination** (Wang Shizhen 王士禛's theory of *shényùn* 神韵, 'spirit and resonance', takes the quatrain as the highest genre: **"不着一字，尽得风流"** ('Not a word is set down, and yet the grace is had entire.' (Our translation.)) — **reserve is not a virtue; it is information theory: the more exact the part that is spoken, the more controllable the part left unspoken**).
:::

::: history The rise and fall of the short-poem form {#sec-xiaoshi}
**The Six Dynasties**: short Wu songs (*wusheng*) in five characters and four lines, such as **Ziye 子夜 ('Midnight') and Duqu 读曲**, were already the quatrain's own ancestors — **the quatrain was born of the love song, not of the regulated poem** (the *yuefu* bloodline, that of the Music Bureau poems and ballads, takes precedence over the theory of a slice cut from the regulated poem; see [[han-to-nanbeichao/folk-songs]]); **the Early Tang**: the regulated quatrain received its settled name on the margins of the departmental examination (*shengshi* 省试) (**five-character extended regulated verse was full dress for the examination hall; the quatrain was a handkerchief for polite exchange**); **the High Tang**: **the golden age of the seven-character quatrain — Wang Changling the "sage hand" and Li Bai honoured side by side, and Gao Shi and Cen Shen 岑参 each firing a quick shot of the frontier**; **the Middle Tang**: Liu Yuxi 刘禹锡's 'Bamboo Branch' (竹枝) **joined the quatrain to folk song once again** ("**东边日出西边雨，道是无晴却有晴**" ('The sun comes out in the east, the rain falls in the west; they say there is no *qing* — no clear sky, 晴, which is also no feeling, 情 — and yet there is.' (Our translation.)) — a punning gag enters the poem; the quatrain's umbilical cord to folk song was never cut); Du Mu 杜牧 brings the manner of historical argument into the quatrain ("**东风不与周郎便**" ('The east wind did not grant young Zhou that chance.' (Our translation.)) — **twenty-eight characters fight one counterfactual battle**); Li Shangyin 李商隐, "**夕阳无限好**" ('The evening sun is boundlessly fine.' (Our translation.)) — **the most famous stopping-halfway in the history of the quatrain**; **the Song**: **Wang Anshi 王安石, Su Shi 苏轼 and Lu You 陆游 treated the quatrain as diary and jottings ("the living method", *huófǎ* 活法: the quatrain became the most frequent genre of Song poetry — the victory of the short-poem form = the victory of everyday writing**); **the Jin and the Yuan**: Yuan Haowen 元好问 wrote a set of thirty quatrains discussing poetry — **the quatrain also works a second post, as a genre of criticism (the poem on poetry takes its opening example from Du Fu 杜甫's 'Six Quatrains Playfully Written', 戏为六绝句)**; **the Ming and the Qing**: the school of *shenyun* revered the quatrains of Wang and Meng (Wang Wei and Meng Haoran 孟浩然) as the music of heaven — **the quatrain was enshrined as the spirit tablet of "reserve"**; **today**: **poems on paintings, poems of gift and reply, the friends' circle — the quatrain is the last slot of everyday speech still held by Classical Chinese (*wényán* 文言): whenever someone fits four lines to a view, this format is adopted of itself — a genre lives in the cases where it is used, and not in the textbook**.
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls in reading the quatrain
**Throwing the ancient-style quatrain back as an "unqualified regulated quatrain"** (**they are two different papers: oblique rhyme, and lines that are not in parallel, are a feature of the ancient-style quatrain, not a stain — use the marking-sheet from the previous chapter first, and only then set pen to paper**); **at every turn, going in search of a "hidden meaning"** (**the turn of the third line may be nothing but the weather, or one lifting of the head; the function of the turn is structure, not a cipher — do not read every bend as a signpost**); **supposing that short = easy** (**four lines admit no patch: not a single padding word can be hidden; the quatrain is the genre with the highest rate of elimination inside recent-style verse — only the quantity written by Tang hands, and the selecting done by Song hands, have left these few hundred "famous lines"**); **letting a famous line stand in for the whole poem** (**circulation by cutting, of the "春蚕到死丝方尽" type ('The spring silkworm's silk is ended only at its death.' (Our translation.)): once a quatrain is taken apart into single lines, it is only a maxim — read the whole poem, and count how it makes its way to the fourth line**).
:::

::: exercise Surgery on the third line {level=1}
Take a ready-made first two lines of doggerel ("深夜加班人未眠" ('Deep at night, working late, and still not asleep.' (Our translation.)) and so on — what you write yourself is what counts), and **add a third and a fourth line so as to complete one "turn + releasing the flood"**: the third line must bring in **a concrete image from outside the frame of the first three lines**, and the fourth line must contain no adjective of emotion.
::: solution
Marking: **the turn has to "crash in"** (outside the window, the night-shift bus, a message popping up on the phone — **the contemporary equivalent of the willow-colour at the head of the lane**); the fourth line **has only an action or a scene** (of the order of "把改了八版的文件命名为最终版" ('Name the file, revised through eight drafts, as the final version.' (Our translation.)) — **the emotion is held for the poem by the reader**); if the third line is only a further step in the same direction ("何况还挨领导骂" ('Let alone being scolded by the boss as well.' (Our translation.))) = no turn has taken place, the operation has failed, and it goes back into the furnace.
:::
:::

::: exercise Matching rhyme to a temperature {level=2}
Write a short poem of four lines: **first settle the "temperature" of the rhyme group** (cold: -u/-ü, gathered in; damp: -n, muffled; open: -ang, a long cry; urgent: entering-tone characters used in a run — **present-day pronunciation will do; state which system you are using**), and only then fill in the matter; the title must be the opposite of the temperature of the rhyme words (if, for example, you rhyme on "雪、灭", write high summer).
::: solution
Marking: **the temperature of the rhyme words is consistent with itself and can be told by ear** (a test by reading aloud: a partner guesses the temperature from the sound; a correct guess is a pass); **the contrast holds** (the place where the title and the rhyme words fight = the poem's tension — **the cold of 'River Snow' depends on the entering tone; what does your heat depend on?**); **the system is stated** (the "new rhymes" based on Putonghua / the Pingshui rhymes / an account by dialect — the clause of "the law in force at the time of the act", from the previous chapter, takes effect here).
:::
:::

::: exercise A short essay: is the short close to the perfect? {level=3}
Write from six hundred to a thousand characters, assessing the claim that "**the quatrain is Chinese poetry's experiment in becoming haiku: it proves that the shortest form can bear the deepest experience — the price is zero fault-tolerance**". You must draw on at least these four: **the mechanism of the turn in the third line**, **the tone-colour privilege of oblique rhyme in the ancient-style quatrain**, **singing and transmission at the Flag Pavilion (distribution format)**, **circulation by cutting, once a poem has been turned into a "famous line"**; and you must deal with one opposing view: **modern new poetry has almost given up short forms of the quatrain type (apart from competitions in the "three-line poem") — which shows that the aesthetic of leaving a blank has already failed** (a possible rebuttal: bullet comments, signature blocks and the quotable-line form are precisely the quatrain's ecological niche).
::: solution
Points for marking: the four mechanisms (**the turn = the structural engine; oblique rhyme = a weapon of tone-colour; the single = a distribution format; cutting = a reduction of dimension in how a poem circulates**); the argument for zero fault-tolerance (**the quatrain's history of elimination: several thousand pieces by Tang writers survive, and a few hundred are generally acknowledged — the short form has no place for a "work of the middle rank"; every piece is either a famous line or it disappears** — **this is hunger marketing on the part of the genre**); the handling of the opposing view (**for reference: leaving a blank has never failed; what has failed is "the institution of reciting in common" — contests in the three-line poem, signature blocks and the refrains of song lyrics are all competing for the ecological niche the quatrain held in its day; the real change is the move from "singable" to "fit to be forwarded": let the distribution format be upgraded again, and the genre will go on moving house**); if the conclusion runs "**short is not a saving; it is spending the whole of one's strength on how to stop — the last question on the paper, for every genre, is the ending**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The account for half a machine, closed: the regulated quatrain (**the slice account and the Six Dynasties bloodline account stand together: four lines are one move, and the third line decides life or death**), the ancient-style quatrain (**the entering-tone rhyme of 'River Snow': rhyme rhymes a temperature out — identify the paper before you give a mark**), the single (**the Flag Pavilion chart = the streaming of Tang poetry; genre is a distribution format**), the blank left open (**resolution exchanged for bandwidth: reserve is information theory, not a virtue**). The method, in one sentence: **in reading a quatrain, count only two things — where it turns the corner, and where it brakes**. The next chapter weighs parallelism: how the symmetry of two lines becomes an art of its own — and why the paired couplet (*duilian* 对联) is the smallest literary unit of the Chinese.
:::
