---
title: "The *qu*"
---

In the year the prosody of the song lyric (_ci_) involuted itself to death, its younger brother took over the music: the **qu** (song or aria). The previous chapter ended by saying that the _ci_ "用文字给声音留座" ('uses writing to keep a seat for the sound'); the answer of the _qu_ is: **keep what seat? Add seats, add dishes, add people — the largest "loosening experiment" in the history of metre**. Loosening does not mean there is no rope: _gongdiao_ ('modes'), tune titles (_qupai_) and rhyme positions are no fewer; only the knot is now tied on the **beat** (_ban_), and no longer on the **character**.

## A new constitution {#def-xianfa}

::: definition _Zhongyuan yinyun_: a certificate of retrospective recognition for phonetic reality {#def-zhongyuan}
Zhou Deqing 周德清, in the _Zhongyuan yinyun_ 中原音韵 (_Rhymes of the Central Plain_, 1324), sets down three articles, each of them aimed at the corpse of Song _ci_ prosody. **(1) The entering tone is distributed among the other three** (in the living speech of the north the entering tone had already dispersed, and the rhyme dictionary registers the fact as it stands — **the _qu_ no longer consults the poetic rhymes: an entering-tone character is demoted from a "hard target" to an "ordinary citizen", and may be assigned to serve as level, rising or departing**); **(2) the level tone divides into yin and yang** (the accomplished fact that clear and muddy initials, voiceless and voiced, split the tone is put on file — **the level tone of the _qu_ is two slots; writing "level" on the score is still not precise enough**); **(3) nineteen rhyme groups** (a great merger of rhyme groups: codas in -m are taken in — **the breadth of _qu_ rhyme is bought with phonetic fact: it protects no sound that is already dead**). Set against the prosody of the _ci_: a _ci_ score attends upon **melodies that already exist**; a _qu_ score attends upon **plays that are being sung** — **the _ci_ is classicism (honour the score); the _qu_ is realism (honour the ear)**. Zhou Deqing's pride is in the tone of his own preface — **rhyming is renamed from "依书" ('according to the book') to "依音" ('according to the sound'): a rhyme dictionary writes itself up as a news bulletin, and not as a legal code** (its later fate is ironic and glorious: **the rhyme dictionary that readers of Putonghua, standard Mandarin, have by nature as a mother tongue** — see the example).
:::

::: definition Three new tools: padding words, repeated rhyme, and the vernacular {#def-gongju}
**(1) Padding words** (_chenzi_: characters added outside the number fixed by the score. "我是蒸不烂、煮不熟、捶不匾、炒不爆、响珰珰一粒铜豌豆" — 'I am one copper pea, ringing and clanging, that cannot be steamed rotten, boiled soft, pounded flat or fried to bursting.' (Our translation.) Characters such as "我" and "一粒" may all be padding: **a bypass permit in the metre — characters may be added, the beat may not. The _qu_ changes "a fixed quantity" into "a fixed beat"**, and the mouth of the one who speaks and sings is for the first time allowed to overtake without being late); **(2) the rhyme word may be repeated** (the same rhyming character is used again and again — the ban on a repeated rhyme in the regulated poem (_lüshi_) is abolished here. A run of rhymes in the manner of "**由他，由他**" ('let him, let him') (Our translation.) is used as a drumbeat: **the _ci_ treats a repeated rhyme as an accident; the _qu_ treats it as an effect**); **(3) a sharp turn in language policy** (the _qu_ holds that "**文随语走**" ('writing follows speech'): **function words, rhotacisation (_erhua_), wisecracks and two-part allegorical sayings (_xiehouyu_) are all entered on the books — the proportion of refined to vulgar in the _qu_ is the hottest of any form in any age**. The nickname "蒜酪味" ('a flavour of garlic and curds') says that it goes down with rice: **the _qu_ does not lay a banquet; it lays out a night market** — **from "词为诗余" ('the _ci_ is what poetry leaves over') to "曲为词余" ('the _qu_ is what the _ci_ leaves over'), the relay of disparaging names shows that every expansion of a genre is entered once as a demerit by the guards of the old genre**).
:::

## Two kinds of release {#def-faxing}

::: definition The _xiaoling_ and the song suite: the single and the concept album {#def-xiaoling}
**The _xiaoling_** = one tune title, released once ('Autumn Thoughts', to the tune _Tianjingsha_, 《天净沙·秋思》: "**枯藤老树昏鸦，小桥流水人家，古道西风瘦马。夕阳西下，断肠人在天涯。**" — 'Withered vines, an old tree, crows at dusk; a small bridge, flowing water, people's houses; an ancient road, the west wind, a lean horse. The evening sun goes down in the west; a heartbroken person is at the edge of the sky.' (Our translation.) — **twenty-nine characters; the first three sentences have zero verbs: nine nouns set side by side in three groups, the limit of _liejin_ (a brocade of listed images) in Chinese rhymed writing. In the last line "断肠人" ('the heartbroken one') comes on and draws the net in. The whole song is like a short film of nothing but empty shots, with a voice-over appearing for one second at the end**); **the song suite** (_taoshu_) = several tune titles under one and the same _gongdiao_, joined into a single set and rhyming through to the end (**a concept album: one tonality, the _gongdiao_, runs several pieces together as a medley, and a closing tune brings it to an end — the sung scenes of a play narrate by this way of grouping tunes**); **dramatic _qu_** = a song suite + spoken dialogue (_binbai_) + stage business (_kejie_). In _The Story of the Western Wing_, to the tune 【端正好】 (Duanzhenghao): "**碧云天，黄花地，西风紧，北雁南飞。晓来谁染霜林醉？总是离人泪。**" — 'A sky of azure clouds, a ground of yellow flowers; the west wind is keen, the northern geese fly south. At dawn, who dyed the frosted wood drunk? It is all the tears of those who part.' (Our translation.) — **the first four lines are bought entire from Fan Zhongyan 范仲淹's _Sumuzhe_ 《苏幕遮》. The _qu_ is not ashamed: "碧云天，黄叶地" — 'A sky of azure clouds, a ground of yellow leaves.' (Our translation.) — The _qu_'s view of appropriation by assembled lines (_jiju_): a famous line of the previous dynasty is taken as cloth and cut into a new garment, and a cut that shows its stitching only displays the craft**; the boldness of the figure "泪染霜林" ('tears dye the frosted wood'): **the _ci_ says that sorrow is like spring water; the _qu_ simply uses the tears as the dye — the grammar of lyric feeling is upgraded from figurative comparison to a craft**.
:::

::: example _Tianjingsha · Autumn Thoughts_: this lesson's final paper {#ex-tianjingsha}
Take the twenty-nine characters of _Tianjingsha_ and, treating the song as **a condensed paper of the whole lesson**, do five questions. (1) Mark level and oblique tones (a hint about the system: once the entering tone has been distributed among the other three, this song follows the _Zhongyuan yinyun_ — does "夕" still count as an entering tone? **Look it up yourself first**); (2) mark the rhyme positions (the rhyme words are in the original: 鸦、家、马、下 — **level, rising and departing, the three tones, cross-rhyme**: what _shi_ prosody cannot do, the _ci_ only just can, and the _qu_ does lawfully); (3) count the verbs (in the first three lines); (4) if, following the "opposition" of regulated verse (recent-style poetry), one rewrites "小桥流水人家" as "中桥旱地人家", what is lost; (5) why **this "qu without rules" is exactly fit to be the leaving certificate of a course in metre** — let the three new tools of this lesson and the previous chapter's argument of "delayed delivery" each supply half of the answer.
::: solution
(1) **A change of system.** Judged by the _Zhongyuan yinyun_, the "夕" of "夕阳" has already been assigned to the level tone (the authorities differ slightly in the method of assignment; in the _qu_ one follows the living sound) — **the same character is an entering tone in the Pingshui system and a level tone in the system of the _Zhongyuan yinyun_: change the code and you change nationality** (the highest application of the "law at the time of the act" clause in Chapter 2). (2) The rhyme words: **鸦、家、马、下、涯 (the _ma_ rhyme group, 麻: level, rising and departing cross-rhyme)** — **cross-rhyming of the three tones lets the rhyme words fall naturally, as speech does; the regulated poem's "one rhyme all the way through, and the same tone" is a gallery, and the rhyme positions of the _qu_ are a pavement** ("涯", read yá, is fully compatible with the modern sound — **the affinity of _qu_ rhyme with Putonghua: the previous chapter's "fossil backup" is, in the _qu_, a fresh backup**). (3) **Zero verbs:** three lines, nine nouns — **_liejin_ is not the absence of grammar; it hands the grammar over to the joining of shots (montage is the syntax of the _qu_)**; (4) what is lost is **the class composition of the menu**. "小桥流水人家" ('a small bridge, flowing water, people's houses') is the gentle cut of a tourist film: set against the desolation of "枯藤／古道" ('withered vines / the ancient road'), it makes exactly a **reverse shot**. "中桥旱地" ('a middle bridge, dry ground') is more "工" ('finished') in its parallelism, and the whole song's **cross-cut between cold and warm** is scrapped — **what the _qu_ wants is not finish, but a true aim** (the verdict of the lesson on parallelism is retried in the _qu_). (5) Why it earns the certificate: **it demonstrates at once that metre is there (_gongdiao_, tune-patterns and rhyme positions are no fewer) and that metre is slack (places for padding words, cross-rhyming, zero verbs) — a living specimen of "fix the beat, do not fix the quantity". As for "delayed delivery": that a vernacular anthology today reads it with no obstacle at all says precisely that** it rhymes on a sound that is still alive — the _qu_ is the last child in the family of metres who still remembers what music looks like.
:::

::: intuition The mechanics of loosening {#int-songbang}
From regulated verse to the _ci_ to the _qu_ is a **history of constraints migrating**: the number of characters is welded shut (regulated verse) → the number of lines is loosened, and the four tones are welded shut (the _ci_) → the four tones and the number of characters are loosened, and the beat is welded shut (the _qu_) — **each generation of a form takes over the previous generation's free variables as its defaults, yields the new free variables to performance, and withdraws discipline by one layer: from the face of the paper to the face of the beat**. A regularity emerges: **a literary form does not "decline"; it only changes its load-bearing wall. Every "liberation" shifts the cost onto the next link** (the _qu_ shifts the cost onto the actors and the musicians: someone has to be able to catch the padding words — **where the metre is loose, the performance is strict**. Ming–Qing southern drama (_chuanqi_), the other way about, tightens again: the tones of the characters are welded shut once more — **loosening is a non-renewable resource: one generation uses it up, and the next generation uses it**).
:::

::: history The accounting period of the _qu_ {#sec-zhangqi}
**Jin to early Yuan:** the _zhugongdiao_ ('a narrative through many modes'; Dong Jieyuan 董解元, _Xixiang ji zhugongdiao_ 西厢记诸宫调, _The Story of the Western Wing_ as a _zhugongdiao_ — **an experiment in writing suites for a long work of speech and song: the direct father of Wang Shifu's variety play**); **Yuan:** **the golden twenty years of Yuan variety plays (_zaju_), in the twin cities of Dadu and Hangzhou: Guan Hanqing 关汉卿, Wang Shifu 王实甫, Ma Zhiyuan 马致远, Bai Pu 白朴 — a group of scholars, after the examinations had been suspended, transferred their talent to the theatre: the finest brains after Yuanyou, re-employed**; Wang Guowei 王国维, in _Song Yuan xiqu kao_ 宋元戏曲考 (_An Examination of Song and Yuan Drama_), holds a coronation for the _qu_ ("**一代之文学**", 'the literature of an age', joins the rank: the _sao_ of Chu, the rhapsodies of the Han (_fu_), the poetry of the Tang, the song lyrics of the Song, the **_qu_ of the Yuan** — **a posthumous title, the latest to be received and the least disputed — because it had long since stopped competing**); **late Yuan to Ming:** **the refining of _sanqu_, the non-dramatic song (Zhang Kejiu 张可久 and his fellows wrote the _qu_ back into _ci_) + the rise of southern _qu_ (_Pipa ji_ 琵琶记, _The Story of the Lute_ — the _gongdiao_ system has its blood changed: northern _qu_ dies of being refined; southern _qu_ is born in the localities**); **Ming:** the water-milled tune of Kunqu (_shuimo diao_) welds the tones of characters shut again (**Wei Liangfu 魏良辅 and his fellows "依水磨定腔" ('set the melody by the water-mill') — a restoration in the prosody of the _qu_: the loosening experiment ends, and one returns to honouring the score**); **Qing:** the _Qinding qupu_ 钦定曲谱 (_Qu Scores Authorised by Imperial Order_) + the rise of the _huabu_ ('flower section') — **the court compiles the scores, and the people sing wild: with every victory the _yabu_ ('elegant section') ages by one degree, and _luantan_ ('mixed strumming') seizes the market by being "illegal" — the history of genres plays the history of dynasties through once more**; **today:** **the rhyme-density of song lyrics and of speak-singing returns to the level of "dense-rhyme bombardment" (the copper pea's run of rhymes comes back to life in hip-hop); the "shaking out of the bundle" in comic sketches and talk shows is, in its structure, surnamed "诨" (_hun_, comic stage-banter) — the _qu_ has the least corpse left; every organ has been transplanted**.
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls in reading the _qu_
**Checking the _qu_ by the prosody of the _ci_** (**a padding word is not a leftover character; it is part of the rhythm fixed by the score. Take the padding out of "我蒸不烂铜豌豆" — 'I, a copper pea that will not steam rotten.' (Our translation.) — and you break the beat instead: understand the beat first, then count the characters**); **taking the "vulgar" for a demotion** (**the vernacular of the _qu_ is a technique: nominal sentences, asides and hyperbole are all fossils of instructions to the stage — the "racket" of the text is the "accuracy" of the performance**); **ignoring the two systems, northern _qu_ and southern _qu_** (**the modes, the tones of the characters, and the accompaniment (clapper and drum set against the strings) are all different: there is no single "prosody of the _qu_"; what exists is two constitutions governing, one after the other**); **supposing that Yuan _qu_ = Yuan variety plays** (**_sanqu_ exists on its own: a _xiaoling_ or a song suite may be sung in _qingchang_ ('pure singing', with no staging) outside the theatre — to read every _qu_ as a play-text is to take every popular song for a musical**).
:::

::: exercise The padding-word workshop {level=1}
Take one five-character line of regulated verse (for example "**床前明月光**" — 'Bright moonlight before the bed.' (Our translation.)) and **expand it into a sentence, flavoured like the _qu_, that carries padding words**: bring it up to 12–15 characters, **keep the original rhyme word, and keep a rhythm that can still be beaten out**. Then reverse the operation: delete every padding word in the sentence you expanded, and see whether what collapses is the rhetoric or the information.
::: solution
Marking: **the rhyme word holds, and the beat is not thrown out** (the clapping test: the added characters must be read so that they "slide past" — **a padding word is luggage on a weak beat, not a roadblock on a strong beat**); **the deletion test**: what collapses, in a good padding word, is tone of voice and personality (of the order of "我" 'I', "呀" a particle, and "那" 'that'), not fact — **a padding word is the "quota of human flavour" inside the metre**. Extra marks to anyone who dares to add dialect padding such as "哎" ('ai') or "那答儿" ('over there', in the old vernacular) and can give a reason on the stage for it.
:::
:::

::: exercise The street where three tones cross-rhyme {level=2}
In the spirit of the _Zhongyuan yinyun_, write a short rhymed piece of 6–8 lines (doggerel, _shulaibao_ — clapper patter — or a passage of speak-singing will all do). **Level, rising and departing must cross-rhyme inside one and the same rhyme group**, and **the same rhyme-word character must be used at least twice** (repeated rhyme as a drumbeat). Test one another: can the other person hit your rhyme positions accurately at the first clap?
::: solution
Marking: **the cross-rhyming is natural** (the difference of timbre among three tones in one rhyme group is absorbed by the rhythm — a verification at first hand of "what _shi_ prosody cannot do, the _ci_ only just can, and the _qu_ does lawfully"); **the repeated rhyme has a function** (a rhyme on a repeated character serves as a refrain or as a heavy hammer-blow, and is not a poverty of vocabulary); **it can be clapped, one rhyme to one beat** (this is the real acceptance standard of the _qu_: **if the mouth can keep up = the metre is passed** — the end-of-term signature on "fix the beat, do not fix the quantity").
:::
:::

::: exercise A short essay: is loosening progress? {level=3}
Write between six hundred and one thousand characters, assessing the claim that "**from regulated verse to the _qu_, metre has not grown looser; it has only changed what is welded shut: the number of characters gives way to the beat, and reading gives way to performance**". You must draw on at least four items: **the three articles of the _Zhongyuan yinyun_**, **the logic of beat-position in the padding word**, **cross-rhyming of the three tones, and repeated rhyme**, and **the Ming–Qing "restoration" (Kunqu welds the tones of characters shut again)**; and you must deal with one argument from the other side: **if every generation swings between "welding shut again and loosening again", then the history of metre is only a cycle, with no direction — do you accept that, or do you refute it?**
::: solution
Points for marking. The argument that the weld-point has changed (**a migration of constraints: the face of the paper → the face of the beat → (today) the screen — every link has a real cost and a real return: making the performance strict is the cost of the _qu_, not a defect**). The restoration (**what Kunqu shows is that loosening cannot be kept up because the trade needs standard parts: troupes moving from place to place, and oral teaching from master to pupil, all require a "score" — the moment freedom has succeeded as a commodity, it at once needs a new compliance**). The question of the cycle (**a model refutation: a spiral that has a direction — each swing is built on the increment of skill tamed in the round before. People of the Yuan could use padding words because the _ci_ had already trained a stamina for dividing into stanzas; speak-singing today can use dense rhyme because the history of Chinese copy has packed the stock of a feel for rhyme quite full — the amplitude of the swing is widening, and the baseline is moving up**. Anyone who accepts the cycle must explain "why the dialect and the vernacular of each round are different", which is equally a valid position). If the conclusion can be written thus: "**the history of metre is the history of 'who is responsible for not being late': from counting characters, to counting beats, to counting traffic — the question does not change; the clock that keeps the time is replaced**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The loosening experiment, filed. The constitution (**a certificate of retrospective recognition: it protects no sound that is already dead — the irony is that, for this reason, it is what most resembles Putonghua**); the tools (**a padding word = the quota of human flavour; a repeated rhyme = a drumbeat; the vernacular = a fossil of instructions to the stage**); the release (**the twenty-nine-character short film of 'Autumn Thoughts'; tears dyeing the frosted wood: lyric feeling is upgraded from figurative comparison to a craft**); the mechanics (**a migration of constraints: where the metre is loose, the performance is strict; loosening is a non-renewable resource**); the accounting period (**crowned latest, and disputed least; "the _qu_ has the least corpse left; every organ has been transplanted"**). The method, in one sentence: **first find out for whom it is keeping the time, then judge whether it is loose or tight**. The last chapter: imagery and allusion — inside the vessel of metre, what, after all, is it holding?
:::
