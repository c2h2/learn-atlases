title: "Yuan drama"

The previous chapter closed at the door of the *washe* 瓦舍 (the entertainment quarters); this chapter lifts the door-curtain and goes straight in — **Yuan variety play (*zaju*) is the first time in Chinese literature that 'how long one person can sing' was taken as a structural problem: four acts and a wedge, one lead singer, a division of labour among the role types: three technical constraints forced out a concentrated art rare in the history of world theatre, and also forced out China's first batch of playwrights put into circulation**.
## Definition: the troupe is not a theatre, it is a production team {#def-xiban}

::: definition Three constraints of the institution {#def-santiao}
**① Four acts and a wedge** (**one play in four acts, with a wedge (*xiezi* 楔子) used now and then to open, or to fill a gap in the account (the system as commonly stated) —** the four acts (*zhe* 折) are not curtain-acts (*mu* 幕); they are four gears of emotion (the old terms 起, 端, 传, 结: 'opening', 'setting out', 'carrying through', 'close'): length is priced jointly by 'how long one performance can be listened to' and 'one lead performer's voice' (the problem the Song did not solve in Lesson 5's study of structure, which the troupe solved first: **length is a physiological quantity, not an aesthetic quantity**); **② One lead singer** (**the male lead (*zhengmo* 正末) or the female lead (*zhengdan* 正旦) sings through to the end, and the other role types have only speech (*bai* 白) —** the lyric prerogative of the whole play is concentrated in a single throat: this is not poverty, it is design: **the tragic quality of *zaju* is an acoustic phenomenon (the sympathy of the whole house is forcibly supplied along a single line, which is why unjust convictions, wives longing at home, and partings by death suit it especially (Chapter 4 on point of view: the lyric 'I' is reinstated in drama as 'the lead singer' (the institutional explanation of Lesson 15's 'the impossibility of zero focalisation in drama'**); **③ The role-type system** (**the names of the role types — male lead (*zhengmo* 正末), secondary male (*fumo* 副末), extra male (*waimo* 外末), female role (*dan* 旦), *jing* 净, child role (*lai'er* 俫儿), and the rest (the common account) —** an actor is a type, not an individual: the *jing*'s comic interpolations (*chake* 插科) are a statutory breather valve (the contract version of Lesson 12's 'humour as a pressure-reducing device': **laughter is not an ingredient added in; it is one column on the rehearsal schedule**.
:::

::: definition Troupe, stage and private performance {#def-xitai}
**Where it is played** (**the court; command performances summoned by government offices; itinerant players (*luqi* 路歧) chasing a ground; the music-and-dance walls and the stages of temple fairs (the common account: Jin and Yuan stages and murals in Shanxi and elsewhere are the material evidence) —** a stage open on three sides: there is no curtain, so 'on — off' has to be accounted for by the character announcing his own name and station (*zibao jiamen* 自报家门) (the compulsory version, in drama, of Chapter 4's 'the narrator shows himself': **the audience can see the actor's face, so the play has to let the character name himself**); **Economic structure** (**the troupe-leader's share, the actor's name, the literatus's brush: the three are pieced into one playbill (the common line of development: by the turn of the Yuan and the Ming, famous actors of the kind of Hua Lilang 花李郎 are already found in the records) —** the script is a consumable: it is written one performance at a time, and if the audience coughs the plot-episode (*guanmu* 关目) is changed (the stage version of Chapter 9's 'the listener is the manuscript reviewer'; storyteller and playwright part company here: one revises tonight, the other revises the printed copy**); The group of writers** (**the *Register of Ghosts* (《录鬼簿》, Zhong Sicheng 钟嗣成) records the names of more than a hundred *zaju* playwrights (the common account) —** the first literary history in the form of 'a roll of the dead': it writes into a genealogy people the official histories leave unrecorded (the scene of Lesson 15's 'retrospective recognition': **the Guan Hanqings 关汉卿 were, while they lived, a circle of friends among performers; after death they are 'masters'**).
:::

## Dou E: the limiting form of an appeal {#def-dou}

::: definition The three vows {#def-shiyuan}
《感天动地窦娥冤》 (*The Injustice to Dou E*) (the common account: by Guan Hanqing; the principal editions that survive differ by Ming revision) — structure: the first two acts are the complete failure of judicial procedure (falsely accused, put to torture, and, in order to protect her mother-in-law, admitting a capital offence); the third act is the execution ground; the fourth act is the ghost seeking redress (note this proportion of failure: three quarters of the length is used to prove that the institution cannot put an error right, and the last quarter is handed to the supernatural — this is a script in which the 'concession paragraph' is pushed to an extreme (Lesson 6: first grant that the other side's claim holds in full, then overturn it with a single exception); “地也，你不分好歹何为地！天也，你错勘贤愚枉做天！” — 'Earth, if you do not tell good from bad, what are you as earth? Heaven, if you misjudge the worthy and the foolish, you are heaven in vain!' (Our translation.) — the technical place of railing at heaven and earth: it is not arguing; it is breaking the contract in a notary's presence (the execution-ground version of Chapter 8's 'to rhyme is to notarise': when the yamen will not set its seal, literature comes and sets it); The three vows (blood flying onto the white silk, frost falling in the sixth month, and three years of great drought in Chuzhou; the common account) — their status as a kind of writing is 'falsifiable prophecy': the author sets the date of verification after the performance has broken up, and what is bet is that the place really did suffer a drought (**this is the costliest wager in Chinese narrative:** celestial signs are made to stand surety for a dead person's innocence (the extreme case of Chapter 9's 'a recommendation is a prophecy, and the rate at which it is honoured is the wage': **Dou E's reputation is paid out according to the disaster**)); **The close** (**in the fourth act Dou Tianzhang 窦天章 (her father) retries the case and closes it; the common account) —** a solution in the manner of the upright official and of paternal authority: **the wrong can be washed clean, on condition that the dead person's father has the authority (the reverse of Lesson 1's 'leaving a blank is a quota one has the means to keep': those at the bottom can only write an appeal as a miracle**.
:::

::: definition Ji Junxiang and the earliest world-readers {#def-guer}
《冤报冤赵氏孤儿》 (*The Orphan of Zhao*) (the common account: Ji Junxiang 纪君祥; cut from the two lines of record in the *Zuo Tradition* (《左传》) and in the 'Hereditary House of Zhao' of the *Records of the Grand Historian* (《史记·赵世家》)) — the structure is the reverse of Dou E's: here the institution fails utterly (the whole clan is killed), and hope is staked on an outsider; the 'swap of one's own child' by Tu'an Gu 屠岸贾, Cheng Ying 程婴 and Gongsun Chujiu 公孙杵臼 (the common account: in the play Cheng Ying offers his own son in order to save the orphan) — the focus of this lesson's moral audit: the one who is sacrificed has no right to speak: the infant who is killed in another's place never, in the whole play, has a chance to consent (Lesson 6's 'steel-man test' brought onto the stage: **this play's strongest Argument is that it will not let you rebut 'loyalty and righteousness'; what is most modern in it is the infant who does not speak, whom it leaves behind**); **"World-readers"** (**the common account: *The Orphan of Zhao* is the earliest Chinese play translated and introduced to Europe (by way of the eighteenth-century Jesuits' system of correspondence), and was adapted and staged in France and in Britain —** a Yuan *zaju* about 'loyalty', read in the Europe of the Enlightenment as a moral play of 'reason and sacrifice': **the moment a text leaves the country, the right to interpret it belongs to the customs (the transoceanic scene of Lesson 15's 'influence': canonisation is sometimes an accident of translation**.
:::

## The ledger of tragedy {#def-zhangben}

::: definition Wang Guowei's two rulers {#def-zhizi}
《宋元戏曲考》 (*A Study of Song and Yuan Drama*) (completed at the end of the 1910s; the common account) (the first scholarly book to write of Chinese opera (*xiqu* 戏曲) as 'the literature of an age' (一代之文学): the discipline was built by later people, but the foundation was laid by people of the Yuan (the capping case of Lesson 15's 'literary history is a project of retrospective recognition': the claim that the song lyric (*ci*) is 'the literature of an age', and the claim that *qu* is, are both inventions of periodisation from the Ming and Qing on); His words on tragedy (the common account: Wang Guowei 王国维 accords works of the kind of *The Orphan of Zhao* and *The Injustice to Dou E* the grant “列之于世界大悲剧中亦无愧色” — 'Placed among the great tragedies of the world, they would have no cause to blush.' (Our translation.) — and explains them by the background of penal government falling out of order in the Yuan — note that the ruler has two heads: one is the universal form of tragedy (the innocent who suffers), the other is the 'nowhere to appeal' (*wugao* 无告) peculiar to China (the mechanism of appeal has failed); **The technical difference** (**Greek tragedy relies on necessity (Oedipus commits the crime without knowing); Yuan *zaju* relies on disorder (Dou E knows, and dies in another's place) —** the former makes one hold cause and effect in awe; the latter makes one demand compensation (which is why Chinese narrative developed a compulsion about 'reunion': **it is not that the aesthetic is low-grade; it is a tradition of settling claims (Chapter 9's account of the mechanism of the 'grand reunion' makes its first payment in this chapter: what a Chinese audience wants is not catharsis, but a writ of execution**).
:::

::: definition An audit of the reunion mania {#def-tuanyuan}
**The density of 'closing with a reunion'** (**a great many *zaju*, and southern plays (*nanxi* 南戏), close on the golden examination list, a return from the dead, father and son reunited, or a marriage concluded (the common account) —** often denounced as vulgar; this lesson reads it as a clause of the contract: what the audience pays is the price of entry and two hours of attention, and the refund policy reads 'a result must be seen' (Chapter 9's box-office constraint); The more precise function (reunion = a judicial pronouncement: a judgement has to be read aloud before the case counts as closed (even if the judgement is issued by a dream, a ghost, or Bao Zheng 包拯 (a near relation of Chapter 5's 'prosody is the last apparatus of order': when procedure in the real world cannot be relied on, the audience will insist that the procedure inside the narrative be complete — reunion is a rehearsal of procedural justice.
:::

::: example Changing the fourth act {#ex-sizhe}
The fourth act of *The Injustice to Dou E* is the ghost pleading to her father, a retrial, and the clearing of the wrong. Change it to 'there is no fourth act' (the whole play ends at the three vows on the execution ground), and write a 'note on the changed ending' of 120 characters, explaining to the leader of the troupe: **what the audience will be short of, what the actors will earn more of, and what the nature of this play will become**.
::: solution
**What the audience is short of is the settlement of the claim** (**they will leave carrying an uncashed cheque on 'whether the vows come true':** the payoff turns into suspense, satisfaction falls, but its value as a topic rises (the dramatic version of Chapter 9's titles and of the clauses of a prospectus)); what the actors earn more of is that act's tragic solo (the execution-ground act is already the peak of the singing in the whole play; to cut the 'father decides the case' that follows is to give the ending to the *dan*: the acoustic logic of the lead-singer system will support this change); The change in nature (from an appeal-play of 'unjust case — the wrong cleared' to an indictment-play of 'unjust case — silence': the judgement is moved off the stage, and the audience is appointed judge (this is an operation the modern theatre uses as a habit, and its Yuan version appears precisely by cutting the upright official out (in marking, see whether the answer points out: 'reunion' is not ornament; it is this play's judicial apparatus; dismantle it, and the play turns from an appeal into a prosecution)); The troupe-leader's algorithm (a conclusion for reference: it cannot be cut for a temple-fair ground (pilgrims offering incense want a result); it can be cut for a literati private hall-performance (*tanghui* 堂会) (scholar-officials love the aftertaste of 不平则鸣 ('an injustice will cry out')) — one and the same script can have two endings, because there are two audiences: this is the commercial reason that transmitted texts of Yuan *zaju* are full of variant readings (it is not that the copying went wrong.
:::

::: intuition Compression is dignity {#int-yasuo}
**Why 'four acts, one person singing' can produce masterpieces** (**the constraint cuts away the right to choose, so the whole budget is spent on a very few scenes: *The Injustice to Dou E* writes only one execution ground, and *The Orphan of Zhao* writes only one saving of an infant in the manner of a difficult labour —** the mark of a great script is that it has no scene in reserve (the dramatic version of Lesson 10's 'minimum runnable package'); The illusion of 'contemporaneity' in Yuan *zaju* (the respects in which it resembles modern spoken drama (*huaju* 话剧) — division into curtained acts, a protagonist system, social subject matter — are mostly accidental; the respects in which it does not — singing, stage business (*ke* 科) and speech set out in separate columns, a ghost coming on, announcing one's own name and station — are precisely its own trade (measure *zaju* with the ruler of spoken drama, and you measure out a heap of 'defects'; measure it with the ruler of the performance system, and all of it is design (Lesson 15: first confirm where the ruler was made).
:::

::: history The Yuan on the stage {#sec-yuandai}
**1234** the Jin falls; **1279** the Song falls (**the common line of development:** the northern and southern systems of performance flow together in a unified market: *zaju* in the northern tunes (*beiqu* 北曲) goes south along the post roads and with the command performances summoned by government offices (**war does not create literature, but war reorders the schedule: the fall of a dynasty clears an audience for another kind of stage**)); **before the 1300s** the *Register of Ghosts* records the writers and their famous works (**a mixed social circle of actors and literati who were “都不屑饥寒之备” — 'None of them would stoop to providing against hunger and cold.' (Our translation.) (the common account):** the sociology of literature is visible for the first time in the Yuan: because the literati had no other way up, social ties became the only asset); late Yuan and early Ming *The Lute* (《琵琶记》) and the rise of southern drama (a preview of Chapter 2: when the *zaju* throat grows old, the south hands the story to the whole troupe (several people dividing the songs) — the dissolution of the lead-singer system is the greatest silent revolution in the history of Chinese drama); early Ming staging at court and in the *jiaofang* 教坊 (the court office of entertainers), and the embryo of the play that uses the whole set of roles (the common account: the single lead singer gives way to the role types dividing the songs among them: at the same time as the lyric prerogative is democratised, the tragic acoustics of *zaju* disappear with it (a redistribution of Chapter 1's 'quota'); the Qing a rediscovery after *A Selection of Yuan Plays* (《元曲选》); the 1900s Wang Guowei establishes the field (the common account: Yuan *zaju* turns from 'being performed' to 'being read': a literature of the stage comes back to life through the textbook two hundred years after it lost the stage — part of its 'literariness' is what we supplied for it.
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of Yuan *zaju*
Taking the 'four masters of Yuan *qu*' for a society (the joint name of Guan, Ma, Zheng and Bai (the common account) is a classification made by people of the Ming and Qing; the four had no common programme and no common stage (their times do not even match) — the volume bound as one turns up again (the clause in Chapter 4): a joint name is a convenience of anthologies, not a fact of literature); Using Ming printings to fix the Yuan stage as fact (most *zaju* that survive depend on Ming printings (of the kind of *A Selection of Yuan Plays*): what we read is Yuan *zaju* twice refitted, by Ming troupes and by booksellers (an awareness of editions is not a detail; it is a method (Lesson 1's layering of the sources)); Calling the 'grand reunion' low-grade (passing one further sentence with modernism's aesthetic of pain (first ask what the audience wants, and how much money it will pay: reunion is a product of the demand side, not national character)); **Reading Dou E as a report of social criticism** (**it is that, but it is first a rhetorical argument carried at the cost of a death:** ignore its technique (the structure of the written judgement, the design of the three vows, the acoustics of the lead singer) and you have reduced it to a historical source (the reverse of Lesson 12's 'the price of theme is technique': **the price of technique is to be absorbed by the theme — Dou E is most easily quoted into the line that is least easily understood**).
:::

::: exercise Rewriting under the constraints of the institution {level=1}
Rewrite one item of social news (several hundred characters) as an outline in 'four acts and a wedge', with these requirements: **one lead singer, one emotional gear in each act, and the *jing*'s comic business placed before the third act**; mark the material you were forced to cut.
::: solution
Marking: **the column 'forced to cut' is the whole of this question** (**the price of the lead-singer system: those who are not the lead singer cannot sing = their inner lives are confiscated:** what you have cut is how many people's voices (the politics of point of view in Chapter 4**); the gears must be ones one can point to** (**you must be able to say which act is the 'carrying through' (the tightest place), rather than the place where the plot is cleverest**).
:::

::: exercise Write one line for the infant {level=2}
In *The Orphan of Zhao* the infant who is exchanged and killed in the orphan's place has no speech from start to finish. Write for him (or for his mother) one line of dialogue of no more than 20 characters, and put it into the play; then assess it yourself: **will this one line make the whole play's Argument of 'loyalty and righteousness' stronger, or more brittle**.
::: solution
Marking: **the line must be in the scene and must not steal it** (**go beyond 20 characters and it becomes another play**); **the self-assessment must really carry out the steel-man** (**for reference: any 'consent' that makes a hostage of the speaker will make the sacrifice shameful:** silence is this play's moral load-bearing wall — pull it down, and the play collapses; leave it standing, and whether the play is tragedy or propaganda depends on the audience (this is why the history of adaptation turns its attention to Cheng Ying's state of mind).
:::

::: exercise Short essay: the ethics of the lead singer {level=3}
Write between six hundred and a thousand characters, assessing '**A dramatic form that hands the whole lyric prerogative to one person is declaring: other people have no inner life. This is not a defect; it is the politics of this apparatus — what it is best at presenting is one person with nowhere to appeal, and a crowd of institutions that do not speak; and when the lyric prerogative is shared out equally among the whole company of actors, fiction replaces drama and becomes the next age's machine for dealing with "many people having inner lives at the same time"**'. You must draw on **the physiological budget of the four acts**, **one lead singer = sympathy along a single line**, **the notarial function of the three vows**, **the silent infant of *The Orphan of Zhao***, **the judicial pronouncement of reunion**, **the methodological standing of the Ming printings**, at least these six; and you must decide: **whether a genre's 'capacity' and its 'blind spot' have the same source — argue it from one work in this chapter that is 'strong because of the institution, and also mute because of the institution', and infer what the long vernacular novel (*baihua*) took over**.
::: solution
Points for marking: the six mechanisms (**budget, acoustics, notarisation, silence, pronouncement, edition**); the decision (**for reference: the same-source view — the lead-singer system gives suffering a volume, at the price that those with no part are reduced to props; the novel took over the problem of 'many people having inner lives at the same time' (the whole thread of Chapters 4 to 9 is planted here in advance:** the parallel biographies of *Water Margin*, the everyday of *The Plum in the Golden Vase*, and the polyphony of *Dream of the Red Chamber* are all at work on the acoustic problem that *zaju* left behind (in marking, see whether 'the relay' is described as a transfer of the problem rather than as progress in level**); if the conclusion can say '**form is not a container; it is a scheme of distribution: who has the right to utter, who can only be spoken of, and who does not even deserve to be spoken of — to understand an age's drama is to understand the seating plan of its right to speak', that is an A.
