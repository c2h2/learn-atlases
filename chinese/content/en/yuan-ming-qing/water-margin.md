---
title: "*Water Margin*"
---

The algorithm of the previous chapter assigned everything to great men; this chapter moves it to the rivers and lakes (*jiānghú* 江湖) — **what *Water Margin* conducts is a moral experiment: it puts “义” (*yì*, 'rightness') to run in an environment where there are no public rules; the result is both deep feeling of the Wu Song 武松 kind, and, at the raid on the execution ground, indiscriminate killing that “不问青红皂白” ('does not ask which is black and which is white'); the novel honestly keeps two sets of accounts, and this is where it is more modern than any tale of the knight-errant (*xiá* 侠)**.

## Definition: *bìngzhuàn* 并传 ('linked biographies') — the most expensive relay {#def-bingzhuan}

::: definition Twenty chapters, one pair of eyes {#def-jieli}
**Structure** (**Lu's ten chapters, Lin's ten chapters, Yang Zhi 杨志, Wu Song, Song Jiang 宋江…… once a character's home ground is played out, the camera is handed to the next arrival (in the common account, “套结构／连环结构”, 'nested structure / chain structure') — **an expensive relay in the history of literature:** each baton changes one pair of eyes, and the price is that no one person sees the whole (the question at the end of Chapter 3 is answered here: it does not speak of omniscience, it speaks of handing on: **authority turns from one narrator into a string of witnesses (the narrativisation of Chapter 8's "independent sources": the truth is testified in stretches by people who do not know one another**)）；**Technical dividend** (**a new character always comes on first by “被谈论” ('being talked about'): Shi Jin 史进 speaks of Lu Da 鲁达, the teahouse speaks of Lin Chong 林冲, the wineshop speaks of Song Jiang — **reputation arrives first, the person afterwards:** the reader meets the guest with a ledger in hand (Chapter 9's remark on recommendation: the social currency of the *Water Margin* world is a nickname plus one piece of hearsay (“杀人须见血，救人须救彻” ('When you kill you must see blood; when you save you must save to the end.') (Our translation.), and maxims of the rivers and lakes of that kind (the common account) — **a nickname is an evaluation set solid: cited by the community in this way, a person is priced in this way**)）；**Structural risk** (**the protagonist drifts, heavy at the front and light at the back: after the amnesty (*zhāo'ān* 招安, 'accepting the court's amnesty') the rhythm runs out of control (a criticism in the common account) — **this is not a failing of the brush, it is the genre changing gear: the *bingzhuan* form is naturally good to read when it writes “入伙” ('joining the band') (each reason for going up the mountain is different), and naturally bad to read when it writes “结伙” ('the band once formed') (a collective has no point of view**.
:::

## Lin Chong: cancelling the options item by item {#def-linchong}

::: definition The arithmetic of endurance {#def-ren}
**Seven concessions** (**he endures before blundering into White Tiger Hall, endures being tattooed and exiled to Cangzhou, still endures on the road when Dong Chao 董超 and Xue Ba 薛霸 are about to strike, endures in the convict camp at Cangzhou, endures while guarding the fodder yard, and endures even when Lu Qian 陆谦 comes to deceive him — **the chain of plot in the common account:** only when, at the Mountain God Temple, he hears with his own ears that he is about to be burned to death does he kill (“忍” ('endurance') is not a matter of character, it is a list of ways out; cross the list off item by item, and the item that remains is the arithmetic of violence (the negative experiment on Lesson 2's "limiting conditions": **once every limit a person has has been cancelled by the institution, you can no longer explain him by "his character has changed"**)）；**Wind and snow at the Mountain God Temple** (**that fire is the destruction of a file:** his last identity as a law-abiding commoner is burned away — **in the section “论‘费厄泼赖’应该缓行” ('On "fair play" having to be deferred') of Lu Xun's 鲁迅 *Huagai ji* 华盖集 ('The Canopy Collection'), “山神庙” ('the Mountain God Temple') and “野猪林” ('Wild Boar Forest') are used as figures (the chain of citation in the common account) — **the modern reader, too, keeps the accounts for Lin Chong:** the morality of “不犯我不犯他” ('If he does not offend me, I do not offend him.') (Our translation.) fails in the face of people who make a business of breaking the law, and this is why *Water Margin* was cited again and again through the twentieth century**）；**Even on going up to Liangshan 梁山, one must still hand in a *tóumíngzhuàng* 投名状 ('a pledge of one's name on joining')** (**even the greenwood requires results:** even rebellion has a procedure for taking up a post (Chapter 6, "the institution before the will": Liangshan is not a league of free men, it is an armed company with a KPI**.
:::

## Song Jiang: the issuer of credit {#def-songjiang}

::: definition The angel investment of Timely Rain {#def-shiyu}
**The mechanism of “及时雨” ('Timely Rain')** (**at a time when he still has no story, he has already paid in advance for every story: at the first meeting he gives money for a coffin, enters a bond of *yi*, and buries the dead — **the angel investment of moral credit:** his principal is not money, it is the expectation “此人必不卖我” ('This man will certainly not sell me out.') (Our translation.) (the credit version of Chapter 9's "a recommendation is a prophecy, and the rate of fulfilment is the wage": **Song Jiang's rate of paying out is extremely high, and so his name becomes currency in circulation (on the rivers and lakes the five characters “山东呼保义” can stand in for an army (**the economics of language: a nickname is a token, and Song Jiang is the mint**)))；**The two columns of the ledger** (**the column for brothers is filled: *yìqì* 义气 ('loyalty among one's own'), shared hardship, dying together; the column for commoners is often empty (**a criticism in the common account: the cost of a Li Kui 李逵 kind of “杀将去” ('killing one's way on') is paid by the bystanders — **this is not moral corruption in the author, it is the bookkeeping of the genre: the novel keeps accounts only for those who have a surname and a given name (Chapter 8's "a numerator with no denominator": at Liangshan's moments of glory, it has never counted how many sellers of steamed cakes died in the street**)）；**The banner-study of “替天行道” ('Carry out the Way on Heaven's behalf')** (**one banner rewrites plunder as the powers of an expectant public office:** the brand is repositioned, and the long-term aim is written into the contract (the complete case of policy-claim 4 — **who is the executing subject? “天” ('Heaven') does not sign; the only one who can sign is the emperor: so the amnesty is not Song Jiang's miscalculation, it is the only way this banner can be paid out (the tragedy is the necessity of the business model**.
:::

## The two faces of *yi* {#def-yi}

::: definition The legitimisation of private punishment {#def-wusong}
**The chain of Wu's ten chapters** (**the case of Ximen Qing 西门庆 and Pan Jinlian 潘金莲: the accusation fails (the plot in the common account: the authorities have been bought, and the plaint is not granted) → Wu takes evidence on his own account (the neighbours stand as witnesses, and the confession is signed) → execution by private punishment → of his own accord he goes to the authorities and gives himself up (**the plot in the common account — **a technical judgement: this is the most brilliant substitution of procedure in the world of *Water Margin*:** taking evidence, witnessing and giving oneself up, all three are present: private punishment is carried through as a court made by oneself (the reverse of Chapter 8's "procedure is the only weapon the weak can afford": **when procedure cannot be bought through, the weak can only build a procedure of their own, and the cost of a procedure one has built oneself is a life**); **Sima Qian's 司马迁 old ledger-entry** (《史记·游侠列传》, *Records of the Grand Historian*, 'Biographies of the Wandering Knights': “**以匹夫之细，窃杀生之权，君子是讥**” ('**Low as a common man is, he usurps the power over life and death; the gentleman condemns it.**') (Our translation.) (a quotation in the common account) — **the official historians of the Han had already set a price on this matter: the *yi* of the people and the authority of the state collide; the historian condemns, and the knight-errant acts; *Water Margin* writes this disagreement out as the table of income and outlay of an entire novel**.
:::

::: definition The account of indiscriminate killing {#def-lansha}
**The raid on the execution ground at Jiangzhou 江州** (**in the text of the common account, descriptions of the sort in which Li Kui “**不问军民，一斧劈将去**” ('**Not asking whether they were soldiers or civilians, he chopped his way forward with one axe.**') (Our translation.) (the gist of the quotation follows the common account) — **the novel does not avoid it, and does not even comment:** it only sets these two sets of numbers side by side (the axe of the man of *yi*, and the heads of the onlookers (**this is the moral courage of *Water Margin*:** it leaves the reader's cheer and the reader's unease on the same page (the critical line of the common account: from the notebooks of Ming writers (*bǐjì* 笔记) to modern research on the history of fiction, all of them carry the sneer of “羡杀” ('envying the killing') (the reverse of Lesson 1's "not giving out the incident": **not giving out an explanation is also a kind of honesty in narrative**)); **The taxonomy of “义”** (**reciprocity towards brothers (recoverable), sympathy towards the weak (occasional), destruction of the enemy (no name left) — **three kinds of “义” share one character, and therefore often cover for one another (Lesson 2, "one word must have one sense": the whole technique of reading *Water Margin* is to ask, every time the character “义” appears, one question: which column?**).
:::

## Language, commentary and downstream {#def-xiayou}

::: definition From the coarse to the refined {#def-yuyan}
**The linguistic base of Song material for storytelling and singing (*shuōchàng* 说唱)** (**the line of the common account: the book took shape “以白话为语言底本、累代层积” ('with the vernacular (*báihuà* 白话) as the base text of its language, accumulated in layers across the ages'), and Ming writers already had the judgement “**与粗致精**” ('**from the coarse to the refined**'), a quotation in the common account — **dialect, the jargon of the trades, and the set phrases of the yamen enter the writing:** people in the story do not argue principle; people in the story get things done (the victory, in the storyteller's scripts (*huàběn* 话本) of Chapter 9, of 我们, 你 and 我 ('we', 'you', 'I') entering the main text is settled on the books here: **the first author of *Water Margin* is not Luo 罗, but the voice of oral storytelling (*shuōhuà* 说话) in Bianjing 汴京 and Shandong**); **Technical detail** (**the system of address in “那婆娘” ('that woman'), “那厮” ('that fellow'), “官人” ('sir') and “大郎” ('eldest brother') = social coordinates; within a single sentence it locates speaker and addressee for rank and for closeness (the textbook page of Lesson 7's "a term of address is a receipt for power"**.
:::

::: definition Jin Shengtan 金圣叹 cuts *Water Margin* {#def-zhan}
**The seventy-chapter edition** (****the common account: the edition with Jin's commentary takes the opening of the chapter “惊马” ('Startled Horse') (the celestial writing on the stone tablet) as the point where the whole book stops, adds besides “卢俊义做梦、英雄尽戮” ('Lu Junyi dreams, and the heroes are slaughtered to the last.') (Our translation.) as the close (the common account), and for its authority falsely claims a “贯华堂古本” ('ancient edition of the Guanhuatang 贯华堂') — **the critic's discontent is written out as Chapter 71:** he cannot tolerate the amnesty, and so adds to the whole book a dream of a collective execution (the reverse of Chapter 1's "reunion = a judicial pronouncement": **a nightmare in place of the verdict**)）；**The power to rewrite an ending** (**this is the only time in the history of Chinese criticism that "rewriting the ending" has successfully entered the edition in circulation (the line of the common account) — **a way of reading can make a classic, and can also make an edition:** half of what we say today, “《水浒》到梁山泊英雄排座次为止” ('*Water Margin* stops where the heroes of Liangshan Marsh are ranked in their seats.') (Our translation.), is the fruit of Jin Shengtan's teaching (Chapter 2's "the commentary edition is the established text", and the trilogy of the Mao 毛 *Dufa* (《读法》, 'How to Read') in Chapter 3, sound together here: **three editors of the seventeenth century (Li Zhuowu 李卓吾, Jin Shengtan, Mao Zonggang 毛宗岗) determine what we read more than does any author of the fourteenth century**)).
:::

::: definition From thirty-six men to one hundred and eight {#def-xiawu}
**The thinness of the sources** (**the common account: at the end of the Northern Song, Song Jiang rose in Heshuo 河朔, the histories record thirty-six men, and around Haizhou 海州 they accepted the amnesty (the line of the common account) — **in the notebooks of Song writers (of the kind of *Xuanhe yishi* 宣和遗事, 'Anecdotes of the Xuanhe era') and in the local gazetteers, this band has already been written as a list of “三十六猛将” ('thirty-six fierce generals') (the specimen of the layering of sources in Lesson 1: **between "Song Jiang's thirty-six men" and "one hundred and eight men" lie three hundred years of storytelling and two floods**)）；**Downstream of the amnesty** (**figures of Liangshan in the variety plays (*zájù* 杂剧) of the Yuan often have a “正果” ('the proper fruit'); sequels by Ming writers, and the Qing *Dangkou zhi* 荡寇志 ('Quelling the Bandits'), have them wiped out instead (the line of the common account) — **one and the same band of robbers is, under different dynasties, by turns amnestied and campaigned against:** the amnesty policy of literary history is adjusted with the political climate (the *Water Margin* version of Chapter 3's "three standpoints stacked"**.
:::

::: example Open a columnar ledger for “义” {#ex-zhang}
Choose any one episode at Liangshan (“三打祝家庄”, 'the three attacks on the Zhu Family Manor', the common account), and for the “义” in it open a ledger of three columns: **towards brothers (who benefits) / towards the community (who set a rule) / towards a third party (who pays the bill)**, and write: **if this battle were witnessed by one manor-hand of the Zhu Family Manor, which sentence of the novel would disappear**.
::: solution
**The three columns** — **the column for brothers** (**those who join share the spoils, the wounded are carried back, and in a retreat someone covers the rear:** *yi* that can be got back, and the books are clear (the principle of reciprocity in Chapter 9**); **the column for the community** (**military orders of the kind “三日内破庄” ('Break the manor within three days.') (Our translation.) and the strategic aim “先破祝家以立威” ('First break the Zhu family, so as to establish awe.') (Our translation.):** *yi* is converted into the discipline of an organisation, and into credit (establishing awe is an advertisement shown to possible allies and to enemies (the economics of the shop-sign in Chapter 3**); **the column for third parties** (**manor-hands, the household of Zhongli 钟离, the wife of Dengyun 登云, and a family of old and young:** on the books there are only numbers of people, and verbs of the kind “尽数诛戮” ('slaughter every one of them') (Chapter 8, "numerator and denominator" — **this is why the chapter reads as both exhilarating and uneasy**); **“the sentence that would disappear if a manor-hand were the witness”** — **every labelling phrase that calls the other side “淫妇” ('wanton woman') or “丑虏” ('vile captives'), and the banner-slogan “替天行道” itself:** change the witness by one, and the account-heading of the same act changes entirely: this is not a question of editions, it is the physical nature of point of view (Chapter 4: what is called “客观叙述” ('objective narration') is only that no one has had a chance to speak**).
:::
:::

::: intuition Two uses of the *xia* {#int-xia}
**“侠” (*xia*), throughout Chinese narrative, is a reserve institution** (**it is not praised as freedom; it is tacitly taken for a remedy: only when the authorities fail does private violence obtain a licence (Chapter 6, "a concession must be able to be taken back": the licence in *Water Margin* is one the story is at every moment calling in — **Wu Song gives himself up, Song Jiang gives his loyalty to the end, Li Kui is poisoned to death (**three times the whole book takes back the authority of “侠”: the novel understands, better than its readers do, that violence needs a clearing-up afterwards**); **The modern use** (**from the martial knight-errant fiction (*wǔxiá* 武侠) of the Republican period to film and television, the story of Liangshan is rewritten over and over as "resistance to oppression" (the line of the common account) — **every version deletes some third-party entries:** the history of adaptation is a history of the columns (the political version of Chapter 7's "to select is to delete and to alter": **who is crossed off the ledger is the most honest autobiography of an ideology**).
:::

::: history The promotion of a book about bandits {#sec-shengzhi}
**1120s** Song Jiang rises, and surrender is accepted (**the common account:** history gave only thirty-six names and one submission (the division of the accounts in Lesson 1's "skeleton and flesh" begins from these two numbers**)); **1200s—1300s** *Xuanhe yishi*, and the figures of the *Water Margin* story in storytelling and in variety plays (**lists, nicknames and the embryos of episodes gradually pile up:** collective writing, inside popular literature, is a mode of production (the clause of Chapter 2**)); **1400s—1500s** the hundred-chapter elaborate recension (*fánběn* 繁本) and the printing of the Jiajing years (**the line of the common account:** for a thread about bandits to be written out as a long novel, the bookshops must first believe that length can be sold for money (the second realisation of the clause of Chapter 1**)); **1600s** Li Zhuowu's commentary edition, and the theory that it was “发愤之所作” ('a work produced by giving vent to indignation') (the line of the common account — **the critics applied, on its behalf, for the standing of literature:** a book that tells of killing was read as a book that tells of injustice**); **1640s—1660s** Jin Shengtan's seventy-chapter edition (**the ending cut, a nightmare added, an ancient edition forged:** the editor's victory**); **1700s** *Dangkou zhi* (**authority taken back the other way:** Liangshan is wiped out by the king's army — **Qing writers, with one sequel, changed a novel's verdict back (the extreme case of Chapter 3's "an edition is an ideology"**)); **1900s—now** from the "revolutionary novel" to gangster narrative and excerpts in textbooks (**the independent fate of 《智取生辰纲》 ('Taking the birthday convoy by guile'):** the selections tell rebellion as wisdom, and tell the severed head as the default of the cost (the long case of Chapter 7**).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of *Water Margin*
**Treating “农民起义” ('peasant uprising') as the vocation by which the book is explained** (**once a book is explained all the way down by one mode of production, it no longer has an internal moral problem:** it is, first of all, a novel about how an organisation of violence legitimises itself (this topic holds good in any age**)); **Reading “义” as a virtue without conditions** (**if the three columns are not distinguished, indiscriminate killing can be given cover as heroic boldness (**each time the character “义” appears, ask one question: which column (the clause of the taxonomy**))); **Taking the Jin edition as an ancient edition** (**the seventy-chapter edition is an editorial product of the seventeenth century:** a citation has to name the edition; “原貌” ('the original appearance') is an illusion here (the fourth sounding of the methodology of editions in Lesson 1**)); **Making a psychological diagnosis of the characters** (**Li Kui is a narrative device, not a case of illness:** his function is to push “义” to the point of not calculating, so that the reader sees the cost of not calculating (Chapter 3: character is the result of a distribution**.
:::

::: exercise A list of ways out {level=1}
For any one person (Lin Chong, or one you choose yourself), draw up a "list of ways out" (five grounds on which he might originally not have rebelled), then cross them off in the order of the plot; write out **the last one to be crossed off**.
::: solution
Marking: **the grounds must be concrete down to an institution** (**registration in the army, land, a contract of marriage, a superior's old goodwill, a procedure by which one can appeal:** the more concrete, the more it hurts when it is crossed off**); **the last item is usually "acknowledgement of one's status", not one's life** (**for reference:** what kills him is not Gao Qiu 高俅, it is cancellation: what *Water Margin* has always written is not bad men holding the road, but the arithmetic after the possibility of appeal has vanished (the reverse of the clearing of the ground in Chapter 6 — here no one offers a step down**).

::: exercise Short essay: violence needs a clearing-up {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**the most honest thing about *Water Margin* is that, after it has written the heroes' deeds, it still shoots a scene in which accounts are settled: it knows that after an axe has chopped down there must be someone to sweep the floor, and so it has Song Jiang give his loyalty to the end, has Li Kui poisoned, and has Lu Junyi 卢俊义 dream of the executioners — it does not love violence any more than the reader does; it has only asked, earlier than anyone else, one question: and then what?**". You must draw on at least six items: **the relay of the linked biographies, and its blind spot**, **the cancelling, item by item, of the ways out**, **Song Jiang's mechanism of minting**, **the logic by which the banner and the amnesty are paid out**, **private punishment as a self-made court**, **Jin Shengtan's nightmare**; and you must decide: **whether “侠”, as a reserve institution, can in narrative end only in failure — you must take "if Liangshan greatly succeeds" as a thought-experiment, and write what that novel which might have existed would look like (and why it would not be good to read)**.
::: solution
Points for marking: the six mechanisms (**relay, cancellation, minting, paying out, the self-made court, the nightmare**); the thought-experiment (**for reference: if, after the amnesty, men of merit were enfeoffed as princes and shared a time of peace, the story would become a register of offices conferred:** the narrative value of Liangshan comes precisely from the way it is dissolved (a band of violence cannot be legitimised in institutions, and so it can only be tragic in narrative — "and then what?" is the only sequel a novel of this kind has (in marking the script, see whether it is pointed out: **after *Water Margin*, *Dangkou zhi* and a withdrawal from the world in the manner of Jin Yong 金庸 are two answers to the same "and then what?": one passes sentence on it, the other has it leave the stage**); if the conclusion can be written "**every novel that glorifies the striking of a blow gives itself away in one 'and afterwards?': *Water Margin* writes this question into the main text, and so it is a classic, and not a gratification story (爽文)**", that is an A.
:::

::: summary
The two sets of accounts, filed: structure (**eyes handed on: authority comes from a string of witnesses**), Lin Chong (**endurance = a list of ways out: what kills him is cancellation**), Song Jiang (**a nickname is a token, and he is the mint; the ledger has two columns**), the banner (**Heaven does not sign: the amnesty is the only way to pay it out**), private punishment (**taking evidence, witnessing, giving oneself up: the cost of a self-made court is a life**), afterwards (**the editor decides the edition: Jin Shengtan's nightmare entered the edition in circulation**). The method, in one sentence: **when you read the character “义”, first ask which column; when you reach a place that feels exhilarating, first ask who is sweeping the floor**. :::
