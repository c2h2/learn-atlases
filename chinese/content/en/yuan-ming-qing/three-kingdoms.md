---
title: "*Romance of the Three Kingdoms*"
---

The previous chapter took a maidservant's defence to its highest point; this chapter is about a novel that rewrites a whole stretch of the histories as an explanation machine — **the algorithm of *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* is this: translate the consequences of history into the character of persons; what it teaches the reader is not the Three Kingdoms, but the single rule that "a story must be explainable", and this is also its greatest risk, because real history often cannot be explained**.

## Definition: the algorithm of historical storytelling {#def-suanfa}

::: definition The split of accounts between skeleton and flesh {#def-fenzhang}
**The layer of materials** (**the *Records of the Three Kingdoms* of Chen Shou 陈寿, and Pei's commentary, are the bone; the flesh is Song–Yuan storytelling (*shuōhuà* 说话), “说三分” ('telling the Three Kingdoms'), together with the variety plays (*zaju*) and plain tales (*pínghuà* 平话) of the Yuan and the Ming (the usual outline)** — **the making of the book is cumulative: the Jiajing printed edition, in twenty-four *juan* (volumes) and two hundred and forty sections, is the earliest text now known; Mao Zonggang 毛宗岗 revised it into the one hundred and twenty chapters in general circulation (the usual account)**); **the reading “七实三虚” ('seven parts fact, three parts invention')** (**the usual account judges the book by this** — **this lesson does not accept that it is a question of proportion:** **the histories are responsible for a skeleton that can be checked, and the novel is responsible for motives that can be understood: the two are not mixed into each other; they stay together by dividing the work** (here the graduation piece of Chapter 1 is completed, "the genre contract of historical storytelling (*jiǎngshǐ* 讲史): great events may not be changed; every small incident must be supplied")); **the core of the algorithm** (**wherever there is a victory or a defeat, a reason must be given; wherever there is a reason, it must land on a person** — **and so a campaign is a financial statement of character (Chibi, Jieting and Yiling are all like this:** **the causal chain is shortened until it is one person: this is the permanent cost-cutting project of popular narrative** (where Lesson 2, "compress many causes into a person", is cashed in: historical storytelling remakes world history as a personality test))).
:::

::: definition The first sentence already gives the ending away {#def-diyiju}
“话说天下大势，分久必合，合久必分。” (a quotation in the usual account) — The story says: the great trend of the world is that what has long been divided must unite, and what has long been united must divide. (Our translation.) — **The opening finishes broadcasting the endings of both turns of the book:** **suspense is not the outcome, but the how** (the classical solution to the spoiler clause in Chapter 9: **once the listeners already know the ending, the whole income of the narrative comes from the process: this is the business model of historical storytelling, and it is the same contract as today's label “根据真实事件改编” ('adapted from true events')**); **the function of a cyclical view of history** (**it is not a philosophy of history; it is a device for managing expectations:** **the reader does not need to worry “汉室会不会亡” ('Will the house of Han fall?') (it will), and needs only ask “谁在崩坏时还做事” ('Who is still doing things while it collapses?'): moral attention shifts from the outcome to the posture** (the large-scale version of Chapter 1's “团圆＝司法宣告” ('reunion = a judicial declaration'): **the cycle is the greatest promise of reunion — however great the disorder, the accounts balance in the end**)).
:::

## The triangle of orthodox succession {#def-zhengtong}

::: definition Moral intuition, the theory of orthodox succession, and the box office {#def-sanbian}
**People of the Song had already taken sides** (Su Shi 苏轼, in *Zhilin* 志林 (*Forest of Records*), notes the marketplace listening to “说三国” ('the telling of the Three Kingdoms'): “闻刘玄德败，顰蹙有出涕者；闻曹操败，即喜唱快” (a quotation in the usual account) — When they heard that Liu Xuande 刘玄德 was defeated, they knit their brows, and some shed tears; when they heard that Cao Cao 曹操 was defeated, they at once rejoiced and cried out in delight. (Our translation.) — **popular moral intuition precedes the literati theory of orthodox succession (*zhèngtǒng* 正统) by two hundred years: the novel does not invent a stance; it makes out a bill for a stance that already exists**); **the added force of the Learning of Principle (*lǐxué* 理学)** (**the dispute over the orthodox succession of the Eastern Jin and of Shu-Han is raised again and again in the discussions of the Confucian scholars of the Song (the usual outline)** — **the theory of orthodox succession supplies the legal principle of “为什么该这样站” ('why one ought to stand on this side'); the marketplace supplies the demand of “我就这样站” ('I simply stand on this side'): the *Romance* is the two bound in one volume**); **the box-office supplement** (the leading figures on the Shu-Han line are the best suited to be adapted as plays: loyalty, rightness (*yì* 义), ardour and clumsiness, each with an act of its own (the usual account: plays on the Three Kingdoms have lasted without fading): **readers need “忠” ('loyalty') to have somewhere it can be invested, and so the narrative issues legitimacy to a man who wove mats and sold sandals** (**bloodline is the ticket in; virtue is the currency: between pedigree and ability, this novel in the end chooses the one asset “人心” ('the people's hearts')**)); **the price** (from Cao Pi 曹丕 downward, everyone becomes a supporting part, and the main line of Wei and Jin is written thin: **the bill for a narrative of orthodox succession is paid by the latter half of the history** (Chapter 8, “省话地图” ('the map of words saved'): the stance saves the reader a whole chapter on the decay of institutions)).
:::

## The character generator {#def-shengcheng}

::: definition Two theories of legitimacy {#def-lianglun}
**Cao Cao's words, “宁教我负天下人，休教天下人负我”** (a quotation in the usual account, the episode of Lü Bochi 吕伯侈) — I would rather that I betray the people of the world than that the people of the world betray me. (Our translation.) — **a self-defence uttered during one flight is written up as an axiom for a whole life;** **the novel's technique:** **give one political logic a face that can be quoted** (Lesson 7, “口号是压缩饼干” ('a slogan is a compressed biscuit'): these twelve characters are the sheet of directions for a violent consequentialism); **Liu Bei's 刘备 words, “举大事必以人为本，今人归吾，吾何忍弃去”** (a quotation in the usual account, the episode of crossing the river with the people in company) — To undertake a great affair one must take the people as the root; now that the people have come over to me, how could I bear to abandon them and leave? (Our translation.) — **the other algorithm inside the same rout:** **he treats “人心” ('the people's hearts') as an asset that can be accumulated, and not as ornament;** **the conclusion of reading the two together** (**the two characters are not a good man and a bad man; they are the embodiments of two theories of legitimacy:** **one says that order comes from force, the other that force comes from consent** (the political-philosophy version of Lesson 2, “价值主张 vs 事实主张” ('a value claim vs a factual claim'): **the deepest line in the *Romance* is that it gives each of these two claims a character, and lets them quarrel for a thousand years on the reader's behalf**)).
:::

::: definition The mechanism of writing too far {#def-guotou}
**The usual account cites Lu Xun's 鲁迅 criticism** — “欲显刘备之长厚而似伪，状诸葛之多智而近妖” (*Zhongguo xiaoshuo shilüe* 中国小说史略, *A Brief History of Chinese Fiction*) — Wishing to display Liu Bei's kindness and magnanimity, it makes him seem false; portraying Zhuge's 诸葛 great intelligence, it brings him near to a demon. (Our translation.) — **the technical meaning of this criticism:** **when a virtue or an ability is written as a general policy, it loses its exceptions, and a character with no exceptions looks like acting** (the version, for characters, of Lesson 2, “强度三档透支” ('an overdraft on three grades of intensity'): **what is overdrawn is not the force of the author's brush, but the reader's credit limit of trust**); **Kongming's 孔明 double project** (**he is at once a statesman and a master of occult arts: the three visits, the response at Longzhong, the borrowing of the east wind, the rite to turn the star away** — **the novel upgrades him all the way from a capacity for institutions to a capacity for the weather, and the faster the upgrade, the less intelligible the character becomes** (the demon is an inflation of ability)); **“多智近妖” ('great intelligence, nearing the demonic') and “长厚似伪” ('kind magnanimity, seeming false') have one source** (**both come from the cost-cutting of historical storytelling: an explanation must be simple, and the simple must be pure** — **the pure person is therefore distorted on the page:** **the novel's best characters (Cao Cao, Guan Yu 关羽, Zhou Yu 周瑜) are precisely the few who were not written pure**).
:::

::: definition Guan Yu: the accountancy of reputation {#def-guanyu}
**“土山三约” ('the three conditions at Tushan')** (**the plot in the usual account: surrender to the Han and not to Cao; provide for the two ladies; the moment he knows where his elder brother is, he goes** — **“降” ('surrender') is split into conditional clauses: this is not hypocrisy; it is the journal-entry method of moral accounting:** **one liability is split into three dealings that can be written off** (the heroic version of the limiting words in Lesson 2: **a limit is a bumper; the contrarian specialises in the hero who has no limit**)); **hanging up the seal and sealing the gold, and the lone journey of a thousand *li*** (**obligations managed as a checklist:** **he pays them out item by item, and can therefore carry the whole of his reputation away with him** (**Guan Yu's “义” (*yì*, 'rightness') is in essence a credit record: what he does all his life is keep that record continuously open to inspection**) — a *li* is a traditional unit of distance); **the Huarong road** (**a military pledge and a private debt of gratitude come into conflict, and he settles it by a “私释” ('private release'):** **a conflict of duties that cannot be reconciled inside the institution has to be completed by the character breaking the law — this is the novel's standard way of handling a moral dilemma: it does not give a conclusion; it gives a cost** (Lesson 6: a concession must be recoverable; here it can no longer be recovered, and so death and defeat are used to pay (the narrative necessity of Guan Yu's death is planted here))); **downstream** (**Emperor Guan 关帝, the merchant leagues, and the witnessing of contracts (the usual outline:** **a literary character becomes a guarantor of commercial credit; this is the most extravagant “文本外胜利” ('victory outside the text') in Chinese history** (the upper limit of Lesson 15, “经典＝持续被引用” ('a classic = being cited without cease'): **those who cite him are not readers, but draft banks (*piàohào* 票号)**))).
:::

## Structure, commentary and parallel lines {#def-jiegou}

::: definition Parallel zero-focalisation {#def-bingxing}
**“花开两朵，各表一枝”** (**the ordinary use, inside a long work, of this stock phrase of the storyteller's script (*huaben*), as the usual account has it)** — Two flowers have opened; each branch will be told on its own. (Our translation.) — **It is a public announcement that the point of view is about to change:** **the narrator admits that he cannot be in two places at once, and so hands the choice over to historical accident** (the price of zero-focalisation in Chapter 4: omniscient, but with no point of view — **what the reader is given is a surveillance video-recorder, not a pair of eyes** (**this is precisely the formal reason for the “人物多而人心少” ('many characters, and few hearts') of *Three Kingdoms*, and it is also the first problem that *Water Margin* (*Outlaws of the Marsh*) and *Dream of the Red Chamber* (*The Story of the Stone*) each have to solve**)); **the multithreading of the wars** (**Guandu and Chibi each take several dozen chapters: a weave of three lines, main line, branch line and foreshadowing** (the usual account: Mao Zonggang's commentary (*píngdiǎn* 评点) is especially fond of marking the methods “隔年下种” ('sowing the seed the year before') and “山后放炮” ('firing a cannon from behind the hill')) — **the commentator tells the narrative as a chess manual:** **for the first time the novel is read as a building** (the sister event of Chapter 2's “读法” ('how to read'): **Jin Shengtan 金圣叹 teaches people to read a passage; Mao Zonggang teaches people to read a whole work**)).
:::

::: definition Mao Zonggang's *How to Read* {#def-maodufa}
**Revision and commentary** (**in the early Qing, the joint commentary edition of the Mao father and son revised the wording and cut old episodes, and became the text of one hundred and twenty chapters in general circulation (the usual account)** — **another case of the commented text being the established text** (the Jin Shengtan clause of Chapter 2: **the *Three Kingdoms* we read is the *Three Kingdoms* that people of the Qing compiled for us**)); **“读三国志法” ('How to read the *Records of the Three Kingdoms*')** (**the usual account of how it marks such examples as “最妙” ('most wonderful'), “隔年下种” ('sowing the seed the year before') and “两臂相应” ('the two arms answering each other')** — **it teaches reading as a hunt for hooks:** **a historical novel is rewritten as a textbook of writing** (it fully deserves its standing as the old textbook of Lesson 10's project of the sentence at the head of a paragraph, and of Lesson 5's set of four)); **the politics of commentary** (**the stance of orthodox succession is strengthened in the comments, and a number of old episodes are deleted (the usual outline)** — **to cut and alter is to hold the trial again:** **the Mao text “尊刘” ('honours Liu') more than the Jiajing text, so the curve of intensity of “尊刘贬曹” ('honour Liu and disparage Cao') is a stack of three directions, Song, Ming and Qing, and is not Luo Guanzhong's 罗贯中 original intention** (an edition is an ideology (the three bangs in a row of Lesson 1's “分账” ('splitting the account') and Chapter 2's “版本要注明” ('the edition must be stated')))).
:::

::: example Five characters and two thousand characters {#ex-wugezi}
*Records of the Three Kingdoms*, Book of Shu, Biography of Zhuge Liang 诸葛亮: “凡三往，乃见。” — In all he went three times, and only then was he received. (Our translation.) — The “三顾茅庐” ('three visits to the thatched cottage') of the *Romance* is written out over two whole chapters (the going and returning, the wind and snow, the boy servant, the dismounting, the poem inscribed, the friends, the younger brother and the father-in-law, and only at the end the plan offered in reply). Please list the novel's **inventory of added value**, and point out: **among these additions, which item is necessary to the narrative, which is ideological, and which is only good for selling**.
::: solution
**The list of added value** — ① **the failure of three journeys there and back** (**necessary to the narrative:** **it turns “见” ('being received') into a cost, and cost produces weight:** **a genius who is seen at the first attempt is not worth one departure from the hills** (the version, for a character, of Lesson 3, “带完整出处的句子有溢价” ('a sentence that carries its full source has a premium'))); ② **the sequence of setting (wind and snow, the village, zither and books)** (**necessary to the narrative:** **virtue is written as an ecology — the setting this person has chosen conducts the first round of the defence on his behalf**); ③ **a circle of “不仕者” ('those who will not take office'), Cui Zhouping 崔州平, Shi Guangyuan 石广元, Meng Gongwei 孟公威, Huang Chengyan 黄承彦 and others** (**ideological:** **they preach over and over the recluse's thesis “天下无道” ('All under Heaven is without the Way'),** **and so prepare in advance a counter-thesis for the one who does go out to serve:** **Zhuge Liang must choose “做” ('to do') inside the clamour of “不做” ('not to do')** (the concession paragraph of 6 is extended across a whole chapter: the actors on the other side are paid very high fees, because they are charged with making the thesis believable)); ④ **the map-style analysis in *Longzhong dui* 隆中对 (*The Response at Longzhong*)** (**all three at once:** **a policy white paper as the turn with which he comes on** (Lesson 4: give the framework first and the loyalty after; a character = his framework of analysis) — and at the same time it writes “三分” ('the division into three') as Zhuge Liang's invention, which belongs to the bookkeeping of the narrative of orthodox succession); ⑤ **the inscribed poem, the couplet on the gate, and the boy servant's replies** (**good for selling:** **they are slots for performance, where the reader may rest his feet, where the bookshop may put in illustrations, and where later ages may cut and alter:** **the “水” ('water'; the padding) of popular literature is not a defect; it is the rest area it gives the reader** (the Ming version of Chapter 9, “字数与节奏” ('character count and rhythm'): a serial played in a continuous run needs one episode that does not advance the plot)).
:::
:::

::: intuition The addiction to explainability {#int-jie}
**This novel trains up a national habit of reading** (**wherever there is a defeat, the person must be investigated; wherever there is a person, the heart must be investigated** — **“读史” ('reading history') becomes “读人” ('reading people'), and reading people becomes a search for faults** (the popular version of Lesson 1's “省话地图” ('the map of words saved'): **to translate a problem of institutions into a problem of conduct is the explanation that saves the most labour, and the one most easily become addicted to**)); **its antidote is in the same book** (**the loyalty of Guan Yu, the loss of Jieting, the taking of Jingzhou — beside every perfect closing of a causal chain there is a failure to close** (**the best moments of the *Romance* are the failures of its algorithm: Heaven's will, illness, the direction of the wind, the years of a life, those passages where “无从怪谁” ('there is no one who can be blamed')** (the failure of the rite against the star, on the Wuzhang plain, is the algorithm stating its own case: words of the sort “谋事在人，成事在天” (the gist of the sentence as given in the usual account) — The planning of affairs lies with human beings; the accomplishing of affairs lies with Heaven. (Our translation.) — **at its highest climax the novel admits that its own model has a boundary**))).
:::

::: history The mass production of an algorithm {#sec-liangchan}
**1400s—1500s** The making of the book, and the early printings (**the usual account: Luo Guanzhong was a man of the late Yuan and the early Ming, and the Jiajing text is the earliest block-printed edition now known** — **the bookshops discovered that length can be sold for money** (the clause in Chapter 1): **“演义” (*yǎnyì* 演义, the elaborated romance) from this point becomes a format that can be copied**); **1500s—1600s** A burst of *yanyi* (**《列国》 (*Lieguo*, 'The Various States'), 《两汉》 (*The Two Han*), 《唐书》 (*Tangshu*, 'The Book of Tang') and 《宋史》 (*Songshi*, 'The History of Song') follow on in a line (the usual account)** — **once the algorithm is made public, the subject matter begins to involute (*nèijuǎn* 内卷):** **historical storytelling becomes the Ming's slot for genre films** (the industrial version of Chapter 3, “文类=协议” ('a genre = a protocol'): **what the reader buys is the same explanation machine with the dynasty changed**)); **1600s** Prefaces and comments by Yuan Yuling 袁于令 and others (**the usual outline: sayings of the sort “正史以事，野史以情” — Official history proceeds by events; unofficial history proceeds by feeling. (Our translation.) — were formulated again and again in the late Ming** — **the theoretical self-awareness of popular fiction arises in prefaces and colophons, the money put up by merchants and the names signed by literati** (the accelerated version of Chapter 1, “批评的滞后” ('the lag of criticism'): this time the lag was only thirty years)); **1660s—1700s** Mao Zonggang's revised and commented edition is settled under a single authority (**the war of editions ends in the patience of one editor**); **1900s—the present** From “国民读物” ('reading matter for the nation') to a textbook of strategy and a film-and-television IP (**one and the same book is read, in turn, as: a textbook of loyalty and rightness, a manual of stratagems, a specimen of national psychology, a case in management, and material for games** (**every rereading is a borrowing of a new algorithm: the definition of a classic is “可被反复误读的精度” ('a precision that can be misread again and again')**)).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of the Three Kingdoms
**Taking “尊刘贬曹” ('honour Liu and disparage Cao') as a constant of Heaven** (**it is a stance stacked from three directions, Song, Ming and Qing (the curve of the editions can be checked), and not a constant of human nature:** **to read it today one must at the same time read out the institutional problems omitted on the Cao-Wei side (the great clans, the provinces and commanderies, the military system** (Chapter 8, the map of words saved))); **using the novel as a historical source** (**things of the sort “空城计” ('the empty-city stratagem') and “借东风” ('borrowing the east wind') either come out of notebook literature (*biji*) or are pure invention (the usual account)** — **its function is to get the characters told clearly, not to get the events recorded:** **to cite it, one must change subject** (Chapter 3, “引文的邻居是法庭” ('the neighbour of a quotation is a court of law'))); **taking the characters as psychology** (**Zhou Yu's “量窄” ('narrowness of measure') is a narrative function, not a medical history:** **character in the novel is the result of an allocation (who needs to die so that the death is worth something)**, **not the result of a diagnosis** (Chapter 4, on point of view: to read a character's “缺点” ('defects') as the author's arrangement comes closer to the fact than to read them as a medical file)); **a superstitious faith in the “谋略学” ('stratagem studies') way of reading** (**to take *Three Kingdoms* as a textbook of management studies is the same as to take a tragedy as a travel guide:** **its real course is the boundary of the causal model — every effort to simplify the world into a predictable game of chess will find, in that failure on the Wuzhang plain, its own end-of-course examination**).
:::

::: exercise An audit of causes {level=1}
Choose one defeat in the *Romance* (any one of Jieting, Yiling or Jingzhou), list the causal chain the novel gives (no more than five levels), and then, for each level, find a “非人格” ('non-personal') substitute cause (supply, terrain, climate, institutions).
::: solution
Marking: **the five-level chain must be identifiable in the original wording** (**who said it, who heard it, who did not go**); **the column of substitute causes must hold for at least two levels** (**if none of them hold, that shows that the causation of this narrative has already closed until it cannot be refuted —** **and that is precisely the lesion of the stratagem-studies way of reading** (Lesson 1: an explanation that cannot be refuted is not an explanation; it is a stance)).
:::

(Our translation.)
