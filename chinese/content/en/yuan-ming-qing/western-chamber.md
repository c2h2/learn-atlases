---
title: "*The Story of the Western Wing*"
---

The end of the previous chapter said that the acoustic apparatus of the variety play (*zaju*) cannot handle "several people having an inner life at the same time"; this chapter writes the play that dismantles the apparatus from the inside — **the contribution of *The Story of the Western Wing* (*Romance of the Western Chamber*) is not that it wrote a pair of lovers, but that it rewrote *qíng* 情 ('feeling') from a state of mind into a qualification: from then on, Chinese narratives of love have one legislative clause: to have feeling is to have a ground**.

## Definition: an eight-hundred-year campaign of rewriting {#def-gaixie}

::: definition Three versions, three juries {#def-sanban}
**① Yuan Zhen, *The Tale of Yingying* (*Yingying zhuan*) (the common account)** (**seduction at the start and abandonment at the end, and Zhang Sheng has a speech in his defence: "**尤物害人**" ("An extraordinary creature harms people." (Our translation.)) "**予之德不足以胜妖孽，是用忍情**" ("My virtue is not sufficient to overcome the demon, and therefore I restrain my feeling." (Our translation.)) — **the ending of a Tang tale (*chuanqi*) is a document of self-defence: the right to decide the narrative sits with the man, and the injured party is left only a passage of zither sound (a specimen of Chapter 4's "who is speaking": **this is a story that writes a breach of faith as cultivation**)**); **② Dong Jieyuan, *The Story of the Western Wing* as *zhūgōngdiào* 诸宫调 ('a storytelling-and-singing form') (Jin, the common account)** (**a storytelling-and-singing rewrite: the conflict is changed from "a faithless heart" to "a parent blocking the marriage": **the jury is changed from scholar-officials to townspeople — the person who tells and sings has to win the listeners a reckoning (Chapter 9's box-office constraint: the motive for changing the ending is written into the structure of the income**)**); **③ Wang Shifu, *The Story of the Western Wing* (the common account)** (**a variety play in five plays, following the reunion of Dong's *Western Wing* and then adding a statute: "**愿普天下有情的都成了眷属**" ("May all everywhere under heaven who have feeling be joined as spouses." (Our translation.)) — **what matters is not the "reunion" but "everywhere under heaven" and "those who have feeling": one exception is rewritten as a universal rule (the lyric version of Lesson 2's right to define: **he made a new category — "those who have feeling", whose condition of admission is feeling, not family rank, a betrothal, or a parent's command**)**). **Conclusion of the audit** (**the same story was tried three times: **each time, what the rewriting really takes as its object is not the plot, but the moral intuition of the listeners of that moment: the history of versions is the developing solution of the history of morals (a rare continuous specimen of Lesson 15's "the text and the age": one play exposes an eight-hundred-year list of juries**)**).
:::

## Transgressing the system {#def-yuegui}

::: definition Five plays and divided singing {#def-wuben}
**Length** (**five plays and twenty-one acts (the form in the common account) — **the standard play of the variety play is the "physiological quantity" of four acts and one person (Lesson 1); *The Western Wing* turns it into a long play performed over several days in succession: **love needs duration, because an inner life is a project that has a construction schedule (the second sense of Chapter 1's "length is a physiological quantity": long enough that hesitation, misunderstanding and longing each stay a full night**)**); **divided singing** (**Zhang Sheng sings, Yingying sings, Hongniang sings: **the right to lyric utterance is confiscated from one person's hands and distributed anew (the acoustic problem at the end of Chapter 1 is here solved head-on: **the price of sympathy along a single line is that other people have no inner life, whereas love happens to take place inside "other people's inner lives"**)**); **the technical after-effect** (**after the singing is divided, "the play is in whose throat" becomes "the play is in the gaps of the relationship" — **the centre of gravity of Chinese theatre moves from the lyric monologue towards the scene between two players (after Yuan variety plays, Ming–Qing southern drama (*chuanqi*) divides the singing throughout; Chapter 7): ***The Western Wing* is the first full-length novel to stand on a stage (its faults and its greatness have the same cause: it is too good at writing individual persons, while the variety play was prepared for one person**)**).
:::

::: definition Hongniang: winning inside the other side's language {#def-hongniang}
**The structure of *Kao Hong* 拷红 ('Interrogating Hongniang')** (**the old lady interrogates Hongniang — Hongniang's reply does not deny the facts, but turns the master's whole system of values round and points the gun the other way (the gist of the common account: the credit you want and the façade you want were already in contradiction with each other, and now both will be destroyed together because you have broken faith**)**); **a breakdown of the method** (**① do not argue the facts (the facts have already collapsed); ② do not use our side's language (words of the sort "free love" do not exist); ③ use only the other side's highest values (good faith, the family gate, respectability) to derive the other side's self-contradiction — **this is the most complete performance in drama of piercing a shield with that shield's own spear (the joint assignment of Lesson 6's steel-man test and Lesson 4's reduction to absurdity: **Hongniang is one of the strongest amateur advocates in the whole history of literature, because she never proposes a new value; she only requires the old lady's words and deeds to agree**); ④ **and give them a step down** (**the gist of the common account: to grant it is seemly; to refuse it makes the family gate still uglier — **working out the account for the other side is the last kilometre of persuasion (Lesson 6's clearing of the ground: leave the opponent with only one seemly action, which is to sign**)**).
:::

## The Long Pavilion: writing a parting as a breach of contract {#def-changting}

::: definition 【Duanzhenghao】 and 【Yisha】 {#def-duanzhenghao}
"**碧云天，黄花地，西风紧，北雁南飞。晓来谁染霜林醉？总是离人泪。**" ("Blue-cloud sky, yellow-flower ground, the west wind keen, northern geese flying south. At dawn, who has dyed the frosted wood drunk? It is all the tears of people parting." (Our translation.)) (a quotation in the common account) — **the first four lines are a list of nouns (sky, ground, wind, geese); the fifth line suddenly interrogates a verb ("dye"); the sixth line gives the verdict (tears); **the "*yāomiǎo*" 要眇 ('subtle and remote') of the Song song lyric (*ci*) is taken apart into a problem in reasoning: first set out the physical evidence, then identify the real culprit (the confluence, inside the words of a *qu*, of Chapter 3's study of evidence and Chapter 7's "digging out the mechanism": **a *qu* has to be understood by ear, so its expression of feeling must carry the scaffolding of an argument**); "**夕阳古道无人语，禾黍秋风听马嘶**" ("On the old road in the evening sun, no one speaks; amid millet and the autumn wind, one hears a horse neigh." (Our translation.)) (a quotation in the common account) — **both lines are an absence of hearing, and something that comes uninvited: **the horse's neigh is the sound effect of leaving; no one speaking is the volume of what stays (the Yuan *qu* version of Lesson 12's "writing stillness by means of sound"; also of the same family as Chapter 2's "the camera turns to the window" — **here there is not even anyone looking out of the window**); "**蜗角虚名，蝇头微利，拆鸳鸯在两下里**" ("Empty fame on a snail's horn, trifling profit on a fly's head, and the mandarin ducks are split onto two sides." (Our translation.)) (a line quoted in the common account) — **it writes rank and fame from the examinations as the direct cause of the parting: **how narrow the scholar's road was in the Yuan is not written in the arguments of the dialogue; it is written in the physical cause of the plot (a negative example of Chapter 8's "the pretext is not the thesis": here the pretext is the system itself**).
:::

::: definition The problem of Zhang Sheng {#def-zhangsheng}
**A history of bad reviews for the male lead** (**in Ming–Qing commentary and in modern criticism, Zhang Sheng has long been called "weak" and "rash", and the business of his "jumping the wall" has in particular become a laughing-stock (the common account) — **this is not the audience being harsh; it is the after-effect of an unequal allotment of the right to lyric utterance: Yingying has an inner monologue, and Zhang Sheng has only entreaty and a loss of composure: **if a character cannot sing his own shame, his shame can only be seen by other people (the final settlement of Chapter 1's "whoever has the sung words = whoever has the inner life"**)**); **the other half of the defence of the "man who restrains himself"** (**Zhang Sheng's speech of defence in *Huizhen ji* 会真记 ('Record of Meeting a True One') is cut away in large part in *The Story of the Western Wing*: **to delete the defence is to withdraw his qualification to reopen the case for himself — a change of version is a change of verdict (an extreme case of Chapter 9's "neighbours of the quotation": **to delete a stretch of speech from the book is more thorough than writing a thousand characters of rebuttal**)**).
:::

## Commentary and publishing: a super-IP {#def-pingdian}

::: definition Jin Shengtan's *The Sixth Book of Genius* {#def-jinshengtan}
**Cutting the continuation** (**Jin Shengtan's commented edition breaks off at the one act *Kao Yan* 拷艳 ('interrogating the amorous affair'), and does not take the later line of "the startled dream" and "the reunion" (the common account: the Jin edition comments up to after "interrogating Hongniang" and then stops, and denounces the brush that continues) — **delete the reunion, and a comedy is changed into an unfinished verdict: **the commentator exercises an author's rights for the first time (the reverse operation of Chapter 1's "reunion = a judicial pronouncement": **no written judgement is given; only the case file is left**)**); **how to read** (**the several entries of "how to read" at the head of his volume treat reading a play as reading prose (the common account) — **criticism is raised from prefaces and colophons to the skeleton of a book: **a play text comes with a set of instructions for reading; the publishing trade of the late Ming had already made "how to read" into a selling point (the home ground of Chapter 11's critical genres is rehearsed here; the mirror image of Lesson 10's project of the paragraph-opening sentence: **Jin Shengtan wrote six paragraph-opening sentences for the reader**)**); **the IP ecology** (**printed editions, illustrations, commentaries, reversals of the case, and continuations of *The Western Wing* in the Ming and Qing are extremely numerous (the thread of the common account) — **it is not "one work"; it is a universe that can be licensed: **the audience knows it so well that they need look only at whether the details are new (the burden of "new" from then on presses on wording and manner, not on the plot: **the aesthetic price of a super-IP is that its readers already know the ending, and so the whole tension of the text is shifted onto "how it is said" (this is one reason *The Western Wing* became "the sage among *qu*": it has been heard until it is too familiar, and can win only by its language (the Ming-market version of Lesson 15's "familiarity and defamiliarisation"**)**).
:::

::: definition Banned and not banned {#def-jinbu}
**"非圣之书"** ("a book not of the sages") (**through the Ming and Qing there were repeatedly proposals to ban and destroy it, yet the bookshop printings did not decline (the thread of the common account) — **the effect of a ban is a label, not a sluice-gate: a list of banned books is the most effective list of recommendations (the ancient reverse use of Chapter 8's "the item you most want to forward is the first to be autopsied": **the book you most want to ban is the first to sell**)**); **one and the same coin, "诲淫" and "言情"** ("inciting to lust" and "speaking of feeling") (**what the moralists of the Way see is inducement; what the readers see is authorisation — **a work's power is confirmed by the two ways of reading together (the study of sources for Lesson 15's "reader-response criticism": **the materials of the history of criticism are often the materials of the history of censorship, and the number of bans is the best count of influence**)**).
:::

::: example The rhetorical account of Interrogating Hongniang {#ex-kaohong}
Rewrite Hongniang's reply as a modern written plea (150 characters), with the addressee changed to a "college disciplinary committee" (a student pleading for a fellow student who has been disciplined). The requirements: **use only the college's own three values (academic integrity, the school's reputation, responsibility towards students), propose no new value, and at the end give the other side two options (one of which must damage its reputation)**.
::: solution
**A model plea** (“**委员会最重三条：诚信、校誉、责任。其一，本次记录的取得过程未经本人确认；若以不诚信的手段惩罚所谓不诚信，本院将先失去诚信这一前提。其二，此事已在校外流传，唯一致歉的处理是公布完整的认定标准与申诉结果——继续保密只会被解读为选择性执法。其三，对学生的责任，意味着我们不能让一个学生的成本去替制度的缺口付账。故请择一：公布全过程，或书面说明标准并允许复议；二者皆拒，本申辩将默认校方选择了第三种：以声誉为由牺牲声誉。**” "The committee holds three things most important: integrity, the school's reputation, and responsibility. First, the process by which this record was obtained has not been confirmed by the person concerned; if a so-called lack of integrity is punished by means that lack integrity, this college will first lose integrity, which is its premise. Second, the matter has already circulated outside the school; the only handling that amounts to an apology is to publish the complete criteria of the finding and the result of the appeal — to go on keeping it secret will only be read as selective enforcement. Third, responsibility towards students means we cannot let one student's costs pay the bill for a gap in the system. Therefore please choose one: publish the whole process, or state the criteria in writing and allow a reconsideration; if both are refused, this plea will take it that the school has chosen a third: sacrificing reputation on the grounds of reputation." (Our translation.)); **marking** — **all three values must come from the other side** (**to introduce a new value such as "fairness" or "youth" is a breach: **the reason Hongniang cannot be rebutted is that every word she says is a word of the old lady's**); **the ending must be two options plus one self-destroying consequence** ("**sacrificing reputation on the grounds of reputation" = the modern grammar of the old lady's "the family gate grows still uglier" (the clearing-the-ground clause of Lesson 6**)); **extra credit** (**someone writes in "Hongniang's position": she is pleading on someone else's behalf, and therefore she is not deterred by that discipline — **the speaker's position is also part of the argument (Chapter 8's four questions on the source: the fact that her interest is not involved is the load-bearing wall of this written plea**)**).
:::
:::

::: intuition One play's gender account {#int-xingbie}
**Yingying's three hesitations** ("**闹简**", "**赖简**" and that run of scenes ('the row over the letter', 'repudiating the letter'): she hands over a love poem and then turns her face (the plot in the common account) — **often read as "affectation"; this lesson reads it as a situation of information: **she cannot speak (if she speaks, she proves her own loss of virtue); he need not speak (his pursuit is, under the system, exempt from inspection): **one and the same hesitation costs, under the two systems, amounts that differ by a factor of two, and so this play understands asymmetry better than its age does (the gendered version of Chapter 8's "the cost of a mistake"**)**); **Hongniang cannot be replaced** (**she is the only person who can speak, because her status is low enough that she is not fully bound by the reputation of the family gate — **the right to speak sometimes comes from not being qualified enough (a supplementary clause to Chapter 1's "who has the sung words": **sometimes the rank of a role-type gives some role-type an exception: the *jing* may tell the truth, and a maid may talk reason**)**).
:::

::: history From a speech in defence to a clause {#sec-tiaokuan}
**800s** *The Tale of Yingying* (**Tang: a breach of faith is written as cultivation**); **1100s–1200s** a retrial inside Song and Jin storytelling-and-singing (**the thread of the common account: the various storytelling-and-singing forms compete to adapt it; this lesson takes only the line of Dong Jieyuan: **performers begin to retry this case on Yingying's behalf**); **around the 1300s** Wang Shifu's *The Story of the Western Wing* (**"**愿普天下有情的都成了眷属**" enters the play ("May all everywhere under heaven who have feeling be joined as spouses." (Our translation.)): **from winning the suit to legislating**); **1500s–1600s** the age of printed editions and commentary (***The Western Wing* becomes a classic at home both on the desk and on the stage (the common account): **a play is read as a model for the study of prose: a volume binding together the Kunqu stage and the booksellers**); **1640s–1660s** Jin Shengtan's commented edition (**cutting the continuation, and "how to read": **the critic's signature enters the title of the work**); **1700s–1800s** banned again and again, and block-printed again and again (**the count of bans = the curve of influence**); **1900s–now** from *The Western Wing* to textbooks and adapted plays (**the separate fates of *Ting Qin* 听琴 ('Listening to the Qin') and *Kao Hong* ('Interrogating Hongniang'): **anthologies rewrite the emphasis once more: it is split into three shares, lyric feeling, humour and criticism, and handed to three kinds of reader (the hundred-year case of Chapter 7's "to select with care is to cut and alter"**)).
:::

::: warning Four pitfalls of the Western Wing
**The belated verdict of "anti-feudal"** (**treat the old lady as a feudal symbol, and this play becomes a narrative of victory: **the old lady is the only person in the whole play who is taking responsibility for her child (her failure is a failure of information and a failure of credit, not a class crime (Lesson 6's steel man: to write the opponent as the system is a cheap saving of labour**)**); **reading "愿天下有情的都成了眷属" ("May all under heaven who have feeling be joined as spouses." (Our translation.)) as a declaration of free love** (**it is a wish, not a bill of rights: **the variety play cannot give an institutional scheme for the freedom to decide one's own marriage; it gives only one line: to take lyric expression as a programme is a political task that later anthologies added for it (the old version of Chapter 7's "the exchange rate of sublimation"**)**); **ignoring Zhang Sheng's bad reviews** (**praising only the splendour of the diction and not asking why the male lead is disliked: **a character's being disagreeable is often the consequence of the system's allotting the right to lyric utterance (Chapter 1's acoustics**)**); **taking the Jin edition for "the original face"** (**a commented edition is an adapted edition: **the *Western Wing* we quote is often the *Western Wing* that Jin Shengtan has cut: the edition has to be stated, just as a Yuan variety play has to have its Ming printing stated (a renewal of the subscription to Lesson 1's method of editions**)**).

::: exercise A verdict on the versions {level=1}
Compare Zhang Sheng's speech of defence in *The Tale of Yingying* with the handling in *The Story of the Western Wing* (the speech is deleted, and Zhang Sheng is written as a man of infatuation). Write 120 characters: **who has had the qualification to speak withdrawn? Is this progress, or another kind of imbalance?**
::: solution
Marking: **do not answer only "progress"** (**you must point out Wang Shifu's price: **he gave Yingying an inner life, but wrote Zhang Sheng as a person who can only entreat: redistributing the right to lyric utterance at the same time produces a new flat character (the bill for Chapter 1's system of a single lead singer**)**); **the difference in function between "infatuation" and "defence"** (**a speech of defence is evidence of complicity with the system; infatuated feeling is the result of an exemption by the system**).
:::

::: exercise Use only the other side's words {level=2}
On any issue that is disputed over and over, write a plea of 120 characters, under a hard constraint: **value-words of one's own side are forbidden** ("fairness", "freedom", "understanding" and the like may not appear at all; only the other side's word-list may be used).
::: solution
Marking: **if a forbidden word appears, rewrite** (**the aim of this exercise: to let you feel the creativity of a moment when rhetorical resources have been confiscated: **Hongniang's strength is precisely the strength of having been stripped of a vocabulary of her own (the historical reason for Lesson 7's "the power of a limit"**)**); **the structure of an effective plea** (**a concession on the facts → a reductio on the values → two options: of the three sections, not one may be missing**).

::: exercise Short essay: the legislation of feeling {level=3}
Write from six hundred to one thousand characters, assessing "**把一种感受写成一项资格，是文学能对制度做的最有力的事：它不请求宽恕，它修改分类表——‘有情’这个词进了戏，从此婚姻的理由多了一栏，而这一栏既无法验收也无法取消，因为它的存在形式是一句人人都记得的台词**" ("To write a kind of feeling as a qualification is the most powerful thing literature can do to an institution: it does not ask for pardon; it revises the table of categories — the word 'having feeling' enters the play, and from then on the reasons for a marriage have one column more, and this column can neither be signed off nor cancelled, because the form in which it exists is a line that everyone remembers." (Our translation.)). You must call on at least six items: **three versions and three juries**, **divided singing and the redistribution of inner lives**, **Hongniang's spear and shield**, **the lyric scaffolding of 【端正好】**, **Jin Shengtan's cutting of the continuation**, **the counting method of banning and not banning**; and give a verdict: **whether a memorable line can change the institution of marriage more than an enforceable law can — you must answer in the two columns "what it brought" and "what it failed to stop"**.
::: solution
Points for marking: six mechanisms (**juries, acoustics, reductio, scaffolding, cutting the continuation, the count of censorship**); the verdict (**the side of the line** — what it changes is the default reason ("feeling" enters the vocabulary of legitimacy), and it cannot be revoked and needs no budget; **the side of the law** — what it changes is justiciability (who is in breach, who pays compensation), but **there is no column which says that a lawsuit of "having feeling" can be filed: literature supplies reasons, and does not supply procedure (both columns must be filled in: what it failed to stop — seduction at the start and abandonment at the end still happens, the double standard goes on as before, "hesitation" continues to be convicted by a new discourse ("having feeling" becomes a new compulsion: not being ardent enough is also blamed — marking looks at whether you have written "a new category also grows new people who fail to qualify"**)); if the conclusion can say "**the line changes the table of categories; the law changes the burden of proof; only when both are changed at the same time can the people in the story really marry — and *The Western Wing* changed only the table of categories, so its reunion forever carries a little of the smell of having been begged for**" ("The line changes the table of categories; the law changes the burden of proof; only when both are changed at the same time can the people in the story really marry — and *The Western Wing* changed only the table of categories, so its reunion forever carries a little of the smell of having been begged for." (Our translation.)), that is an A.
