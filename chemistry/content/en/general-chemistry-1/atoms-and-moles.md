## Atomic structure and isotopes
::: definition {#atomsandmoles-def-1}
An element is defined by its proton number; isotopes of an element differ only in neutron number. Relative atomic masses are weighted averages of isotopic masses, which is why chlorine's atomic mass sits near 35.45.
:::
## Molar mass and counting
::: definition {#atomsandmoles-def-2}
The mole is the counting unit of chemistry. Since the 2019 SI revision, one mole contains exactly 6.02214076 x 10^23 specified entities, the fixed numerical value of the Avogadro constant.
:::
## Formula units
::: definition {#atomsandmoles-def-3}
Molar mass in g/mol equals atomic or formula mass in daltons, so the balance becomes an atom counter. Formula mass applies to ionic compounds, whose smallest representative unit is the formula unit.
:::

::: summary
- Elements are defined by proton number; isotopes differ in neutrons.
- Relative atomic masses are abundance-weighted averages.
- One mole contains exactly 6.02214076 x 10^23 entities.
- Molar mass bridges the macroscopic and molecular scales.
- Formula units apply to ionic compounds.
:::
::: history
Avogadro proposed in 1811 that equal gas volumes contain equal particle numbers; Perrin named the constant after him in 1909. The 2019 SI revision fixed the Avogadro constant exactly.
