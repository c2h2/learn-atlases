下面是三个结构相同的汉语句子，都是“如果……就……”：

- 如果明天下雨，我们就不去了。
- 如果我有钱，我就买一辆车。
- 如果你昨天来了，你就见到她了。

在汉语中，动词的形式不变：条件是可能的、不太可能的还是不可能的，留给上下文以及“明天”“昨天”“早就”这类词来表示。在英语中，这三句话需要三种不同的动词形式：

- *If it **rains** tomorrow, we **won't go**.*（真实的可能性）
- *If I **had** money, I **would buy** a car.*（我没有钱：非真实的现在）
- *If you **had come** yesterday, you **would have seen** her.*（你没有来：非真实的过去）

英语条件句的时态同时承担两项任务：它把条件放在时间之中，同时告诉听话人说话人认为这个条件是真实的还是非真实的。只看到第一项任务的学习者会说 ✗ *If I have money, I will buy a car*（听起来好像钱随时都可能到手），或者 ✗ *If you came yesterday, you saw her*（意思完全不同）。这一课从整体上讲解这个体系：真实条件和非真实条件，标记非真实的时态“后退一步”，混合条件句，*if* 的替代说法，以及书面英语中的正式条件句和隐含条件句。它以 [[complex-sentences/adverbial-clauses]] 中的状语从句为基础；愿望和其他非真实形式见下一课 [[complex-sentences/unreal-past]]。

## 真实条件和非真实条件

::: definition 真实条件句和非真实条件句 {#def-conditional}
**条件句**（conditional sentence）有一个**条件**（*if* 分句，也叫前件，protasis）和一个**结果**（主句，也叫后件，apodosis）。

- 在**真实**（或**开放**）条件句中，说话人对条件现在或将来是否为真不作判断：*If it rains tomorrow, we'll stay at home.* *If you heated the milk, it's probably still warm.*
- 在**非真实**条件句（也叫**远离现实的**、**假设的**条件句，涉及过去时叫**反事实**条件句）中，说话人把条件表述为假的、不太可能的或仅仅是想象的：*If I were taller, I'd play basketball.* *If we had left earlier, we'd have caught the train.*
:::

课堂教材常常给主要的句型编号。这些编号是有用的标签，但只涵盖了英语实际用法的一部分：

| 标签 | *if* 分句 | 主句 | 意义 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| “零类” | 现在时 | 现在时 | 普遍真理或习惯 | *If you **heat** ice, it **melts**.* |
| “第一类” | 现在时 | *will* / 情态动词 / 祈使句 | 真实的将来可能性 | *If it **rains**, we'**ll stay** in.* |
| “第二类” | 过去时 | *would* / *could* / *might* + 动词 | 现在或将来非真实或不太可能 | *If I **won** the lottery, I'**d travel**.* |
| “第三类” | 过去完成时 | *would* / *could* / *might* **have** + 分词 | 非真实的过去（反事实） | *If she **had asked**, I'**d have helped**.* |

::: theorem 真实条件句 {#rule-real}
在真实条件句中，每个分句都采用它自己的时间和意义所要求的时态，与其他任何句子一样：

1. **将来**：*if* 分句用现在时，主句用 *will*、情态动词或祈使句：*If you **need** help, **call** me.* *If the price **goes** up, we **might** cancel.*
2. **普遍真理和习惯**：两个分句都用现在时（*if* 接近于 *when* 或 *whenever*）：*If I **drink** coffee after six, I **can't** sleep.*
3. **过去和现在**：任何合乎情理的组合：*If she **was** at the meeting, she **knows** about the plan.* *If you **didn't** get the email, it **went** to the wrong address.* *If he**'s** been working all night, he**'ll** be tired.*

*if* 分句中在两种情况下用 *will*：当它表示**意愿**或礼貌的请求时（*If you**'ll** wait here, I'll fetch the manager.* *If you **will** insist on driving fast, …* =“如果你非要……”），以及当 *if* 分句是主句的**结果**而不是原因时：*If it **will** help you, I'll lend you my notes*（我借笔记在先，对你有帮助在后）。
:::

::: proof
夸克等（Quirk et al.，1985）、赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆（Huddleston and Pullum，2002）以及斯旺（Swan，2016）一致认为，真实条件句没有特殊的时态格式：指将来时“*if* 分句用现在时”的规则与时间状语从句的规则相同（[[complex-sentences/adverbial-clauses#rule-time-tense]]），而规则3中的组合在实际会话中和教科书上的类型一样常见。真实的语篇中有许多像规则3那样不属于任何编号类型的 *if* 句，所以应当把编号看作一个起点，而不是对这个体系的描写。规则1最后一段中 *will* 的两种用法在三部语法中都有描述；在这两种用法之外，✗ *If it will rain tomorrow* 是错误的。
:::

::: theorem 非真实条件句：后退一步 {#rule-unreal}
要使条件变成非真实的，英语把动词的时态**后退一步**：

1. **非真实的现在或将来**：*if* 分句用过去时；主句用 *would*（或 *could*、*might*）+ 不带 to 的不定式。*If I **knew** her number, I **would call** her.*（我不知道。）*If you **left** now, you **could** still **catch** the train.*（你不太可能现在走。）
2. **非真实的过去**：*if* 分句用过去完成时；主句用 *would*（*could*、*might*）+ ***have*** + 过去分词。*If I **had known** her number, I **would have called** her.*（我当时不知道。）
3. 在非真实的现在中，***were*** 可以用于所有人称：*If I **were** you, …* *If she **were** here, …* 在正式书面语中以及在固定短语 *If I were you* 中，*were* 是标准的选择；在非正式英语中，***was*** 很常见，也可以接受：*If she **was** here, she'd know what to do.*
4. ***Would*** 通常**不**出现在 *if* 分句中：✗ *If I would know her number…* ✗ *If I would have known…*
:::

::: proof
这里的过去时并不指过去的时间：*If I knew her number* 说的是现在。赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆（2002）称之为**情态过去式**（modal preterite），斯旺（2016）称之为**表示非真实情况的过去时**：过去形式表示与现实的距离，正如它在普通用法中表示与现在的距离，在礼貌请求中表示与听话人的距离（*I wondered if you could help*）。非真实的过去需要用过去完成时，因为一般过去时已经被占用了。关于 *were*，赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆称之为**非现实 *were***（irrealis *were*），并指出除正式文体以外，*was* 越来越多地取代了它；斯旺也给出了同样的语域区别。*if* 分句中的 *would*（*If I would have known*）在非正式的美式口语中很常见，但在大西洋两岸的标准书面语中都不被接受（Swan 2016）；例外是表示礼貌的 *would*（*If you **would** come this way…* =“如果您愿意”）和 *would like*（*If you **would like** a copy, …*）。
:::

::: example 真实还是非真实？ {#ex-real-unreal}
每句话告诉你说话人对事实持什么看法？(a) *If he's at home, he'll answer the phone.* (b) *If he were at home, he'd answer the phone.* (c) *If he was at home, he didn't answer the phone.* (d) *If he had been at home, he'd have answered the phone.*
::: solution
(a) 真实：说话人不知道他在不在家。如果在，他会接电话。

(b) 非真实的现在：说话人认为他**不**在家（也许是因为没人接电话）。

(c) 真实的过去：说话人不知道他当时在不在家；如果在，那么他是选择了不接（或者没听见）。注意这里的过去时是真实的过去，而不是后退一步。

(d) 非真实的过去：他在那个时候不在家；所以他没有接电话。

(b) 和 (c) 的 *if* 分句里都有过去时，但只有 (b) 有 *would*：主句显示了要表达的是哪种理解。
:::
:::

::: warning *if* 分句中的 *will* 和 *would*
✗ *If it will be sunny tomorrow, we'll go to the beach.* ✓ *If it **is** sunny tomorrow, we'll go to the beach.*

✗ *If I would have more time, I would learn Japanese.* ✓ *If I **had** more time, I would learn Japanese.*

✗ *If I would have known, I would have come.* ✓ *If I **had known**, I would have come.*

*will*/*would* 放在主句里。学习者常常在两个分句里都用它，因为两个分句都指将来或者想象的情况；在英语中，*if* 本身已经带有这层意思。
:::

::: quiz
哪个句子表示说话人**没有**学医？
- [ ] *If I studied medicine, I'll become a doctor.*
- [ ] *If I study medicine, I'll become a doctor.*
- [x] *If I had studied medicine, I'd be a doctor now.*
- [ ] *If I studied medicine, I would become a doctor.*
::: solution
*Had studied* 是非真实的过去：说话人没有学医，因此现在不是医生。第二句对将来不作判断；第四句想象一个不太可能的将来（说话人大概不会去学医，但并不一定是已经错过了）。第一句把真实的过去和将来混在一起，说不通。
:::
:::

## 混合条件句

*if* 分句和主句不一定指同一时间。如果它们指的时间不同，时态就是**混合的**：每个分句保持适合它自己的时间的形式。

::: theorem 混合条件句 {#rule-mixed}
1. **非真实的过去条件，非真实的现在结果**：过去完成时 + *would* + 不带 to 的不定式。*If I **had taken** the job in Shenzhen, I **would be** rich now.*（我没有接受那份工作；我现在不富有。）
2. **非真实的现在条件，非真实的过去结果**：过去时 + *would have* + 分词。*If I **spoke** French, I **would have understood** the film last night.*（我不会说法语——这是一个长期的事实——所以我没看懂。）
3. 真实条件句也可以自由地混合时间，如 [[#rule-real]] 所示：*If you **missed** the lecture, you **can** borrow my notes.*
:::

::: proof
所有主要的语法书都描述了混合条件句（Quirk et al. 1985；Swan 2016）；它们表明，英语条件句不是固定的公式，而是两条规则的组合——表示非真实的后退一步，以及每个分句按照它自己的时间使用普通时态。句型1比句型2常见得多，因为对过去决定及其现在结果的后悔是一个常见的话题。在句型2中，*if* 分句通常描述一种长期的状态（*If I were taller*、*If she weren't so shy*）。
:::

::: example 构成混合条件句 {#ex-mixed}
为每种情况写一个条件句。(a) *I didn't buy a ticket. Now I can't go to the concert.* (b) *She isn't careful. She made a lot of mistakes in the test.* (c) *We didn't book a table. We're standing in a queue now.* (d) *He's very tall. He didn't fit in the small car.*
::: solution
1. 确定每个分句的时间：条件是过去的还是现在的？结果呢？
2. 把每个非真实的分句后退一步：现在 → 过去（主句用 *would*），过去 → 过去完成（主句用 *would have*）。

(a) 过去的条件，现在的结果：*If I **had bought** a ticket, I **could go** to the concert.*

(b) 现在的条件（一种长期的品质），过去的结果：*If she **were** more careful, she **wouldn't have made** so many mistakes in the test.*

(c) 过去的条件，现在的结果：*If we **had booked** a table, we **wouldn't be standing** in a queue now.*

(d) 现在的条件，过去的结果：*If he **weren't** so tall, he **would have fitted** in the small car.*（在非正式英语中也可以用 *wasn't*。）
:::
:::

## 表达条件的其他方式

::: theorem 表示条件的连词 {#rule-conjunctions}
1. ***Unless*** 的意思大致是“如果不”或“除非”：*We'll eat outside **unless** it rains.*（= 如果不下雨）。当否定的条件是结果的**原因**时，尤其是涉及感情时，用 *if … not*，而不用 *unless*：*I'll be upset **if** she **doesn't** come*（✗ *unless she comes*）；在非真实的过去条件句中通常也是如此：*If you **hadn't** helped me, I would have failed*（这里很少用 *unless*）。
2. ***Provided (that)***、***providing (that)***、***as long as*** 和 ***on condition that*** 强调条件是必要的：*You can borrow the car **as long as** you're careful.* *On condition that* 是正式的，常用于协议中。
3. ***Only if*** 表示这是唯一的条件；***even if*** 表示无论条件是否满足，结果都成立（[[complex-sentences/adverbial-clauses#rule-concession]]）。*only if* 位于句首时，主句要倒装：***Only if** all members agree **can** the rule be changed*（[[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]]）。
4. ***Suppose***、***supposing*** 和 ***what if*** 引出一个想象的情况，用真实或非真实的时态：***Suppose** you **lost** your passport — what would you do?* ***What if** she **says** no?*
5. ***Otherwise***（副词）和 ***or***（并列连词）表示条件**不**满足时的结果：*Hurry up, **otherwise** / **or** we'll miss the bus.*
6. ***But for*** + 名词、***without*** + 名词、***if it weren't for*** 和 ***if it hadn't been for*** 用名词短语表示非真实条件：***If it hadn't been for** your help, I would have failed.* ***But for** your help, …*（正式）***Without** your help, …*
:::

*unless* 和 *if not* 之间的关系是一个很好的逻辑实例。在下图中，设 *p* 为“你赶紧”，*q* 为“你会错过公共汽车”。

::: widget truthtable

compare: p | q
caption: *You'll miss the bus unless you hurry*——也就是 *if you don't hurry, you'll miss the bus*（¬p → q）。比较结果显示，这在逻辑上与 *you hurry or you miss the bus*（p ∨ q）相同：两者只在你没有赶紧却也没有错过公共汽车的那一行为假。在会话中，听话人通常还会理解为，如果你**确实**赶紧了，就不会错过——这是一个额外的推断，不属于逻辑本身。试着用 *p xor q* 作比较，看看这种更强的理解。

::: example 用 *unless*、*if* 还是 *in case*？ {#ex-unless}
选择最合适的词并说明理由。(a) *I won't sign the contract (unless / if) my lawyer reads it first.* (b) *I'd be surprised (unless / if) he didn't win.* (c) *Write down my number (in case / if) you need it.* (d) *You can't come in (unless / in case) you have a ticket.* (e) *(Unless / If) you hadn't reminded me, I'd have forgotten.*
::: solution
(a) *unless*——“除非我的律师先看过”。（用 *if* 会使律师看合同成为不签字的原因，说不通。）

(b) *if*——*didn't win* 是感到意外的原因；*unless he won* 的意思正好相反。

(c) *in case*——预防：现在就记下来，因为以后可能需要。

(d) *unless*——“除非你有票”。

(e) *If*——带否定原因的非真实过去；*unless* 极少用于非真实的过去条件。
:::
:::

## 正式条件句和倒装

有三种形式是正式英语——公函、说明书、学术和法律文书——的典型形式：

1. *if* 分句中的 ***should*** 使条件显得可能性较小，或者只是更礼貌：*If you **should** need any help, please contact me.*
2. ***were to*** + 不定式把将来的条件表述为假设的：*If the government **were to** raise taxes, prices would rise.*
3. **不用 *if* 的倒装**：助动词放在主语之前。
   - *Should you need any help, please contact me.*
   - *Were the government to raise taxes, prices would rise.* *Were I in your position, I would resign.*
   - *Had I known about the problem, I would have acted.*

在否定的倒装条件句中，*not* 放在主语之后，并且不缩写：***Had*** *I* ***not*** *seen it myself, …*（✗ *Hadn't I seen it myself*）。在现代英语中，只有 *should*、*were* 和 *had* 能这样倒装；✗ *Did I know her number, I would call* 是不可能的。

::: proof
夸克等（1985）、赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆（2002）以及斯旺（2016）都把条件倒装描述为正式用法，并且只限于这三个助动词。比伯等（Biber et al.，1999）发现，倒装条件句在会话中很少见，集中在书面语域中，尤其是小说和学术文章。*Should you need* 在商务信函和电子邮件中很常见，在那里它显得礼貌而不是生硬。
:::

## 隐含条件和其他条件意义

条件常常不用 *if* 也存在：

- **在上下文中**：*I **wouldn't** do that.*（= 如果我是你）*It **would** be nice to see you.*（= 如果你来的话）
- **在短语中**：***With** a bit more practice, you **could** win.* ***In** your place, I'd refuse.* ***Without** a visa, you **can't** enter.*
- **在祈使句 + *and* / *or* 中**：***Press** this button **and** the door opens.*（= 如果你按……）***Hurry up, or** we'll be late.*（= 如果你不赶紧……）

::: example 把条件说明白 {#ex-implied}
把每个句子改写成带 *if* 的完整条件句。(a) *Without a passport, you can't board the plane.* (b) *Leave now and you'll catch the last bus.* (c) *I wouldn't trust him.* (d) *Be careful, or you'll cut yourself.* (e) *But for the rain, the harvest would have failed.*
::: solution
1. 找出隐藏的条件：一个短语（*without*、*but for*）、一个祈使句，或者上下文。
2. 判断它是真实的还是非真实的，并选择时态。

(a) *If you don't have a passport, you can't board the plane.*——真实。

(b) *If you leave now, you'll catch the last bus.*——真实；祈使句 + *and* = *if*。

(c) *If I were you, I wouldn't trust him.*——条件在上下文中：非真实的现在。

(d) *If you aren't careful, you'll cut yourself.*——祈使句 + *or* = *if … not*。

(e) *If it hadn't rained (If it hadn't been for the rain), the harvest would have failed.*——非真实的过去。
:::
:::

::: quiz
哪个倒装条件句是正确的？
- [ ] *Hadn't we left early, we would have missed the train.*
- [ ] *Did I know the answer, I would tell you.*
- [x] *Were it not for the subsidies, the farm would close.*
- [ ] *Should you will need a taxi, ask at reception.*
::: solution
在条件句中只有 *should*、*were* 和 *had* 能倒装，而且 *not* 放在主语之后，不缩写：*Had we not left early…*。*Were it not for* 是 *If it weren't for* 的正式说法。*Should* 后面跟不带 to 的不定式：*Should you need a taxi…*
:::
:::

有些 *if* 句根本与因果无关。在 *If you're hungry, there's some soup in the fridge* 中，不管你饿不饿，汤都在那里：*if* 分句给出的是这条信息**相关**的条件。这类句子常被称为**言语行为**条件句（或相关性条件句，speech-act / relevance conditional）。另一些是礼貌的套语：*If I could just say something…*，*If you don't mind me asking, …*，*If I remember rightly, …*。

::: note 英汉对比
- **时态与上下文。** 汉语用“如果”“要是”“假如”或“假设”构成的条件句（条件末尾常加“的话”，结果中常用“就”）不通过改变动词来标记非真实。李讷和汤普森（Li and Thompson，1981）指出，假设或反事实的理解来自上下文。英语语法迫使说话人作出选择：真实还是非真实，现在还是过去。学习者的任务是在写动词之前先问自己：“我相信这个条件是真的吗？”
- **汉语中的反事实信号。** 汉语确实有一些指向非真实过去的词：“要不是”（‘if it hadn't been for’），“早知道”（‘if I had known’），以及结果中的“本来”和“早就”。“要不是你提醒我，我早就忘了”就是 *If you hadn't reminded me, I'd have forgotten long ago*；“早知道这样，我就不来了”就是 *If I'd known it would be like this, I wouldn't have come.* 这些词是提示使用英语过去完成时 + *would have* 的好信号。
- **不加标记的条件。** 汉语常常完全不用连词来表达条件（意合）：你不去我也不去——*If you don't go, I won't go either*；下雨就别来了——*Don't come if it rains.* 英语句子需要 *if*、*unless*、*as long as*，或者祈使句 + *and*/*or*。
- **关于反事实的争论。** 1981年，心理学家阿尔弗雷德·布卢姆（Alfred Bloom）在《思维的语言塑造》（*The Linguistic Shaping of Thought*）中提出，由于汉语缺乏语法上的反事实形式，说汉语的人进行反事实推理更困难。Terry Au（1983）用翻译得更好的材料重复了他的实验，没有发现这种差异，后来的研究也支持她的结论。汉语缺乏一种语法形式，这是关于语法的事实，而不是关于说汉语的人怎样思维的事实：这种意义是通过其他手段表达的。
:::

::: history
自古以来，逻辑学家就对条件句感兴趣，语言学家对它的兴趣则较晚。约瑟夫·格林伯格（Joseph Greenberg）的语言共性清单（1963）中有一条观察：在所有语言的条件陈述中，条件通常位于结论之前，正如在大多数英语 *if* 句中一样。1971年，迈克尔·盖斯（Michael Geis）和阿诺德·兹威基（Arnold Zwicky）描述了“诱导推理”（invited inference），即听话人把 *If you mow the lawn, I'll give you five dollars* 理解为暗含 *If you don't, I won't*——也就是上面真值表图中那种额外的理解。伊丽莎白·特劳戈特（Elizabeth Traugott）等人主编的文集《论条件句》（*On Conditionals*，1986）汇集了类型学、逻辑学和历史方面的研究，其中包括伯纳德·科姆里（Bernard Comrie）关于各种语言怎样标记假设条件的综述。“第一类”“第二类”和“第三类”条件句这些教学标签在20世纪下半叶成为英语教学中的标准说法；主要的参考语法不使用它们，而是用真实条件和非真实条件来描写这个体系。
:::

## 后续内容

时态后退一步的现象也出现在 *wish*、*if only*、*it's time* 和 *would rather* 之后：见 [[complex-sentences/unreal-past]]。*only if*、*not until* 之后以及正式条件句中的倒装见 [[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]]。结果分句中的情态动词（*might have*、*could have*、*should have*）在 [[modality/past-modals]] 中学习，论证中 *if* 的逻辑在 [[essay-writing/arguing-a-point]] 中学习。

::: summary
- **真实**条件句对条件是否为真不作判断；**非真实**条件句把条件表述为假的、不太可能的或想象的（[[#def-conditional]]）。
- 真实条件句使用各自时间所要求的时态；指将来时，*if* 分句用现在时，主句用 *will*（[[#rule-real]]）。
- 非真实条件句把时态**后退一步**：指现在或将来用过去时 + *would*，指过去用过去完成时 + *would have*；在正式英语中所有人称都用 *were*（[[#rule-unreal]]）。
- *Will* 和 *would* 通常属于主句，而不属于 *if* 分句（表示意愿或礼貌时除外）。
- **混合**条件句把非真实的过去条件和现在的结果结合起来，或者反过来（[[#rule-mixed]]）。
- *Unless* ≈“除非”；*provided*、*as long as*、*on condition that* 强调必要性；*in case* 表示预防，不表示条件；*but for* / *without* / *if it hadn't been for* + 名词（[[#rule-conjunctions]]）。
- 正式英语使用 *should*、*were to* 和倒装：*Should you need…*、*Had I known…*
- 汉语通过上下文以及“要不是”“早知道”这样的词来标记非真实；英语通过时态来标记。
:::

## 习题

::: exercise 真实条件句 {level=1}
用动词的正确形式填空。(a) *If you (press) this button, the machine (start).* (b) *If it (snow) tomorrow, the school (close).* (c) *I (call) you if I (hear) any news.* (d) *If you (not like) spicy food, don't (order) the mapo tofu.* (e) *If she (leave) at eight, she (arrive) by now.*
::: solution
(a) *press … starts*（普遍真理）。(b) *snows … will close*（或 *might close*）。(c) *'ll call … hear*。(d) *don't like … order*。(e) *left … will have arrived* / *should have arrived*（真实的过去条件，对现在的推断）。
:::
:::

::: exercise 非真实的现在和过去 {level=1}
为每种情况写一个条件句。(a) *I don't have a car, so I can't drive you.* (b) *He didn't study, so he failed.* (c) *It's raining, so we can't play tennis.* (d) *They didn't invite me, so I didn't go.* (e) *I'm not you — but my advice is to accept.*
::: solution
(a) *If I had a car, I could drive you.* (b) *If he had studied, he wouldn't have failed*（或 *would have passed*）。(c) *If it weren't (wasn't) raining, we could play tennis.* (d) *If they had invited me, I would have gone.* (e) *If I were you, I would accept.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 改错 {level=1}
(a) *If I will see her, I will tell her.* (b) *If I would have money, I would buy a house.* (c) *If you would have told me, I would have helped.* (d) *Unless you don't hurry, you'll be late.* (e) *If I knew it was your birthday, I would have bought you a present.*
::: solution
(a) *If I **see** her, …* (b) *If I **had** money, …* (c) *If you **had told** me, …* (d) ***Unless** you hurry, …* / ***If** you don't hurry, …* (e) *If I **had known** it was your birthday, …*
:::
:::

::: exercise 混合条件句 {level=2}
完成每个句子。(a) *If I hadn't missed the flight, I ___ (be) in Bangkok now.* (b) *If she weren't so stubborn, she ___ (accept) the offer last week.* (c) *If you ___ (eat) breakfast, you wouldn't be hungry now.* (d) *If I ___ (have) a better memory, I wouldn't have forgotten your name.*
::: solution
(a) *would be* (b) *would have accepted* (c) *had eaten* (d) *had*（一种长期的现在状态）
:::
:::

::: exercise 其他连词 {level=2}
用括号中的词改写。(a) *If you don't apologise, I won't speak to you again.*（*unless*） (b) *You can stay, but you must be quiet.*（*as long as*） (c) *Your help saved me; without it I would have given up.*（*if it hadn't been for*） (d) *Take a jacket; it might get cold.*（*in case*） (e) *If you need anything, please let me know.*（*should*——用倒装）
::: solution
(a) *Unless you apologise, I won't speak to you again.* (b) *You can stay as long as you're quiet.* (c) *If it hadn't been for your help, I would have given up.* (d) *Take a jacket in case it gets cold.* (e) *Should you need anything, please let me know.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 正式倒装 {level=2}
不用 *if*，用正式文体改写。(a) *If I had known the truth, I would never have agreed.* (b) *If the company were to close, two thousand jobs would be lost.* (c) *If you should have any questions, contact the front desk.* (d) *If we had not received the warning, many lives would have been lost.*
::: solution
(a) *Had I known the truth, I would never have agreed.* (b) *Were the company to close, two thousand jobs would be lost.* (c) *Should you have any questions, contact the front desk.* (d) *Had we not received the warning, many lives would have been lost.*（✗ *Hadn't we received…*）
:::
:::

::: exercise 翻译条件 {level=3}
译成英语。判断每个条件是真实的还是非真实的。(a) 要是你明天有空，我们一起吃饭吧。 (b) 要是我有你那么聪明就好了。 (c) 早知道路这么堵，我就坐地铁了。 (d) 要不是他帮忙，我们现在还在山上呢。 (e) 你不说，谁知道？
::: solution
(a) 真实：*If you're free tomorrow, let's have dinner together.*

(b) 非真实的现在（一个愿望）：*If only I were as clever as you!* / *I wish I were as clever as you.*（[[complex-sentences/unreal-past]]）

(c) 非真实的过去：*If I'd known the traffic would be this bad, I'd have taken the underground (subway).*

(d) 混合（过去的条件，现在的结果）：*If it hadn't been for his help, we'd still be on the mountain now.* / *Without his help, we'd still be up the mountain.*

(e) 修辞性问句，非真实：*If you hadn't said anything, who would have known?* 或者，如果时间是现在：*If you didn't tell us, how would anyone know?*
:::
:::

::: exercise 一段关于后悔和计划的文字 {level=3}
就你生活中的一个决定（选择学校、工作、城市）写一段80–120词的文字。至少使用一个真实条件句、一个非真实的现在条件句、一个非真实的过去条件句和一个混合条件句，以及 *unless*、*as long as* 或 *provided* 中的一个。
::: solution
答案因人而异。参考答案：

*Five years ago I chose to study engineering instead of music. **If I had chosen music, I would probably be teaching piano now**, and I sometimes wonder what that life would be like. **If I were braver, I would have taken the risk** — but I was eighteen and worried about money. In fact, **if I hadn't studied engineering, I would never have met** my closest friends. I still play every weekend, and **as long as I can do that**, I'm happy. **If I get the chance next year, I'll join** an orchestra.*

检查：混合（*had chosen … would be teaching now*），混合的非真实现在 → 过去（*were braver … would have taken*），非真实的过去（*hadn't studied … would never have met*），*as long as*，真实的将来（*get … 'll join*）。
:::
:::
