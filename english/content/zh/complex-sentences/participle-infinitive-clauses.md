一篇游记里的两句话：

> *Having spent three days on the train, we arrived in Lhasa exhausted.*
>
> ✗ *Having spent three days on the train, the city looked wonderful.*

第一句是很好的书面英语：*Having spent three days on the train* 是一个没有主语也没有时的分句，读者从主句中理解它的主语——在火车上度过三天的是 *we*。第二句开头完全相同，但它的主句以 *the city* 开头，所以按照语法，在火车上度过三天的是这座城市。读者会注意到这种失误，它叫作**垂悬分词**（dangling participle）；在严谨的写作中，即使意思通常很清楚，它也是一个错误。

以 *-ing* 形式、*-ed* 分词和带 *to* 的不定式构成的分句是书面英语中最有用的工具之一。它们使语篇比一连串限定分句更简短、更紧凑，让写作者把背景信息放在背景里，是优秀的叙事文体和学术文体的典型特征。这一课说明怎样使用它们：作为简化的关系从句（*the man **sitting by the door***、*a bridge **built in 1890***、*the first person **to arrive***），作为状语分句（*Arriving late, she…*），带有自己的主语（*The weather being fine, …*），以及其他不定式句型。这一课也说明它们在什么情况下会出错。这些形式所简化的限定关系从句和状语从句已经在 [[complex-sentences/relative-clauses]] 和 [[complex-sentences/adverbial-clauses]] 中讲过。

## 形式

::: definition 分词分句和不定式分句 {#def-nonfinite}
**分词分句**（participle clause）是以分词为基础构成的非限定分句（[[complex-sentences/clause-structure#def-finite]]）：

| 形式 | 主动 | 被动 |
|---|---|---|
| *-ing* 分词 | *taking* | *being taken* |
| 完成式分词 | *having taken* | *having been taken* |
| *-ed* 分词 | — | *taken* |

**不定式分句**（infinitive clause）以带 *to* 的不定式为基础构成：*to take*、*to be taken*、*to have taken*。

两者都没有时，通常也都没有主语：时间和主语从主句中理解。
:::

*-ing* 分词与动名词形式相同（*Swimming is good for you*）；区别在于分句的作用。作名词用的分句（*I enjoy swimming*）属于 [[complex-sentences/noun-clauses]] 和 [[verb-patterns/ing-or-infinitive]] 的内容。这一课讲的是作用像关系从句和状语从句的分句。

## 简化的关系从句

::: theorem 名词之后的分词 {#rule-reduced-relative}
名词之后的分词分句可以承担限制性或非限制性关系从句的作用：

1. ***-ing*** = 主动，常常表示持续或长期的情况：*the man **sitting** by the door*（= who is sitting）；*people **living** near the airport*（= who live）；*a road **leading** to the sea*。
2. ***-ed*** = 被动：*the cars **made** in this factory*（= which are made）；*a bridge **built** in 1890*（= which was built）；*the people **injured** in the accident*。
3. ***being* + *-ed*** = 被动，正在进行：*the building **being repaired** across the road*（= which is being repaired）。

非限制性的简化分句要加逗号：*The palace, **built** in the fifteenth century, is now a museum.*
:::

::: proof
夸克等（Quirk et al.，1985）和比伯等（Biber et al.，1999）把这些分句描述为关系从句的非限定对应形式。比伯等发现，它们在新闻和学术文章中远比在会话中常见：它们把信息压缩进名词短语，而这正是信息性写作所需要的。*-ing* 形式不一定对应于进行体：*Anyone **knowing** the answer should raise their hand*（= who knows）是正确的，尽管 *know* 很少用进行体。修饰名词的 *-ed* 分词在意义上总是被动的，只有少数不及物动词例外：*a **retired** teacher*，*the train **just arrived** at platform 4*（罕见而正式）。
:::

这种用法有限制。名词之后的 *-ing* 分句描述的是与主句同时的情况，或者长期的情况；它不用于主句时间之前的**单次已完成动作**，也不用于将来的动作。

- ✗ *The man **breaking** the window has been arrested.* ✓ *The man **who broke** the window has been arrested.*
- ✗ *The students **arriving** tomorrow will stay in Block C.*——在非正式的安排性用语中可以，但 ✓ *The students **who arrive** / **are arriving** tomorrow …* 更稳妥。
- ✓ *The man **standing** at the bus stop is my uncle.*（现在）
- ✓ *Thousands of people **living** in the valley had to leave.*（当时的一种长期情况）

::: theorem 名词之后的不定式分句 {#rule-infinitive-relative}
带 *to* 的不定式也可以跟在名词后面：

1. 在 ***the first***、***the last***、***the only***、***the next*** 和最高级之后，名词作主语：*She was **the first woman to climb** the mountain.*（= the first woman who climbed）*He is **the only person to have read** the whole report.*
2. 说明对这个名词可以或应该做什么，名词是不定式的宾语或介词的补足成分：*a book **to read** on the train*，*something **to eat***，*a place **to stay***，*a pen **to write with***，*the man **to ask***。不同的主语用 ***for*** 引出：*a book **for children to read***。
3. 用被动形式，表示必须做的事情：*the problems **to be solved***，*the next decision **to be made***。
:::

在第2条中，宾语或介词宾语的空位不能填上：✗ *a pen to write with it*，✗ *something to eat it*。这条规则与 [[complex-sentences/relative-clauses#rule-gap]] 中的空位规则相同。

::: example 简化关系从句 {#ex-reduce}
在可能的地方缩短关系从句。如果不能缩短，说明原因。(a) *Passengers who are travelling to Guangzhou should change at Changsha.* (b) *The paintings which were stolen last year have been found.* (c) *The woman who sold me the ticket gave me the wrong change.* (d) *She was the youngest player who had ever won the title.* (e) *I need somebody who can help me with the move.*
::: solution
1. 主动动词 → *-ing*；被动 → *-ed*；在 *first*/*only*/最高级之后 → 带 *to* 的不定式。
2. 检查限制：之前的单次已完成动作不用 *-ing*。

(a) *Passengers **travelling** to Guangzhou should change at Changsha.*

(b) *The paintings **stolen** last year have been found.*

(c) 不能用 *-ing*：卖票是主要事件之前的单次已完成动作。✗ *The woman selling me the ticket gave me…* 的意思会是她找零钱的时候还在卖票，这勉强说得通，但用限定分句更好。

(d) *She was the youngest player **ever to win** the title*（或 *to have won*）。

(e) *I need somebody **to help** me with the move.*——不定式包含了 *can* 的意思。
:::
:::

## 作状语的分词分句

位于主句之前或之后的分词分句可以表示时间、原因、结果、条件或伴随情况。它比限定的状语从句更简短、更正式，并且常常把确切的关系留给读者去判断。

| 意义 | 分词分句 | 对应的限定分句 |
|---|---|---|
| 时间（同时） | ***Walking** home, I met an old friend.* | *While I was walking home, …* |
| 时间（紧接在前） | ***Opening*** *the letter, she began to read.* | *When she had opened the letter, …* |
| 时间（较早） | ***Having finished** the report, he went home.* | *After he had finished the report, …* |
| 原因 | ***Not knowing*** *the way, we took a taxi.* | *Because we didn't know the way, …* |
| 原因（被动） | ***Written*** *in simple English, the book is easy to follow.* | *Because it is written in simple English, …* |
| 条件 | ***Used*** *carefully, this knife will last for years.* | *If it is used carefully, …* |
| 结果 | *The storm closed the airport, **causing** long delays.* | *…, and this caused long delays.* |
| 伴随情况 | *She sat by the window, **reading** a newspaper.* | *…, and she was reading a newspaper.* |

::: theorem 作状语的分词分句 {#rule-adverbial-participle}
1. 分词分句隐含的主语是**主句的主语**。
2. 简单的 *-ing* 形式描述与主要动作**同时**或紧接在它之前的动作。**完成式分词**（*having done*）表示这个动作在主要动作**之前已经完成**：***Having lived** in Japan for years, she spoke Japanese well.*
3. *-ed* 分词以及 *being* / *having been* + *-ed* 是**被动的**：***Shocked** by the news, he sat down.* ***Having been warned** about the traffic, we left early.*
4. 否定形式把 ***not*** 放在分词之前：***Not having** a car, I rarely leave the city.*
5. 可以保留从属连词，使意思清楚：***After** leaving school, …*，***While** waiting, …*，***When** asked, …*，***Although** knowing the risks, …*，***If** used correctly, …*，***Since** joining the company, …*
6. 用 *-ing* 表示结果的分句放在主句**之后**，用逗号隔开：*Prices rose sharply, **forcing** many families to cut back.*
:::

::: proof
夸克等（1985）和斯旺（Swan，2016）描述了这些分句以及用完成式分词表示较早动作的用法；斯旺指出，只有当两个动作紧接着发生时，才用简单的 *-ing* 形式表示较早的动作（*Taking a key from her bag, she opened the door*）。比伯等（1999）发现，作状语的分词分句在会话中很少见，在小说和学术写作中很常见，所以在口语中用限定分句通常更自然。规则6中的结果分句在新闻和学术写作中尤其常见。它的 *-ing* 指的是前面整个分句，而不是它的主语：在 *Prices rose sharply, forcing families to cut back* 中，迫使人们节俭的是价格上涨，而不是价格本身。
:::

::: example 从限定分句到分词分句 {#ex-participle}
用分词分句改写每个句子。(a) *Because she felt ill, Amina left the meeting early.* (b) *After he had read the instructions, Diego assembled the desk.* (c) *The house was built on a hill, so it has wonderful views.* (d) *Because I didn't want to wake anyone, I took my shoes off.* (e) *The company closed two factories, and this put 800 people out of work.*
::: solution
(a) ***Feeling** ill, Amina left the meeting early.*——原因，同时。

(b) ***Having read** the instructions, Diego assembled the desk.*——较早且已完成：用完成式分词。（*After reading the instructions, …* 也可以。）

(c) ***Built** on a hill, the house has wonderful views.*——被动，原因。

(d) ***Not wanting** to wake anyone, I took my shoes off.*——否定词放在分词之前。

(e) *The company closed two factories, **putting** 800 people out of work.*——结果，放在逗号之后。

在每一句中，主句的主语都是分词分句隐含的主语：阿米娜感到不舒服，迭戈读了说明书，房子是建造的对象，我不想吵醒任何人，而关闭工厂使人们失业。
:::
:::

## 垂悬分词

::: warning 垂悬分词
✗ *Walking into the room, the lights suddenly went out.*（灯并没有走路。）✓ *As I walked into the room, the lights suddenly went out.* ✓ *Walking into the room, **I** noticed that the lights had gone out.*

✗ *Having finished dinner, the dishes were washed.* ✓ *Having finished dinner, **we** washed the dishes.*

✗ *Born in Hangzhou in 1990, her first novel was published at 21.* ✓ ***Born** in Hangzhou in 1990, **she** published her first novel at 21.*

✗ *Once opened, you should keep the jar in the fridge.* ✓ *Once opened, **the jar** should be kept in the fridge.*
:::

::: theorem 让分词依附在正确的主语上 {#rule-dangling}
在严谨的写作中，作状语的分词分句（或 *Once opened* 这样的简化分句）的隐含主语必须是**主句的主语**。如果不是，就改变主句，使它的主语成为动作的执行者，或者把分词分句变成有自己主语的限定分句。

有些分词已经变成了**介词或连词**，使用时不需要依附于某个主语：*generally speaking*、*strictly speaking*、*considering*、*given*、*judging by*、*assuming (that)*、*provided (that)*、*regarding*、*according to*、*owing to*、*including*。***Considering** the weather, the turnout was good.* ***Judging by** his accent, he's from Scotland.*
:::

::: proof
自福勒的《现代英语用法》（*Modern English Usage*，1926）把它们称为“无依附分词”以来，用法指南就一直告诫人们不要使用垂悬分词；夸克等（1985）和赫德尔斯顿与普勒姆（Huddleston and Pullum，2002）都描述了隐含主语必须是主句主语这一要求。赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆还指出，垂悬分词在已出版的文字中很常见，其中许多并不造成理解困难；使一个垂悬分词不可接受的，是读者先把分词依附到了错误的名词上，得到一幅荒谬的画面。科学写作中的一种常见情况是分词加被动的主句：*Using this method, the samples were analysed.* 这里完全没有提到动作的执行者（研究人员）。这种句型很普遍，在科学文献中常常被容忍，但许多编辑和文体指南更喜欢 *Using this method, we analysed the samples*，因为现在大多数学术领域都接受主动的 *we*（[[academic-writing/academic-style]]）。规则中的固定形式在词典中被列为介词或连词；*based on* 的用法相同，但有些编辑仍然反对这样用。
:::

::: quiz
哪个句子有垂悬分词？
- [ ] *Feeling hungry, I made a sandwich.*
- [x] *Looking out of the plane window, the mountains seemed very small.*
- [ ] *Generally speaking, the trains here are punctual.*
- [ ] *Built in 1912, the station is the oldest in the city.*
::: solution
*Looking out of the plane window* 的隐含主语是一个人，但主句的主语是 *the mountains*。正确的说法：*Looking out of the plane window, I thought the mountains seemed very small*，或 *From the plane window, the mountains seemed very small.* *Generally speaking* 是固定表达，在最后一句中，车站是建造的对象。
:::
:::

::: example 依附正确还是垂悬？ {#ex-dangling}
判断每个分词的依附是否正确，并改正不正确的。(a) *Arriving at the hotel, our room was not ready.* (b) *Arriving at the hotel, we found that our room was not ready.* (c) *Considering the price, the food was excellent.* (d) *Having been repaired twice, I decided to sell the car.* (e) *Once finished, the report should be sent to the manager.*
::: solution
对每个句子，问一问是谁或什么执行了分词的动作，并把它与主句的主语相比较。

(a) 垂悬：房间并没有到达。→ *When we arrived at the hotel, our room was not ready.*

(b) 正确：到达的是 *we*。

(c) 正确：*considering* 是一个类似介词的固定形式，不需要依附于主语。

(d) 垂悬：修过两次的是车，不是 *I*。→ *The car had been repaired twice, so I decided to sell it.* / *Having been repaired twice, the car was still unreliable, so I sold it.*

(e) 正确：报告被完成，然后被送出。（✗ *Once finished, you should send the report…* 就会垂悬。）
:::
:::

::: quiz
哪个句子正确地使用了非限定分句？
- [ ] *The woman winning the race last Sunday is my cousin.*
- [ ] *This exercise is too hard to do it without help.*
- [x] *Not wanting to be late, she took a taxi.*
- [ ] *Having been born in Harbin, the cold doesn't bother me.*
::: solution
*Not wanting to be late* 的否定词 *not* 放在分词之前，它的主语是 *she*。其他几句应当是 *who won the race last Sunday*（过去的单次已完成动作）、*too hard to do without help*（不用 *it*）和 *Having been born in Harbin, I'm not bothered by the cold*。
:::
:::

## 带自己主语的分句

分词分句也可以有自己的主语。这叫作**独立**分句（absolute clause，夸克等称之为带主语的补充分句）。

- *The weather **being** fine, we ate outside.*（正式，相当陈旧）
- ***All things considered**, the trip was a success.*（固定表达）
- *She stood at the door, **her heart pounding**.*（文学用语）
- ***With prices rising** so fast, many families are struggling.*（中性；常用 *with*）
- ***With** the children asleep, the house was quiet.*（无动词，带 *with*）

独立分句避免了垂悬的问题，因为它们的主语是明确说出来的。*with* 结构在现代英语中最自然，在口语和书面语中都是如此。不带 *with* 的独立分句主要是正式的或文学性的，在描写人物时，它常常描写身体的一部分或一种感觉（*her hands shaking*、*his voice trembling*）。

## 更多的不定式分句

不定式分句除了修饰名词以外，还有许多作用。在这个水平上最重要的有：

- **目的**：*She went to Shanghai **to look** for work.*（见 [[complex-sentences/adverbial-clauses#rule-purpose]]）
- ***too* 和 *enough* 之后的结果**：*The box was **too heavy to lift**.* *He's **old enough to vote**.*
- **出乎意料的结果**，用 *only to*：*We hurried to the station, **only to find** that the train had gone.*
- **在形容词之后**，句子的主语被理解为不定式的宾语：*This book is **easy to read**.* *The instructions were **difficult to follow**.* *She is **hard to please**.*
- **用 *for* 引出主语**：***For a child to travel** alone is risky.* → *It's risky **for a child to travel** alone.* *I'm waiting **for the shop to open**.*
- **作为对整个句子的评论**，用于固定短语：***To be honest**, …*，***To tell the truth**, …*，***To begin with**, …*，***To sum up**, …*

::: warning *Easy to read it*
✗ *This question is difficult to answer it.* ✓ *This question is difficult to answer.* ✓ ***It** is difficult to answer this question.*

✗ *The film was too long to watch it in one evening.* ✓ *The film was too long to watch in one evening.*

在这些句型中，句子的主语同时也是不定式的宾语，所以宾语的位置是空着的。加上 *it* 就填补了一个必须空着的空位——就像关系从句中的复指代词一样。如果句首用了先行词 *it*，就需要宾语：*It is difficult to answer **this question***。
:::

::: example 用非限定分句合并句子 {#ex-combine}
把每组句子合并成一个句子，至少使用一个分词分句或不定式分句。(a) *Liu Yang finished university. She moved to Berlin. She wanted to study music.* (b) *The museum is closed for repairs. The repairs will take two years.* (c) *We got to the cinema. We discovered that the film had already started.* (d) *The bridge was damaged in the flood. It has been closed to traffic.* (e) *The children were tired. They fell asleep in the car.*
::: solution
(a) ***Having finished** university, Liu Yang moved to Berlin **to study** music.*——较早的动作用完成式分词，目的用不定式。

(b) *The museum is closed for repairs **expected to take** two years.*——简化的关系从句（*which are expected to take*）。

(c) *We got to the cinema, **only to discover** that the film had already started.*——*only to* 表示令人不快的结果。

(d) ***Damaged** in the flood, the bridge has been closed to traffic.*——被动分词，原因。

(e) ***Tired**, the children fell asleep in the car.* / *The children, **being** tired, fell asleep in the car.*——无动词分句最自然；*Being tired, …* 正确但笨重。
:::
:::

::: note 英汉对比
- **汉语没有分词。** 汉语动词没有分词形式，所以“坐在门口的那个人”和“一九〇〇年建的桥”都是放在名词前面的“的”字修饰语。英语在名词后面用分词分句：*the man **sitting** by the door*，*the bridge **built** in 1900*。在 *-ing*（主动）和 *-ed*（被动）之间作选择，在汉语中没有对应的现象，学习者常常把两者用反：✗ *the problems discussing in the meeting* ✓ *the problems **discussed** in the meeting*。
- **话题链与垂悬分词。** 在汉语中，一连串分句可以共用一个隐含的话题，而且每个分句在主语清楚时都可以把它省去（李讷和汤普森1981年称之为零形回指）：“到了北京，天气很热”——话题是“我们”，而“天气”只是第二个分句的主语。把这种结构作为分词分句带进英语，就会产生垂悬分词：✗ *Arriving in Beijing, the weather was very hot.* 英语需要 ✓ *When we arrived in Beijing, the weather was very hot* 或 ✓ *Arriving in Beijing, we found the weather very hot.*
- **Easy to read。** 汉语的“这本书很容易读”和英语的 *This book is easy to read* 形状相同：主语被理解为“读”/*read* 的宾语。说汉语的学习者在这里很少加 *it*，但句子较长时就会出现这个错误（✗ *This kind of problem is not easy for students to solve it*）。
- **为了和结果。** “为了”+ 动词对应于表示目的的不定式（*to*、*in order to*），而“结果”/“导致”分句常常对应于表示结果的 *-ing* 分句：工厂关闭了，导致八百人失业 → *The factory closed, **putting** 800 people out of work.*
:::

::: history
独立分词分句进入英语，部分是由于模仿拉丁语；拉丁语的**独立夺格**（ablative absolute，*urbe capta*“城市被攻占之后”）是说明背景情况的标准方式。古英语的译者用独立与格来翻译它，后来的作家则用主格构成英语的独立结构（*the city taken*、*this done*）。福勒的《现代英语用法》（1926）给垂悬分词起名为“无依附分词”，使它成为用法建议中的固定话题。后来，语料库语言学显示了这些分句的语域分布：道格拉斯·比伯（Douglas Biber）的《口语和书面语的变异》（*Variation across Speech and Writing*，1988）把分词分句作为区分精心计划的信息性书面语与会话的特征之一，《朗文英语口语和书面语语法》（*Longman Grammar of Spoken and Written English*，1999）证实它们在口语中很少见。
:::

## 后续内容

分词分句是密集的学术句子的一个关键特征：见 [[academic-reading/dense-sentences]] 和 [[style-and-rhetoric/concision]]。前置的分词分句加倒装（*Standing at the door was a tall man*）见 [[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]]。更一般意义上的省略词语是 [[complex-sentences/ellipsis-substitution]] 的主题。

::: summary
- 分词分句和不定式分句是非限定的：没有时，通常也没有主语；两者都从主句中理解（[[#def-nonfinite]]）。
- 在名词之后，*-ing* = 主动（*the man sitting there*），*-ed* = 被动（*the cars made here*）；*-ing* 不用于较早的单次已完成动作（[[#rule-reduced-relative]]）。
- *The first / last / only / next* + 名词 + 带 *to* 的不定式；*something to eat*、*a pen to write with*——空位保持空着（[[#rule-infinitive-relative]]）。
- 作状语的分词分句表示时间、原因、条件、结果和情况；较早的动作用 *having done*；*not* 放在分词之前（[[#rule-adverbial-participle]]）。
- 隐含的主语必须是主句的主语；否则分词就会**垂悬**（✗ *Walking into the room, the lights went out*）。*generally speaking* 和 *considering* 这样的固定形式是例外（[[#rule-dangling]]）。
- 独立分句有自己的主语：*With prices rising, …*；*The weather being fine, …*（正式）。
- 不定式分句还表示目的、*too/enough* 的结果、*only to* 的结果，并且跟在形容词之后（*easy to read*——不用 *it*）。
:::

## 习题

::: exercise 用 *-ing* 还是 *-ed*？ {level=1}
选择正确的分词。(a) *The woman (standing / stood) at the gate is my aunt.* (b) *Most of the rice (growing / grown) here is exported.* (c) *Anyone (wishing / wished) to attend should register by Friday.* (d) *The letter (sending / sent) yesterday hasn't arrived.* (e) *Children (living / lived) in cities often have less space to play.*
::: solution
(a) *standing* (b) *grown* (c) *wishing* (d) *sent* (e) *living*
:::
:::

::: exercise 用不用完成式分词？ {level=1}
用分词分句改写。如果第一个动作在第二个动作之前已经完成，就用 *having* + 分词。(a) *She put on her coat and went out.* (b) *After he had lost his keys twice, he bought a key ring with a bell.* (c) *He was sitting in the garden. He was reading a book.* (d) *Because they had seen the film before, they didn't go.*
::: solution
(a) *Putting on her coat, she went out.*（紧接在前）(b) *Having lost his keys twice, he bought a key ring with a bell.* (c) *He was sitting in the garden, reading a book.* (d) *Having seen the film before, they didn't go.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 名词之后的不定式 {level=1}
用带 *to* 的不定式完成句子。(a) *Neil Armstrong was the first person ___ on the Moon.*（*walk*） (b) *Have you got anything ___ ? I'm thirsty.*（*drink*） (c) *We need a bigger table ___ .*（*work at*） (d) *She was the last ___ the building.*（*leave*） (e) *There are still a few questions ___ .*（*answer*——被动）
::: solution
(a) *to walk* (b) *to drink* (c) *to work at* (d) *to leave* (e) *to be answered*
:::
:::

::: exercise 改正垂悬分词 {level=2}
用两种方法改写每个句子，使它正确。(a) *Driving to work, a deer ran across the road.* (b) *Having missed the bus, the teacher was angry with me.* (c) *Written in a hurry, I made a lot of mistakes in the essay.* (d) *After eating lunch, the plane took off.*
::: solution
(a) *While I was driving to work, a deer ran across the road.* / *Driving to work, I saw a deer run across the road.*

(b) *Because I had missed the bus, the teacher was angry with me.* / *Having missed the bus, I arrived late and the teacher was angry with me.*

(c) *Because I wrote the essay in a hurry, I made a lot of mistakes in it.* / *Written in a hurry, the essay was full of mistakes.*

(d) *After we had eaten lunch, the plane took off.* / *After eating lunch, we took off*（如果是“我们”在飞机上）。
:::
:::

::: exercise 简化分句 {level=2}
缩短黑体分句，或者说明为什么不能缩短。(a) *The data **which were collected in 2023** show a clear trend.* (b) *The man **who phoned you yesterday** left no number.* (c) ***When she was asked about the plan**, she refused to comment.* (d) ***As I didn't have an umbrella**, I got wet.* (e) *Students **who wish to change courses** must see their tutor.*
::: solution
(a) *The data **collected in 2023** show a clear trend.* (b) 不能用 *-ing*：过去的单次已完成动作（✗ *The man phoning you yesterday*）。(c) ***When asked** about the plan, she refused to comment.* (d) ***Not having** an umbrella, I got wet.* (e) *Students **wishing** to change courses must see their tutor.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 翻译 {level=2}
用分词分句或不定式分句译成英语。(a) 在会上讨论的问题都很重要。 (b) 不知道怎么办，她给妈妈打了电话。 (c) 他是第一个到达终点的人。 (d) 大雨持续了三天，造成了严重的洪水。 (e) 这个问题很难回答。
::: solution
(a) *The issues **discussed** at the meeting were all important.* (b) ***Not knowing** what to do, she phoned her mother.* (c) *He was **the first (person) to reach** the finish line.* (d) *The heavy rain lasted three days, **causing** serious flooding.* (e) *This question is difficult **to answer**.*（不用 *it*）
:::
:::

::: exercise 修改实验报告 {level=3}
这段学生实验报告中的文字有三个垂悬分词和另一个非限定分句的错误。用带 *we* 的主动语态改写。*Having prepared the solutions, the temperature was raised to 60 °C. Using a digital thermometer, the temperature was checked every minute. After stirring for ten minutes, the colour changed to blue. The results obtaining are shown in Table 1.*
::: hint
对每个分词都问一问是谁执行了这个动作；并检查 *obtaining* 应该是主动的还是被动的。
:::
::: solution
*Having prepared the solutions,* ***we raised*** *the temperature to 60 °C. Using a digital thermometer,* ***we checked*** *the temperature every minute. After* ***we had stirred the mixture*** *for ten minutes, the colour changed to blue.*（或 *After ten minutes of stirring, the colour changed…*）*The results* ***obtained*** *are shown in Table 1.*

前两个垂悬分词通过把 *we* 作为主语而得到改正。在第三句中，搅拌的不是颜色，所以需要一个限定分句或一个名词短语。*Obtaining* 应当是被动分词 *obtained*（= which were obtained）。
:::
:::

::: exercise 写一段短小的叙述 {level=3}
写一段文字（80–120词），讲述一次出了问题的旅行。至少使用五个不同类型的非限定分句：一个完成式分词、一个被动分词、一个逗号之后的结果分句、一个 *only to* 分句和一个带 *with* 的独立分句。检查你的分词没有一个是垂悬的。
::: solution
答案因人而异。参考答案：

***Having booked*** *the cheapest flight we could find, my brother and I set off for Kunming at four in the morning.* ***With the roads still empty,*** *we reached the airport early,* ***only to discover*** *that the flight had been cancelled.* ***Offered*** *a seat on a plane the next day, we decided to take the train instead,* ***arriving*** *in Kunming thirty hours later.* ***Exhausted*** *but happy, we agreed that it had been the best part of the trip.*

检查：*Having booked*（我们），*With the roads still empty*（独立分句），*only to discover*（出乎意料的结果），*Offered*（被动；我们被提供了座位），*arriving*（结果/先后；我们到达了），*Exhausted*（无动词；我们筋疲力尽）。
:::
:::
