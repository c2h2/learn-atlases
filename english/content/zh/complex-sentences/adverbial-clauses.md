下面是一个学生关于志愿活动项目的报告中同一段文字的两个版本：

> *Because we had no money, we asked local shops for help. Because they agreed, we bought paint. When we finished the walls, we painted the doors. Although it rained, we worked outside. Although we were tired, we finished on time.*

> *As we had no money, we asked local shops for help, and once they had agreed, we could buy paint. By the time we had finished the walls, the doors were the only thing left. We worked outside even though it rained — tired as we were, we finished on time.*

两个版本都正确。第一个版本用了三个从属连词，每个用了两次，而且总是放在句首。第二个版本用了六个，放在句首、句中和句末，并且每一个都是为了表达一个准确的意思而选择的：*as* 表示读者无须讨论就能接受的原因，*once* 表示必须先完成的条件，*by the time* 表示某件事在某个时间点之前已经发生，*even though* 表示一个出人意料的事实，*tired as we were* 表示正式的让步。

基本的从属连词——*because*、*although*、*when*、*if*、*so that*——以及与之相配的逗号已经在 [[sentences-and-paragraphs/subordination]] 中讲过。这一课扩大范围：列出状语从句的主要意义，每种意义下不太常见但很重要的连词，近义连词之间的区别（*even if* 和 *even though*、*in case* 和 *if*、*while* 和 *whereas*），时间状语从句要求的时态，以及正式书面语中使用的简化分句。条件从句有专门的一课：[[complex-sentences/conditionals]]。

## 状语从句的意义

::: definition 状语从句 {#def-adverbial-clause}
**状语从句**是在上级分句中充当状语的从句：它告诉我们主要情况在什么时候、在哪里、为什么、在什么条件下、尽管有什么、为了什么目的、带来什么结果或以什么方式成立。它由一个**从属连词**（subordinator，如 *because*、*although*、*once*、*in case*）引导，可以是限定的、非限定的或无动词的：

- 限定：***Once the guests had left**, we cleared up.*
- 非限定：***Having cleared up**, we went to bed.*
- 无动词：***Once inside**, she locked the door.*
:::

| 意义 | 常用的从属连词 | 较正式或较少用的 |
|---|---|---|
| 时间 | *when*、*while*、*as*、*before*、*after*、*until*、*since*、*as soon as*、*once* | *by the time*、*the moment*、*whenever*、*now that*、*no sooner … than* |
| 地点 | *where*、*wherever* | |
| 原因 | *because*、*since*、*as* | *given that*、*seeing that*（非正式）、*in that*、*now that* |
| 条件 | *if*、*unless*、*as long as* | *provided (that)*、*on condition that* → [[complex-sentences/conditionals]] |
| 让步和对比 | *although*、*though*、*even though*、*while*、*even if* | *whereas*、*much as*、*however* + 形容词、*no matter* + *wh* 词、形容词/副词 + *as* |
| 目的 | *so that*、*so*（非正式） | *in order that*、*lest*、*for fear that*；预防：*in case* |
| 结果 | *so … that*、*such … that*、*so* | |
| 方式和比较 | *as*、*like*（非正式）、*the way* | *as if*、*as though* → [[complex-sentences/unreal-past]] |

许多从属连词有不止一种意义：*since*（时间或原因），*as*（时间、原因或方式），*while*（时间或对比），*so that*（目的或结果）。时态和上下文通常可以决定意义，但如果一个句子可以有两种理解，就选择只有一种意义的词（*because*、*whereas*、*in order that*）。

## 时间

::: theorem 时间状语从句中的时态 {#rule-time-tense}
1. 关于**将来**的时间状语从句用**现在**时，不用 *will*：*I'll call you **when I arrive**.*（✗ *when I will arrive*）
2. 要表示一个将来动作在另一个之前**完成**，用**现在完成时**：*You can go out **when you've finished** your homework.* *I'll tell him **as soon as I've spoken** to the manager.*
3. 在过去，**过去完成时**表示一个动作在另一个动作之前已经完成：***After she had read** the letter, she burnt it.* 由于 *after* 和 *before* 本身已经表明了先后顺序，用一般过去时也正确：***After she read** the letter, she burnt it.*
4. ***Since***（表示时间）后面用一般过去时表示一个时间点，主句用完成时：*I've lived here **since I was ten**.* *It's two years **since we last met**.*
5. ***By the time*** 从一个时间点往回看：主句用完成时：***By the time** we arrived, the film **had started**.* ***By the time** you read this, I'**ll have left**.*
:::

::: proof
规则1–2适用于引导关于将来的分句的所有时间和条件从属连词（*when*、*as soon as*、*before*、*after*、*until*、*once*、*by the time*、*if*、*unless*）；见 [[tense-and-aspect/future-forms#rule-time-clauses]] 和 [[sentences-and-paragraphs/subordination#rule-present-future]]。将来的意义已经由主句提供，时间状语从句把它的事件呈现为一个固定的参照点，用来确定主要事件的位置；于是英语用现在时（Quirk et al. 1985；Swan 2016）。只有当 *when* 分句不是状语时，*when* 后面才可以用 *will*——在名词性从句中（*I don't know **when** she **will** arrive*）或关系从句中（*the day **when** you **will** finally understand*）。
:::

那些不太常用的时间从属连词值得按意义成对地学习：

- ***once***“在某事完成之后，并且作为其结果”：***Once** you've paid, you'll get a receipt.*
- ***the moment*** / ***the minute*** / ***as soon as***“一……就”：*Call me **the moment** you hear anything.*
- ***until***“直到……的时候”（主要情况持续，然后停止）与 ***by the time***“在……之前或之时”：*I waited **until** he came.*——*I had eaten **by the time** he came.*
- ***whenever***“每当”或“在任何……的时候”：***Whenever** I see that photo, I smile.*
- ***now that***“既然现在”——时间和原因合在一起：***Now that** the exams are over, we can relax.*
- ***as***“当……的时候，在……的同时”，常常用于两个同时发生的变化：***As** the sun rose, the temperature climbed.*

::: example 时间状语从句中的时态 {#ex-time}
用括号中动词的正确形式填空，并说明理由。(a) *I'll lend you the book once I (finish) it.* (b) *By the time the guests (arrive), we (cook) everything.*——过去的情境。(c) *We (not see) each other since we (leave) school.* (d) *Can you wait until I (find) my keys?* (e) *By the time you (get) this message, I (land) in Nairobi.*
::: solution
(a) *have finished*——将来在借书之前完成：时间状语从句中用现在完成时，而不用 *will have finished*。

(b) *arrived … had cooked*——过去的 *by the time*：做饭在客人到达之前已经完成。

(c) *haven't seen … left*——*since* + 一般过去时表示起点；主句用现在完成时表示直到现在的这段时间。

(d) *find*（或 *'ve found*）——将来的意义，*until* 后面用现在时。

(e) *get … will have landed*——将来的 *by the time*：时间状语从句用现在时，主句用将来完成时。
:::
:::

::: quiz
哪个句子是正确的？
- [ ] *I'll wait here until you will come back.*
- [ ] *Since I have moved to Xi'an, I made many friends.*
- [x] *Once you've tried it, you'll never go back.*
- [ ] *By the time we got there, the shop closed.*
::: solution
*Once* + 现在完成时描述将来的完成，主句用 *will*。其他几句应当是 *until you come back*；*Since I moved to Xi'an, I have made many friends*；以及 *the shop had closed*（或 *had already closed*）。
:::
:::

## 原因

[[sentences-and-paragraphs/subordination]] 比较了 *because*、*since* 和 *as*：*because* 把原因作为新的、重要的信息说出来；*since* 和 *as* 给出读者可能已经接受的原因，通常放在前面。在这个水平上，还有三个有用的连词。

- ***given that***（中性到正式）“考虑到……这一事实”：***Given that** the budget is limited, we should prioritise.*
- ***seeing that*** / ***seeing as***（非正式）“因为，你也看到了”：***Seeing as** you're here, you could help me.*
- ***in that***（正式）“在……这一点上，因为”：*The study is unusual **in that** it follows the same people for forty years.*

作连词的 *for*（*She was tired, **for** she had worked all night*）是文学用语或旧式用法；它像 *and* 一样连接主句，不能用来开始句子的第一个分句。

## 让步和对比

::: theorem 让步和对比 {#rule-concession}
1. ***Although***、***though*** 和 ***even though*** 引出一个使主句显得出人意料的事实。*Though* 较不正式；*even though* 语气更强。***Although** it was late, she kept working.*
2. ***Even if*** 引出一种**可能性**，而不是一个事实：无论这种可能性是否发生，主句都成立。***Even if** it rains, the match will go ahead.*（可能下雨，也可能不下。）比较 ***Even though** it's raining, the match will go ahead.*（正在下雨。）
3. ***While*** 和 ***whereas*** 比较两个不同的事实，没有意外的意味：*My brother loves football, **whereas** I prefer tennis.* *Whereas* 较正式，在学术写作中很常见。
4. ***However*** + 形容词/副词、***no matter*** + *wh* 词，以及 ***whatever***、***whoever***、***wherever***，表示无论取什么值主句都成立：***However hard** I try, I can't remember her name.* ***No matter what** you say, I won't change my mind.*
5. 在正式英语和文学英语中，形容词、副词或名词可以放在 ***as*** 或 ***though*** 之前：***Tired as** she was, she kept going.*（= 尽管她很累……）***Much as** I like him, I can't agree.*
6. *Despite* 和 *in spite of* 是**介词**，后面跟名词短语或 *-ing* 形式，而不跟分句：***Despite** the rain* / ***Despite** feeling ill* / ***Despite the fact that** it rained*。
:::

::: proof
夸克等（Quirk et al.，1985）区分了**让步**（concession，一种情况从另一种情况来看出人意料：*although*）和**对比**（contrast，两种情况只是相互比较：*whereas*），斯旺（Swan，2016）对 *even if* 和 *even though* 作了同样的区分。规则5——有时叫作让步倒装，尽管主语和动词并没有倒装——在两部语法中都被描述为正式用法；美式的变体 *As tired as she was, …* 也可以见到。规则6是错误的常见来源，因为 *despite* 的意思与 *although* 相同，却属于不同的词类（[[sentences-and-paragraphs/subordination]] 中有基本的提醒）。还要注意，*while* 在时间和对比之间有歧义：*While I was in Paris, my sister was in Rome* 可以有两种理解，而且两种理解同时成立。
:::

::: widget truthtable
title: *Even if p, q*
formula: (p -> q) & (~p -> q)
compare: q
caption: 设 *p* 为“下雨”，*q* 为“比赛照常进行”。*Even if it rains, the match will go ahead* 表示：*p* 为真时 *q* 成立，*p* 为假时 *q* 也成立——即左边的公式。比较结果显示它与单纯的 *q* 等价：说话人承诺比赛会照常进行。*even if* 增加的不是一个不同的真值条件，而是一个信号：人们本来可能以为 *p* 会阻止比赛。*Even though it's raining, the match is going ahead* 同时断言了 *p* 和 *q*：试着把公式改成 *p & q*。
:::

::: warning *Although … but* 和 *despite* + 分句
✗ *Although the hotel was expensive, but it was very dirty.* ✓ *Although the hotel was expensive, it was very dirty.* ✓ *The hotel was expensive, but it was very dirty.*

✗ *Despite it was raining, we went out.* ✓ *Despite the rain, we went out.* ✓ ***Although** it was raining, we went out.* ✓ *Despite **the fact that** it was raining, we went out.*

✗ *Even it was late, he called me.* ✓ ***Even though** it was late, he called me.*——单独的 *even* 是副词，不是连词。
:::

::: example 选择让步连词 {#ex-concession}
选择最合适的词并说明理由。(a) *(Even if / Even though) I had a map, I got lost.* (b) *I wouldn't sell this house (even if / even though) you offered me a million.* (c) *The north of the country is dry, (whereas / although) the south gets heavy rain.* (d) *(However / Whatever) tired you are, you must finish the report.* (e) *(Despite / Although) her injury, she won the race.*
::: solution
(a) *Even though*——我有地图：这是事实。

(b) *even if*——出价是假设的（注意非真实的 *offered … wouldn't*，[[complex-sentences/conditionals]]）。

(c) *whereas*——比较两个事实，从一个来看另一个并不出人意料。（用 *although* 会暗示北方干燥使南方多雨显得出人意料。）

(d) *However*——*however* + 形容词。（*Whatever* 需要跟名词或分句：*Whatever happens, …*）

(e) *Despite*——后面跟名词短语。（*Although she was injured, …*）
:::
:::

## 目的和预防

::: theorem 目的状语从句 {#rule-purpose}
1. ***So that*** + 情态动词（*can*、*could*、*will*、*would*、*may*、*might*）表示目的：*I'll write it down **so that** I **don't** forget.* *We left early **so that** we **would** miss the traffic.* 在非正式英语中，常常省略 *that*：*… **so** we'd miss the traffic.*
2. ***In order that*** 是正式的：*Dates are announced early **in order that** students **may** plan ahead.*
3. 当目的的主语与主句主语相同时，更常用**带 *to* 的不定式**：*She left early **(in order) to** catch the train.* 否定形式是 ***so as not to*** 或 ***in order not to***：*We spoke quietly **so as not to** wake the baby.*（单用 *not to* 表示目的也可以，但不太常见。）
4. ***In case*** 表示对可能发生的事情的**预防**：*Take an umbrella **in case** it rains.*（= 因为可能会下雨）。它的意思不是 *if*：*Take an umbrella **if** it rains* 的意思是只有在下雨的时候才带伞。
5. ***Lest***（正式或文学用语）和 ***for fear that*** 的意思是“以免”：*He spoke softly **lest** he (should) wake the child.*
:::

::: proof
斯旺（2016）和夸克等（1985）描述了目的状语从句中的情态动词，以及主语相同时用不定式的情况。关于 *in case*，英式英语严格保持预防的意义；斯旺指出，在美式英语中 *in case* 也用来表示 *if*（*In case you need me, I'll be in my office*），而 *in case of* + 名词（*In case of fire, break the glass*）在两种变体中都表示“如果有……”。*Lest* 在英式英语中很少见，在美式英语中较常见，常常后接虚拟语气（*lest he wake*）；比伯等（Biber et al.，1999）和斯旺都把它归为在任何地方都属正式的用法。
:::

::: quiz
哪个句子的意思是“带些现金，因为刷卡机可能不能用”？
- [ ] *Take some cash if the card machine doesn't work.*
- [x] *Take some cash in case the card machine doesn't work.*
- [ ] *Take some cash so that the card machine doesn't work.*
- [ ] *Take some cash unless the card machine works.*
::: solution
*In case* 表示预防：在知道刷卡机能不能用之前，现在就带上现金。*If* 的意思是“只有当你发现它不能用的时候”——那时再带现金就太晚了。*So that* 给出一个荒谬的目的，*unless* 的意思是“如果不……”（只有在刷卡机不能用时才带现金），与 *if* 有同样的问题。
:::
:::

## 结果

::: theorem 结果状语从句 {#rule-result}
1. ***so*** + 形容词/副词 + ***that***：*The film was **so** long **that** I fell asleep.* *She spoke **so** quickly **that** nobody understood.*
2. ***such***（+ *a/an*）（+ 形容词）+ 名词 + ***that***：*It was **such a** long film **that** I fell asleep.* *They were **such** good friends **that** …*
3. ***so much / so many / so few / so little*** + 名词 + ***that***：*There were **so many** people **that** we couldn't get in.*
4. ***so***（不用 *that*），放在逗号之后，在非正式和中性的文体中表示结果：*The shop was closed, **so** we went home.* 这个 *so* 是并列连词，像 *and* 一样（[[sentences-and-paragraphs/coordination]]）。

在非正式英语中，*so* 和 *such* 之后常常省略 *that*：*I was so tired __ I fell asleep.*
:::

*so* 和 *such* 的区别在于词类：*so* 与形容词或副词连用，*such* 与名词短语连用。✗ *It was so a long film.* ✗ *It was such long.* 正式的倒装形式 *So long was the film that…* 和 *Such was the noise that…* 在 [[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]] 中讲解。

::: example *So that*：目的还是结果？ {#ex-so-that}
黑体分句表示目的还是结果？你是怎么知道的？(a) *She saved for two years **so that she could travel**.* (b) *The bridge was closed, **so that we had to drive round**.* (c) *Put the milk in the fridge **so that it doesn't go off**.* (d) *He trained every day, **so that by June he was ready**.*
::: solution
带 *so that* 的目的状语从句通常含有情态动词，前面没有逗号，描述的是希望达到的事情。结果状语从句通常跟在逗号后面，描述的是已经发生的事情。

(a) 目的——有 *could*，没有逗号，是希望达到的事情。

(b) 结果——有逗号，是一个事实的过去时；*so that* = *and so*。（这里单用 *so* 更常见。）

(c) 目的——现在时表示将来意义，是希望得到的结果。（用现在时的时候，表示目的的 *so that* 是非正式的；也可以说 *won't go off*。）

(d) 结果——有逗号，是一个事实；这也可能是他的目的，但这句话陈述的是发生了什么。
:::
:::

## 方式和比较

*As* 引导方式或比较分句：*Do it* ***as*** *I showed you.* *As I expected, she was late.* 在非正式英语中，尤其是美式英语中，***like*** 有同样的用法：*Do it* ***like*** *I showed you.* *Like I said, she was late.* 用法指南长期以来反对把 *like* 用作连词，正式的书面语中至今仍然避免这种用法，但词典把它记录为口语和非正式书面语中的标准用法（斯旺2016年指出，它在英式英语中是非正式的，在美式英语中非常常见）。在正式的书面语中，用 *as* 或 *the way*：*Do it* ***the way*** *I showed you.*

*As if* 和 *as though* 引出与某种可能并不真实的情况所作的比较：*He talks **as if** he knew everything.* 它们之后的时态选择属于 [[complex-sentences/unreal-past]] 的内容。

## 地点

*Where* 和 *wherever* 引导地点状语从句：*Sit **where** you like.* ***Wherever** she goes, people recognise her.* 它们没有其他状语从句那么常见，在描写中，英语通常更喜欢用介词短语（*by the window*）或关系从句（*the place where…*）。

## 简化的状语从句

在正式英语中，尤其是在书面语中，当状语从句的主语与主句的主语相同，并且它的动词是 *be* 或者可以变成非限定形式时，可以把它简化：

- ***When** in Rome, do as the Romans do.*（= When you are in Rome）
- ***Although** tired, the team played well.*（= Although they were tired）
- ***If** necessary, we can change the date.*（= If it is necessary）
- ***Once** opened, the jar should be kept in the fridge.*
- ***While** waiting for the bus, I read the paper.*
- ***Before** leaving, please switch off the lights.*

隐含的主语必须是主句的主语。✗ *Once opened, you should keep the jar in the fridge* 意味着被打开的是“你”。这就是垂悬修饰语的问题，在 [[complex-sentences/participle-infinitive-clauses]] 中与分词分句一起详细讲解。

::: example 提升一段文字 {#ex-upgrade}
改写这段文字，至少使用这一课中五个不同的从属连词，每个只用一次。*Because the train was late, we missed the first talk. When we arrived, the hall was full. Although we had tickets, we had to stand. Because the second speaker was very good, we stayed. When the conference ended, we were exhausted.*
::: solution
1. 确定每个句子中的关系：原因、时间（带完成的意义）、让步、原因、时间。
2. 选择能准确表达这种关系的从属连词，并变换位置。
3. 在关系跨越句子的地方把句子合并。

参考答案：

*As the train was late, we missed the first talk, and **by the time** we arrived the hall was full. **Even though** we had tickets, we had to stand. The second speaker was **so** good **that** we stayed to the end, and **once** the conference was over, we were exhausted.*

从属连词：*as*（读者可以接受的原因），*by the time*（在某个时间点之前完成——注意 *was full* 是状态），*even though*（出人意料的事实），*so … that*（结果，取代了第二个 *because*），*once*（完成之后）。
:::
:::

## 从句的位置

状语从句通常可以放在主句之前或之后。这种选择并不是随意的：它取决于句子讲的是什么，以及什么是新信息。

- 句首的从句为主要陈述设定一个**框架**——一个时间、一个条件、一个已知的原因：***When the war ended**, my grandfather returned to Shanghai.* 这在段落开头很常见，并与前文衔接。
- 句末的从句提供**新信息**，往往是句子的要点：*My grandfather returned to Shanghai **when the war ended**.*（回答“他什么时候回来的？”）。*Because* 从句比 *since* 从句和 *as* 从句更常放在句末，因为原因通常是新信息。
- 目的和结果从句几乎总是放在句末，因为它们跟在动作之后。

::: note 英汉对比
- **成对的关联词。** 汉语用成对的关联词——虽然……但是，因为……所以，既然……就，即使……也——而英语只用一个连词（[[sentences-and-paragraphs/subordination#rule-one-link]]）。✗ *Although … but*、✗ *Because … so* 就是典型的结果。要么选择从属连词，要么选择并列连词。
- **省去关联词（意合）。** 汉语常常不标明分句之间的关系：下雨了，我们就不去了（原因），他来了我就走（时间或条件），天再冷也得去（让步）。英语通常需要一个从属连词，译者必须选择正确的那一个：*Since it's raining, we won't go*；*I'll leave when (as soon as) he comes*；*However cold it is, we have to go*（连淑能1993讨论了许多这样的例子）。
- **语序。** 在汉语中，表示时间、原因、条件或让步的从句通常放在主句**之前**：“因为下雨，我们没去”远比“我们没去，因为下雨”常见，后者听起来像是事后补充。李讷和汤普森（Li and Thompson，1981）把这描述为汉语背景在前、前景在后的通常顺序。英语两种顺序都可以自由使用，并且常常把原因从句放在最后。学习者写英语时可以变换语序，把新信息放在末尾。
- **即使和虽然。** “虽然”（事实）对应于 *although* / *even though*；“即使”“就算”（可能性）对应于 *even if*。说汉语的人有时用 *even though* 表示“即使”：✗ *Even though it rains tomorrow, we'll go* ✓ *Even if it rains tomorrow, we'll go.*
- **以便和以免。** “以便”对应于 *so that*，“以免”“免得”对应于 *so as not to*、*in case* 或 *lest*：带把伞，以免下雨——*Take an umbrella in case it rains.*
:::

::: history
按意义对状语从句的传统分类——时间、地点、原因、条件、让步、目的、结果、方式、比较——来自拉丁语语法，在18和19世纪进入英语的学校语法；主要的现代语法保留了这种分类，并作了细化（夸克等1985年把让步与对比分开）。此后，语言学家对各种语言的状语从句作了比较。桑德拉·汤普森（Sandra Thompson）和罗伯特·朗埃克（Robert Longacre）的综述《状语从句》（“Adverbial clauses”，1985）发现，大多数语言都有表示时间、条件、目的和原因的分句的专门形式，而且条件从句往往放在前面；约瑟夫·格林伯格（Joseph Greenberg）在1963年就已提出，在所有语言的条件陈述中，条件通常位于结论之前。1975年，哲学家格赖斯（H. P. Grice）用 *but* 和 *therefore* 说明，有些词增加了一种意义——对比、结果——而这种意义并不属于句子的真值，他称之为规约含义（conventional implicature）：这和本课中 *even if* 的真值表所说明的是同一个道理。
:::

## 后续内容

条件从句——*if*、*unless*、*provided* 以及非真实条件句——是下一课的内容：[[complex-sentences/conditionals]]。简化分句和分词分句见 [[complex-sentences/participle-infinitive-clauses]]；*no sooner*、*hardly* 和 *so … that* 之后的倒装见 [[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]]。句子之间的连接词（*however*、*therefore*、*nevertheless*）及其在段落中的用法在 [[sentences-and-paragraphs/coherence]] 和 [[essay-writing/signposting]] 中讲授。

::: summary
- 状语从句说明主要情况在什么时候、在哪里、为什么、在什么条件下、尽管有什么、为了什么目的、带来什么结果或以什么方式成立（[[#def-adverbial-clause]]）。
- 关于将来的时间状语从句用现在时（表示完成时用现在完成时）；*by the time* 和 *since* 要求主句用完成时（[[#rule-time-tense]]）。
- *Even though* 引出事实，*even if* 引出可能性；*whereas* 比较两个事实；*however*/*no matter*/*whatever* 的意思是“不管怎样/什么”；*despite* 后接名词或 *-ing* 形式，不接分句（[[#rule-concession]]）。
- 表示目的用 *so that* + 情态动词；主语相同时用带 *to* 的不定式；*in case* 的意思是“以防”，而不是“如果”（在英式英语中）（[[#rule-purpose]]）。
- 表示结果用 *so* + 形容词/副词 + *that*、*such* + 名词短语 + *that*（[[#rule-result]]）。
- *Like* 用作连词是非正式的；在正式书面语中用 *as* 或 *the way*。
- 在正式书面语中，当从句主语与主句主语相同时，可以把从句简化（*Although tired, …*、*If necessary, …*）。
- 每一处连接只用一个关联词：✗ *although … but*、✗ *because … so*。汉语把从句放在前面；英语允许你把新信息放在末尾。
:::

::: exercise 选择时间从属连词 {level=1}
填入 *until*、*by the time*、*once*、*since* 或 *whenever*。每个只用一次。(a) *I've known her ___ we were at primary school.* (b) *Wait here ___ I come back.* (c) *___ I hear this song, I think of my grandmother.* (d) *___ you've registered, you can borrow books.* (e) *___ the ambulance arrived, the man had stood up and walked away.*
::: solution
(a) *since* (b) *until* (c) *Whenever* (d) *Once* (e) *By the time*

::: exercise 用 *even if* 还是 *even though*？ {level=1}
(a) *___ she's only fifteen, she's already at university.* (b) *I'm going to the party ___ I'm not invited — well, if I'm not, I'll go anyway.* (c) *___ you apologise, I won't forgive you.* (d) *He didn't stop ___ he saw the red light.* (e) *___ we left now, we'd still be late.*
::: solution
(a) *Even though*（事实）。(b) *even if*（可能性——说话人不知道）。(c) *Even if* (d) *even though* (e) *Even if*（假设：*left … would*）。
:::

::: exercise 用 *so* 还是 *such*？ {level=1}
(a) *It was ___ a beautiful day that we went to the beach.* (b) *The soup was ___ hot that I burnt my tongue.* (c) *There were ___ many applicants that the interviews took a week.* (d) *They make ___ good coffee that I go there every morning.* (e) *He drove ___ carefully that we arrived late.*
::: solution
(a) *such* (b) *so* (c) *so* (d) *such* (e) *so*
:::

::: exercise 改正句子 {level=2}
改正错误。有一个句子是正确的。(a) *Because I was ill, so I stayed at home.* (b) *I'll phone you when I will get to the hotel.* (c) *Despite he had a cold, he went to work.* (d) *Bring a jumper in case it gets cold in the evening.* (e) *Even the weather was bad, they went climbing.* (f) *By the time we got to the shop, it already closed.*
::: solution
(a) *Because I was ill, I stayed at home.* / *I was ill, so I stayed at home.* (b) *… when I **get** to the hotel.* (c) ***Although** he had a cold, …* / ***Despite having** a cold, …* (d) 正确。(e) ***Even though** the weather was bad, …* (f) *… it **had already closed**.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 用所给的词改写 {level=2}
改写句子，不改变意思。(a) *She's very rich, but she isn't happy.*（*although*） (b) *I'll give you my number. You might need to contact me.*（*in case*） (c) *He left the light on because he didn't want the house to look empty.*（*so that*） (d) *It doesn't matter how much I eat — I never put on weight.*（*however*） (e) *I really like Paris, but I couldn't live there.*（*much as*）
::: solution
(a) *Although she's very rich, she isn't happy.* (b) *I'll give you my number in case you need to contact me.* (c) *He left the light on so that the house wouldn't look empty.* (d) *However much I eat, I never put on weight.* (e) *Much as I like Paris, I couldn't live there.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 简化分句 {level=2}
在可能的地方把状语从句简化。如果不能简化，说明原因。(a) *When you are in doubt, ask a member of staff.* (b) *Although he was exhausted, he finished the race.* (c) *Although the road was icy, the bus kept going.* (d) *If it is necessary, we can meet on Saturday.* (e) *While I was waiting, I phoned my sister.*
::: solution
(a) *When in doubt, ask a member of staff.* (b) *Although exhausted, he finished the race.* (c) 不能简单地简化：从句的主语（*the road*）不是主句的主语（*the bus*）。✗ *Although icy, the bus kept going* 的意思会变成公共汽车结了冰。(d) *If necessary, we can meet on Saturday.* (e) *While waiting, I phoned my sister.*
:::

::: exercise 翻译关联词 {level=3}
译成英语。选择最能表达分句关系的从属连词，汉语原文可能没有把这种关系明说出来。(a) 你不早点说，我已经买好票了。 (b) 再忙也要吃早饭。 (c) 他一到家就给我打了电话。 (d) 既然你来了，就留下来吃饭吧。 (e) 带上手机，万一迷路了可以打电话。
::: solution
参考译文：

(a) *I wish you'd said so earlier — I've already bought the tickets.* 或 *Why didn't you say so earlier? I've already bought the tickets.*（汉语原句是一种责备；用从属连词可能不是最好的选择。）

(b) ***However** busy you are, you should have breakfast.* / *Even if you're busy, …*

(c) *He called me **as soon as** (**the moment**) he got home.*

(d) ***Since** (**Now that**) you're here, stay for dinner.*

(e) *Take your phone **in case** you get lost — then you can call.*
:::

::: exercise 写一篇短报告 {level=3}
写一段100–130词的文字，比较你熟悉的两个地方（两座城市、两所学校、两个工作单位）。至少使用六个不同的状语从句：时间、原因、让步或对比、目的、结果各至少一个，外加一个简化分句。变换从句的位置。
::: solution
答案因人而异。参考答案：

*I grew up in Harbin, but I have lived in Guangzhou* ***since I started university.*** *The two cities could hardly be more different. Harbin is cold and dry,* ***whereas*** *Guangzhou is hot and humid for most of the year.* ***Although I love the snow,*** *I do not miss Harbin's winters, which are* ***so*** *long* ***that*** *spring seems to arrive in June.* ***Because Guangzhou is a trading city,*** *its streets are busy at all hours, and I have learned to leave home early* ***so that I don't get stuck in traffic.*** ***When asked which I prefer,*** *I say Guangzhou for work and Harbin for holidays.*

检查：时间（*since*）、对比（*whereas*）、让步（*although*）、结果（*so … that*）、原因（*because*）、目的（*so that*）、简化分句（*When asked*）。
