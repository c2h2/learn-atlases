星期一上午，周敏的经理对她说：

> *"I'm going to Singapore tomorrow. Can you send me the sales figures before I leave? And don't forget the meeting with the designers — it's been moved to Thursday."*

星期三，周敏把经理的话告诉一位同事。用汉语，她可以保留大部分原话：经理说他明天去新加坡……——只是“明天”现在不对了，她会把它改成“星期二”。在英语中，要改变的东西多得多：

> *She said **she was going** to Singapore **the next day** and asked **if I could send her** the sales figures before **she left**. She also reminded me **that** the meeting with the designers **had been moved** to Thursday.*

*I* 变成了 *she*，*me* 变成了 *I* 和 *her*；*am going* 变成了 *was going*；*tomorrow* 变成了 *the next day*；一个疑问句变成了 *asked if…*；*don't forget* 变成了 *reminded me that*。这些变化没有一个是机械的。每一个都源于这样一个事实：转述时的说话人、时间和地点与原话不同。好的转述还会选择转述动词——*said*、*asked*、*reminded*——来表明原话是哪一种言语行为。

这一课讲解转述陈述句、疑问句和命令句，时态的后移以及什么时候不需要后移，代词以及时间和地点词语的变化，还有一大类转述动词（*claim*、*deny*、*suggest*、*insist*、*admit* ……）及其句型。它以 [[complex-sentences/noun-clauses]] 中的名词性从句和 [[verb-patterns/object-and-verb]] 中的动词 + 宾语 + 不定式句型为基础。

## 直接引语和间接引语

::: definition 直接引语和间接引语 {#def-reported}
**直接引语**（direct speech）给出说话人的原话，放在引号里：*"I'm tired," she said.*

**间接引语**（reported speech，也叫 indirect speech）用转述者自己的话、从转述者的角度给出所说的内容，通常放在一个**转述动词**之后的从句里：*She said (that) she was tired.*

**转述动词**（reporting verb，*say*、*tell*、*ask*、*explain*、*claim* ……）描述说话这一行为；**被转述的分句**（reported clause）给出内容。
:::

直接引语用于故事、对资料来源的引用，以及会话中说话人想把某人说的话表演出来的时候。间接引语在概要、会议记录、新闻报道和学术写作中更常见，在这些场合内容比原话更重要。直接引语的标点——英国的出版物大多用单引号，美国的用双引号——见 [[sentences-and-paragraphs/punctuation#rule-quotes]]。

### *Say* 和 *tell*

- ***say*** + (*that*) 分句；如果提到听话人，需要用 *to*：*She **said** (**to me**) that she was tired.*（✗ *She said me*）
- ***tell*** + 听话人 + (*that*) 分句；听话人必须出现：*She **told me** that she was tired.*（✗ *She told that she was tired*）
- 不带听话人的 *tell* 只用于固定短语：*tell the truth*、*tell a lie*、*tell a story*、*tell the time*、*tell the difference*。

## 间接引语中的时态

::: theorem 时态后移 {#rule-backshift}
当转述动词是**过去**时（*said*、*told*、*asked*）时，被转述分句中的动词常常在时间上后退一步，因为原来的现在现在已经成为过去：

| 原话 | 在 *said* 之后转述 |
|---|---|
| 一般现在时：*"I **work** in Xi'an."* | 一般过去时：*She said she **worked** in Xi'an.* |
| 现在进行时：*"I'**m** **leaving**."* | 过去进行时：*She said she **was leaving**.* |
| 现在完成时：*"I'**ve finished**."* | 过去完成时：*She said she **had finished**.* |
| 一般过去时：*"I **finished** at six."* | 过去完成时或一般过去时：*She said she **had finished** / **finished** at six.* |
| *will*：*"I'**ll** call you."* | *would*：*She said she **would** call me.* |
| *can*：*"I **can** help."* | *could*：*She said she **could** help.* |
| *may*：*"I **may** be late."* | *might*：*She said she **might** be late.* |
| *must*：*"I **must** go."* | *must* 或 *had to*：*She said she **must** / **had to** go.* |

过去完成时以及 *would*、*could*、*should*、*might* 和 *ought to* 不变：*"I'd love to come"* → *She said she'**d** love to come.*
:::

::: theorem 什么时候时态不必改变 {#rule-no-backshift}
当被转述的情况在转述时**仍然真实**或**仍在将来**时，时态后移是**可选的**：

- *"I live in Xi'an."* → *She told me she **lives** in Xi'an.*（她仍住在那里）或 *… she **lived** in Xi'an.*
- *"The exam will be on Friday."* →（星期三转述）*The teacher said the exam **will be** on Friday.* 或 *… **would be** on Friday.*
- *"Water boils at 100 °C."* → *He explained that water **boils** at 100 °C.*

当转述动词是**现在时**、现在完成时或将来时时，**不**后移：*She **says** she**'s** tired.* *He**'s told** me he **can't** come.* *I'**ll tell** them you**'re** busy.*

当情况已经结束，或者转述者想表明这只是说话人的说法而不是事实时，保留后移：*He said he **was** innocent*（中性，或表示怀疑）——*He said he **is** innocent*（转述者承认这仍然是真的，或者至少他仍然这样声称）。
:::

::: proof
夸克等（Quirk et al.，1985）、赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆（Huddleston and Pullum，2002）以及斯旺（Swan，2016）对时态后移的处理相同：它不是一种自动的转换，而是从较晚的时间点进行转述的正常结果。如果原来的**现在**在转述时仍然是现在，那么用现在时同样正确，在会话中也很常见。赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆指出，后移的和不后移的两种说法在转述者承担什么责任上略有不同：用现在时暗示转述者也认为内容目前是真实的。一般过去时 → 过去完成时的后移最常被省略，因为没有它，事件的先后顺序通常也很清楚（Swan 2016）。“总是把 *will* 变成 *would*”这类规则是教学上的简化；把正确的、未后移的现在时判为错误的考官，是在运用一条语法书并不支持的规则。
:::

::: widget graph
title: 过去时转述动词之后的时态后移
nodes: am / is / are@-5,6; was / were@0,6; have done@-5,4; had done@4,4; did@0,4; will@-5,2; would@0,2; can@-5,0; could@0,0; may@-5,-2; might@0,-2; must@-5,-4; had to@0,-4

:::

::: example 后移还是不后移？ {#ex-shift}
在 *said* 之后转述每个句子，两种说法都可以时都给出来，并说明理由。(a) 昨天，阿里说：*"I'm learning Chinese."*（他现在仍在学。）(b) 去年，阿里说：*"I'm learning Chinese."*（他夏天就放弃了。）(c) 今天早上，天气预报员说：*"It'll rain this afternoon."*（现在是中午。）(d) 你的朋友说：*"The Earth is about 4.5 billion years old."* (e) 十分钟前，你的姐姐说：*"I've lost my keys."*（她还在找。）
::: solution
(a) *Ali said he **is** learning Chinese* 或 *… he **was** learning Chinese.* 两者都可以：这仍然是真的。

(b) *Ali said he **was** learning Chinese.* 只能后移：情况已经结束。

(c) *The forecaster said it **will** rain this afternoon* 或 *… it **would** rain this afternoon.* 下午仍在将来。

(d) *My friend said the Earth **is** about 4.5 billion years old.* 普遍的事实：后移听起来好像它已经改变了。（也可以见到 *was*，但用现在时更好。）

(e) *My sister said she **has** lost her keys* 或 *… she **had** lost her keys.* 两者都可以，因为情况仍在持续；现在完成时听起来更直接。
:::
:::

## 代词、时间和地点

::: theorem 改变视角 {#rule-deixis}
取决于**谁**在说话、**什么时候**说和**在哪里**说的词，要根据新的情况作出改变——不是自动地改，而只在情况已经改变时才改：

| 原话 | 转述（如果时间或地点已经改变） |
|---|---|
| *I*、*me*、*my*、*we*、*you* | *he*、*she*、*they*、*I* ……（视所指而定） |
| *now* | *then*、*at that time* |
| *today*、*tonight* | *that day*、*that night* |
| *yesterday* | *the day before*、*the previous day* |
| *tomorrow* | *the next day*、*the following day* |
| *last week*、*next week* | *the week before*、*the following week* |
| *ago* | *before*、*earlier* |
| *here* | *there*（或地点的名称） |
| *this*、*these* | *that*、*those*、*the* |
| *come*、*bring* | *go*、*take*（如果地点不同） |

如果转述是在同一天、同一地点进行的，*today*、*tomorrow* 和 *here* 不变：*She said she'd see me **tomorrow***（当天转述）。
:::

::: proof
这些词叫作**指示**（deictic）表达：它们的意义取决于说话的情境。斯旺（2016）和夸克等（1985）给出了通常的变化，两者都强调这些变化源于事实，而不是源于间接引语的某条规则。上表列出的是**常常**需要的变化，转述者应当始终问自己：从我现在所处的位置来看，指的是哪一天、哪个地方或哪个人？*The next day* 和 *the following day* 是中性的；在会话中，说话人通常直接说出是哪一天：*She said she was going on **Tuesday***。
:::

::: example 从不同的时间和地点进行转述 {#ex-deixis}
5月3日星期五，在成都，鲁思对你说：*"I arrived here yesterday, and I'm flying back to London tomorrow. Can you meet me at this hotel tonight?"* 转述她的话：(a) 当天晚上，在酒店大堂；(b) 两周以后，在上海。
::: solution
1. 找出指示词：*I*、*here*、*yesterday*、*tomorrow*、*you*、*this hotel*、*tonight*。
2. 对每一次转述都问：日子相同吗？地点相同吗？

(a) 同一天，同一地点：*Ruth said she **arrived here yesterday** and **is flying** back to London **tomorrow**, and asked if I **could** meet her at **this hotel tonight**.*——只有代词改变。时态可以不变，因为航班仍在将来；*could* 无论如何都用来转述请求。

(b) 两周以后，在别处：*Ruth said she **had arrived** in Chengdu **the day before** and **was flying** back to London **the next day**, and asked if I **could** meet her at **her hotel that night**.*——*here* 变成了地点的名称，*this hotel* 变成了 *her hotel*（或 *the hotel*），时间都移到了过去。在会话中，你大概会直接说出是哪一天：*… had arrived on Thursday and was flying back on Saturday.*
:::
:::

::: quiz
星期一，你的朋友说：*"I'll finish the essay by Thursday."* 现在是星期二。哪一个转述是**不**可接受的？
- [ ] *She said she'll finish the essay by Thursday.*
- [ ] *She said she'd finish the essay by Thursday.*
- [x] *She said she'll have finished the essay yesterday.*
- [ ] *She told me she would finish the essay by Thursday.*
::: solution
星期四仍在将来，所以 *will* 和 *would* 都正确；*told me* 也没问题。第三句改变了内容，并且把将来形式和 *yesterday* 混在了一起。
:::
:::

## 转述疑问句

被转述的疑问句是间接疑问句，遵循 [[complex-sentences/noun-clauses#rule-word-order]] 中的规则：陈述句语序，不用 *do*，一般疑问句用 *if* 或 *whether*。

- *"Where do you live?"* → *He asked me **where I lived**.*（✗ *where did I live*，✗ *where do I live*）
- *"Are you coming?"* → *She asked **if / whether I was coming**.*
- *"Who told you?"* → *They wanted to know **who had told me**.*
- *"Shall I open the window?"* → *He asked **if he should open** the window.*（提议）或 *He offered to open the window.*

转述疑问句的动词有 *ask*、*want to know*、*wonder*，在正式文体中还有 *enquire*（英式）或 *inquire*（美式和英式）。*Ask* 可以把听话人作宾语（*She asked **me** whether…*）；*wonder* 和 *want to know* 不可以。

## 转述命令、请求和建议

命令、请求、建议和警告通常用**动词 + 宾语 + 带 *to* 的不定式**来转述（[[verb-patterns/object-and-verb#rule-obj-to]]）。否定形式是 *not to*。

- *"Sit down, please."* → *She asked us **to sit** down.*
- *"Don't touch it!"* → *He told the children **not to touch** it.*
- *"You should see a lawyer."* → *My friend advised me **to see** a lawyer.*
- *"Remember to lock the door."* → *She reminded me **to lock** the door.*
- *"Don't go out alone at night."* → *They warned us **not to go** out alone at night.*

请求也可以用 *that* 从句加情态动词来转述：*She asked **if we could** sit down.* *Tell* + *that* 从句转述的是信息，而不是命令：*She told us **that** we **should** sit down* 转述的是建议；*She told us **to** sit down* 转述的是命令。

::: quiz
*"Please don't tell anyone," Kenji said to me* 的正确转述是哪一个？
- [ ] *Kenji said me not to tell anyone.*
- [ ] *Kenji asked me don't tell anyone.*
- [x] *Kenji asked me not to tell anyone.*
- [ ] *Kenji told to me not to tell anyone.*
::: solution
*Ask* + 宾语 + *not to* 转述否定的请求。*Say* 不能不加 *to* 就带听话人，也不用于宾语 + 不定式的句型；*don't* 不能跟在 *asked me* 后面；*tell* 直接带听话人，不用 *to*。
:::
:::

## 选择转述动词

*Say*、*tell* 和 *ask* 转述的是话语本身。其他转述动词转述的是说话人用这些话在**做**什么——许诺、拒绝、承认、抱怨——而且常常使转述更简短、更准确。每个动词都有自己的句型。

::: theorem 转述动词的句型 {#rule-reporting-verbs}
| 句型 | 动词 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 动词 + *that* 从句 | *add*、*admit*、*agree*、*announce*、*argue*、*claim*、*complain*、*confirm*、*deny*、*explain*、*insist*、*mention*、*point out*、*promise*、*reply*、*state* | *She **admitted that** she had made a mistake.* |
| 动词 + 听话人 + *that* 从句 | *tell*、*inform*、*assure*、*remind*、*warn*、*convince*、*persuade* | *He **assured us that** everything was fine.* |
| 动词 + 带 *to* 的不定式 | *agree*、*offer*、*promise*、*refuse*、*threaten*、*claim*、*decide* | *He **refused to** answer.* *She **offered to** help.* |
| 动词 + 宾语 + 带 *to* 的不定式 | *ask*、*tell*、*advise*、*encourage*、*invite*、*order*、*persuade*、*remind*、*urge*、*warn* | *They **urged me to** apply.* |
| 动词 + *-ing* | *admit*、*deny*、*mention*、*recommend*、*suggest* | *He **denied taking** the money.* |
| 动词 +（宾语）+ 介词 + *-ing* | *apologise for*、*insist on*、*object to*；*accuse* sb *of*、*blame* sb *for*、*congratulate* sb *on*、*thank* sb *for*、*warn* sb *against* | *She **accused him of lying**.* *He **apologised for being** late.* |
| 动词 + *that* + 虚拟语气 / *should* | *suggest*、*recommend*、*propose*、*insist*、*demand* | *She **suggested that** we **leave** early.* |

常见错误：✗ *suggest someone to do*，✗ *explain someone that*，✗ *deny to do*，✗ *accuse someone for*。见 [[verb-patterns/object-and-verb#rule-that-clause]]。
:::

::: proof
这些句型是斯旺（2016）和学习型词典中给出的，学习型词典在每个动词的词条中都列出了它的句型；COBUILD《语法句型》（*Grammar Patterns*）的动词卷（Francis, Hunston & Manning 1996）根据语料库证据把动词按句型分组。转述动词的选择从来都不是中性的。*Admit* 和 *confess* 把内容表述为真实的、说话人不愿意承认的；*claim* 和 *allege* 转述内容，但转述者并不接受它；*point out*、*show* 和 *demonstrate* 使转述者承认其为真（它们是叙实动词，[[complex-sentences/noun-clauses]]）；*insist* 暗示别人不同意。在新闻和学术写作中，这种选择是作者表明立场的主要方式之一（[[academic-reading/stance-and-hedging]]）。
:::

::: example 选择转述动词 {#ex-verbs}
用 [[#rule-reporting-verbs]] 中合适的动词转述每个句子，不用 *say*、*tell* 或 *ask*。(a) *"I'll pay you back on Friday, I promise," said Raj.* (b) *"I didn't take your phone!" said Lucy.* (c) *"OK, yes, I broke the vase," said my son.* (d) *"Why don't we take the train?" said Mei.* (e) *"Congratulations on your new job!" Grandma said to me.* (f) *"If you don't pay, I'll call the police," the shopkeeper said to the man.*
::: solution
(a) *Raj* ***promised to pay*** *me back on Friday.* / *Raj promised that he would pay me back on Friday.*

(b) *Lucy* ***denied taking*** *my phone.* / *Lucy denied that she had taken my phone.*（✗ *denied to take*）

(c) *My son **admitted breaking** the vase.* / *… admitted that he had broken the vase.*

(d) *Mei* ***suggested taking*** *the train.* / *Mei suggested that we (should) take the train.*（✗ *suggested us to take*）

(e) *Grandma **congratulated me on** my new job.*

(f) *The shopkeeper **threatened to call** the police if the man didn't pay.*
:::
:::

::: warning 不带宾语 + *to* 的转述动词
✗ *He suggested me to see a doctor.* ✓ *He suggested (that) I (should) see a doctor.* ✓ *He advised me to see a doctor.*

✗ *She explained me that the office was closed.* ✓ *She explained (to me) that the office was closed.*

✗ *He denied to have stolen the money.* ✓ *He denied stealing / having stolen the money.* ✓ *He denied that he had stolen the money.*

✗ *They accused him for cheating.* ✓ *They accused him **of** cheating.*
:::

## 口语、新闻和小说中的转述

**会话。** 说话人常常用直接引语转述，并且常常换成现在时，使故事生动（[[tense-and-aspect/tenses-in-texts]]）：*So she **says** to me, "Where have you been?"* 在非正式英语中，尤其是年轻人中间，*be like* 和 *go* 用来引出直接引语，甚至引出一个动作：*And I **was like**, "No way!"* *Then he **goes**, "Are you serious?"* 这些引述语在英式、美式和其他变体的非正式口语中已经很稳固，但正式书面语中不用。

**新闻。** 新闻报道使用 *say* 远比其他任何转述动词多，因为它是中性的；在行为本身很重要的地方，再加上准确的动词（*announced*、*denied*、*warned*）。作者没有证实的说法要加以标记：*according to*、*reportedly*、*is said to*、*is thought to*、*allegedly*。被动句型 *It is said that…* 和 *X is said to…* 见 [[verb-patterns/the-passive]]。

**小说。** 小说常常使用**自由间接引语**（free indirect speech），它有间接引语的后移时态和改变了的代词，但没有转述动词，并且保留人物自己的用词和疑问：*She looked at the clock. Where **was** he? He **had** promised to be home by six — and now it **was** nearly nine.* 读者通过叙述者的声音听到人物的想法。这在 [[reading-literature/narrative]] 中讨论。

::: example 用会议记录转述一次会议 {#ex-minutes}
为下面这段对话写一份正式的会议记录（用间接引语，不用直接引语）。**Chair:** *"Let's start. Has everyone read the report?"* **Dr Liang:** *"Yes, and I think the budget is too small. We can't finish the project on time without two more staff."* **Ms Brown:** *"I disagree. We've finished similar projects with fewer people."* **Chair:** *"Could you both send me your figures by Friday? We'll decide next week."*
::: solution
1. 选择能表明每位说话人在做什么的转述动词：开会、询问、论证、反对、请求。
2. 时态后移（会议记录是在以后阅读的），改变代词和时间词，疑问句用陈述句语序。

*The Chair opened the meeting and **asked whether** everyone **had read** the report. Dr Liang **confirmed that** she had and **argued that** the budget **was** too small: in her view, the project **could not** be finished on time without two additional staff. Ms Brown **disagreed**, **pointing out that** similar projects **had been** completed with fewer people. The Chair **asked** both members **to send** their figures by Friday and **said that** a decision **would be** taken the following week.*

注意 *the following week*（会议记录是以后阅读的），会议记录中典型的被动语态（*had been completed*、*would be taken*），以及最后宣布决定时用的中性的 *said*。
:::
:::

::: note 英汉对比
- **“说”对应 *say* 和 *tell*。** 汉语的“说”和“告诉”与 *say* 和 *tell* 并不一一对应：“他跟我说”是 *he told me* 或 *he said to me*。✗ *He said me* 来自把 *say* 当成“告诉”来用。记住：*tell* someone，*say* something（*to* someone）。
- **汉语没有时态后移。** 汉语动词没有时，所以转述时动词不变，只调整时间词：“他说他明天来”在以后转述时可以说成“他说他第二天来”。英语还要加上时态的变化，并且要作出选择：情况已经结束就后移，情况仍然真实则可后移可不后移。
- **汉语口语中直接引语和间接引语混同。** 在汉语口语中，“他说我明天来”有歧义：它可以表示‘he said, "I'll come tomorrow"’（直接引语），也可以表示‘he said that I would come tomorrow’（间接引语）。汉语书面语用引号（“……”）并在“说”后加冒号来标记直接引语，但在口语中两者常常混在一起。英语通过代词、时态和语序把两者区分开，所以转述者必须决定指的是哪一种。
- **疑问句。** 汉语转述的疑问句保持直接疑问句的语序（他问我住在哪儿），英语则不然（[[complex-sentences/noun-clauses]]）。汉语的“问”+“是不是”/“吗”对应于 *ask if / whether*。
- **用“让”和“叫”的命令。** “老师让我们明天交作业”是一个转述的命令：*The teacher told us to hand in our homework the next day*——*tell* + 宾语 + 带 *to* 的不定式。
:::

::: history
转述话语长期以来一直是学校语法的一部分，时态变化的“规则”在19世纪就已经是英语教学中的固定内容，与拉丁语严格得多的时态呼应规则并行教授。1912年，瑞士语言学家查尔斯·巴伊（Charles Bally）描述了法国文学中的 *le style indirect libre*——自由间接引语；叶斯柏森（Otto Jespersen）在《语法哲学》（*The Philosophy of Grammar*，1924）中以“再现引语”（represented speech）的名称讨论了英语中的同一种手法。后来，语料库语法显示了转述在不同语域中的差异有多大：比伯等（Biber et al.，1999）发现，在会话和新闻中，*say* 是迄今为止最常用的转述动词。1990年，卡尔·布莱斯（Carl Blyth）、西格丽德·雷克滕瓦尔德（Sigrid Recktenwald）和珍妮·王（Jenny Wang）发表了第一项关于美国口语中新的引述语 *be like* 的研究，此后这种用法已经传遍世界各地的非正式英语。
:::

## 后续内容

在学术写作中用 *argue*、*claim*、*suggest*、*find* 转述资料来源中的话，在 [[academic-reading/paraphrase-and-quotation]] 和 [[academic-writing/citation-referencing]] 中讲授。小说中的自由间接引语见 [[reading-literature/narrative]]，会话中作为一种技巧的引述见 [[listening-and-conversation/storytelling]]。被动的转述句型 *It is said that…* 见 [[verb-patterns/the-passive]]。

::: summary
- **间接引语**从转述者的角度给出所说的内容；直接引语给出原话（[[#def-reported]]）。
- *Say*（*to* someone）+ 分句；*tell* + someone + 分句。
- 在过去时的转述动词之后，时态通常**后移**一步；过去完成时和 *would/could/might/should* 不变（[[#rule-backshift]]）。
- 当内容仍然真实或仍在将来时，时态后移是**可选的**；在现在时的转述动词之后不后移（[[#rule-no-backshift]]）。
- 只有当说话人、时间或地点已经改变时，代词以及时间和地点词语才改变（[[#rule-deixis]]）。
- 转述的疑问句用 *if*/*whether* 或 *wh* 词，并用**陈述句语序**；命令和请求用 *tell/ask* + 宾语 +（*not*）*to*。
- 转述动词有固定的句型（*deny* + *-ing*、*refuse* + *to*、*accuse* sb *of*、*suggest* + *that*/*-ing*），并能表明立场（*claim* 与 *point out*）（[[#rule-reporting-verbs]]）。
:::

## 习题

::: exercise 转述陈述句 {level=1}
在 *said* 或 *told* 之后转述每个句子，使用时态后移。(a) *"I'm tired," said Anna.* (b) *"We've already eaten," they told us.* (c) *"I'll be there at eight," Tom said.* (d) *"I can't find my passport," said my father.* (e) *"The train left ten minutes ago," the guard told me.*
::: solution
(a) *Anna said (that) she was tired.* (b) *They told us (that) they had already eaten.* (c) *Tom said (that) he would be there at eight.* (d) *My father said (that) he couldn't find his passport.* (e) *The guard told me (that) the train had left ten minutes before (earlier).*
:::
:::

::: exercise 转述疑问句 {level=1}
(a) *"Where are you from?" she asked me.* (b) *"Did you enjoy the film?" my friend asked.* (c) *"What time does the bank open?" I asked a passer-by.* (d) *"Have you ever been to Tibet?" he asked us.* (e) *"Who left the door open?" the teacher wanted to know.*
::: solution
(a) *She asked me where I was from.* (b) *My friend asked if / whether I had enjoyed the film.* (c) *I asked a passer-by what time the bank opened.* (d) *He asked us if / whether we had ever been to Tibet.* (e) *The teacher wanted to know who had left the door open.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 用 *say* 还是 *tell*？ {level=1}
(a) *She ___ me she was leaving.* (b) *He ___ that he was sorry.* (c) *Can you ___ me the time?* (d) *What did the doctor ___ to you?* (e) *Don't ___ lies.*
::: solution
(a) *told* (b) *said* (c) *tell* (d) *say* (e) *tell*
:::
:::

::: exercise 命令和请求 {level=2}
用 *tell*、*ask*、*advise*、*warn*、*remind* 或 *invite* 转述。(a) *"Don't swim here — the current is very strong," the lifeguard said to us.* (b) *"Could you speak more slowly?" I said to the guide.* (c) *"If I were you, I'd take the job," my uncle said to me.* (d) *"Would you like to come to dinner on Saturday?" the Wangs said to us.* (e) *"Don't forget to bring your ID," the receptionist said to the visitors.*
::: solution
(a) *The lifeguard warned us not to swim there because the current was very strong.* (b) *I asked the guide to speak more slowly.* (c) *My uncle advised me to take the job.* (d) *The Wangs invited us to (come to) dinner on Saturday.* (e) *The receptionist reminded the visitors to bring their ID.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 转述动词 {level=2}
用括号中的动词改写每个句子。(a) *"It wasn't me who broke the printer," said Omar.*（*deny*） (b) *"I'm sorry I'm late," said Ella.*（*apologise*） (c) *"You cheated in the exam!" the teacher said to Max.*（*accuse*） (d) *"I won't sign it," said the director.*（*refuse*） (e) *"Let's go for a walk," said my sister.*（*suggest*） (f) *"OK, I'll lend you the money," said Dad.*（*agree*）
::: solution
(a) *Omar denied breaking (having broken) the printer.* (b) *Ella apologised for being late.* (c) *The teacher accused Max of cheating in the exam.* (d) *The director refused to sign it.* (e) *My sister suggested going for a walk.* / *… suggested that we (should) go for a walk.* (f) *Dad agreed to lend me the money.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 改正转述 {level=2}
改正错误。(a) *She said me that she will call later.*（昨天说的）(b) *He asked me where did I buy my shoes.* (c) *The doctor suggested me to do more exercise.* (d) *My teacher explained us how the system works.* (e) *They told that the concert was cancelled.*
::: solution
(a) *She told me (that) she would call later.*（或 *said to me*）(b) *He asked me where I had bought (bought) my shoes.* (c) *The doctor suggested (that) I (should) do more exercise.* / *The doctor advised me to do more exercise.* (d) *My teacher explained to us how the system works*（仍然是真的，所以 *works* 可以不变）。(e) *They told us / They said that the concert had been cancelled.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 转述一段对话 {level=3}
把这段对话改写成一段间接引语，就好像你在一周以后把这件事讲给朋友听。至少使用四个不同的转述动词。**Landlord:** *"I'm putting the rent up next month."* **Wang Lei:** *"But you raised it in January! Why?"* **Landlord:** *"Costs have gone up. If you don't like it, you can leave."* **Wang Lei:** *"I'll pay the old rent until you fix the heating."* **Landlord:** *"All right, I'll send someone tomorrow."*
::: hint
想一想每个说话人在做什么：宣布、抗议、解释、威胁、许诺。
:::
::: solution
参考答案：

*Wang Lei's landlord* ***announced*** *that he* ***was putting*** *the rent up the following month. Wang Lei* ***protested that*** *he* ***had*** *already* ***raised*** *it in January and* ***asked*** *him* ***why.*** *The landlord* ***explained that*** *costs* ***had gone*** *up and* ***said*** *that if Wang Lei didn't like it, he could leave. Wang Lei* ***refused to pay*** *more than the old rent until the heating was fixed, and in the end the landlord* ***promised to send*** *someone the next day.*

注意 *the following month* 和 *the next day*（一周以后转述），以及简化为 *asked him why* 的疑问句。
:::
:::

::: exercise 转述中的立场 {level=3}
新闻报道可以从不同的角度呈现同样的话。把这句话转述三次：一次中性地转述，一次暗示转述者怀疑它，一次暗示转述者接受它。*The company spokesperson: "Our products are completely safe."*
::: solution
参考答案：

中性：*A company spokesperson **said** that its products **were** completely safe.*

怀疑：*A company spokesperson **claimed** that its products **were** completely safe.* / *The products are, **according to** the company, completely safe.*

接受：*A company spokesperson **confirmed** that its products **are** completely safe.* / *… **pointed out** that the products are completely safe.*

*Claim* 和 *according to* 使转述者保持距离；*confirm* 和 *point out* 把内容表述为事实。用现在时 *are* 而不用后移的 *were*，也暗示转述者接受这一说法目前是真实的。
:::
:::
