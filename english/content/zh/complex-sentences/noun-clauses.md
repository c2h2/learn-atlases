下面是学习者写的电子邮件中的四句话，每一句都容易看懂，但每一句都不太像英语：

- ✗ *Could you tell me where is the registration office?*
- ✗ *Is very important that every student attends the first meeting.*
- ✗ *I am not sure if or not the room is free.*
- ✗ *We discussed about that the price was too high.*

四句话都含有一个承担名词作用的分句：它是 *tell* 的宾语，是 *is important* 的主语，是 *sure* 的补足成分，是一个介词的补足成分。英语对这类分句怎样引导、采用什么语序、可以放在哪里有严格的规则——而这些规则与汉语不同：在汉语里，“我不知道他住在哪儿”和“他住在哪儿”语序相同，“很重要”也不需要主语。

这一课讲解四种限定名词性从句——*that* 从句、*wh* 从句、*whether*/*if* 从句，以及意为“……的东西”的 *what* 从句——还有 *it* 作为替代主语或替代宾语的用法。非限定名词性分句（*I want **to leave***、*I enjoy **swimming***）是 [[verb-patterns/ing-or-infinitive]] 和 [[verb-patterns/object-and-verb]] 的内容，这里只用来作比较。

## 什么是名词性从句

::: definition 名词性从句 {#def-noun-clause}
**名词性从句**（noun clause，也叫 nominal clause；赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆的**内容分句**，content clause，涵盖了其中的大部分）是承担名词短语作用的从句。它可以作：

- **主语**：***What he said** was true.*
- **宾语**：*I know **that she's right**.*
- *be* 的**补语**：*The problem is **that we have no money**.*
- **形容词**的补足成分：*I'm sure **(that) she'll come**.*
- **名词**的补足成分：*the fact **that he lied***
- **介词**的补足成分：*It depends on **whether it rains**.*

[[complex-sentences/clause-structure#rule-functions]] 中的替换测试可以识别它：名词性从句通常可以用 *it*、*that* 或 *something* 替换。
:::

根据引导词的不同，名词性从句主要分为四种：

| 种类 | 引导词 | 意义 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| *that* 从句 | *that*（常常省略） | 一个陈述或想法 | *I think **(that) it's going to rain**.* |
| *wh* 疑问分句 | *who*、*what*、*which*、*when*、*where*、*why*、*how* | 询问信息的间接疑问句 | *I don't know **where she lives**.* |
| *whether*/*if* 从句 | *whether*、*if* | 间接的一般疑问句 | *I wonder **whether she'll come**.* |
| 名词性关系从句 | *what*、*whatever*、*whoever*、*where* …… | “……的东西（人、地方）” | *I'll do **what I can**.* |

## *That* 从句

::: theorem *That* 从句的位置 {#rule-that}
*That* 从句可以作：

1. 表示说、想、知道和感觉的动词的宾语：*say*、*tell (someone)*、*explain*、*think*、*believe*、*know*、*realise*、*hope*、*feel*、*agree*、*suggest*、*notice*。*She **realised** that she had left her keys at home.*
2. *be* 的补语：*The trouble **is** that nobody told us.*
3. 表示确信和感情的形容词的补足成分：*sure*、*certain*、*aware*、*afraid*、*glad*、*sorry*、*surprised*、*disappointed*。*I'm **afraid** that we're fully booked.*
4. *fact*、*idea*、*belief*、*news*、*hope*、*claim*、*possibility*、*rumour*、*suggestion*、*evidence* 等名词的补足成分：*the **news** that the bridge was closed*。
5. 主语，但实际上通常把它移到句末，在前面用 *it*（[[#rule-it]]）。

*That* 从句**不能**跟在介词后面（见 [[#rule-preposition]]）。
:::

::: theorem 省略 *that* {#rule-that-omission}
*That* 可以省略：

- 在常用的表示说和想的动词之后，尤其是 *think*、*say*、*know*、*suppose*、*hope*、*guess*：*I think __ you're right.* *She said __ she'd be late.*
- 在表示感情和确信的形容词之后：*I'm sure __ it'll be fine.* *I'm glad __ you came.*

*That* 通常保留：

- 当从句作**主语**时：***That** he was guilty was clear to everyone.*（✗ *He was guilty was clear*）
- 在**名词**之后：*the fact **that** he was guilty*，*the idea **that** we could win*
- 在不太常用的或正式的动词之后：*The report **states that** …*，*She **argued that** …*
- 当动词和从句之间隔着其他词时：*He told me **on Friday that** he was leaving.*
- 一般在正式的书面语中。
:::

::: proof
比伯等（Biber et al.，1999）发现，在会话中，绝大多数 *that* 从句都省略了 *that*，而在学术文章中，大多数 *that* 从句都保留了它；在最常用的动词（*think* 和 *say*）之后，以及当从句的主语是一个短代词时（*I think __* ***it's…***），省略最为常见。斯旺（Swan，2016）和夸克等（Quirk et al.，1985）给出了同样的条件。在其他位置保留 *that* 是出于理解处理上的原因：在句首、在名词之后或者在插入成分之后，读者需要一个信号，表明一个新的分句开始了。没有 *that*，*He told me on Friday he was leaving* 就可能被理解为 *He told me (something) on Friday* + 一个新句子。
:::

::: warning *That* 和 *what*
✗ *I think what he is right.* ✓ *I think (that) he is right.*

✗ *What I know is what he is right.* ✓ *What I know is **that** he is right.*

*That* 引导一个陈述，本身没有意义。*What* 的意思是“……的东西”（或者用来提问），它填补分句内部的一个空位：在 *I know **what** he wants __* 中，*what* 是 *wants* 的宾语。在 *he is right* 中什么也不缺，所以不能用 *what*。
:::

### 名词 + *that*：内容还是关系？

在名词之后，*that* 可以引导两种不同的分句。

- *The news **that the bridge was closed** surprised us.*——名词性从句：它说明消息的**内容**。消息 = *the bridge was closed*。这个分句是完整的；*that* 在其中不承担任何作用，也不能用 *which* 替换。
- *The news **that we heard __ on the radio** surprised us.*——关系从句：*that* 是 *heard* 的宾语，有一个空位，可以用 *which* 替换。

只有某些名词能带内容分句：即 [[#rule-that]] 中列出的表示说、想和事实的名词。✗ *the book that the bridge was closed* 是不可能的，因为书没有这种意义上的“内容”。

## 作替代成分的 *it*

::: theorem 先行词 *it* {#rule-it}
1. **主语。** 作主语的 *that* 从句、带 *to* 的不定式分句或 *wh* 从句通常被移到句末，原来的位置由 ***it*** 占据：*It's clear **that she's upset**.*（而不说 *That she's upset is clear.*）*It doesn't matter **what you wear**.* *It's important **to arrive early**.* 这叫作**外置**（extraposition）。
2. **宾语。** 在 *find*、*make*、*think*、*consider* 和 *take* + 形容词或名词之后，必须把分句移到句末，用 *it* 作宾语：*I find **it** strange **that nobody called**.* *She made **it** clear **that she disagreed**.*（✗ *She made clear that…* 很常见，但语法书和编辑更喜欢 *made it clear*；✗ *I find strange that…*）
3. **Seem、appear、happen。** 这些动词用 *it* + *that*：*It seems **that** the train is late.*（或者说 *The train seems to be late*。）

英语需要这个 *it*，因为每个限定分句都需要主语（[[core-grammar/sentence-patterns#rule-subject]]）。✗ *Is important that…* ✓ ***It** is important that…*
:::

::: proof
夸克等（1985）和赫德尔斯顿与普勒姆（2002）都把外置描述为正常的语序：处于主语位置的分句很重，而英语把重的成分放在末尾（[[complex-sentences/clause-structure#rule-main-visible]] 中的尾重原则）。比伯等（1999）发现，在每一种语域中，外置的 *that* 从句都远比作主语的 *that* 从句常见；处于主语位置的 *that* 从句只限于正式的书面语，在那里它可以与前一句衔接（*That the policy failed is now widely accepted.*）。这个形式上的 *it* 没有意义：它只是填补主语的位置。不要把它和 *there* 混淆，*there* 引出某物的存在（*There is a problem*）；也不要把它和分裂句中的 *it* 混淆（*It was Mei who called*，[[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]]）。
:::

::: example 把分句移到句末 {#ex-extraposition}
用先行词 *it* 改写每个句子。(a) *That the meeting was cancelled is a pity.* (b) *To finish the report by Friday will be difficult.* (c) *Whether we win or lose doesn't matter.* (d) *Nobody had told us. We thought this was strange.* (e) *Learning a language takes time. Everyone knows this.*
::: solution
(a) *It's a pity (that) the meeting was cancelled.*——分句一旦移到句末，在非正式文体中甚至可以省略 *that*。

(b) *It will be difficult to finish the report by Friday.*

(c) *It doesn't matter whether we win or lose.*

(d) *We thought* ***it*** *strange that nobody had told us.*（正式）/ *We thought* ***it was*** *strange that nobody had told us.*（中性）。用 *find*：*We found it strange that nobody had told us.*

(e) *It is well known that learning a language takes time.* / *Everyone knows (that) learning a language takes time.*——(e) 表明 *it* + 被动语态（*It is known that*）是先行词 *it* 的另一种用法，在学术写作中很常见（[[verb-patterns/the-passive]]）。
:::
:::

## 间接疑问句：*wh* 从句和 *whether*/*if*

::: theorem 间接疑问句的语序 {#rule-word-order}
放在另一个句子内部的疑问句——**间接**疑问句或**嵌入**疑问句（indirect / embedded question）——采用**陈述句**的语序：主语在动词之前，不用 *do*、*does* 或 *did*。

| 直接疑问句 | 间接疑问句 |
|---|---|
| *Where **is the station**?* | *Can you tell me where **the station is**?* |
| *What **does she want**?* | *I don't know what **she wants**.* |
| *Why **did they leave**?* | *Nobody knows why **they left**.* |
| *How much **does it cost**?* | *Do you know how much **it costs**?* |
| *Who **called**?*（*wh* 词作主语） | *I asked who **called**.*（不变） |

句末用不用问号取决于**主句**：*Do you know where she lives?*（疑问句）——*I don't know where she lives.*（陈述句）
:::

::: proof
在直接疑问句中，英语把助动词放在主语前面（倒装），如果没有别的助动词就用 *do*。倒装是英语主句的标志；从句保持基本的主语–动词语序（Quirk et al. 1985；Swan 2016）。表中的例外是有规律的：当 *wh* 词本身是主语时，直接疑问句也不倒装（*Who called?*），所以什么也不变。在某些英语变体中，特别是爱尔兰英语和印度英语中，以及一般的非正式口语中，嵌入疑问句确实会出现倒装语序（*I asked him where was he going*），而且在这些变体中是系统性的；但在标准书面英语和考试中，要求用陈述句语序。
:::

::: warning 用 *Could you tell me…* 提出的礼貌疑问
✗ *Could you tell me what time does the museum open?* ✓ *Could you tell me what time **the museum opens**?*

✗ *Do you know where is the toilet?* ✓ *Do you know where **the toilet is**?*

✗ *I wonder why did she say that.* ✓ *I wonder why **she said** that.*

这类间接疑问句是英语中最常见的礼貌形式之一（[[pragmatics/requests-refusals]]），所以这个错误非常显眼。疑问已经包含在 *Could you tell me* 或 *Do you know* 里了；其余部分是陈述句。
:::

::: theorem 用 *whether* 还是 *if*？ {#rule-whether}
间接的一般疑问句用 *whether* 或 *if*。在 *ask*、*know*、*wonder*、*see*、*find out* 和 *check* 等动词之后，两者都可以用；*if* 在口语中更常见，*whether* 在正式书面语中更常见：*I asked **if / whether** the shop was open.*

**只能用 *whether*** 的情况：

1. 从句作**主语**，或者位于主句之前：***Whether** the plan will work is unclear.*
2. 在**介词**之后：*It depends **on whether** it rains.* *There was a debate **about whether** to continue.*
3. 在**带 *to* 的不定式**之前：*I can't decide **whether to go**.*
4. 紧接在 ***or not*** 之前：*I don't know **whether or not** she'll come.*（*I don't know if she'll come or not* 是可以的，*or not* 放在末尾。）
5. 在严谨的书面语中，作 *be* 的补语或名词的补足成分：*The question is **whether** we can afford it.* *the question (of) **whether** …*

在 *if* 可能被理解为条件的地方，也要避免用 *if*：*Let me know **if** you can come* 可以表示“告诉我你能不能来”，也可以表示“如果你能来，就告诉我”；*whether* 只有第一种意思。
:::

::: proof
斯旺（2016）和夸克等（1985）列出这些限制时用语几乎相同。这些限制源于 *if* 主要是一个条件连词：在条件从句不能出现的位置上（作主语、在介词之后、在不定式之前），表示疑问的 *if* 也不能出现。赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆（2002）指出，在非正式口语中，*if* 在其中某些位置上是可以接受的（*The question is if…*），但用 *whether* 总是稳妥的。
:::

::: example 只能用 *whether*，还是两者都可以？ {#ex-whether}
填入 *whether* 或 *if*。如果两者都可以，请说明。(a) *I'm not sure ___ I locked the door.* (b) *___ we go or not depends on the weather.* (c) *She asked me ___ to bring anything.* (d) *They argued about ___ the results were reliable.* (e) *Ask the driver ___ this bus goes to the airport.* (f) *I don't know ___ or not he got my message.*
::: solution
(a) 两者都可以——*if* 较不正式。

(b) *Whether*——从句作主语。

(c) *whether*——在带 *to* 的不定式之前。

(d) *whether*——在介词之后。

(e) 两者都可以。

(f) *whether*——紧接在 *or not* 之前。（如果语序是 *I don't know if he got my message or not*，就可以用 *if*。）
:::
:::

::: quiz
哪个句子是正确的？
- [ ] *Can you tell me how much does this coat cost?*
- [ ] *I wonder if or not they will accept the offer.*
- [x] *It's unclear whether the shop is still open.*
- [ ] *I don't remember where did I put my glasses.*
::: solution
其他三句都违反了间接疑问句的规则：需要用陈述句语序（*how much this coat costs*、*where I put my glasses*），而且 *if* 不能紧接在 *or not* 之前（*whether or not they will accept*）。

## *What* 从句和 *-ever* 从句

::: definition 名词性关系从句 {#def-nominal-relative}
**名词性关系从句**（nominal relative clause，赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆称之为**融合关系从句**，fused relative）是由 *what*、*whatever*、*whoever*、*whichever*、*where*、*when* 或 *how* 引导的分句，其中先行词和关系词融合为一体：*what* =“……的东西”；*whoever* =“任何……的人”；*where* =“……的地方”。

- ***What you need** is a holiday.*（= 你需要的东西……）
- *I'll eat **whatever you cook**.*（= 你做的任何东西）
- ***Whoever finishes first** gets a prize.*
- *This is **where I work**.*
:::

名词性关系从句看起来像间接疑问句，但意思不同：

- *I asked **what she wanted**.*——间接疑问句：“我问：‘你想要什么？’”
- *I gave her **what she wanted**.*——名词性关系从句：“我给了她她想要的东西。”

这种区别对翻译很重要：第一句是“我问她想要什么”，第二句是“我给了她她想要的东西”。

::: example 疑问还是“……的东西”？ {#ex-what}
判断每个黑体分句是间接疑问句还是名词性关系从句，并把句子译成汉语。(a) *I don't understand **what he means**.* (b) ***What he means** is that we should wait.* (c) *She asked **who was responsible**.* (d) ***Whoever is responsible** will have to pay.* (e) *Tell me **where you bought it**.* (f) *I'll put it back **where I found it**.*
::: solution
问一问这句话讲的是一个未知的答案（疑问），还是一个事物、人或地方（名词性关系从句）。用“……的东西”改述来检验。

(a) 两种理解都可以，而且结果是一样的：“我不明白‘他是什么意思？’的答案”——我不明白他是什么意思。

(b) 名词性关系从句：“他所指的东西”——他的意思是我们应该等一等。

(c) 间接疑问句：她问：“谁负责？”——她问谁负责。

(d) 带 *-ever* 的名词性关系从句：“任何负责的人”——不管是谁负责，都得赔。

(e) 间接疑问句——告诉我你在哪儿买的。

(f) 名词性关系从句：“我找到它的地方”——我会把它放回原处。

表示询问和不知道的动词（*ask*、*wonder*、*not know*、*tell me*）带间接疑问句；表示给予、做和放置的动词带名词性关系从句。
:::
:::

::: quiz
在哪个句子中，黑体分句是**名词性关系从句**（“……的东西”），而不是间接疑问句？
- [ ] *She wondered **what the letter said**.*
- [x] *She read out **what the letter said**.*
- [ ] *Nobody could tell her **what the letter said**.*
- [ ] *She asked **what the letter said**.*
::: solution
人朗读的是一个东西——“信上写的话”——所以这个分句是名词性关系从句。*Wonder*、*tell her* 和 *ask* 涉及的是一个问题的答案：“信上写了什么？”
:::
:::

*-ever* 词也可以引导让步状语从句（***Whatever** you do, don't panic*——“不管你做什么”），这些在 [[complex-sentences/adverbial-clauses]] 中讲解。

## 介词之后的名词性从句

::: theorem *That* 前面不用介词 {#rule-preposition}
介词后面可以跟 *wh* 从句、*whether* 从句或 *-ing* 分句，但**不能**跟 *that* 从句。

| 在名词或 *wh*/*-ing* 之前 | 在 *that* 从句之前：去掉介词 |
|---|---|
| *I'm aware **of** the risk.* | *I'm aware **that** it's risky.* |
| *She insisted **on** paying.* | *She insisted **that** she pay.* |
| *We were surprised **at** his reaction.* | *We were surprised **that** he reacted so strongly.* |
| *It depends **on** whether we have time.* | — |
| *We talked **about** what to do.* | *We agreed **that** we would…* |

如果必须保留介词，就在 *that* 从句前面加上 *the fact*：*Despite* ***the fact that*** *it rained, …*（✗ *Despite that it rained*）。少数固定的连词以介词 + *that* 结尾：*in that*（正式，意为“因为”）、*except that*、*now that*、*given that*、*in order that*。
:::

::: proof
夸克等（1985）和赫德尔斯顿与普勒姆（2002）都指出，英语介词不带 *that* 从句作补足成分（上面列出的少数固定说法除外），而 *insist on*、*aware of*、*afraid of* 这类动词和形容词的介词在 *that* 从句之前干脆消失。学习者的错误 ✗ *We discussed about that…* 把两个问题合在了一起：*discuss* 不带介词（[[verb-patterns/prepositional-verbs]]），而 *about* 后面不能跟 *that*。
:::

::: example 改正电子邮件中的名词性从句 {#ex-email}
改正错误。*Dear Mr Haddad, I am writing to ask if or not there are still places on the summer course. I would also like to know how long does the course last and what is the fee. Is important for me that the course includes speaking practice, because I am worried about that my speaking is weak. Could you also tell me that whether accommodation is provided?*
::: solution
1. *if or not there are* → ***whether** there are*（或 *if there are … or not*）。紧接在 *or not* 之前需要用 *whether*。
2. *how long does the course last* → *how long **the course lasts***——陈述句语序。
3. *what is the fee* → *what **the fee is***——陈述句语序。
4. *Is important* → ***It is** important*——先行词 *it*。
5. *worried about that my speaking is weak* → *worried **that** my speaking is weak*（去掉介词），或者 *worried **about** my speaking*。
6. *tell me that whether* → *tell me **whether***——只用一个引导词。

改正后：*Dear Mr Haddad, I am writing to ask whether there are still places on the summer course. I would also like to know how long the course lasts and what the fee is. It is important for me that the course includes speaking practice, because I am worried that my speaking is weak. Could you also tell me whether accommodation is provided?*
:::
:::

## *That* 从句中的动词：三种特殊情况

1. **在表示建议和必要的动词和形容词之后**——*suggest*、*recommend*、*insist*、*demand*、*propose*、*essential*、*important*、*vital*——*that* 从句中的动词可以用原形（虚拟语气）、*should* + 原形，或者普通的时态：*It's essential that he **be** / **should be** / **is** told.* 这一点在 [[verb-patterns/object-and-verb#rule-that-clause]] 中已经介绍过，在 [[complex-sentences/unreal-past]] 中有完整的讲解。
2. **在过去时的转述动词之后**，从句中的时态常常后移：*She said that she **was** tired.* 这是 [[complex-sentences/reported-speech]] 的内容。
3. **在表示情感和评价的说法之后**——*It's strange that…*、*I'm surprised that…*——英式英语常常用 ***should*** 表示一个想法而不是一个事实：*It's strange that he **should say** that.* 普通的时态（*that he said that*）也正确，在美式英语中是常用的（Swan 2016）。

### 事实和看法

有些动词把它们的 *that* 从句的真实性视为理所当然：*I **regret** that I told her*，*She **realised** that the door was open*，*He **knows** that she's right*。使用这些动词的说话人承认从句是真的。另一些动词则没有这种承诺：*She **believes** that the door was open*，*He **claims** that she's right*。语言学家把第一类叫作**叙实动词**（factive verb）。这种区别在转述别人的话时（[[complex-sentences/reported-speech]]）和在学术写作中都很重要：在学术写作中，*X **showed** that…* 表示作者认同，而 *X **claimed** that…* 则保持距离（[[academic-reading/stance-and-hedging]]）。

::: note 英汉对比
- **没有对应 *that* 的词。** 汉语没有引导陈述分句的词：我知道他来了，我觉得你说得对。这是说汉语的人在 *think* 之后省略 *that* 很少有困难的原因之一——但也是他们在英语需要 *that* 的地方，即名词之后和句首，也把它省掉的原因（✗ *The fact he lied…*，✗ *He lied was clear*）。
- **汉语疑问句保持原来的语序。** 在汉语中，疑问词停留在答案所在的位置（你住在哪儿？——我住在北京），嵌入疑问句的形式与直接疑问句完全相同：我不知道他住在哪儿。英语直接疑问句把 *wh* 词移到句首，并把助动词倒装；嵌入疑问句移动 *wh* 词，但**不**倒装。练习过 *Where does he live?* 的学习者会把倒装带进嵌入疑问句，而汉语没有给他们任何理由去预料到这一点。解决的办法是把嵌入疑问句当作陈述句：*where he lives*。
- **一般疑问句。** 汉语的嵌入一般疑问句用“A不A”形式或“是否”：我不知道他来不来，不清楚他是否同意。两者都对应于 *whether*：*I don't know **whether** he's coming*，*It's not clear **whether** he agrees.* ✗ *I don't know he comes or not comes.*
- **无主句。** 汉语的“很重要”或“看来他不会来了”不需要主语。英语需要一个形式上的 *it*：*It's important…*，*It seems (that) he isn't coming.* 汉语的“重要的是……”与 *What is important is…*（*wh* 分裂句，[[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]]）非常接近。
- **话题在前。** 汉语常常把一个分句作为话题放在句首：他为什么走，我不知道。英语为了强调也可以这样做（*Why he left, I don't know*），但普通的语序是把分句放在动词后面：*I don't know why he left.*
:::

::: history
对名词性从句——语言学中常称为**补足分句**（complement clause）——的研究在20世纪60年代迅速发展。彼得·罗森鲍姆（Peter Rosenbaum）的《英语谓语补足结构的语法》（*The Grammar of English Predicate Complement Constructions*，1967）是最早的专著之一，研究哪些英语动词带 *that* 从句、不定式和 *-ing* 形式。在一篇题为《事实》（“Fact”，1970）的有影响的论文中，保罗·基帕斯基和卡罗尔·基帕斯基（Paul and Carol Kiparsky）表明，预设补足成分为真的动词（*regret*、*resent*、*be aware*）在语法上的表现与不预设其为真的动词（*believe*、*claim*、*suppose*）不同，并引入了**叙实**（factive）这一术语。后来，《朗文英语口语和书面语语法》（*Longman Grammar of Spoken and Written English*，1999）的语料库统计显示，*that* 的省略与会话以及少数几个非常常用的动词有多么紧密的联系。
:::

## 后续内容

转述陈述和疑问要用 *that* 从句和间接疑问句：见 [[complex-sentences/reported-speech]]。*suggest* 和 *essential* 之后的虚拟语气见 [[complex-sentences/unreal-past]]。用于强调的 *what* 从句（*What I need is a rest*）见 [[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]]。关于转述研究成果以及在叙实动词和非叙实动词之间作选择，见 [[academic-reading/stance-and-hedging]]。

::: summary
- 名词性从句承担名词短语的作用：作主语、宾语、*be* 的补语，或者形容词、名词、介词的补足成分（[[#def-noun-clause]]）。
- 在常用的动词和形容词之后可以省略 *that*，尤其是在口语中；在名词之后、句首和正式书面语中保留它（[[#rule-that-omission]]）。
- 作主语的分句通常用**先行词 *it*** 移到句末：*It's clear that…*；*I find it strange that…*（[[#rule-it]]）。
- 间接疑问句用**陈述句语序**，不用 *do*：*Can you tell me where the station is?*（[[#rule-word-order]]）
- *Whether* 和 *if* 都可以引导间接的一般疑问句；作主语、在介词之后、在带 *to* 的不定式之前和在 *or not* 之前只用 *whether*（[[#rule-whether]]）。
- *What* =“……的东西”（名词性关系从句）；*that* 引导一个陈述，本身没有意义（[[#def-nominal-relative]]）。
- 介词后面从不跟 *that* 从句：去掉介词，或者用 *the fact that*（[[#rule-preposition]]）。
:::

## 习题

::: exercise 构成间接疑问句 {level=1}
用所给的词开头，把每个疑问句改写成间接疑问句。(a) *Where does Mr Tanaka work?* — *Do you know …* (b) *When will the results be published?* — *I'd like to know …* (c) *Is the museum open on Mondays?* — *Could you tell me …* (d) *Who broke the window?* — *Nobody knows …* (e) *Why did the train stop?* — *I wonder …*
::: solution
(a) *Do you know where Mr Tanaka works?* (b) *I'd like to know when the results will be published.* (c) *Could you tell me whether / if the museum is open on Mondays?* (d) *Nobody knows who broke the window.* (e) *I wonder why the train stopped.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 用 *that*、*what* 还是什么都不用？ {level=1}
在每个空格中填入 *that* 或 *what*，如果什么都不需要就留空。给出所有的可能。(a) *I knew ___ she wouldn't come.* (b) *___ she said made everyone laugh.* (c) *The idea ___ we could finish in a day was unrealistic.* (d) *Show me ___ you've written.* (e) *___ he was innocent soon became clear.*
::: solution
(a) *that* 或不用。(b) *What*。(c) *that*（名词之后的内容分句；通常保留）。(d) *what*。(e) *That*——作主语的 *that* 从句，需要 *that*（更自然的说法：*It soon became clear that he was innocent*）。
:::
:::

::: exercise 加上 *it* {level=1}
在需要的地方加上 *it*。(a) *Is a pity you can't come.* (b) *I find difficult to concentrate in the library.* (c) *Seems that the flight has been delayed.* (d) *She made clear that she was unhappy.* (e) *Doesn't matter who pays.*
::: solution
(a) ***It** is a pity …* (b) *I find **it** difficult …* (c) ***It** seems that …* (d) *She made **it** clear that …* (e) ***It** doesn't matter who pays.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 改正句子 {level=2}
改正错误。有一个句子是正确的。(a) *We need to discuss about whether we should hire more staff.* (b) *She was proud of that her daughter had won.* (c) *I'm not sure what is the answer.* (d) *Whether the plan will succeed depends on the budget.* (e) *Everybody is talking about if the company will close.* (f) *I'm worried about that I might fail.*
::: solution
(a) *We need to discuss whether we should hire more staff.* (b) *She was proud that her daughter had won.*（或 *proud of her daughter's win*）(c) *I'm not sure what the answer is.* (d) 正确。(e) *… talking about **whether** the company will close.* (f) *I'm worried **that** I might fail.*（或 *worried about failing*）
:::
:::

::: exercise 名词性从句还是关系从句？ {level=2}
这个 *that* 分句是名词性（内容）从句还是关系从句？试着用 *which* 来检验。(a) *The rumour that the factory would close spread quickly.* (b) *The rumour that she spread was untrue.* (c) *There is evidence that the drug works.* (d) *The evidence that the police found was destroyed.* (e) *He made the claim that he had been robbed.*
::: solution
(a) 名词性从句（谣言 = 工厂要关闭；不能用 *which*）。(b) 关系从句（*that* = *spread* 的宾语；可以用 *which*）。(c) 名词性从句。(d) 关系从句。(e) 名词性从句。
:::
:::

::: exercise 汉译英 {level=2}
用名词性从句把下列句子译成英语。(a) 我不知道他明天来不来。 (b) 他说的话没人相信。 (c) 很明显，这个计划行不通。 (d) 问题是我们没有足够的时间。 (e) 请告诉我会议几点开始。
::: solution
(a) *I don't know whether / if he's coming tomorrow.* (b) *Nobody believed what he said.* (c) *It's clear (that) this plan won't work.* (d) *The problem is (that) we don't have enough time.* (e) *Please tell me what time the meeting starts.* / *Could you tell me what time the meeting starts?*
:::
:::

::: exercise 改进段落 {level=3}
改写这段学生报告中的文字，使它正确、通顺。至少使用一个先行词 *it*、一个 *whether* 从句和一个 *what* 从句。*Is well known people who sleep less get ill more often. But is not clear the reason is. Some researchers think is because of stress, others believe that sleep itself protects the body. My question is if or not students know this. I asked twenty students how many hours did they sleep. That they said surprised me.*
::: hint
找出缺失的主语、倒装的嵌入疑问句、只能用 *whether* 却用了 *if* 的地方，以及需要 *what* 却用了 *that* 的地方。
:::
::: solution
参考答案：

*It is well known that people who sleep less get ill more often. However, it is not clear what the reason is. Some researchers think (that) it is because of stress; others believe that sleep itself protects the body. My question was whether students know this. I asked twenty students how many hours they slept, and what they told me surprised me.*

修改之处：先行词 *it*（两处）；*what the reason is*（陈述句语序，用 *what* 而不是 *the*）；*think it is*（补上主语）；用 *whether* 代替 *if or not*（作 *be* 的补语，并且在 *or not* 之前）；*how many hours they slept*（陈述句语序）；用 *what they told me* 代替 *That they said*（*what* =“……的东西”）。
:::
:::

::: exercise 写一封礼貌的询问信 {level=3}
你想参加一个周末摄影课程。给组织者写一封简短的电子邮件（60–90词），提出五个问题——关于日期、价格、器材、水平和证书——全部用间接疑问句或名词性从句，不用直接疑问句。
::: solution
参考答案：

*Dear Ms Petrova,*

*I saw your advertisement for the weekend photography course and I would like to know* ***when the next course starts.*** *Could you also tell me* ***how much it costs*** *and* ***whether I need to bring my own camera?*** *I am a beginner, so I am not sure* ***if the course is suitable for my level.*** *Finally, I wonder* ***whether participants receive a certificate.***

*I look forward to hearing from you.*

*Best regards,*
*Chen Hao*

检查每个嵌入疑问句是否用了陈述句语序（*when the next course starts*，而不是 *when does the next course start*），以及在不能用 *if* 的地方是否用了 *whether*。
