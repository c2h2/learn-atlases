含有“直到……才”的汉语句子是翻译中常见的难题：

> 直到半夜他才回家。

逐字翻译得出 ✗ *Until midnight he just came home*，这不是英语。*He didn't come home until midnight* 是正确的，但失去了对时间之晚的强调。英语有两种结构可以保留这种强调：

- *It was **not until midnight that** he came home.*（分裂句）
- ***Not until midnight did he** come home.*（倒装——正式）

两者都把句子的一部分移到一个突出的位置上。英语的语序相当固定——主语、动词、宾语、状语——分句中最后一个重要的词通常承载主重音和新信息。为了让别的成分得到特别的分量，英语有一套专门的结构：用 *it* 和 *what* 构成的**分裂句**、**前置**，以及在某些词之后主语和动词的**倒装**。这些是严谨的口语，尤其是书面语的特征，因为书面语没有声音来表示强调。这一课说明这些结构怎样构成、起什么作用，以及各自属于哪种语域。

## 已知信息和新信息

::: definition 已知信息和新信息，句末焦点 {#def-focus}
在一个分句中，**已知**信息（given information）是听话人或读者从上下文中已经知道的东西；**新**信息（new information）是说话人补充的东西。英语倾向于把已知信息放在前面，把新信息放在后面。口语中的主重音通常落在分句中最后一个重要的词上，即**焦点**（focus）：*I bought a new **bike**.* 这种倾向叫作**句末焦点**（end-focus）。

与之相关的一种倾向是**尾重**（end-weight），即把长而复杂的成分放在靠近末尾的地方（[[complex-sentences/clause-structure#rule-main-visible]]）。
:::

普通英语在口语中通过移动重音来表示强调：*I bought a new bike*（不是别人），*I bought a **new** bike*（不是二手的）。在书面语中这做不到，本课中的结构通过改变词序来完成同样的任务。被动语态就是这样一种工具，它把动作的执行者放在最后（[[verb-patterns/the-passive]]）；分裂句和倒装是另外几种。

## *It* 分裂句

::: definition *It* 分裂句 {#def-it-cleft}
***it* 分裂句**（*it*-cleft）把一个分句分成两部分：

*It* + *be* + **焦点** + *that*/*who* + 分句的其余部分

*Mei broke the vase yesterday.* →

- *It was **Mei** who broke the vase yesterday.*（不是汤姆）
- *It was **the vase** that Mei broke yesterday.*（不是那个碗）
- *It was **yesterday** that Mei broke the vase.*（不是今天）

焦点是新的或用于对比的部分；*that* 分句包含被当作已知的内容（这里是：某人在某个时候打破了某样东西）。
:::

::: theorem 构成 *it* 分裂句 {#rule-it-cleft}
1. 焦点可以是名词短语（主语或宾语）、介词短语或状语，包括状语从句：*It was **because he was ill** that he missed the exam.* 焦点通常不能是动词：要强调一个动作，用 *wh* 分裂句（[[#rule-wh-cleft]]）。
2. *Be* 通常是 *is* 或 *was*（与分句的时间一致），也可以带情态动词：*It **might have been** Mei who broke it.*
3. 焦点是人时用 ***who*** 或 ***that***，其他焦点用 ***that***。焦点不是主语时，在非正式用法中可以省略 *that*：*It was the vase __ she broke.*
4. **一致**：*that* 分句中的动词与焦点一致：*It is **the children** who **are** suffering.* 用代词时，*It's **me** who's wrong* 是正常的说法；*It is **I** who **am** wrong* 非常正式。
5. ***It was not until** X **that** …* 把一个时间作为焦点，表示事件直到那时才发生：*It was not until 2010 that the bridge was finished.*
:::

::: proof
夸克等（Quirk et al.，1985）、比伯等（Biber et al.，1999）以及赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆（Huddleston and Pullum，2002）都是这样描述 *it* 分裂句的。它的作用是把分句中的一个部分标记为焦点——通常是与另一种可能相对比——同时把其余部分表述为已知的或预设的。这就是为什么 *It was Mei who broke the vase* 暗含有人打破了花瓶（一个预设），并说明是谁打破的。比伯等发现，*it* 分裂句在书面语中，尤其是在新闻和学术文章中，比在会话中更常见；在会话中，同样的对比通常只用重音来表示。带 *not until* 的分裂句是英语表达“直到……才”的一种常见而自然的方式。
:::

::: example 选择焦点 {#ex-it-cleft}
用一个 *it* 分裂句回应每个陈述，纠正其中的错误。(a) *"Li Na won the prize, didn't she?"*（不——是陈杰。）(b) *"You lost your keys on the bus?"*（不——是在火车上。）(c) *"Shakespeare wrote Don Quixote."*（不——是塞万提斯。）(d) *"You decided to leave because of the money?"*（不——是因为人。）(e) *"The museum opened in 1990?"*（不——直到2005年。）
::: solution
1. 错误的信息成为焦点，放在 *It was* 之后。
2. 句子的其余部分是双方都接受的，放进 *that*/*who* 分句。

(a) *No, it was **Chen Jie** who won the prize.*

(b) *No, it was **on the train** that I lost them.*

(c) *No, it was **Cervantes** who wrote Don Quixote.*（莎士比亚和塞万提斯是同时代人——混淆是可以理解的。）

(d) *No, it was **because of the people** that I decided to leave.*

(e) *No, it **wasn't until 2005** that it opened.*

在口语中，被强调的词承载主重音；*who* 或 *that* 之后的分句说得较轻，因为它是已知的。
:::
:::

## *Wh* 分裂句

::: theorem *Wh* 分裂句 {#rule-wh-cleft}
***wh* 分裂句**（*wh*-cleft，也叫**准分裂句**，pseudo-cleft）把已知部分放在前面的 *what* 分句里，把新的部分放在 *be* 之后：

*What* + 分句 + *be* + **焦点**

- *What I need **is a holiday**.*
- *What surprised me **was his reaction**.*
- *What she did **was (to) call** the police.*——强调一个动作：*what … do* + *be* + 不定式（带不带 *to* 都可以）。
- *What happened **was that** the bridge collapsed.*——强调整个事件。

变体：**倒置的** *wh* 分裂句（*A holiday is* ***what I need.***），*all*（*All I want is some sleep* = 唯一的东西），以及用 *the thing*、*the reason*、*the place*、*the person*、*the one* 代替 *what*：*The thing I don't understand is why she left.* *The reason I'm calling is to ask about the job.*
:::

::: proof
赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆（2002）把这类句子叫作**准分裂句**（pseudo-cleft），夸克等（1985）把它们与 *it* 分裂句放在一起描述。由于 *what* 分句在前，*wh* 分裂句会制造期待：听话人知道有东西要来，于是等着它。这使它在口头解释和演讲中很常见（*What we're going to look at today is…*）；在语料库统计中，*wh* 分裂句在会话中相对比 *it* 分裂句更常见（Biber et al. 1999）。带 *do* 的句型是强调一个动作的通常方式。*What* 指事物，不指人：指人用 *the person who* 或 *it* 分裂句（*It was the doctor who…*，而不说 ✗ *What told me was the doctor*）。
:::

::: warning *What* + 分句 + *is* …以及一致
✗ *What I need it is a holiday.* ✓ *What I need is a holiday.*（不要多加主语）

✗ *The thing what I don't like is the noise.* ✓ *The thing (that) I don't like is the noise.* ✓ *What I don't like is the noise.*

? *What we need are more teachers.*——当焦点是复数时，*is* 和 *are* 都可以见到；*is* 很常见，当焦点明显是复数时，书面语中通常用 *are*（Huddleston & Pullum 2002）。✓ *What we need is more teachers* 和 ✓ *What we need are more teachers* 都可以接受。
:::

::: quiz
哪个分裂句最适合回答 *"Why are you so happy?"* 这个问题？
- [ ] *It's my exam that I passed.*
- [x] *What happened is that I passed my exam.*
- [ ] *It's happy that I am because I passed.*
- [ ] *What I passed is my exam.*
::: solution
整个事件都是新的，所以焦点是整个分句：*What happened is that…*（或者干脆说 *I passed my exam!*）。第一句和第四句把 *my exam* 作为焦点，好像听话人已经知道我通过了某样东西；第三句试图强调一个形容词，而 *it* 分裂句不允许这样做。
:::
:::

::: example 从普通句到 *wh* 分裂句 {#ex-wh-cleft}
把每个回答改写成 *wh* 分裂句，选择合适的类型（*What … is*、*What … did was*、*What happened was*、*All …*、*The reason …*）。(a) *"Why do you look so worried?" — "I don't understand the instructions."* (b) *"How did you get in?" — "I climbed through the window."* (c) *"Why was the road closed?" — "A lorry overturned."* (d) *"Do you want anything else?" — "No, just a glass of water."* (e) *"Why did you choose this university?" — "Its library is excellent."*
::: solution
1. 确定焦点是什么：一个事物、一个动作、整个事件、一个限度，还是一个原因。
2. 把已知部分放进 *what* 分句（或放在 *the reason*、*all* 之后），然后是 *be*，然后是焦点。

(a) 一个事物：***What I don't understand is** the instructions.*（焦点是复数，也可以用 *are*。）

(b) 一个动作：***What I did was** climb through the window.*——*what … did* + *be* + 不带 to 的不定式或带 *to* 的不定式。

(c) 整个事件：***What happened was that** a lorry overturned.*

(d) 一个限度：***All I want is** a glass of water.*

(e) 一个原因：***The reason I chose it is that** its library is excellent.*（非正式地也说 *The reason is because…*，严谨的写作者认为它重复而避免使用。）
:::
:::

::: quiz
哪个句子**不**正确？
- [ ] *Down the hill ran the children.*
- [x] *Down the hill ran they.*
- [ ] *Down the hill they ran.*
- [ ] *There goes the last train.*
::: solution
当主语是代词时，地点或方向状语之后不发生完全倒装：*Down the hill they ran*。主语是名词时（*the children*、*the last train*），倒装是正确的。
:::
:::

## 前置

**前置**（fronting）把通常位于动词之后的成分移到分句开头，不作其他任何改变。

- **宾语和补语**，用于对比或与前文衔接：*Some of the questions I could answer; **the others** I had never seen before.* ***That** I can't believe.*——这种用法不常见，富于表现力；在大多数语境中，正常的语序更好。
- 时间、地点和方式**状语**，非常常见，用来设定场景或连接句子：***In 1998**, the company moved to Shenzhen.* ***Slowly**, she opened the box.*
- **比较级和 *more/most* + 形容词**，在正式书面语中伴随倒装：***More important** is the question of cost.* ***Most surprising of all** was his reaction.*

把整个分句前置，是把它作为话题的一种方式：*Why she left, nobody knows.* 正如 [[complex-sentences/noun-clauses]] 所指出的，这在汉语中很常见，在英语中则是有标记的。

## 否定词和限制词之后的倒装

::: theorem 否定倒装 {#rule-negative-inversion}
当一个**否定**或**限制**的词或短语为了强调而放在分句开头时，主语和助动词要**倒装**，就像疑问句一样。如果没有助动词，就加上 *do*。

| 前置成分 | 例子 | 正常语序 |
|---|---|---|
| *never*、*rarely*、*seldom* | ***Never have I** seen such a mess.* | *I have never seen …* |
| *hardly … when*、*scarcely … when*、*barely … when* | ***Hardly had we** sat down **when** the phone rang.* | *We had hardly sat down when …* |
| *no sooner … than* | ***No sooner had she** arrived **than** she had to leave.* | *She had no sooner arrived than …* |
| *not only … (but also)* | ***Not only did he** forget my birthday, **but** he **also** …* | *He not only forgot …* |
| *not until* | ***Not until** the next morning **did we** realise …* | *We didn't realise … until …* |
| *only* + 时间/条件/方式 | ***Only then did I** understand.* ***Only by** working together **can we** succeed.* | *I understood only then.* |
| *at no time*、*on no account*、*under no circumstances*、*in no way* | ***Under no circumstances should you*** *open this door.* | *You should not open this door under any circumstances.* |
| *little*（= 一点也不） | ***Little did she** know what was waiting for her.* | *She had no idea …* |
| *nowhere* | ***Nowhere** else **will you** find such prices.* | |

***not until*** 和 ***only*** + 分句之后，倒装发生在**主句**中，而不在前置的分句中：***Only when** the rain stopped **did we** leave.* ***Not until** I got home **did I** notice the damage.*
:::

::: proof
夸克等（1985）、赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆（2002）以及斯旺（Swan，2016）对否定倒装的描述相同，都把它标为正式或修辞性的用法，是书面语和演讲的典型特征。只有当否定或限制的意义作用于整个分句时，才会引发倒装。赫德尔斯顿和普勒姆把 *In no time **he had** finished*（不倒装：*in no time* 的意思是“很快”，分句是肯定的）与 *At no time **did he** complain*（倒装：他从未抱怨）相对比。同样的检验解释了为什么 *Not far away **stood** a hotel* 有另一种倒装（见下文），而 *Not long ago, **I** met her* 不倒装：*not long ago* 的意思是“不久前”。在会话中，通常用不倒装的说法；*Hardly had we…* 和 *No sooner had she…* 是文学用语。注意固定的搭配：*hardly/scarcely … **when***（或 *before*）和 *no sooner … **than***（✗ *no sooner … when* 有时也能见到，但被认为是错误）。
:::

::: example 用否定倒装写句子 {#ex-inversion}
用所给的词开头改写每个句子。(a) *I had never felt so nervous.*（*Never …*）(b) *We had just got home when it started to snow.*（*Hardly …*）(c) *She didn't only win the race; she broke the record.*（*Not only …*）(d) *You must not leave your luggage unattended at any time.*（*At no time …*）(e) *I didn't realise how ill he was until I saw him.*（*Not until …*）(f) *He had no idea that he was being followed.*（*Little …*）
::: solution
1. 把否定或限制的短语放在前面。
2. 把第一个助动词和主语倒装；如果没有助动词，就用 *do/does/did*。
3. 在 *not until* 和 *only* + 分句之后，在主句中倒装。

(a) ***Never had I** felt so nervous.*

(b) ***Hardly had we** got home **when** it started to snow.*

(c) ***Not only did she** win the race, **but** she **also** broke the record.*（加上了 *did*；第二个分句不倒装。）

(d) ***At no time should you** leave your luggage unattended.*（或 *must you*）

(e) ***Not until** I saw him **did I** realise how ill he was.*——在主句中倒装。

(f) ***Little did he** know that he was being followed.*
:::
:::

::: warning *Not only* 倒装与不倒装
✗ *Not only she speaks French but also German.* ✓ *She speaks **not only** French **but also** German.*（不倒装：*not only* 不在分句开头）✓ ***Not only does she** speak French, **but** she **also** speaks German.*

✗ *Only when I got home I realised.* ✓ ***Only when** I got home **did I** realise.* ✓ *I realised **only when** I got home.*

✗ *Never I have seen such a thing.* ✓ ***Never have I** seen such a thing.* ✓ *I have **never** seen such a thing.*
:::

### 前置的 *so* 和 *such*

***so*** + 形容词/副词或 ***such*** + *be* 位于句首时，主语和动词倒装，后面跟一个表示结果的 *that* 分句。两者都是正式的或文学性的：

- ***So strong was** the wind **that** trees were blown down.*（= The wind was so strong that …）
- ***Such was** the demand **that** the tickets sold out in an hour.*（= The demand was so great that …）

## 地点和方向之后的倒装

::: theorem 地点状语之后的完全倒装 {#rule-locative}
在位于分句开头的**地点**或**方向**状语之后，表示位置或运动的不及物动词（*be*、*stand*、*lie*、*sit*、*come*、*go*、*run*、*fly*、*walk*）可以放在主语**之前**。整个动词移动，不用 *do*：

- ***On the hill stood** an old castle.*
- ***Into the room walked** a tall woman in a grey coat.*
- ***Here comes** the bus.* ***There goes** our last chance.*
- ***Down came** the rain.* ***Off went** the lights.*

如果主语是**代词**，就不倒装：***Here it comes**.* ***Off they went**.*（✗ *Here comes it.*）
:::

这种倒装把一个新的参与者引到句末，即新信息所在的位置：它在描写和故事中很常见，而 *Here comes…* / *There goes…* 是日常的英语口语。前置的分词或形容词之后也有类似的倒装，把描写与前文连接起来：***Standing** at the door **was** a man I had never seen.* ***Far more serious was** the damage to the roof.*

其他倒装在别处讲解：***So do I*** / ***Neither can she*** 见 [[complex-sentences/ellipsis-substitution]]；条件句中的 ***Had I known…*** 见 [[complex-sentences/conditionals]]；还有故事中直接引语之后可选的倒装，*"Stop!" **shouted the guard***，主语是代词时不倒装（*"Stop!" **he shouted***）。

::: example 辨认倒装的类型 {#ex-kinds}
说出每个句子中倒装的类型，并说明它是正式的还是中性的。(a) *Rarely does the river flood in summer.* (b) *Here's your coffee.* (c) *Under the bridge lay a small boat.* (d) *Had we known, we would have helped.* (e) *"I'm Chen," said the new student.* (f) *I've never been to Tibet, and neither has my sister.*
::: solution
(a) *rarely* 之后的否定倒装，加了 *do*——正式。

(b) *here* 之后用 *be* 的完全倒装——中性，日常口语。

(c) 前置地点状语之后的完全倒装——书面语，描写性的。

(d) 不用 *if* 的条件倒装——正式。

(e) 直接引语之后的倒装——在故事中很常见（*said the student* 或 *the student said*）；主语是代词时不倒装。

(f) 省略式回应中 *neither* 之后的倒装——中性，在口语中非常常见。
:::
:::

## 增加强调的其他方式

- **强调的 *do***：*I **do** like your new haircut.* *She **did** tell you — I heard her.* *Do sit down.*（礼貌的坚持）。在口语中这个助动词要重读。
- 口语中的**重音**：*I didn't say **she** took it.*（而是别人拿的）——见 [[connected-speech/sentence-stress]]。
- **强化语和强调词**：*the **very** person I wanted to see*；*no reason **whatsoever***；*not **at all***；*What **on earth** are you doing?*；*I **myself** have never seen it*（强调的反身代词，[[verb-patterns/reflexive-reciprocal]]）。
- 为了修辞效果而使用的**重复和排比**，在 [[style-and-rhetoric/figures-of-speech]] 中讨论。

::: note 英汉对比
- **汉语是话题突出的语言。** 李讷和汤普森（Li and Thompson，1976）把汉语归为一种**话题突出**的语言，在这种语言中，把话题放在前面（这本书我看过；那个人我认识）是一种正常的、中性的结构。英语是**主语突出**的语言：把宾语前置是不寻常的，而且带有很强的对比意味。翻译“这本书我看过”时，用正常的语序 *I've read this book*，或者重读的 *I have read **that** one*，而不说 ✗ *This book I have read*（听起来带有对比或诗意）。
- **“是……的”和 *it* 分裂句。** 汉语的“是……的”常常强调过去事件的时间、地点或方式：我是昨天到的（‘it was yesterday that I arrived’）；他是坐火车来的（‘he came by train’）。只有当焦点带有对比意味时，英语的 *it* 分裂句才合适；通常英语用一个普通的句子，把重音放在焦点上：*I arrived **yesterday**.* *He came **by train**.*
- **“直到……才”和“只有……才”。** “直到……才”对应于 *not until*（*He didn't come home until midnight*；*It was not until midnight that…*；正式的 *Not until midnight did he…*）。“只有……才”对应于 *only if* / *only when*；当 *only* 短语放在前面时，主句要倒装：只有努力才能成功——*Only by working hard **can you** succeed.*
- **“一……就”和“不但……而且”。** “一……就”可以是 *as soon as*，在正式书面语中也可以是 *No sooner had … than* / *Hardly had … when*。“不但……而且”是 *not only … but also*，只有当 *Not only* 位于分句开头时才倒装。
- **“连……都/也”。** “连他都不知道”——强调一种出人意料的情况——是 *Even he doesn't know*；英语在汉语用“连”的地方用 *even*。
:::

::: history
**分裂句**（cleft sentence）这一术语是叶斯柏森（Otto Jespersen）在20世纪30年代提出的，他用它来描述英语怎样把一个句子分成两个分句，以突出其中的一部分。在布拉格，维莱姆·马泰修斯（Vilém Mathesius）和他的追随者研究了各种语言怎样安排已知信息和新信息的顺序，称之为**功能句子观**（functional sentence perspective）；1967年，韩礼德（M. A. K. Halliday）的《英语及物性和主位札记》（“Notes on transitivity and theme in English”）提出了关于已知信息、新信息和主位的分析，后来的英语语法都采用了这一分析。李讷（Charles Li）和桑德拉·汤普森（Sandra Thompson）的论文《主语和话题：一种新的语言类型学》（“Subject and topic: a new typology of language”，1976）把英语这样的主语突出语言与汉语这样的话题突出语言加以对比，这解释了为什么汉语只需把话题放在前面就能做到的事，英语需要专门的结构来完成。
:::

## 后续内容

段落中的信息顺序——把话题放在开头、把新的要点放在末尾——在 [[style-and-rhetoric/cohesion-emphasis]] 中讲授。条件句中的倒装见 [[complex-sentences/conditionals]]，简短的倒装回应 *So do I* 和 *Neither can I* 见 [[complex-sentences/ellipsis-substitution]]。口语中表示强调的重音和语调见 [[connected-speech/sentence-stress]] 和 [[connected-speech/intonation-meaning]]。

::: summary
- 英语把**新**信息放在最后（**句末焦点**）；需要时，专门的结构会移动焦点（[[#def-focus]]）。
- ***It* 分裂句**——*It was Mei who broke the vase*——强调一个名词短语或状语，把其余部分表述为已知的，在书面语中很常见；*It was not until … that* =“直到……才”（[[#def-it-cleft]]、[[#rule-it-cleft]]）。
- ***Wh* 分裂句**——*What I need is a holiday*、*What she did was call the police*——把已知部分放在前面，可以强调一个动作（[[#rule-wh-cleft]]）。
- 在英语中，**前置**宾语带有很强的对比意味；前置状语很常见。
- 在 *never*、*rarely*、*hardly … when*、*no sooner … than*、*not only*、*not until*、*only* + 短语、*under no circumstances*、*little* 之后用**否定倒装**：助动词 + 主语，需要时加 *do*；正式（[[#rule-negative-inversion]]）。
- 在 *only when* 和 *not until* + 分句之后，倒装**主句**。
- 地点和方向状语之后用**完全倒装**（*On the hill stood a castle*、*Here comes the bus*），但主语是代词时不倒装（[[#rule-locative]]）。
- 汉语的话题和“是……的”在汉语中是中性的；英语中对应的说法常常是带重音的普通句子。
:::

## 习题

::: exercise *It* 分裂句 {level=1}
把每个句子改写成 *it* 分裂句，强调黑体部分。(a) ***My brother** told me the news.* (b) *I met her **in Chongqing**.* (c) *We need **more time**, not more money.* (d) *He became famous **after his second film**.* (e) *The noise woke me up, **not the light**.*
::: solution
(a) *It was my brother who (that) told me the news.* (b) *It was in Chongqing that I met her.* (c) *It's more time that we need, not more money.* (d) *It was after his second film that he became famous.* (e) *It was the noise that woke me up, not the light.*
:::
:::

::: exercise *Wh* 分裂句 {level=1}
用所给的词开头，改写成 *wh* 分裂句。(a) *I really want a cup of tea.*（*What …*）(b) *His rudeness annoyed me.*（*What …*）(c) *She sold her car and bought a bike.*（*What she did …*）(d) *I only want to sleep.*（*All …*）(e) *I'm calling because I'd like to book a table.*（*The reason …*）
::: solution
(a) *What I really want is a cup of tea.* (b) *What annoyed me was his rudeness.* (c) *What she did was (to) sell her car and buy a bike.* (d) *All I want is to sleep*（或 *All I want to do is sleep*）。(e) *The reason I'm calling is (that) I'd like to book a table.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 倒装还是不倒装？ {level=1}
改正错误的句子。有两个句子是正确的。(a) *Never I have been so tired.* (b) *Here comes the bus.* (c) *Here comes it.* (d) *Only after the meeting I understood the problem.* (e) *Not only he is clever, but he is also kind.* (f) *Rarely do we see such talent.*
::: solution
(a) *Never have I been so tired.* (b) 正确。(c) *Here it comes.* (d) *Only after the meeting did I understand the problem.* (e) *Not only is he clever, but he is also kind.* (f) 正确。
:::
:::

::: exercise 否定倒装 {level=2}
用所给的词开头，以正式文体改写。(a) *As soon as I had closed my eyes, the alarm went off.*（*No sooner …*）(b) *You should not touch the exhibits under any circumstances.*（*Under no circumstances …*）(c) *We only realised the danger later.*（*Only later …*）(d) *I had never been so embarrassed.*（*Never before …*）(e) *He didn't apologise once.*（*At no time …*）
::: solution
(a) *No sooner had I closed my eyes than the alarm went off.* (b) *Under no circumstances should you touch the exhibits.* (c) *Only later did we realise the danger.* (d) *Never before had I been so embarrassed.* (e) *At no time did he apologise.*
:::
:::

::: exercise *Not until* {level=2}
用两种方法翻译每个句子：用 *not until* 加 *it* 分裂句，以及用 *not until* 加倒装。(a) 直到他走了，我才想起他的名字。 (b) 直到二〇一五年，这个村子才通了电。 (c) 直到考试前一天，他才开始复习。
::: solution
(a) *It was not until he had left that I remembered his name.* / *Not until he had left did I remember his name.*

(b) *It was not until 2015 that the village got electricity.* / *Not until 2015 did the village get electricity.*

(c) *It was not until the day before the exam that he started revising.* / *Not until the day before the exam did he start revising.*

普通的、中性的说法也值得掌握：*I didn't remember his name until he had left.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 哪种结构合适？ {level=2}
为每个语境选择最好的接续，并说明理由。(a) *Everyone thinks Zhang Wei designed the logo, but actually …* — (i) *Liu Fang designed it.* (ii) *it was Liu Fang who designed it.* (b) *Dear passengers, please note: …* — (i) *under no circumstances should bags be left unattended.* (ii) *never leave bags.* (c) *We walked through the forest for hours. At last we came to a clearing, and in the middle …* — (i) *a small wooden hut stood.* (ii) *stood a small wooden hut.*
::: solution
(a) (ii)——*it* 分裂句标出了与大家的看法之间的对比。（在口语中，(i) 重读 *Liu Fang* 也可以。）

(b) (i)——正式的告示用否定倒装表示严格的禁止；(ii) 不完整。

(c) (ii)——在描写中，前置的地点状语之后用完全倒装，把新的参与者 *a small wooden hut* 放在末尾。
:::
:::

::: exercise 改写以加强语气 {level=3}
改写这段演讲中的文字，使它更有力，使用一个 *it* 分裂句、一个 *wh* 分裂句、一个否定倒装和一个 *so … that* 倒装。保持原意。*The volunteers made this festival possible. They gave up their weekends. We have never had so much support. The response was very strong, and we have already started planning next year's festival. We need your ideas now.*
::: solution
参考答案：

*It was the volunteers who made this festival possible — they gave up their weekends for it. Never have we had so much support. So strong was the response that we have already started planning next year's festival. And what we need now is your ideas.*

检查：*it* 分裂句（*It was the volunteers who…*），否定倒装（*Never have we had…*），*so … that* 倒装（*So strong was the response that…*），*wh* 分裂句（*what we need now is…*）。在真正的演讲中，用一两个就够了；一连用四个修辞色彩非常浓。
:::
:::

::: exercise 话题、分裂句还是普通句？ {level=3}
译成自然的英语。判断每句英语需要分裂句、倒装，还是只需要一个普通的句子。(a) 这部电影我看过三遍了。 (b) 我是在网上买的。 (c) 连小孩子都知道这个道理。 (d) 只有在周末他才有时间陪孩子。 (e) 他一进门就开始抱怨。
::: solution
(a) 普通句：*I've seen this film three times.*（汉语的话题是中性的；在英语中前置 *this film* 会显得带有对比。）

(b) 普通句，重读焦点：*I bought it **online**.* 只有在纠正别人时才可以用 *it* 分裂句（*It was online that I bought it*）。

(c) *Even* + 普通语序：***Even** children know that.* / *Even a child knows that.*

(d) 倒装或普通句：*Only at weekends* ***does he have*** *time for his children.*（正式）/ *He only has time for his children at weekends.*（中性；美式说 *on weekends*）

(e) 普通句或正式的倒装：*He started complaining* ***as soon as*** *he came in.* / *No sooner had he come in than he started complaining.*（文学用语）
:::
:::
