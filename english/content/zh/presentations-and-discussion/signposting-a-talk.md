下面是一次关于城市如何降低夏季高温的讲话中间同一段四十秒的两个版本。内容完全相同，只有几个词不一样。

**版本 A**：*Trees lower street temperatures through shade and evaporation. In one study, streets with full tree cover were several degrees cooler in the afternoon. Trees take decades to grow. White roofs reflect sunlight. They can be installed in a day. Their effect is mainly on the building itself.*

**版本 B**：*So that's the first approach: trees, which cool the street through shade and evaporation. In one study, streets with full tree cover were several degrees cooler in the afternoon. The catch, of course, is that trees take decades to grow. Which brings me to my second approach — one that works much faster: white roofs. White roofs reflect sunlight, and they can be installed in a day. But — and this is the important point — their effect is mainly on the building itself, not on the street.*

读版本 A，你能弄清它的结构：你可以回头看，发现前三句讲的是树，后三句讲的是屋顶。听众却不能回头看。在版本 B 中，说话人结束了一部分（*So that's the first approach*），宣告了下一部分（*Which brings me to my second approach*），标出了一个对比（*The catch, of course*），并点明了关键之处（*and this is the important point*）。这几个词就是**路标语**，它们对讲话的作用，相当于标题、段落和粗体字对一页文字的作用。

本章按照[[presentations-and-discussion/planning-a-talk]]中的规划，讲授陈述中的路标语：引入、预告、开始、转换、离题和回到正题、强调、总结和作结论。书面段落内部的连接词——*however*、*moreover*、*therefore*——在[[essay-writing/signposting]]中讲授；这里的重点是口语，在口语中，路标语更频繁、更明确，而且既靠词语也靠声音来传达。

## 什么是路标语

::: definition 路标语 {#def-signpost}
**路标语**（signpost，或 signposting expression）是说话人谈论自己讲话的词或短语：他们要说什么、已经说了什么、各部分之间是什么关系、什么最重要。*I'll look at three things*、*Let's move on to…*、*To go back to my first point…*、*What I want to stress is…* 和 *To sum up* 都是路标语。语言学家把关于话语本身的语言称为**元话语**（metadiscourse）。
:::

路标语有大小两种。**宏观路标语**（macro-signposts）标示大结构：目的、预告、每个主要观点的开始和结束、总结和结论。**微观路标语**（micro-signposts）是连接句子的小词：*so*、*well*、*now*、*and then*、*right*、*OK*。

::: theorem 大路标语对听众帮助最大 {#rule-macro}
在讲话中，对听众——尤其是第二语言听众——帮助最大的路标语，是宣告和标示结构的**宏观路标语**。*so* 和 *well* 这样的小标记很自然，在节奏上也有用，但它们不能取代对讲话正在做什么的明确陈述。
:::

::: proof
肖德龙和理查兹（Craig Chaudron and Jack Richards 1986，*Applied Linguistics* 7:2）让第二语言听众听一段录音讲座的不同版本。带有宏观标记的版本——例如 *What I'm going to talk about today is…* 和 *Let's go back to the beginning* 这样的表达——提高了对讲座内容的回忆，而只有 *well*、*OK*、*so* 等微观标记的版本没有产生相应的提高。后来的研究（例如 Jung 2003，*Modern Language Journal* 87:4）也发现，明确的信号有助于第二语言听众回忆讲座内容。解释就是[[listening-and-conversation/how-listening-works]]中给出的那一个：听众构建意义部分是自上而下的，而宏观路标语提供了框架。微观标记是节奏和话轮的信号，不是内容的信号。
:::

## 讲话各部分的路标语

下面的说法按功能分组。你不需要全部用上；为每种功能选两三个你觉得自然的说法，反复使用，直到不假思索就能说出来。

### 引入题目和目的

- *Today I'd like to talk about…* / *I'm going to talk about…*
- *In this talk, I want to argue that…* / *What I'd like to do today is show you…*
- *The aim of this presentation is to…*（较正式）
- *I've been asked to give you an update on…*

### 预告结构

- *I'll look at three things: first…, then…, and finally…*
- *I've divided my talk into three parts.*
- *I'll start by…, then I'll move on to…, and I'll finish with…*
- *There are two questions I want to answer.*

### 开始第一个观点

- *So, let's start with…* / *Let's begin with…*
- *First of all, …* / *To begin with, …*

### 结束一个观点并转到下一个

- *So that's [the first point]. Now let's turn to…*
- *That brings me to my second point, which is…*
- *Moving on to…* / *Let's move on to…*
- *So we've looked at the problem. What about the solutions?*
- *OK — that's the background. Now for the main part.*

### 举例、补充、对比

- *Let me give you an example.* / *Take…, for instance.* / *A good example of this is…*
- *Another thing to bear in mind is…* / *On top of that, …*（非正式）
- *On the other hand, …* / *The catch is…* / *The problem with that is…*

### 离题和回到正题

- *Just as an aside, …* / *Incidentally, …* / *I'll come back to that later.*
- *Anyway, getting back to…* / *Where was I? Yes — the second reason.*

### 回指和预指

- *As I mentioned earlier, …* / *Going back to what I said about…*
- *I'll say more about this in a moment.* / *We'll come back to this when we look at…*

### 强调

- *What I want to stress is…* / *The key point here is…*
- *This is really important.* / *If you remember one thing from today, …*
- *And — this is the interesting part — …*

### 总结和作结论

- *So, to sum up, …* / *To summarise, …* / *Let me just recap.*
- *So, in conclusion, …*（较正式） / *To wrap up, …*（非正式）
- *So what does all this mean? …*
- *Thank you for listening. I'd be happy to take any questions.*

::: theorem 先结束旧的部分，再开启新的部分 {#rule-transition}
讲话中主要观点之间的过渡通常有**两半**：一个**结束**前一部分的短语，常常带一个简短的总结（*So that's how the heat builds up*），以及一个**开启**下一部分的短语，使用与预告中相同的标签（*Now let's look at what we can do about it*）。在短讲话中可以省去其中一半，但不能两半都省。
:::

::: proof
结束的那一半告诉走神的听众一部分已经结束，以及它归结为什么；开启的那一半告诉所有人讲话现在到了哪里。两者合在一起，相当于报告中两部分之间的空白和标题在口语中的对应物。使用预告中的标签使这张地图前后一致：如果预告承诺了 *three approaches — trees, roofs and water*，听众就可以逐一打勾：*That brings me to the third approach — water.* 换一个新标签（*Now, about fountains…*）会让听众怀疑这是第三个观点还是额外的内容。
:::

::: example 标出路标语 {#ex-label}
下面是医院药剂师 Ngozi Eze 面向新护理人员的一次简短讲话的开场和第一次过渡。在路标语下面画线，并说明每一个的作用。

*Good morning, everyone, and welcome. I'm Ngozi, one of the pharmacists here. What I'd like to do in the next fifteen minutes is show you how medicines are ordered on the wards, so that you know who to call when something goes wrong. I'll cover three things: how an order is made, how it reaches you, and what to do when it doesn't. Let's start with the order itself. … So that's how an order is made. You'll notice I've said nothing yet about urgent medicines — I'll come back to those at the end. Now, the second part: how the order actually reaches your ward.*
::: solution
- *What I'd like to do in the next fifteen minutes is show you…*——引入目的（并说明了时长，听众会感激这一点）。
- *I'll cover three things: …, …, and …*——预告结构，带三个平行的标签。
- *Let's start with…*——开始第一个观点。
- *So that's how an order is made.*——用一个总结结束第一部分（[[#rule-transition]]）。
- *I'll come back to those at the end.*——预指后面的内容，从而回答了听众可能正在形成的问题。
- *Now, the second part: how the order actually reaches your ward.*——开启下一部分，重复预告中的标签（*how it reaches you*）。

大约一百个词里有六个路标语——比书面文本会用的多得多——而说出来时没有一个显得多余。
:::
:::

## 声音也是路标

路标语的词语只是它的一半。听众也从声音中听出结构：开始新的一部分时，说话人会停顿、吸一口气，并在音高范围中从较高处开始；结束一部分时，说话人会稍稍放慢，让音高降到低处。这些“口语段落”——有时称为**语调段**（paratones）——即使听众没听清词语，也能让过渡被听出来。[[connected-speech/intonation-meaning#rule-range]]描述了调群从较高处开始如何标示某个内容是新的；在讲话中，同样的信号标示一个新的部分。

::: theorem 在宏观路标语前后停顿 {#rule-pause-signpost}
在宏观路标语之前要**停顿**——在讲话中，停一整秒或更久是正常的。把路标语放在单独的调群里说，起点比前面的话**更高**，调核落在新标签上：*‖ That brings me to my ↘**sec**ond point ‖ — ↘**cost**.* 总结之后，先让音高**降到低处**，再停顿。
:::

::: proof
没有停顿和音高重置，路标语就会和周围的句子连成一片，被听成又一项内容；有了它们，路标语就作为一个边界凸显出来。这也是念讲稿往往会抹平结构的原因：人们朗读时往往按标点而不是按意义停顿，自然讲话中标示各部分的长停顿常常被缩短（[[presentations-and-discussion/delivery]]）。停顿还给第二语言听众留出时间，在下一部分开始之前处理完上一部分。
:::

::: quiz
在一次预告为 *I'll talk about the causes, the effects and the solutions* 的讲话中，哪个过渡对听众最有帮助？
- [ ] *Moreover, solutions.*
- [ ] *Now let me talk about some other things.*
- [x] *So those are the main effects. That leaves the most hopeful part: the solutions.*
- [ ] *Furthermore, the third point is that we should plant more trees.*
::: solution
第三项结束了前一部分（*So those are the main effects*），用预告中的标签（*the solutions*）开启了下一部分，甚至说明了它为什么重要。*Moreover* 和 *Furthermore* 是在论证内部补充一个观点的书面连接词；它们不标示新部分的开始，在口语中听起来很生硬。*Some other things* 没有给出标签。
:::
:::

## 口语路标语和书面路标语

学习者从写作中熟悉的许多路标语，在讲话中听起来生硬或书卷气。口语偏爱较短、较个人化、带 *I*、*we*、*you* 和 *let's* 的形式。

| 书面（文章） | 口语（讲话） |
|---|---|
| *Firstly, … Secondly, …* | *First, …* / *Let's start with…* / *The first thing is…* |
| *Moreover, …* / *Furthermore, …* | *And another thing is…* / *On top of that, …* / *Also, …* |
| *In conclusion, …* | *So, to sum up, …* / *So what does this all mean?* |
| *As stated above, …* | *As I said a minute ago, …* |
| *This essay will examine…* | *I'm going to look at…* / *Today I want to show you…* |
| *It should be noted that…* | *What's worth noticing here is…* |

这是一种倾向，而不是规则：正式讲座或会议主旨演讲比团队简报使用更多的书面形式，*In conclusion* 在大多数讲话中也完全可以接受。但满是 *moreover* 和 *furthermore* 的讲话，听起来就像在念一篇文章——而它往往正是这样。

::: example 从书面路标语到口语路标语 {#ex-spoken}
Wang Jiahui 写了下面这段话，作为讲稿的一部分。改写它，使它听起来像口语，内容保持不变。

*Firstly, the causes of the problem shall be discussed. Moreover, it should be noted that the data were collected in 2023. Secondly, solutions will be proposed. In conclusion, it is hoped that the audience will understand the significance of the issue.*
::: solution
*I'll start with what's causing the problem — and I should say up front that the data I'm using are from 2023. Then I'll suggest a few solutions. And by the end, I hope you'll see why this matters.*

改动：被动结构（*shall be discussed*、*will be proposed*、*it is hoped*）变成了以 *I* 开头的句子，更短，听起来像一个人在说话；*Firstly … Secondly* 变成了 *I'll start with … Then*；*Moreover, it should be noted that* 变成了 *I should say up front*；*In conclusion*——这里用错了，因为这是预告而不是结论——变成了 *by the end*。这是一个预告，所以它应该出现在讲话的第一分钟里。
:::
:::

::: warning *Next, I will introduce…*
✗ *Next, I will introduce the second part. Next, I will introduce the data. Next, I will introduce our conclusion.*
✓ *That brings me to the second part.* / *Let's look at the data.* / *So what can we conclude?*

英语的 *introduce* 通常指把一个人介绍给别人、引入新事物（*introduce a law*，提出一项法律），或者第一次开启一个话题。每次过渡都用它——这是从“下面我来介绍……”带过来的常见习惯——会让讲话听起来很机械；而每个边界都重复 *Next*，只告诉听众还有别的内容要来，却没有说是什么。要变换路标语，并说出内容的名称。
:::

## 强调和重复

表示强调的路标语告诉听众，在听到的众多内容中该记住哪些。有两种语法特别有用。

- **wh- 分裂句**（wh-clefts）把重要的部分放在句末，这是英语放置新信息的位置：*What really matters here is the cost.* / *What surprised us was the speed.*（见[[complex-sentences/emphasis-inversion]]。）
- **直接称呼**听众，把他们拉进来：*If you remember one thing from this talk, let it be this: …* / *Notice that…* / *You might be wondering why…*

::: theorem 有意识地重复 {#rule-repeat}
在讲话中，**中心思想至少重复三次**——在开场、在主体中一个重要时刻，以及在结论中——并在预告、过渡和总结中**重复关键标签**。周围句子的措辞可以变化，但标签要保持不变。
:::

::: proof
读者错过一个要点可以重读；听众只有在说话人再说一遍时才能再听到它。因此，口语中的重复不是凑字数，而是口语中相当于重读的东西。保持标签不变很重要，因为一个先听到 *the cost*、再听到 *the financial side*、又听到 *the money question* 的听众，可能以为这是三件不同的事——在书面上，这种变化显得优雅；在口语中，它会造成混乱。标签周围的措辞可以而且应当变化，这样重复就不会听起来很机械。
:::

::: example 太多和太少 {#ex-balance}
下面是一次五分钟讲话中的两个过渡。说出每个过渡的问题，并写一个更好的。

1. *OK. So. Right. Now, as I said in my introduction, I'm now going to move on from my first point, which was about the causes, to my second point, which is going to be about the effects, which I will now talk about.*
2. *Effects. People get ill.*
::: solution
1. **路标语过多。**三个微观标记（*OK. So. Right.*），然后用三十个词来说“第一点讲完了，第二点开始”。在五分钟的讲话里，这占了明显的一部分时间，内容还没到，听众的注意力就下降了。更好的说法：*So those are the causes. What about the effects?*
2. **路标语过少。**只有一个光秃秃的标签，接着就是内容。正在记笔记的听众根本没听到这个过渡。更好的说法：*So that's why it happens. Now — what does it actually do to people? The first effect is on health.*

合适的量取决于讲话的长度和正式程度：四十分钟的讲座比三分钟的进展汇报需要更明确的路标语（以及讲话中途的小结）。
:::
:::

::: example 为一段小讲话加路标语 {#ex-minitalk}
给 Rahul Mehta 这段没有路标语的两分钟讲话加上路标语，使它有引入、预告、过渡、强调和结论。

*Our team wants to change the weekly report. The report takes about three hours a week to write. Most people only read the first page. We propose a one-page summary with a link to the full data. It would save time. People would read it. The data would still be available. We'd like to try it for a month.*
::: solution
*Good morning, everyone. I'd like to suggest a small change to the weekly report — and I'll keep this short. I'll explain what the problem is, what we're proposing, and why we think it'll work.*

*So, first, the problem. The report takes about three hours a week to write — and as far as we can tell, most people only read the first page.*

*That brings me to our proposal: a one-page summary, with a link to the full data for anyone who needs it.*

*Why do we think it'll work? Three reasons. It would save time. People would actually read it. And — this is the important part — none of the data would be lost; it would all still be there.*

*So, to sum up: shorter report, same data, more readers. What we'd like is to try it for a month and then review it. Thanks — any questions?*
:::
:::

- [ ] 说话人总结讲话。
- [ ] 说话人回答听众的一个问题。

::: note 英汉对比
- **“下面”“接下来”和 *Next*。**中文陈述常用“下面我来介绍……”或“接下来”在各部分之间转换。它们的直接对应说法 *Next, I will introduce…* 合乎语法，但反复使用就显得机械（[[#rule-transition]]）。要变换说法：*Let's turn to…*、*That brings me to…*、*Now, what about…?*
- ***Firstly … lastly*** 可以用，但在口语中 *first … second … finally* 或 *the first thing … the second thing … and lastly* 听起来更自然。✗ *At last, I will talk about the cost*——*at last* 的意思是“等了很久之后”（终于）；要用 *finally* 或 *lastly*。
- ***In a word*（总之）。**英语的 *In a word* 引出的是用一个词作的总结（*In a word: no.*）。总结一次讲话要用 *To sum up* 或 *So, in short*。
- **“好，那么”和 *OK, so*。**说任何语言的人都常用一个小词填充过渡。适度使用时，*OK, so…* 和 *Right, …* 是自然的英语口语；每句话前都重复，就成了听众会注意到的口头禅（[[#ex-balance]]）。
- **“我的报告就到这里”**与 *That's all from me* 或 *That brings me to the end of my talk* 对应得很好——但先要给出总结（中英文讲话都重视总结），再交接：*Thank you. I'm happy to take questions.*
:::

::: history
**元话语**（metadiscourse）一词通常归功于语言学家哈里斯（Zellig Harris），他在1959年用它指文本中评论文本本身的部分。这个概念在20世纪80年代被用于写作研究，尤其是范德·科普尔（William Vande Kopple，1985）的研究，并通过海兰（Ken Hyland）的《元话语：探索写作中的互动》（*Metadiscourse: Exploring Interaction in Writing*，2005）而广为人知。在口语方面，对大学讲座的研究——随着20世纪90年代起英国和美国录制讲座语料库而发展起来——显示了讲课人使用路标语之多；而对第二语言听众的研究，从肖德龙和理查兹（1986）开始，显示了明确的宏观路标语有助于理解。从那以后，学术陈述和商务陈述的教材一直在教授路标语。
:::

## 后续内容

承载路标语的声音——停顿、音高重置和语速——在[[presentations-and-discussion/delivery]]中展开。用于幻灯片和图表的路标语（*As you can see here…*）见[[presentations-and-discussion/visual-aids]]，讨论中的路标语（*Going back to what Ana said…*）见[[presentations-and-discussion/seminars-discussion]]。对听众来说，路标语是使记笔记成为可能的线索：比较[[listening-and-conversation/gist-and-detail]]。它们在书面上的对应物见[[essay-writing/signposting]]。

::: summary
- **路标语**是元话语：关于讲话本身的语言（[[#def-signpost]]）。
- **宏观路标语**——目的、预告、过渡、总结——对听众的帮助远远大于 *so* 和 *well* 这样的小标记（Chaudron and Richards 1986；[[#rule-macro]]）。
- 过渡要**结束**前一部分并**开启**下一部分，使用预告中的标签（[[#rule-transition]]）。
- 在宏观路标语之前**停顿**，并从较高处开始说；总结之后让音高降到低处（[[#rule-pause-signpost]]）。
- 口语路标语比书面连接词更短、更个人化：说 *Let's turn to…*，而不是 *Moreover, …*。
- 有意识地**重复**中心思想和关键标签；变换它们周围的词语（[[#rule-repeat]]）。
- 既要避免路标语过多，也要避免光秃秃的标签；不要在每个边界都说 *Next, I will introduce*。
:::

## 练习

::: exercise 把路标语和功能配对 {level=1}
把每个路标语和它的功能配对：引入、预告、转换、离题、回到正题、强调、总结。

(a) *Anyway, getting back to the budget…* (b) *The key point here is that nobody was hurt.* (c) *I've divided my talk into three parts.* (d) *Incidentally, the building is older than the university.* (e) *So, to recap: cheaper, faster and safer.* (f) *Today I'd like to talk about how we choose suppliers.* (g) *That brings me to the second question.*
::: solution
(a) 回到正题 (b) 强调 (c) 预告 (d) 离题 (e) 总结 (f) 引入 (g) 转换
:::
:::

::: exercise 改正路标语 {level=1}
改正或改进每个用于口头讲话的路标语。

(a) *At last, I will talk about the advantages.* (b) *Next, I will introduce the second part.* (c) *Now let's moving on to the results.* (d) *In a word, there are three main problems: cost, time and safety.* (e) *As I mentioned before at the beginning earlier, …*
::: solution
(a) *Finally, / Lastly, I'll talk about the advantages.* (b) *That brings me to the second part.* / *Let's turn to the second part.* (c) *Now let's move on to the results.* / *Now, moving on to the results.* (d) *To sum up, there are three main problems: …* (e) *As I mentioned at the beginning, …*
:::
:::

::: exercise 过渡的两半 {level=1}
为每一对部分写一个两半式过渡（[[#rule-transition]]）。

(a) 从 *why students skip breakfast* 到 *what it does to concentration*；(b) 从 *the history of the festival* 到 *how it is celebrated today*；(c) 从 *the results of our survey* 到 *our recommendations*。
::: solution
参考答案：(a) *So those are the main reasons students skip breakfast. Now, what does it actually do to their concentration?* (b) *So that's where the festival comes from. Let's look at how people celebrate it today.* (c) *So that's what the survey told us. Which brings me to what we think should happen next.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 改成口语 {level=2}
改写这段讲稿摘录，使它听起来像口语。

*Firstly, the background of the project will be presented. Furthermore, the methodology adopted will be explained. Thereafter, the findings will be discussed. Lastly, conclusions shall be drawn.*
::: solution
参考答案：*I'll start with a bit of background on the project, and then explain how we went about it. After that I'll show you what we found, and I'll finish with what we think it means.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 为离题加路标语 {level=2}
在一次关于在城市养蜂的讲话中，你想简短地提一下：据报道，在古代墓葬中发现的蜂蜜仍然可以食用；然后回到你的第二个观点 *where city bees find food*。写出三句话：出去、插话、回来。
::: solution
参考答案：*Just as an aside — honey keeps remarkably well; archaeologists have reported finding honey in ancient tombs that had not spoiled. Anyway, back to the bees themselves. My second point is where city bees actually find their food.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 标出声音 {level=2}
在下面这个过渡中，标出你会在哪里停顿（‖）、哪个音节带调核，以及音高在哪里从较高处开始（↑）：*So that's the problem. Now let's look at the solution. There are two.*
::: solution
*So that's the ↘**prob**lem. ‖ (pitch falls low) ↑ Now let's look at the so↘**lu**tion. ‖ There are ↘**two**.*——在 *problem* 之后完整停顿，*Now* 从高处开始，调核落在新标签 *solution* 上，在 *two* 之前短暂停顿，因为 *two* 是新信息。其他标法也可以接受，只要新部分之前的停顿最长，并且新标签带有调核。
:::
:::

::: exercise 修补一次讲话 {level=3}
下面是 Ivana Kovač 一次四分钟讲话主体部分的转写文本，题目是 *why our club should move its meetings online*（我们俱乐部为什么应该把会议改到线上）。运用[[#rule-transition]]和[[#rule-repeat]]，加上或改进路标语，至少加一处强调，并去掉多余的路标语。

*OK so right, um, the first thing. Members live far away. Some travel an hour. Moreover, the room costs money. Furthermore, it is often cold. OK so now next I will introduce point number two of my presentation which is the advantages of online. People can join from home. Recordings. Lastly, the disadvantages. Some people prefer meeting in person. Thank you.*
::: hint
找出这次讲话本该有的预告，并在每个过渡中使用它的标签。有没有总结？
:::
::: solution
参考答案：

*I want to suggest that we move our meetings online, and I'll look at three things: the problems with our current room, what we'd gain online, and what we might lose.*

*So, first, the problems with the room. Many of our members live a long way out — some travel an hour each way. The room also costs money, and in winter it's often cold.*

*That brings me to what we'd gain by going online. People could join from home, and — this is the part I think matters most — we could record the talks for anyone who can't make it.*

*Now, to be fair, there's a cost too. Some people really prefer meeting in person, and we'd lose some of the chat over coffee.*

*So, to sum up: less travel, no rent, recordings for everyone — but less face-to-face contact. On balance, I think it's worth trying for a term. Thank you — any thoughts?*

成串的微观标记（*OK so right, um*；*OK so now next*）去掉了；有了一个带三个标签的预告，每个过渡都重复一个标签，*Moreover* 和 *Furthermore* 被替换了，关键之处得到强调，结尾进行了总结并交接。
:::
:::

::: exercise 口语：一次带路标语的三分钟讲话 {level=3}
以 *a skill everyone should learn*（人人都应该学会的一项技能）为题，准备一次有三个主要观点的三分钟讲话。要用到：一句目的陈述、一个预告、每两个观点之间一个两半式过渡、一处插话、一处强调，以及一个重复预告中标签的总结。录下来，数一数你的路标语。然后再听一遍：过渡前的停顿是否比其他停顿更长？
::: solution
答案各异。检查录音中是否有：(1) 在头三十秒里有一个带三个标签的预告；(2) 两个过渡处各有结束的一半和开启的一半；(3) 一处出去和回来都有标示的插话；(4) 一处强调（*What I really want to stress is…*）；(5) 一个使用相同三个标签的总结；(6) 每个过渡前有一秒或更长的停顿，并从较高处开始。如果你在三分钟里数出大约十五个以上的路标语，就找找有没有可以去掉的成串微观标记。
:::
:::
