题目是：*Should cities make public transport free for all passengers?*（城市是否应该让所有乘客免费乘坐公共交通？）下面是这道题三篇引言的最后一句。

> **A.** *This essay will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of free public transport.*

> **B.** *Public transport is used by millions of people every day.*

> **C.** *Free public transport is a poor use of a city's money: the people it most needs to attract — drivers — care more about speed and convenience than about the fare, so the same money would cut more car journeys if it were spent on faster and more frequent services.*

A 告诉读者文章要讲什么，而题目已经说过了。B 陈述了一个没有人会争议的事实。只有 C 回答了问题：它表明了立场（*no*），以一种可以被反驳的形式给出了主要理由，并告诉读者文章的其余部分必须证明什么。C 是一个**论点句**。你可以不同意它——很多人都会不同意——而这恰恰是它成为论点的原因。

上一课 [[essay-writing/understanding-the-question]] 讲了怎样读懂题目、缩小话题。本课把答案变成论点句，并说明怎样写一篇把读者引向论点句的引言。论点句和引言合在一起，是读者看到的第一份证据，证明作者知道自己在想什么。

## 论点句

::: definition 论点句 {#def-thesis}
文章的**论点**（thesis）是它的中心主张：作者对问题的回答，文章的其余部分都在解释和支撑它。**论点句**（thesis statement）是表明论点的那一句话（偶尔是两三句）。在大多数英语文章中，它出现在引言里，通常在引言末尾或接近末尾的地方。
:::

这个词来自希腊语 *thesis*“放置，命题”：提出来要加以捍卫的东西。论点句对整篇文章的作用，就像主题句对一个段落的作用（[[sentences-and-paragraphs/topic-sentences]]）：它表明其他一切都在支撑的那个要点。

::: theorem 好论点的标准 {#rule-good-thesis}
一个好的论点句：

1. **回答所提出的问题**，而且按问题本身的要求回答（*to what extent* 要回答程度，*should* 要作出决定）；
2. **可争辩**（arguable）：一位通情达理、了解情况的读者可能不同意它，所以它需要支撑；
3. **具体**（specific）：它准确地说明主张什么、关于谁、到什么程度，而不是说 *has many effects* 或 *is a complex issue*；
4. **统一**（unified）：它的各部分合成一个立场，而不是几个互不相关的主张；
5. **能够支撑**（supportable）：在现有篇幅内，用作者实际能提供的证据来支撑。
:::

::: proof
文章是一个论证，而论证需要有所主张。一个无可争议的陈述（上面的 B）不需要写文章，一个只宣布话题的陈述（A）不让作者承担任何主张，所以读者无从判断主体段是否成功。具体性之所以重要，是因为模糊的主张无法检验：*Technology has many effects on education* 不管各段说什么都是对的。统一性之所以重要，是因为一个包含两个不相关主张的论点会产生两篇半截的文章。主张还必须与证据和篇幅相称：*Free public transport has failed everywhere it has been tried*（免费公共交通在所有试行的地方都失败了）所需要的证据，远远超过一篇1,000词的文章所能提供的；而 C 提出的是一个关于机制（司机在意什么）的主张，一篇短文可以用理由和几个例子来支撑它。
:::

::: example 改进薄弱的论点句 {#ex-weak-thesis}
说出每个论点句的问题并改写。题目是 *Should universities require all students to take a course in a foreign language?*

(a) *Learning foreign languages is very important in today's world.* (b) *This essay will look at both sides of this question.* (c) *Universities should require a foreign language course, and many students also need help with mental health.* (d) *Foreign language courses are good and bad.*
::: solution
1. **(a)** 没有回答问题（*should universities require it?*），而且太笼统，无法争辩：没有人会否认语言很重要。改写：*Universities should require a foreign language course only of students whose degrees involve international work, because for others the time is better spent on courses they choose.*
2. **(b)** 是一个预告，不是立场。改写为有立场的句子：*Universities should require every student to take one year of a foreign language, because it is the only way to make sure that students who would never choose it discover what they are capable of.*
3. **(c)** 不统一：心理健康是另一个话题。删掉第二个主张：*Universities should require a foreign language course, because learning a language develops understanding of other cultures in a way no other course does.*
4. **(d)** 很模糊：哪方面好，哪方面不好？改写，把程度说具体：*A compulsory language course benefits students who start with some knowledge of the language, but for complete beginners one year is too short to reach a useful level.*

四个改写代表四种不同的立场。它们的共同点是：每一个都回答了问题，而且都可能有人不同意。
:::
:::

### 开放式论点句和封闭式论点句

::: definition 开放式论点和封闭式论点 {#def-open-closed}
**封闭式论点**（closed thesis，有时称为带**文章路线图**的论点，essay map）表明立场，并按文章将给出的顺序列出主要理由：*Universities should record lectures, because recordings help students review difficult material, include students who cannot attend, and cost little once the equipment is installed.* **开放式论点**（open thesis）表明立场，但不列出理由：*Universities should record their lectures.*
:::

::: theorem 在开放式和封闭式之间选择 {#rule-open-closed}
1. 在短文和考场作文中，封闭式论点，或者开放式论点后面加一句勾勒文章结构的短句，可以帮助读者和阅卷人一眼看出结构。
2. 在较长的文章中，只列出论证的几条主线，而不是每个论点，并把论点句控制在一两句话以内。
3. 如果你列出了理由，主体段就必须按同样的顺序展开。
4. 论点句可以包含立场的主要限制（*although …*、*except …*）：这常常使它更精确，而不是更弱。
:::

::: proof
读者如果事先知道文章的安排，处理起来会更容易；引言末尾的“路线图”是英语说明性写作的一个由来已久的惯例，在古典修辞学中称为 *partitio*，即在论证之前宣布的主题划分。但一个句子里的长串列举会变得难读，在长文中这串列举还可能变成一张目录。让步从句（第4条）是一种尽早表明作者看到了另一面的方式：*Although free transport helps low-income passengers, it is a poor way to reduce car use*（虽然免费交通对低收入乘客有帮助，但它不是减少汽车使用的好办法）比单独的任何一半都更精确地回答了问题，而且为文章将要处理的反方论点做好了铺垫（[[essay-writing/counter-arguments]]）。
:::

::: quiz
对于 *To what extent has remote work benefited employees?*，哪一个是最好的论点句？
- [ ] *Remote work has become very common since 2020.*
- [ ] *Remote work has advantages and disadvantages for employees.*
- [x] *Remote work has benefited most employees in time and flexibility, but it has disadvantaged those early in their careers, who learn less without colleagues nearby.*
- [ ] *I will discuss the benefits of remote work in this essay.*
::: solution
第三个回答了程度问题（*most employees … but … those early in their careers*），具体说明了是哪种好处、对谁，而且可以争辩。第一个是事实；第二个是套在任何题目上都行的无效回答；第四个是预告。
:::
:::

::: warning 只预告，不论证
✗ *In this essay, I will discuss whether cities should make public transport free.*
✗ *This essay will explore the advantages and disadvantages of …*
✗ *There are many different opinions on this issue.*

这些句子重复题目或描述文章，而不是作出回答。一句告诉读者文章怎样组织的话，放在论点句**之后**是可以接受的——有时还很有用——在较长的学术文章中也很常见（*Section 2 reviews …*）：✓ *Free public transport is a poor use of a city's money … This essay first examines why fares matter less to drivers than speed, and then compares the cost of free fares with the cost of better services.* 薄弱的是**用预告代替**论点句。
:::

## 引言

::: definition 引言及其语步 {#def-intro}
文章的**引言**（introduction）为论点句做好准备并表明论点。它通常完成下面部分或全部**语步**（moves），大致按这个顺序：

1. **开头**（opening）：把话题和读者关心或熟悉的事物联系起来；
2. **背景**（background）：理解题目所需的语境；
3. **聚焦**（narrowing）：从总的话题收窄到具体的问题，包括界定关键术语或说明文章的范围；
4. **论点句**；
5. 可选：对文章结构的简短**概述**（outline）。

由于引言从一般走向具体，人们常把它比作一个**漏斗**（funnel）。
:::

::: widget graph
title: 引言的语步
nodes: opening@0,5; background@2,4; narrowing and definitions@4,3; thesis statement@6,2; outline of the essay@8,1; body paragraph 1@8,-0.5
edges: opening>background; background>narrowing and definitions; narrowing and definitions>thesis statement; thesis statement>outline of the essay; outline of the essay>body paragraph 1; thesis statement>body paragraph 1
caption: 典型文章引言的“漏斗”，从一般到具体。论点句是漏斗的窄口；文章结构的概述可以跟在它后面，也可以融入其中，第一个主体段从这里开始。在短文中，有些语步只是一个分句，开头和背景也常常合在一起。可以拖动各语步比较其他顺序。
:::

::: theorem 怎样写引言 {#rule-intro}
1. **篇幅**：在大多数文章中，约占全文的十分之一——短文两到五句，长文一两段。
2. **从贴近题目的地方开始。** 开头应该很快引到**这个**问题上，而不是引到世界史上。
3. **只提供读者理解论点所需要的背景。**
4. **以论点句结尾**（或论点句加简短概述），让读者从它直接进入第一个主体段。
5. **最后写或最后重写引言**，那时你已经知道文章论证的是什么（[[essay-writing/writing-process#rule-draft]]）。
:::

::: proof
太长的引言用掉了主体部分需要的字数，离题目太远的开头让读者等待。从一般到具体的形状反映了读者的需要：足够的语境，让他看出问题为什么重要，然后是精确的主张。斯韦尔斯（Swales，1990）以类似的方式描述研究论文的引言，把它看作一系列语步：确立研究领域，指出其中的空白，然后填补这一空白。文章的引言要简单一些，但原理——先语境，再作者的贡献——是相同的，研究性写作中的这一原理在 [[academic-writing/research-questions]] 和 [[academic-writing/abstracts-titles]] 中展开。
:::

### 应避免的开头和有效的开头

有些开头太常见了，阅卷人只把它们看作拖延：

- **“Since ancient times …”**、**“Throughout history …”**、**“Nowadays, with the development of society …”**——太笼统，什么也没说，而且往往不符合事实。
- **一个常用词的词典释义**：*The Oxford Dictionary defines technology as …* 只有在术语的含义有争议时才去界定它，而且要用自己的话、为自己的目的界定（[[essay-writing/understanding-the-question#rule-define]]）。
- **一连串修辞性问句**：*Is technology good? Is it bad? What does the future hold?*
- **一句不能引出论点的名言**，只是用来显得有分量。
- **逐字重复题目。**

有效的开头通常做到下面其中一点：

- **一个具体的事实或数字**，并注明来源：*In 2013 Tallinn, the capital of Estonia, began offering free public transport to its registered residents.*
- **一个简短的具体情景**：*At seven in the morning, a student in a large city can spend longer travelling to her first lecture than listening to it.*
- **争论本身**：*Few questions divide city planners as sharply as whether public transport should be free.*
- **一个文章将加以修正的普遍看法**：*It seems obvious that making buses free would take cars off the road.*

::: example 改写一篇空洞的引言 {#ex-rewrite-intro}
为题目 *Should schools teach financial skills such as budgeting and saving?* 改写这篇引言。

*Since ancient times, money has played an important role in human life. Nowadays, with the rapid development of the economy, money is more and more important. What is money? According to the dictionary, money is "a medium of exchange". Should schools teach financial skills? This is a controversial question. There are many advantages and disadvantages. This essay will discuss them.*
::: solution
1. **删掉空洞的语步**：*Since ancient times …*、*Nowadays, with the rapid development …*、词典释义（没有人需要 *money* 的定义）、*This is a controversial question*、*There are many advantages and disadvantages*、*This essay will discuss them*。
2. **找一个贴近题目的具体开头**：年轻人第一次作出财务决定的情景。
3. **补上读者需要的背景**：这里的 *financial skills* 指什么。
4. **写一个回答 *should* 的论点句。**

> *Many young people make their first important financial decisions — signing a phone contract, renting a room, taking out a student loan — within a year or two of leaving school, often with no more preparation than advice from family. By financial skills, this essay means practical abilities such as budgeting, understanding interest and comparing contracts, rather than investment. Schools should teach these skills, because they are needed by every student, are learned unequally at home, and can be taught through mathematics lessons that already exist.*

新引言比旧的短，从贴近题目的地方开始，界定了唯一需要界定的术语，并以一个封闭式论点句结尾，其中的三个理由可以变成三个段落。
:::
:::

::: example 分析一篇范文引言 {#ex-analyse-intro}
找出这篇引言（为本图谱而写）中的各个语步，题目是 *To what extent has the growth of food delivery apps changed eating habits in cities?*

*(1) In many large cities, a hot meal can now arrive at the door within half an hour of a few taps on a phone. (2) Food delivery apps, which connect customers, restaurants and couriers, have grown rapidly over the past decade, especially among younger, working adults. (3) "Eating habits" here means where, when and what people eat, rather than how much they spend. (4) The apps have changed where and when people eat far more than what they eat: they have moved restaurant meals into homes and offices and made late meals easier, but the dishes ordered are largely those that restaurants already served.*
::: solution
1. 第 (1) 句是**开头**：一个具体的、熟悉的情景，直接引出话题。
2. 第 (2) 句提供**背景**：这些应用是什么，谁在用。它避免使用无法支撑的确切数字；在真正的文章中，这里用一个注明出处的数字会很有用。
3. 第 (3) 句**聚焦并界定**：它说明 *eating habits* 指什么，从而告诉读者可以期待哪种证据。
4. 第 (4) 句是**论点句**：一个程度上的回答（*far more than*），带有论证的主线（*where and when* 与 *what*）。主体部分的结构已经暗含其中：在哪里吃，什么时候吃，然后吃什么。

没有单独的概述句：在短文中，论点句承担了这项工作。
:::
:::

::: example 论点句放在哪里 {#ex-thesis-place}
一名学生的引言在第一句就表明了论点，然后是三句背景。这样错了吗？如果改写有帮助，就改写：*Schools should start later in the morning. Teenagers' sleep patterns change during adolescence. Many teenagers find it hard to fall asleep before eleven at night. Most secondary schools start lessons before half past eight.*
::: solution
1. **错了吗？** 没有错。把论点放在最前面是可以的，在某些体裁中——报告、新闻、电子邮件——这还是常规。不过在文章中，读者期待先读到背景，论点句则在引言末尾，由它直接引入主体部分。
2. **这里的代价**：在论点句之后，那三句背景读起来像是论证的开始，读者可能意识不到引言在哪里结束。
3. **改写**，让背景引出论点，并使论点更具体：

*During adolescence, the body clock shifts later, and many teenagers find it hard to fall asleep before eleven at night. Yet most secondary schools start lessons before half past eight. Schools should move the start of the day later for older students, because the earlier start costs them sleep that they cannot make up and that affects both their health and their learning.*
:::
:::

::: quiz
哪一个开头句最直接地引向题目 *Should supermarkets be banned from selling single-use plastic bags?*
- [ ] *Since ancient times, humans have carried their belongings in bags.*
- [ ] *The dictionary defines plastic as "a synthetic material".*
- [x] *Many countries already charge for plastic bags at the checkout, and some have gone further and banned the thinnest ones.*
- [ ] *Is plastic good? Is it bad? Can we live without it?*
::: solution
第三句提供了相关背景——收费和部分禁止——直接引向全面禁止的问题。其他几句是上面描述的空洞开头：一个模糊的历史说法、一个不必要的定义、一连串修辞性问句。
:::
:::

::: note 英汉对比
- **要点出现在哪里。** 在英语学术文章中，论点通常在引言里表明，文章其余部分加以支撑——这是一种*演绎*（deductive）顺序，从主张到支撑。有些汉语文章传统更为渐进地推向要点，中国学校的写作也常常看重优美的引子和铺垫。海因兹（Hinds，1990）认为，汉语、日语、韩语和泰语的说明文可能是“准归纳式”（quasi-inductive）的，把论点推迟提出。另一些研究者则告诫不要把这些当作固定的文化模式：柯克帕特里克（Kirkpatrick，1997）发现，20世纪90年代中国大陆学校使用的写作教材所建议的结构，与英语国家所教的十分相似，而开门见山、立即表明观点的汉语文章也广受推崇。实际的要点很简单：在英语文章中，要在引言里表明你的回答。
- **引语和固定说法。** 中国学校的作文常常用一句名言、一行诗或一个成语开头，这在汉语中可以很见功力。译成英语后，这类开头往往显得空泛或只是装饰（*As an old Chinese saying goes, …*）。只有当一句引语能直接引出你的论点时才使用它，并说明它与论点的关系。
- **“随着社会的发展”。** “随着社会的发展”和“随着科技的进步”是中国学校作文的常见开头；它们的英语译文（*With the development of society/science and technology, …*）是学习者英语作文中最容易辨认的开头之一。用一个具体的事实或情景来代替它们。
- **“In my opinion”。** 论点本来就是你的观点；在它前面加 *In my opinion* 或 *I think* 并没有错，但在正式文章中通常没有必要，许多作者都不加。有些考试题目直接问**你的**观点；这时 *I believe that …* 或 *In my view, …* 就很自然。
:::

::: history
引言的各个部分比文章这种形式古老得多。古典修辞学手册，例如佚名的《致赫伦尼乌斯修辞学》（*Rhetorica ad Herennium*，公元前1世纪）和昆体良（Quintilian）的《雄辩术原理》（*Institutio Oratoria*，公元1世纪末），把一篇演说分为几个部分：赢得听众注意和好感的 *exordium*（开场白），陈述事实的 *narratio*（叙述），说明有待证明的要点的 *partitio*（划分），加以证明的 *confirmatio*（证实），回应对方的 *refutatio*（驳斥），以及总结并打动听众的 *peroratio*（结语）。这些术语在欧洲学校里教了几个世纪，现代文章的引言、论点句和路线图、主体、反方论点和结论，至今仍紧紧沿用这一框架。*Thesis statement* 这个说法本身则属于20世纪美国的作文教学；五段式文章——以三部分论点句结尾的引言、三个主体段和一个结论——也是在那里成为标准的学校练习的。
:::

::: summary
- 论点句表明文章的中心主张——作者对问题的回答——通常位于引言末尾（[[#def-thesis]]）。
- 好的论点按问题本身的要求作答，而且可争辩、具体、统一，并能在现有篇幅内加以支撑（[[#rule-good-thesis]]）。
- 封闭式论点列出主要理由；如果列出了，主体部分就必须按顺序展开。让步从句可以使论点更精确（[[#def-open-closed]]、[[#rule-open-closed]]）。
- 不要用预告（*This essay will discuss …*）代替论证；论点句之后加一句简短概述是可以接受的。
- 引言从开头和背景，经过聚焦和界定，走向论点句，篇幅通常约占全文的十分之一（[[#def-intro]]、[[#rule-intro]]）。
- 避免空洞的开头——*since ancient times*、词典释义、修辞性问句、装饰性的引语——用一个具体的事实、情景或看法，从贴近题目的地方开始。
:::

## 练习

::: exercise 是不是论点句？ {level=1}
下面哪些是论点句（回答问题的、可争辩的主张）？(a) *Many students work part-time while they study.* (b) *Students should not work more than fifteen hours a week during term, because longer hours harm their grades more than the income helps them.* (c) *This essay will examine part-time work.* (d) *Part-time work gives students skills that their degree courses do not, and employers value these skills more than grades.* (e) *Part-time work is an interesting topic.*
::: solution
(b) 和 (d) 是论点句。(a) 是事实；(c) 是预告；(e) 是对话题的模糊看法，不回答任何问题。
:::
:::

::: exercise 说出问题 {level=1}
每个论点句都有一个问题：*不可争辩*、*太模糊*、*不统一* 或 *没有回答问题*。题目是 *Should children learn to swim at school?* (a) *Swimming is a sport.* (b) *Swimming lessons have many benefits.* (c) *Schools should teach swimming, and school lunches should be healthier.* (d) *Swimming is my favourite sport.*
::: solution
(a) 不可争辩（是事实）。(b) 太模糊（哪些好处？而且没有说 *should*）。(c) 不统一（两个不相关的主张）。(d) 没有回答问题（个人喜好）。
:::
:::

::: exercise 排列引言 {level=1}
把这些句子排列成一篇引言。(a) *This essay argues that such programmes help students most when they are linked to their studies.* (b) *Many universities now encourage students to spend a semester abroad.* (c) *Supporters claim these programmes develop independence and language skills, while critics point to their cost and to the time they take from the degree.*
::: solution
(b)、(c)、(a)：先是开头和背景，然后是争论，最后是论点句。
:::
:::

::: exercise 使论点更鲜明 {level=2}
使每个论点句更具体、更可争辩。(a) *Social media affects teenagers.* (b) *Tourism has good and bad effects.* (c) *Exams are important.*
::: solution
答案不唯一。范例：

(a) *Social media harms teenagers' sleep more than it harms their friendships, because the main damage comes from late-night use rather than from what they see.*
(b) *Mass tourism benefits the economies of historic towns but drives residents out of their centres, unless the number of short-term rentals is limited.*
(c) *Final exams remain the fairest way to assess school students, because coursework is easier for families to influence.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 从开放式到封闭式 {level=2}
给每个开放式论点句加上主要理由，把它变成封闭式的，然后写出每个主体段的主题。(a) *Cities should plant more trees along streets.* (b) *Schools should not ban mobile phones completely.*
::: solution
答案不唯一。范例：

(a) *Cities should plant more trees along streets, because trees cool streets in summer, reduce flooding after heavy rain and make walking more pleasant.* 各段：降温；防涝；步行。

(b) *Schools should not ban mobile phones completely, because phones are useful in some lessons, help parents contact children after school, and a total ban teaches less than rules for responsible use.* 各段：课堂使用；与家人联系；学会负责任地使用。
:::
:::

::: exercise 修改开头 {level=2}
把每个空洞的开头换成一个能引向题目 *Should public libraries stay open late in the evening?* 的开头。(a) *Since ancient times, people have loved reading.* (b) *What is a library? According to the dictionary, it is a place where books are kept.*
::: solution
答案不唯一。范例：(a) *For students who share small flats and for adults who work during the day, a quiet place to read or study in the evening can be hard to find.* (b) *Public libraries have changed from places that lend books into places where people study, use computers and meet, but most still close at the end of the working day.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 写一篇引言 {level=3}
为题目 *To what extent should universities judge applicants on interviews as well as exam results?* 写一篇70–100词的引言。包括开头、背景、必要时的界定或聚焦，以及一个程度上的论点句。
::: hint
先决定你的程度回答——例如，某些专业面试而其他专业不面试，或者面试只作为第二步。
:::
::: solution
答案不唯一。一个范例：

> Exam results tell a university what an applicant has achieved, but not always what they could achieve with the right teaching. For this reason, some universities interview applicants as well, while others rely on results alone. Interviews should play only a limited part in admissions: they are valuable for courses such as medicine and teaching, where communication is part of the work, but for most subjects they add little that results do not show, and they favour applicants who have been coached.

检查：具体的开头；关于当前做法的背景；一个回答 *to what extent* 的论点句，带有限度和理由。
:::
:::

::: exercise 评价两篇引言 {level=3}
比较题目 *Should employers allow staff to work a four-day week?* 的这两篇引言。说出哪一篇更有效，为什么，并结合本课的语步和规则加以说明。

**A.** *Nowadays, with the development of society, work is more and more important in our lives. Some people think we should work four days a week, while others disagree. Both sides have their reasons. In this essay I will discuss both sides and give my opinion.*

**B.** *Several companies have experimented with a four-day week in which staff keep their full pay. Supporters report that employees are less tired and more focused; critics doubt whether the results last, or whether they are possible in shops and hospitals. Employers should offer a four-day week where work is measured by results rather than hours, but it cannot simply be copied in jobs that need someone present every hour of the day.*
::: solution
B 更有效。

- **开头**：A 以一个空洞的概括开始（*Nowadays, with the development of society*）；B 以一个贴近题目的具体进展开始。
- **背景**：A 只说人们意见不一；B 说明了每一方主张什么。
- **论点**：A 没有论点——它预告了一场讨论，把观点推迟到后面。B 给出了一个带条件（*where work is measured by results*）和限度（*jobs that need someone present*）的回答，它可争辩、具体，并暗示了主体部分的结构。
- **篇幅**：两篇都很短，但 B 的每一句都在发挥作用。
:::
:::
