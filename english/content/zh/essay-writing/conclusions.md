题目还是 [[essay-writing/thesis-introduction]] 中的那一道：*Should cities make public transport free for all passengers?* 作者的论点是：免费公共交通不是城市用钱的好办法，因为司机更在意速度和便利，而不是票价；文章主体用三段论证了这一点，并回应了一个反对意见。下面是三个可能的最后一段。

> **A.** *In conclusion, free public transport is a poor use of a city's money: the people it most needs to attract — drivers — care more about speed and convenience than about the fare, so the same money would cut more car journeys if it were spent on faster and more frequent services.*

> **B.** *In conclusion, there are both advantages and disadvantages to free public transport. Another problem is that free buses may attract people who would otherwise have walked or cycled, which is bad for their health. Every city must decide for itself, and we should all try to use our cars less in order to protect the planet for future generations.*

> **C.** *Making buses and trains free would help the passengers who already use them, and for some of them, on low incomes, that help would matter. But a city that wants fewer cars on its roads needs to win over people who now drive, and for them the price of a ticket is rarely what decides. The money that free fares would cost is therefore better spent on what does decide — services that come often, run late and reach where people actually need to go. Free travel for those who cannot afford a ticket can be part of that policy; free travel for everyone should not be its centre.*

A 是把论点句原封不动地再说一遍；读者从中得不到任何东西。B 做了三件结论不应该做的事：它从论点退回到 *advantages and disadvantages*，加进了一个主体部分从未讨论过的新论点（步行和骑车），并以一个与票价没什么关系的拯救地球的号召结尾。C 才是一个结论。它用新的话给出了和论点相同的回答，把主体部分的各个理由串成一条思路，承认反方说得对的地方，最后说明由此得出什么——城市实际上应该怎么做。

本课讲文章的最后一段：它的作用是什么，怎样重申论点而不重复它，怎样结尾，以及哪些内容不该写。结论只能把文章已经做过的事情归拢起来，所以它以 [[essay-writing/thesis-introduction]] 和 [[essay-writing/counter-arguments]] 为基础。

## 结论的作用

::: definition 结论 {#def-conclusion}
文章的**结论**（conclusion）是它的最后一段（在长文中是最后一部分）。它以最终的形式给出文章对问题的回答，说明主体部分的要点怎样支撑这一回答，并说明这一回答为什么重要或由此得出什么。它通常完成下面部分或全部语步：

1. **重申**（restatement）论点，用新的话，并结合主体部分的内容；
2. **综合**（synthesis）主要论点：说明它们怎样结合在一起，而不是罗列它们；
3. 在相关时，作出**限定**（qualification）：回答的限度，或者反方说得对的地方；
4. **收尾语步**（closing move）：一个推论、一项建议、一个后果，或者这一回答引出的一个问题。
:::

引言从一般走向具体：从话题到论点（[[essay-writing/thesis-introduction#def-intro]]）。结论通常方向相反，从具体的回答向外扩展到它更广泛的意义。教材常常把两者画成一个漏斗和一个倒置的漏斗；只要结论在扩展视野时不回到世界史上去，这个比喻就很有用。

::: theorem 结论要回答问题 {#rule-conclusion-answer}
1. 结论必须对所提出的问题给出**清楚的回答**——与论点相同的回答，尽管现在可能更精确。
2. 它必须**从主体部分得出**：其中的每一个主张，都应该有文章已经论证过的内容作支撑。
3. 它**不能加入新的论点或新的证据**。如果某一点重要到需要写进去，它就属于主体段。
4. 它应该**简短**：在大多数文章中，大约和引言一样长——约占全文的十分之一。
:::

::: proof
结论是读者最后读到的部分，也常常是阅卷人在决定分数时重读的部分。如果它没有给出清楚的回答，读者可能会断定文章根本没有回答。如果它给出的回答与论点不同，读者就分不清哪个才是作者真正的立场，而本来为支撑论点而写的主体部分，现在支撑的却是别的东西。新论点出现时没有解释也没有证据，而且是在作者已经无法展开它们的地方，这会让人觉得主体部分不完整。篇幅是一个惯例，而不是定律。
:::

:::

- [ ] 一个在主体部分没有篇幅写的第三个理由

## 重申而不重复

大多数结论都以再次表明回答开始。这并不是多余：读者现在已经知道了理由。但重申应该表明，作者从引言出发以后已经走过了一段路。

::: theorem 用新的话重申论点 {#rule-restate}
1. **不要照抄**论点句。用不同的话、通常也用不同的句子结构来表达同一个立场。
2. **利用主体部分已经确立的内容。** 重申时可以把理由当作已知的东西来提（*because drivers rarely choose by price*），而不是当作许诺。
3. **如果主体部分已经说明了怎样使论点更精确，就使它更精确。** 如果一个反方论点使你限定了自己的立场，重申就应该包括这个限定。
4. **保持同一个回答。** 一个没有解释就比论点更强、更弱或与之不同的重申，看上去像是改变了主意。
:::

::: proof
逐字重复一个句子不会告诉读者任何新东西，还会引得读者跳过去；改述则迫使作者思考文章实际上表明了什么，而这常常与引言所许诺的略有不同。如果主体部分表明的主张比论点窄，要么修改论点使之相符（[[essay-writing/writing-process#rule-revise]]），要么在这里表述那个较窄的主张。改述论点所用的技巧与改述来源相同——既改变用词，也改变结构，在没有好的同义词时保留关键术语（[[academic-reading/paraphrase-and-quotation]]）。
:::

::: example 重申论点 {#ex-restate}
一篇文章的论点是：*Universities should record most lectures, because recordings help students learn and widen access, but recording should exclude discussion and protect students' privacy.* 为它的结论写一个重申，并说明你改动了什么。
::: solution
找出论点的各个部分——立场（*record most lectures*）、两个理由（*learning*、*access*）和一个限制（*discussion*、*privacy*）——然后改变结构，像主体部分那样描述理由，并保持同一个回答：

> *A recording lets a student hear a difficult explanation a second time, and lets a student who could not be there hear it at all. Those benefits are large enough to justify recording most lectures as a matter of course, provided that seminars and discussions are left unrecorded and that students can ask questions without being filmed.*

改动：新的句子结构（两个句子，理由在前）；像主体部分那样具体地描述理由；保留了限制并使之具体化（*seminars*、*ask questions without being filmed*）。回答没有变。
:::
:::

::: warning 乔装打扮的论点句
改几个词不等于重申：

✗ 论点：*Schools should start later because teenagers need more sleep.* 结论：*To conclude, schools ought to begin later since adolescents require more sleep.*

这只是用同义词替换后的论点句，读者一眼就能认出来。重申时要加上主体部分已经表明的东西——机制、证据、限制：✓ *Teenagers do not choose to stay up late; their body clocks shift during adolescence, and an early start takes away sleep they cannot recover at weekends. A later start for older students is the simplest way to give it back.*
:::

## 综合，而不是总结

::: definition 总结与综合 {#def-synthesis}
结论中的**总结**（summary）重复主体部分的要点，通常按它们出现的顺序：*Firstly …, secondly …, finally …*。**综合**（synthesis）则说明各要点之间的关系——哪一点最重要，一点怎样依赖另一点，它们合起来说明了什么单独任何一点都说明不了的东西——并把它们与回答联系起来。
:::

简短的总结对长文或报告的读者可能有帮助；而在一篇短文中，读者刚刚读完主体部分，把各段主题句列一遍是结论所能提供的最没用的东西。

::: theorem 把各个理由归拢起来 {#rule-synthesis}
要把总结变成综合，就针对主体部分的要点提出下面一个或几个问题，并在结论中回答：

1. **哪一点最重要**，为什么？（*The decisive reason is …*）
2. **各要点之间有什么联系？** 是一点导致另一点，还是其中两点是同一个问题的两个方面？（*Both problems come from the same source: …*）
3. **各要点合起来说明了什么**单独任何一点都说明不了的东西？（*Taken together, these suggest that …*）
4. **反方论点怎样改变了全局？**（*The objection about cost does not reverse this conclusion, but it does mean that …*）
:::

::: proof
贝赖特和斯卡达玛利亚关于知识陈述与知识转化的区分（[[essay-writing/writing-process]]）尤其适用于结论。总结式的结论按写作的顺序再一次告诉读者作者知道什么。综合则要求作者把论证当作一个整体来思考——作文题正是为检验这种思考而设计的，而阅卷人在结论中比在任何其他地方都更能清楚地看到这种思考。四个问题中通常回答一个就够了。
:::

::: example 从总结到综合 {#ex-synthesis}
把这个总结式的结论改写成综合式的。文章回答的是 *Is it better to study alone or in a group?*，论点是答案取决于学的是什么。

*In conclusion, studying alone and studying in groups both have advantages. Studying alone is good for concentration. It is also good for memorising. Studying in a group is good for discussing difficult ideas. It is also more motivating. So both are good.*
::: solution
这一段罗列了四个要点，以 *both are good* 结尾，比论点还弱。问一问这些要点之间有什么联系：专注和记忆是练习的需要，讨论是理解的需要——这正是论点本身所作的区分。动力则是次要的。

> *Whether to study alone or with others is less a matter of personal preference than of the task. Practice and memorisation need the long, uninterrupted concentration that only solitary study gives, while difficult ideas are often understood only when they have to be explained to someone else, or defended against their questions. A student who studies alone when she needs explanation, or in a group when she needs repetition, is choosing the wrong tool. The best plan for most courses is therefore a sequence: understand a topic with others, then fix it alone.*

新的结论重申了论点（*a matter … of the task*），把各要点分成两类而不是罗列它们，并以一项由这一区分得出的建议结尾。
:::
:::

## 结束文章

最后一句常常是最难写的，因为作者觉得需要写点令人难忘的东西。真正需要的，是写点顺理成章的东西。

::: theorem 选择一个从论证中得出的收尾语步 {#rule-closing}
以下面其中一种方式收尾，根据题目和文章来选择：

1. **推论**（implication）：这一回答对一个更广泛的问题意味着什么。*If drivers do not choose by price, cities that want fewer cars must compete on speed.*
2. **建议**（recommendation）：当题目问 *should* 时，应该做什么。*Schools should move the start of the day for older students first.*
3. **限定**（qualification）：如果条件很重要，说明回答在什么条件下成立。*This applies to cities with existing networks; a town without buses faces a different problem.*
4. **回到开头**：根据论证重新审视引言中的情景或例子。*The student who spends longer travelling than listening needs a faster bus more than a free one.*
5. **一个留待进一步思考的问题**：文章引出了却无法回答的一个真正的问题。要少用，而且只有在文章本身的问题已经回答之后才用。

无论用哪种收尾语步，都不能引入支持论点的新论证，而且它的范围不能超出文章所能支撑的程度。
:::

::: proof
这些语步都在扩展回答，却没有为它增加新的支撑。推论和建议说明从一个已经确立的回答中得出什么；限定标出它的限度；回到开头则给整篇文章加上一个框架，这就是中国的写作老师所说的“首尾呼应”（见下面的注）。禁止新论点的规则并不禁止新的**想法**：推论是新的，但它是论证的结果，读者可以根据已经读到的内容接受它，而不是需要新证据的支撑。最常被打破的是范围的限制：一篇关于公交票价的文章可以以城市应该怎样使用交通预算结尾，却不能以地球的未来结尾。
:::

::: example 用哪种收尾语步？ {#ex-closing}
为每篇文章建议一种合适的收尾语步，并写出最后一句。(a) *To what extent do food delivery apps change eating habits in cities?*（论点：它们改变的主要是人们在哪里吃、什么时候吃，而不是吃什么。）(b) *Should museums be free to enter?*（论点：国家博物馆应该免费，特展收费。）
::: solution
1. **(a) 推论。** 程度类问题需要的是推论，而不是建议：*If the apps have changed the setting of meals more than the meals themselves, their main effect may be on restaurants and on the evening routines of households, not on diet — a reason to look for their consequences in those places first.*
2. **(b) 建议**，因为题目问的是 *should*：*Keeping the permanent collections free and charging for special exhibitions would protect both the museums' income and the principle that a public collection belongs to everyone.*
每个最后一句都是从论点得出的，不需要新的证明。
:::
:::

::: warning 应避免的结尾
- **新的论点或新的证据。** ✗ *Another advantage of free entry is that it would help tourism.*——如果它重要，就把它移到主体部分。
- **从论点后退。** ✗ *In conclusion, there are advantages and disadvantages, and it is hard to say which is better.* 文章正是为了说明哪个更好而写的。
- **道歉。** ✗ *Of course, this is only my opinion, and others may disagree.* 每篇文章都是作者的观点；读者知道有人不同意，而反方论点段已经处理过他们的意见了。
- **空泛的行动号召。** ✗ *We must all work together to build a better world for our children.* 行动号召属于演讲或宣传单；在文章中，它必须具体，并且是从论证中得出的。
- **装饰性的引语。** ✗ *As the saying goes, "Where there is a will, there is a way."* 结尾的引语和开头的引语一样，除非它说出了文章需要的东西，否则只是装饰。
- **悬念式结尾。** ✗ *Only time will tell.* 这是一种不作回答的方式。
:::

## 不同类型文章的结论

各类文章的基本语步是一样的，但它们所重申的回答因题目类型而异（[[essay-writing/essay-types]]）。

| 文章类型 | 结论给出…… | 典型的收尾语步 |
|---|---|---|
| 议论型（*Should …?*、*Do you agree?*） | 立场和决定性的理由 | 建议 |
| 讨论型（*Discuss both views*） | 在两种观点之间作出的判断，以及一方更有力的理由 | 限定：另一种观点在什么情况下有分量 |
| 程度类问题（*To what extent …?*） | 程度：*largely*、*only partly*、*in one respect but not another* | 推论 |
| 比较对照型 | 一个裁决：哪个更好，在哪方面更好，或者比较说明了什么 | 一个实际的判断 |
| 因果型 | 哪些原因或结果最重要，它们之间有什么联系 | 关于什么能改变它们的推论 |
| 问题—解决型 | 哪个解决办法最好，为什么，以及它取决于什么 | 第一步，或成功的条件 |

::: theorem 讨论型文章的结论必须作出判断 {#rule-discursive-conclusion}
当一篇文章摆出两种观点时，它的结论必须说明哪一种更有力——或者每一种在哪些方面是对的——以及**为什么**。不给理由地选择一方（*In my opinion, the second view is better*），或者宣布两种观点不分高下（*Both sides have a point*），都不是结论。引出有理由的判断的说法包括 *On balance, …*、*Weighing these arguments, …*、*The stronger case is …, because …*、*While X is right that …, Y's argument is more convincing on …*。
:::

::: proof
讨论型题目要求两件事，第二件就是判断。如果判断没有理由，主体部分看上去就像两张并排摆放的清单（[[essay-writing/essay-types#rule-discursive-form]]）。*On balance* 声称已经进行了权衡，所以后面必须跟着权衡的结果。
:::

::: example 一个讨论型文章的结论 {#ex-discursive-conclusion}
一篇文章讨论题目 *Some people think that children should start learning a foreign language at primary school; others think secondary school is early enough. Discuss both views and give your opinion.* 主体部分论证了：(1) 年幼的孩子学发音更容易，开口说话时也不那么紧张，但是 (2) 年龄较大的孩子在课堂上学语法和词汇更快，而且 (3) 只有在课时频繁、教师训练有素时，早期教学才有帮助。写出结论。
::: solution
> *On balance, the case for starting at primary school is the stronger one, but only under conditions that many schools do not meet. Young children's ease with pronunciation and their willingness to speak are real advantages, yet with a single short lesson a week they are easily outweighed by the faster progress older learners make once lessons begin. Where an early start means frequent lessons with teachers who speak the language well, it gives children a foundation that secondary school can build on; where it does not, those who argue for waiting have the better argument. The decision, then, should depend less on children's age than on what a school can actually provide.*

它作出了判断，给出了理由，说明了另一种观点在什么情况下是对的，并以一个推论结尾。
:::
:::

## 结论的语言

| 功能 | 语言 |
|---|---|
| 标示结论 | *In conclusion, …*、*To conclude, …*、*To sum up, …*（较不正式）、*Overall, …*、*In short, …*——或者完全不用标记 |
| 重申回答 | *It is clear, then, that …*、*This essay has argued that …*、*The evidence considered here suggests that …*、*X is, therefore, …* |
| 综合 | *Taken together, …*、*What these … have in common is …*、*The decisive factor is …*、*Above all, …*、*Both … and … point to …* |
| 权衡 | *On balance, …*、*Weighing these arguments, …*、*The stronger case is …*、*While …, …* |
| 限定 | *This is not to say that …*、*At least in …*、*This applies mainly to …*、*Where …, however, …* |
| 推论和建议 | *It follows that …*、*This suggests that …*、*If this is right, …*、*… should therefore …*、*The first step would be …* |

::: theorem 标示结论 {#rule-signal-conclusion}
1. *In conclusion* 是正确的，广泛用于文章最后一段的开头。只在结论开头用**一次**，不要用于段落的结尾。
2. 标记常常**没有必要**：一个在第一句就重申回答的段落，一看就是结论。许多有经验的作者不用标记，或者在第一句中用 *then* 或 *therefore*（*It is clear, then, that …*）。
3. *To sum up* 和 *All in all* 比 *In conclusion* 稍不正式；*In a word* 引出一个只有一个词的总结，不用来引出一个段落；*At last* 的意思是“等了很久之后终于”，从来不是结论的标记（[[sentences-and-paragraphs/coherence]]）。
4. *This essay has argued / shown / discussed …* 在学术写作中很常见；用 *argued* 或 *shown* 比用 *discussed* 好，因为 *discussed* 描述的是文章的活动，而不是它的回答。
:::

::: proof
标记告诉读者他们处在什么位置。在短文中，读者知道最后一段就是结论，所以 *In conclusion* 告诉他们的东西很少；在长文中，标记的帮助更大。不鼓励用 *In conclusion* 的老师，反对的通常不是这个说法本身，而是它后面往往跟着的东西——对引言的重复。在整篇文章中滥用标记，是 [[essay-writing/signposting]] 的主题。
:::

::: quiz
哪一个开头最适合一篇正式文章的最后一段？
- [ ] *At last, I want to say that free museums are a good idea.*
- [ ] *In a word, museums should be free because they help people, they are public and the cost can be covered.*
- [x] *Free entry, then, is not a gift to visitors but a condition of a museum doing its public job.*
- [ ] *All in all, there are good and bad sides to free museums.*
::: solution
第三句用新的话重申了论点，用 *then* 表示它是从主体部分得出的。第一句误用了 *At last*，而且只是宣布而没有回答；第二句把 *In a word* 误用于一串列举；第四句较不正式，而且从论点后退了。
:::
:::

::: note 英汉对比
- **首尾呼应。** 汉语作文教学常常建议文章的结尾与开头呼应（首尾呼应），回到开头的一个意象、一句话或一个问题。这可以很好地迁移到英语中：回到开头的情景是 [[#rule-closing]] 中的收尾语步之一。呼应应该给回答加上框架，而不是代替回答。
- **升华和结尾的道理。** 许多中国学校的作文在结尾把主题提升到更高的层次（升华主题）——从个人经历上升到人生道理，或者从一个局部问题上升到社会的福祉。英语议论文也可以在结尾扩展视野，但方式是陈述一个从论证中得出的推论，而不是一个可以放在任何文章结尾的道理。*We should all cherish our time* 或 *Only in this way can our society develop better* 在大多数英语读者看来都很空泛；一个具体的推论则不会。
- **“总之”及其译法。** “总之”和“总而言之”常被译成 *In a word* 或 *In short*，“最后”常被译成 *At last*。*In a word* 引出一个只有一两个词的总结；*At last* 的意思是“等了很久之后终于”。作为文章的结论，通常的选择是 *In conclusion*、*To conclude*、*Overall*，或者不用标记。
:::

::: history
古代修辞学给演说的结尾起了名字，也规定了任务。亚里士多德的《修辞学》（公元前4世纪，第三卷）说，结束语有四项任务：使听众对演说者产生好感、对对手产生恶感，放大或淡化主要事实，激起适当的情感，以及唤起听众对已讲内容的记忆。拉丁语手册把结尾称为 *peroratio* 或 *conclusio*，佚名的《致赫伦尼乌斯修辞学》（公元前1世纪）把它分为三部分：总括（*enumeratio*）、铺陈（amplification）和唤起听众的同情。现代文章保留了总括，表现为重申和综合；也以一种更平和的形式保留了铺陈，表现为说明回答为什么重要的推论；而诉诸情感，在法庭或集会上自然适得其所，在一位评判推理的读者面前通常就不合时宜了。
:::

## 后续内容

结论是文章最后修改的部分，检查它是否回答了问题、是否与论点一致，是 [[essay-writing/revising-editing]] 的内容之一；在考试的最后几分钟写结论，见 [[essay-writing/timed-essays]]。标示结论的标记，以及为什么一个往往就够了，属于 [[essay-writing/signposting]]。校准最终主张的强度在 [[modality/hedging-boosting]] 中讲授。在研究写作中，结论变成讨论部分，用来解读结果、说明局限并建议进一步的研究（[[academic-writing/discussion-conclusions]]）；在演讲结束时进行总结，见 [[presentations-and-discussion/signposting-a-talk]]。

::: summary
- 结论以最终的形式给出文章的回答，把主体部分的理由归拢起来，可以说明回答的限度，并以由此得出的东西结尾（[[#def-conclusion]]）。
- 它必须回答问题，从主体部分得出，不加入新的论点或证据，篇幅与引言大致相当（[[#rule-conclusion-answer]]）。
- 用新的话和新的结构重申论点，利用主体部分已经确立的内容；保持同一个回答，如果主体部分已经说明了怎样使它更精确，就使它更精确（[[#rule-restate]]）。
- 综合优于总结：说明哪一点最重要，各要点之间有什么联系，或者它们合起来说明了什么（[[#def-synthesis]]、[[#rule-synthesis]]）。
- 以推论、建议、限定、回到开头或一个真正的问题收尾——绝不以道歉、后退、装饰性引语或拯救世界的号召收尾（[[#rule-closing]]）。
- 讨论型文章的结论必须在两种观点之间作出判断，并说明理由（[[#rule-discursive-conclusion]]）。
- *In conclusion* 在最后一段开头用一次是正确的，而且常常没有必要；*In a word* 和 *At last* 不是结论的标记（[[#rule-signal-conclusion]]）。
:::

## 练习

::: exercise 保留还是删去？ {level=1}
在一篇主张城市应该多种行道树的文章的结论中，下面哪些句子应该保留？给每句标上*保留*或*删去*，并说明理由。(a) *Street trees, then, repay their cost many times over in cooler streets and cleaner air.* (b) *Another benefit is that trees provide homes for birds.* (c) *Of course, this is only my personal opinion.* (d) *The decisive argument is the summer heat: in a warming climate, shade is no longer a luxury.* (e) *Only time will tell.*
::: solution
(a) *保留*——对论点的重申，提到了主体部分确立的理由。(b) *删去*——一个新论点；如果它重要，就移到主体部分。(c) *删去*——削弱文章的道歉。(d) *保留*——综合：说明哪个理由最重要。(e) *删去*——回避回答的悬念式结尾。
:::
:::

::: exercise 改正标记 {level=1}
改正或改进每个最后一段开头的标记。(a) *At last, schools should teach cooking.* (b) *In a word, online shopping saves time, saves money and offers more choice, so it is better than shopping in person.* (c) *In conclusion, in conclusion, the plan should go ahead.* (d) *Last but not least, I think the government should act.*
::: solution
(a) *At last* 的意思是“等了很久之后终于”：*In conclusion, schools should teach cooking*（或者不用标记：*Schools, then, should teach cooking*）。(b) *In a word* 引出的是一个只有一个词的总结，而不是一串列举：用 *Overall, …* 或 *In short, …*。(c) 重复是笔误：*In conclusion, the plan should go ahead.* (d) *Last but not least* 引出列举中的最后一项，通常用在主体段或演讲中，而不是结论中：*In conclusion, the government should act …*，后面接具体的行动。
:::
:::

::: exercise 说出收尾语步 {level=1}
每个最后一句用的是哪种收尾语步——推论、建议、限定、回到开头还是问题？(a) *If the apps have changed where people eat more than what they eat, nutrition campaigns aimed at them may be looking in the wrong place.* (b) *Councils should therefore begin with the streets around schools.* (c) *This holds for large cities; in villages, where the nearest library may be an hour away, a mobile service raises different questions.* (d) *The commuter waiting at the stop in the opening paragraph does not need a free ticket; she needs a bus that comes.* (e) *Whether the same pattern holds for adult learners is a question this essay has not been able to answer.*
::: solution
(a) 推论。(b) 建议。(c) 限定。(d) 回到开头。(e) 留待进一步思考的问题。
:::
:::

::: exercise 重申论点 {level=2}
为结论重申每个论点，用新的话和新的结构，保持同一个回答。(a) *Secondary schools should teach financial skills, because they are needed by every student, are learned unequally at home, and can be taught in existing mathematics lessons.* (b) *Working from home benefits employees more than employers, because the time saved on travel goes to the employee while the costs of coordination fall on the organisation.*
::: hint
从主体部分已经表明的东西开始，而不是从立场开始；也不要只是用同义词替换词语。
:::
::: solution
答案不唯一。范例：

(a) *Every student will one day sign a contract, borrow money or plan a budget, but not every student will learn how at home. Since mathematics lessons can already teach the skills involved, leaving them out of the curriculum makes an unequal situation worse for no good reason.*

(b) *The hours that working from home saves are saved by the employee; the difficulties it creates, in arranging meetings and training new staff, are borne by the organisation. That imbalance explains why employees tend to value the arrangement more than their employers do.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 找出问题 {level=2}
每个结论都有一个主要问题。说出问题并改写结论。(a) *To sum up, public libraries are important. Firstly, they lend books. Secondly, they offer free internet. Thirdly, they are quiet places to study. In addition, libraries could also lend tools, which would reduce waste.* (b) *In conclusion, although I have argued that cities should ban cars from their centres, it is true that many people need cars, so maybe a ban is not a good idea after all.*
::: solution
(a) 对主体部分的总结，后面跟着一个**新论点**（出借工具）。改写：*Public libraries are among the few places where anyone can read, go online or study without paying or being asked to buy something. That combination — free, open to all, and useful in more than one way — is what makes them worth funding even as fewer people borrow printed books.*

(b) 结论**从论点后退**，提出了一个反方论点却不作回应。改写：*A ban on private cars would make the centre cleaner, safer and more pleasant. The people who depend on cars — disabled drivers, deliveries, night workers — are a reason to design exemptions carefully, not to keep the traffic.* 如果作者真的改变了主意，就必须修改论点和主体部分。
:::
:::

::: exercise 综合 {level=2}
一篇关于 *Why do many people stop exercising after a few weeks?* 的文章，主体部分给出了三个原因：(1) 他们定的目标过高；(2) 锻炼要与工作和家庭争夺时间；(3) 他们选择了自己不喜欢的锻炼方式。写一个60–90词的结论，综合而不是罗列这些原因。
::: hint
这三个原因有没有什么共同之处？哪一个最重要，或者哪一个最容易改变？
:::
::: solution
答案不唯一。一个范例：

> People rarely give up exercise because they lack willpower. More often, they have chosen a plan that does not fit their life: too much, at times they do not have, in a form they do not enjoy. All three causes are failures of fit rather than of character, which suggests a simple remedy. Someone starting to exercise should choose something small enough to do on a busy week and pleasant enough to look forward to, and add to it only once it has become a habit.

这个综合找出了各个原因的共同之处（*failures of fit*“不匹配”），并由此得出一个推论。
:::
:::

::: exercise 写一个讨论型文章的结论 {level=3}
一篇关于 *Some people think that universities should prepare students mainly for employment; others think their main purpose is education for its own sake. Discuss both views and give your opinion.* 的文章，主体部分论证了：(1) 雇主需要具有实用技能的毕业生，许多学生上大学是为了改善就业前景；(2) 广博的教育所培养的思维习惯——分析、论证、判断——本身在工作中就很有价值；(3) 针对当前工作的狭窄训练可能会过时。写一个100–140词的结论，在两种观点之间作出判断，并给出理由。
::: solution
答案不唯一。一个范例：

> On balance, the two purposes are less opposed than the question suggests, and where they do conflict, education should come first. Students are right to expect that a degree will help them find work, and universities that ignore this fail many of the people they serve. But the qualities employers value most over a career — the ability to analyse a problem, argue a case and judge evidence — are the products of a broad education, not of training for a particular job, and they do not go out of date as specific skills do. A university that teaches students to think well is therefore preparing them for employment, including for jobs that do not yet exist; one that teaches only today's skills is preparing them for a shorter future.

检查：*On balance* 后面跟着一个判断及其理由；另一种观点说得对的地方；对第2点和第3点的综合；结尾的推论；没有新论点。
:::
:::

::: exercise 修改一个结论 {level=3}
下面是一名学生关于 *Should secondary school students be required to do voluntary work in their community?* 的文章的引言和结论。修改结论，使它回答问题、与论点一致，并遵循本课的建议。写大约100词。

*Introduction (last sentence): Compulsory community work would benefit both students and their communities, as long as students can choose the kind of work they do.*

*Conclusion: In conclusion, there are many advantages and disadvantages of compulsory voluntary work. On the one hand, it helps students. On the other hand, some students do not want to do it. Also, it can be difficult for schools to organise. As a famous saying goes, "It is better to give than to receive." We should all help others in our society.*
::: hint
这个结论从论点后退，罗列要点，加入了一个新论点（组织工作），并以一句引语和一个泛泛的道理结尾。哪种收尾语步适合 *should* 类题目？
:::
::: solution
答案不唯一。一个范例：

> Requiring students to work in their communities can seem a contradiction — voluntary work that is not voluntary. Yet the benefits argued here, from practical skills to a better understanding of the people around them, do not depend on students having chosen to begin; they depend on the work being worth doing. That is why choice matters more than compulsion: a student who can choose between a library, a sports club and a care home is more likely to keep going after the requirement ends. Schools should therefore require a modest number of hours and offer a wide range of placements.

改动：回答与论点一致；对学生不情愿这一反对意见作了回应，而不是罗列；关于组织工作的新论点删去了；引语和道理换成了一项建议。
:::
:::
