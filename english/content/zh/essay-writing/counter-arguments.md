设想你读到一篇文章，它主张每个城市的市中心都应该禁止私家车。文章给出了三个理由——空气更清洁、街道更安全、给人留出更多空间——每一个都有充分的支撑。但它从来没有提到受影响最大的那些人：顾客开车来购物的店主，没法走到公交车站的残障居民，在末班地铁之后才下班的护士。一个立刻想到这些人的读者会认为作者没有想到，从而对整篇文章的信任打折扣。

现在设想同一篇文章多了一段：

> *The strongest objection to a ban is that it would cut off people who depend on cars: residents with disabilities, workers on night shifts, and businesses whose customers drive in. This objection is serious, but it is an argument for exemptions and better services rather than against the ban itself. Most of the cities that have closed their centres to private cars allow access for disabled drivers, deliveries and emergency vehicles, and a ban can be timed to begin only once night buses and park-and-ride services are in place.*

这一段做了三件事。它公正地陈述了反方观点——实际上是以其最有力的形式陈述的。它承认其中正确的部分。它还回应了这一观点，说明它改变的是政策的细节，而不是支持政策的理由。文章现在更有说服力，而不是更弱了，因为读者能看出作者考虑过另一方，并且仍然坚持自己的立场。

本课说明怎样陈述和回应反方观点、把它们放在文章的什么位置，以及让步和反驳的语言。它以 [[essay-writing/arguing-a-point]] 中的段落为基础。

## 反方论点、让步和反驳

::: definition 反方论点、让步和反驳 {#def-counter}
- **反方论点**（counter-argument，也叫 *opposing view*“反方观点”、*objection*“反对意见”）是一位通情达理的读者可能提出的、反对作者立场或其中某个主张的论证。
- **让步**（concession）是作者承认反方论点中某些内容是正确的或有分量的。
- **反驳**（rebuttal，也叫 *refutation*、*response*）是作者对反方论点的回答：为什么它推翻不了作者的立场，或者作者的立场怎样容纳了它。

在文章中，三者通常一起出现：*Opponents argue that X*（反方论点）。*It is true that …*（让步）。*However, …*（反驳）。
:::

::: theorem 回应最有力的反方观点 {#rule-strongest}
1. 在议论文中，至少处理**一个**严肃的反方论点——通常是一位深思熟虑的读者最可能提出的那个。
2. **公正地**陈述它，用它的支持者会认可的形式，最好是以其最有力的形式。
3. **回应它**：绝不要提出一个反对意见却不作回答，除非你有意承认这一点并修改你的论点。
4. 反方论点要比你自己的论证**简短**，但要长到足以被认真对待。
:::

::: proof
论证是写给尚未同意的读者看的，而这些读者心里有反对意见。先提出最有力的那个，表明作者思考过这个问题；回应它，则消除了一个怀疑的理由。关于说服的研究支持这一点。奥基夫（O'Keefe，1999）综述了大量实验，发现承认反方论点*并加以驳斥*的信息，平均而言比单方面的信息更有说服力；而提到反方论点却不作回答的信息，说服力不如单方面的信息。一个薄弱或被歪曲的反方观点（**稻草人**，*straw man*）效果正好相反：持这种观点的读者会注意到歪曲，不持这种观点的读者以后可能遇到真正的论证，从而感到被误导。以对方观点最有力的形式来陈述它，这一原则有时称为**宽容原则**（principle of charity）。
:::

::: example 公正还是不公正？ {#ex-fair}
论点是 *Secondary schools should not set homework for students under fourteen.* 哪一种反方论点的表述是公正的？改写不公正的那一个。

(a) *Some people think children should do homework because they enjoy making children suffer.* (b) *Supporters of homework argue that practice outside lessons helps students remember what they have learned, and that it teaches them to work independently — habits they will need in later years.*
::: solution
1. **(a)** 是一个稻草人。它给对方安上了一个没有哪个家庭作业支持者会认可的动机，而且根本没有给出论证。回答它什么也证明不了，支持家庭作业的读者也会不再真诚地读下去。
2. **(b)** 是公正的。它陈述了两个真正的论证——记忆和独立学习——而且是布置作业的老师们会认可的说法。
3. 改写 (a)，保留它关于家庭作业带来压力的意思，但对另一方公正：*Supporters of homework accept that it can be stressful, but argue that a moderate amount teaches children to manage their time and to work without a teacher's help.*

文章现在必须回答 (b)，这比回答 (a) 难。关键就在这里：一篇经受住了最有力的反对意见的文章，会因此而更有力。
:::
:::

## 回应反方论点的方式

::: theorem 四种反驳 {#rule-rebuttal}
作者回应反方论点主要有四种方式：

1. **驳倒**（refute）：说明它是错误的，或者没有证据支持。*The claim that free transport is always overcrowded is not borne out by …*
2. **压倒**（outweigh）：承认它是对的，但说明支持立场的理由更重要。*Free transport does cost money, but the costs of congestion are greater.*
3. **限定**（limit）：承认它在某些情况下适用，并收窄立场，把这些情况排除在外。*The objection holds for rural areas, where buses are rare; this essay's proposal concerns cities.*
4. **反转**（turn）：说明这个反对意见经过正确理解后，反而支持作者的立场，或者只要求改变细节而不是原则。*The concern about disabled residents is an argument for exemptions, not against the ban.*

选择证据所支持的回答方式。如果哪一种都行不通，这个反对意见就是决定性的，论点必须修改。
:::

::: proof
这四种回应对应反对意见与主张之间的几种主要关系：它可能是错的（驳倒），对但不那么重要（压倒），在某些情况下对（限定），或者对、并且与另一种形式的主张相容（反转）。在古典修辞学中，*refutatio*（驳斥）是演说的一个固定部分，放在演说者自己的证明之后；此后，论证的教师们把直接驳斥与让步、限定这些更微妙的回应区分开来。限定主张并不是失败：一个已经收窄到与证据相符的论点是更好的论点。这就是为什么处理反方论点的阶段也是修改的阶段（[[essay-writing/writing-process#rule-revise]]）：一个作者回答不了的反对意见，就是修改论点的信号。
:::

::: example 选择回应方式 {#ex-response}
论点是 *Universities should record all lectures.* 为每个反对意见选择一种反驳类型，并写一两句话。

(a) *Recording lectures will reduce attendance.* (b) *Recordings cost money to make and store.* (c) *Lecturers will be less willing to discuss controversial material if they know they are being recorded.*
::: solution
1. **(a) 限定或压倒。** 关于出勤率的证据并不一致，作者不能声称它明确指向任何一个方向。一个审慎的回答：*Attendance may fall in some courses, but students who choose recordings over lectures are often those who could not attend in any case, because of work, illness or care for relatives; for them, the choice is not between a lecture and a recording but between a recording and nothing.*
2. **(b) 压倒。** *Recording does have costs, but once equipment is installed, the extra cost of each recording is small compared with the cost of the teaching it preserves.*（如果作者有可靠的数字，应该放在这里。）
3. **(c) 反转或限定。** *This concern is real, but it is an argument for letting lecturers pause the recording for discussion, not for refusing to record at all.* 这也稍微改变了论点：*all lectures* 变成了 *all lectures, with the option to pause for discussion*。

第三个反对意见改进了论点。这很常见：认真对待反对意见的作者，最终会得到一个更精确的立场。
:::
:::

## 反方论点放在哪里

常见的模式有三种：

1. **单独的反方论点段**，放在作者自己的理由之后、结论之前。这是短文中最常见的模式，也是大多数考官所期待的。
2. **在每个主体段内部处理反对意见**：每个理由后面跟着一个可能的反对意见及其回答。这适合每个理由都有自己明显的反对意见的文章。
3. **先陈述反方观点**：文章一开始就摆出它要反驳的论点，然后回应它，再建立自己的论证。这适合以一个待评价的主张为形式的题目（*"X is the best way to …" Discuss.*），以及写给持反方观点的读者的文章。

::: theorem 安排反方论点的位置 {#rule-placing}
1. 把最有力的反方论点放在读者最可能提出它的地方——常常是在你的主要论证之后、结论之前。
2. 不要以反方论点结束文章：读者最后读到的应该是你的反驳和你的结论。
3. 清楚地标示反方论点（*Critics of this view argue that …*），使读者不会把它误认为你自己的立场。
:::

::: proof
读者对开头和结尾记得最牢，一篇以没有回答的反对意见结束的文章，留给读者的是怀疑，而不是作者的论证。标示之所以重要，是因为一个没有标记的反方论点看上去像是改变了主意：在一篇反对汽车的文章中间读到 *Cars are essential for many people*，读者会怀疑作者是不是换了立场。下一节中的说法就是防止这种情况的标记。
:::

::: quiz
反方论点放在哪里**最没有**效果？
- [ ] 在作者主要理由之后单独成段
- [ ] 在主体段内部，后面紧跟反驳
- [x] 在最后一段，文章结束前，没有反驳
- [ ] 在文章开头，作为要反驳的观点
::: solution
在文章最后留下一个没有回答的反方论点，读者最后读到的是对方的论证。另外三种安排都是有经验的作者会用的，前提是反对意见得到了回答。
:::
:::

## 让步和反驳的语言

| 语步 | 语言 |
|---|---|
| 引出反方观点 | *Critics argue that …*、*Opponents of … claim that …*、*It is sometimes argued that …*、*Some would object that …*、*A common objection is that …* |
| 让步 | *It is true that …*、*Admittedly, …*、*Granted, …*、*There is some truth in this.*、*This is a fair point.*、*… certainly …* |
| 一句话内的让步 | *Although …*、*Even though …*、*While …*、*Whilst …*（英式，正式）、*Despite …* / *In spite of …* + 名词或 *-ing* |
| 反驳 | *However, …*、*Nevertheless, …*、*But …*、*This argument overlooks …*、*This does not mean that …*、*The evidence suggests otherwise.*、*This is less convincing when …* |
| 压倒 | *… but this is outweighed by …*、*… but the benefits are greater.*、*Even so, …* |

::: theorem 让步的语法 {#rule-concession-grammar}
1. *Although*、*even though* 和 *while* 引导一个分句，在同一个句子里**不**再接 *but*：✗ *Although it is expensive, but it is effective.* ✓ *Although it is expensive, it is effective.*（见 [[sentences-and-paragraphs/subordination]]。）
2. *Despite* 和 *in spite of* 是介词：后面接名词短语或 *-ing* 形式，不接分句：✗ *Despite it is expensive* ✓ *Despite its cost* / *Despite being expensive* / *Despite the fact that it is expensive*。
3. *Admittedly* 和 *Granted* 在句首表示让步；反驳在**下一个**句子或分句中出现，通常用 *but* 或 *however*：*Admittedly, the scheme is expensive. However, …*
4. *It is true that X, but Y* 是一个自然的两段式结构；*X may be true, but Y* 也是：*Recordings may reduce attendance, but …*
:::

::: proof
英语只标记一次让步关系：要么在让步分句上用从属连词（*although*），要么在主要观点上用并列连词或连接词（*but*、*however*）；两者都用就重复了这一关系，所以 *although … but* 在标准英语中不合语法（Swan 2016；Quirk et al. 1985），这与汉语的“虽然……但是”不同。*Despite* 属于另一个词类，所以它后面的分句必须变成名词短语。情态结构 *X may be true, but …* 在论证中特别有用，因为 *may* 承认了可能性，却不让作者为这一主张承担责任（[[modality]]）。
:::

::: theorem 转述动词与立场 {#rule-reporting-verbs}
用来引出反方观点的动词，显示了作者对它的态度：

- **中性**：*argue*、*say*、*suggest*、*maintain*、*contend*（正式）；
- **保持距离**——作者对它有怀疑：*claim*、*allege*、*assert*；
- **认可**——作者接受它：*point out*、*show*、*demonstrate*、*note*。

选择与你的回应相符的动词：✗ *Critics point out that cars are essential; this is wrong.*（*point out* 已经接受了这一主张）✓ *Critics claim that cars are essential; this overlooks …*
:::

::: proof
*Point out* 和 *show* 是叙实动词：它们把后面的内容当作事实来呈现，所以一个用了它们之后又否定这一主张的作者是自相矛盾的。*Claim* 表示作者不为这一陈述负责，常常用在反驳之前。不过，对每一个自己不同意的观点都用 *claim*，听起来可能有轻蔑之意；*argue* 是稳妥的中性选择。在学术写作中转述来源时，同样的区别也很重要（[[academic-writing/citation-referencing]]）。
:::

::: example 一个反方论点段 {#ex-paragraph}
为论点 *Museums should be free to enter* 写一个反方论点段，回应关于成本的反对意见。
::: solution
1. **公正地引出反对意见**，以其最有力的形式：博物馆需要收入，免费开放可能意味着用于藏品和员工的钱减少。
2. **让步**，承认正确的部分。
3. **反驳**，这里用的是*压倒*和*限定*：存在其他收入来源，而且公共利益更大。
4. **回到**论点。

> *The most serious objection to free entry is financial. Museums need money to conserve their collections, pay specialist staff and mount exhibitions, and critics argue that giving up ticket income would force them to cut exactly these things. This concern is reasonable: free entry is not free to provide. However, it does not follow that visitors at the door must pay. Museums that do not charge for entry raise money in other ways, from public funding, donations, shops and paid special exhibitions, and these can be expanded. More importantly, a ticket price falls hardest on the people a public museum most needs to reach — families with low incomes, students and local residents who might visit often — so the cost of free entry is better seen as the price of a public service than as a loss.*

这一段清楚地陈述了反对意见，承认了其中的道理，并从两个方面加以回应，最后落在作者的立场上。
:::
:::

::: example 改正让步句 {#ex-correct}
改正错误。(a) *Although online learning is flexible, but many students find it lonely.* (b) *Despite online learning is flexible, many students find it lonely.* (c) *Admittedly, online learning is flexible, many students find it lonely.* (d) *Critics show that online learning is lonely, but this is not true.*
::: solution
1. **(a)** *although* + *but*：去掉一个。✓ *Although online learning is flexible, many students find it lonely.* 或 ✓ *Online learning is flexible, but many students find it lonely.*
2. **(b)** *despite* + 分句：改用名词短语。✓ *Despite its flexibility, online learning is lonely for many students.* 或 ✓ *Despite the fact that online learning is flexible, …*
3. **(c)** *Admittedly* 之后出现逗号粘连：✓ *Admittedly, online learning is flexible. However, many students find it lonely.* 或 ✓ *Admittedly, online learning is flexible, but many students find it lonely.*
4. **(d)** *show* 把主张当作事实来呈现，与反驳相矛盾。✓ *Critics claim (argue) that online learning is lonely, but this is true only for …*
:::
:::

::: warning 让步太多，或太少
两种相反的毛病：

- **削弱了论点**：作者承认了反对意见却不作回答，或者回答得很无力。✗ *Critics say free transport is too expensive. This is true, and cities do not have enough money.*——而文章却照样写下去，好像论点仍然成立。如果你回答不了一个反对意见，就收窄你的论点（[[#rule-rebuttal]] 第3条）。
- **装点门面的反方论点**：只用一句话点出一个反对意见，随即把它打发掉。✗ *Some people disagree, but they are wrong.* 这句话没有告诉读者任何东西，还暗示作者没有考虑过另一方。
:::

::: quiz
哪一个句子是正确的？
- [ ] *Even though the plan is popular, but it is too expensive.*
- [ ] *In spite of the plan is popular, it is too expensive.*
- [x] *While the plan is popular, it is too expensive to carry out.*
- [ ] *Despite of its popularity, the plan is too expensive.*
::: solution
*While* 引导让步分句，主句跟在后面，不用 *but*。第一句把 *even though* 和 *but* 连用；第二句 *in spite of* 后接分句；第四句在 *despite* 后加了 *of*（*despite* 后面从不接 *of*：*despite its popularity* 或 *in spite of its popularity*）。
:::
:::

::: note 英汉对比
- **虽然……但是。** 汉语成对使用这两个连词；英语只用一个。*Although … but* 是以汉语为母语者英语作文中最常见的错误之一，而且往往恰好出现在反方论点段中。检查初稿中的每一个 *although*、*even though* 和 *while*。
- **立论和驳论。** 汉语议论文区分确立一个观点的文章（立论）和驳斥一个观点的文章（驳论），并分别教授两者的技巧。英语文章通常把两者结合起来：作者确立一个立场，并在同一篇文章中驳斥最有力的反对意见。
- **在写作中表达不同意见。** 在许多文化中，都有一些作者觉得公开反对别人的观点是不礼貌的，或者宁愿在纸面上避免冲突。在英语议论文和学术写作中，反对一个*论证*是理所当然的，不是人身攻击；读者确实会觉得不礼貌的，是歪曲或嘲笑另一方。礼貌在于公正，而不在于同意。
- **“凡事都有两面性”。** *Every coin has two sides*（凡事都有两面性）和 *There are advantages and disadvantages* 是学习者作文中引出另一方观点的常见方式。它们很笼统，没有说出是哪种观点、为什么；要具体地说出那个反对意见。
:::

::: history
回应另一方是论证中最古老的成分之一。在古典修辞学家所教的演说中，*refutatio*（驳斥）有自己固定的位置，放在演说者自己的证明之后。中世纪的大学通过 *disputatio*（论辩）来训练学生，那是一种陈述反对意见并加以回答的正式辩论，当时的学术写作也遵循同样的模式：托马斯·阿奎那（Thomas Aquinas）的《神学大全》（*Summa Theologiae*，13世纪）每一条都先陈述反对其立场的意见，然后是立场及其理由，再逐一回答每个反对意见。20世纪，理查德·扬（Richard Young）、奥尔顿·贝克（Alton Becker）和肯尼斯·派克（Kenneth Pike）的《修辞：发现与改变》（*Rhetoric: Discovery and Change*，1970）把心理学家卡尔·罗杰斯（Carl Rogers）的思想改造成今天所教的**罗杰斯式论证**（*Rogerian argument*）：首先公正地陈述反方观点，公正到它的支持者认可你的概括，找出共同点，然后才提出你自己的立场。它的目的不是击败另一方，而是打动那些否则会不再听下去的读者。
:::

## 后续内容

讨论型文章在作出判断之前权衡两种观点，它是 [[essay-writing/essay-types]] 中的类型之一；结论怎样为争论作出裁决，见 [[essay-writing/conclusions]]；标示反方论点的路标语——*admittedly*、*however*、*nevertheless*——见 [[essay-writing/signposting]]。阅读时辨识作者对他人观点的立场，在 [[academic-reading/stance-and-hedging]] 中练习；口头辩论见 [[presentations-and-discussion/debate-persuasion]]；在文献综述中回应持不同观点的学术研究，见 [[academic-writing/literature-review]]。

::: summary
- 反方论点是对作者立场的反对意见；让步承认其中正确的部分；反驳回答它（[[#def-counter]]）。
- 回应最有力的反方观点，公正地陈述它，并作出回答；承认并驳斥反对意见的论证，往往比单方面的论证更有说服力（[[#rule-strongest]]）。
- 通过驳倒、压倒、限定或反转反对意见来反驳；如果哪一种都行不通，就修改论点（[[#rule-rebuttal]]）。
- 把反方论点放在读者会提出它的地方，清楚地加以标示，绝不以没有回答的反对意见结束（[[#rule-placing]]）。
- *Although*、*even though* 和 *while* 后面不接 *but*；*despite* 和 *in spite of* 后接名词短语或 *-ing*；*admittedly* 之后在新的分句中给出反驳（[[#rule-concession-grammar]]）。
- 转述动词显示立场：*argue* 是中性的，*claim* 保持距离，*point out* 和 *show* 表示认可（[[#rule-reporting-verbs]]）。
:::

## 练习

::: exercise 反方论点、让步还是反驳？ {level=1}
给每个句子贴上标签。(a) *Some argue that uniforms limit students' self-expression.* (b) *It is true that students cannot choose their clothes.* (c) *However, uniforms reduce pressure to compete over expensive brands.* (d) *Opponents of the plan claim that it will raise taxes.* (e) *This overlooks the savings the plan will bring.*
::: solution
(a) 反方论点。(b) 让步。(c) 反驳。(d) 反方论点。(e) 反驳。
:::
:::

::: exercise 改正让步句 {level=1}
改正每个句子。(a) *Although the hotel was cheap, but it was clean.* (b) *Despite it rained, we went out.* (c) *In spite of the price is high, many people buy it.* (d) *Even though he trained hard, but he lost.*
::: solution
(a) *Although the hotel was cheap, it was clean.*（或 *The hotel was cheap, but it was clean.*）(b) *Despite the rain, we went out.*（或 *Although it rained, …*）(c) *In spite of the high price, many people buy it.*（或 *Although the price is high, …*）(d) *Even though he trained hard, he lost.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 选择转述动词 {level=1}
选择与作者立场相符的动词。(a) 作者接受这一主张：*As researchers have (claimed / shown), regular exercise reduces stress.* (b) 作者对它有怀疑：*Some advertisers (point out / claim) that their product can improve memory in a week.* (c) 作者持中立：*Opponents (argue / demonstrate) that the scheme is unfair.*
::: solution
(a) *shown*。(b) *claim*。(c) *argue*。
:::
:::

::: exercise 说出反驳类型 {level=2}
每个回应用的是哪种反驳——驳倒、压倒、限定还是反转？论点：*Schools should teach coding to all students.* (a) *It is true that not every student will become a programmer, but every student will use software that coding helps them understand.* (b) *Critics say there are not enough qualified teachers; this is a reason to train teachers, not to drop the subject.* (c) *The claim that coding is too difficult for younger children is contradicted by the many primary schools that already teach it with visual tools.* (d) *For students with severe learning difficulties, a compulsory course may not be suitable; the proposal applies to mainstream classes.*
::: solution
(a) 压倒。(b) 反转。(c) 驳倒。(d) 限定。
:::
:::

::: exercise 使它公正 {level=2}
把每个稻草人式的反方论点改写成公正的。(a) *People who oppose bicycle lanes just hate cyclists.* (b) *Those who want to keep exams simply want students to suffer.*
::: solution
答案不唯一。范例：(a) *Opponents of new bicycle lanes argue that they take space from cars and deliveries on roads that are already congested, and that few people cycle in winter.* (b) *Supporters of exams argue that they are the fairest form of assessment, because every student answers the same questions under the same conditions, without help from home.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 用让步连接 {level=2}
用两种方式连接每组句子：一次用 *although*，一次用 *despite*。(a) *The course is expensive. Many students enrol.* (b) *The town has a long history. Few tourists visit it.*
::: solution
(a) *Although the course is expensive, many students enrol.* / *Despite the cost of the course*（或 *Despite its high cost*），*many students enrol.*

(b) *Although the town has a long history, few tourists visit it.* / *Despite its long history, the town attracts few tourists.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 写一个反方论点段 {level=3}
为论点 *Cities should replace some car parking spaces in the centre with parks and seating.* 写一个100–140词的反方论点段。公正地陈述最有力的反对意见，承认其中正确的部分，并加以反驳。
::: hint
最有力的反对意见可能与依赖开车顾客的商店和企业有关。哪种反驳最合适？
:::
::: solution
答案不唯一。一个范例：

> The strongest objection comes from shopkeepers, who argue that fewer parking spaces will mean fewer customers. Their concern deserves to be taken seriously: a shop that loses even a small share of its customers may not survive. However, the objection assumes that most customers arrive by car, and shopkeepers often overestimate how many do. In centres where many shoppers already walk, cycle or come by bus, a pleasant street where people stay longer can bring more trade than the parking spaces it replaces. Where shops do depend on drivers, the answer is to keep some short-stay spaces and loading bays nearby, not to abandon the change altogether.

检查：一个公正的反对意见，一个让步，一个既驳倒了一个假设又限定了提议的反驳，并以作者的立场结尾。
:::
:::

::: exercise 修改论点 {level=3}
一名学生的论点是 *All schools should replace textbooks with tablets.* 在计划时，她列出了这些反对意见：*tablets are expensive; screen time affects sleep and eyesight; some families cannot repair or replace a broken tablet; many textbooks are not available digitally in some subjects.* 判断她能回答哪些反对意见、怎样回答，并改写她的论点，以照顾到那些她无法完全回答的反对意见。
::: solution
答案不唯一。一个范例：

- *Tablets are expensive*：压倒——几年下来，数字材料的花费可能比购买新的纸质课本少，而且一台设备可以取代许多本书。（她需要针对自己所在环境的真实数字。）
- *Screen time*：限定——阅读课本只占屏幕时间的一小部分，学校也可以制定规则；但对年龄较小的孩子，这个反对意见有一定分量。
- *Families cannot repair or replace tablets*：反转——这是让学校拥有和维护设备的理由，而不是反对使用设备的理由。
- *Not available digitally*：无法对每个科目都完全回答——这是一个真正的限制。

修改后的论点：*Secondary schools should move most of their textbooks to school-owned tablets, keeping printed books for subjects without good digital materials and for younger pupils, because over time tablets cost less and allow materials to be updated.*

这些反对意见使论点更窄，也更站得住脚。
:::
:::
