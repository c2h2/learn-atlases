下面是一段天气预报和一段新闻报道。

*Heavy rain is **likely** in the north tonight, and there is a **strong chance** of thunderstorms. Temperatures will **probably** stay below ten degrees.*

*The minister **appears** to have resigned. According to colleagues, she was **reportedly** unhappy with the new policy, although she has **apparently** made no public statement.*

这五个句子中只有一个情态动词（*will*）。然而每一句都表达了情态：*likely*、*a strong chance*、*probably*、*appears*、*reportedly* 和 *apparently* 都告诉我们作者有多大把握，或者信息来自哪里。情态动词是情态的语法中心，但在真实的文本中，它们与副词、形容词、名词和实义动词分担工作。在学术写作和新闻写作中，这些其他形式常常比情态动词更常用，因为它们使作者能够准确地陈述把握程度，把它放在突出的位置，或者转述别人的观点。

本章概述这些表达情态的“词汇”手段、它们的语法——学习者在这方面犯错最多——以及怎样在它们之间作出选择。它以 [[modality/deduction]] 中的确定性等级为基础；下一章 [[modality/hedging-boosting]] 把它们用于学术写作。

## 四种词类

::: definition 词汇情态 {#def-lexical-modality}
**词汇情态**（lexical modality）是由普通词语而不是情态助动词表达的情态意义：**副词**（*certainly*、*probably*、*perhaps*）、**形容词**（*possible*、*likely*、*necessary*）、**名词**（*possibility*、*chance*、*need*）和**实义动词**（*seem*、*appear*、*tend*、*suggest*、*require*）。和情态动词一样，它们既表达认识意义（某事有多大可能），也表达道义意义（什么是必要的或允许的）。
:::

| 意义 | 副词 | 形容词 | 名词 | 动词 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 确定 | *certainly*、*definitely*、*undoubtedly* | *certain*、*sure*、*bound to* | *certainty*、*no doubt* | *know*、*show*、*prove* |
| 很可能 | *probably*、*presumably*、*likely*（美式英语） | *likely*、*probable* | *likelihood*、*probability*、*a good chance* | *expect*、*think*、*believe* |
| 可能 | *possibly*、*perhaps*、*maybe* | *possible* | *possibility*、*chance* | *suggest*、*wonder whether* |
| 看来如此，有证据 | *apparently*、*evidently*、*seemingly* | *apparent*、*evident* | *appearance*、*evidence* | *seem*、*appear*、*look*、*indicate* |
| 一般为真 | *usually*、*generally*、*typically* | *typical* | *tendency* | *tend to* |
| 必要 | *necessarily* | *necessary*、*essential*、*compulsory* | *need*、*necessity*、*obligation* | *require*、*need*、*oblige* |
| 允许 | — | *allowed*、*permitted* | *permission* | *allow*、*permit*、*let* |

## 副词

::: theorem 情态副词及其位置 {#rule-adverbs}
1. 大多数情态副词（*probably*、*certainly*、*definitely*、*possibly*、*presumably*、*clearly*）通常位于**句中位置**：在 *be* 或第一个助动词之后，在主要动词之前：*She is **probably** right.* *They will **certainly** object.* *He **probably** knows.*
2. 遇到**否定助动词**时，它们通常位于其**前面**：*She **probably won't** come.* *He **certainly doesn't** want to.*（✗ *She won't probably come* 自然得多。）*Definitely* 也可以位于否定词之后，但那时意义就变了（见下文）。
3. ***Perhaps*** 和 ***maybe*** 通常位于**句首**：***Perhaps** she's right.* ***Maybe** they missed the bus.* *Maybe* 是会话的典型用词；*perhaps* 用于口语和书面语，是正式文本和学术文本中的通常选择。
4. 任何一个都可以放在句首以示强调，书面语中后面加逗号：***Clearly**, the plan has failed.*
5. 单用作副词的 ***likely***（*She'll **likely** be late*）主要用于**美式英语**；英式英语更喜欢用 *probably*、*very likely* 或 *most likely*（Swan 2016）。
:::

::: proof
这些位置遵循对整个分句作评论的副词的一般规则（夸克等 Quirk et al. 1985 称之为“外加语”，disjuncts；比伯等 Biber et al. 1999 称之为“立场状语”，stance adverbials）。处于句中位置时，它们紧挨着助动词，而英语正是在助动词上标记时、否定和情态，所以它们自然位于否定助动词之前：说话人的判断（*probably*）作用于整个否定陈述（*she won't come*），而不是反过来。规则2中的区别是辖域的区别：*I definitely won't go* 的意思是“我确定我不会去”，而 *I won't definitely go* 的意思是“我去不去还不确定”。
:::

有两个副词需要特别注意。

***Surely*** 并不简单地等于 *certainly*。它表达说话人的信念并寻求对方同意，常常表示对某事可能不是真的感到惊讶：***Surely** you're not leaving already?*（我不相信你这就要走了。）*There must be a mistake, **surely**.* 表示单纯的确定，用 *certainly* 或 *definitely*（Swan 2016）。

***Apparently***、***evidently***、***reportedly*** 和 ***allegedly*** 是**示证性的**（evidential）：它们表明信息的来源。*Apparently* 通常的意思是“我听说 / 看来”，而不是“显然”：***Apparently** the shop is closing*（有人告诉我的）。*Allegedly* 和 *reportedly* 是新闻的典型用词，作者用它们表示不对某个说法负责：*He **allegedly** took the money.*

::: warning *Surely*、*apparently* 和副词的位置
✗ *I will surely pass the exam — I've worked very hard.* ✓ *I'**ll definitely** / **I'm sure I'll** pass the exam.*

✗ *Apparently, the earth goes round the sun.*（好像这是传闻似的）✓ ***Obviously** / **Of course**, the earth goes round the sun.*

✗ *He doesn't probably know.* ✓ *He **probably doesn't** know.*

*Surely* 寻求同意或表示惊讶；*apparently* 转述你听到的事。
:::

::: theorem 情态动词与副词连用 {#rule-harmonic}
**强度相同**的情态动词和情态副词相互加强：*He **may possibly** be there.* *She **must certainly** have known.* *It'**ll probably** rain.* 莱昂斯（Lyons 1977）把这样的组合称为**和谐的**（harmonic）。强度**不同**的组合不常见，通常听起来自相矛盾（? *It **must possibly** be true*），除非副词修饰的是意义的另一部分（*You **should certainly** apply* = “我当然建议你申请”）。
:::

::: example 从一条消息中读出证据和态度 {#ex-message}
胡静收到同事发来的这条消息。对每个加粗的副词，说明它表达的是作者的把握程度、信息的来源，还是寻求同意，以及胡静可以得出什么结论。

*Hi Jing — **apparently** the client meeting has moved to Thursday. It's **probably** because their director is travelling. I'll **definitely** be there, but **perhaps** you could bring the slides? **Surely** they won't change it again!*
::: solution
***apparently***——来源：作者是从别人那里听说的。胡静在改变计划之前也许应该核实一下。

***probably***——把握程度（中等）：作者在猜测原因。

***definitely***——把握程度（强）：一个坚定的承诺。

***perhaps***——在这里它是在缓和一个请求，而不是表达对事实的怀疑（“你能带上幻灯片吗？”）。请求中的情态是 [[modality/modality-politeness]] 的主题。

***Surely***——寻求同意，夹杂着轻微的恼火：作者希望、但并不确定日期不会再变。它的意思不是“肯定”。
:::
:::

## 形容词

::: theorem 情态形容词的句型 {#rule-adjectives}
1. ***It is*** + 形容词 + ***that*** 从句：*It is **possible / likely / probable / certain / unlikely** that the meeting will be cancelled.*
2. 主语 + ***be*** + 形容词 + 带 ***to*** 的不定式，只用于 **likely**、**unlikely**、**certain**、**sure** 和 **bound**：*The meeting **is likely to** be cancelled.* *She **is sure to** win.* *Prices **are bound to** rise.*
3. ***Possible*** 和 ***probable*** **不能**用句型2：✗ *He is possible to win.* ✗ *The meeting is probable to be cancelled.* 用句型1、情态动词或 *likely*：*It is possible that he will win* / *He may win* / *He is likely to win*。
4. ***It is possible for*** + 人 + 带 ***to*** 的不定式表达某人能做什么（动力的或道义的，不是认识的）：*Is it **possible for** me **to** change my seat?* *It isn't **possible for** children **to** enter alone.*
5. ***Sure*** 和 ***certain*** 有两种意义。*She is **sure to** win* =“我（说话人）确定她会赢”。*She is **sure (that)** she will win* =“她很有信心”。*Bound to* 只有第一种意义：*She's **bound to** win.*
6. 道义形容词——***necessary***、***essential***、***vital***、***important***、***compulsory***——用 *it is … for … to* 或 *it is … that*：*It is **necessary for** all staff **to** attend.* *It is **essential that** the form **be** / **is** / **should be** signed.*（见 [[modality/obligation-necessity#rule-formal-obligation]]。）不说 ✗ *You are necessary to attend.*
:::

::: proof
句型2常被称为“提升”（raising）：*that* 从句的主语（*It is likely that the meeting will be cancelled* 中的 *the meeting*）变成主句的主语（*The meeting is likely to be cancelled*）。只有少数形容词允许这样做，这个清单必须记住；夸克等（1985）、Huddleston & Pullum（2002）和斯旺（2016）给出的是同一小组词。*Possible* 和 *probable* 在意义上接近 *likely*，但在语法上不同，对于母语中情态副词和形容词是同一个词的学习者来说，这是最常见的错误之一（[[#ex-adjectives]] 列出了几个）。规则5中 *sure* 和 *certain* 的两种意义来自同样的对比：带 *to* 不定式时，确定是说话人的；带 *that* 从句时，确定是主语的。
:::

::: example 改正形容词句型 {#ex-adjectives}
改正有错误的句子并加以解释。(a) *Our team is possible to win the cup.* (b) *It is likely that the train will be late.* (c) *The new law is probable to pass.* (d) *Students are necessary to register by Friday.* (e) *He is bound to be late — he always is.* (f) *Is it possible to me to leave early?*
::: solution
(a) *Possible* 不能用主语 + *to* 不定式：*It is possible that our team will win the cup* / *Our team could win the cup* / *Our team is likely to win the cup*（更强）。

(b) 正确。

(c) *Probable* 也不能用这个句型：*The new law is likely to pass* / *It is probable that the new law will pass* / *The new law will probably pass.*

(d) 在这个意义上，*necessary* 不能以人作主语：*It is necessary for students to register by Friday* / *Students must / need to register by Friday.*

(e) 正确：*bound to* =“肯定会”（说话人的确定）。

(f) 人要用 *for* 引出：*Is it possible for me to leave early?*（或 *Could I leave early?*）
:::
:::

::: widget venn
sets: 2
labels: that pattern; to pattern
expr: that pattern & to pattern
caption: 哪些情态形容词用哪种句型？**两种**句型都可以（阴影部分）：*likely*、*unlikely*、*certain*——*It is likely that prices will rise* 和 *Prices are likely to rise*。**只能用 *it … that* 句型**（左）：*possible*、*probable*、*impossible*、*clear*、*doubtful*——*It is possible that he'll win*，但不说 ✗ *He is possible to win*。**只能用主语 + *to* 句型**（右）：*sure*、*bound*——*She is bound to win*，但通常不说 ✗ *It is bound that she will win*。（*that pattern* = *It is* + 形容词 + *that* 从句；*to pattern* = 主语 + *be* + 形容词 + *to* 不定式。）输入另一个集合表达式，如 *that pattern - to pattern*，可以给其他区域加阴影。
:::

::: solution
只有 *likely*（以及 *unlikely*、*certain*、*sure* 和 *bound*）能用主语 + *be* + 形容词 + *to* 不定式。其他的需要用 *it is … that* 或别的结构：*It is possible to finish the project by June*、*Prices will probably rise*、*You need to bring your passport.*

## 名词

::: theorem 情态名词的句型 {#rule-nouns}
1. ***There is a possibility / chance / likelihood that*** + 分句：*There is a **possibility that** the flight will be delayed.* *There's a good **chance** (that) we'll finish early.*
2. ***The chances are (that)…***（非正式）和 ***in all likelihood***（正式）：***The chances are** she's already left.* ***In all likelihood**, the vote will be close.*
3. ***Chance of*** + *-ing* 或名词表示**可能性**；***chance to*** + 不定式表示**机会**：*What are the **chances of** winning?*（有多大可能）/ *I had a **chance to** visit Kyoto*（一个机会）。***Possibility of*** + *-ing* 也表示可能性：*the **possibility of** finding life on Mars*。✗ *the possibility to* 在学习者英语和某些国际商务英语中很常见，但标准的说法是 *opportunity to* 或 *possibility of*。
4. ***No doubt***（=“很可能，我想”）和 ***without doubt*** / ***undoubtedly***（=“肯定”）强度不同：***No doubt** you've heard the news* 是一种有把握的推测，而不是证明。
5. 道义名词：***There's no need to*** + 不定式（= *don't have to*）：*There's **no need to** apologise.* *be under **an obligation** to*、*have **permission** to*、*a **requirement** that*。
:::

::: proof
名词使作者能够把情态变成句子的主语或宾语，然后对它加以描述：*a **small** possibility*、*a **strong** likelihood*、*a **real** chance*。这就是为什么它们在学术写作和官方写作中很常用（Biber et al. 1999），在这些文本中，它们还使作者能够提出一个论断而不说出是谁持有这个论断。*chance of* 和 *chance to* 的区别（规则3）见于学习型词典和斯旺（2016）。规则4让许多学习者感到意外：*no doubt* 听起来比实际的意思强，在口语中它常常不过是“我想”的意思。
:::

## 动词

::: theorem 具有情态意义的实义动词 {#rule-verbs}
1. ***Seem*** 和 ***appear*** + *to* 不定式，或 ***it seems / appears that***：说话人报告一种印象或推论，而不完全承诺。*The results **seem to** confirm the theory.* ***It appears that** the email was never sent.* 带完成不定式时，它们回顾过去：*She **seems to have** left.* 与 *there* 连用：***There seems to be** a problem.*
2. ***Look***（***as if / as though***、***like***）是非正式的、视觉上的对应说法：*It **looks as if** it's going to rain.*（*look like* + 分句很常见，尤其在美式英语中，但不正式。）
3. ***Tend to*** + 不定式把一个概括变成一种倾向：*Young people **tend to** use social media more.* 它是一个关键的学术模糊限制语（[[modality/hedging-boosting]]）。
4. ***Suggest***、***indicate***、***show*** 和 ***prove*** 报告证据所支持的内容，强度依次增加：*The data **suggest** / **indicate** / **show** that…*。（*Prove* 很少用于实证数据。）
5. **心理状态动词**——***think***、***believe***、***suppose***、***expect***、***assume***、***doubt***，非正式英式英语的 ***reckon*** 和非正式美式英语的 ***guess***——把一个陈述标示为说话人的看法：***I think** she's right.* ***I doubt** (whether) they'll agree.*
6. 对于 *think*、*believe*、*suppose* 和 *expect*，英语通常把否定放在**心理动词**上，而不是放在从句中：***I don't think** he's coming* 比 *I think he isn't coming* 更自然（Quirk et al. 1985；Huddleston & Pullum 2002）。
7. **道义动词**：***require***、***oblige***、***force***（必要）和 ***allow***、***permit***、***let***（许可）：*The rules **require** all players **to** wear helmets.* *Visitors are not **permitted to** take photographs.*
:::

::: example *Seem* 和 *appear* {#ex-seem}
用 *seem* 或 *appear* + *to* 不定式改写每个句子，选择不定式的正确形式。(a) *It seems that the computer is broken.* (b) *It appears that the fire started in the kitchen.* (c) *It seems that they are enjoying themselves.* (d) *It seems that there is a mistake in the bill.* (e) *It appears that she has been working here for years.*
::: solution
(a) *The computer seems to be broken.*——现在的状态用一般不定式。

(b) *The fire appears to have started in the kitchen.*——过去的事件用完成不定式。

(c) *They seem to be enjoying themselves.*——正在进行的活动用进行不定式。

(d) *There seems to be a mistake in the bill.*——保留 *there* 作主语。

(e) *She appears to have been working here for years.*——完成进行不定式。

不定式的形式与情态动词之后的形式相同（[[modality/what-modals-do#rule-modal-grammar]]）：*seem to have left* 与 *must have left* 相对应。
:::
:::

::: quiz
要表达你认为他不会来，哪种说法最自然？
- [ ] *I think he isn't coming.*
- [x] *I don't think he's coming.*
- [ ] *I think he doesn't come.*
- [ ] *I not think he's coming.*
::: solution
对于 *think*，英语通常把否定放在心理动词上。*I think he isn't coming* 合乎语法，但不太常用，听起来更强调。第三个选项对将来的安排用错了时态，第四个不是英语。
:::

## 选择形式

同样程度的确定性可以用许多方式表达：

- *The project **may** be delayed.*（情态动词）
- ***Perhaps** the project will be delayed.*（副词）
- *It is **possible** that the project will be delayed.*（形容词）
- *There is a **possibility** that the project will be delayed.*（名词）
- *The project **seems likely** to be delayed.*（动词和形容词）

它们并不是在任何地方都同样自然。选择取决于三件事。

1. **语域。** 会话用情态动词和 *maybe*、*probably*、*I think*；学术写作和官方写作更多地用形容词、名词以及 *suggest* 和 *appear* 这样的动词（Biber et al. 1999）。
2. **焦点。** 放在句首（*Perhaps…*、*It is possible that…*）使情态突出；情态动词或句中位置的副词则使它处于背景中。
3. **来源。** *It is said that*、*reportedly*、*apparently* 和 *seem* 把判断归于别人或归于表象；*I think* 和 *in my view* 使它带有个人色彩；*it is likely that* 使它非个人化。

::: example 一个论断，四种语域 {#ex-registers}
分别 (a) 在和朋友的会话中，(b) 在新闻报道中，(c) 在学术论文中，(d) 在求职面试中作为个人看法，表达“远程办公很有可能变得更加普遍”这个意思。
::: solution
(a) *I reckon more people will probably work from home in future.* / *Loads more people are going to be working from home, I bet.*——心理动词（*reckon*，非正式英式英语），副词 *probably*，非正式词汇。

(b) *Remote working is likely to become more common, according to a new survey.*——形容词 *likely* 用提升句型，并注明了来源。

(c) *It is likely that remote working will become increasingly common.* / *Remote working appears likely to become more widespread.*——非个人化的 *it is likely that*，以及用 *appears* 使作者进一步保持距离。情态处于最突出的位置。

(d) *I think it's very likely that remote working will become more common, and that's one reason I'm interested in this role.*——*I think* 使之带有个人色彩，*very likely* 给出强度，说话人把这个论断与自己联系起来。
:::
:::

::: note 英汉对比
- **“可能”是动词、副词和形容词——*possible* 只是形容词。** 他可能赢 → *He* ***may*** *win* / *He* ***could*** *win* / ***Perhaps** he'll win* / *It's* ***possible*** *(that) he'll win*。不说 ✗ *He is possible to win*：这是这个领域中学习者最常见的错误。同样，他很可能赢 → *He is (very) likely to win*，而不是 ✗ *He is very possible to win*。
- **“好像”“似乎”“看起来”。** 他好像病了 → *He **seems** to be ill* / *He **looks** ill* / ***It looks as if** he's ill*。“似乎”更接近 *seem* 和 *appear*，“看起来”更接近 *look*。
- **“据说”“听说”。** 据说他要辞职 → ***Apparently*** *he's going to resign* / *He **is said to** be resigning* / *He is **reportedly** resigning*（新闻）。*Apparently* 是口语中自然的选择。
- **“往往”“一般”。** 年轻人往往更喜欢网购 → *Young people **tend to** prefer online shopping*。在学术写作中，*tend to* 常常比 *usually* 或 *always* 是更好的译法。
- **“我觉得……不”。** 我觉得他不会来 → *I **don't think** he'll come*。汉语把否定放在从句中；英语通常把它移到 *think* 上。
- **“没有必要”和“必要”。** 没有必要道歉 → *There's **no need to** apologise* / *You **don't need to** apologise*。✗ *It is no necessary to* 是一个常见错误；*necessary* 也不能以人作主语（✗ *You are necessary to…*）。
- **“肯定”“一定”（副词）与 *definitely*。** 我肯定去 → *I'll **definitely** go*，而不是 ✗ *I will surely go*；*surely* 寻求同意或表示惊讶。
:::

::: history
很长时间里，语法书把情态看作情态动词的特性，表达情态的其他方式则留给词典去处理。珀金斯（Michael Perkins）的《英语中的情态表达》（*Modal Expressions in English*，1983）是最早把情态副词、形容词、名词和动词与情态助动词放在一起描述的著作之一，而莱昂斯的《语义学》（*Semantics*，1977）已经注意到情态动词和副词怎样“和谐地”组合（*may possibly*）。随后，语料库语言学显示了这些形式有多么常用。比伯和法恩根（Douglas Biber and Edward Finegan）的文章《英语中的立场风格》（"Styles of stance in English"，1989）和《朗文英语口语和书面语语法》（1999）把它们归入**立场**（stance）——说话人态度和把握程度的表达——并统计了它们在各种语域中的使用情况。霍伊（Leo Hoye）的《英语中的副词与情态》（*Adverbs and Modality in English*，1997）详细研究了情态动词与副词的组合。
:::

## 后续内容

本章的形式是学术写作中模糊限制与强化（[[modality/hedging-boosting]]）以及间接、礼貌的语言（[[modality/modality-politeness]]）的主要工具。学术阅读中的转述动词和立场在 [[academic-reading/stance-and-hedging]] 中讨论，会话与学术写作之间的语域差别见 [[collocation-and-register/register]]。

::: summary
- 情态不仅由情态动词表达，也由副词、形容词、名词和实义动词表达，既有认识意义，也有道义意义（[[#def-lexical-modality]]）。
- 情态副词通常位于句中位置，在否定助动词之前（*She probably won't come*）；*perhaps* 和 *maybe* 位于句首；单用作副词的 *likely* 主要用于美式英语（[[#rule-adverbs]]）。
- *Surely* 寻求同意或表示惊讶；*apparently* 转述你听到的事。
- 强度相同的情态动词和副词和谐地组合：*may possibly*、*must certainly*（[[#rule-harmonic]]）。
- 只有 *likely*、*unlikely*、*certain*、*sure* 和 *bound* 能用主语 + *be* + 形容词 + *to*：决不能说 ✗ *He is possible to win*（[[#rule-adjectives]]）。
- *Chance of* = 可能性，*chance to* = 机会；*no doubt* 的意思是“很可能”；*there's no need to* = *don't have to*（[[#rule-nouns]]）。
- *Seem*、*appear*、*tend*、*suggest* 和 *indicate* 使作者与论断保持距离；对于 *think*，否定通常放在心理动词上（[[#rule-verbs]]）。
- 根据语域、焦点和来源选择形式。
:::

## 练习

::: exercise 副词的位置 {level=1}
把副词放在最自然的位置。(a) *She will be late.*（probably）(b) *He doesn't know the answer.*（probably）(c) *They are right.*（certainly）(d) *We'll need more chairs.*（perhaps）(e) *I won't be there.*（definitely）
::: solution
(a) *She will probably be late.* (b) *He probably doesn't know the answer.* (c) *They are certainly right.* (d) *Perhaps we'll need more chairs.* (e) *I definitely won't be there.*
:::
:::

::: exercise *Likely* 还是 *possible*？ {level=1}
用 *likely* 或 *possible* 填空；有两个句子两者都可以。(a) *It's ___ that the shop will close early today.* (b) *She's ___ to get the job — she's the best candidate.* (c) *Is it ___ to book online?* (d) *Snow is ___ in the mountains this weekend.* (e) *It's ___ that I made a mistake.*
::: solution
(a) *likely* 或 *possible*（强度不同）。(b) *likely*（只有 *likely* 能用主语 + *to*）。(c) *possible*（= 能不能……？）。(d) *likely*（天气预报中也有 *Snow is possible* 的说法，意义较弱）。(e) *likely* 或 *possible*。
:::
:::

::: exercise *Chance of* 还是 *chance to*？ {level=1}
(a) *What are the ___ rain tomorrow?* (b) *I didn't get a ___ say goodbye.* (c) *There's little ___ finding the ring now.* (d) *This is your ___ show what you can do.*
::: solution
(a) *chances of*（可能性）(b) *chance to*（机会）(c) *chance of*（可能性）(d) *chance to*（机会）
:::
:::

::: exercise 不用情态动词改写 {level=2}
用括号中的词，不用情态动词改写每个句子。(a) *The results may be wrong.*（possibility）(b) *She must be over thirty.*（certainly）(c) *Students don't have to buy the book.*（need）(d) *The fire might have started in the kitchen.*（appear）(e) *Prices will probably rise.*（likely）(f) *Women usually live longer than men.*（tend）
::: solution
(a) *There is a possibility that the results are wrong.* (b) *She is certainly over thirty.* (c) *There is no need for students to buy the book.* (d) *The fire appears to have started in the kitchen.* (e) *Prices are likely to rise.* / *It is likely that prices will rise.* (f) *Women tend to live longer than men.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 改错 {level=2}
(a) *It is possible for the train to be late today.*（作为猜测）(b) *He is probable to resign.* (c) *I think she doesn't like me.*（改得更自然）(d) *Surely I will come to your party!*（意思是：我一定会来）(e) *We have the possibility to visit the factory next week.* (f) *It is necessary that you to come.*
::: solution
(a) *The train may / might be late today.* 或 *It's possible that the train will be late today.*（*possible for … to* 表达能做什么，而不是猜测。）(b) *He is likely to resign.* / *He will probably resign.* (c) *I don't think she likes me.* (d) *I'll definitely come to your party!* (e) *We have the opportunity to visit the factory* / *We have a chance to visit…* (f) *It is necessary for you to come.* / *It is necessary that you come.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 示证副词 {level=2}
选择 *apparently*、*obviously*、*allegedly* 或 *surely*。(a) *___ you're not going out in this rain?* (b) *___, the new manager used to be a professional footballer — that's what Sara told me.* (c) *The man ___ stole three cars before he was arrested.*（新闻）(d) *___ we need water to live.*
::: solution
(a) *Surely*（惊讶，寻求同意）。(b) *Apparently*（传闻）。(c) *allegedly*（尚未证实的说法）。(d) *Obviously*（常识）。
:::
:::

::: exercise 翻译 {level=3}
译成英语，至少有三个答案不用情态动词。(a) 他很可能已经知道了。 (b) 据说这家餐厅很贵。 (c) 中国学生往往更重视书面考试。 (d) 我觉得这个计划行不通。 (e) 你没有必要那么早到。 (f) 看起来要下雨了。
::: hint
想一想 *likely*、*apparently* / *is said to*、*tend to*、*I don't think*、*no need*、*look as if*。
:::
::: solution
(a) *He is very likely to know already.* / *He very probably knows already.* / *He has very probably heard already.* (b) *Apparently this restaurant is very expensive.* / *This restaurant is said to be very expensive.* (c) *Chinese students tend to place more importance on written exams.* (d) *I don't think this plan will work.* (e) *There's no need for you to arrive so early.* / *You don't need to arrive so early.* (f) *It looks as if it's going to rain.* / *It looks like rain.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 分析一篇新闻 {level=3}
找出这篇简短新闻中每一个表达情态的说法，说明它属于哪种词类，以及它有多强。

*Officials said yesterday that the bridge is likely to remain closed for at least a month. Engineers appear to have found cracks in two of its supports, although the extent of the damage is still uncertain. There is a strong possibility that traffic will be diverted through the city centre, which will almost certainly cause delays. Local businesses are reportedly worried.*
::: solution
*is likely to*——形容词（提升句型）：很可能。*appear to have found*——动词：根据表象作出的推论，与来源保持距离。*uncertain*——形容词：未知。*There is a strong possibility that*——名词，带一个加强语气的形容词：相当可能。*will*——情态动词：预测。*almost certainly*——副词，带一个模糊限制语（*almost*）：非常可能。*reportedly*——示证副词：信息来自别人，而不是记者。七个情态表达中只有一个情态动词（*will*），这是新闻写作的典型特点。
:::
:::
