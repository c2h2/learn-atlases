团队聚餐后的第二天早上，玛丽亚姆（Mariam）和她的同事赵磊在聊天。

**Mariam:** *You weren't at the dinner last night! You **should have** come — it was great.*

**Zhao Lei:** *I know. I* ***could have*** *come, but I was so tired. Anyway, nobody told me where it was.*

**Mariam:** *Really? Tom **must have** forgotten to send you the address. He **can't have** sent it to everyone.*

**Zhao Lei:** *Or it **might have** gone to my junk folder. I **needn't have** worried about the dress code, though — I bought a new shirt specially!*

玛丽亚姆和赵磊对话中的每一句都用了同一个结构：**情态动词 + *have* + 过去分词**，每一句都在回顾过去。然而它们做的事情很不相同。*Must have forgotten* 和 *can't have sent* 是关于发生了什么的**结论**。*Should have come* 是对一件没有发生的事的**批评**。*Could have come* 描述的是一个**没有抓住的机会**。*Needn't have worried* 说的是某事做了，但**没有必要**。

汉语表达这些意义用的是很不相同的手段——一定是忘了，你本来应该来的，我本来可以来，其实不用担心——所以英语的这个结构必须当作一个系统来学。本章介绍这个形式、它所承载的两类意义，以及每个情态动词的细节。它以 [[modality/ability-possibility]]、[[modality/obligation-necessity]] 和 [[modality/deduction]] 中的现在时意义以及 [[tense-and-aspect/present-perfect]] 中的现在完成时为基础。

## 形式

::: definition 情态完成式 {#def-modal-perfect}
**情态完成式**（modal perfect）是情态动词后接**完成不定式**：*have* + 过去分词（*must have gone*、*should have told*、*could have been*）。由于情态动词没有自己的过去时，完成不定式是把情态意义与**过去时间**结合起来的主要方式。进行形式（*must have been sleeping*）和被动形式（*should have been told*）也是可能的。
:::

| 情态动词 | 肯定 | 否定 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| *must* | *must have* + 过去分词 | （*can't have* + 过去分词） | *She **must have** left early.* |
| *can't*、*couldn't* | — | *can't / couldn't have* + 过去分词 | *He **can't have** seen us.* |
| *may*、*might* | *may / might have* + 过去分词 | *may not / might not have* + 过去分词 | *They **might have** missed the bus.* |
| *could* | *could have* + 过去分词 | *couldn't have* + 过去分词 | *I **could have** won.* |
| *should*、*ought to* | *should / ought to have* + 过去分词 | *shouldn't have* + 过去分词 | *You **should have** asked.* |
| *will*、*would* | *will / would have* + 过去分词 | *won't / wouldn't have* + 过去分词 | *They'**ll have** landed by now.* |
| *need* | — | *needn't have* + 过去分词（主要用于英式英语） | *You **needn't have** paid.* |

::: theorem 情态完成式的发音和拼写 {#rule-weak-form}
1. 在正常的口语中，情态动词之后的 *have* 不重读，弱化为 /əv/，甚至 /ə/：*should have* /ˈʃʊd əv/，*could have* /ˈkʊd əv/，*must have* /ˈmʌst əv/。在表现口语的书面语中用缩略形式：*should've*、*could've*、*would've*、*might've*、*must've*。
2. 弱化的 *have* 听起来和 *of* 的弱读式一样，这就是为什么 ✗ *should of*、✗ *could of* 这样的拼写在非正式的书面语中很常见。它不是标准的，在经过编辑的英语中被视为错误。
3. 与现在完成时不同，情态动词之后的完成不定式可以与**明确的过去时间状语**连用：*She must have left **at six***（✓），但 ✗ *She has left at six*（[[tense-and-aspect/present-perfect#rule-pp-past]]）。
:::

::: proof
规则3可以用情态系统中的空缺来解释。现在完成时是一种选择：想说出一个过去时间的说话人会改用一般过去时。情态动词没有一般过去时，所以无论是否说出时间，*must have left* 都是把确定性与过去事件结合起来的唯一方式。Huddleston & Pullum（2002）正是因此把情态动词之后的完成体描述为一种伪装的过去时。完成不定式也可以有它通常的完成意义，即时间一直延续到现在或延续到将来的某一点：*They **should have** finished by now*（到现在为止），*I'**ll have** finished by Friday*（到将来的某一点为止；见 [[tense-and-aspect/future-forms#rule-future-perfect]]）。
:::

::: warning *Should of*、*must had*、*should told*
✗ *You should of told me.* ✓ *You **should have** (should've) told me.*

✗ *He must had forgotten.* ✓ *He **must have** forgotten.*

✗ *I should told you yesterday.* ✓ *I **should have told** you yesterday.*

✗ *She must left early.* ✓ *She **must have left** early.*

在情态动词之后，表示过去总是用 *have* + 过去分词。*Had* 从不跟在情态动词之后，过去分词也不能直接跟在情态动词之后。
:::

## 两类意义

情态完成式与现在时的情态动词一样分为两类（[[modality/what-modals-do#def-epistemic]]），但有一个重要的变化。

::: theorem 情态完成式的意义 {#rule-two-uses}
1. **认识的（对过去的推断）。** 说话人**现在**判断某事**发生过**的可能性有多大：*She must have left*（我确定她走了）。*He might have called*（也许他打过电话）。说话人不知道发生了什么。
2. **反事实的（过去的根意义）。** 说话人说的是**当时**什么是必要的、可取的或可能的，而句子暗示实际发生的情况正好相反：*You should have called*（但你没打）。*I could have won*（但我没赢）。*You needn't have paid*（但你付了）。说话人知道发生了什么。
:::

::: proof
变化在第二类中。现在时的根情态动词指向一个可能发生、也可能不发生的将来行动：*You should call* 让事情悬而未决。过去的根情态动词回顾的是一个已经确定的情况，如果事情按预期进行了，说话人通常会直接说出发生了什么（*You called — good*）。因此，选用 *should have called* 就暗示它没有发生。夸克等（Quirk et al. 1985）和斯旺（Swan 2016）描述了 *should have*、*could have*、*might have* 和 *needn't have* 的这种隐含意义。它是一种隐含意义，而不是意义中严格的组成部分：*You should have taken the train — and you did, luckily* 是可以说的。一个句子属于哪一类，和现在时的情态动词一样，取决于情态动词和语境；*could have* 和 *might have* 两类都属于。
:::

## 对过去的推断

::: theorem 认识的情态完成式 {#rule-past-deduction}
1. ***Must have*** + 过去分词：有把握地断定某事发生了：*The streets are wet — it **must have rained** in the night.*
2. ***Can't have*** / ***couldn't have*** + 过去分词：有把握地断定某事**没有**发生：*She **can't have heard** you — she had her headphones on.*（英式英语不说 ✗ *mustn't have heard*；美式英语有时用 *must not have*，和现在时一样：[[modality/deduction#rule-must-cant]]。）
3. ***May have***、***might have***、***could have*** + 过去分词：某事可能发生了：*I **may have left** my phone in the taxi.* *He **could have taken** the wrong road.*
4. ***May not have*** / ***might not have*** + 过去分词：某事可能**没有**发生：*They **might not have received** the invitation.*
5. ***Should have*** / ***ought to have*** + 过去分词可以表达对某件到现在应该已经完成的事的预期：*The parcel **should have arrived** by now*——常常暗示说话人开始担心了。
6. ***Will have*** + 过去分词：根据日常规律或一般知识作出的有把握的推测：*It's ten o'clock — the meeting **will have started**.* *You **won't have heard** the news yet.*
:::

::: example 发生了什么？ {#ex-deduction}
阅读情境，用一个情态完成式作出推断。解释其强度。(a) 迈赫迪的车不在停车场，他的外套也不见了。(b) 你的朋友说上周在巴黎见到了你，可你当时在杭州。(c) 你昨天发给索菲娅的电子邮件，她还没有回复。(d) 盘子干干净净，蛋糕不见了。(e) 现在是晚上九点，你哥哥的飞机预定八点半降落。
::: solution
(a) *He **must have gone** home.*——两条证据指向同一个方向；说话人很有把握。

(b) *She **can't have seen** me. It **must have been** someone else.*——说话人知道自己当时在杭州，所以朋友的说法是不可能的。

(c) *She **may / might not have seen** it yet* 或 *It **might have gone** to her junk folder.*——只是可能：有好几种解释。

(d) *Someone **must have eaten** the cake.*——有把握。（或者，幽默地说：*The children **must have been** hungry.*）

(e) *His plane **will have landed** by now*（根据时刻表作出的推测），或 *His plane **should have landed** by now*（预期——如果他还没打电话，也许还带有一丝担心）。
:::
:::

::: quiz
*I can't find my keys. I ___ them at the office.*
- [ ] *must leave*
- [x] *might have left*
- [ ] *should have left*
- [ ] *might left*
::: solution
说话人不知道钥匙在哪里，所以这是对过去的推断，用 *might have* + 过去分词。*Should have left* 的意思是把钥匙留在那里才是对的（但没有这样做）。*Might left* 不是一个可能的形式。
:::
:::

## 批评与遗憾：*should have* 和 *ought to have*

::: theorem *Should have* 和 *shouldn't have* {#rule-should-have}
1. ***Should have*** / ***ought to have*** + 过去分词表示某事本来是应该做的，但**没有做**。主语是 *you* 时通常是批评；主语是 *I* 或 *we* 时是遗憾：*You **should have told** me you were ill.* *I **should have studied** harder.* *We **ought to have booked** a table.*
2. ***Shouldn't have*** / ***ought not to have*** + 过去分词表示某事是错的，但**做了**：*You **shouldn't have shouted** at him.* *I **shouldn't have eaten** so much.*
3. *You shouldn't have!* 也是收到礼物时一种约定俗成的热情回应：“你太客气了（还买了这个）”。
4. 在英式英语中，***might have*** 和 ***could have*** + 过去分词可以表示**责备**，通常重读情态动词或主要动词：*You **might** have told me!* *You **could** at least have said thank you.*（= 你本来应该这样做，可你没有——我很不高兴。）
:::

::: proof
规则1是把 [[#rule-two-uses]] 中的反事实隐含意义应用于建议：建议来得太晚了，所以它只能是批评或遗憾。遗憾也常常用 *I wish I had…* 或 *If only I had…* 来表达（见 [[complex-sentences/unreal-past]]）。规则4中的责备是从可能性的意义发展而来的：*You might have told me* 字面上说的是告诉我是可能的——并暗示，既然这是可能的、容易的，不这样做就是一种失职。斯旺（2016）和夸克等（1985）把这种用法描述为英式英语的典型用法；它在很大程度上取决于语调。
:::

::: warning 过去的义务与批评
✗ *Yesterday I should go to the dentist, so I left early.* ✓ *Yesterday I **had to** go to the dentist, so I left early.*（一项已经履行的义务）

✗ *You should tell me yesterday.* ✓ *You **should have told** me yesterday.*（批评：你没有告诉我）

*Had to* 报告过去的义务（通常暗示行动已经做了）。*Should have* 报告没有被遵从的建议。
:::

## 错过的机会：*could have* 和 *might have*

::: theorem 反事实的 *could have* 和 *might have* {#rule-could-have}
1. ***Could have*** + 过去分词表示某事本来是**可能的**，或某人本来**能够**做到，但它**没有发生**：*I **could have been** a doctor, but I chose music.* *You **could have been** killed!*（这是可能的，但幸好没有发生。）*We **could have walked** — it was only ten minutes.*
2. ***Couldn't have*** + 过去分词表示某事是**不可能的**：*I **couldn't have done** it without you*（= 是你使它成为可能）。*The weather **couldn't have been** better*（= 天气好极了）。
3. ***Might have*** + 过去分词也用于没有发生的可能性：*If you'd asked, she **might have helped**.* *It was a dangerous road — we **might have had** an accident.*
4. 对于 ***could have*** 和 ***might have***，同样的词既可以是认识的，也可以是反事实的：*She could have taken the train* 既可以表示“也许她坐了火车”（我们不知道），也可以表示“她本来可以坐火车，但她没有”。由语境决定。
:::

::: proof
规则2显示了 *couldn't have* 与比较级和 *without* 连用的一种常见用法：*It couldn't have been easier* 是“这极其容易”的一种有力的说法。规则4中的歧义来自两类意义：*could have* 把可能性（可以是认识的，也可以不是）与过去时间结合在一起。说话人借助语境消除歧义，常用 *but*（反事实）或 *perhaps*（认识）。

用 ***may have*** 表示反事实的可能性——*Without the seatbelt, he **may have died***——在现代英语中很常见，尤其是在新闻写作中，但许多用法指南建议，在已知事件没有发生时用 *might have*，因为 *may have* 暗示我们不知道他是否死了。斯旺（2016）既提到了这一建议，也提到了实际用法。在严谨的写作中，用 *might have* 可以避免任何歧义。
:::

::: example 批评、遗憾还是错过的机会？ {#ex-counterfactual}
对每个句子，说明实际发生了什么，以及说话人的感受。(a) *You shouldn't have left the door unlocked.* (b) *I could have gone to university in Canada.* (c) *She ought to have warned us about the traffic.* (d) *You might have offered to help!* (e) *We couldn't have chosen a better day for the wedding.* (f) *I should have listened to my mother.*
::: solution
(a) 门没有锁。批评。

(b) 说话人没有去加拿大上大学，尽管那是可能的。对错过的机会的中性陈述，也许带有一点遗憾。

(c) 她没有提醒我们。批评。

(d) 听话人没有主动提出帮忙。责备，带有不满（英式英语，重读 *might*）。

(e) 我们选了一个完美的日子。非常满意：*couldn't have* + 比较级的意思是“这是最好的了”。

(f) 说话人没有听。遗憾。
:::
:::

::: example 回顾一个项目 {#ex-review}
团队负责人恩戈齐（Ngozi）正在为一次失败的产品发布写一份简短的回顾。她的笔记写道：*(1) Not sure why the website crashed — perhaps too many visitors, perhaps the new server. (2) Load testing was not done; that was a mistake. (3) We had the option of a soft launch for one city, but didn't take it. (4) We printed 5,000 leaflets; the event was online, so they were not needed. (5) I'm sure the press release went out late — journalists only wrote about it the next day.* 把每条笔记改写成一个带情态完成式的句子，并解释所作的选择。
::: solution
(1) *The website **may have crashed** because of the number of visitors, or the new server **might have been** the problem.*——认识的：原因不清楚，所以说话人提出几种可能性。

(2) *We **should have done** load testing.*——反事实的：没有做，这是一个错误。在报告中，*should have* 表达一条吸取的教训，而不指责某个人。

(3) *We **could have launched** in one city first.*——错过的机会：有这个选择，但没有用。

(4) *We **needn't have printed** 5,000 leaflets.*——传单印了，但没有必要。（美式英语：*We didn't need to print 5,000 leaflets*，由语境表明传单已经印了。）

(5) *The press release **must have gone out** late.*——根据证据（报道的日期）作出的有把握的推断。

这份回顾把两类意义混在一起：(1) 和 (5) 是关于发生了什么的，恩戈齐对此不确定；(2)、(3) 和 (4) 评判的是她知道结果的决定。
:::
:::

## 不必要的行动：*needn't have* 和 *didn't need to*

::: theorem *Needn't have* 和 *didn't need to* {#rule-neednt-have}
1. ***Needn't have*** + 过去分词表示某事**做了**，但**没有必要**：*You **needn't have bought** me a present*（但你买了）。*I **needn't have worried** — the exam was easy.*
2. ***Didn't need to*** + 原形和 ***didn't have to*** + 原形表示某事**没有必要**；它们通常暗示这件事**没有做**，但并不明说：*I **didn't need to** go to work, so I stayed in bed.* *She **didn't have to** pay, because it was free.* 带重音时，它们也可以报告一个不必要的行动：*I **didn't need** to buy it, but I wanted it.*
3. *Needn't have* 主要用于**英式英语**。美式英语通常用 *didn't need to*、*didn't have to* 或 *shouldn't have bothered*，由语境表明行动已经做了：*You **didn't have to** bring anything!*
:::

::: proof
两种形式的区别在于把什么放在过去。*Didn't need to* 把必要性放在过去：当时没有必要。*Needn't have done* 把行动放在过去，并从现在来评判它：它发生了，结果证明没有必要。夸克等（1985）、Huddleston & Pullum（2002）和斯旺（2016）都用同样的方式描述了这种对比。由于情态动词 *need* 在美式英语中很少见（在英国书面语中也在减少：Leech et al. 2009），*needn't have* 在各地都是两者中较少用的形式，但它是说“做了，但没有必要”的唯一简短方式。
:::

::: example *Needn't have* 还是 *didn't need to*？ {#ex-neednt}
完成每个句子并加以解释。(a) *We ___ (take) a taxi — the station was only five minutes away. We wasted twenty pounds!* (b) *The hotel had towels, so I ___ (pack) any.* (c) *You ___ (wash) the dishes — I was going to do them. But thanks!* (d) *Luckily, I ___ (cook) last night; my sister brought dinner.*
::: solution
(a) *needn't have taken*——我们坐了出租车，而这没有必要。（美式英语：*We didn't need to take a taxi*，由语境表明我们坐了。）

(b) *didn't need to pack*——没有必要，语境暗示说话人没有带。（如果说话人带了毛巾，就适合用 *I needn't have packed any*。）

(c) *needn't have washed*——碗已经洗了。（美式英语：*You didn't have to wash the dishes!*）

(d) *didn't need to cook*（或 *didn't have to cook*）——没有必要，也没有做。
:::
:::

::: quiz
*"I brought an umbrella, but it didn't rain."* 哪个句子报告了这个情况？
- [ ] *I didn't need to bring an umbrella, so I didn't.*
- [x] *I needn't have brought an umbrella.*
- [ ] *I mustn't have brought an umbrella.*
- [ ] *I couldn't have brought an umbrella.*
::: solution
伞带了，但没有用上：*needn't have brought*。第一个选项说的是说话人没有带伞。*Mustn't have* 不这样用，*couldn't have* 的意思是这是不可能的。
:::

## 条件句中的 *would have* 和其他情态动词

*Would have* + 过去分词是第三类条件句中的情态完成式：*If I had known, I **would have** come.* 它也可以单独使用，条件不言自明：*I **would have** helped you*（如果你开口的话——但你没有）。*Could have* 和 *might have* 也出现在同样的位置：*If we'd left earlier, we **might have** caught the train.* 条件句和混合条件句在 [[complex-sentences/conditionals]] 中全面讨论，情态完成式在间接引语中的用法（*She said she might have lost it*）见 [[complex-sentences/reported-speech]]。

::: note 英汉对比
- **没有完成不定式。** 在这类句子中，汉语用“了”“已经”“是……的”或时间词标示过去，情态动词保持不变：他一定已经走了 / 他一定是走了 → *He must have left*。直译往往会造出 ✗ *He must left* 或 ✗ *He must leave already*。记住：情态动词 + *have* + 过去分词。
- **“本来”“早该”与 *should have*。** “你本来应该告诉我的”和“你早该告诉我” → *You should have told me.* “本来”和“早”这两个词标示了反事实的意义，而英语仅凭完成不定式就承载了这个意义。
- **“本来可以”与 *could have*。** 我本来可以去北京读书 → *I could have studied in Beijing*（但我没有）。“差点儿”和“险些”常常对应 *could have* + 过去分词或 *nearly*：我差点儿被车撞了 → *I could have been hit by a car* / *I was nearly hit by a car*。
- **“其实不用”与 *needn't have*。** 你其实不用买礼物的 → *You needn't have bought a present*（英式英语）/ *You didn't have to buy a present*（两种变体都可用）。
- **“早知道”与遗憾。** 早知道就不买了 → *I shouldn't have bought it* 或 *If I'd known, I wouldn't have bought it*。第二种译法用的是第三类条件句（[[complex-sentences/conditionals]]）。
- **过去的“一定不”。** 他一定没看见 → *He can't have seen it*，而不是 ✗ *He mustn't have seen it*。
:::

::: history
在古英语中，情态动词的过去形式是真正的过去时。*should* 的祖先 *sceolde* 的意思是“不得不，有义务”，*might* 的祖先 *mihte* 的意思是“能够”。在中古英语和早期现代英语时期，这些形式越来越多地表达委婉和假设，而不是过去时间，于是完成不定式接过了把情态判断放到过去的任务——这就是本章描述的系统。丹尼森（David Denison）的《英语历史句法》（*English Historical Syntax*，1993）等历史语法著作详细追溯了情态动词的发展。把非重读的 *have* 拼作 *of*（*would of*）是弱读式 /əv/ 在现代造成的结果：两个词听起来一样，只有拼写能把它们区分开。
:::

## 后续内容

常与 *should have* 一起出现的、用 *wish* 和 *if only* 表达的遗憾，见 [[complex-sentences/unreal-past]]；第三类条件句和混合条件句见 [[complex-sentences/conditionals]]。情态完成式在学术写作中用作模糊限制语——*This may have been caused by…*、*The sample might have been too small*——在 [[modality/hedging-boosting]] 中讨论。

::: summary
- 情态完成式是情态动词 + *have* + 过去分词；它可以与明确的过去时间状语连用，在口语中 *have* 弱化为 /əv/——但决不能写成 *of*（[[#def-modal-perfect]]、[[#rule-weak-form]]）。
- 认识的情态完成式是对过去的推断：*must have*（确定发生了），*can't/couldn't have*（确定没有发生），*may/might/could have*（也许），*will have*（推测），*should have*（预计到现在已经发生）（[[#rule-past-deduction]]）。
- 反事实的情态完成式暗示发生的是相反的情况：*should have* = 应该做，但没有做；*shouldn't have* = 不该做，但做了（[[#rule-two-uses]]、[[#rule-should-have]]）。
- *Could have* 和 *might have* 表达错过的机会；*couldn't have* + 比较级的意思是“这是最好的了”；在严谨的写作中，对没有发生的事优先用 *might have* 而不是 *may have*（[[#rule-could-have]]）。
- *Needn't have* = 做了，但没有必要（主要是英式英语）；*didn't need to* / *didn't have to* = 没有必要，通常没有做（[[#rule-neednt-have]]）。
- 在英语用完成不定式的地方，汉语用“本来”“早该”“已经”和“是……的”。
:::

## 练习

::: exercise 形式 {level=1}
改正形式。(a) *She must had missed the train.* (b) *You should of asked me first.* (c) *They can't have saw us.* (d) *I might left my wallet at home.* (e) *He shouldn't to have said that.*
::: solution
(a) *She must have missed the train.* (b) *You should have (should've) asked me first.* (c) *They can't have seen us.* (d) *I might have left my wallet at home.* (e) *He shouldn't have said that.*
:::
:::

::: exercise *Must have* 还是 *can't have*？ {level=1}
(a) *The lights are off. They ___ gone to bed.* (b) *He ___ written this — he doesn't speak Russian.* (c) *You ___ been very happy when you heard the news.* (d) *She ___ finished already — she only started ten minutes ago.* (e) *Someone ___ taken my pen; it was here a minute ago.*
::: solution
(a) *must have* (b) *can't have* (c) *must have* (d) *can't have* (e) *must have*
:::
:::

::: exercise *Should have* 还是 *shouldn't have*？ {level=1}
为每个情境写一个句子。(a) 你没带外套，现在觉得冷。(b) 你的朋友吃了一整个比萨饼，现在觉得不舒服。(c) 你的同事没有保存她的工作，电脑死机了。(d) 你熬夜到凌晨三点，错过了早上的课。
::: solution
(a) *I should have brought a coat.* (b) *You shouldn't have eaten a whole pizza.* (c) *You should have saved your work.* (d) *I shouldn't have stayed up so late.* / *I should have gone to bed earlier.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 推断还是批评？ {level=2}
说出每个句子是推断（认识的），还是批评、遗憾或错过的机会（反事实的），以及发生了什么。(a) *They should have arrived by now — I hope they're all right.* (b) *You should have seen his face!* (c) *She could have been injured.* (d) *She could have gone home already — her bag isn't here.* (e) *He may have forgotten.*
::: solution
(a) 认识的预期：我们不知道他们是否已经到了；说话人预计他们已经到了。(b) 反事实的，但是约定俗成的说法：你没看到他的表情——那非常好笑或令人吃惊（这是说“他的反应很不寻常”的一种常见方式）。(c) 反事实的：她没有受伤，但那是可能的。(d) 认识的：也许她已经回家了。(e) 认识的：也许他忘了。
:::
:::

::: exercise *Needn't have* 还是 *didn't need to*？ {level=2}
(a) *I ___ (get up) early, so I slept until ten.* (b) *We ___ (book) — the restaurant was half empty. We could have just walked in.* (c) *She ___ (study) for the test, because it was cancelled before she started.* (d) *You ___ (bring) flowers — but they're beautiful, thank you.*
::: solution
(a) *didn't need to get up* (b) *needn't have booked*（美式英语 *didn't need to book*）(c) *didn't need to study* (d) *needn't have brought*（美式英语 *didn't have to bring*）
:::
:::

::: exercise 用情态完成式改写 {level=2}
用情态完成式改写每个句子。(a) *I'm sure she forgot.* (b) *It was a mistake to sell the house.* (c) *Perhaps they didn't get the message.* (d) *It's impossible that he wrote this.* (e) *It was possible for us to win, but we didn't.* (f) *It wasn't necessary for you to wait for me, but you did.*
::: solution
(a) *She must have forgotten.* (b) *We / They shouldn't have sold the house.* (c) *They might not / may not have got (AmE gotten) the message.* (d) *He can't / couldn't have written this.* (e) *We could have won.* (f) *You needn't have waited for me.*（美式英语 *You didn't have to wait for me.*）
:::
:::

::: exercise 翻译 {level=3}
译成英语。(a) 你本来应该早点告诉我的。 (b) 他一定是坐错车了。 (c) 我们其实不用这么早出发。（但我们已经出发了） (d) 她不可能看过这封信。 (e) 我差点儿就错过了飞机。 (f) 早知道你也来，我就等你了。
::: hint
(c) 是关于一件已经做了的事；(f) 是一个条件句。
:::
::: solution
(a) *You should have told me earlier.* (b) *He must have taken the wrong bus / train.* (c) *We needn't have left so early.*（美式英语 *We didn't need to leave so early.*）(d) *She can't / couldn't have read this letter.* (e) *I nearly missed the plane.* / *I could have missed the plane.*（*nearly* 更接近“差点儿”，因为它报告的是差得有多近）(f) *If I'd known you were coming too, I would have waited for you.*
:::
:::

::: exercise 写一件过去的事 {level=3}
写一段话（100–140词），讲一次出了问题的经历——一次旅行、一场考试、一次会议。说明发生了什么，对你不确定的原因至少作出两个推断，至少表达两个遗憾或批评，并提到一个错过的机会和一件做了但没有必要的事。
::: solution
答案不唯一。范文如下：

*Last spring my friends and I went camping, and almost everything went wrong. When we arrived, the tent poles were missing. Someone* ***must have taken*** *them out of the bag to dry, and we* ***can't have checked*** *before we left — or perhaps they* ***might have fallen*** *out in the car. We* ***should have made*** *a list. It also rained all night; I* ***should have checked*** *the forecast, and we* ***shouldn't have chosen*** *a site so close to the river. We* ***could have stayed*** *in a cheap hotel in the village, but we were too proud. The funny thing is that I* ***needn't have bought*** *new hiking boots — we never went hiking at all.*

检查：推断（*must have taken*、*can't have checked*、*might have fallen*），遗憾和批评（*should have made*、*should have checked*、*shouldn't have chosen*），错过的机会（*could have stayed*），不必要的行动（*needn't have bought*）。
:::
:::
