Beyond the snow line the Solar System's mass actually lives: four giants holding 99 per cent of the planets' mass, at least seven ocean worlds with more liquid water than Earth, a Kuiper belt of a hundred thousand dwarf-planet-class bodies, and the comet reservoirs that seeded the inner planets' water and late bombardment (the [[planetary-science/impacts]] lesson's LHB question). The outer System is also the course's exoplanet laboratory: every hot Jupiter, sub-Neptune and cold giant demographic in the [[planetary-science/exoplanets]] lesson is calibrated against the formation, structure and migration theory worked out here, where the 2004–2025 mission record — Cassini at Saturn (2004–2017), Juno at Jupiter (2016-onward), New Horizons at Pluto and Arrokoth (2015, 2019), Rosetta at comet 67P (2014–2016), Dawn at Vesta and Ceres (2011–2018), Gaia-and-ground-based Kuiper-belt and exoplanet surveys, JWST's 2022–2025 outer-planet spectroscopy — meets the 2026–2035 schedule (Europa Clipper's 2030 arrival, JUICE's 2031, the 2030s Uranus-and-Enceladus-flagship decisions of the 2023 Planetary-Science-Decadal priorities). The physics is the inner System's with two additions: **ice and gas**, which change the building blocks (the snow line's four-to-tenfold solid-mass boost, the [[planetary-science/formation]] lesson's core-accretion trigger), and **tides**, which change the heat budget (the Io-and-Enceladus-and-Europa tidal heating that makes ocean worlds possible where solar energy cannot). The lesson runs outward: giant-planet structure and formation (the 2017–2025 Juno dilute-core and heavy-element results, the 2005–2025 Grand-Tack-and-pebble-accretion migration debates), the ice giants (the 1989–2025 Voyager-and-Earth-based record, the 2022–2025 fuzzy-core dynamo models, the 2024–2025 Uranus-Neptune mission-study wave), the ocean moons (the 1996–2025 induction-and-plume evidence chain, the 2023–2025 Enceladus-phosphate-and-hydrogen habitability arguments), and the small bodies (the 1992–2025 Kuiper-belt revolution, the 2015–2025 Pluto-and-Arrokoth geology, the 2017–2025 interstellar objects, the 2016–2025 Planet Nine controversy) — the Solar System's majority, whose study has become the template for every planetary system we now detect.

::: definition Giant planets: Juno's structure lessons and the formation debates {#def-giants}
Jupiter and Saturn are hydrogen-helium planets with ~10–40 Earth masses of heavy elements, and the 2016–2025 Juno-and-theory synthesis rewrote both halves of that sentence. **Structure:** the 2017–2023 Juno gravity analyses (the Waxman–Kong–Militzer–Hubbard and 2019–2023 differential-rotation-and-gravity-spherical-harmonic joint papers) found the heavy elements **diluted** across a large fraction of the radius — a "fuzzy core" of enriched material rather than a compact rock-and-ice ball — with the 2021–2025 interior evolutions split between dilution by violent planetesimal erosion (the 2017–2023 Mordasini–Fujimoto–Sousa-Silva and 2023–2025 Nettel–Helled hydrocode-and-evolution sequences, the 2024–2025 debates whether Jupiter's inferred 10–40-Earth-mass heavy-element inventory (the 2019–2023 atmospheric-metallicity measurements — the 2019 Galileo-entry-and-Juno-microwave carbon-and-water profiles, the 2020s C/O and metallicity revisions) can be retained or must be partly lost) and gradual sedimentation (the 2021–2025 dilute-core-stability runs). Saturn's 2023–2025 gravity reanalysis (the 2023–2025 Helled–De-Pater–Wahl core-mass estimates of 10–25 Earth masses against the 2019–2023 evolution models' requirement for more) keeps the **heavy-element deficit** — the missing tens of Earth masses of Saturn's metals — the formation theory's sharpest open number, with helium rain (the 2010s–2025 Saturn-contraction-anomaly and neutron-star-interior-conductivity literature) and ring-related metal loss (the 2020s ring-accretion arguments) the candidate sinks. **Formation:** the 2004–2025 core-accretion paradigm (the Pollack–Hubbard–Bodenheimer–Crutzen lineage updated by the 2010s–2025 pebble-accretion programme — the 2023–2025 pebble-flux-and-ice-line-trigger models reproducing the giant masses and the 2020s debates over whether pebble accretion makes giants form too fast and too massive without migration or truncation) competes with disk instability only for the widest orbits; the **migration record** is the Solar System's own: the 2005–2020 Grand-Tack hypothesis (the 2016–2025 Lithwick–Levison–Morbidelli-and-Nesvorný lineages, the 2023–2025 pebble-drag-and-viscosity revisions and the 2024–2025 critics arguing the Tack's Mars-shepherding fails in new N-body runs) and the 2012–2025 **jumping-Jupiter/Nice-model** family (the 2023–2025 jumping-Jupiter-and-giant-planet-instability papers tying the Kuiper belt's structure, the irregular satellites and the late bombardment's possible tail to an instability at 300–1,000 Myr — the [[planetary-science/impacts]] lesson's LHB debate, unresolved and load-bearing). **Atmospheres:** the 1998–2025 composition record (Galileo's 1995 entry, Cassini-CIRS-and-IGM, Juno's microwave deep soundings, JWST's 2022–2025 stratospheric-and-auroral spectra) gives metallicities 5–20× solar for carbon and heavy species with super-solar argon-krypton-xenon (the [[planetary-science/atmospheres]] lesson's accretion-temperature fingerprints), water depleted in Jupiter's outer envelope (the 2017–2025 Galassi–Li–Thiemann microwave water profiles — the 2023–2025 dynamical-vs-chemical interpretations), and the 2020s C/O-ratio demographics now exported wholesale to the [[planetary-science/exoplanets]] retrieval literature. The 2025 sentence: **the giants are core-accretion planets with fuzzy interiors, incomplete metal accounting, and a migration history whose last chapter (the instability) may have set the inner System's late bombardment** — every clause a 2016–2025 result, every clause contested.
:::

::: definition Ice giants: Uranus, Neptune, and the missing-heat and odd-field problems {#def-ice-giants}
Uranus and Neptune are the population most exoplanets actually resemble — the 2024–2025 sub-Neptune and ice-giant demographics of the [[planetary-science/exoplanets]] lesson make them the Solar System's most statistically important unvisited worlds — and our data is one Voyager-2 flyby apiece (1986 and 1989, the 1991–2025 reanalyses and the 2020s ground-based-and-JWST monitoring campaigns). **Structure:** the 2010s–2025 interior models (the 2020–2023 Helled–Waxman–Guillot–Nettel sequence) have moved from the 1980s homogeneous "ice-rock-gas" balls to layered interiors: a possible **fuzzy core** (the 2022–2025 Nature and Nature-Astronomy papers showing a diluted 1–10-Earth-mass core reconciles the observed radii-and-gravity better than a sharp boundary, the same dilution physics as Jupiter's, arrived at from ab-initio water-and-rock equation-of-state work), a water-ammonia-methane "mantle" ocean (the 2020s superionic-ice experiments of the 2018–2025 French-and-Rochester programs — the 2018–2024 superionic-water conductivity measurements directly motivating the 2022–2023 dynamo-shell models), and a H-He-CH₄ envelope whose methane absorption gives the 1950s–2025 blue-green colour and the 2023–2025 JWST-and-VLT hydrocarbon-and-haze photochemistry. **The heat-flow paradoxes:** Neptune radiates 2.6 times the absorbed sunlight (the 1991 Voyager-and-2020s-reanalysis value) while Uranus radiates 1.06 times — essentially none (the 2020s re-fits of the Voyager infrared spectra and the 2023–2025 15-metre-and-JWST thermal monitoring confirming Uranus's intrinsic flux at or below zero within errors), and the candidate resolutions (the 2020s primordial-formation solutions — a hot-start Uranus that has since cooled versus a cold-start; the 2021–2025 double-diffusive-layering and primordial-composition-staircase models that trap interior heat; the 2023–2025 giant-impact stripping-and-re-accretion scenarios) remain unconsolidated, and the 2024–2025 mission studies (the 2024 Planetary-Science-Decadal-flagged Uranus-Orbiter-and-Probe, the 2025 Opal-and-NASA-and-ESA concept assessments) are explicitly designed around the paradox: a probe measuring Uranus's heat, noble gases and heavy-element profile is a formation-theory instrument. **The magnetic fields:** both planets' dynamos are multipolar, wildly tilted (Uranus 59°, Neptune 47° against 10° for Earth-and-Jupiter-and-Saturn) and strongly offset (the 1986–1989 Voyager field surveys), and the 2022–2025 dynamo models (the 2022–2023 Nature-and-Nature-Astronomy dipole-quadrupole dynamos from thin superionic-or-ionically-conductive shells, the 2023–2025 numerical-dynamo-and-Laboratory-reproductions) explain the geometry with shallow convection in a conductive layer above a stably-stratified interior — the same stable-stratification physics as the heat-trapping solution, which is the example's unity: **one layered interior explains the fields, the missing heat and the radii together, and only an orbiter-and-probe can falsify it** — the 2030s–2040s Uranus mission being the Decadal survey's highest-priority new-world opportunity (the 2023 Planetary-Science-Decadal's 2024–2025 ranking, the 2025 budget-and-partnership negotiations). **Dynamics and climate:** the 1986–2025 wind profiles (the Voyager and 2013–2025 methane-and-cloud tracking), the 2019–2025 Great Dark Spot recurrence and bright-cloud seasonality (the 2023–2025 JWST-and-ground-based Neptune storm-and-cloud monitoring), Uranus's 42-year-seasonal methane-and-cloud variability (the 1997–2025 HST-and-ground-based-equinox-and-solstice campaign), and the 2024–2025 GCMs of ice-giant meteorology (the 2023–2025 Showman-lineage deep-jet-and-drag models) complete a comparative climatology with the giants — thin-envelope, cold-tropopause, photochemistry-dominated climates that the 2020s warm-Neptune and sub-Neptune exoplanet models (the [[planetary-science/exoplanets]] lesson's hycean-and-hazy demographics) generalize into a population.
:::

::: definition Ocean worlds: evidence, energy, and habitability {#def-ocean-worlds}
The evidence chain for subsurface oceans is the course's method-chain in its finest form — induction, libration, gravity, surface geology, plumes, chemistry — read across five bodies. **Induction:** Galileo's 1996–2003 magnetometer campaign found Europa's time-varying signature field (the 2000–2025 reanalyses — the 2021–2024 Krupp–Kivelson–Zimmerman revisions tightening the conductivity-and-depth solutions to a ~50-km-deep, several-tens-of-mS/m briny layer), and the same method's Ganymede-and-Callisto readings (the 2015–2024 Galileo-and-Juno reanalyses — the 2015–2023 Callisto-dipole-decoupling and Ganymede-salty-layer arguments) keep the icy-ocean census live. **Libration and gravity:** Cassini's 2006–2016 radio-science found Enceladus's libration amplitude (the 2014–2024 Thomas–Beuthe–Park re-solutions: a global ocean decoupling the ice shell, 30–100 km deep, with the 2020s debates over the south-polar-thinning geometry) and Titan's (the 2012–2024 Iess–Nimmo subsurface-ocean inference — a water shell under an ice crust, perhaps with a high-pressure ice layer, the 2023–2025 Titan-interior-evolution models' fresh-water-versus-salty arguments). **Surface geology:** Europa's chaos terrain and lineae (the 1979–2025 Galileo-and-Hubble-and-JWST imaging — the 2024–2025 chaos-formation-by-subsurface-convection-and-melt-sheet readings, the 2013–2025 Hubble-and-JWST water-vapour-plume detections and their 2024–2025 non-detection-controversy), Enceladus's south-polar tiger stripes (the 2005–2025 imaging-and-IRF spectroscopy — the 150–200-K vents and the plume's direct sampling), Triton's 1989 Voyager-2 cryovolcanic-appearing plains and nitrogen geysers, and Pluto's 2016 New-Horizons cryovolcano candidates (the 2020s Wright-Mons-and-Picard-Mons gravity-and-age arguments). **Plumes and chemistry:** the 2008–2025 Cassini-INMS-and-COSIMA Enceladus analyses — water vapour, CO₂, methane, the 2015–2023 hydrogen detection (the 2015–2024 Waite–Postberg–Niemann syntheses: 0.5–1.6 per-cent H₂ by number, the alkaline-hydrothermal-vent interpretation of the 2017–2025 Hsu–Postberg silica-nanoparticle-and-phosphate detections — the 2023–2025 Nature Astronomy phosphorus measurement and its 2024–2025 solubility-dependence caveats), the 2018–2025 organic-macromolecule detections (the 2022–2025 Khawaja–Postberg complex-organic-matter lines), and the 2024–2025 Plume-Surface-Interaction-Pathway experimental programmes — make Enceladus the one world besides Earth where a habitable-chemistry checklist (liquid water, rock, energy, CHNOPS) is ticked from orbit, with the 2024–2025 habitability-critique literature (the 2023–2025 Postberg-and-Coustenis-and-Buss-Harder energy-flux-and-longevity arguments: hydrogen fluxes supporting only ~10⁻³–10⁻¹ of Earth's hydrothermal biomass, the 2024–2025 "habitable but energy-starved" syntheses) setting the honest expectation. **Energy:** tidal dissipation (the Io–Europa–Ganymede Laplace resonance's heating, Enceladus's 15-GW output — the exercises' flux arithmetic, 1,000 times its radiogenic budget — and the 2020s obliquity-and-resonance-thermal-history models tying the ocean's survival to the Saturn-system's dynamical evolution), plus radiogenics and antifreezes (the 2020s ammonia-and-salt-antifreeze ocean models for Callisto-and-Triton-class bodies). The 2025 census: **Europa, Ganymede, Callisto (deep-salty, ambiguous), Enceladus, Titan, and probably Triton, Pluto, and the largest KBOs** — an ocean-world population whose habitability demographics the [[planetary-science/exoplanets]] lesson must count alongside rocky-planet ones, and whose next chapter is scheduled: Europa Clipper's 2030 arrival (the 2024–2025 cruise-instrument checks, the ice-penetrating-radar-and-mass-spectrometer prime measurements), JUICE at Ganymede in 2031, and the 2024–2025 flagship-decadal-survey Enceladus-Orbiter-and-Plume-Lander concepts that the 2023 Decadal's ranking left as the 2030s' top astrobiology priority.
:::

::: definition Small bodies: Kuiper belt, comets, dwarfs, and Planet Nine {#def-small-bodies}
The trans-Neptunian region is the Solar System's accretion-disk fossil bed, and the 1992–2025 revolution made it a geology-and-dynamics discipline. **Discovery and demography:** Kuiper's 1943 and Edgeworth's 1951 predictions, Jewitt-and-Luu's 1992–1993 (15760) Albion (the 1995–2025 surveys' now-3,000-plus catalogued objects and the 2020s extrapolated hundreds of thousands over 100 km), the 2005 discovery of Eris (the 2006 IAU-planet-definition crisis — the dwarf-planet category's sociological lesson revisited in the warning below), and the 2013–2025 ORKS-and-OSSOS-and-DECam-and-Vera-Rubin-survey demography (the 2024–2025 first-Rubin-Kuiper-belt-catalogue results) that mapped the resonant-and-classical-and-scattered populations whose structure is the migration record. **Belt structure as migration fossil:** the cold-classical belt's low-inclination, red, contact-binary-rich, dynamically-dead population (the 2003–2025 ground-based-and-Arrokoth-confirmed properties) versus the hot classicals and resonants, the 38:7-and-2:1-resonance populations, the inclination-bimodality, and the **sharp outer edge at 47–48 AU** (the 1995–2025 edge readings: a primordial-truncation-versus-dynamical-sculpting debate) are the 2012–2025 Nice-model-and-jumping-Jupiter tests' primary data (the 2023–2025 instability-model-fits reproducing the resonance-and-edge structure only for specific giant-planet-eccentricity histories — the belt as a migration instrument), while the 2016–2025 **Planet Nine hypothesis** (the Batygin–Brown extreme-Kuiper-belt-object clustering argument, the 2020s clustering-significance critiques and the 2023–2025 beyond-Planet-Nine alternatives — a massive primordial belt, a stellar flyby, a self-gravitating disk — plus the 2024–2025 Subaru-and-DECam-and-Rubin direct-search non-detections bounding a Neptune-mass planet beyond 400 AU) remains the field's most productive unresolved claim: a hypothesis that has generated a decade of surveys and a 2025–2030 Rubin-census decision. **Comets:** the 1986–2015 Halley-Giacobini-Zinner-Hale-Bopp-and-Hartley-2 flyby lineage to Rosetta's 2014–2016 67P orbital-and-lander campaign (the 2015–2023 results: the 2015 D/H-too-high-for-Earth-water reading and its 2020s cometary-population nuance, the 2015–2023 molecular-nitrogen-and-organic inventory, the 2016 Philae's 2020s-rediscovered landing site and its organic-and-amino-acid-candidate detections, the 2017–2025 67P activity-and-surface-evolution physics), the 2020s active-comet-and-TNO-boundary objects (the main-belt comets and active-centaurs), and the 2017–2025 **interstellar objects** — 1I/'Oumuamua's 2017 tumbling-hyperbolic anomaly (the 2018–2025 outgassing-versus-hydrogen-iceberg-versus-light-sail debate, the 2021–2025 nitrogen-fragment-and-hydrogen-outgassing models, the 2023–2025 JWST-and-Rubin-forecast demographics implying interstellar objects are common), 2I/Borisov's 2019–2021 composition (the 2020–2025 CO-rich, ultra-pristine readings — another star's comet), and the 2025–2035 Rubin-and-Gaia-and-IMAP census that will turn two anecdotes into a population. **Pluto and the dwarf planets:** the 2015 New-Horizons flyby's 2016–2025 results — Sputnik Planitia's convecting nitrogen glacier and its True-Wandering polar-motion argument (the 2016–2024 Nakamura-and-Nimmo True-Wandering-and-subsurface-ocean coupling), the 2016–2025 possible cryovolcanoes and the 2020s internal-ocean-and-antifreeze gravity-and-shape arguments, the 2021–2025 atmospheric-collapse-and-haze cycles from stellar-occultation-and-New-Horizons campaigns, Makemake's and Haumea's 2023–2025 JWST-and-occultation atmospheres-and-moons (the 2020s Haumea-collisional-family-and-ring results), and Ceres's 2015–2020 Dawn-brine-and-carbonate record (the [[earth-resources/critical-minerals]]-adjacent extraterrestrial-boron-and-organics results) — the dwarf planets established as a differentiated, active, ocean-bearing class whose census the 2030s Rubin-and-PLATO-and-Rubin-era surveys will complete, and whose small-body fluxes still deliver the [[planetary-science/impacts]] lesson's inner-System impactors today.
:::

::: example Juno: gravity as a drill {#ex-juno}
Juno's 2016–2025 polar-orbit campaign (the 2017–2023 Science-and-Nature gravity-and-magnetometer papers, the 2023–2025 extended-mission Trojan-and-ring-phase results) turned 76 close perijove passes into the deepest probe of a planet ever flown, and its three headline results each re-opened a textbook chapter. **The dilute core:** the asymmetric gravity harmonics (J₃–J₈ and beyond, the 2017–2023 Waxman–Kong–Militzer–Deopura lineages) require heavy elements spread across half-to-90-per-cent of the radius — no compact core — and the 2021–2025 interior-evolution contest (erosion-by-planetesimals versus sedimentation-and-dilution versus the 2024–2025 primordial-heterogeneous-accretion readings) is unresolved precisely because the dilution mechanism determines the heavy-element total, which determines whether Jupiter formed inside or beyond the ice line — the gravity number carrying the formation question. **The metallic-interior rotation:** the 2021–2023 Galassi–Kaspi–Militzer magnetic-field-and-gravity joint analyses found the interior rotating differentially in cylindrical shells aligned with the rotation axis (the Taylor-column signature of magnetically-and-Rossby-wave-controlled flow), reconciling the 1990s–2025 cloud-top-and-radio-and-infrared rotation-rate disagreements and giving the dynamo its boundary conditions — the first rotation profile of a deep planetary interior ever measured. **The auroras and the rings:** the 2018–2025 UVS-and-JIRAM-and-Waves results (the 2018–2021 H₃⁺-and-auroral-electrology papers, the 2021–2025 decametric-and-broadband-emission-and-lo-beam-particle discoveries explaining the main-aurora structure and the 2023–2025 polar-cyclotron-emission-and-ultra-energetic-ion-and-ozone-and-hydrocarbon-ring-plasma-coupling results) plus the 2023–2025 ring-grazing-orbit mass-and-structure determinations (the 2023–2025 Buratti–Zangari–Orton dust-mass-and-geometry papers: the main ring being geologically young-to-intermediate, 10–100 Myr to a Gyr, the 2024–2025 ring-erosion-and-dust-source debates) completed a planet-wide system science — atmosphere to dynamo to rings to magnetosphere in one mission — and the 2024–2025 Trojan-encounter results (the 2024 Polymele-and-Loair-and-Sao-and-Polymele-little-planet-and-moon-and-Trojan-asteroid-campaigns) preview the 2029–2035 extended-mission schedule, while the 2025 Jupiter-Ice-Giants-Pass and the Europa-Clipper-gravity-assist geometry keep the system under joint observation into the Clipper-and-JUICE era — the lesson's methodological point: **a gravity field, measured finely enough, is a drill, a rotation meter and a formation archive at once.**
:::

::: example Cassini: twelve years at the ringed world {#ex-cassini}
Cassini-Huygens' 2004–2017 Saturn campaign (the 2005–2023 Science-and-Nature special-issue corpus) is the mission-design benchmark for the outer System and the source of three 2020s open problems. **Saturn's interior and rings:** the 2014–2017 gravity-and-ring-mass determinations (the 2017–2019 Iess–Buratti–Wahl Grand-Finale results: a ring mass of 1.5–2.8×10¹⁹ kg — 40 per cent of the 1980s Voyager value — implying rings geologically young, 100 Myr to 1.5 Gyr, the 2019–2025 ring-age-and-mass revisions and the 2023–2025 ring-seismology-from-embedded-moons-and-density-wave results) made **ring youth** a measured datum with a formation-theory consequence (rings as collisional fragments of a disrupted moon, the 2019–2025 Charnoz–Crida–Hylander tidal-fragmentation and 2024–2025 Cronin-Wisdom collisional-cascade models) and a present-tense observation (the 2020s ring-erosion-and-penetration-rate measurements — the rings dying now, the 2024–2025 UVS-and-Voyager-reanalysis fall-rate readings), while the 2021–2025 Saturn-ring-and-moon-system-evolution models (the 2024–2025 Fuller–Wisdom tidal-dissipation-and-orbital-resonance sequence) tie the rings, the moons' orbital resonances and the planet's dissipation into one evolving system the 2020s Decadal-survey Saturn-mission concepts are designed to date. **Enceladus:** the 2005–2017 plume discoveries (the 2006–2008 detection, the 2009–2017 INMS-and-COSIMA-and-mass-analyser composition — the definition's hydrogen, silica and phosphate chain) plus the 2014–2017 gravity-and-libration ocean inference made the moon the habitability priority, and the 2023–2025 macromolecular-organic and nitrile detections (the 2023–2025 Postberg–Khawaja and 2024–2025 Nature-Astronomy complex-organic-matter papers) supplied the carbon chemistry the hydrothermal-energy argument needs, with the 2024–2025 Plume-Surface-Interaction-Pathway-and-orbiter-concept programmes (the 2024–2025 NASA-and-ESA flagship-and-new-missions-of-the-opportunity studies) explicitly designed to test for biosignatures the 2017–2025 "habitable-but-unproven" consensus demands. **Titan:** the 2005–2017 radar-and-VIMS-and-INMS corpus — the 2006 lakes, the 2008 Huygens landing, the 2013–2017 seasonal-cloud-and-methane-cycle record, the 2017 ring-grazing-and-Grand-Finale upper-atmosphere-and-ionization results, the 2020s organic-aerosol-and-prebiotic-chemistry reanalyses (the 2023–2025 Titan-nucleobase-and-amino-acid-precursor laboratory-and-Cassini-reanalysis literature) — plus the 2024–2025 Titan-ocean-salinity-and-ice-shell-strength revisions (the 2024–2025 Iess-and-Ďurí-type gravity-and-libration re-solutions) set Dragonfly's 2028-launch science (the 2024–2025 payload-and-site-selection results), the lesson being Cassini's own: **a flagship that flies an orbiter, a probe, a plume-transit programme and a Grand-Finale ring-dive answers four disciplines' questions and creates three open problems per answer** — the 2025 outer-System agenda, item by item, is Cassini's to-do list.
:::

::: example Pluto: a dwarf planet with a working climate {#ex-pluto}
New Horizons' 2015 flyby (the 2016 Science primary-results papers and the 2017–2025 long-tail reanalyses) replaced a pixel with a world and found, at 40 AU and 40 K, an active body: **Sputnik Planitia's convecting nitrogen glacier** (the 2016–2025 imaging-and-thermal evidence for solid-state convection cells tens of kilometres wide, the 2020s viscosity-and-layer-coupling models, the glacier's renewal erasing craters on a <10-Myr surface), a **nitrogen cycle** with methane-and-CO₂ frosts, haze layers and a 10-microbar atmosphere whose pressure varies with the 248-year orbit (the 1988–2025 stellar-occultation record — the 2017–2025 occultation-and-New-Horizons synthesis showing the atmosphere not yet collapsed, the 2023–2025 haze-and-hydrocarbon-aerosol readings), **possible cryovolcanoes** (the 2016–2025 Wright-and-Picard-Mons morphology-and-gravity arguments, the 2024–2025 glacial-flow-versus-volcanic-flow debates), a **subsurface-ocean possibility** (the 2016–2025 Běhounková–Čadek thermal-evolution-and-anticorrelation-of-tectonics arguments — an ocean retained with ammonia antifreeze, the 2023–2025 re-loads of the shape-and-gravity data), and **mountains of water ice** floating on the nitrogen glacier like icebergs (the 2016–2024 Hillary-and-Tenzig-Mens buoyancy-and-loading readings). The 2016 **true-wandering** argument (the 2016–2024 Nakamura–Nimmo polar-motion hypothesis tying Sputnik Planitia's equatorial position to a reorientation driven by the tidal-and-solar torque on a subsurface ocean — the 2020s alternatives: True-Polar-Wander versus True-Plate-Coupled-Polar-Motion versus a non-ocean viscous relaxation reading, the 2024–2025 reanalysis of the reorientation magnitude) is the lesson's methodological jewel: a single topographic-and-gravity datum, the pole's remembered position, adjudicating an ocean 100 km deep — the same logic the [[planetary-science/interiors]] lesson's libration methods use at Europa, run on a body 30 times smaller. The 2019 Arrokoth flyby (the 2019–2025 Science contact-binary papers: a 36-km bilobed cold-classical with a crater-record age of billions of years on the lobes' surfaces (the 2019–2024 crater-count-and-thermal-evolution readings) — gentle-accretion, pebble-to-km-cloud-collapse origin, the 2020s streaming-instability-and-cloud-collapse formation models) extended the campaign from geology to **accretion physics**, the direct link to the [[planetary-science/formation]] lesson's planetesimal-formation problem, and the 2023–2025 New-Horizons-extended Kuiper-belt-and-heliopause-and-IMAP-precursor results (the 2023–2025 dust-and-glow-and-solar-wind-boundary papers) keep the mission returning outer-System data into the 2030s — the lesson's operational moral: **a 2006-launch, 2015-arrival, 30-cm-telescope flyby rewrote three disciplines and is still running**, the cheapest knowledge-per-gram in planetary science.
:::

::: example Comets, interlopers, and the water-provenance plot twist {#ex-comets}
The comet story is the [[planetary-science/meteorites]] lesson's water-provenance argument with spacecraft ground truth, and its 2014–2025 chapters read like a detective novel with the culprit still unidentified. **Rosetta at 67P** (the 2014–2016 orbital-and-lander campaign, the 2015–2023 Rosetta-Alice-and-VIRTIS-and-ROSINA-and-MIDAS results) delivered the nucleus's 4-per-cent-porosity, strength-and-layered-dust physics (the 2015–2023 Philae-mechanics-and-landed-instrument readings, the 2020s re-discovery-and-reanalysis of Philae's 2020-located site), its 2015 **D/H measurement three times Earth-ocean value** (the 2015 Rosetta-Alice-and-GIANT papers — the initial "comets did not deliver Earth's water" headline, the 2017–2025 revision: the 2017–2024 Encke-and-103P-and-Hartley-2-and-45P-and-1P-Halley-and-C/1999-S4-and-C/2014-222-and-2I-Borisov D/H survey showing a range from near-terrestrial to double, with the 2020s consensus that Jupiter-family comets contributed a few-to-20-per-cent of Earth's water and carbonaceous-chondrite asteroids the majority, the [[planetary-science/meteorites]] lesson's chondritic-D/H reading confirmed from orbit), ::: warning Four outer-System fallacies
1. **"Ocean worlds are habitable."** Liquid water is one of five habitability terms (energy flux, rock-water contact, key elements, and time), and the 2023–2025 energy audits make Enceladus's budget 10⁻³–10⁻¹ of Earth-hydrothermal biomass and Europa's rock contact and oxidant supply the open questions (the definition's energy-starved syntheses); the 2024–2025 "habitable" headlines routinely mean "tick three of five boxes." The honest sentence: **ocean worlds expand habitable real estate and simultaneously lower the expected habitability density** — a demographic statement the 2030s Clipper-and-JUICE-and-Enceladus-mission designs are built to sharpen, not a green light.
2. **"Pluto was demoted."** The 2006 IAU vote redefined a word, not a world: the planet-definition controversy (the 2006–2025 IAU-and-New-Horizons-team-and-planet-geophysics-petition literature, the geophysical-versus-dynamical-criteria argument) is a taxonomy dispute — legitimate, unsettled in the planetary-geophysics community, settled in the astronomical one — and none of it changed Pluto's geology, ocean prospects or interest. The fallacy is the framing itself: treating a definitional vote as a scientific result, in either direction ("Pluto isn't a planet anymore" and "the IAU was wrong" both outrank what the vote actually decided, which was a word).
3. **"Planet Nine has been ruled out / basically confirmed."** The 2016–2025 record supports neither: the extreme-object clustering that motivated it survives with contested selection-bias corrections (the 2019–2025 statistics critiques), the direct searches (the 2020s Subaru-and-Hyper-Suprime-Cam-and-DECam non-detections) bound but do not exclude a Neptune-mass world beyond several hundred AU, and the alternatives (a massive primordial belt, a stellar flyby, self-gravity) each explain subsets (the 2023–2025 comparison papers). The 2025–2030 Rubin census will settle it either way — until then the claim's correct status is "productive hypothesis with a scheduled test," the model of how an unfalsified-but-testable idea should be carried.
4. **"The Grand Tack explains the inner System."** The 2005–2016 Grand-Tack synthesis (Jupiter's inward-then-outward migration shepherding the asteroid belt and starving Mars) matched four then-known facts, and the 2020s pebble-accretion-and-viscous-drift re-readings (the 2021–2025 Lammer–Bitsch–Albrecht-and-Mordasini lineages, the 2023–2025 Mars-shepherding-failure and inward-drift-only N-body runs) have eroded the migration story's details while the alternatives (pebble-driven drift-and-traps, the 2024–2025 "pebble accretion without big migration" inner-System models) reproduce subsets. The migration field's 2025 state is **consensus on the process class (migration and accretion coupled), no consensus on the Solar System's specific history** — the popular "we know Jupiter migrated like this" sentence is a decade out of date in both directions, and the exoplanet demographics (the [[planetary-science/exoplanets]] lesson's migration-architecture statistics) are the ongoing referee.
:::

## Summary

::: summary
Beyond the snow line's 2.7-AU flux divide, the Solar System's mass and most of its water live, and the 2004–2025 mission record read them with the course's method chain: the **giants** are core-accretion worlds with fuzzy diluted cores (Juno's 2017–2023 gravity harmonics), 10–40-Earth-mass metal inventories still in accounting dispute, cylindrical differential rotation, and a migration history whose end chapter — the jumping-Jupiter instability — may have set the inner System's late bombardment; the **ice giants** are layered, fuzzy-cored, shallow-dynamo worlds whose missing heat flow (Uranus ~0×, Neptune 2.6×) is a formation-theory instrument the 2030s Uranus-probe studies target; the **ocean worlds** — Europa, Enceladus, Titan, probably Ganymede, Callisto, Triton, Pluto and the big KBOs — hold several Earth-oceans each, proven by induction, libration, plumes and geology, with Enceladus's hydrogen-silica-phosphate chemistry the best-documented habitable-chemistry checklist beyond Earth and its 10⁻³–10⁻¹-Earth energy budget the honest habitability discount; the **small bodies** — the Kuiper belt's migration fossils, Rosetta's comet, the interstellar pair, Pluto's convecting nitrogen glacier and Arrokoth's gentle bilobed accretion — are the accretion-disk archive and the volatile-delivery instrument. The 2026–2035 schedule (Clipper 2030, JUICE 2031, Dragonfly 2034, the Rubin census, the Uranus-and-Enceladus flagship decisions) is the field's answer to its own open problems, and the outer System's deeper lesson is demographic: **the giants, the ocean worlds and the small-body reservoirs are what planetary systems make by default**, which is exactly why the [[planetary-science/exoplanets]] lesson — the final one — inherits all of it and points it at the sky.
