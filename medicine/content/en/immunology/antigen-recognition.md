Antigen recognition is where immunology becomes molecular: **lymphocytes survey the world not as whole organisms but as peptides in grooves, antibody shapes against protein surfaces, and the entire adaptive response is organised around two questions - what is present and is it dangerous.** The answers (MHC presentation, T-cell receptors, immunoglobulins, and the co-stimulation that decides their meaning) are also the technology of modern medicine: transplantation, antibody drugs, checkpoint inhibitors and CAR-T all descend from this chapter.

This chapter covers MHC biology and antigen presentation, T-cell receptor recognition, immunoglobulin structure and generation, and the clinical diseases of each.

the great surprise - Tonegawa's 1976-78 gene-segment-somatic-reassembly proof in mouse myeloma and embryonic DNA, that lymphocytes rearrange their own genes (with Dreyer and Bennett's prescient 1965 hypothesis and Jerne's selective-theory-prehistory and Burnet's clonal-selection-clincher) and followed by Davis and Chien and Tonegawa's TCR-cloning and the Zinkernagel-Doherty MHC-restriction that together explain how a finite genome makes 10^11 receptors by combinatorics and junctional sloppiness rather than inheritance; the structural and engineering payoff - the crystal structures of peptide-MHC (Bjorkman-and-Wiley's 1987 class I and the followed class II) and TCR docking, the antibody-engineering-humanisation-through-CDR-grafting-that-turned-murine-mAbs into drugs and the phage-display-of-Smith-and-Winter (a Nobel shared with Winter in 2018) that made antibodies an evolved-materials science - [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]]; and the therapy wave it licensed - the rituximab-and-Herceptin-and-Humira lineage, the checkpoint inhibitors (Allison's CTLA-4 and Honjo's PD-1, Nobel 2018), the CAR-T from Grossman-and-Sadelain-and-Essex's first designs to the CD19 approvals and the bispecific and the engineered-autoimmunity-depletion and the HLA-typing-to-tissue-matching-to-eplet-matching policy-science that still allocates kidneys weekly - [[immunology/transplant-tumour-immunology]], [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]], [[medical-ethics-law/research-ethics]].**
:::

::: summary
- Lymphocytes see peptides-in-grooves and surfaces-in-context; MHC class I reads the cell's interior, class II reads the meal, and cross-presentation connects the two for immunity to things the APC itself did not infect.
- T-cell activation is arithmetic: signal 1 without signal 2 causes anergy, signal 3 writes the differentiation programme, and the checkpoint molecules are tolerance mechanisms that cancer therapy has borrowed.
- Antibody diversity is built by recombination first and somatic mutation second; the germinal centre is where affinity and isotype are earned and where lymphoma translocations occasionally go wrong.
- HLA is the strongest disease-association locus known and the reason transplantation is an immunogenetics exercise; it also carries the cleanest pharmacogenetic wins (abacavir, carbamazepine, allopurinol).
- Presenting, recognition and antibody machinery each have a named human disease - bare lymphocyte, hyper-IgM, agammaglobulinaemia - and knowing the mechanism tells you the infection risk and the vaccine answer.
:::
