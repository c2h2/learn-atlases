Adaptive responses are the second wave and the memory: **T-helper subsets orchestrate which effector mechanism runs, B cells deliver antibody with the right class and affinity, cytotoxic cells delete infected cells, and the whole system leaves behind cells that make the second encounter faster and stronger - the biological fact that vaccination and every immunological memory phenomenon, good and bad, depends on.**

This chapter covers T-cell differentiation and effector help, humoral immunity in practice, memory and its failure, and the cytokine grammar that names clinical syndromes.

and the immunotherapy feedback loop - the checkpoint-inhibitor and adoptive-transfer era re-discovering exhausted-memory-states-first-described-in-LCMV-chronic-infection-mouse-models-and-then-in-HIV-HBV-and-cancer-cohorts as a therapeutic-target-list and the tissue-resident-memory studies that changed how vaccinologists think about routes of immunisation (the intranasal-and-mucosal-vaccine-programmes as the frontier the pandemic-era respiratory-vaccine work finally funded) - [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]], [[immunology/transplant-tumour-immunology]].**
:::

::: summary
- Signal 3 is destiny: IL-12 writes TH1, IL-4 writes TH2, IL-6/IL-1/IL-23 write TH17, IL-6/IL-21 write TFH, and TGF-beta plus IL-2 write tolerance.
- Human knockouts of these programmes present with predictable organisms: mycobacteria-Salmonella for the IFN-gamma-IL-12 axis, candida for IL-17, staphylococci-and-helminth-and-allergic phenotypes for TH2-axis disease.
- Cytotoxicity is a pathway with genetic diseases; fever plus cytopenias plus ferritin after EBV is haemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis until excluded.
- Antibody executes by neutralisation, opsonisation, complement and ADCC; the conjugate-vaccine idea exists because infants and the asplenic fail at T-independent polysaccharide responses.
- Memory lives in long-lived plasma-cell niches and tissue-resident T cells; measles erases it, ageing and lymphodepletion distort it, and boosters exploit it.
- When a vaccine fails, check technique, host epidemiology and antigen-generalisation before invoking an immune defect.
:::
