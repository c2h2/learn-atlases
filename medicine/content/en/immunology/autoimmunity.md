Autoimmune disease is what tolerance failure looks like at population scale: **a dozen mechanisms, a thousand genome-wide risk variants and a few hundred clinical syndromes that nonetheless obey three organizing rules - autoimmunity is systemically predisposed but organ-specifically executed, antibody and T-cell pathology travel together, and the same pathways (B-cell help, interferon, IL-17, TNF, complement) now define the drug list.** This chapter teaches the general mechanism, then the prototype diseases by system.

This chapter covers the architecture of autoimmunity, SLE and the systemic autoimmune diseases, the organ-specific syndromes and autoimmune cytopenias, and the treatment logic that connects them.

**Autoimmunity was considered impossible until it was unavoidable: the von Behring-era horror of auto-aggression ("Horror autotoxicus", Ehrlich's phrase) and the Metchnikoff-hypersensitivity-experiments and Landsteiner's haemolysins and the Donath-Landsteiner paroxysmal-cold-haemoglobinuria proof that serum factors damage cells; the witebsky-and-orr-l-coons-era-1939-40s-rehabilitation-of-the-concept (the Coombs-test-adapted-to-clinical-medicine-1940s and the Hench-and-Kendall-cortisone-revolution-of-1949 that turned rheumatoid arthritis and lupus from death sentences into managed diseases and the Nobel and the adrenal-axis consequences still governing today's steroid prescriptions) - [[endocrine/adrenal]], [[physiology/hormones]]; the immunofluorescence-and-autoantibody-explosion (the Coons fluorescent-antibody technique of 1941 and the Hargraves 1949 LE-cell observation, the Doniach-and-Anderson 1957 reinterpretation of Hashimoto thyroiditis as autoimmune and the Holborow anti-nuclear-factor era and the the anti-DNA-attitudes and the McCarty 1948 discovery of rheumatoid factor from sheep-cell agglutinins) - [[blood/anaemia]], [[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]]; the animal-model-and-mechanism era - the NZB-NZW lupus mouse colonies of Murphy and Dixon, the BioBreeding diabetic rat, the NOD mouse and the experimental-allergic-encephalomyelitis guinea-pig models and the Witebsky's-postulates-formalisation and Burnet's negative-selection prediction that autoimmunity follows tolerance failure - [[immunology/tolerance-regulation]]; and the target-era - the TNF-blockade-serendipity-from-the-Maini-and-Feldmann-mouse-antibody-experiments-and-the-1993-lancet-paper-to-the-etanercept-infliximab-adalimumab-arms-race that changed RA and IBD and psoriasis medicine - [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]], [[musculoskeletal/skin]], [[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]]; the belimumab and anifrolumab B-cell-and-interferon-targeted lupus era; the anti-CD20-lymphoma-drug-turned-autoimmune-drug-rituximab-repurposing; and the newest twist - the CD19-CAR-T-cases-of-refractory-lupus-reported-from-Erlangen-and-beyond (the "reset" hypothesis versus the realistic expectation of remission-duration uncertainty) and the antigen-specific-tolerance-tries (the nanoparticle-and-peptide and anti-CD19-antigen-specific-approaches) still waiting to beat the honest scepticism built up over a century of partial cures - [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]].**
:::

::: summary
- Autoimmunity requires three permissive layers: susceptibility (HLA and polygenic immune regulation), a trigger (EBV and other infections, drugs, checkpoint blockade, tissue injury) and a permissive tissue environment.
- Sex, ancestry and environment matter: SLE, RA, MS, Sjogren and thyroid disease each have their own demographic signature that should raise or lower clinical suspicion.
- Serology is Bayesian: high-specificity antibodies (anti-CCP, ANCA, PR3/MPO, dsDNA with low complement) help, but ANA and RF without compatible clinical findings mostly harm.
- The prototypes to know cold: SLE (with its nephritis), antiphospholipid syndrome and ANCA vasculitis systemically; thyroid, type 1 diabetes, myasthenia, coeliac and ITP as organ-specific archetypes.
- Early, protocol-driven immunosuppression prevents damage; hydroxychloroquine, steroid-sparing, vaccination, cardiovascular and bone protection are part of every plan.
- The therapeutic frontier is pathway-specific (B-cell and interferon targets, complement blockade, antigen-specific tolerance) rather than broadly myeloablative, with CAR-T and tolerance protocols as promising, unproven extremes.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Choose the biologic {level=1}

::: solution
**Comorbidity-driven biologic selection ([[#def-systemic]], [[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]], [[immunology/tolerance-regulation]]): the shortlist - the TNF-inhibitors (the strongest-RA-evidence and the convenience of biosimilars and the demyelination and heart-failure-and-chronic-infection contraindications and the latent-TB-screening-protocol-with-the-chest-imaging-and-the-treat-LTBI-before-starting-rules) and the IL-6-receptor-blockade (the ferritin-and-CRP-blanking and the weight-and-lipid-and-bowel-perforation-cautions) and the T-cell-costimulation-blockade-abatacept (the COPD-and-older-and-hepatic-adjacency and the once-monthly convenience) and the rituximab (the seropositivity-and-lymphoma-history logic and the pre-treatment-HBV-and-immunoglobulin-checks) and the JAK inhibitors (the oral convenience and the age-over-65-and-cardiovascular-and-VTE-and-smoking-safety-signals that matter for this patient) - [[infectious-diseases/tuberculosis]], [[cardiovascular/hypertension]]; the comorbidity read - the uveitis history favouring not the JAK nor rituximab and the monoclonal-TNF (the adalimumab-class evidence for uveitis while etanercept is weaker) and the smoking-and-anti-CCP-erosive-severity-and-lung-risk conversation with cessation as a co-prescription - [[public-health/prevention-promotion]]; and the practicalities - the vaccination-update-before-immunosuppression-and-the-live-vaccine-rules-and-the-household-advice and the pregnancy-and-breastfeeding-plans (the certolizumab-pegol-placental-transfer-advantage) and the surgery-and-dental-procedure-timing and the cost-and-biosimilar-substitution-policies and the treat-to-target follow-up schedule with the DAS28-CRP arithmetic and the steroid-taper-calendar - [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]], [[surgery/perioperative-care]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]].**

::: exercise Interpret a low C4 alone {level=1}
Two patients: (a) treated lupus, C4 0.06, C3 1.0, dsDNA normal, no symptoms; (b) 19-year-old with arthralgia, C4 0.05, C3 1.0, negative everything else, family history of angioedema. Manage each.
::: solution
**Complement-interpretation-medicine ([[#def-serology]], [[immunology/innate-immunity]]): (a) the isolated low C4 in a treated lupus patient - the causes (the complement-consuming-active-disease with low C3 too, the genetic C4-deficiency-lupus-predisposition with its low-or-absent-C4-and-normal-C3-pattern - often the explanation in a clinically quiet patient, the lab-variation and cold-activation-artefact check and the acquired-C1-inhibitor-deficiency screen where relevant) and the trap (treating a number as a flare, escalating steroids for a genotype) and the rational actions (the urinalysis-and-creatinine-and-dsDNA-and-blood-pressure trend as the truth and the repeat-testing and the C4-null-testing-and-family-counselling-and-encapsulated-bacterial-vaccine-if-deficient) - [[renal/filtration]], [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]; (b) young arthralgia with isolated low C4 - the hereditary-C4-deficiency-versus-early-lupus-versus-lab-noise differential and the monitoring-not-immunosuppression plan (the urinalysis-and-FBC-and-ANA-with-specific-antibody-only-if-positive strategy and the angioedema-family-history follow-up with the C1-INH-and-C4-during-symptoms-and-not-later-testing-and-the-HAE-diagnosis-implications for dental-and-surgery-safety) - [[immunology/hypersensitivity]], [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]]; and the general rule - the complement system's genes are polymorphic and low values are often constitution not activity, so the clinical picture decides; and the reporting etiquette (the "isolated low C4, likely constitutional, repeat if clinically indicated" comment that saves the next clinician a steroid prescription) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[genetics/genetic-testing]].**
