Hypersensitivity is immunology's four-way car-pile: **the Gell-Coombs classification (antibody-mediated, antibody-dependent, immune-complex and T-cell-mediated) remains the most useful clinical map in allergy, because it predicts the timing, the mediators and the treatment of every reaction from anaphylaxis to serum sickness to the rash on day nine of amoxicillin.** This chapter organises clinical allergy around that map, then adds the pseudo-allergic and mast-cell borders.

This chapter covers type I disease (anaphylaxis, asthma and rhinitis allergic mechanisms, food allergy), types II and III, type IV and contact allergy, and mast-cell and non-IgE reaction syndromes.

**Allergy is medicine's oldest patient-history science: the Corpus-Hippocraticum note that cheese can cause ill health and the medieval "strawberry rash" of Erasmus and the the sixteenth-century observations of rose-catarrh (the hay-fever description anticipating the Victorian epidemic); the Victorian-celebrity-hay-fever-epidemic (the Blackley's-1859-grain-of-pollen-proof-and-the-pollinosis-sufferers'-club-of-the-professions and the Charles Blackley experiments with his own nostrils) and the automatic-mechanistic-reversals of the era - [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]]; the anaphylaxis-and-prophylaxis arc - the Portier-and-Richet's-1902-Sea-of-Corsica-siphonophore-and-dog-anaphylaxis-naming (the "backwards-protection" of their phrase) and the Freund-adjuvant-era-mechanism-work and the Levine-and-Ovary's-principal-reactions-in-mice-and-the-atopic-allergy-of-dogs-with-ascaris-sensitisation-and-the-human-IgE-discovery-of-Ishizaka-and-Johansson-and-the-Cox-and-Ovary-schools' mast-cell pharmacology, the adrenaline-recognised-first-with-the-asthma-adrenaline-nebulisers-and-the-1970s-epinephrine-autoinjector-invention; the drug-allergy-epidemiology - the the 1960s the RAST-radioimmunosorbent-technique-revolution-of-Johansson-Wide-and-the-venom-and-latex-and-chlorhexidine-and-cisplat-in-anaesthesia-anaphylaxis-epidemiology that keeps the allergy clinic fed; and the modern-molecular-allergy-era - the component-diagnosis-multiplex-platforms-and-the-allergen-structure-database-and-the-IL-4R-and-IgE-and-TSLP-blockade-for-atopic-disease and the oral-immunotherapy-licensing-era-for-peanut and the recombinant-allergen-vaccine-hopes (the Bet-v-1-peptide-conjugate trials) that attempt the tolerance induction that the 1911 Noon's-pollen-extracts accidentally discovered empirically and a century of extracts delivered imperfectly - [[immunology/tolerance-regulation]], [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]].**
:::

::: summary
- The four types are a timing map: minutes (type I), hours-days (type II and III cytopenias and complexes), days-weeks (type IV drug rashes) - the clock is the diagnosis.
- Anaphylaxis is treated with intramuscular adrenaline, lying flat and repeat dosing; antihistamines and steroids never substitute, and cofactors explain the "it never happened before".
- Positive skin or blood IgE tests mean sensitisation; only symptoms mean allergy. Treat the history, not the number.
- The amoxicillin-in-glandular-fever rash and the "penicillin allergy" flag are a population patient-safety problem; structured delabelling saves lives.
- Serum sickness-like reactions, hypersensitivity pneumonitis and the immune-complex nephritides are type III diseases with low complements and raised CRP.
- Direct mast-cell activators, bradykinin angioedema and clonal mast-cell disease imitate allergy and each demands its own positive diagnosis and treatment.
:::
