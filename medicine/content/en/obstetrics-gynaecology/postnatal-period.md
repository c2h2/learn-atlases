The puerperium is the most medically neglected 12 weeks in medicine: women are discharged 'delivered and well' while the three biggest risks of the entire pregnancy - haemorrhage, thromboembolism and mental-illness decompensation - are still ahead or peaking. **This chapter covers the postnatal period as a clinical continuum: the immediate management of the third and fourth stages including the haemorrhage protocol, routine postnatal care and its evidence-weak checklists, the puerperal complications (infection, VTE, breast problems, urinary and pelvic-floor injury, thyroid and anaemia), contraception and the interpregnancy interval, and the perinatal mental health that is the leading cause of maternal death in the confidential-enquiry years. Its organising idea: the physiological recovery takes six weeks and the system's attention takes six hours, and almost every preventable maternal death happens in the gap.**

## The third and fourth stages

::: definition Postpartum haemorrhage and immediate care {#def-pph}
**Physiology first: the third stage (birth of the placenta) is managed actively (uterotonic given with or immediately after the birth - oxytocin 10 units IM/IV or the ergometrine combination where not hypertensive, delayed cord clamping or cord-exploration per policy, controlled cord traction with uterine counter-support) or physiologically (no uterotonic, upright birth, physiological expulsion, with the retained-placenta thresholds slightly different) - active management cuts haemorrhage risk by roughly two-thirds and is why the option is offered, with the trade-offs (nausea and BP from ergometrine, more retained placentas needing removal) honestly discussed for the low-risk woman who asks. Puerperal inversion (the rare catastrophic 'what is this mass' with shock disproportionate to blood loss - recognise, stop the breastfeeding-and-oxytocic-driven contraction from locking it, the Johnson-O'Sullivan-Stepman repositioning sequence, tocolysis to relax the ring, and the laparotomy-and-hydrostatic last resorts) is the one diagnosis the whole team must never miss. Postpartum haemorrhage - primary (within 24 hours, >500 mL minor, >1000 mL major; the measured, not estimated, blood loss: calibrated drapes and weighing swabs because visual estimation under-calls by half and the trend matters more than the single number) runs on the four Ts: tone (70 percent - the boggy uterus of overdistension, oxytocin exposure, chorioamnionitis, precipitate labour: the fundal-massage-and-bimanual-compression-and-uterotonic-ladder - oxytocin infusion, ergometrine if not hypertensive, carboprost if not asthmatic, misoprostol, and the tranexamic acid early per the WOMAN trial which cut death from bleeding when given within three hours), trauma (the cervix, vagina, perineum and the OASI-adjacent tears - examine properly under analgesia or in theatre, the third-and-fourth-degree repair is a consultant-and-specialist-interest job with the antibiotic-and-laxate-and-pelvic-floor programme and the long-term incontinence-and-dyspareunia follow-up that the audits demand), tissue (the retained placenta or clots - ultrasound if uncertain, removal in theatre with the accreta-thought when the plane feels wrong), and thrombin (the pre-existing or acquired coagulopathy - the HELLP and abruption and amniotic-fluid-embolism contexts, the viscoelastic-and-fibrinogen-guided replacement, the massive-transfusion-protocol with the calcium-and-temperature-and-acidosis vigilance); and the escalation ladder that is really a communications drill: call-out, second-runner, bimanual-and-IV-access-and-fluids-and-drugs, senior-anaesthetic-and-obstetric-review, the balloon tamponade (Bakri and the equivalents) that buys hours, the interventional-radiology-embolisation option where the anatomy and the minute allow, and the laparotomy-with-compression-sutures-uterine-artery-ligation-and-hysterectomy-when-needed - with hysterectomy-for-accreta-adjacent-planned-versus-desperate being the difference between a morbid operation and a fatal one. Secondary PPH (24 hours to 12 weeks - the 'soaking pads at home' with the fever-and-off-colour lochia: endometritis-and-retained-products - antibiotics plus/then-evacuation, the clot-with-a-caesarean-wound-infection nuance) completes the 24-hour-to-six-week vigilance that every discharge leaflet and GP letter should contain because the majority of readmissions for bleeding happen in the first 72 hours at home - [[blood/haemostasis-bleeding]], [[emergency-critical-care/shock-fluids]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/labour-birth]].**
:::

## Routine care and its complications

::: definition The postnatal weeks: checks, complications, and the puerperal triad {#def-routine}
**Routine care is short on evidence and long on harm-avoidance: the UK schedule (the day-1-to-10 community maternal-and-baby checks with the 'new birth-maternal review' at 6-8 weeks replacing the older routine 6-week appointment), the content that matters (blood pressure in the hypertensive-risk women with the day-3-to-5 peak remembered, the wound-and-lochia-and-breast-and-bladder-and-bowel questions, the mental-health screen that is actually asked with a tool like the Edinburgh rather than assumed from appearances, and the contrition-defining question 'how are you feeding, and is it going OK?'). The puerperal triad: Endometritis and wound infection (the fever-and-uterine-tenderness-and-offensive-lochia after the risk factors - caesarean especially without prophylaxis, prolonged rupture, multiple vaginal exams; the broad-spectrum combination per local formulary with the 'not responding' workup - retained products, wound collection or necrotising fasciitis with pain-out-of proportion-and-systemic-toxicity which is a surgeon-not-a-phone-call situation); VTE (the pregnancy-associated hypercoagulability peaking postpartum, the risk-factor sums per RCOG Green-top-style guidance - BMI, immobility, caesarean, pre-eclampsia, thrombophilia-and-previous-VTE - with the antenatal and postnatal LMWH prophylaxis decisions and durations, the diagnostic pathway that avoids reflex CT imaging (the D-dimer is useless postpartum and leg USS and V-Q or perfusion-only CT strategies with abdominal-pelvic compression ultrasound for iliac suspicion), the 'collapse-and-cyanosis-in-bed-two' massive-embolism resuscitation, and the DOAC-versus-warfarin-versus-LMWH breastfeeding nuances); and breast disease (the cracked nipple and feeding pain that is mastitis until the breast is hot-and-wedge-shaped-and-feverish - keep draining the breast, antibiotics for the cellulitic phase, the ultrasound-and-aspiration-for-the-fluctuant-abscess-not-incision-first-reform, and the inflammatory-cancer-that-looks-like-a-non-resolving-mastitis-in-a-young-postpartum-breast rule everyone must tattoo on their brain). Add the pelvic floor and urinary retention-after-epidural-and-instrument (the catheter-and-bladder-scan-discipline), the stress-incontinence-and-prolapse-symptoms-that-are-physiotherapy-referrals-not-fate, the symptomatic-anaemia-iron-repletion that should be routine in the ferritin-depleted, the postnatal thyroiditis that presents as the 'anxious and exhausted' at 3-6 months, and the surgical-and-medical conditions that happen to occur postpartum (the new cardiomyopathy-of-pregnancy presenting as 'I can't catch my breath holding the baby', the post-dural-puncture headache that is diagnosable and epidural-blood-patch-treatable and should not end in a neurology MRI odyssey when the postural history is classic). The cross-cutting principle: a breastfeeding mother still needs her medicines - the UK Drugs in Lactation Safety Database and the specialist perinatal psychiatry service exist so that 'stop everything' is a decision, not a default - and every condition above can present as 'tired mummy', which is why the vital signs remain part of the assessment of any unwell postpartum woman until her blood pressure, pulse, temperature and urine have been normal and documented on the day she is being dismissed - [[paediatrics/newborn]], [[renal/water-sodium]], [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]].**
:::

## Contraception, fertility and mind

::: definition Postpartum contraception and perinatal mental health {#def-after}
**Contraception starts at day 21, not at the six-week check: ovulation can precede the first bleed, the fertile window is back before the period, and the LARC conversation (implant and coil postpartum insertion programmes - the implant at caesarean or within 48 hours or after week 4, the IUD inserted within 48 hours or after 4 weeks with the perforation-and-expulsion counselling, DMPA's timing-and-breastfeeding position, and the progestogen-only-pill-start-any-time-with-2-day-precaution simplicity versus the combined-pill-breastfeeding-and-VTE-risk exclusion until 6 weeks-or-more-per-guidance) is one of primary care's and maternity's most consequential missed appointments - the audit question being how many women had the conversation antenatally and the method fitted before discharge, not how many came back. Fertility and interpregnancy: the evidence-based advice that short interpregnancy intervals (under 6 months to a year) carry higher risks of adverse outcomes informs the spacing conversation alongside the woman's age, obstetric history and desires - and the post-miscarriage-and-post-term-ovulation-return facts that replace the old 'wait three cycles' folklore. Perinatal mental health is the chapter's mortality story: the baby-blues (days 3-10, self-limiting, the support-and-reassurance the name deserves but not the medicalisation), postnatal depression (any prevalence from 10 percent upward, the insidious-or-acute presentation, the EPDS as conversation-opener not verdict, the first-question being 'have you been depressed before or in a previous pregnancy', and the treatment ladder - peer support and voluntary-sector and partner-included practical help for mild, psychological therapy, and the antidepressants with lactation-safety data where breastfeeding matters, choosing on her history including the previous-response-not-family-preference myth), the disorders needing psychiatry now (the pre-existing bipolar illness where late pregnancy and the first two weeks postpartum are the relapse-risk peak of a lifetime - the pre-delivery planning with the psychiatrist about lithium continuation-versus-stop-before-delivery, the feed-or-medicate conversation had in advance with written plans, and the sleep-protection strategies that are treatment not indulgence; puerperal psychosis - the elation-or-confusion-and-insomnia-and-paranoia within days to weeks, which is a psychiatric admission usually with the mother and the baby kept together where safely possible, the rapid response to mood-stabilising treatment, and the rarity of the attachment-infanticide headlines that makes it newsworthy - the risk being frighteningly concentrated in the untreated acute weeks), and the birth-trauma-PTSD (the intrapartum fear-of-dying re-experienced, the avoidance of appointments and the future-pregnancy-decision distortion, treated with trauma-focused therapy - and the prevention being the debrief-and-believe culture of the previous chapter). The service architecture that the confidential enquiries keep demanding: the specialist perinatal mental-health services with open-access community and inpatient beds, the 24-hour phone number midwives actually have, the joint maternal-mental-health clinics for the high-risk women before delivery rather than after the crisis, the safeguarding balance (asking about thoughts-of-harm directly, documenting, and knowing that most women with mental illness are not risking harm and an automatic mother-and-baby-separation reflex teaches every other mother not to disclose), and the equity and workforce truth that racial and deprivation disparities in maternal mental healthcare are as large as in physical care. The chapter ends where maternal medicine always ends: the woman who is tired is not a diagnosis, the mother who asks for help about her mind deserves the same vital signs - a proper examination, a named plan and a follow-up with a date - and the system that discharges her from both books at once is the one the next report will name - [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/contraception-fertility]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]].**
:::

::: example The day-4 readmission {#ex-readmit}
A 32-year-old, day 4 after an emergency caesarean for cord prolapse, re-presents: 'wound weeping', temperature 38.9, CRP 210, pulse 118, BP 105/62, breast fine, lochia not offensive, and the wound has a 2 cm necrotic edge with surrounding purple dusky skin; she says the pain is 'the worst of my life'.
::: solution
**Necrotising-fasciitis-until-proven-otherwise ([[#def-routine]]): the recognition - 'caesarean plus day-4 sepsis plus pain-out-of-proportion plus dusky-necrotic-wound-edge is necrotising fasciitis until a surgeon has disproved it at operation; CRP and lactate and the trajectory support the picture but the diagnosis is visual and surgical, and the window is hours - the "broad-spectrum antibiotics and review in the morning" decision has its own serious-incident file in every country'; the bundle within the hour - 'the consultant obstetrician and the consultant surgeon both told now, the sepsis bundle (cultures, antibiotics covering gram-positives including MRSA where local, gram-negatives and anaerobes - the piperacillin-tazobactam-plus-clindamycin-or-meropenem-plus-clindamycin-per-local-formulary thinking, clindamycin for its toxin-suppression rationale), fluids-and-lactate-and-urine-output monitoring, the anaesthetic-and-theatre-preparation, and the VTE-and-ICU-planning because she will need either or both; the wound gets inspected by the operating surgeon, not photographed for the handover'; the after - 'repeated debridements, the ICU-and-abdominal-wall-reconstruction-adjacent wound management, the partner-informed-early conversation, the antibiotic-course directed by cultures and surgery, the secondary-PPH-and-wound-dehiscence vigilance, the thromboprophylaxis-and-antibiotic-breastfeeding-compatible choices, the mental-health-screen-and-bonding-support because she missed her newborn's first week in theatre, and the incident-report-accurate timeline - because the difference between the survivors of this diagnosis is the number of hours between the words "worst pain of my life" and the first incision' - [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]], [[emergency-critical-care/critical-care]].**

::: example The 10-week-old's mother who is 'not herself' {#ex-pnd}
A first-time mother at the 10-week check: not sleeping even when the baby sleeps, 'nothing matters, not even her', intrusive frightening thoughts she calls 'mad', EPDS 18, and she disclosed nothing at the 2-week visit because 'everyone was so pleased for me'.
::: solution
**Postnatal-depression-seen-at-last-and-not-too-late ([[#def-routine]], [[#def-after]]): the assessment now - 'ask the two key questions and the safety ones directly and kindly: the anhedonia ("can you feel anything good at all when she smiles?"), the intrusive-thoughts clarification ("are they frightening ego-dystonic images of harm that you would never act on, which are common in postnatal anxiety-depression, or are they welcomed, command-like or tied to beliefs about the baby, which are a psychiatric emergency"), the sleep-and-function picture, and the past psychiatric and obstetric history that should have been taken at booking; and the physical exclusion that is not optional - thyroid, anaemia, vitamin D where indicated - "tired mummy" is a differential not a verdict'; the action - 'the treatment matched to severity: for this moderate-severe picture with intrusive thoughts - a psychological therapy referral with the perinatal-mental-health-team involved (not a generic IAPT queue), an antidepressant discussion with lactation-compatible evidence (sertraline as the common first choice with the breastfeeding-risk-both-ways framing), the practical prescriptions that make recovery possible (protected sleep, help with the house, partner educated about what depression is not), and a follow-up with a date in this room; and the escalation threshold documented and given to her and her partner in writing; and the system note - 'the screening questions asked at 2 weeks "because the protocol says so" and the missed bipolar history at booking are the two service failures the case review will name, and the one that saved her this time was that the health visitor asked again and believed the answer' - because the enquiry-era lesson is that the illness is missed in the checking, not that checking is the cure' - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-antenatal]].**
:::

::: example The secondary PPH phone call at 11 pm {#ex-secpph}
Day 11 after an instrumental birth, a breastfeeding mother rings the ward: 'soaked two towels last night', passing clots, mild cramping, temperature 37.4, and she feels 'a bit floaty when I stand'.
::: solution
**Secondary-PPH-triage ([[#def-pph]]): the advice rule - 'day 11 with heavy bleeding and clots plus a symptom of postural change is an assess-tonight instruction, not a watch-and-wait: the "floaty" sentence is the one that decides, because secondary haemorrhage decompensates in the living room; she is told to come in with her handheld notes and to bring someone, not drive'; the in-hospital work - 'the bimanual-and-speculum-examination-for-the-cervical-tear-she-never-had-checked-and-the-concealed-bleeding-clues, FBC-and-coagulation-and-group-save, the transvaginal-or-abdominal-scan-for-the-retained-products-thickness-debate-with-a-protocol-not-a-guess, and the endometritis-thought-given-the-low-grade-temperature'; the treatment fork - 'if she is stable and the scan is unhelpful, the broad-antibiotic-plus-ergometrine-or-oxytocic-adjacent-medical-pathway-with-a-review-time-limit-is-legitimate; if products are suspected or the bleeding persists, theatre for examination and evacuation with the uterine-instruments-and-blood-ready-and-the-accreta-thought-for-the-instrumented-and-infected-uterus - evacuation of the septic postpartum uterus is a specialist operation, not a day-unit list item'; and the lessons recorded - 'the original discharge card worked in one direction only: she called and came; the woman who does not have the card or does not believe she is allowed to ring at 11 pm is the one the next report will quote - the audit of secondary-PPH outcomes is time-from-first-symptom-to-reviewed-by-a-clinician, and the system's job is shortening it by telling women loudly, repeatedly and in their language that this bleeding is a reason to ring' - [[blood/haemostasis-bleeding]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/labour-birth]].**

::: example The breast that will not heal {#ex-IBC}
A 34-year-old, 5 months postpartum, has had 'recurrent mastitis' of the left breast for 7 weeks: a persistent wedge of erythema and peau d'orange, no fever with the last two courses, axillary 'fullness', and the baby still feeding; she has taken two antibiotic courses from the GP and the nipple fissure 'improved'.
::: solution
**Inflammatory-breast-cancer-until-proven-otherwise ([[#def-routine]]): the rule - 'mastitis that does not resolve within 1-2 weeks of appropriate antibiotics and drainage, or that recurs in the same quadrant, or that presents without systemic sepsis while looking angrier each visit, needs imaging and biopsy now - inflammatory cancer and the lymphoma-and-granulomatous-mastitis-mimics hide in the "lactational abscess" folder of every breast unit's morbidity history, and pregnancy-associated breast cancer is diagnosed at a later stage precisely because the breast is expected to be troublesome'; the pathway - 'urgent bilateral ultrasound-and-mammography-with-biopsy-of-the-skin-or-mass, the breast-unit referral as a suspected-cancer pathway not a "wait another course", and the core-biopsy-of-the-persistent-mass-plus-the-punch-biopsy-of-the-peau-d-orange-decision-made-by-the-specialist-with-the-mRI-adjacent-staging-if-confirmed'; the feeding conversation - 'diagnosis does not automatically end breastfeeding but the decision is hers with the oncology team's honest framing (treatment timing, surgery-and-chemotherapy-adjacent-relactation-and-suppression-options, the bromocriptine-type-suppression-discussion-with-the-headlines-that-frightened-her-weighed-against-the-evidence-that-the-prolactinoma scare was wrong at these doses)'; and the system - 'the GP-to-maternity-to-breastfeeding-helpline-roundabout-this-woman-spent-7-weeks-in-is-why-the-postpartum-breast-problem-should-have-one-owner-with-a-record, and the "how many weeks from first symptom to biopsy in postpartum breast cancers" is an audit your unit can run this year and should, because it is the measure of how well you separate mastitis from cancer' - musculoskeletal/skin-cancer], [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]].**

::: exercise Prescribe postnatal analgesia {level=1}
Give a 48-hour multimodal plan after caesarean for a breastfeeding woman with a 9/10 pain score and a previous opioid-naive grandfather who says 'give her something strong'.
::: solution
**Postnatal-analgesia ([[#def-routine]]): the plan - 'regular paracetamol and an NSAID (both breastfeeding-compatible and the opioid-sparing evidence base for caesarean pain is strong), the local-anaesthetic-adjuncts-if-used-for-the-index-procedure, a weak-or-strong-opioid-rescue-dose-with-a-reason-and-a-review-in-the-notes-not-a-TLC-box-trickle, the stool-softener-and-fluid-started-with-the-first-opioid-dose-because-the-caesarean-bowel-and-a-rectus-diastasis-tenesmus-episode-will-break-her-trust-faster-than-her-incision, and the non-drug-necessities - the splinted-cough-pillow, the-positioning-teaching-so-she-can-lift-the-baby-without-her-abdomen, and the "this is the worst-pain day and it falls from here" prognosis sentence that reduces the 3am-call-volume better than another dose'; the grandfather - 'strong medicine exists and we are choosing the plan that lets her think clearly while breastfeeding and move safely; the pain scores and the function ("can you sleep, can you hold her") decide escalation, which reassures the family and the clinician simultaneously'; and the vigilance - 'the sedated-not-pain-free combination of opioid-plus-promethazine-adjacent-antiemetics-has-baby-sedation-reports-so-the-safe-sleep-talk-happens-with-the-prescription-and-the-same-day-follow-up-includes-the-baby-feeding-sleepily-in-chair-two' - [[surgery/anaesthesia-pain]], [[paediatrics/newborn]].**

::: exercise Decide the IUD {level=2}
Three women at the postnatal contraception visit: (a) day 26 after an uncomplicated vaginal birth, wants a coil; (b) 5 months postpartum, breastfeeding, no periods, "not relying on that"; (c) 3 weeks post-caesarean, on 6-week LMWH prophylaxis, wants 'the implant but worried about the old one I gained weight on'. Advise each.
::: solution
**Three-devices [[#def-after]]): (a) yes - after 4 weeks the copper-or-hormonal-IUD insertion is appropriate with the technique-and-perforation-counselling, self-or-clinic insertion where available, and the 'what would worry you' card; the 26-day gap is handled with a bridging method (the POP or the 2-day-precaution arithmetic) so the window closes; (b) the lactational-amenorrhoea-contraception rule taught properly - LAM is >98 percent effective only while amenorrhoeic and fully-fed-on-demand and under-6-months, and the first bleed or any top-up ends it, which is why 'I haven't had a period' is the most common sentence before an unintended pregnancy at 13 weeks postpartum; offer the implant, POP, DMPA or coil with the one-method-fits-her-life framing; and (c) the implant's old side-effect history answered with the actual evidence (weight-change data modest and individual, the bleeding-pattern counselling that predicts discontinuation best) and the alternative options priced in her values - and the LMWH context is contraception-neutral but a reason to coordinate her injection-and-follow-up-dates; each answer names the next contact because postpartum contraception is a 12-month relationship, not a prescription - [[obstetrics-gynaecology/contraception-fertility]].**

::: exercise Catch the cardiomyopathy {level=2}
A 31-year-old para 3, 3 weeks postpartum after an emergency caesarean, attends for 'breathlessness when I walk to the shops' and 'palpitations at night'; the health visitor has documented 'anxious new mother'; SpO2 96 air, HR 108, BP 104/66, crackles at the bases, and she cannot lie flat.
::: solution
**Peripartum-cardiomyopathy-until-echo-proves-otherwise ([[#def-routine]]): the recognition - 'new heart failure in the last month of pregnancy to five months postpartum is peripartum cardiomyopathy until an echocardiogram says otherwise, and the three sentences that always appear in the reviews: "she was told it was anxiety", "she had a normal antenatal course", and "no one auscultated her chest at the breathlessness visit"; the inability-to-lie-flat-at-3-weeks-postpartum-is-not-normal-and-not-anchored-on-anxiety'; the workup and first management - 'NT-proBNP (rises in heart failure not in new-motherhood), ECG, chest x-ray with the shielding-and-benefit-explanation, and the urgent echo - the same-day-echo-request-is-the-intervention sentence; then the standard heart-failure therapy with the pregnancy-and-lactation modifications (the ACE-inhibitor-in-breastfeeding nuance is actually permissive - captopril-and-enalapril-are-used; the ARNI-and-SGLT2-data-are-the-limiting-factors, and the diuretics-for-congestion-and-the-spironolactone-adjacent-decision-and-the-bromocriptine-suppression-strategy-that-some-protocols-adopt-to-remove-the-lactogenic-prolactin-driver-all belong to the heart-failure-specialist-and-the-obstetric-medicine-pair, not to the night registrar alone)'; and the hard counselling - 'the feed-versus-medicine-versus-maternal-survival-decision-made-explicitly-and-in-writing, the next-pregnancy-risk-conversation-timed-for-recovery-not-chaos (recurrence and the LV-recovery-dependent-danger-of-another-pregnancy must be said in words she can repeat), the multiparity-and-twinship-and-hypertension-and-pre-eclampsia-risk-factor-postmortem-for-the-next-woman-on-her-ward, and the audit that starts by counting her health-visit BP-and-SpO2-and-chest-examination-lines: if they are blank, the service failed before she reached you' - [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]], [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]].**

::: exercise Design the maternal-recovery service {level=3}
Your trust has a 6-week 'check' that is 40 percent attended, no postnatal mental-health follow-up loop, secondary-PPH readmissions with long door-to-assessment times, and midwives doing telephone triage without a protocol for 'bleeding at 3am'. Build the 0-12-week service and the metrics.
::: solution
**Postnatal-service-design ([[#def-pph]], [[#def-routine]], [[#def-after]]): the model - 'the puerperium as a clinical pathway with dates and owners: day-1-to-3-in-hospital-bundle-before-discharge (danger cards in hand and language, contraception plan or method fitted, mental-health screen, iron started), the community midwife contacts on days 3-5-10 with a scripted symptom set and a blood-pressure visit for the hypertensive group, a 24-hour-number-staffed-by-the-maternity-triage-team-with-the-notes-open (the 3am bleeding call gets a protocol: assess tonight if postural symptoms or soak-rate thresholds - the telephone-triage-tool-validated-in-units-like-yours, published and trained, not improvised), a 2-week-and-8-week-maternal-recovery-clinic (physiotherapy-first pelvic and core programme, wound and scar and diastasis assessment, the mood review with a re-screen, lactation-problems-as-clinical-not-lifestyle), and the GP handover document that arrives with the mother, not after her - because the 40-percent-attended-6-week-check-is-a-eulogy-for-a-design-that-assumes-women-come-to-us'; the mental-health loop - 'the EPDS-positive-or-disclosure flag generates a booked 7-day-callback and a warm handover to the perinatal team with consent, the severe-illness-plan-for-the-known-psychiatric-history-exists-before-delivery, and the discharge summary that says "she screens positive and here is who called" so the health visitor is not the safety net of last resort'; the metrics - 'time-from-symptom-to-assessment-for-secondary-PPH-with-the-triage-call-log, eclampsia-and-VTE-and-sepsis-readmission-within-42-days-within-72-hour-reviews, contraception-plan-in-notes-at-discharge, the mental-health-screen-to-treatment interval by deprivation quintile, the "did you understand the danger signs and whom to ring" day-7 telephone-audit percentage, and the maternal-experience question "did anyone ask how you were, not the baby" published because the 6-week clinic attendance rate is a proxy for exactly that'; and the governance - 'the perinatal-mortality-and-morbidity meeting reads the maternity-triage-call-log next to the readmission list, because the pattern in every preventable maternal death since Semmelweis is the same - a contact with a health professional that was not treated as a clinical opportunity - and the service above is one long argument against that sentence' - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**

**The puerperium's history is the history of puerperal fever and the politics of noticing: the woman who 'did well' then 'flagged' was, for three centuries, a problem of ward hygiene (the Semmelweis and Holmes hand-washing heresy of 1843-47, the mortality tables of the lying-in hospitals where 1-3 percent of mothers died and students passing from the mortuary to the labour ward carried the cause in their hands), then of surgical antisepsis (Lister's era reduced the great killer), and only late of the mundane - the endometritis that survived antisepsis until the prophylactic-antibiotics-for-caesarean trials of the 1990s, the retained-products secondary bleeds that keep reading back as 'she should have been told to come back'. The other three deaths followed their own slow curves: the puerperal sepsis-streptococcal-revival-and-the-public-health-laboratory-network, the venous-thromboembolism whose antenatal-and-postpartum-prevention is one of the few success stories the confidential enquiries could report for consecutive eras (the prophylaxis-guideline history - from the 1950s-60s observations that 'childbed fever' struck the legs to the risk-factor-summation protocols of the 2000s-2010s and the LMWH-era decline), and the haemorrhage history whose modern chapters are the balloon-tamponade-1990s adoption, the cell-salvage-and-interventional-radiology-adjacent conservative arsenal that saves uteruses, and the WOMAN-trial tranexamic-acid result of 2017 which did for postpartum bleeding what magnesium did for eclampsia - a cheap drug, a clear mortality signal, and a decade of slow implementation. The mental-health reckoning is the newest and slowest: from the 1930s 'puerperal madness' asylum admissions through the 'maternity blues' research of the 1960s that first normalised the non-pathological, the Cox EPDS of 1987 that made screening a questionnaire, the confidential-enquiry reports from the 2010s that said plainly that suicide was a leading cause of maternal death and that most of these women had contact with services, the Every Mother Matters and Each Baby Counts campaigns, the UK perinatal-mental-health-network expansion of 2016-2023 and the US Momnibus-and-blues-coverage battles, and the still-unfinished argument about screening: the EPDS finds risk and does not find women, and the enquiry findings keep naming the same failure - someone asked, she answered, and nothing with a date and a name followed. The fourth modern front is equity: the same enquiries show that maternal death and severe morbidity concentrate in Black, Asian, migrant, deprived and disabled mothers for reasons of access, comorbidity and treatment-not-given, and the language of 'obstetric anal sphincter injuries' becoming a published-with-audit-trail quality metric and 'continuity of carer' becoming an RCT-proven intervention mark the difference between a chapter of good intentions and a chapter with denominators. The history's refrain is one the first lying-in physicians would have recognised and the first hand-washer would have cheered: the puerperium kills slowly enough to notice, and every generation has to choose to - [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]].**
:::

::: summary
- The third stage: active management halves haemorrhage risk; measure blood loss, run the four-Ts ladder, give tranexamic acid early, and know inversion when the shock outweighs the visible loss.
- Primary PPH is a communications drill with drugs attached; secondary PPH and endometritis belong to the first two weeks and to every discharge conversation.
- The puerperal triad - infection, VTE, breast disease - plus the overlooked thyroid and the 'worst pain of my life' rule that sepsis and necrotising infection outrank the CRP number.
- Contraception is a day-21 conversation with a fitted method, not a six-week prescription; the LARC-postpartum programmes exist because the second pregnancy is often unintentional.
- Perinatal mental illness is the leading cause of maternal death; ask directly, distinguish intrusive thoughts from psychotic ones, protect sleep, and put a named follow-up date on the plan.
- 'Tired mother' is never a diagnosis: vital signs, a differential and a review date apply to the puerperium as much as the antenatal period.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Run the PPH call {level=1}
You are the SHOUT-equivalent on-call: 900 mL measured blood loss 40 minutes after a vaginal birth with an atonic uterus, oxytocin infusion running, midwife asking 'should I come back to theatre?'. Give your verbal orders in sequence.
::: solution
**The-PPH-first-call ([[#def-pph]]): the orders - 'yes, this is a major-PPH-protocol call: I am coming now and the second-midwife-and-consultant-obstetric-and-anaesthetic-calls happen with you, not after me; two large-bore cannulae with bloods including group-save-and-fbc-coags-fibrinogen; tranexamic acid 1 g IV now; bimanual compression while someone prepares the ergometrine (check BP) and carboprost (check asthma) doses; keep the oxytocin infusion running fast; catheterise and measure urine; start balanced crystalloid restricted to the needed volume and get the blood bank on the phone for the group-specific-or-O-negative units; and someone is writing the timeline aloud'; and the decisions en route - 'the next question is tissue-and-trauma: is the placenta complete and is the perineum and cervix sutured - the examination happens with the resuscitation, not after it; the balloon-tamponade decision is made at 1500 mL with the uterus still atonic rather than at 3000 mL; and the transfer-to-the-unit-with-radiology-and-blood-and-a-theatre-for-accreta-possibilities question is asked when the second drug fails, not at midnight' - [[blood/haemostasis-bleeding]], [[emergency-critical-care/shock-fluids]].**
:::
:::

::: exercise Sort the postpartum fever {level=2}
Fever day 2 after caesarean: (a) 38.4, wound clean, lochia mild, breast tender-lobular, urinalysis nitrite-positive; (b) 39.2, uterine tenderness, offensive lochia, on cefuroxime-prophylaxis; (c) 39.8, pain out of proportion at wound, HR 125, BP 96/58. Management for each.
::: solution
**Three-fevers ([[#def-routine]]): (a) the common mix - treat the UTI with a compatible antibiotic, the breast is engorgement-not-mastitis until the wedge-and-redness-and-rigors appear so drainage-and-feeding-advice, and the wound stays watched; nitrite-positive-plus-caesarean-plus-fever earns a urine culture and a renal-tract thought if it recurs; (b) post-caesarean endometritis on cephalosporin prophylaxis: escalate to the broad combination per local formulary (the clindamycin-plus-gentamicin-or-piperacillin-tazobactam-adjacent thinking with the 'what did she actually receive' check) and image for the retained-products-and-wound-collection-not-responding-in-48-hours questions - the non-responder gets an ultrasound and a senior review, not a second drug added by email; and (c) the surgical emergency described above - resuscitate, escalate to both consultants, and to theatre for the wound to be opened and inspected, because necrotising infection's mortality is the hours-before-debridement number, and (c) plus fever plus tachycardia outranks every blood test in the decision; the three cases are the three errors: treating the wrong fever, treating the right fever too narrow, and treating the lethal fever with antibiotics alone - [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]], [[renal/urinary-tract]].**
:::
:::

::: exercise Advise the bipolar mother antenatally {level=2}
A 28-year-old at 34 weeks, bipolar I stable for 3 years on lithium, asks whether she should 'stop for the birth and the milk' as her mother advises. Give the plan, not just the risks.
::: solution
**Lithium-at-34-weeks ([[#def-routine]], [[#def-after]]): the evidence and the shape - 'the relapse risk if lithium stops around delivery is high and early - the puerperium is the highest-risk window of her illness, and relapse costs her and the baby more than the well-managed medication risk; lithium is the most effective mood-stabiliser and is lactation-compatible with monitoring - the old "stop for breastfeeding" reflex has been replaced by a negotiated plan, not a ban'; the plan - 'the joint pre-delivery meeting (her psychiatrist, obstetrician, anaesthetist and midwife, with her): lithium continued with levels monitored in late pregnancy (the renal-clearance change means doses and levels are tracked like a transplant drug), the delivery-day decision on holding-or-continuing the dose (the fluid-shift-and-toxicity caution - often the dose is reduced or held in labour with a plan to restart, which the unit writes rather than improvises), the sleep-protection rota (the 4-hours-of-blocked-sleep rule as treatment), the warning-sign list the partner can read, the community mental-health-and-psychiatric-services follow-up days not weeks after discharge, and the feeding plan she actually wants with the baby's-and-her-lithium-level-and-thyroid-and-renal-monitoring if she feeds - the RCOG-NICE-adjacent shared-decision documented'; and the words for her mother - 'this is a plan, not a gamble: the statistics we both care about are her staying well enough to be the mother she already is' - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]].**
:::
:::

::: exercise Build the 72-hour discharge bundle {level=3}
Your unit's data: most PPH readmissions and two eclampsia presentations happen days 1-4 at home; 40 percent of women had no contraception conversation before discharge; the newborn is followed by health visitors while the mother is 'signed off'. Design the discharge bundle and its audit.
::: solution
**The-discharge-bundle ([[#def-pph]], [[#def-routine]], [[#def-after]]): the bundle content - 'every woman leaves with: the four named danger symptoms (bleed-through soaking or clots with faintness, fever with uterine pain, calf or chest symptoms, headache-visual-change-epigastric-pain) in a card that fits a wallet and states which number to ring (the 24-hour unit line answered by someone with the notes), the measured-blood-loss-and-haemoglobin record travelling with her summary, the iron-and-laxative-and-pelvic-floor-physiotherapy-references with actual appointments, the mental-health screen with a date attached for the high-risk history, and the contraception conversation documented with a method plan - implant-and-coil discussions antenatally when any caesarean-or-instrument-birth is planned, not "discuss at 6 weeks"; the appointment architecture - 'the day-3-to-5 community blood-pressure check for the hypertensive cohort done as a booked midwifery visit not a hope, the caesarean-wound review with a photograph policy, and the day-10-to-14 GP-shared check for the anaemic-and-depleted, and the 8-week maternal recovery review reframed from a form-exercise into the "what still hurts, what still frightens you, is contraception sorted, and how is your mood" conversation that the newborn-visit model currently gives the baby and not the mother'; the equity layer - 'the danger-symptom card in the ten languages of the maternity list, the "who rings if she gets worse at 3am and cannot drive" conversation with the household present, and the asylum-and-refugee-mother discharge with a named midwife because her next appointment is contingent on an address she does not have - and the bundle is not optional based on the woman's English or the midwife's shift'; and the audit - 'readmissions-per-1000-births-for-bleeding-and-eclampsia-days-1-to-7, the percentage-of-danger-cards-actually-in-her-hands-on-questioning-at-day-5, contraception-method-plan-in-notes-at-discharge, and the eclampsia-and-PPH-readmission-reviews-counting-the-delay-to-first-contact-her-side-and-ours-because-this-chapter's-evidence-is-that-women-come-back-and-the-service-has-to-be-listening-on-the-number-it-published' - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
