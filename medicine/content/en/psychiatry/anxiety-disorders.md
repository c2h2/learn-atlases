Anxiety disorders are the most common psychiatric presentations after depression, and the most mismanaged: partly because anxiety is a normal emotion whose disorder-status depends on degree, persistence and interference; partly because the disorders overlap each other, depression and every medical condition; and partly because the treatments - exposure-based therapy and time-limited medication - are well-evidenced but under-delivered while sedatives are over-delivered. **This chapter organises the family around one core abnormality - threat-learning that no longer updates - and shows the assessment that separates the disorders and their mimics.**

## The core problem

::: definition Fear, anxiety and the threat system {#def-threat}
**Fear is the response to present danger; anxiety is the anticipation of future danger with somatic vigilance - and the disorders are best understood as the defence-system apparatus (the amygdala-centred threat circuit, hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal activation, brainstem autonomic and startle reflexes, and prefrontal regulation that appraises safety) mis-calibrating: over-generalising danger, under-updating with safety experience, and being maintained by the avoidance that prevents disconfirmation (the exposure logic in one sentence: safety learning requires the feared outcome not to happen while the person is present, and avoidance robs the brain of that evidence) - [[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]], [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]]; the normality boundaries that cause most clinical argument: panic attacks as non-disorder events (common, benign, sometimes situational) versus panic disorder (recurrent unexpected attacks with persistent worry about the next and behavioural change), social anxiety that shades into cultural shyness and into avoidant personality, and the diagnostic-threshold philosophy relevant to every chapter here (the ICD-11 and DSM-5 descriptions plus the distress-disability-impairment test used in practice) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the medical mimics that must be excluded by history pattern not by test battery (palpitations-and-tremor: thyrotoxicosis, phaeochromocytoma, arrhythmias, hypoglycaemia, asthma inhalers and steroids; recurrent acute attacks: the cardiac and seizure differentials; chronic worry-and-fatigue: anaemia, arrhythmia, sleep apnoea) - [[endocrine/thyroid]], [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]], [[respiratory/control-breathing-tests]]; and the psychiatric neighbours whose co-existence is the rule not the exception (the depression-anxiety comorbidity with its shared treatment and separate prognostic weight, the substance use that self-medicates and then amplifies - alcohol's rebound anxiety and benzodiazepine tolerance-withdrawal cycles, stimulant-and-cannabis-induced anxiety states) - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]], [[nervous-system/epilepsy]].**
:::

## The disorders

::: definition Panic, GAD, phobias and OCD-adjacents {#def-anx-disorders}
**Panic disorder: abrupt surges of terror with somatic catastrophe-reading (the heart-pounding interpreted as infarction, the derealisation as madness - the catastrophic misinterpretation cycle that therapy dismantles), the agoraphobia that follows in a third to half (the avoidance of trains, shops and tunnels because attacks happened there), and the cardiology-and-A&E circulation this presentation runs (the ECG-and-thyroid minimum workup, the re-attribution letter that stops the next attendance) - [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]], [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]]; generalised anxiety disorder: the six-month pervasive worry with muscle tension, restlessness, irritability, sleep and concentration complaints (the geriatric presentation with 'nerves' idioms, the childhood separation-and-generalised forms covered in the lifespan chapter, and the depression-and-alcohol overlap that must be treated first or together) - [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; phobias in their three families - specific (blood-injury-injection syncope with its unusual biphasic vagal physiology and applied-tension technique, animals, situations), social (the embarrassment-anticipation loop with its performance-safety-behaviour maintenance and its alcohol-and-cannabis self-medication rates, and the selective-serotonin-reuptake-inhibitor plus CBT evidence base with social-rhythm adjuncts), and agoraphobia now a separate diagnosis - the exposure-hierarchy logic being the shared treatment grammar; and the disorders the classification moved out of 'anxiety' with reason: obsessive-compulsive disorder (the obsessions-compulsions cycle with its double inhibition and uncertainty pathology, the exposure-and-response-prevention therapy that outperforms reassurance, the serotonin-reuptake-inhibitor doses-and-durations higher-and-longer than depression, the insight specifiers and the body dysmorphic and hoarding relatives) and the trauma disorders (acute stress, post-traumatic stress with its intrusion-avoidance-cognitions-arousal clusters, the dissociative and developmental variants, and prolonged grief) whose management has its own trauma-focused therapy evidence (the trauma-focused CBT and EMDR programmes and the watch-and-wait-with-screening policy that replaced immediate debriefing) - [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]], [[psychiatry/eating-personality-disorders]], [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]].**
:::

## Treatment

::: definition Treating anxiety {#def-anx-treatment}
**Exposure is the active ingredient across the family: graded, repeated, prolonged-in-session-and-between, with inhibitory-learning discipline (the expectancy-violation emphasis that replaced pure habituation theory, the safety-behaviour removal that otherwise smugglers-disconfirm nothing, and the between-session homework that carries the effect) delivered by CBT programmes for panic (the interoceptive exposure - the straw-breathing and chair-spinning that teach the body it is not dying), social anxiety (the video-feedback and behaviour experiments), OCD (the exposure-and-response-prevention hierarchies with family-coaching to stop accommodation), PTSD (the narrative and imaginal protocols and EMDR's dual-attention mechanism whose efficacy is real and whose explanation is contested) and phobias (the single-session-virtual-reality-era treatments and their access promise) - [[clinical-skills/communication]]; medication, used well: serotonin-reuptake inhibitors first across panic, GAD, social anxiety and OCD (the same pharmacology as depression with slower onset even, the early-anxiety-worsening warning that causes first-week drop-out when unmentioned, the dose-titration-and-duration plans and the discontinuation-syndrome taper discipline) - [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]], [[pharmacology/patient-variation]]; benzodiazepines: the honest ledger - genuinely effective short-term for severe acute anxiety, panic and situational terror, and genuinely harmful when continued (the tolerance, the memory-and-driving-and-hip-fracture harms in older adults, the dependence-and-withdrawal-seizures, the disinhibition, the sleep-architecture damage, and the tapering-individual-dose-reduction-with-psychological-support evidence from the tapering-programme literature) - the clinical rules that follow: time-limit with an exit date, avoid in substance-use histories, prefer alternatives in older adults with the fall arithmetic, and never stop long-term users abruptly - the tapers are slow and the relapse-of-anxiety-during-taper is expected not failure (the benzodiazepine-deprescribing audits and the nurse-led tapering clinics) - [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; second lines with real evidence (the pregabalin-GAD debate with its misuse-drift warnings, the beta-blocker for performance-only social anxiety with its honest effect boundaries - the tremor not the thought, the azapirones' weak reputation, the hydroxyzine niche, and the MAOI and clomipramine OCD-and-panic backups with their monitoring), and the newer-and-adjunct layer (the D-cycloserine-as-exposure-enhancer research line, the psychedelics-in-PTSD-and-treatment-resistant-anxiety trials read with the same critical appraisal as any phase II enthusiasm, and the neurostimulation and digital-delivery research line) - [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]]; and the lifestyle-and-system layer that is not optional: caffeine-and-alcohol-and-cannabis review, sleep restoration, exercise with genuine effect sizes, patient education about the anxiety physiology itself (the interoceptive literacy that makes exposure make sense), the guided-self-help and digital-first step care with its equity monitoring, and the referral-pattern honesty that most anxiety never reaches psychiatry and gets primary-care management that should follow the same evidence - [[public-health/prevention-promotion]], [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]].**
:::

::: example The cardiology revolving door {#ex-panic-cardio}
A 41-year-old has had four A&E attendances in six months for 'heart attacks'; each time normal ECG and troponin; now he avoids driving, the tube and being home alone; he drinks 'a couple to take the edge'.
::: solution
**Panic-disorder-with-agoraphobia-in-practice ([[#def-threat]], [[#def-anx-disorders]], [[#def-anx-treatment]]): the diagnosis made positively, not by exclusion-residue (the attack semiology - abrupt peak, palpitations, choking, derealisation, the catastrophe-reading of body signals - explained to him in the language of his own attacks, with the two-line medical minimum: ECG review of the records, TFTs, and the ambulatory-monitor question only if the history says true syncope or palpitation-attacks-with-risk-factors) - [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]], [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]]; the alcohol interrogation done early and kindly (the self-medication pattern with its morning-rebound-anxiety amplification, the liver-and-quantity screen and the integrated plan, because treating the panic while the drinking escalates fails) - [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; the treatment offer stated as a programme not a prescription (the CBT-with-interoceptive-exposure referral with the waiting-list psychoeducation and the workbook-or-digital interim, the SSRI offered in parallel with the early-worsening warning, titration calendar and twelve-plus-month plan discussion, the breathing retraining taught as slow diaphragmatic breathing rather than 'don't hyperventilate' advice, and the driving-and-work graded-return plan) - [[clinical-skills/communication]]; and the system repair - the A&E-and-record note that names the diagnosis (the letter to the next clinician that stops the fifth troponin, the mind-body clinic and the anxiety-disorder nurse-led service as the step before re-attendance, and the alcohol-and-depression re-screen at follow-up because the trajectory of this presentation is comorbidity without active management) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]].**

::: example The tablets that stopped working {#ex-benzo-taper}
A 67-year-old has taken diazepam 5mg nocte for twenty-two years, increasingly for 'leg restlessness'; her falls clinic flags it; she says 'if I stop I'll die of the withdrawal you people caused'.
::: solution
**Benzodiazepine-Deprescribing ([[#def-anx-treatment]], [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]): the facts, said without blame - long-term benzodiazepines lose anxiolytic and sleep benefit with tolerance while the harms accumulate (the fall-fracture arithmetic at her age, the memory and driving impairment, the night-time dosing covering morning with its 'restless legs' possibly akathisia-adjacent or sleep-maintenance failure the drug created), and abrupt cessation is genuinely dangerous (the withdrawal-seizure risk after two decades is real - her fear is not ignorance, it is the dangerous half of the truth, and it must be honoured with a slow-medical taper not continued drift) - [[musculoskeletal/fractures]], [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]]; the taper architecture - convert to an equivalent long-acting dose (often diazepam itself), reduce by small steps (the 5-10-percent-per-two-to-four-weeks Ashton-derived practice with patient-paced pausing rules and the plateau-then-retry honesty), the nurse-led clinic model with contact-at-every-step, the withdrawal-symptom diary and the plan for bad-weeks (step-back-not-stop), and the reassurance that rebound anxiety during taper is transient and survivable and expected rather than proof of dependence-shame - [[clinical-skills/communication]]; the substitution questions - what was diazepam treating (the insomnia gets CBT-I with its durable superiority, the anxiety gets an SSRI or therapy, the restless legs get iron studies and a proper workup before any dopaminergic label), the opioid-and-alcohol-and-z-drug audit for stacked sedation, and the seizure-and-alcohol-withdrawal-cross-tolerance history that changes taper speed - [[nervous-system/movement-disorders]]; and the goal-setting - not every patient tapers to zero on your timetable (the harm-reduction version: lowest effective dose, no escalation, fall-proofing, driving counselling, review intervals), the patient's autonomy over pace documented, and the therapeutic alliance protected because a rushed taper that ends in a fight gets abandoned at 25-percent-reduction and discredits the whole programme) - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
:::

::: example The vet with snake phobia {#ex-adult-phobia}
A 26-year-old veterinary graduate cannot enter the reptile-housing ward; she has declined three jobs; she asks for 'a pill to take the fear off' before an assessment in six weeks.
::: solution
**Specific-phobia-in-a-deadline ([[#def-anx-treatment]], [[#def-threat]]): the honest medication ledger - no daily medicine reliably cures specific phobia (the SSRI evidence is weak-to-absent here), the benzodiazepine she is really asking for impairs the very learning exposure requires (state-dependent practice and amnesia-of-success are why sedated approach fails), and the propranolol trick belongs to performance tasks, not phobic avoidance - all said kindly because her instinct to pharmacise is normal - [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]]; the treatment that fits six weeks - one-to-one graded exposure with a therapist (the hierarchy from photographs to presence to handling, the session-pacing her fear curve allows, the expectancy-violation debriefs), the virtual-reality bridge if anxiety spikes at first contact, and the self-directed workbook-and-app support between sessions with the predicted-symptom diary that turns the bodily storm into witnessed data - [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]]; the practical scaffolding - the assessment-team conversation about what impairment remains and what accommodations are temporary (the honest occupational question: some careers need this fear managed, others route around it, and both answers are legitimate), the peer-support and the graduated-exposure-hours arrangement with the employer, and the relapse-prevention plan naming the first avoidance as the warning; and the short-term pharmacology honestly placed - situational sedation declined with the reason explained, and if she cannot engage in exposure at all, the short-therapist-intensive-single-session protocols (the one-session-virtual-reality trials and their genuine effect sizes) offered rather than a script that would sabotage her - [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]].**

## Where this leads

Assessment and differential in [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the depressive comorbidity in [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; trauma-adjacent management in [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]]; childhood and perinatal anxiety in [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]]; the pharmacology in [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]] and [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]].

::: history
**The anxiety disorders were the last to become respectable diseases: Freud's 1895 anxiety-neurochemistry argument that anxiety was a real clinical entity with a somatic basis (the 'actual neurosis' versus neuroses distinction, and his theory's long retreat) put anxiety into psychiatry before its mechanisms were imaginable; the war neuroses of 1914-18 (shell-shock's contested legitimacy, the W.H.R. Rivers heroics at Craiglockhart and the eventual acceptance that trauma could cause illness without cowardice) and again in Vietnam (the 1980 DSM-III introduction of post-traumatic stress disorder, explicitly modelled on the Holocaust-survivor and rape-trauma research of the 1970s) made the trauma disorders a diagnosis against military and psychoanalytic resistance - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the psychopharmacology arrived twice: the 1960s benzodiazepine era, chlordiazepoxide and then diazepam becoming the most-prescribed drugs on earth and then the iatrogenic-dependence crisis of the 1980s (the benzodiazepine-discontinuation-syndrome description by Hollister and the patient-led abolition campaigns that made prescribing law in several countries), and the 1987-1988 panic-disorder trials - Donald Klein's imipramine-versus-propranolol dissociation studies at the New York State Psychiatric Institute defining panic as a distinct entity and starting the SSRI era; and the behavioural therapy counter-revolution - the 1950s-60s systematic-desensitization of Joseph Wolpe (the reciprocal-inhibition dogma and its later theoretical correction), the Marks phobia-clinic work at the Maudsley, Meyer's compulsions-and-rituals exposure studies that made response prevention the OCD treatment, and the Barlow-and-Salkovskis-and-Clark panic-cognitive programmes whose expectancy-and-catastrophe models turned exposure into a teachable, researchable technology - [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; and the modern synthesis - the inhibitory-learning reformulation of exposure (the Bouton-and-Craske era's renewal-extinction context effects explaining relapse honestly), the trauma-focused-therapy trials and the NICE watchful-waiting policy for acute stress (the debriefing-evidence reversal as a lesson in doing nothing better than doing something wrong), the anxiety-sensitivity-and-interoceptive-exposure research, the digital-access wave and its equity audits, and the neurocircuitry era (the amygdala-prefrontal-model with its optogenetic refinement and its persistent gap between circuit mechanism and clinical formulation) - [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]].**
:::

::: summary
- Anxiety disorders are threat-learning disorders maintained by avoidance; the diagnosis is made positively from the attack-and-avoidance structure, not as a residue of negative tests.
- Medical mimics (thyroid, arrhythmia, phaeochromocytoma, substances) are excluded by history pattern and a short test list; A&E repetition means the diagnosis was never announced.
- Exposure-based psychotherapy is the specific treatment across panic, phobia, OCD and PTSD; medication (SRIs) is parallel and time-limited, not instead.
- Benzodiazepines have narrow, time-limited indications and large harms with continuation; long-term users need slow, supported tapers, never abrupt stops.
- Self-medication with alcohol and cannabis is part of the illness trajectory and must be treated in the same plan.
- Expectation management is therapy: the SSRI early-worsening warning, the taper timeline and the exposure homework rationale each prevent drop-out.
:::

**First-Rx-clinic ([[#def-anx-treatment]], [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]]): panic - SSRI at the lowest dose with a double-titration schedule (the activation warning explicit, the 2-4-week-settle-6-8-week-effect timeline, the 12-month-plus plan and the slow-taper-booked-ahead), benzodiazepine only as a clearly-exited bridge if terror prevents engagement, CBT referred in parallel - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; GAD-72 - prefer psychological-first (the CBT-and-exercise-and-sleep plan), and if medication then the hyponatraemia-history changes the calculus (the SSRI-SIADH-recurrence risk weighed against the mirtazapine-and-better-sleep-sedation profile and the fall arithmetic, sodium checked in week two, amlodipine-continued question), the pregabalin option declined for misuse-and-sedation reasons documented - [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[renal/water-sodium]]; OCD-26 - restart an SSRI (a different agent if the discontinuation syndrome was brutal, or the same with slower micro-titration), dose-titrate toward the licensed OCD ceiling with patience (the 10-12-week judgment rule), ERP rather than supportive counselling specified in the referral, and the discontinuation-syndrome plan written before the first taper-attempt (the gradual-reduce-not-abrupt rule and the 2D6-interaction-and-half-life selection between fluvoxamine-and-paroxetine-versus-venlafaxine-and-fluoxetine) - [[pharmacology/patient-variation]]; and the finals student - the CBT-and-skills route first (the group programmes and the digital CBT have the evidence for this severity), the performance-situation beta-blocker only if the profile is performance-specific (the propranolol dose-trial in advance and the honest effect boundary: tremor and voice not thoughts), and SSRI only with the finals-timeline discussed (the initial-worsening landing in exam week is a foreseeable adherence disaster and a treatable preventable one) - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]].**

::: exercise Manage the reassurance-seeker {level=2}
A patient with health anxiety books repeated tests for 'the palpitations', demands a heart scan, is angry at 'being told it's anxiety'; another asks for her benzodiazepine refill. Handle both with the same underlying skill.
::: solution
**Reassurance-and-safety-behaviour-management ([[#def-threat]], [[#def-anx-treatment]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]): the shared grammar - both patients are running an anxiety-maintenance machine (the checking-and-reassurance that reduces distress for hours and raises vigilance for months, the refill that trains tolerance-and-withdrawal-relief as 'needing the drug'), so the skill is warm refusal of the maintaining behaviour plus a stated alternative treatment (the two sentences every health-anxiety consultation needs: 'I can order tests when they change what we do; I will not order tests to make doubt disappear, because that's the thing the illness feeds on - here's what works instead' and 'the refill would help tonight and make this harder next year; here is the plan that fixes the sleep') - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]; health anxiety specifics - the cognitive model (the catastrophic-interpretation of normal sensation, the googling-and-checking compulsion, the doctor-shopping cycle), the CBT-for-health-anxiety evidence and its self-directed versions, the agreed symptom-monitoring-not-doctor-monitoring compromise, the single-coordination-point (the named GP and the letter that ends the A&E-testing treadmill), and the boundary that real new symptoms still get assessed (the safety-netting that avoids both over-medicalising and dismissing) - [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]]; the refill specifics - the dependence physiology named without accusation (the withdrawal-relief-misinterpreted-as-need explanation reduces shame and often produces cooperation), the taper offer with nurse support, the short-bridge-with-strict-quantity exception only when tapering is already underway, and the continuity of care threatened by hard-stop letters (the abandoned-taper worse-than-slow-taper ledger) - [[psychiatry/substance-use]], [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]].**
