Psychiatry does not begin at 18. **From the toddler's attachment system to the octogenarian's apathy, mental disorders are developmental phenomena: they declare at the ages their biology permits, they are measured against developmental norms, and they shape every later trajectory. Child psychiatry is mostly education-and-family work, adolescent psychiatry is mostly negotiating autonomy without abandoning limits, and old-age psychiatry is mostly distinguishing depression from dementia from delirium from living-well-with-limits - each with its own pharmacology, ethics and service machinery.**

## Childhood

::: definition Neurodevelopmental disorders {#def-neurodev}
**The group defined by early-onset differences in brain development with enduring functional consequences: autism spectrum disorder (the dyad of social-communication difference and restricted-repetitive behaviour with sensory features now officially included, the spectrum's true range from non-speaking-intellectual-disability to diagnosed-at-40-after-a-lifetime-of-masking, the raised-in-girls-diagnosis-delay and the female-camouflage literature, the genetic-architectures-huge-and-heterogeneous with heritability among psychiatry's highest and de-novo-and-rare-variant contributions, the co-occurrence with ADHD-anxiety-catatonia-in-adolescence-epilepsy-and-intellectual-disability, and the service-ethics of diagnosis - the medical-model-versus-neurodiversity tension handled by offering description-and-access without promising cure, and the eating-disorders-and-anxiety-overlaps that dominate later care) - [[paediatrics/neurodevelopment]], [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]]; attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (the inattention-hyperactivity-impulsivity structure requiring cross-setting-persistence-and-impairment, the differential from the child-who-is-anxious-or-traumatised-or-under-challenged or sleeping-badly which is why the school-and-home-questionnaires-and-sleep-history precede diagnosis, the persistent-into-adulthood rate overestimated-by-clinics-and-underestimated-by-epidemiology read honestly, the safety-and-driving-and-occupational stakes that make undertreatment a real risk, and the cardiovascular-and-sudden-death-screening debates around stimulants resolved toward history-and-family-questioning rather than routine ECG in low-risk children); intellectual disability (the adaptive-plus-IQ definition, the aetiology sweep from the genetic-and-fetal-and-perinatal-and-postnatal causes - the Down-syndrome-and-fragile-X-and-tuberous-sclerosis-and-CDKL-adjacent single-gene diagnoses changing management, and the regression-that-demands-a-neurological-workup rule), the specific learning and communication and motor disorders whose school-noticeboards belittle their prevalence and whose accommodations-legislation made diagnosis consequential, and the tic-and-Tourette trajectory (the prepubertal-waxing-waning-normalise-by-adulthood natural history, the alpha-2-and-atypical-antipsychotic ladder reserved for severe cases and the CBIT-behavioural-therapy-first evidence and the post-streptococcal-PANDAS controversy kept in its evidential lane); ## Adolescence and later life

::: definition Adolescence and the second developmental half {#def-lifespan-adolescent}
**Adolescence as psychiatry's incidence storm: the peak onsets of psychosis, bipolar disorder, eating disorders, self-harm, depression and anxiety-sociogenic-escalation between 12 and 25 (the prefrontal-limbic-maturation-asynchrony and synaptic-pruning biology with its cannabinoid-vulnerability corollary, the sleep-phase-delay that makes 8am school a pharmacology-adjacent injustice and the melatonin-and-start-time evidence), the boundary-services problem (the child-to-adult transition as a mortality-and-relapse hazard documented in the epilepsy-and-diabetes-and-psychiatry literatures alike, and the transition-coordination and transfer-of-care programmes that are the cheap fix rarely funded), the confidentiality-capacity interface for under-16s (the Fraser/Gillick competence tradition for treatment consent, the confidentiality-with-limits conversation, the parent-involvement-with-versus-without-consent judgement and the safeguarding override done transparently), the consultation-liasion-and-ED adolescent population (the self-harm functions work, the school-refusal cycle, the gender-incongruence presentations requiring exploratory-not-affirmative-versus-dismissive therapy of the highest quality, the fertility-and-teratogen conversations started earlier than other specialties think, and the criminal-exclusion-zone of children-in-custody whose disorders are psychiatric until proven otherwise); and old-age psychiatry's core discriminations - the dementia-with-depression-and-delirium-threesome (the psychomotor-and-appetite-and-guilt texture, the informant-history, the MoCA-and-ACE-III cognitive screening with education adjustment, the delirium-clocks-and-4AT, the 'depression-is-prodrome-or-reaction-or-cause' debate kept modest), the behavioural-and-psychological-symptoms-of-dementia era of practice (the non-pharmacological-first ladder - music-and-dementia-friendly-environments-and-activity-and-pain-and-constipation-and-sensory-impairment correction - the antipsychotics-with-their-mortality-and-stroke-caveats-and-their-real-distress-relief reserved for severity with consent-and-capacity processes, the cataract-glasses-hearing-aids-as-psychiatric-interventions principle, and the driving-and-firearms-and-financial-exploitation and power-of-attorney conversations that are the actual risk-management), the late-life-suicide-epidemiology and its older-male-means-and-bereavement-drivers (covered in the prevention chapter), the apathy-versus-depression-versus-frontal-syndrome differential, and the Parkinson-and-stroke-and-epilepsy neuropsychiatry interfaces where the movement-disorder and epilepsy chapters carry the primary pharmacology - [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]], [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]], [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]].**

## Treating across the ages

## Where this leads

The paediatric bases in [[paediatrics/neurodevelopment]] and [[paediatrics/growth-development]] and [[paediatrics/safeguarding]], the geriatric frames in [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]] and [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], the disorder chapters [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[psychiatry/eating-personality-disorders]], and the legal architecture in [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]].

::: history
**Child psychiatry was born from the quarrel over whether children could have illness at all: the 19th-century 'moral management' schools and Itard's wild-child of Aveyron experiment (the 1800-1807 education-as-treatment case that launched developmental disability science and the nature-nurture argument simultaneously), the 'child guidance' movement's 1896-1920s clinic wave (the Tuke-school and the Boston-psychopathic-day-nursery lineage and the shift from defective-childhood moralism toward family-and-development), and the WWI-era shell-shock-of-children controversies that proved anxiety could be a child's illness too; the attachment revolution - the 1951 Bowlby 'Maternal Care and Mental Health' report and the Robertson documentaries and the Skeels Iowa-adoptee and Romanian-orphan (Rutter-era) deprivation follow-ups whose developmental-recovery data underwrote both attachment theory and the modern foster-and-adoption psychiatry, plus Ainsworth's strange-situation taxonomy - [[paediatrics/growth-development]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]]; the autism-stratigraphy: Bleuler's 1911 coinage and Asperger's 1944 'autistic psychopathy' in Vienna (its own Nazi-hereditary-biology-entanglement now properly excavated), Kanner's 1943 early-infantile-autism eleven cases, the refrigerator-mother-era-wreckage and Bettelheim's retreat, Rimland's parent-organised biological-model rebuttal and the 1970s-80s Wing-and-Gould epidemiology and the triad and then dyad re-categorisation to the DSM-5-and-ICD-11 autism-spectrum present; adolescence as a discovered life-stage - the 1904-1907 G-Stanley-Hall 'Adolescence' textbook era and Anna Freud's Hampstead Child-Lecture-Clinic lineage and the 1990s-2000s neuroimaging of the adolescent brain that made prefrontal-limbic-asynchrony household neuroscience and reframed risk-taking as maturation; and old-age psychiatry's emergence from the gerontological-asylum: the the Klein and Roth tradition in Britain (the 1950s-60s Maudsley school separating late-life depression and dementia and the 'confusion' state's delirium-clarification), the 1907 Alzheimer-and-his-1910-Kraepelin-textbook case that eventually split presenile from senile dementia and then re-united them under Alzheimer disease's biomarker era (the 1995-2011 amyloid-cascade and NIA-AA research-framework and ATN-biomarker revisions), the behavioural-and-psychological-symptoms reform of the 2000s-2010s (the Tom Kitwood life-history-and-dementia-care-mapping revolution and the later antipsychotic-reduction policies and DICE-and-fiT-formulation tools), and the 2011 and 2024 WHO-dementia-plans era with its risk-reduction-commissioning (the Lancet-commission's 40-percent-modifiable-risk estimates) alongside the ongoing de-stigmatisation of the word itself - [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]], [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]].**
:::

::: summary
- Psychiatric incidence follows development: each age band has its characteristic onsets, and 'at what age do disorders like this start' is half the differential.
- Childhood presentations are educational and family problems until proven otherwise; diagnoses require multi-informant data, and school-only problems are school contexts first.
- Adolescence concentrates the incidence of serious mental illness while services hand over patients twice; transition planning is a clinical intervention.
- Late-life psychiatry is discrimination work - depression versus dementia versus delirium versus medication effect - and the non-pharmacological ladder outperforms prescriptions for most behavioural symptoms.
- Psychopharmacology at the ends of life demands forecasting (growth, metabolism, falls, cognition) and deprescribing discipline more than novel agents.
- Capacity, consent and safeguarding frameworks differ by age and jurisdiction; clinicians must know which law governs the child, the 17-year-old and the 80-year-old in front of them.
:::

and the surrounding machinery - the reintegration-meeting with the SENCo-and-pastoral-lead (the bullying-and-rumour-management plan, since peer knowledge travels faster than staff policy, and the anti-stigma-education-offer the class might need, delivered by the young-person-not-the-patient-as-case-study principle), the exam-access-applications-now-not-May (the evidence-based-access-from-recent-illness route the regulations provide and schools forget), and the shared-review-date with the education-wellbeing-team so the letter is a process with follow-up, not a permission slip; and the audit-principle - that the next clinician can see what was disclosed, to whom, and with what consent (the record-of-disclosure entry protecting him from future over- and under-sharing alike) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**

::: exercise Deprescribe the eighties {level=2}
An 83-year-old takes: zopiclone (7 years), diazepam (12 years, 'for nerves'), oxybutynin, amitriptyline (low-dose, 'for bladder'), omeprazole (no indication recorded), paracetamol prn, ferrous sulphate ('an anaemia once'), and aspirin 75 (primary prevention). Nothing is 'working'. Run the clinic.
::: solution
**Late-life-deprescribing-clinic ([[#def-lifespan-treatment]], [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]]): the burden arithmetic named first (the anticholinergic-and-sedative-stack - memory, falls, constipation, urinary-urgency-worsening-then-treated-by-oxybutynin-and-amitriptyline, daytime-sedation-and-nocturnal-confusion-from-zopiclone-and-diazepam, the prescribing-cascade-map drawn where she can see it, which is usually the moment the clinic turns from defence to collaboration), then the order of attack - the diazepam-most-slowly (the 12-year-benzo-withdrawal-seizure-risk with the micro-taper-and-nurse-contact schedule and the rebound-anxiety-expectation-setting), the zopiclone-adjacency (the CBT-I-and-sleep-restriction alternative with its durability-versus-hypnotics data, and the one-drug-at-a-time rule with 4-6-week spacing), the bladder-cascade-untangling (the oxybutynin-versus-mirabegron-versus-pelvic-floor-and-toilet-timing-and-constipation-treatment review, since the constipation may be causing the urgency it treats, and the amitriptyline-removed-with-the-urge-to-re-check-renal-and-diabetes-and-overflow-then-stop-both-anticholinergics-not-just-one), and the no-indication items (the PPI-retrial-or-stop-with-reflux-plan-and-LFT-and-B12-and-magnesium-context, the iron-recheck-then-stop-without-a-cause-once-censored, and the primary-prevention-aspirin-in-83-with-fall-risk-and-no-cardiovascular-disease - the bleeding-versus-negligible-benefit arithmetic that the deprescribing guidelines and the fall-fracture reality settle); the monitoring-with-withdrawal (the anxiety-and-sleep-and-BP-and-fall-diary-and-named-person-phone-calls-and-the-reassurance-that-withdrawal-symptoms-are-transient-not-proof-of-need), and the goal-language (not 'fewer tablets' but 'better memory and steadier legs and no more daytime napping', the outcomes she ranked highest, with her priorities written first in the plan); and the follow-up structure - the next review booked, the GP-lettered-with-progress-not-blame, and the medication-list-as-living-document principle recorded (the annual 'does this still need to be on the list' question, the cheapest intervention in geriatric medicine) - [[cardiovascular/vascular-disease]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**

::: exercise Argue the early-intervention-for-psychosis-in-youth case {level=3}
Your commissioners propose closing the early-intervention service for 16-25 year-olds because 'adult services can manage'. Build the case, the counter-arguments and the decision process.
::: solution
**Service-closure-defence ([[#def-lifespan-adolescent]], [[#def-lifespan-treatment]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]): the evidence case - the duration-of-untreated-psychosis-outcome-relationship and the early-intervention-trial-bodies' 2-5-year advantages, the 16-25-incidence-window-where-half-of-serious-mental-illness-onset-concentrates (closing a service at peak-incidence ages is epidemiologically inverse), the education-and-employment-years-lost-measured-as-lifelong-earnings-and-benefits-and-career (the cost-of-illness models and the intergenerational-costs argument), the transition-gap-mortality-and-relapse-data-showing-adult-services-receiving-17-and-three-quarter-year-olds-mid-crisis-are-a-cost-not-a-saving, and the young-people's-and-families'-consultation-record (the lived-experience-evidence-commissioners-cannot-dismiss); the counter-arguments-steel-manned - the-EIS-effect-size-shrinkage-in-contemporary-trials (treatment-as-usual-improving-narrowing-the-difference), the-implementation-drift-realism (understaffed EIS teams drifting to case-management and losing the model-fidelity that made trials positive - the answer being fix-fidelity-and-access-not-close), the-comparison-with-high-income-countries-running-universal-early-intervention, the adult-services'-readiness question (the 'who does it instead' costed with the 16-25-neurodevelopmental-and-forensic-and-substance-overlap-caseload-mapped), and the workforce problem stated (the dual child-and-adult training pipeline years too short to fix by decree); the decision process - a full equalities-and-rights-impact-assessment-with-young-people-and-families-as-participants-not-respondents, the transparency-with-timetable-and-evidence-pack (the 'consultation-theater' pattern that destroys trust in every future proposal), the-pilot-and-evaluation-alternative (protect the service with an outcome-agreement-and-publication-requirement), and the escalation-and-legal-reality (the public-sector-equality-duty, the children-and-adolescents'-mental--services-transformation-programme commitments and the select-committee-and-coroner-adjacent accountability when the preventable case arrives); and the final argument stated plainly - the cheap decade-long saving is bought with a cohort's life-course, and psychiatry's own evidence says so, which makes this not a value judgement but an implementation error with a graph attached - [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[paediatrics/neurodevelopment]].**
