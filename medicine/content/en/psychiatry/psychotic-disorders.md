Psychotic disorders are transformations of how the brain assigns significance. **Psychosis - loss of shared-reality testing - is a symptom-cluster with many causes, and the clinical work is threefold: recognise the syndrome (hallucinations, delusions, disorganisation of thought and behaviour, negative symptoms), exclude the causes that kill or reverse (toxic, metabolic, autoimmune, neurological), and treat the illness that usually declares itself in late adolescence with a course that determines lives more than any single intervention.** The antipsychotic era made treatment possible; the early-intervention and recovery eras are making it humane and effective.

## The syndrome and its causes

::: definition Psychosis, schizophrenia, and the differential {#def-psychosis}
**The positive-symptom apparatus: hallucinations (auditory-verbal experiences ranging from heard-whispers to running-commentary and conversing-voices, with the distinguishing questions - perceived-as-external versus inner-speech, insight-preserved-or-not, and the normality-boundaries of hypnagogic experiences and grief-voices and cultural practice), and delusions (fixed-false-beliefs held with conviction beyond cognitive penetration - the persecutory and referential and grandiose and nihilistic and control-and-passivity phenomena whose Schneiderian first-rank features proved less specific than textbooks claimed), plus the formal-thought-disorder read from speech (the derailment and loss-of-goal sequences scored with instruments, and the negative-symptom overlap with medication sedation) and the psychomotor poles of catatonia and agitation - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the syndrome-to-diagnosis step: acute-and-transient psychoses and brief reactive psychoses, schizophrenia-spectrum with its criterion-variants (the six-month-course tradition and ICD-11's one-month allowance with its clinical-criteria revisions, the schizoaffective and schizotypal neighbourhood and the wisdom of treating course not labels), and the mood-with-psychosis presentations whose treatment and prognosis differ enough to change management daily - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; the organic-and-toxic differential organised by speed and texture: delirium (fluctuating and visual and frightened), substances (cannabis-and-stimulant-and-hallucinogen-and-alcohol-withdrawal psychoses with the tox-screen and time as diagnostic tools), autoimmune encephalitis (the subacute psychiatric-then-neurological progression with movement disorder and seizures and autonomic features, the antibody tests and the urgency the outcome data justify), endocrine and metabolic (the steroid psychoses and Addison and Cushing and thyroid and hypoparathyroid-calcium pages), neurological (temporal-lobe seizure phenomena and tumours and Wilson disease under 40), and the sensory-deprivation and dementing psychoses of later life - [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]], [[endocrine/adrenal]], [[nervous-system/epilepsy]], [[nervous-system/movement-disorders]]; and the neurobiology worth teaching: the dopamine-oversynthesis model as circuit-specific (mesolimbic-striatal excess for positive symptoms with antipsychotic D2 occupancy as the human proof-of-concept, and the cortical-and-glutamate-and-GABA-interneuron accounts for negative and cognitive symptoms that D2 blockade does not touch), the inflammation-and-pruning findings, the large polygenic architecture with tens of thousands of common variants plus the high-penetrance rare copy-number variants (the 22q11.2-deletion and other syndromic risk loci with their medical comorbidity implications), and the cannabis-and-childhood-adversity-and-urbanicity-and-immigration environmental effects with their dose relationships - [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]], [[genetics/genomic-medicine]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]].**
:::

## Treatment

::: definition Antipsychotics and psychosocial care {#def-antipsychotic}
**Antipsychotics, used with craft: the D2-family chemistry and its clinical consequences (the first-generation potency-spectrum from chlorpromazine's sedation-and-alpha-blockade to haloperidol's EPS-liability, and the second-generation profiles - clozapine's unmatched efficacy in treatment-resistant psychosis and its agranulocytosis-and-myocarditis-and-metabolic-and-sialorrhoea monitoring apparatus, olanzapine and quetiapine's metabolic load, aripiprazole and cariprazine and brexpiprazole partial-agonism with akathisia-and-insomnia trade-offs, risperidone and paliperidone's prolactin elevation, amisulpride's QT and low-EPS niche, and the long-acting-injectables as adherence-infrastructure - the relapse-prevention evidence, the monthly and two-monthly and three-monthly formulations, and the autonomy-versus-supervision politics) - [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]]; the side-effect management that determines adherence: the metabolic syndrome mitigation (baseline-and-scheduled weight-and-circumference-and-glucose-and-lipids with the switching-and-addition metformin-and-GLP1-era options, and the honest statement that untreated illness worsens metabolism too), the prolactin-and-sexual-and-weight consequences that patients rank highest and clinicians document least, the EPS-and-akathisia-and-tardive-dyskinesia surveillance (the AIMS examinations, the VMAT2-inhibitor treatment era, and the dose-minimisation discipline), the QT-and-anticholinergic-and-sedation burdens across combinations, and the clozapine-reintroduction-and-myocarditis-alert protocols; and the psychosocial treatments with hard outcomes: cognitive-behavioural therapy for psychosis (modest symptom-level effects and coping benefits, delivered alongside medication not instead), the family-intervention programmes with relapse-rate effect sizes and expressed-emotion mechanisms, the supported-employment and education and CBT-for-psychosis-and-avolition adaptation programmes, the cognitive-remediation evidence, and the coordinated-specialist-care and early-intervention-team models whose effect on 3-5-year outcomes is among psychiatry's best international findings - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**
:::

## Course and the things that determine it

::: definition Early intervention, resistance and prognosis {#def-course}
**Duration of untreated psychosis is the one prognostic variable in the illness itself that the system controls (the shorter-DUP-to-better-outcome association with its confounding debates, and the early-intervention-services whose 2-5-year advantages drove the national-programmes era), which makes the referral-pathway questions - who sees first-episode psychosis within two weeks, who gets the 3-year programme, who is lost to the A&E-recycling loop - outcome questions (the at-risk-mental-states debate: the ultra-high-risk criteria's modest transition rates, the ethics of labelling, and the current answer of offering help without a prophetic diagnosis) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; treatment resistance defined and honoured (the two-adequate-trials-then-clozapine rule with its decades of guideline consistency and its notorious implementation failure - the years-long clozapine delay measured internationally, the barriers from phlebotomy-logistics to therapeutic-pessimism, and the ECT-augmentation and clozapine-plus strategies for the clozapine-resistant minority with their honest evidence grades) - [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]; the mortality ledger that psychiatry under-communicates: the 10-20-year life-expectancy gap driven mainly by cardiovascular disease and smoking and diabetes and the iatrogenic-metabolic contribution and under-screening, plus suicide rates that demand the same physical-health-and-risk follow-up as any chronic disease (the metabolic-screening incentives and the smoking-cessation rates that are the highest-yield prescription in this population) - [[cardiovascular/hypertension]], [[endocrine/diabetes]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]]; the dual-diagnosis and forensic interfaces: the substance-use comorbidity that doubles relapse and rehospitalisation (the integrated-treatment requirement), the small-but-real violence risk concentrated in untreated psychosis with command experiences, intoxication and past-violence history (the base-rate honesty, the risk-formulation-not-diagnosis-stigma practice, and the community-orders-and-crisis-plans that reduce it), and the capacity-and-compulsion decisions for the chapter on law; and recovery as the organising aim: the clinical-recovery-versus-personal-recovery distinction (symptom-and-functioning remission in a minority to a much larger group achieving meaningful lives with residual symptoms), the vocational-and-relationship-and-hope outcomes that patients prioritise, the therapeutic-nihilism audit, and the peer-support and recovery-college movements that changed services faster than most trials - [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]], [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]].**
:::

::: example The first episode {#ex-first-episode}
A 21-year-old student is brought in by halls warden after three days of believing the television is sending him instructions; he is frightened, not violent; he refuses 'hospital but not tablets at home'; his parents want 'a scan and something to fix this'.
::: solution
**First-episode-psychosis-management ([[#def-psychosis]], [[#def-course]], [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]): the immediate clinical tasks - the organic sweep appropriate to a first episode (the delirium-exclusion observations and glucose and sodium and calcium and TFTs and FBC and CRP, the substance screen with the synthetic-cannabinoid question asked explicitly, the LP-and-antibody-and-MRI threshold if there is any fluctuation or headache-or-seizure-or-autonomic feature because autoimmune encephalitis is the differential that punishes delay) - [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]], [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]; the admission decision - the frightened-and-untreatable-at-home argument versus the voluntary-alternative ladder (the crisis-house and home-treatment and day-patient options, the parents' presence as therapy not audience, and the Mental Health Act route reserved for genuine criteria with the least-restrictive reasoning documented) - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; the medication conversation with his autonomy kept (the low-dose-atypical-with-metabolic-and-EPS-honesty, the hours-not-weeks-for-acute-agitation reality with the short-acting-IM-when-necessary protocol and the PRN-consent discussion, and the 'this reduces the terror of the voices, here is what it does not do' framing, with the long-acting-injectable offered as a future option not a first-episode condition) - [[clinical-skills/communication]]; and the trajectory plan - the early-intervention-team referral within the week (the 3-year programme, the family work, the school-and-family-communication letter he consents to), the CBT-for-psychosis waiting-list bridge with psychoeducation and sleep protection, the cannabis discussion opened non-judgmentally now (the relapse-data version, saved for rapport rather than the first interview if needed), and the scan-question answered honestly (normal scans do not exclude the illness; ::: example The clozapine delay {#ex-clozapine}
A 29-year-old has failed two antipsychotic trials, is re-admitted four times a year with command hallucinations telling him to 'deal with the neighbours', and the team 'isn't keen on clozapine because of the bloods'; the last episode ended when a neighbour moved.
::: solution
**Clozapine-is-the-treatment ([[#def-antipsychotic]], [[#def-course]]): the indication stated plainly - after two adequate trials this is treatment-resistant psychosis, clozapine's unique efficacy has the largest effect size in psychopharmacology, and the failure to offer it is a prescribing-quality problem with named harms (the re-admissions, the violence-risk he has lived beside, the aggression-and-suicidality signals clozapine carries FDA-adjacent indications for) - [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]]; the objections answered with logistics not attitudes: the blood-monitoring-regimen is a service-design task (the shared-care phlebotomy schedules and the register systems that make UK clozapine safer than most countries' antidepressant prescribing), the myocarditis-and-seizure-and-sedation-and-sialorrhoea-and-constipation-and-metabolic risks managed with the published titration-and-monitoring protocols (the CRP-and-troponin early-phase schedule, the bowel-regimen started with the first dose because ileus is the killer nobody warns about, the dose-plasma-level interface for the smokers-and-non-smokers difference) - [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]], [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]], [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]]; the implementation plan - the honest conversation with him (the side-effect trade he may accept that his clinicians refused to offer, the titration calendar, and the family psychoeducation session on what clozapine changes), the GP-and-community-pharmacist engagement and the driving-and-operating-machinery counselling per licence and law, and the second-generation-lAI-vs-clozapine-vs-ECT-augmentation ladder documented if he refuses; and the audit that prevents recurrence - the service's clozapine-delay metric (time from second-failed-trial to initiation), the the international clozapine-optimisation programmes' findings that most delay is system not patient, and the prescribing-policy change that follows the numbers - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[pharmacology/patient-variation]].**

::: example The voice-healer's dilemma {#ex-voices-culture}

## Where this leads

Assessment and risk in [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]] and [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]], mood-with-psychosis in [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], substance interactions in [[psychiatry/substance-use]], the legal frame in [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], and the neurobiology in [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]] and [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]].

::: history
**The concept of schizophrenia was assembled then dismantled and reassembled: Benedict Morel's dementia précoce and Kraepelin's 1896 dementia-praecox-versus-manic-depression division (the longitudinal case-note method and its prognostic fatalism), Eugen Bleuler's 1911 coinage of schizophrenia with its four-A fundamental symptoms that relocated the essence from course to a split of thought-and-affect, and then the American era in which Bleuler's broadening swallowed the diagnosis until the operational Criteria era (the Feighner and Research Diagnostic Criteria reliability drives and DSM-III's 1980 descriptive restoration, the the cross-national and Roswell Park and International Pilot Study of Schizophrenia findings whose outcome-by-country data reshaped the prognosis literature and the chronic-hospital-era's own contributions to defect) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the treatment revolution came twice from side-observations: the 1950s reserpine-and-phenothiazine era - Laborit's surgical calmant and Delay and Deniker's chlorpromazine trials at Sainte-Anne and the immediate deinstitutionalisation arguments whose unfinished promises the antipsychotic era still carries, and the 1960s haloperidol synthesis from the butyrophenone chemistry - [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]]; clozapine's rescue from the 1960s (the clozapine story: first synthesised in the 1950s and dismissed as non-antipsychotic in the standard models, rescued by the 1988 Kane trial in treatment-resistant schizophrenia, shadowed ever since by the 1975 Finnish agranulocytosis deaths and the blood-monitoring registers that followed) - the single most important psychopharmacological finding still under-used; the social-and-rehabilitation counter-tradition: the therapeutic-community and civil-rights era, the Italian Basaglia reform and its asylum closures, the assertive-community-treatment origins (the New York case-management experiment), the supported-employment evidence that beat the train-then-place orthodoxy, and the recovery movement from survivor and peer organising into policy language - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; and the modern synthesis - the early-intervention-services wave from the 1990s-2000s randomised programmes, the at-risk-mental-states debate, the cognitive-neuropsychiatry era (the predictive-coding and aberrant-salience models of psychosis that made 'abnormal significance-assignment' the shared mechanism account), the genomics of the schizophrenia-working-party-era polygenic architecture and the rare-variant copy-number findings, and the current treatment-pipeline reality: the muscarinic-mixed-receptor-agonist approval that broke the D2 monoculture after 70 years, and the stubbornness of negative and cognitive symptoms as the true therapeutic frontier - [[genetics/genomic-medicine]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]].**
:::

::: summary
- Psychosis is a syndrome with a differential; first episodes and atypical features earn an organic workup, and autoimmune encephalitis is the diagnosis that punishes delay.
- Schizophrenia-spectrum illness is treated with a package: antipsychotic chosen for the person, psychological therapy, family work, and the vocational-and-educational scaffolding that converts symptom control into a life.
- Clozapine for treatment resistance is the strongest evidence in the field and the most-delayed prescription; the delay is a service-design failure.
- Side effects are the treatment's other half: metabolic and movement-disorder monitoring, smoking cessation, and physical-health checks are psychiatric care, not a separate clinic.
- Early-intervention services and shorter duration of untreated psychosis improve outcomes; first contact should be rapid, humane and non-criminalising.
- Recovery, not cure, organises long-term care: most patients with good care achieve meaningful lives, and therapeutic nihilism is an error of evidence as much as compassion.
:::

the middle - the treatment-resistance pathway with the clozapine-delay-metric published and the clozapine-optimisation nurse-and-phlebotomy logistics funded, the LAI access as a choice not a sentence, the CBT-for-psychosis-and-trauma workforce sized to need, the dual-diagnosis integrated services (the SMI-and-substance clinics that stop the two-services-blaming-each-other loop), and the physical-health checks with the practice-payment-and-community-pharmacy-and-smoking-cessation arms pulling the same direction (the cardiovascular-risk-first redesign that addresses the mortality ledger) - [[endocrine/diabetes]], [[cardiovascular/hypertension]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; the edges - the legal-and-rights interface staffed for competence (the AMHP-and-second-opinion-and-tribunal-and-advocacy availability, the reduced-restraint-and-observation-as-therapeutic-not-punitive ward culture, the disparity-audits on ethnicity-and-disability lines published), the transition-from-children's-services and the late-life-psychosis and sensory-impairment pathway (the antipsychotic-deprescribing-in-dementia adjacency and the clozapine-in-age-biology debates handled by people who read geriatric psychopharmacology), and the never-mental-illness-but-homeless-and-in-prison interface with the custody-liaison and the housing-first-with-clinical-support teams; and the accountability - the annual published outcome set: the DUP distributions and 2-week-access rates, the clozapine-delay medians, the metabolic-check completion, the suicide-and-self-harm and violent-victimisation-and-perpetration rates, the employment-and-education-and-relationship indicators patients choose, the CQC-and-tribunal-and-restraint metrics, the ethnic-and-gender-and-disability outcome gaps with the improvement plans, and the service-user-and-carer-co-production forums with real budget-share, because the decade-long studies agree that continuity, hope and work - not any molecule - decide the last column of the table - [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]].**

## Exercises

::: exercise Sort the psychoses {level=1}
Give the most likely category for each and the test of time or tissue that settles it: (a) 19-year-old, 6 months of auditory commentary and withdrawal, functioning decline; (b) 34-year-old, 4 days of grandiosity and insomnia after starting a night-shift stimulant regimen; (c) 47-year-old, 3 weeks of psychiatric symptoms then orofacial dyskinesia and hyponatraemia; (d) 78-year-old, 2 days of visual hallucinations and fear after hip surgery.
::: solution
**Category-sorting ([[#def-psychosis]]): (a) - primary-psychotic-disorder (schizophrenia-spectrum) worked up with the standard organic screen and the course of longitudinal follow-up (the tox screen to close the cannabis window and the negative CT-if-no-indication argument) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; (b) - substance-induced with the two-tests - clearance-on-abstinence and the temporal-association-strength of the stimulant pattern (the persistence-beyond-intoxication-window question that reclassifies a minority toward primary illness, documented by dates) - [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; (c) - autoimmune encephalitis until proven otherwise (the antibody-and-CSF-and-MRI-and-EEG workup urgently, the tumour screen, the immunotherapy time-sensitivity, and the psychiatric-first-weeks pattern the literature hammered into awareness) - [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]]; and (d) - postoperative delirium (the attention-and-fluctuation testing, the 4AT, the precipitant hunt - hypoxia and opioids and anticholinergics and infection and constipation and urinary retention - and the non-pharmacological-environment plus low-dose-antipsychotic-only-if-terrified-and-dangerous management) - [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]], [[surgery/perioperative-care]].**
:::

::: exercise Manage the metabolic bill {level=2}
A 32-year-old on olanzapine 20mg has gained 18 kg, prediabetic, smoking 20/day, sedentary, refuses switching because 'this is the first one that works'. Build the plan without forcing the choice he already lost once.
::: solution
**Metabolic-management-in-psychiatric-care ([[#def-antipsychotic]], [[#def-course]], [[endocrine/diabetes]]): the frame - keep-the-efficacy-and-treat-the-metabolism (the relapse-and-weight-gain-cycle costs of destabilising a working regimen, and the honest statement that the illness itself and the sedation-and-craving mechanics contribute, so this is joint-management, not a morality play) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the interventions in yield-order - the lifestyle-programme with mental-health-adapted delivery (the structured behavioural diet-and-exercise programmes with their RCT weight-loss figures in this population, the exercise-as-adjuvant-for-negative-symptoms bonus, and the OT-and-community-team scaffolding that makes attendance possible), metformin for antipsychotic-associated-weight-gain-and-glycaemic-drift where licensed-or-protocol with the GI-tolerability titration, the GLP1-era conversation (the semaglutide-and-liraglutide psychiatric-cohort data and the adherence-and-monitoring-and-cessation-rebound realities, and the DKA-and-pancreatitis-safety-netting for a population that presents late), and the antipsychotic-discussion-kept-open-not-forced (the aripiprazole-or-ziprasidone-or-lower-olanzapine-dose options tabled and revisited, the long-acting-injectable if adherence drift is the hidden instability, and the slow-dose-optimisation that preserves the gains) - [[endocrine/diabetes-complications]], [[pharmacology/patient-variation]]; the other tobacco conversation - the smoking-cessation-as-highest-yield-drug (the varenicline-and-NRT efficacy-and-safety data in stable psychiatric cohorts, the CYP1A2-smoking-cessation-and-olanzapine-and-clozapine-level-uptitration warning that the hospital-admission-rebound incidentally teaches every year) - [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; and the surveillance machinery - the annual physical-health checklist (the BP-and-circumference-and-lipids-and-HbA1c-and-LFT-and-prolactin-and-bone-risk and the ECG when QT-relevant), the same-measurement-feedback-loop for the team (the physical-health-check completion rates as a service KPI), and the cardiovascular-risk conversation told in his numbers (the life-expectancy figures stated once, kindly, and then the plan - because fear without a plan is the reason these conversations fail) - [[cardiovascular/hypertension]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**

::: exercise Write the relapse-prevention plan {level=2}
For a patient two years into a first episode, stable on aripiprazole LAI, planning university return: build the written plan with warning signs, the substance-and-sleep rules, the adherence-and-dose-change protocol, and the crisis section.
::: solution
**Relapse-prevention-architecture ([[#def-course]], [[#def-antipsychotic]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]): the warning-sign list in his language (the sleep-shortening-first marker and the social-withdrawal-and-music-volume-and-referential-TV-signals that his sister taught the team to ask about, each with a pre-agreed first-move - the extra-support-session and the sleep-protection rules rather than emergency-drug-changes), the sleep-and-substance-and-stress contracts (the all-nighters-as-relapse-precipitant with the timetable-negotiation options, the cannabis rule stated as data not command, the alcohol quantity cap, and the stimulant-and-caffeine-and-energy-drink honesty), the medication section (the LAI dates and the missed-appointment recovery rule, the dose-optimisation-before-switch principle, and the planned-future-taper discussion - the slow-supervised-reduction-if-stable-for-years option documented so he knows withdrawal is a conversation not an ambush, with the maintenance-duration evidence framing that keeps it honest) - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; the crisis section (the contact-tree with the crisis-team and the family-and-friend numbers, the ED-avoidance-alternative routes, the written admission-criteria he agreed while well and the advance-statement option including the preferred-medication and the no-restraint-without-attempted-verbal-de-escalation preferences - recorded and copied to all services), and the who-does-what table (the key-worker-and-GP-and-university-disability-service-and-community-psych-nurse roles with the data-sharing consents signed now, while it is boring, not during the crisis) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]]; and the life section that the relapse-prevention literature keeps proving matters - the university-return plan with the reduced-load and exam-arrangement accommodations, the friendship-and-routine-and-sport continuity, the meaning-and-identity conversation that treats the illness as one chapter, and the review interval that watches for the depression-after-psychosis-insight that peaks when functioning returns (the post-psychotic depression question screened, not assumed) - [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]], [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]].**

::: exercise Argue the involuntary-treatment question {level=3}
A patient with three prior refusals and repeated severe relapse is now refusing again; his mother begs you to 'make him stay'. Appraise the ethical and legal frameworks, the evidence on community-treatment orders and the practice you would defend.
::: solution
**Compulsion-appraisal ([[#def-course]], [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]]): the legal-ethical floor - compulsory treatment requires the statutory criteria (the disorder-plus-risk-or-inability-to-interpret-information test varies by jurisdiction), proportionality and least-restriction, and the capacity assessment done for the actual decision with the illness-affecting-weighing argument made explicitly rather than by diagnosis alone (the 'refusing what I'd choose is not incapacity' principle) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the evidence on the tools - the community-treatment-order literature (the randomised and observational studies consistently showing reduced rehospitalisation in some settings and no effect on many patient-reported outcomes, and the UK-CTO-era and Swedish-register findings on the use-versus-perception gap), the long-acting-injectable-as-involuntary-infrastructure debate (the evidence that willingness-predicted-by-alliance makes consensual-LAI both humane and effective, and that coercion-and-LAI combinations damage the alliance that is itself the protective factor), and the electroconvulsive-in-crisis question under the the urgent-treatment-versus-second-opinion timing rules and the independent-advocacy and tribunal architecture that make any compulsion lawful; the practice answer - the plan built while well (the joint-crisis-and-advance-statement with the specific-circumstances-he-would-want-treatment criteria written, the family's-role-defined-his-terms-with-his-consent, and the LAI offered as his-choice-and-his-lever), the threshold named before the crisis (what has to happen before you would use the Act, documented, so the decision is not invented at 3am), the least-restrictive-ladder exhausted and logged (the home-treatment and crisis-house and voluntary-admission offers with their times and outcomes), and the honest use when criteria are met (the humane-compulsion-versus-abandonment choice: this chapter's mortality data on untreated relapse, on suicide and on violence-victimisation of the psychotic, means refusing-to-act is also a risk decision with a victim); and the after - the debrief with the patient when well, the plan-updated-from-lived-experience, the tribunal-and-advocacy access protected, and the service's-compulsion-rate-and-disparity audit (the ethnic-and-disability-and-age differentials in detention that make compulsion a justice question alongside a clinical one) - [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
