The eating and personality disorders share a clinical grammar: distress about self and body expressed through control, avoidance and relationship turbulence, treated best with long alliances, specific psychological methods and honest about-goals conversations. **Anorexia nervosa carries the highest mortality of any psychiatric disorder; borderline personality disorder is common, treatable and still stigmatised; both are managed by clinicians who can hold risk without iatrogenic harm and hope without denial.** This chapter covers the syndromes, their medical dangers and their evidence-based treatments, with the personality-disorder framework that psychiatry and primary care use daily.

## Eating disorders

::: definition The eating disorder syndromes {#def-eating}
**Anorexia nervosa: restriction of intake producing significantly low weight (or, in atypical presentations, metabolic and behavioural restriction at higher weight), intense fear of gain and body-image disturbance with self-evaluation overweight-shape-bound (the restricting versus binge-purge subtypes carrying different medical and pharmacological profiles), onset in early adolescence with a female preponderance falling as recognition improves, and the mortality ledger that must be stated plainly (the standardised mortality ratio from cardiac death, starvation organ failure and suicide, concentrated in long-illness-course adults, which is why 'watchful waiting' in severe cases is not neutral) - [[paediatrics/growth-development]], [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]]; bulimia nervosa and the binge spectrum: recurrent binge-eating with compensatory behaviours (self-induced vomiting, laxatives and diuretics, fasting and over-exercise) at normal-or-raised weight, the medical toll of purging (the salivary and dental erosion, the parotid swelling, the hypokalaemia-and-quinolone-and-laxative-melanosis physiology, oesophageal tears and the arrhythmia risk when potassium falls) and the close but distinct relationship to binge-eating disorder (the loss-of-control episodes without compensation, the obesity-medicine interface with its set-point-and-stigma sensitivities, and the emerging GLP-1-era questions about weight-drug effects on disordered eating - both protective and provocative depending on the person, so screening before prescribing) - [[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]], [[renal/potassium-calcium-phosphate]], [[endocrine/diabetes]]; ARFID and the avoidant presentations (the sensory-based and fear-of-consequences restriction without body-image distortion, the medical-narrowing and autism-overlap childhood forms and the post-choking and post-illness adult forms, the nutrition-first treatment and the diagnostic relief the category brings to 'picky eaters' who were starving), plus other-specified presentations (atypical anorexia with the same behaviours at higher weight, purging disorder, night-eating) where the behaviours determine treatment more than the label does; and the aetiology and maintenance model every clinician should be able to draw in one minute: genetic-heritability signals (the anorexia-bipolar-schizophrenia and metabolic-trait genetic correlations, honest about effect sizes), pubertal-and-perfectionism-and-anxiety traits, the dieting-trigger sequence (restriction to binging to purging, the starvation-driven set-point escalation with body-image and reward-circuit distortion that makes refeeding a psychological as well as medical intervention), the family-communication factors (experienced as maintainers not causes, a reframe that preceded today's family-inclusive therapies), and the sociocultural amplifiers (the diet-culture and social-media dose-relationships with body dissatisfaction, and the minority-stress and gender-identity intersections) - [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]].**
:::

## Medical danger and refeeding

::: definition Medical complications and refeeding {#def-refeeding}
**Starvation physiology by system: cardiac (bradycardia and hypotension, QT dispersion and the sudden-death risk concentrated in the bradycardic-restricted and purging subtypes, the 'fit athlete' bradycardia that misleads families and sometimes clinicians), renal and fluid (the syndrome-of-inappropriate-antidiuresis with water-loading and low-solute intake, the laxative-and-diuretic abuse patterns masquerading as oedema), haematological (the leucopenia and normocytic anaemia and thrombocytopenia that misdirect oncology referrals), endocrine (the sick-euthyroid and hypothalamic-amenorrhoea and low-testosterone adaptations, the osteoporosis from energy-deficit-and-hypo-oestrogenism with its fracture-years arithmetic - the bone-density and amenorrhoea-duration conversation that justifies oestrogen-replacement-era debates read correctly as hormone-replacement-not-contraception, the growth effects in the young), and hepatic (the transaminitis of starvation-autophagy versus refeeding, the honest reading that rising enzymes early in refeeding is usually not a reason to stop) - [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]], [[endocrine/reproductive-endocrinology]], [[endocrine/bone-calcium]], [[renal/water-sodium]]; refeeding syndrome is the iatrogenic classic: carbohydrate load shifting phosphate, potassium and magnesium intracellularly under insulin drive, causing arrhythmias, weakness, seizures and death in the undernourished - prevented by slow-start-and-advance-with-monitoring protocols (the NICE-and-ASPEN-informed regimens with daily electrolytes for the first week or more, prophylactic thiamine and B-complex before feeds, the phosphate-and-potassium-replacement thresholds, the fluid-and-sodium caution with the oedema that is not heart failure, and the tube-or-NG decision handled with the same psychological care as oral intake) - [[renal/potassium-calcium-phosphate]], [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]; the admission thresholds written down and taught (the bradycardia, hypotension, hypothermia, syncope, potassium and phosphate and sodium numbers, the percentage-of-median-weight and the rate-of-falling curves, the psychiatric indications - suicide risk, rapidly worsening restriction, failed outpatient care, the pregnancy and the diabetes-with-insulin-omission presentations) and the legal-ethical apparatus when weight-restoration is refused (the capacity assessment done carefully because starvation itself impairs judgment, the Second-opinion-and-second-place question, the involuntary-treatment thresholds that differ by jurisdiction for eating disorders, and the long-standing-illness palliative-versus-active-treatment conversations with their own ethics governance) - [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]], [[geriatrics-palliative/end-of-life-care]]; and the longitudinal care truth - most recovery is outpatient and slow, weight-restoration first (the 'food first, therapy lands on a fed brain' principle with its refeeding-and-anxiety management), relapse-windows mapped (the exams, the post-hospital discharge, the diet-culture seasons), and the carers' support services and the specialist eating-disorder teams with their access inequalities (the waiting-list-and-postcode lottery that the national improvement programmes keep documenting) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]].**
:::

## Personality disorder and the therapeutic use of self

::: definition Personality disorder and borderline specifically {#def-personality}
**Personality disorder as enduring patterns of self and interpersonal functioning with pathological traits (the ICD-11 dimensional severity model - mild, moderate, severe - plus trait domains, a departure from the categorical personality cluster list that removed most personality subtypes except the borderline-with-traits pattern, an evidence-driven reform that also reduced stigma and improved reliability) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; borderline personality disorder clinically: the affective-instability-and-abandonment-fear-and-identity-disturbance-and-impulsivity-and-recurrent-self-harm-and-suicidality-and-chronic-emptiness presentation with its transient stress-related psychotic-like experiences and dissociative episodes (the differential with bipolar II and complex PTSD and the ADHD emotion-dysregulation overlap handled with course-and-context data rather than territory arguments), the aetiology as gene-environment layering (the emotion-sensitivity-temperament times invalidating-environment dialectical-behavioural-theory formulation, the high adverse-childhood-exposure rates without naive causation, and the abuse-history sensitivity that must not become a diagnostic assumption) - [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; the treatments with evidence: dialectical-behavioural therapy (the skills-and-individual-and-phone-coaching structure, the suicide-and-self-harm reduction trials and the mechanism-versus-nonspecific debate read fairly), mentalisation-based treatment (the epistemic-hypervigilance repair model and the trial evidence), schema therapy and the general-psychiatric-management plus case-management models (the good-psychiatric-care-delivery format with the community-therapy step), and the honest statements about what does not work (no drug treats the disorder; medications target specific symptoms - the affective-instability and psychotic-like-cognition and comorbid-depression questions - with all the metabolic and dependence caveats of this book), polypharmacy and the PRN-crisis-medicines harm (the overdose-lethality and dependence-acquisition patterns in this population, and the self-harm-medication-access review that saves lives) - [[pharmacology/patient-variation]], [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]]; the service and clinician layer: the crisis plans that replace A&E attendance (the WRAP-and-safety-injection-style plans, the means-restriction counselling that is especially lethal-method-relevant here), the team-boundary and splitting dynamics named early and managed with consistent communication (the 'one voice' rule and the reflective-practice and clinical-supervision requirement, because the countertransference - rescue then rage - is the intervention-killer), the therapeutic nihilism audit (the recovery rates: the longitudinal studies showing most patients improve markedly over years, the remission-and-functioning distinction, and the stigma that keeps services underfunded for a treatable disorder) - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]; and antisocial-and-other-patterns in one honest paragraph (the callous-unemotional-traits and conduct-disorder-childhood trajectory with its early-intervention evidence, the prison-and-probation interface, the untreatable-in-custody myth versus the limited-but-real programme effects, and the personality-aware-not-personality-blamed clinical stance in general hospitals where these patients absorb disproportionate emergency use) - [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]], [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]].**
:::

::: example The school nurse's referral {#ex-school-referral}
A 13-year-old has lost two clothing sizes; 'she eats salad only', runs at lunchtime, does homework till 2am; BMI 16.4 and falling; parents say 'she's always been perfectionist'.
::: solution
**Early-anorexia-nervosa-in-adolescence ([[#def-eating]], [[#def-refeeding]], [[paediatrics/growth-development]]): the urgent-but-calm response - same-week assessment with the medical-safety screen (the bradycardia-hypotension-hypothermia observations, the postural pulse and the ECG when severe, the phosphate-potassium-sodium-glucose and the FT4-and-coeliac-and-urinalysis sweep, the growth-chart trajectory which is the paediatrician's favourite objective evidence) - [[endocrine/thyroid]], [[paediatrics/acute-childhood-illness]]; the first intervention is the parents - family-based treatment (the Maudsley parents-in-charge refeeding model with its trial superiority in this age group, the 'you are not the cause and you are the best resource' speech, the meals-as-treatment framing and the expressed-emotion reduction), with the patient's own ambivalence respected (the illness-ego-syntonic trap - the voice that calls starvation an achievement - named so the family argues with the illness, not the girl) - [[clinical-skills/communication]]; the school-and-sport practicalities (the PE-and-running suspension during refeeding as a medical instruction not a punishment, the homework-and-sleep audit with the perfectionism-driver addressed, the exam-term timing negotiated with the school's informed help) - [[paediatrics/neurodevelopment]]; and the trajectory management - the weekly-then-monthly weight-and-function tracking with relapse-early-warning lists held by the family, the bone-health-and-menstrual-history over the years, the comorbidity screen (the anxiety-and-OCD-and-autism-traits overlap and the self-harm and substance questions as she ages), the specialist-team threshold with the outpatient-versus-day-patient-versus-admission criteria explained in advance, and the social-media-and-diet-culture conversation had with her, not at her - [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]].**

::: example The self-harm that keeps returning {#ex-bpd-spiral}
A 27-year-old with borderline personality disorder has 14 ED attendances this year for cutting, each time after arguments; each time the team 'makes sure she's safe' and books her a GP appointment.
::: solution
**Breaking-the-crisis-cycle ([[#def-personality]], [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]): what the loop is doing - the attendance-relief-and-predictability structure that helps her survive minutes and harms months (the reinforced-coping-replacement problem stated kindly) - so the plan must give her something better, not just refuse the old thing; the joint-crisis-safety-plan (the warning-sign hierarchy and the coping-skills ladder from DBT, the person-to-call list, the means-restriction work with the razors-and-paracetamol-stockpile handled as home-safety not confiscation, the ED-avoidance-alternatives with the crisis-team and same-day-clinic contacts written in her own words), agreed with her and loaded into every service's record so the 15th attendance meets the plan, not a debate - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; and the hope kept on the record - the longitudinal recovery data written where the next clinician will see it, because this presentation is not character fate but a treatable disorder of emotion regulation with most patients improving substantially over time - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**

::: example The diabetes clinic overlap {#ex-diabulimia}
A 24-year-old with type 1 diabetes has HbA1c 12 and keeps 'forgetting' insulin; she admits skipping doses 'to lose weight'; BMI 19 with rapid recent loss and recurrent DKA admissions.
::: solution
**Insulin-omission-for-weight-loss ([[#def-eating]], [[#def-refeeding]], [[endocrine/diabetes]]): the behaviour named - insulin omission as purging-equivalent (the insulin-phobia that is actually a weight-control strategy, the glycosuria-calorie-loss mechanism, and the devastating double toll - the DKA-and-infection acute risk with the microvascular acceleration at brutally fast rates, the retinopathy-and-neuropathy curves in young women who omit insulin being a mortality story the next generation will audit) - [[endocrine/diabetes-complications]]; and the long game - the CBT-e-or-specialist-supportive-therapy for the eating disorder with diabetes-specific adaptations, the CGM-and-HbA1c goals renegotiated as recovery-adjacent not punitive (the targets that assume compliance become lies both sides tell), and the eye-and-kidney-and-nerve surveillance intensified during omission-years with the catch-up logic explained - [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]], [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]].**

::: history
**The eating disorders have a two-century clinical record with a twentieth-century treatment revolution: the seventeenth-century 'ategalia' and Charles Lasègue's and Sir William Gull's paired 1873-74 descriptions of anorexia nervosa (the hysterical-abstinence-versus-primary-nervous-disease debate at the Transatlantic Association meetings with the gendered medicine of the era plainly visible in hindsight), the mid-century psychoanalytic ascendency (the Bruch's mother-interpretation era whose family-blaming was overturned by the British and Israeli cohort studies of the 1980s and the genetic epidemiology that returned causation to the patient's own biology), and the recovery of food as the treatment - the post-war post-starvation-edema diet-rehabilitation lessons, the Minnesota starvation experiment's psychology data (the set-point and the preoccupation and the apathy that every refeeding nurse recognises) and the 1980s-90s family-based and cognitive-behavioural trial programmes (the Eisler and Russell Maudsley lineage, the Garner and Beck's CBT ancestors and the Fairburn transdiagnostic architecture) - [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]], [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]]; bulimia nervosa's emergence as Russell's 1979 description separated the normal-weight purging syndrome from anorexia-bulimic subtype, the antidepressant-and-topiramate pharmacotherapy trials and their modest place, and the iatrogenic-laxative-and-diuretic-abuse clinics that taught medicine the hypokalaemia-rebound-oedema trap still mismanaged today - [[renal/potassium-calcium-phosphate]], [[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]]; personality disorder's long argument: Schneider's psychopathic inferiority and the categorical DSM-I-to-IV personality lists, the psychoanalytic containment tradition (the Frieda Fromm-Reichmann and later Kernberg and Bateman-and-Fonagy mentalisation line) versus the behavioural and dialectical programme of Marsha Linehan built for chronically suicidal patients from Buddhist and CBT materials (the DBT trial lineage and the NHS Talking-Therapies-era expansion), the ICD-11 dimensional-reform argument led by the severity-model proponents and the American categorical-preservation debate, and the UK's Personality Disorder without Marginalisation policy era and its national services - [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]; the ARFID rehabilitation of a forgotten diagnosis (the infantile anorexia and 'feeding problem' literatures merged into the DSM-5 category with the autism-sensory-overlap recognition), the atypical-anorexia argument about medical danger at higher weights, and the GLP-1-era's new epidemiology of weight-drug-adjacent disordered eating with screening debates; and the current mortality reckoning - the long-follow-up cohort studies reporting the standardised mortality ratios that moved anorexia to the top of the psychiatric-mortality table, the serious-and-enduring-illness frameworks and palliative-intent-care pilots for the refractory few, and the specialist-services access audits that keep the equity argument open - [[medical-ethics-law/end-of-life-ethics]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]].**
:::

::: summary
- Eating disorders are psychiatric illnesses with somatic danger; severity is judged by physiology and trajectory, not by weight alone or appearance.
- Refeeding is a medical procedure: slow start, thiamine and vitamins first, daily electrolytes early, phosphate and potassium replaced to protocol.
- Family-based treatment leads for adolescent anorexia; CBT-E and specialist supportive therapy for adults; no medication treats anorexia core pathology.
- Binge-purge cycles respond to CBT and to treating the shame-and-restriction engine; the purging electrolyte and dental toll needs naming and dental referral.
- Personality disorder, especially borderline, is treatable; specific programmes reduce self-harm, and medicines only touch comorbid symptoms.
- Crisis planning, means restriction and team consistency do more than any single admission; recovery is common enough to warrant refusing nihilism.
:::

the PRN and medication review (the prescribing-audit feedback showing reduction with no increase in self-harm events, the as-needed-sedation-restraint protocol with its harm accounting, and the comorbidity-treatment discipline that stops both polypharmacy and therapeutic-nihilism-withholding); and the arguments (you will win the clinical ones - the recovery-rate data and the cost-of-crisis-cycle arithmetic - and lose the identity ones: the 'some personality-disorders are untreatable and therapy is a reward' frame recurs until staff have personally delivered a skills-coach call that worked, so the change programme is contact-plus-data-plus-supervision, and the outcome measures to hold the gain are the re-attendance and self-harm-frequency and functioning scales plus the staff-attitude re-survey - because every audit of this topic shows the same thing: the largest modifiable determinant of care here is what the team believes) - [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**

## Exercises

::: exercise Assess the bradycardia {level=1}
A 17-year-old with anorexia has pulse 44, BP 92/58, temp 35.8, K 3.3, PO4 1.0, ECG shows sinus bradycardia and QTc 470. Decide admission, and list the five immediate orders.
::: solution
**Admit-and-stabilise ([[#def-refeeding]]): this is severe malnutrition with symptomatic-range vitals and early electrolyte vulnerability - inpatient medical or specialist-adolescent-unit admission with cardiac monitoring, the level of care decided by trajectory and resistance as much as numbers - [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]], [[paediatrics/acute-childhood-illness]]; the orders - thiamine-and-B-complex before any carbohydrate and the refeeding protocol with slow-calorie-advance and daily-then-twice-daily electrolytes with replacement thresholds charted, the potassium-and-phosphate correction with the magnesium done together (the calcium-and-potassium interlock), the ECG-and-observation cadence with the nurse-awareness brief for the bradycardia-arrest risk in restricted-sleep-depleted purging patients, the fluid-and-sodium caution with the oedema-not-heart-failure teaching for the family, and the psychological-ward-culture agreement (the meal-support and the no-weighing-or-weighing-graded negotiation and the staff-communication-plan so treatment and trauma are not confused) - [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**
:::
:::

::: exercise Separate the diagnoses {level=2}
Differentiate in a paragraph each and name the discriminating history: (a) 20-year-old with binges and vomiting at normal weight; (b) 30-year-old with restriction since a choking incident, no body-image fear; (c) 45-year-old with restriction and rigidity since childhood, autism traits, lifelong low weight without distress; (d) 15-year-old with 10-kg loss and depression after cyberbullying, restricting to sleep.
::: solution
**Differential ([[#def-eating]]): (a) - bulimia nervosa (the binge-compensate cycle with shape-weight over-evaluation at normal weight, the dental-and-parotid-and-hypokalaemia signs, the CBT-E-and-treat-the-depression plan and the SSRI's real anti-bulimic place) - [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]]; (b) - ARFID post-fear-consequence (the no-body-image-distinction and the fear-conditioning history, the graded-exposure-and-nutrition therapy and the swallowing-and-GI evaluation done once and believed) - [[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]]; (c) - autism-spectrum-restricted-intake-with-lifelong-constitution (the sensory-profile-and-rigidity history without weight-preoccupation, the stable-longitudinal-growth and the accept-and-support-versus-pathologise decision, the energy-deficiency-and-bone-health management without an eating-disorder framework imposed) - [[paediatrics/neurodevelopment]]; and (d) - depressive-and-trauma-related appetite loss versus emerging eating disorder (the bullying-and-PTSD work as the treatment, the weight monitored not moralised, the trajectory re-assessment at 4-6 weeks that decides whether a restriction disorder has rooted, and the school-safeguarding and cyber-safety response as clinical care) - [[paediatrics/safeguarding]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]].**
:::
:::

::: exercise Write the crisis plan that works {level=2}
For a patient with borderline personality disorder and frequent lethal-intent overdoses, build the means-restriction and crisis pathway conversation, including the paracetamol supply, the family's role and the record entries.
::: solution
**Means-and-crisis-architecture ([[#def-personality]], [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]]): the lethal-means facts drive the design (the paracetamol-hepatic-failure arithmetic with the restricted-issue dispensing and pharmacy-lock protocols, the family-held-key-and-stockpile agreement with the disposal schedule, the ligature-and-firearm census done without judgement because these methods decide outcome more than intent does) - [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]], [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]; the pathway - the written joint crisis plan (the warning-sign ladder, the skills-first steps, the named-contacts and the crisis-team direct line, the ED-avoidance-rationale and the alternative-same-day-assessment offer, signed by her and copied to every service), the review cycle that keeps it alive (the pre-crisis refresh at each stable visit and the post-episode update that adds what actually helped), and the therapeutic alternatives to admission offered before it is refused (the home-treatment-team and the crisis-house and the peer-support worker with their limit-setting honesty); the family - the psychoeducation session (the validation-versus-reassurance teaching and the expressed-emotion reduction), the carers' own support and the 'what to do at 3am' card; and the record - the plan filed where pre-hospital and ED clinicians will find it (the shared-care flag and the copy to the GP), the medication-issue cadence recorded and pharmacy informed, and the recovery-events logged (the months without attendance marked as clinical progress in the notes, because a record of only crises teaches the next clinician despair) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**
:::

::: exercise Set the service standard {level=3}
Write the eating-disorder and personality-disorder service specifications for a region: access, workforce, physical-health monitoring, escalation and equity.
::: solution
**Service-specification ([[#def-eating]], [[#def-refeeding]], [[#def-personality]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]): access standards - time-to-first-contact targets by severity (the 1-2-week urgent-physical-risk and 4-week-standard pathways with the same-day-school-nurse-and-GP fast-track and the self-referral portals proven to cut waits), the 'never turned away, always offered something' rule with the no-treatment-without-monitoring exception, and the step-down-and-serious-and-enduring track that keeps contact open when cure is off the table (the recovery-oriented case management and the palliative-intent-care pilots with their explicit ethics governance) - [[medical-ethics-law/end-of-life-ethics]]; the workforce and method specification - the accredited family-based, CBT-E and DBT-or-MBT programmes with supervision and fidelity checks (the intervention-integrity requirement the trials imply), the eating-disorder nurses and psychologist and dietitian-and-OT core team with the personality-disorder-aware primary-care training as the volume solution, and the physical-health-monitoring protocol owned by named clinicians (the observation-and-bloods-and-bone-density-and-ECG schedules with abnormality-escalation criteria and the acute-medicine liaison compendium so EDs and medical wards read the same refeeding rules); and the equity-and-quality layer - the male, adult, transgender, ethnic-minority, older and atypical-weight access audits (the under-diagnosis patterns that these populations report), the deprivation-and-geography waiting-time breakdown with the online-and-hybrid-programme capacity plan, the mortality-and-serious-incident review feeding the training cycle (the starvation-cardiac-arrest and refeeding-hypophosphataemia case reviews that made the protocols), the patient-and-carer-co-produced outcome sets (the functioning-and-relationship-and-pleasure measures beside BMI), and the commissioning honesty that these services are expensive because the alternative - chronic medical admissions, fracture-years, suicide - costs more in money and lives - [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
