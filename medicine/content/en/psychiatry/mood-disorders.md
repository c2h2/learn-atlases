Mood disorders are common, disabling and occasionally fatal. **Depression is not sadness and bipolar disorder is not cheerfulness: both are syndromes of disordered mood regulation with biological, psychological and social drivers, defined by clusters of symptoms, duration, impairment and course.** Treatment works - which is precisely why accurate diagnosis, honest severity assessment and systematic follow-up matter; the biggest errors in this field are missed bipolarity, untreated severity, and therapy-and-medication plans that stop before recovery does.

## Depression

::: definition Unipolar depression {#def-depression}
**The syndrome: persistently low mood or loss of interest and pleasure for at least two weeks, with cognitive (concentration, indecision, guilt, hopelessness, the negative cognitive triad), somatic (sleep disturbance with early-morning waking, appetite and weight change, loss of libido, psychomotor change, pain and fatigue) and neurovegetative features graded by severity from mild to severe-with-or-without-psychotic-features (the melancholic and atypical specifiers retained in ICD-11 as symptom-pattern descriptors; the psychotic features - nihilistic, guilt and persecutory delusions with mood-congruent texture - defining the depression that needs antidepressant-plus-antipsychotic or ECT, not counselling alone) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the differential that protects the diagnosis: bipolar depression (the past-hypomania interrogation that catches half of what the outpatient interview misses, the at-onset-young-and-treatment-resistant-and-postpartum flags), adjustment and normalised grief held open for weeks not years with the cultural-ritual context, the organic mimics (hypothyroidism, Cushing and Addison, strokes and tumours, Parkinson disease, vitamin B12 and folate, the medication list from steroids to beta-blockers to isotretinoin folklore read honestly), and the fatigue-and-pain syndromes where depression is companion not cause (the bidirectional relationship with cardiovascular disease, diabetes and chronic pain that makes depression a risk factor, not just a reaction) - [[endocrine/thyroid]], [[endocrine/adrenal]], [[nervous-system/headache-pain]]; the aetiology as bio-psycho-psycho-social layering: genetic liability of moderate heritability polygenic-plus-stress-sensitisation with the HPA-axis and inflammation and neuroplasticity findings (the 5-HTT-candidate-gene collapse teaching polygenic-and-environment honesty, the adverse-childhood-exposure dose-response, and the cognitive-schema and behavioural-activation models that therapy exploits) - [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]]; and the course that defines management: episodes, recurrences (each episode raising the next episode's probability), the residual-symptom-to-relapse pipeline, the discontinuation-and-maintenance rules (the 6-12-month post-remission continuation and the multiple-episode-maintenance logic), and the high-leverage interventions at population level (the screening-then-treat step-care systems, the exercise-and-sleep evidence effect sizes comparable to medication in mild-moderate depression, and the suicide-risk management covered in the assessment chapter) - [[psychiatry/self-harm-suicide-prevention]], [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]].**
:::

## Bipolar and its minefields

::: definition Bipolar disorders {#def-bipolar}
**Bipolar I is at least one manic episode (elevated, expansive or irritable mood with increased energy, reduced sleep without fatigue, pressured speech, flight of ideas, grandiosity, and goal-directed or reckless activity causing marked impairment or psychosis, one week or hospitalisation), usually with depressive episodes; bipolar II is hypomania plus major depression (the hypomania that families sometimes call 'the good times' until the crash, and whose under-elicitation causes most misdiagnosed bipolarity); and the cyclothymic and unspecified spectra where course-of-illness dominates diagnosis (the ICD-11 simplification of the older manic-depressive insanity, the threshold debates for DSM-5's two types and the soft-bipolar inflation that prescribers should resist, since the label changes treatment and insurance and self-concept) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the management architecture: mania acute - environmental de-escalation, a benzodiazepine bridge, an antipsychotic or valproate or carbamazepine (the haloperidol, olanzapine, risperidone and quetiapine evidence cluster with their metabolic and sedation profiles) and the capacity-and-detention decisions covered in the law chapter; maintenance - the mood-stabiliser evidence (lithium's unique anti-suicide signal alongside the thyroid-parathyroid-renal-monitoring apparatus, the valproate-teratogenicity catastrophe that made pregnancy-prevention programmes mandatory and made prescribers learn the effective-alternatives list before reaching for it, lamotrigine's depression-prevention niche with its rash-titration discipline and the valproate-interaction dose arithmetic, and the carbamazepine-versus-lamotrigine-versus-atypical-antipsychotic maintenance comparisons with their interaction burdens), plus the psychosocial layer that measurably reduces relapse (the psychoeducation and routine-and-interpersonal-social-rhythm therapies, the early-warning-sign plans with family, and the sleep-stability discipline that is pharmacology by other means) - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]], [[nervous-system/epilepsy]]; the antidepressant trap - antidepressant-mono-therapy in bipolar-I risking switch and cycle acceleration (the trial evidence read with nuance for bipolar-II depression where some agents are used with a stabiliser cover) and the treatment algorithm differences that make missing bipolarity the field's commonest consequential error; and the mixed-features era - the dysphoric-manic and agitated-depressive states with their suicide-risk amplification and their irritability-and-insomnia phenotypes (the antipsychotic-versus-lithium-versus-valproate logic and the antidepressant-withdrawal-not-escalation decision, with akathisia distinguished from agitation because the treatments oppose) - [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]].**
:::

## Treatment toolbox

::: definition Antidepressants and psychological therapies {#def-treatments}
**Antidepressants: SSRIs, SNRIs, mirtazapine, trazodone, reboxetine's reputation, agomelatine's liver monitoring, MAOIs' retained efficacy and dietary-pharmacy rules, vortioxetine and others in the selective-pharmacology niche (the receptor-binding profiles behind the side-effect differences - the sexual and weight and sedation and QT and hyponatraemia and bleeding-and-hyponatraemia-in-elderly issues, and the discontinuation syndromes ranked by half-life from fluvoxamine-and-paroxetine's-chaos to fluoxetine's self-taper) - the net effect honest: meaningful benefit in moderate-to-severe depression (the severity-dependent benefit shown by individual-patient-data analyses and the large placebo response of trials), small-but-real in mild, and the risk-benefit flip in under-25s requiring the suicidality-monitoring consent conversation that changed prescribing forever - [[pharmacology/patient-variation]], [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]]; the practical art: adequate dose-and-duration (the four-to-six-week-at-therapeutic-dose rule before judging, the switch-versus-augment-versus-combine algorithms after two adequate failures with their modest cross-trial remission rates), the titration-and-expectation-setting that determines adherence (the early-anxiety-worsening warning, the sexual-side-effect conversation had before it happens so it can be managed rather than silently abandoned), and the pharmacogenetic footnote kept honest (the CPIC-guideline dose adjustments exist but routine CYP testing has not shown consistent outcome benefit except in specific accumulation scenarios) - [[pharmacology/patient-variation]]; psychological therapies with depression evidence: behavioural activation (the activity-scheduling that outperformed its own complexity when dismantled), CBT (the thought-and-behaviour method with the strongest trial base across severity and formats, including guided digital versions and their access-equity promise and limitations), IPT's role and interpersonal maintenance, the third-wave additions (the MBCT relapse-prevention programme for three-or-more-episodes with its meta-analytic backing and its accessibility caveats), and the CBASP and couples-and-family adjuncts; and the procedural options for the ill who are not responding - rTMS's modest effect and access realities, ketamine-and-esketamine's rapid-then-transient advantage and the service-model questions (the monitoring, the dissociation management and the relapse-after-days problem defining them as bridges not answers), and ECT's retained position as the most rapidly effective treatment for severe, psychotic, catatonic, suicidal and food-refusing depressions, with the consent law, the memory-side-effect counselling done properly, and the continuing political rather than evidential controversy - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[nervous-system/epilepsy]].**
:::

::: example Lithium at the interface {#ex-lithium-interface}

## Where this leads

Assessment craft in [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]], anxiety and trauma disorders in [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]], psychosis overlaps in [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], and the legal and treatment-system frame in [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; perinatal mood disorders in [[obstetrics-gynaecology/postnatal-period]] and later-life in [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]].

::: history
**Melancholia is medicine's oldest diagnosis - the Hippocratic black bile and the Galenic temperament, retained for two millennia as a humoral idea until the nineteenth century made it a disease entity: Esquirol and then Griesinger's separation of manic-depressive insanity from dementia praecox, and Kraepelin's longitudinal case-note method that made mood-course the criterion (the 1896-1915 editions and the Cologne clinic records that still reward reading) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the twentieth century split and rejoined the syndromes: Kleist and Leonhard's unipolar-versus-bipolar distinction (the 'cycloid' and phased-endogenous vocabularies now half-revived as mixed-features specifiers) and the Angst and Perris family-study work that made bipolar II a research and then clinical entity, against the clinical-epidemiology school of Winokur and Clayton who mapped depression's subtypes and inheritance; and the treatment revolution that made all this urgent - the iproniazid serendipity of the 1950s sanatorium (the mood-elevating side effect noticed by Loomer and the Kline teams and Selikoff's reports of cheerful, talkative tuberculosis patients), the imipramine finding at the Burghölzli, the mianserin-to-fluoxetine safety-by-design lineage (the tricyclic-to-SSRI shift measured in overdose deaths and the still-debated activation-suicidality signals) - [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]]; lithium's own career - John Cade's 1949 report on lithium in mania from an Australian ward, the US introduction after lithium chloride as a salt substitute was withdrawn, and the placebo-controlled relapse-prevention trials of the early 1970s that established maintenance psychopharmacology; and ECT's trajectory from the 1938 Cerletti-and-Bini era (with its own monstrous contemporary echoes covered in ethics) through the suction-and-modern-technique reform and the durable political controversy; and the modern plural era - the WHO disability-tables raising depression's public-profile, the national depression-service-improvement programmes, the delivery-trials (the stepped care and the collaborative-care outcome effect being among psychiatry's most robust findings), the ketamine-repurposing wave and the digital-therapy and precision-psychiatry programmes - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]].**
:::

::: summary
- Depression is a syndrome of mood, cognition and body with graded severity; bipolarity must be actively excluded because its treatment inverts the unipolar plan.
- Antidepressants help moderate-severe depression, need weeks at dose, carry manageable side effects whose anticipation protects adherence, and should continue 6-12 months past remission (longer with recurrences).
- Lithium, valproate, lamotrigine and antipsychotics each have a maintenance niche in bipolar disorder; valproate is a teratogen and prescribed only inside prevention programmes.
- Psychological therapies are disease-modifying in depression; behavioural activation and CBT have the strongest bases, MBCT prevents relapse.
- Postpartum and late-life mood presentations demand the organic and bipolar sweep before the treatment-resistance label.
- Measurement (PHQ-family, functional goals, side-effect checklists) turns mood care from opinion into a feedback loop.
:::

::: solution
**First-antidepressant-clinic ([[#def-treatments]], [[#def-bipolar]], [[pharmacology/patient-variation]]): the student - SSRI-with-panic-education (the initial-anxiety-worsening warning with the start-low-slow plan and the benzodiazepine-bridge decision documented, CBT referral with the waiting-list bridge (the guided-digital or bibliotherapy interim), the activation-and-suicidality monitoring in under-25s as a consented conversation, and the therapy-then-maintenance sequencing) - [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]]; the 71-year-old - mirtazapine-at-night-at-hypnotic-dose fits the insomnia-and-weight-loss profile (the sedation-and-next-morning-driving-and-falls discussion, the sodium with furosemide-and-SSRI-hyponatraemia risk, and the the anticholinergic-load-and-postural-hypotension re-check), or trazodone's short-course niche read against its evidence; the epileptic on carbamazepine - the enzyme-inducer interactions (the lamotrigine-and-oral-contraceptive-and-warfarin-and-DOAC-antipsychotic-metabolic-ripple that carbamazepine creates, the bupropion-contraindication and the SSRI-seizure-safety profile, and the dose re-check when the inducer is ever changed) - [[nervous-system/epilepsy]], [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]]; and the pregnancy-planner - the pre-conception risk conversation (the sertraline-versus-paroxetine-avoidance-versus-mirtazapine data with the honest effect sizes, the untreated-depression costs that make 'stop everything' equally wrong, the folate-and-teratology-and-neonatal-adaptation liaison, and the breastfeeding plan and the postpartum-relapse-prevention that needs writing before conception) - [[obstetrics-gynaecology/early-pregnancy]], [[pharmacology/patient-variation]].**

::: exercise Run the deterioration plan {level=2}
A bipolar-I patient on lithium and olanzapine becomes increasingly grandiose and stops attending; his mother telephones the clinic. What is the sequence, who decides what, and what documents matter?
::: solution
**Relapse-trajectory-management ([[#def-bipolar]], [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]], [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]): the early-window work - the agreed early-warning-sign plan (the sleep-length-first marker), the outreach and crisis-team contact attempted and logged before thresholds are crossed, the third-party information received and recorded (with what said back to the patient about it - the confidentiality-with-care line), and the home-visit option that converts refusal into a conversation; the legal thresholds - the Mental Health Act assessment only when criteria are met (the health-or-safety-and-protection logic, the least-restrictive-alternative reasoning documented, the community-treatment-order-versus-conditional-discharge mechanics and the nearest-available-PSW-and-approved-doctor logistics), against the voluntary-admission persuasion done with dignity and the family-meeting-alternative; the capacity layer - the financial and medical refusals assessed decision-by-decision (the grandiosity-versus-capacity overlap where the belief itself may destroy the weighing ability), the Lasting-Power-of-Attorney and advance-statement search, and the deprivation-of-liberty safeguards if he is settled informally in a care-like setting; and the after - the relapse post-mortem with the patient once well (what worked, what he hated, what to change in the plan the next time, since there is usually a next time), and the adherence-machinery redesign (the long-acting-injectable option with its relationship-preserving potential, the once-daily simplification and the side-effect accounting that usually underlies 'I'm fine now') - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**

::: exercise Critique the antidepressant-depression narrative {level=3}
'Antidepressants don't work; they're placebo; pharma hid the trials; everyone who got better should stop.' Appraise each clause with the evidence, and formulate the clinical message you would give a patient facing this online.
::: solution
**Narrative-appraisal ([[#def-treatments]], [[epidemiology-ebm/critical-appraisal]], [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]]): 'don't work/placebo' - the meta-analytic truth: statistically significant mean differences that exceed placebo in group terms, with severity-dependence (the benefit larger in severe episodes, the placebo and remission-over-time responses large in mild ones), and the individual-level point that a 10-15 percent absolute advantage with a 40-55 percent response rate means some people's benefit is invisible in the averages - the NNT-versus-number-needed-to-harm arithmetic stated per person not per population - [[epidemiology-ebm/statistics-for-clinicians]]; 'pharma hid the trials' - largely documented and partly remedied (the trial-registration and all-results-reporting norms, the IPD-meta-analysis corrections, the residual publication-and-guest-authorship problems, and the honest consequence that guideline evidence bases are cleaner now than the archives that produced them) - [[epidemiology-ebm/systematic-reviews]], [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]]; 'should stop' - the dangerous clause (the relapse-on-abrupt-discontinuation-versus-true-relapse distinction that requires slow planned reductions, the discontinuation syndromes that scare patients into 'addiction' narratives they then manage badly, and the maintenance-episode arithmetic for recurrent and severe illness where stopping is a relapse intervention); and the consultation answer - acknowledge the legitimate criticisms (they are true enough and admitting them buys credibility), reframe to decisions about this severity and this history and this side-effect profile (the measurement-based plan with review dates), keep therapy-and-lifestyle in the first sentence rather than the footnote, and never let the online narrative or the old prescriber's complacency replace the two questions that matter: is the depression improving, and what are you prepared to continue? - [[clinical-skills/communication]].**
