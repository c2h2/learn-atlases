Suicide is a public-health problem with a clinical signature: most people who die by suicide were in contact with health or social services in the preceding year, almost always with a story that reads differently backwards. **Suicide prevention in medicine is therefore not a single dramatic intervention but a chain of small competencies: asking directly, stratifying honestly, restricting means concretely, treating the drivers, following up through the high-risk windows, and learning from every death.** Self-harm, the strongest predictor in the field, is addressed here as a clinical object in its own right, not merely a warning shot.

## Asking and judging

::: definition Assessment of suicide risk {#def-assessment}
**The asking - direct, specific, normalised and repeated: the evidence that asking about suicide does not increase risk or plant ideas (the patient-satisfaction and safety literature consistent on this) and the question sequence that works (from the general-meaning-losing frame through thoughts-of-not-wanting-to-wake to intent-planning-method-rehearsal-timeline, with the detail itself informing the response), asked standing up never, in privacy, with the time taken (the 'any thoughts about ending things lately?' screened into every low-mood and self-harm and even chronic-pain and postnatal contact per guidelines); the actuarial layer - the factors that actually move probability (previous attempt and self-harm as the dominant item, male-lethal-method epidemiology and the age-and-method pattern, psychiatric disorders with depression-psychosis-bipolar-and-childhood-trauma-and-conduct-traits prominent, the personality-and-impulsivity and abuse-history pathways, chronic pain and illness and TBI, the family-suicide-history-and-contagion effect, access to firearms and other lethal means, recent loss and discharge and legal-and-financial crises as precipitants, and protection factors - children-as-reasons-for-living and future-orientation and religious-and-social-connection - elicited explicitly because they change management and the patient's own hope-calibration) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; the structured aids kept in their place - the scales as communication props not oracles (the C-SSRS and Beck-family instruments' modest predictive value at the individual level, the no-instrument-replaces-the-question 'what were you thinking when...' and the narrative timeline of the recent episode reconstructed with the patient), and the risk-formulation output stated as a scenario (the drivers, the triggers, the protective anchors, the means picture, the plan and the review interval - the formulation handed to the patient as much as the file, since a person who can see their own risk-logic is enrolled in its management); and the two errors that dominate inquiries - dismissive-underestimation (the 'attention-seeking' label for the highest-predictor population, the low-communication-intent-presenters who die as often as the dramatic ones, and the 'contract for safety' fiction - a promise that obliges nobody and delays the actual plan) and anxious-overestimation (the defensive-admission cascade that iatrogenically reinforces the self-harm loop, the indefinite-observation cultures, and the opportunity cost to the person's real life - all three fixed by the same thing: a specific written plan proportionate to the formulation) - [[psychiatry/eating-personality-disorders]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**
:::

## Self-harm as its own object

::: definition Self-harm: functions, management, and the systems around it {#def-selfharm}
**The functions assessment before any intervention (the affect-regulation and self-punishment and communication-and-protest and dissociation-anchoring and anti-dissociation-arousal reasons each predicting different treatment and different prognosis, documented in person-first language because the next clinician's framing changes this patient's care trajectory), the epidemiology truth (the adolescent-and-young-adult female predominance with rising help-seeking, the rising-method diversity and the media-and-peer-contagion components of the clusters, and the large untreated majority - screening studies showing many times more self-harm than presentations) - [[psychiatry/eating-personality-disorders]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]]; the presentation pathway that the NICE-derived self-harm services run (the medical assessment and injury-and-ingestion management - the paracetamol-and-ibuprofen-and-extended-release-formulation toxicity arithmetic with the nomograms-and-time-since-ingestion logic, the corrosive-and-clotting-and-airway dangers, the wound-and-tendon-and-nerve examination with the hand-surgery referral thresholds, and the never-discharge-without-the-psychosocial-assessment that is the actual treatment for most attenders) - [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]], [[surgery/wounds-surgical-infection]]; the psychosocial intervention offer (the short structured psychological interventions - the problem-solving and the CBT-and-problem-adaptation-treatment with their repeat-episode reduction numbers in defined populations, the dialectical-behavioural and mentalisation programmes for the frequent-presenter group and the personality framework, and the honest limits of the 'assessment only' trial findings for heterogeneous attenders that shaped the guidance), the means-restriction conversation for self-harm as for suicide (the medication-stockpile and-sharps-and-alcohol-home review done as collaborative safety-planning, the pharmacy dispensing-limits and the family-involvement-with-consent), and the follow-up contact that measurably works (the post-dispatch card and the telephone follow-up - the heads-up and contact-after-assessment traditions with their self-harm-rate reductions, cheap enough to be a policy and neglected often enough to be a scandal) - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]]; and the system-culture layer (the language reform - no 'frequent attender' as a verdict, the self-harm-and-suicide prevention strategies with their cross-agency means-and-media-and-school components, the emergency-department-ambience-and-taint-and-privacy improvements patients rate as treatment-quality, the chaperoned-and-adolescent-capacity-and-Mental-Capacity-Act nuances for the under-16s including the confidentiality-versus-parenting judgement calls, and the workforce-support for staff after patient deaths, which is prevention for the next patient) - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]].**
:::

## Windows, learning and the public health view

::: definition Prevention across time and population {#def-prevention}
**The time-structure of risk: the inpatient-and-immediately-post-discharge windows (the first days-to-months-after-discharge elevated rates that make discharge-planning a suicide-prevention procedure - the follow-up appointment before leaving, the medication-supply-with-lethality-checked and the means-conversation with family-with-consent and the crisis-card and the GP-informed-same-week-communication), the post-self-harm months (the elevated medium-term rates that justify sustained-contact rather than one-assessment medicine), the bereavement-and-exposure windows (the suicide-bereaved families' own elevated risk and the NICE-level-ONR support services), the contagion windows (the media-guidelines for reporting - the method-and-location-and-sensationalism effects documented in the Werther-and-then-Papageno-literature, the workplace-and-school-cluster protocols with their 'tell-the-truth-carefully' communications, and the social-media-era amplification the platforms' duty-of-care rules are slowly catching) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]]; the means-restriction evidence base, psychiatry's most underused textbook chapter (the barbiturate-and-coal-gas-and-domestic-gas detoxification history of method-specific availability and falling deaths, the firearm-access counselling with the temporariness-and-storage framing from the suicide-prevention literature, the vehicle-exhaust and bridge-and-rail barrier-and-design programmes with their site-specific total-effect data and the method-substitution objection mostly not materialising in the follow-ups, and the pharmacy blister-and-limited-quantity dispensing rules read together with the paracetamol-purchase-restriction evaluations) - [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]]; the treat-the-drivers layer (depression-and-bipolar-and-psychosis and substance treatment at population coverage, the lithium-and-clozapine-and-ECT anti-suicide evidence in their niches, the follow-through with the pain-and-TBI-and-chronic-illness pathways and the social-prescribing-and-benefits-and-housing navigation where despair has a postcode; the school-based and gatekeeper programmes with their mixed-but-improving evidence, and the the connectedness-and-belonging research whose drivers are not clinical at all) - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]], [[nervous-system/headache-pain]]; and the learning-from-deaths system (the clinical case reviews and prevention-priority-areas programmes that convert each death into system-change questions rather than individual-blame hunts, the coroner's-inquest-and-narrative-prevention-role, the duty-of-candour to families whose questions are usually 'why didn't anyone ask me', the family-advisited reviews done with rather than about bereaved relatives, and the research-and-data infrastructure - the confidentiality-safe linkage studies of service-contact-before-death that are the epidemiology of prevention itself) - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]].**
:::

::: example The discharge that should have been different {#ex-discharge-window}
A 34-year-old man is discharged at 11pm after a week's admission for suicidal depression, improved on medication, next GP appointment 'in about two weeks'; he lives alone; the taxi is paid.
::: solution
**Discharge-as-prevention-procedure ([[#def-prevention]], [[#def-assessment]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]): the risk window named - that evening and fortnight are the highest-risk of his life (the discharge-suicide epidemiology with its early-morning-after-specific cluster, the improvement-of-energy-before-hope phase of antidepressant recovery, the aloneness and the evening and the unfinished business of his flat), so 'improved on the ward' is not the discharge criterion - ' survivably re-embedded' is; the plan that should replace the taxi - the follow-up contact inside 24-72 hours with a named person and number (the telephone-follow-up evidence), the GP communication sent not handed (the same-day phone for the weekend-with-no-medication-refill trap, the sick-day-and-crisis-card with his own warning signs), the medication supply lethality-reviewed (the quantity and the paracetamol-and-alcohol conversation and the family-or-friend who holds the stock if he agrees), and the means conversation documented (what is in the flat, what moves tonight, and who knows his plan) - [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]]; the re-embedding (the daytime-structure plan - the work-or-volunteering-or-day-service bridge, the friend-or-sibling invited to the final ward conversation with consent, the alcohol rule, the gym-and-sleep-and-light schedule that treats depression physiology on a bank holiday), and the evening-hour-access truth (the crisis-team and helpline numbers that actually answer, and the open-door instruction 'come back before you are at the point, we will not turn you away' said while he can believe it); and the system audit this story invites - the discharge-time-and-weekend-follow-up rates as quality metrics, the late-evening-discharge practice reviewed (the sleep-and-orientation-and-nothing-to-eat arrivals to empty flats), and the bereaved-family-reviews finding 'nobody called' repeated until services make the call the un-skippable step - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
:::

::: example The teenager's texts {#ex-adolescent-selfharm}
A 15-year-old is brought after superficial arm cuts found by her mother, who also found 'pro-cutting' group chats; she is furious at the phone-confiscation, minimises ('it was accidental'), and refuses school.
::: solution
**Adolescent-self-harm-with-online-contagion ([[#def-selfharm]], [[#def-assessment]], [[paediatrics/safeguarding]]): the assessment frame - interview her alone first (the confidentiality-with-limits conversation she needs to hear to be honest), then the mother separately (the collateral on function and escalation), then the joint plan (the 'your daughter is frightened and coping' reframe that converts punishment-energy into worry-energy, since the confiscation fight will cost the intervention its ally) - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]]; the risk reading age-appropriate (the accidental-versus-intentional ambiguity itself common and not deceptive, the cutting-function assessment - pressure-release and belonging more than suicide intent at this stage but with the elevated future risk documented, the group-chat-learned-behaviour dimension distinguishing social-contagion from covert-planning, the bullying-and-sexting-and-exploitation screen online-safety-officers take seriously, and the low-threshold depression-and-abuse-and-neurodevelopmental comorbidity check) - [[paediatrics/neurodevelopment]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; the intervention stack - the CAMHS-and-school joint plan (the education-negotiation that keeps her in school-with-adjustments because disengagement is the actual trajectory-risk, the brief-psychological-intervention or group-skills access, the family-communication coaching with the mealtimes-and-privacy truce), the online-safety response that is not naive (the group-chat reporting and the platform-tools and the counter-narrative work - the peer-support and recovery-story accounts that the contagion literature suggests compete with the pro-self-harm ones, the sleep-and-phone-curfew negotiated not imposed because autonomy is the growth-task), and the means conversation with the mother modelled as harm-reduction (the sharps-and-medication-inventory as house-protocol not punishment) - [[public-health/prevention-promotion]], [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]]; and the follow-through (the review-within-the-week that tests the plan not the mood, the school's designated-safeguarding-lead briefed within consent-limits, the escalation-criteria card shared, and the hope communicated out loud - the recovery-rate statistics of adolescent self-harm told to the family as the true denominator, because this presentation more often marks a phase than a fate) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[paediatrics/growth-development]].**
:::

::: example The improving patient who is not safe {#ex-early-improvement}
Three weeks into sertraline for severe depression with suicidal intent, a patient's sleep and appetite have returned; he cancelled his last two appointments; the nurse notes he 'looks so much better' and his PHQ fell from 24 to 16.
::: solution
**Improvement-is-a-risk-window ([[#def-assessment]], [[#def-prevention]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]): the clinical trap named - recovery of energy and initiative before recovery of hope is the classic suicide-risk inflection (the psychomotor-retardation-release argument, the treatment-emergent-suicidality data in the young, and the appointment-cancelling-and-sudden-compliance-or-sudden-disengagement patterns that precede completed acts), so 'looks better' triggers re-asked risk questions, not relief - [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]; the 48-hour actions - direct re-contact attempts escalating to collateral-with-consent and the GP alert (the did-not-attend-follow-up policies exist for exactly this population-segment), the re-ask done properly (the 'the part of you that wanted to die - what is it doing now?' phrasing, the plan-and-means re-inventory including the stockpiled-paracetamol-and-insulin-and-firearm checks the first assessment found boring), and the medication-quantity-and-alcohol-and-lithium-or-antipsychotic-consideration review with the psychiatrist (the adherence-as-self-harm-method dimension, the switch-and-augmentation timing, and the akathisia screen that catches iatrogenic-restlessness misread as improvement) - [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]]; the plan re-tune - the safety plan re-written for the new phase (the coping-and-contacts list stress-tested against his actual evenings, the family-involvement-consent revisited now that he has energy to refuse or accept), the follow-up interval shortened not lengthened during early improvement (the counterintuitive scheduling rule taught once and forgotten annually), and the work-and-disability pressure-cooker examined (the 'getting better enough to be fired' and return-to-work terror that populate inquest files); and the meta-teaching - the PHQ-drop-is-the-green-light fallacy (the score improving while risk pivots is the pattern the scales cannot see, so the risk formulation lives beside the score, updated and dated, and the team that reads only numbers is the team that gets surprised) - [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
:::

::: example The older farmer's decision {#ex-older-male}
A 74-year-old widower, three months after his wife's death, mentions 'sorting the farm and then no more bother for anyone'; he is 'eating fine' per the district nurse; he has not been seen by anyone else since the funeral.
::: solution
**Older-male-suicide-prevention ([[#def-assessment]], [[#def-prevention]], [[geriatrics-palliative/ageing-frailty]]): the language of risk in this demographic - the 'no more bother' and affairs-in-order and legacy-speech as the communication style older men actually use (the help-not-seeking and 'burden' cognitions with their strong predictive weight, the weapon-and-method access - firearms-and-vehicles-and-agrochemicals - and the bereavement-and-physical-health-and-driving-loss cascade), so the GP-and-district-nurse opportunity is now, not at the memory-clinic referral; the asking adapted (the 'you've had a cruel loss - some men in your position think it would be better not to carry on' phrasing that keeps his masculinity intact and the specificity that follows, the agrochemical-and-firearm-and-vehicle-cab inventory handled as practical safety with the son-or-neighbour asked to hold things - the temporary-storage counselling evidence applied to a farm, not a suburb), and the physical-health-screen done with the question (the pain-and-alcohol-and-sleep-and-weight-loss review, the delirium-and-depression-dementia differential in the very old, and the 'eating fine' contradicted by the notional-weight-chart the nurse can check) - [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]]; the activation of non-clinical scaffolding (the bereavement-support and community-and-faith-and-male-space options negotiated through what he'd tolerate - the farm-visit-not-voluntary-booking reframe, the neighbouring-and-veterans-and-tribes-of-practical-men principle, the agricultural-medical-scheme and helpline materials placed where he'd actually see them, and the family briefed to hear 'I'm all right' as data not conclusion); and the follow-up discipline (the named-person continuity - the district nurse as the relationship that can ask twice, the scheduled review at 2 weeks and at the anniversaries of the death, and the documentation that tells the next clinician the risk narrative in his words so a future 'low mood' appointment starts with the farm-and-burden language already understood) - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**

## Where this leads

The risk assessment lives in [[psychiatry/psychiatric-assessment]]; the drivers in [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]] and [[psychiatry/eating-personality-disorders]]; the legal edge in [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; poisoning management in [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]]; population prevention in [[public-health/prevention-promotion]].

::: history
**Suicide prevention is older than psychiatry and younger than epidemiology: the legal-and-moral history - the suicidal-estate-forfeiture and attempted-suicide-as-crime traditions (the Christian burial denials and the 'felo de se' verdicts whose reform, and the 1961 and 1967 British decriminalisations, turned the subject from sin-and-crime toward medicine), Durkheim's 1897 Suicide establishing the social-facts framework (the integration-and-regulation typology still visible behind every contemporary 'deaths of despair' series), and the 20th-century invention of the prevention-impulse as a clinical specialty - the 1958-61 founding of the 'crisis centres' (the London Suicide Prevention Project's telephone line and the Oxford record-linkage case-review studies of service contact before death, the cap-on-the-domestic-gas story as the founding proof that prevention works, and the later workplace-institution prevention programmes as the corporate template) - [[public-health/prevention-promotion]], [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]]; the risk-factor epidemiology era - the Aaron Beck-lineage hopelessness studies (the Hopelessness Scale and the follow-up cohorts that made cognition a risk factor rather than commentary), the perfectionism-and-aggression-and-impulsivity longitudinal work, and the genetics-and-family-cluster studies separating transmission-of-risk from contagion - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; the treatment-evidence turn - the lithium-anti-suicide signal from the 1970s-80s clinics (the the early clinic-based maintenance-lithium suicide-rate comparisons and the later meta-analyses) and the clozapine FDA-suicidality indication from the InterSePT trial, the ketamine-anti-suicidal-ideation trials whose rapid-effect claims came with the short-duration caveats, and the CBT-for-suicide-prevention safety-planning programmes; and the public-health modernisation - the media-guidelines lineage (the Canadian and then WHO and IASC reporting recommendations built on the Werther-effect literature and the Papageno-effect protective-reporting studies), the national-strategies and the SUPREME programme, the zero-suicides-in-sentenced-custody campaigns, the US Air Force and VA re-engineering of means-and-culture as institutional proof-of-concept, the Zimbabwean-and-Indian community models with their task-shifted friendship-bench evidence, and the current era of restriction-of-access programmes (the bridge-barriers-and-exhaust-catalyst data, the firearm-temporary-storage counselling) alongside the recognition that most deaths are the end of a long social causal chain whose clinical endpoints are only the last mile - [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[public-health/global-health]].**
:::

::: summary
- Ask directly and specifically; asking is safe, and the detail of the narrative is the assessment.
- Prior self-harm and attempt, lethal-means access, the discharge and bereavement windows, and untreated illness drive probability; protection factors are elicited and used, not ticked.
- Means restriction is the most evidence-backed, least-resisted and most-neglected intervention in the field.
- Self-harm is managed by function, with the psychosocial assessment-plus-follow-up as the evidence unit; contracts-for-safety are not.
- Discharge is a procedure with safety steps: contact within days, means review, family-with-consent involvement, and medication quantities that assume the worst week.
- Every death is a data point owed to the learning system and the family owed candour; prevention is a chain, and medicine holds several of its links.
:::

Your board has received five serious incidents after suicides in one year. Design the response system: learning review method, family partnership, metrics, and the two failure modes (blame-culture and closure-culture) you must refuse.
::: solution
**Learning-from-deaths-system ([[#def-prevention]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]]): the review method - each death gets a multidisciplinary learning review within defined time (the terms-of-reference template covering access-to-care and risk-assessment and documentation and discharge-and-follow-up and means-and-cross-agency questions, the chronological timeline the family co-authors, and the prevention-priority-areas style cross-case synthesis that finds the fifth death's pattern in the first four's themes), with the coroner-liaison and duty-of-candour processes aligned so families get answers and the system gets truth without the legal-chill trade (the 'what will you change' answer given in writing, the date named) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]]; the family partnership - the family-support-and-adviser service from week one (the independent advocacy and the bereaved-family-members on the review panels and the strategy's governance with vote-share not decoration), the 'we will ask what happened, not who to blame' contract stated early (because inquiries historically produced careers-ended-not-systems-fixed and families know it), and the learning-publication commitment with sensitivity review; the metrics - the chain-of-care indicators the deaths exposed (the time-to-first-contact and did-not-attend-follow-up rates, the discharge-window-appointment-fill rates, the physical-health-and-risk-formulation documentation quality audits, the means-advice rates in self-harm discharges), the equity read (whose deaths get reviewed and whose get filed - the inpatient-versus-prison-versus-nowhere-in-system differential), and the board-level harm dashboard with the strategy-progress report the public can read; and the two refusals - blame-culture (the individual-scapegoat finding that ends learning, and the defensive-documentation and risk-averse-exclusion it breeds - the 'don't assess, don't assessable' pathology that makes care worse for every future patient) and closure-culture (the 'no lessons, tragic illness' epitaph that families rightly revolt against and that forecloses the one legitimate gain each death can produce), holding the true position: most deaths had multiple system-contributing links and no single villain, accountability means answering, and the measure of the system is the sixth death's preventability-list getting shorter - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**

## Exercises

::: exercise Ask the questions {level=1}
Write (in words) the five-step suicide enquiry for a primary-care depression review, from opening to safety plan.
::: solution
**The-five-step-enquiry ([[#def-assessment]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]): the opening - 'with everything you've described, some people find life not worth continuing - have you had thoughts like that?' (the normalising preamble that lowers shame and raises accuracy); the elaboration - frequency, duration, the internal-script detail, the 'what stops you' protection question asked genuinely (the reasons-for-living that become plan-material); the intent-and-plan probe - method thought about, steps taken, rehearsal and timeline, the written-note-and-affairs-in-order markers, and the 'when did you decide you'd act if you decided?' future-orientation test; the means inventory - what's at home, what's accessible, the stockpile and sharps and alcohol and firearm questions asked without accusation with the storage-and-temporariness counselling offered (the lethal-means-removal-with-consent-and-family-help conversation); and the closing safety structure - the warning-sign-and-coping-and-contact ladder written with her, the medication-supply decision made to the plan, the follow-up appointment booked before she leaves with a named person, and the crisis-route stated twice (the number and the 'come before the worst hour' instruction), plus the record entry another clinician could act on tonight - [[#def-prevention]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**

::: exercise Manage the ingestion {level=2}
A 29-year-old ingests an unknown quantity of her boyfriend's slow-release paracetamol 'about 14 hours ago' after an argument, now feeling fine; she asks to 'just go home'. Handle the medicine and the person in one plan.
::: solution
**Paracetamol-ingestion-plus-psychosocial-duty ([[#def-selfharm]], [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]): the toxicology non-negotiables - modified-release paracetamol with a late-presenter timeline means the nomogram cannot be relied on alone (the four-hour-plus-level and repeat-testing strategy, the treatment-line decision and the NAC started when levels or history warrant it with the hepatic-injury trajectory monitored by INR-and-transaminases-and-creatinine-and-lactate and the King's-College-transplant-criteria alertness), the unknown-quantity and co-ingestant history chased (the boyfriend's tablets and the alcohol and sedatives on board and the paracetamol-containing-cold-remedies hidden in the count) - [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]; the 'feeling fine' trap named (the asymptomatic-window-then-hepatotoxicity arc that makes discharge-on-appearance the classic fatal error, explained to her as the reason for the stay, because honesty keeps her cooperative); the psychiatric duty once medically safe - the full self-harm assessment (the argument-as-precipitant-function analysis, the previous-episodes and intent calibration and the means review including the boyfriend's medicines, and the relationship-and-housing-and-alcohol context worked through), the consent-for-collateral conversation (the person she called and the person she didn't), and the follow-up-and-means-plan with the paracetamol-access rule written into it - [[clinical-skills/communication]]; and the legal-and-ethics edge if she insists on leaving (the capacity assessment for this decision documented with the risk-information she was given, the nurse-and-doctor-escalation and the same-night-psychiatric-review offered, the 'you may leave but the treatment works and we will be here if you come back' position with the written warning signs and the safety-netting level of the 12-hour-and-24-hour bloods arranged or declined in the record, and the incident report and review that make the next late-presenter safer) - [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**

::: exercise Support the bereaved {level=2}
A GP practice and a school each face a suicide death in their community in the same week. Give the first-72-hours playbook for each and the shared mistakes to avoid.
::: solution
**Post-suicide-community-response ([[#def-prevention]], [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]]): the shared spine - tell-the-truth-carefully (the naming of suicide to those directly affected, since euphemism breeds rumour and the contagion-risk narrative fills silence), the risk-stratified outreach (the close-contacts-and-exposed-persons list with the bereaved-family-support and the school's-vulnerable-students-known-list prioritised, the follow-up contacts scheduled for the funeral and the first anniversary and the 'anniversaries' calendar maintained, and the means-and-media-hygiene advice to the family and institution including the social-media memorial management that platforms' policies now cover), and the staff-support-line (the adults-who-are-grieving-too-and-expected-to-hold-children briefings with the debriefs scheduled and the clinician-and-teacher own-risk screened) - [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]]; the practice version - the death-review-with-learnings done with the family's involvement where they wish, the re-attendance-and-record-flagging for the deceased's relatives (the elevated-risk-acknowledged not surveillance-stigmatised), the QOF-and-incident documentation and the coroner-and-prevention-review-referral pathways explained to the family so the process has a guide, and the practice-team reflective session with the 'what would we have asked' question held without self-flagellation (the self-blame-trajectory recognised as its own risk factor); and the school version - the trained-postvention protocol (the classroom-language scripts and the no-assembly-serialisation of grief and the counselling-support with the outside-agency coordination), the friendship-group-and-cluster monitoring (the copycat-risk literature's warning-sign list briefed to staff), and the note-to-parents written with the public-health guidance rather than improvised (the content-level specifics mattering as documented in the media-effect studies); and the three shared mistakes - the silence (the 'we won't mention it to protect them' error the bereaved uniformly reject), the sensational detail (the method-and-scene talk that spreads imagery and contagion), and the abandonment-after-week-two (the support-drop-off at exactly when grief and risk deepen - the anniversary-effect the calendars exist to catch) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**

::: exercise Write the prevention strategy {level=3}
Your region's suicide rate has plateaued after a decade of decline, with rising male deaths 35-54 and a cluster in one industrial town. Draft the multi-agency strategy: evidence components, the hard trade-offs, governance and measurement.
::: solution
**Regional-strategy-draft ([[#def-prevention]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]]): the analysis first - the demographic-and-method breakdown (the male-labouring-age-lethal-method pattern named and its sites - heights and vehicles and town-specific access points), the service-contact studies (what proportion were in primary or mental-health or A&E or custody contact, and where the chain broke), and the structural drivers stated plainly (the redundancy-and-debt-and-family-breakdown-and-housing sequence visible in the coroners' files - because a strategy that treats only illness will miss the majority of this cohort); the evidence-component menu with the trade-offs costed - means work (the site-and-method restriction programmes with the local-authority-and-industry-partnership engineering, the firearm-and-tool-and-vehicle-access counselling campaigns through the trades-union-and-employer channels that reach the demographic, and the pharmacy-dispensing-limit enforcement), the gatekeeper-and-help-seeking layer (the the barbershop-and-pub-and-workplace mental-health programmes with their honest modest evidence graded and their cultural-fit design, the men's-groups-and-sports-club partnerships and the peer-support workers with lived experience as the credibility infrastructure), the clinical-chain fixes (the discharge-follow-up compliance as a board metric, the alcohol-and-pain-and-depression pathways integrated, and the digital-and-helpline-access continuity including the text-and-chat options whose uptake matches the cohort), and the economic-levers the health partners can only advocate (the debt-and-benefits-and-employment navigation and the housing-first capacity - stated as a cross-directorate ask with named owners, since health alone cannot fix a mortality curve whose causes are mostly not clinical); the governance - the joint-strategy with the local authority and police and coroner and education and employers and faith-and-licence venues, the lived-experience-and-bereaved representation with real vote-share, the confidentiality-safe data-sharing agreement and the rapid-review protocol for each death and the cluster-response plan; and the measurement - the mortality-and-self-harm trends by sex-and-age-and-method (the method-substitution question tracked), the time-from-death-to-family-support-contact and postvention-delivery metrics, the means-restriction-completion and site-work audits, the equity readout (who the strategy reaches and who it misses - the ethnic-minority and rural and incarcerated and LGBTQ+ differentials), and the honesty rule for publication (a strategy that cannot state its baselines and its failure-detection thresholds is advocacy, not prevention) - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
