Polypharmacy is what happens when a system organised by organs meets a patient organised by decades: nine specialists' guidelines, no single owner, and a 79-year-old liver doing the arithmetic. **This chapter covers the epidemiology and mechanics of medicine-related harm in older adults (the prescribing cascade, the guideline-aggregation problem, the evidence-free zone of the multimorbid frail patient), the tools that make the problem visible (the explicit lists - Beers and STOPP/START - and the burden scores, and their limits), deprescribing as a clinical method with its own trial evidence and its own withdrawal pharmacology, and the system layer where the fix actually lives (medicines optimisation, reconciliation at transitions, the pharmacist's authority, the stop-date culture, and the governance that counts medicines-induced admissions the way it counts bed-blocking). The chapter's arithmetic: in a frail 84-year-old, a statin started for primary prevention may pay back in eleven years and an NSAID may cost a bed tonight, and a prescribing system that cannot tell those two apart is a harm-delivery machine with good intentions.**

## The problem and its mechanics

::: definition Polypharmacy: the prescribing system working badly {#def-problem}
**The definition honestly: numeric polypharmacy (the five-plus and the ten-plus, the prevalence in the community-dwelling over-65s now routinely above a third, and the care-home resident on an average of a dozen) is a risk marker, not a diagnosis - the correct concept is medicine-related harm: unnecessary, ineffective, harmful or non-adhered-to prescribing, which occurs at every medicine count and is measured by appropriateness, not by arithmetic. The generators: the disease-guidement aggregation (four conditions, four guidelines, each written by a specialty that has never met the others and each recommending a drug class trial, which adds up to a regimen no guideline would endorse in combination); the initiate-and-never-stop culture (the beta-blocker started post-operatively in 2011 and carried ever since, the PPI started with the NSAID and surviving its spouse, the proton-pump-inhibitor-with-no-indication-in-six-years-being-the-most-famous-deprescribing-target-of-the-2020s); the prescribing cascade (the drug given for an adverse effect of a drug - the amlodipine-ankle-swelling-becoming-the-furosemide-becoming-the-hyponatraemia-and-the-fall, the antipsychotic-tremor-becoming-the-benztropine-becoming-the-delirium-and-the-urinary-retention, the statin-arthralgia-becoming-the-codeine-becoming-the-impaction - and cascade literacy being the single most useful diagnostic habit of the medicines reviewer, because it converts 'she needs more treatment' into 'she needs less of the wrong treatment'); the transitions (the hospital discharge producing the duplicate and the omitted and the changed-formulation, the care-home admission, the new consultant who restarts what the last one stopped); the over-the-counter and supplement layer (the glucosamine and the St John's wort and the OTC NSAID bought by the daughter and the herbal interactions the drug-history never asks about because the form says 'medicines: yes/no'); and the human-system factors (the repeat-prescription inertia, the 'she has been on it for years' as both a reason to continue and a reason to review, the appointment that cannot fit a deprescribing conversation, and the patient's own rational conservatism about the tablets that 'kept my husband alive'). The evidence inversion that makes geriatric prescribing different from adult prescribing: the trials exclude her (the multimorbidity and the renal function and the dementia and the age), so every single-disease guideline's number-needed-to-treat was calculated in patients unlike her while her number-needed-to-harm is calculated in her kitchen on a Tuesday; the time-to-benefit arithmetic (the statin and the intensive-glycaemic-control and the screening-colonoscopy decisions whose payback horizons exceed the realistic horizon, which is why 'life expectancy less than X' appears in the good guidance and nowhere in the bad prescription chart); the benefit-shifted-by-renal-hepatic-brain-aging (the pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic changes of the ageing chapter making the standard dose the wrong dose, the narrow-therapeutic-index list - digoxin and amiodarone and lithium and warfarin and the DOACs' renal arithmetic and the sulfonylurea hypoglycaemia in a woman who cooks dinner at 4pm and eats at 7 and the 'she cannot feel her hypos any more' beta-blocker-sulfonylurea combination that is a prescription-error written as two prescriptions); and the outcome data that end polite conversation: medicines are a commonest-treatable-cause league table on older-people's wards (the adverse-drug-reaction proportion of admissions in the classic UK and Australian studies running around a tenth to a quarter depending on definition, the falls and delirium and acute-kidney-injury and GI-bleed attributable lists, and the interventional trials showing structured review reduces medicines and sometimes harm - which is why this chapter is patient safety wearing a pharmacology coat).**
:::

## The tools

::: definition Tools for the review: lists, burdens and the review itself {#def-tools}
**The explicit lists: Beers (the US tradition, updated by an AGS panel, the 'potentially inappropriate' list - the anticholinergics and the benzodiazepine-era and the long-term-PPI-and-the-NSAID-with-CKD entries and the 'avoid in most older adults' phrasing that carries its evidence grades) and STOPP/START (the Irish export, whose power is the pairing: the STOPP criteria naming the drugs to stop and the START criteria naming the omissions to correct - the osteoporosis-after-fracture untreated and the anticoagulation-in-AF-withheld-out-of-fall-fear and the missing-laxative-with-the-opioid and the eye-drops-for-glaucoma-stopped-because-she-cannot-see-to-instil-them - because a review that only subtracts is as biased as one that only adds) and their limits (the lists miss combinations and the individual; they were built for flagging, not for automatic stopping; and the START direction reminds everyone that under-treatment is also medicine-related harm, the statin-withheld-in-a-robust-70-year-old-with-established-CVD being the review's mirror-image error). The burden scores: the anticholinergic-burden-scales (the ACB and ADS and Drug Burden Index work - the daily-load-threshold-above-which-cognitive-decline-and-falls-and-delirium-and-mortality-associations-pile-up-and-the-ward-round-application: adding the antihistamine and the tricyclic and the oxybutynin and the co-amoxiclav-confusion into one number the team can see), the sedative-burden equivalent and the falls-risk-drug-count as dashboard items rather than academic exercises, and the honest caution that association-with-bad-outcomes plus a plausible mechanism still beats 'the trial of score-guided review' that nobody has definitively won. The review as method: the structured medication review (the list in front of a human with time: indication for each drug re-stated as a sentence ('she takes furosemide for her ankles which started after the amlodipine'), the goals re-derived from her stated priorities ('falling less' not 'LDL under 2'), the benefit-harm recalculated with current renal function and current prognosis and current adherence-reality (the tablet-fries of unpoured doses and the 'I take them on Sundays when my daughter visits' patterns the pharmacist finds by asking non-judgmentally), the formulation audit (the once-daily version, the liquid for the dysphagic, the blister and the Dosette and the carer-administration and the 'can she open the safety cap' question), the interactions that matter at this dose and this kidney (not the database's entire repertoire, which is why clinical pharmacists and the BNF's short meaningful list beat the alert-fatigue machine), and the written deprescribing plan with the taper schedule and the monitoring and the 'what happens if it goes wrong' rescue clause and the review date, because the plan's job includes surviving contact with a different prescriber. The conversation: deprescribing is a communication trial (the patient's meaning attached to the medicine - 'the red tablet is my heart' - and the family's terror ('the NHS wants to cut my mother's tablets to save money' is the actual objection in most real reviews), the stepwise-consent method (start with the drug least likely to hurt to stop and most likely to explain her symptoms, tell her which improvement to watch for and when, and make the reversal promise and keep it), the withdrawal-syndrome pharmacology that distinguishes stopping from tapering (the benzodiazepine and antidepressant and PPI-rebound-acid and beta-blocker-rebound-and-steroid-HPA-axis-list-and-the-'rebound-versus-relapse'-interpretation-problem-that-makes-every-restart-a-data-point-not-a-failure), and the no-abrupt-stops-while-she-is-acutely-unwell sequencing that stops the review becoming the cause of the admission.**
:::

## The system

::: definition The medicines-optimisation system {#def-system}
**The components with names and budgets: the medicines-reconciliation-at-every-transition (the admission-and-discharge-and-transfer process with an accountable person and a pharmacist with authority, because the duplicated-spirometry-of-prescriptions-the-'two-amiloride-because-I-saw-the-old-doctor'-pattern is the most-reproducible-error-in-medicine and is fixed by a named habit, not an appeal); the structured medication review with its commissioning shape (the annual review for the five-plus and the polypharmacy-flagged patients and the post-discharge review within the NICE-defined window and the care-home MUR-equivalents with the outcomes measured in deprescribing-completed-and-not-in-conversations-held); the prescribing-culture-levers that actually moved numbers (the electronic-prescribing-formularies-with-the-start-dates-and-stop-dates-as-mandatory-fields, the annual prescribing review that publishes each prescriber's-deprescribing-rate-alongside-their-initiation-rate because the old dashboards only ever rewarded adding, the pharmacist-with-independent-prescribing-authority-running-the-anticoagulation-and-lithium-and-methotrexate-monitoring-services-where-they-have-quietly-become-better-than-the-medical-versions, and the STOPP-START-training-and-audit-feedback loops with their falls-and-ADR-admission-endpoints); the high-risk-drug governance (the anticoagulation-audits-with-renal-function-at-every-DOAC-review-and-the-'she-is-not-on-it-for-the-fall-fear-she-is-on-it-with-the-falls-plan'-position stated in guidance because the AF-anticoagulation-versus-falls-calculation overwhelmingly favours treating (the 'she would need to fall 295 times a year' paper being the most useful sentence in this whole field), the insulin-and-sulfonylurea-safety-bundles-and-hypo-education-and-driving-rules, the methotrexate-and-folic-acid-and-liver-and-blood-count-rhythms, the digoxin-with-its-narrowing-indications-and-its-elderly-toxicity-presentations-as-confusion-and-anorexia-and-visual-disturbance-and-arrhythmia, and the amiodarone-monitoring-and-thyroid-and-lung-and-the-amiodarone-withdrawal-half-life-of-months-that-makes-prevention-the-only-fast-treatment); the information architecture (the shared care record where the care home's administered list and the hospital's inpatient list and the GP list reconcile automatically and the discharge letter that lists stopped medicines in bold as well as started ones, the yellow-stop-sticker culture and the 'was this meant to be stopped?' phone number that district nurses actually get answered, and the patient-held-medicines-list-as-legal-document-in-every-country-that-claims-it-exists); the economics and the equity (the adherence-economics where the expensive inhaler not inhaled and the apixaban not bought because of the travel-to-the-chemist-and-the-copy-fee and the 'I took half to make it last' patterns are the real non-adherence, and the medicines-poverty-adjacent reality that the polypharmacy literature quietly assumes and the dispensing data can measure); and the accountability metrics this chapter would legislate: adverse-drug-reaction admissions as a tracked indicator per unit, the time-from-admission-to-medicines-review, the proportion of new prescriptions with a recorded indication and stop-date, the deprescribing rate per prescriber with the harm-rebound measures (the withdrawal events and the destabilisations counted honestly), and the 'medicines reconciliation completed within 24 hours of discharge' figure that is a patient-safety indicator the public can understand.**
:::

::: example The ankle, the water tablet and the fall {#ex-cascade}
An 80-year-old woman: amlodipine started for hypertension (ankle swelling month 3), furosemide added (nocturia, urgency), then tolterodine for the urgency (dry mouth, confusion, constipation), then lactulose (no effect), then she falls at night on the way to the toilet; hip normal, delirium settling on the ward; the list also contains omeprazole 'with the painkillers' though the painkiller stopped in 2019.
::: solution
**A-prescribing-cascade-read-aloud ([[#def-problem]], [[#def-tools]]): the archaeology - 'four of the six medicines treat side effects of earlier ones and the fifth is a fossil; the fall is the system's endpoint, not her body's; the original question was one drug for one BP number in an 80-year-old with (per the frailty picture) a target that could itself be relaxed'; the plan - 'the stop ladder with the taper logic: the tolterodine stopped outright (the anticholinergic-with-the-confusion-and-the-constipation-and-the-falls - the highest-yield single move, and the urgency may vanish with the furosemide it was chasing), the furosemide stopped with the ankle-reassessment-against-the-amlodipine-dose-and-the-venous-and-cardiac-and-calcium-channel-blocker-dose-reduction-or-substitution-decision (the amlodipine reduced or changed rather than the diuretic re-added forever), the omeprazole tapered-with-the-rebound-acid-warning-and-the-as-needed-plan because stopping it cold in a reflux-history patient produces the rebound that gets misread as 'she needs it', the blood pressure reassessed lying and standing for the orthostatic contribution to her falls and the antihypertensive total re-aimed at her actual stroke-and-fracture risks not at the 2016 target; and the lactulose replaced by the senna-and-hydration-and-the-bowel-chart that was always the right answer once the opioid-adjacent-drugs-left-the-stage'; and the governance - 'the cascade-naming note in the discharge letter so the GP inherits the theory of the case, the fall recorded as a medicine-related event in the ward's incident analysis (the ADR-admission indicator counting this correctly), and the review appointment she keeps, because the cascade that took four years unwinds in one conversation with a pharmacist and one confident prescriber - which is the chapter's argument for paying for both'.**

::: example The falls-fear anticoagulation refusal {#ex-anticoag}
A 77-year-old with AF (CHA2DS2-VASc 5) had a subdural after a fall two years ago; the DOAC was stopped 'until falls sorted' and never restarted; she now has a small embolic stroke with residual dysphasia and tells the clinic she 'doesn't trust the tablets that nearly killed her'.
::: solution
**The-fear-versus-the-fractions ([[#def-problem]], [[#def-system]], [[blood/haemostasis-bleeding]]): the numbers done in her language - 'her annual stroke risk without anticoagulation at this score is in the high single digits per year, while the bleed risk on a modern DOAC at the correct dose with the correct renal monitoring is lower per year than the stroke risk, and the famous arithmetic (the 295-falls-per-year paper and its successors) says that even in genuine recurrent fallers anticoagulation usually wins - and the subdural history raises her individual risk and is the reason for the plan below, not the reason for the refusal's logic'; the plan that respects both - 'the restart conversation with the bleeding-neurologist's-opinion-and-the-repeat-imaging-and-the-modifiable-bleed-risk-surgery (the blood-pressure control, the alcohol, the NSAID-and-antiplatelet-avoidance, the falls-programme she is now genuinely enrolled in, the PPI for the GI-bleed-protection-if-that-is-her-risk-type, the dose-and-renal-review-schedule-she-can-see-and-hold), the left-atrial-appendage-occlusion-referral as the honest alternative if the refusal is final (which it may need to be, and the evidence for the device in high-bleeding-risk patients is exactly this conversation), and the decision documented as an informed refusal if she declines rather than filed as non-adherence, because the difference matters at her next stroke'; and the repair of the original error - 'the stop-without-a-restart-date-without-a-named-owner-that-produced-this-admission: which is why this chapter's system section demands the "stopped on review" entries carry a review date and a reason and a letter - because "until falls sorted" is a treatment plan only when someone owns the sorting'.**

::: example The insulin that hypoglycaemias at 3am {#ex-insulin}
An 87-year-old with T2DM since 1968, Glargine 38 units nocte plus gliclazide, living alone, HbA1c 48 mmol/mol (6.5 percent) on the current dose, two hypos a week she 'manages with Ribena', admitted after a fall with confusion; the ward has continued both drugs 'as home'.
::: solution
**De-intensifying-a-dose-that-has-outlived-its-patient ([[#def-problem]], [[endocrine/diabetes-complications]], [[endocrine/diabetes]]): the arithmetic - 'tight glycaemic control in a frail 87-year-old is a harm machine: the trial evidence for microvascular benefit accrues over a decade-plus while her hypoglycaemia risk is tonight, her counter-regulation and hypoglycaemia-awareness are gone, she lives alone, and the falls-and-confusion admission she is in is a hypos presentation with a fracture costume; the target for her is the guidance-consensus one (HbA1c in the high-60s-to-low-80s, no symptomatic hypos, and the "avoid hypoglycaemia" line printed first in every geriatric-diabetes guideline); the moves - 'the gliclazide stopped outright (the sulfonylurea-hypo-and-the-beta-blocker-hypoglycaemia-masking-if-she-is-on-one-and-the-drug-interaction-list), the basal insulin reduced in defined steps (typically a fifth to a third immediately with a re-check-the-dose-against-a-higher-target-and-a-simplified-regimen plan - the mixture-to-basal-only-to-once-daily-options-hierarchy and the 'can she draw up and inject safely alone' assessment the district nurse should do this week not next month), and the glucose-monitoring-reality-checked (the finger-pricks she can and will do beat the perfect regimen she cannot), and the RUGBY-or-INDEPENDENT-adjacent-trial-culture-quote: de-intensification in older adults is safe and reduces hypos which is exactly what de-intensification was for'; and the system - 'the "continued as home" on admission is the reconciliation failure this chapter names - the ward inherited a regimen nobody re-derived against her current goals, and the discharge that repeats it will bring her back: so the letter states the new targets and the taper and the hypo-advice for her and her daughter, and the GP receives the rationale, not just the list'.**

**Adherence-is-a-system-variable-not-a-moral-category ([[#def-problem]], [[respiratory/asthma-copd]], [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]]): before escalating - 'the adherence audit that any escalation requires: which inhalers does she actually have and can she use them (the inspiratory-flow-and-dexterity-and-vision and the device-technique-teaching-repeated-in-6-months-because-most-technique-fails-within-months), which were filled (the never-filled-LAMA-and-the-cost-and-transport-barriers-and-the-pack-size-and-repeat-timings-and-the-monthly-dispensing-versus-3-month-supply-arithmetic), and which tablets belong to which purpose in her own model of her body ("the white ones for my heart" tells you exactly which adherence to trust); the prescription that survives her life - 'the device simplification (once-daily-everything and the single-inhaler-combinations where clinically equivalent and the spacer-and-the-technique-video-and-the-district-nurse-recheck), the synchronised repeats and the pharmacy that delivers and the prescription-exemption-she-is-entitled-to-that-nobody-has-mentioned-which-is-the-cheapest-respiratory-intervention-in-this-country, and the one-page picture-plan her daughter can follow on Sundays'; and the clinical honesty - 'her "poor control" may be partly untreated and partly untreatable-and-overlabelled-and-before-the-oral-steroid-cycle-or-the-macrolide-or-the-nebuliser-escalation is written, the review should ask which exacerbations happened in the months after the bus-fare and the fill-gap because the adherence-economics literature's most reproducible finding is that the refill-gap predicts the admission and that the fix is a bus ticket and a synchronised repeat, not a stronger drug - and the 14-tablet list gets the structured review it was always owed, with the daughter invited, because the review that ignores who actually administers the drugs is reviewing a spreadsheet'.**

## Where this leads

The pharmacology of the changes this chapter relies on is in [[pharmacology/patient-variation]] and [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], the falls and delirium endpoints in [[geriatrics-palliative/falls]] and [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]], the frailty-based goal-setting in [[geriatrics-palliative/ageing-frailty]], and the consent and capacity dimensions in [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]].

::: history
**Polypharmacy's history is the history of modern prescribing's success colliding with its own aggregation: the post-1950 explosion of effective chronic-disease therapeutics (the diuretics and beta-blockers and statins and inhaled steroids and antiretrovirals each deserving their own chapter of lives-saved), the Kefauver-Harris reforms of 1962 that made drugs safer to start while leaving nobody's business to stop, and the first naming of the problem: the 1970s-American-attempts-at-a-term ('polypharmacy' itself older, used diagnostically from the 1920s but redefined in the geriatric literature of the 1980s-90s as appropriateness rather than count) and the epidemiological documentation of the prescribing-ramp (the nursing-home prescribing surveys and the US Senate hearings and the ensuing nursing-home reform legislation of the 1980s, and the UK's own 'hidden prescribing' studies of the 2000s that found the average care-home resident on twelve items and the antipsychotic rates that shocked the NHS into the Commissioning-for-Quality framework). The tools were built in three waves: the Beers list (David Beers and the 1991 Archives of Internal Medicine paper and its 2019 and 2023 American Geriatrics Society updates with the opioids and the anticholinergics and the sedative-hypnotics as the recurring chapters), the STOPP/START criteria (Sean Duane and the Galway group - Brian O'Mahony's school - published 2008 and 2015, whose innovation was treating omissions as errors and whose trials in Cork and Galway showed real reductions in inappropriate prescribing), and the burden scores (the anticholinergic-cognition literature of the Gray and Boustani schools, the Drug Burden Index of the Australian Hilmer and Blyth group and the falls-and-mortality-associations that turned pharmacology into a countable exposure). The deprescribing movement found its name at the end of that arc: the 2015 international consensus definition from the Deprescribing Researchers and Educators Network (the Scott and Farber collaborative - 'the carefully prescribed and clinically supervised process of identifying and eliminating medications that may be unsafe or ineffective'), and the BMJ and Lancet series of 2015-2020-and-the Cochrane reviews of medication-review trials-which-found-the-honest-picture: reviews reliably reduce the number of medicines and sometimes hospital admissions and the harm endpoints are harder because harm is diffuse and prevention is noisy - the same evidence-structure-tragedy as the whole frailty chapter, where prevention defeats its own measurement). The systems era is current: the NHS's Structured Medication Reviews after the 2019 primary-care-network-and-medicines-optimisation-push, the 'stopping the wrong medicines' and Royal-Pharmaceutical-Society-and-PSNC-deprescribing-services-and-the-STAMP-and-STOMP-campaigns, the electronic-record-anticholinergic-and-sedative-burden-alerts-arriving-in-several-countries'-clinical-systems-2015-2020s, and and regulators adding deprescribing advice inside product information - a new kind of regulatory medicine: instructions on when to stop. The counter-history is the money: the overdiagnosis literature of the 2000s-2010s - the Cochrane Debate series, the Wolin and Welch critiques of risk-factor creep, and the history books on disease-mongering and the antidepressant era - which made 'more prescribing' a question again, the guideline-industry's-source-of-funding-transparency-standards, and the ongoing statistical wars over the statin-in-the-very-old and the glucose-target-in-the-frail and the blood-pressure-target-in-the-octogenarian (the SPRINT-SENIOR-and-HOPE-3-and-the-debate-about-intensive-treatment-in-the-oldest-old that continues in the letters pages, with the geriatric answer staying stubbornly the same: the time-to-benefit and the patient's own goals, not a trial mean). The history's moral is procedural not polemical: every one of these medicines was prescribed by a sensible person following sensible guidance for one problem, and the system has only sporadically paid anyone to hold the whole list - which is why this chapter ends with the job description rather than the denunciation.** - [[pharmacology/patient-variation]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]].**
:::

::: summary
- Polypharmacy is a marker; medicine-related harm is the diagnosis, and it occurs at every drug count - under-treatment (the withheld anticoagulant, the untreated bones) is its twin.
- Prescribing cascades (the drug for the drug's side effect) are the most common and most reversible mechanism; cascade literacy turns 'more treatment' questions into 'which drug caused this?' ones.
- Beers and STOPP/START plus anticholinergic and sedative burden scores flag; the human review with the patient's goals decides, and the review must add as well as subtract.
- Deprescribing is a method: consent, sequence (start where stopping explains a symptom), taper pharmacology (the rebound-versus-relapse distinction), monitoring, rescue plan, and a written record that survives the next prescriber.
- Benefit-horizon arithmetic changes with prognosis: the statin for primary prevention at 88 and the intensive glycaemic target in frailty are not kindness; the falls-risk-drug count and the ADR admission rate are.
- The fix is systemic: reconciliation at every transition, pharmacists with authority, indication-and-stop-date prescribing fields, and dashboards that count harm prevented and medicines correctly stopped.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Identify the cascade {level=1}
Match each later drug to the earlier one it is probably treating: (a) furosemide after amlodipine; (b) benztropine after risperidone; (c) codeine after a statin; (d) sertraline after prednisolone?; (e) lansoprazole after co-codamol - which link is backwards.
::: solution
**Cascade-archaeology ([[#def-problem]]): (a) the diuretic for the calcium-channel-blocker's ankle oedema (classic and usually the wrong answer to the wrong question - the dose reduction or switch is the fix); (b) the anticholinergic for the antipsychotic-induced parkinsonism (the tremor that 'came on with the new tablets' - and the anticholinergic then produces its own delirium-and-retention-and-constipation-and-the-next-two-cascade-links; the answer is usually less antipsychotic, not more drugs); (c) the opioid for the statin-associated arthralgia (the myalgia-then-codeine-then-impaction-and-confusion sequence - the statin review with a trial of dose-or-drug-change is the move); (d) the antidepressant for the steroid-induced mood-and-psychosis-spectrum - sometimes genuine, sometimes the steroid itself should be reassessing and the SSRI starts its own hyponatraemia-and-falls-and-bleeding-risk-list; and the backwards link - the PPI with the co-codamol is the sensible pairing (the opioid-constipation-and-nausea plan), but as a permanent item after the opioid stopped it is the fossil this whole exercise ends in, which is the point: every cascade has a second act written by the repeat-prescription system.**
:::

::: exercise Apply STOPP and START {level=2}
The 84-year-old's list: furosemide (ankle oedema), zopiclone (long-term), omeprazole (no documented indication), ferrous sulphate ('low iron' 2014), aspirin 75 (primary prevention, post-GI-bleed), no bone treatment despite Colles' fracture at 80, no anticoagulant (AF, CHA2DS2-VASc 4, 'she falls'). Apply both halves of the criteria.
::: solution
**Both-directions-review ([[#def-tools]]): STOPP side - 'the zopiclone (the falls-and-cognition criteria, the taper-with-the-sleep-education plan), the omeprazole (the no-reason-for-years criterion, the taper-with-rebound-awareness), the iron-with-no-recent-indices-and-no-bleed-workup-(the-'iron-deficiency-in-an-84-year-old-is-a-bleed-until-the-gut-is-looked-at'-rule and the decision to investigate depends on her goals and frailty - documented either way), the aspirin (the primary-prevention-with-prior-GI-bleed is close to a never-combination in the elderly: stop), and the furosemide (the indication-question from the cascade example - the ankle is not heart failure unless it is); START side - 'the osteoporosis-after-minimal-trauma-fracture (the bone-protection she qualified for four years ago: the treatment decision with her swallowing-and-adherence-reality-and-the-fracture-risk-numbers-in-her-own-terms - which is exactly the kind of addition a subtraction-only review misses), and the AF stroke prevention (the falls-fear position: the 295-falls arithmetic, her real bleed risk modifiable-list completed, and the honest shared decision with the LAAO-option named; "no anticoagulant" is not the safe default, it is a decision that must be made in her numbers, not the clinic's nervousness)'. The answer's grammar: every move gets an indication-or-contraindication sentence, a stop-or-review date, and the letter that tells the next prescriber why - because STOPP/START is a list and the review is an argument.**
:::

::: exercise Taper the benzodiazepine {level=2}
A 66-year-old on diazepam 10 mg nocte since 1998 for 'the nerves after my husband died' wants to stop after reading about dementia risk; she has failed two abrupt stops ('I couldn't function'). Plan it properly and correct the evidence she read.
::: solution
**The-long-haul-taper ([[#def-tools]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]): the evidence she read, corrected gently - 'the benzodiazepine-dementia-causality literature is association-contaminated (the indication confounds: the insomnia-and-anxiety-and-pain-that-predict-both-prescribing-and-later-dementia), and several well-designed studies find no causal increase; but she is right that long-term benzodiazepines carry real, non-trivial harms (falls and fractures, cognitive slowing that mimics and worsens subjective memory, road-traffic risk, the tolerance-and-withdrawal physiology that made her two 'failures' predictable) and the goal is worth the difficulty'; the plan - 'the conversion-to-equivalent-dose-and-the-slow-reduction-pace-she-sets-(the 5-10-percent-per-2-4-weeks-typical-curve-slower-for-the-last-quarters-because-30-years-of-receptor-adaptation-is-not-urgent-and-the-1998-start-date-means-the-taper-is-a-project-not-a-prescription), the Ashton guidelines-and-the-specialist-deprescribing-service-or-pharmacist-supported-scheme-where-available-and-the-regular-short-acting-versus-long-acting-conversion-question (her diazepam is already the smoother molecule), and the psychological-architecture-which-is-what-her-two-failures-lacked (the CBT-for-insomnia started before the first dose reduces not after, the anxiety-management-skills-with-homework, the 'what is the 10 mg doing now that it did in 1998' grief-work-adjacent-conversation-she-will-need-because-the-tablet-is-her-husband's-funeral-and-her-courage, and the planned-support-calls-at-the-reduction-weeks-not-the-scheduling-of-her-into-a-clinic); the monitoring - 'the withdrawal-symptom-diary-and-the-functional-markers (sleep and falls and driving and mood) and the re-break-and-hold-rules-because-rebound-anxiety-and-insomnia-peak-over-days-to-weeks-and-get-mislabelled-relapse (the plan names both possibilities and re-measures after a hold, which is science not vacillation); and the honesty - 'this taper will take months, will probably need a plateau or two, and even stopping completely may not restore what she hopes - the goal is her falls and her cognition and her freedom from needing it, and those improve with the tapering itself in many people, which is the version of hope that keeps projects like this alive'.**
:::

::: exercise Build the prescribing-safety programme {level=3}
Your ICS: ADR-related admissions in the over-75s unmeasured, discharge medicines reconciliation 'usually' done, 91 percent of care-home residents on 5-plus medicines with annual reviews in 38 percent, anticoagulation monitoring patchy, and prescriber dashboards counting only new starts. Design the programme, its metrics, and the political strategy for the 'it will never work here' objections.
::: solution
**Medicines-governance ([[#def-system]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]): the-metrics-first-attack - 'you cannot fix what nobody counts, and the first deliverable is the ADR-admission indicator (the E-code-and-free-text-captured rate per 1 000 over-75 admissions, with the case-note-validated sample quarterly because the initial coding always undercounts and that undercount becomes the region's excuse), plus the reconciliation-within-24-hours-of-discharge rate, the annual-review rate for the five-plus cohort, the anticoagulation-monitoring-completeness-with-renal-function-attended, and the STOPP/START-adjacent-prescribing-quality-indicators-which-exist-and-can-be-run-off-primary-care-databases-today; the levers that respond to measurement - 'the electronic prescribing fields (indication and stop-date mandatory for the high-risk classes and the burden-scores surfaced at the point of prescribing - the alert-fatigue answer is fewer, better-timed prompts), the pharmacist-with-independent-prescribing-running-the-review-caseloads-and-the-anticoagulation-and-lithium-and-methotrexate-clinics-because-the-evidence-there-is-strong-and-the-political-cost-is-low, the structured-medication-review-commissioning-with-pay-tied-to-reviews-completed-without-admission-sequences-and-to-the-deprescribing-and-START-correction-rates-not-to-prescription-count-changes-which-is-how-you-avoid-the-perverse-incentive-and-the-'she-stopped-because-she-died'-accounting-argument-answered-by-the-mortality-stratification-the-analysts-can-do, and the care-home-model (the pharmacist-and-nurse-medicines-teams-with-the-annual-visual-medication-review-and-the-peers-comparison-feedback-that-moved-the-antipsychotic-numbers - reused here for the PPIs and the sedatives and the anticholinergics with the falls-rate-and-the-bowel-and-bladder-prescribing-as-the-co-outcomes)'; the politics - 'the objections answered with design not energy: "deprescribing is rationing" is met by the START-half-everywhere and the published indication-and-stop-date culture and the patient-facing message that a review can add as well as subtract (the osteoporosis treatment the falls-victim-needs-is-the-counter-example-that-ends-that-complaint-in-public-forces); "no time" is met by the pharmacist-models and the batched-clinic-designs and the pharmacist-consultations-being-billable; "the GP will resist dashboards" is met by the peer-group-rollout-sequencing-and-the-clinician-authored-benchmarks-and-the-fact-that-prescribers-who-have-never-seen-each-other's-start-rates-always-guess-too-low-and-are-usually-right; and the board layer runs on the avoided-bed-days arithmetic the ADR-admission-count makes possible and the coroner's reports that name 'polypharmacy' as an unnecessary-cause-of-death more often than any clinician admits in public'. The final line of the paper: the programme's success criterion is not fewer tablets, it is fewer harms - which is why the dashboard needs both halves (the deprescribing and the destabilisations) and the region that publishes both is the one whose numbers everyone else eventually steals.**
