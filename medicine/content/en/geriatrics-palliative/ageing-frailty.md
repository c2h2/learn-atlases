Ageing is not a disease, but its physics is unforgiving: homeostatic reserves narrow, the same insult produces a different clinical picture, and small stresses cascade. **This chapter covers the biology of ageing (the hallmarks, the loss of physiological reserve, and why the 75-year-old with a UTI presents as a fall and a confused episode rather than dysuria), frailty as a measurable clinical state (phenotype and deficit-accumulation models, screening and its honest uses and misuses), sarcopenia and malnutrition and the immobility spiral, the comprehensive geriatric assessment as geriatric medicine's distinctive technology, and the system that either supports or dismantles fragile people: hospital-at-night culture, the care home as a patient, transfers of care, rehabilitation, and planning for the end while still treating the middle. The organising sentence is the geriatrician's: 'treat the person in the bed, not the bed in the person' - which sounds soft until you watch a well-run geriatric ward prevent the delirium, the fall, the pressure ulcer, the inappropriate feeding tube and the funeral that a scattered, organ-siloed admission produces.**

## The biology and the measurement

::: definition The biology of ageing and physiological reserve {#def-biology}
**The explanatory frameworks: the hallmarks-of-ageing (genomic-instability-and-telomere-attrition-and-epigenetic-drift-and-loss-of-proteostasis-and-mitochondrial-dysfunction-and-cellular-senescence-with-the-senescent-cell-secretory-phlogistic-inflammaging-background-and-dysfunctional-nutrient-sensing-and-stem-cell-exhaustion-and-altered-intercellular-communication) which are laboratory biology's real attempt to describe why the odds of everything clinical rise exponentially with decades, and their honest translation: the hallmarks explain the organism-level fact of reduced reserve, and reduced reserve is what you actually treat - the same bacterium, drug, hip or fast produces delirium where the 30-year-old produces fever, and the geriatric syndromes (falls, delirium, incontinence, pressure injury, frailty) are the clinical signature of multi-system-margin-loss rather than single-organ disease. The physiology of the margins: cardiovascular (the stiff ventricle and blunted baroreflex and conduction-system fibrosis that make tachycardia-and-hypotension-and-beta-blockade-and-volume-shifts-all-less-forgiving), renal (the GFR decline that hides in a normal creatinine because the muscle mass that makes creatinine is also declining - the cystatin-C-and-cockcroft-gault-versus-CKD-EPI-prescribing-implications-which-is-why-drug-dosing-in-the-old-is-renal-arithmetic-with-the-kidneys-of-a-person-30-years-younger-absent), thermoregulatory (the hypothermia-at-presentation-in-sepsis and the falls in unheated homes), immunological (immunosenescence-with-the-infection-presentations-and-vaccine-responses-and-the-Lynchadjacent-shingles-reactivation-logic), musculoskeletal (the 1-2-percent-bone-and-1-percent-muscle-loss-per-year-with-the-sarcopenia-obesity-and-vitamin-D-and-protein-intake-questions), and sensory-cognitive (the vision-and-hearing-loss-that-gets-mislabelled-as-confusion-or-low-mood-and-is-in-fact-amenable-contributors-to-isolation-and-delirium-risk-with-glasses-and-hearing-aids-as-geriatric-medicine-not-charity). The endocrine-metabolic margins: the thyroxine-dose-per-kg-and-digoxin-and-lithium-and-benzodiazepine-sensitivity-which-is-pharmacokinetic-and-pharmacodynamic-aging-in-one-prescription-list-sentence-covered-properly-in-the-polypharmacy-chapter. The biology's clinical yield is modest at the bedside (the senolytics and rapamycin and metformin-of-the-TAME-and-DOGMA-adjacent-longevity-trials-are-not-treatment-for-your-patient-who-is-81-and-falling) but enormous in framing: ageing is when the system has no spare parts, so prevention and gentle handling are the therapy, and the question 'what does she live at and who is she with and what did she do before this week' is physiology as much as the bloods, because a care home resident and an affluent amateur-sailor of the same age and the same hip fracture are two different patients in two different trajectories - which is exactly why chronological age predicts worse than function and why every assessment instrument in this chapter measures function, not candles - [[physiology/homeostasis-body-fluids]], [[immunology/innate-immunity]], [[renal/filtration]].**
:::

::: definition Frailty: syndromes, instruments and uses {#def-frailty}
**Two workable models and one warning. The phenotype (Fried's five: unintentional weight loss, self-reported exhaustion, low activity, slow gait speed, low grip strength - three-plus is frail, one-or-two pre-frail) and the deficit-accumulation index (Rockwood's frailty index: count the symptoms, signs, disabilities and abnormal labs against what could be counted; the ratio itself is the frailty, and the striking property is its near-linear-mortality-and-disability-relationship and the theoretical upper limit of incompatibility-with-life-around-0.67-deficits). The clinical instruments you will actually meet: the electronic Frailty Scale (the Rockwood 1-9 of very-fit-through-to-terminally-ill with its memorable pictures and its legitimate triage-research uses), the PRISMA-7 and G8 and the two-question-screen ('do you become exhausted quickly and do you consider that you need help?' and the clinical-frailty-scale-by-inspection-in-a-spare-second-of-the-consultation-which-good-clinicians-do-unconsciously), and the honest criticisms: the tools are not personality tests, they measure disability-and-dependence as much as biology so they can smuggle ageism and classism and gender-norms into 'objective' scores, and the screening-programme-era-outrage-is-the-lesson (the-English-population-frailty-screening-pilot-with-letters-to-people-of-80-plus-which-was-cancelled-after-a-public-and-academic-campaign-about-the-experience-of-being-posted-a-number-without-a-plan - because a score without an intervention is a label and a label without consent is harm). The legitimate uses: predicting who needs the geriatrician around the operation or the admission (the surgical-frailty-project-data and the prehabilitation-and-goals-of-care-before-the-urgent-hip-not-after-the-ICU-day-9), who needs the falls-and-rehabilitation-and-medicines-review-services (the-frailty-as-a-referral-not-a-fate), and who wants the serious conversation now while they can still choose (the advance-care-planning-and-research-participation-and-place-of-living decisions made from the fit-end-of-the-scale are worth more than the same conversations at the sick end). The measurement truth in one sentence: frailty is best used as a gateway to comprehensive assessment and support, never as a reason to withhold treatment - the age-that-does-not-equal-frailty-and-frailty-that-does-not-equal-futility rule which every guideline states and every late-night-acute-assessment-consultation-still-tests.**
:::

## The geriatric syndromes and the assessment

::: definition Sarcopenia, malnutrition and the immobility spiral {#def-sarcopenia}
**The muscle matters: sarcopenia (the AWGS-2 and EWGSOP2-critieria-era definition: low muscle strength first as the trigger - grip strength by dynamometry - then confirmed with low muscle quantity or quality, with severe sarcopenia adding low performance) is the physiological engine of the frailty presentation and has three treatable drivers: inactivity and bedrest (the 10-percent-quadriceps-loss-per-week-of-a hospitalized older adult's immobilisation-is-the-fastest-decline-any-of-us-will-ever-experience and the reason 'move her, on day one, with a name on the plan' is geriatric medicine's first command), undernutrition (the protein requirement that rises with illness-ageing-and-inflammation-to-1-2 g/kg-with-the-dysphagia-and-dentition-and-depression-and-alone-and-on-medication-changes-that-quietly-cut-intake-and-the-MUST-screening-on-admission-and-the-dietitian-who-can-do-more-with-a-food-fortification-advice-sheet-than-a-sip-feed-prescription), and the deficiency-and-endocrine-background (vitamin-D's-modest-but-real-falls-and-fracture-numbers-versus-the-disappointing-high-dose-bolus-trials-which-is-the-daily-not-mega-dose-lesson and the thyroid-and-testosterone-and-steroid-myopathy-and-inflammatory-myositis-differential-of-the-wasting-patient-not-just-'old'). The spiral to prevent: the fall → the fracture → the admission → the delirium → the bedrest → the deconditioning → the 'failure to thrive' → the care home → the decline; every arrow in that chain has an evidence-based circuit-breaker (the hip-fracture-pathway-and-the orthogeriatric joint service-and-the-early-mobilisation-and-the-delirium-bundle-and-the-NMES-and-the-bone-protection-because-a-third-of-people-who-fall-break-a-bone-and-the-fracture-liaison-services-still-miss-the-first-80-percent) and the chapter's job is to know which arrows you are cutting in which patient. The swallowing-and-nutrition-edge-cases: the aspiration-pneumonia-and-the-dysphagia-therapy-assessment-and-the-textures-and-thin-fluids-vs-IDDSI-era-rethinking (the 'thick fluid makes pneumonia worse because nobody drinks it' literature and the mouth-care-as-aspiration-prevention-with-teeth-being-protective-being-under-prescribed), the feeding-tube-in-dementia ethics-and-evidence (the RCTs-showing-no-prevention-of-aspiration-or-death-or-pressure-ulcer-and-the-comfort-feeding-at-end-of-life-instead and the legal-and-family-process-around-withdrawing-or-withholding which belongs to the mental-capacity-law and palliative chapters but is stated here because the tube gets offered in a ward at 3am and the ward needs the evidence at its fingertips), and the unintentional-weight-loss-investigation-boundary-(screen-for-cancer-and-depression-and-thyroid-and-diabetes-and-dementia-and-dental-and-social-causes-while-accepting-that-the oldest-old wasting-is-often-multifactorial-and-the-plan-is-function-and-dignity-and-who-is-visiting not another CT).**
:::

::: definition Comprehensive geriatric assessment and the geriatric service {#def-cga}
**The CGA is the intervention: a multidimensional, interdisciplinary diagnostic process (medical-psycho-social-functional) culminating in a coordinated plan, delivered by a trained team on an assessment-and-rehabilitation-unit-with-admission-restrictions and proactive geriatric co-management - and its evidence is one of general medicine's strongest service facts: the Stuck and Ellis and Kalra and Forsyth trials and their Cochrane syntheses and the orthogeriatric and stroke-unit analogues show increased likelihood of alive-at-home-improved-function-reduced-long-stay-with-the-effect-size-coming-from-the-model-not-the-setting (the multidisciplinary-weekly-round-with-one-named-team-and-a-goal-per-day-and-the-discharge-planning-from-day-one-is-transferable-to-any-ward-which-is-why-age-friendly-health-systems-institutes-extract-the-CGA-into-the-moving-up-guidance-and-the-4Ms-framework: What-Matters, Medication, Mind, Mobility-asked-every-day-of-every-older-admission). The components the exam asks and the ward forgets: the medication review that includes the anticholinergic-and-sedative burden (see the polypharmacy chapter), the cognition screen with delirium-CAM-or-4AT-on-the-daily-obs-chart-not-on-request, the mood (the GDS-and-the-question-about-who-she-has-spoken-to-this-week-and-the-bereavement-history), the falls-with-the-orthostatic-blood-pressure-and-the-footwear-and-the-syncopal-versus-mechanical-versus-multifactorial-attribution-and-the-TU-and-10-meter-walk-and-grip-as-the-physical-exam-geriatrics-actually-uses, the continence (the 'which comes first, the urgency or the immobility' and the pad-versus-radar-sense), the nutrition (the weight trace on the wall of the clinic like blood pressure), the skin (the Waterlow-or-Braden-and-the-heap-foam-mattress-and-the-turning-and-the-nutrition-that-is-wound-care-in-disguise), the sensory (the glasses-clean and the hearing-aids-with-the-batteries-which-is-why-the-bedside-box-of-spare-aids-and-newspaper-sized-information exists), and the social (the carer's own health as a vital sign and the housing and the heating and the finances and the 'who decides when you cannot live alone anymore and how do we make that a choice not an ambush'). The service architecture: the admission-to-assessment-and-treatment-unit-versus-the-liaison-service-versus-the-ward-round-with-the-community-team-versus-the-hospital-at-home-and-the-frailty-elder-with-a-two-hour-ed-attenders-clinic-and-the-day-hospital-rehabilitation-and-the-intermediate-care-and-reablement-and-the-baths-adjacent-utmost-important-note-that-these-services-are-underfunded-and-patchy-because-geriatric-medicine-is-the-most-inequity-exposed-specialty-in-medicine (the healthy-activist-80-year-gets-seen-at-75-and-the-frail-lone-79-year-gets-falls-broken-hip-nursing-home-serial-admissions-and-the-nursing-home-with-a-variation-of-40-fold-in-antipsychotic-use-is-a-public-health-failure-happening-to-people-who-cannot-complain). The model's one weakness: the CGA's benefits require the system to keep the promises the assessment makes - the aid that never arrives and the community-OT referral into a queue and the 'she's not going back there' admission that was actually a social-care failure with a medical label, which is why this chapter ends with integration and not with the instrument.**

::: solution
**The-ward-is-now-the-disease ([[#def-cga]], [[#def-frailty]]): the review - 'this is iatrogenic geriatric syndrome assembly-line: the aspiration-and-hyponatraemia-and-hypoxia-and-hypoglycaemia-and-drug-causes-of-the-delirium-worked-up (the 'UTI' itself needing a midstream-sample-and-a-reason-not-a-urine-dipstick-bacterium-which-is-asymptomatic-bacteriuria-and-over-treated-tremendously-in-exactly-this-cohort) and the new antipsychotic in delirium without psychosis-according-to-guidance-and-the-anticholinergic-and-opioid-and-benzodiazepine-inventory-and-the-taper-decision', the catheter-in (the infection and the tether and the privacy loss), the bedrest (the deconditioning-math-from-the-sarcopenia-section-which-is-why-'mobility-is-treatment'-is-on-the-board), the breakfast (the 7am drug round at 11am with the undressed-tray-and-the-spectacles-off-and-the-hearing-aid-out: the ward's timetable is a deliriogenic exposure and the fix is not novel, it is named and scheduled: the glasses-on-8am and the food-when-it-is-food-and-the-lights-and-noise-and-orientation-and-the-family-who-are-part-of-the-team-if-you-give-them-the-ward-number). ::: example The 81-year-old 'whose creatinine is normal' {#ex-renal}
An 81-year-old woman, 48 kg, widowed, lives with a care worker three days a week; admitted with confusion after a fall; creatinine 88 umol/L 'normal'; started on standard-dose nitrofurantoin for a UTI and ibuprofen for hip pain by the covering team; two days later she is more confused and oliguric.
::: solution
**Kidneys, muscles and prescribing ([[#def-biology]], [[#def-cga]]): the physiology - 'a creatinine of 88 in a 48-kg 81-year-old is not normal renal function - it is the creatinine of a person with little muscle to make it, and the estimated GFR here is roughly 40-45, which makes nitrofurantoin both ineffective-for-the-urine-and-potentially-toxic (the UK guidance treats eGFR under 45 as a no-go and under 30 as dangerous) and ibuprofen a three-way collision of hypoperfused kidney, dehydration and the NSAID; the oliguria and the deepening confusion are the prescription list read back to you'; the immediate work - 'the drug-level audit (the benzodiazepine at home, the codeine, the anticholinergic antidepressant too - count the anticholinergic and sedative burden), the fluid and electrolyte reassessment with the bladder scan for retention and the faecal-impaction-examination, the infection reassessment (is the UTI real, is there another source, is the nitrofurantoin simply not treating a systemic infection), and the falls workup the confusion-dispersion hid'; and the repair - 'stop the ibuprofen, change the antibiotic with the renal arithmetic done properly (the dose-adjusted alternatives with the pharmacist), rehydrate to a monitored plan, and write the cystatin-C-or-calculated-creatinine-clearance into the record so the next covering doctor inherits the kidneys and not the number; and the discharge that does not repeat the admission (the GP letter naming the renal function in ml/min, the analgesia plan without NSAIDs with the paracetamol-and-topical-and-physio ladder, and the medicines-review-referral because this admission was a prescribing adverse event wearing a medical costume - which is reportable, learnable, and in the polypharmacy chapter's core territory'.**

::: exercise Calculate the renal prescribing {level=1}
Estimate the creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault) for the 81-year-old, 48 kg, creatinine 88 umol/L woman above, and state which two of her new prescriptions it changes and how.
::: solution
**The-arithmetic ([[#def-biology]]): convert 88 umol/L to approximately 0.99 mg/dL; CrCl = ((140-81) x 48 x 0.85)/(72 x 0.99) which is roughly 32-34 ml/min (or via the umol version: 1.23 x (140-81) x 48 x 0.85 / 88, same answer). Consequences: nitrofurantoin - at this clearance it will neither concentrate in the urine adequately nor clear safely from the plasma; the UK guidance says avoid below 45 ml/min (and it is contraindicated below 30); change to an agent that treats the actual infection site with the culture; and the ibuprofen - renal prostaglandins are the only thing keeping her perfused kidney perfused when she is unwell, so stop it and use the paracetamol-and-topical-and-regular-review ladder; and note the third - if she were on a renally-cleared opioid (morphine's metabolites) or a DOAC or digoxin or lithium, the same arithmetic drives the dose. The teaching point: 'the creatinine is normal' is a sentence that has killed frail people; the muscle made the number, the kidneys made the patient - and every prescribing decision in a frail older adult should be made against the estimated clearance, which the electronic record should have volunteered and the pharmacist should have seen as a second signature - [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]].**

::: exercise Argue the ethics of a screening label {level=3}

## Where this leads

Delirium and dementia care is its own chapter ([[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]]), medicines science in [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], the falls chapter completes the syndromic set ([[geriatrics-palliative/falls]]), and the ethical machinery is in [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]] and [[geriatrics-palliative/end-of-life-care]].

::: history
**Geriatric medicine was invented by necessity twice and named once: the Charcot-school and Ignatz-Nascher-and-Leopold-Auenbrugger-adjacent-precursors aside, the modern specialty begins with Ignatz Nascher's 1909 coining of 'geriatrics' and his insistence that the field was a clinical not a custodial one, and then the two world-historical moments - the 1930s work of Marjory Warren at the Middlesex Hospital who, from 1931 toured the 'chronic sick' wards of the London workhouse-infirmary system, found thousands of older patients who could have lived differently (the 'they were not old, they were neglected and contracted from sitting in beds for years' observation), and built the first geriatric assessment-and-rehabilitation service with a multidisciplinary team and early mobilisation and the family as the unit - which is the CGA's founding paper in practice. The war and the NHS then professionalised it: the generation of Isaacs, Brooke, Brocklehurst and Kane that built the university departments, the trialists who followed turned Marjory Warren's sense into evidence (the classic 'we were right and now we can prove it' moment of a service-based discipline), and the post-1948-NHS geriatric-medicine boards in Britain made it one of the world's most institutionalised specialties-while-its-us-equivalent-stayed-an-academic-minority-and-its-global-versions-followed-demography-not-plans. The concept history is instructive: the 'infirmary chronic sick' becoming 'the frail elderly' becoming 'frailty as a measurable syndrome' (Clegg and Young and Rockwood and the phenotype-versus-deficit-accumulation argument of 2001-2010, and the electronic Frailty Scale of the Canadian group), and the parallel social history that made the science urgent: the care-home-industry-exposure-and-the-Serious-Incident-Review-era-and-above-all the 2020-21 care-home pandemic mortality, the worst concentration of deaths outside conflict in British history and exposed the specialty's unfinished business about where the boundary of 'community' medicine sits when the community is a building with a manager. The pharmacology caught up late: the 1991-Beers-criteria-from-the-US-internal-medicine-generalists'-and-the-2015-2019-2023-AGS-updates-and-the-STOPP-START-criteria-of-O'Mahony's-Galway-group-from-Ireland-and-the-NICE-2013-and-2018-medicines-review-guidance-and-the-2020s-anticholinergic-burden-cascade-and-benzodiazepine-and-PPI-and-SSRI-deprescribing-trials-which-collectively-made-the-medicines-review-a-clinical-intervention-with-numbers-instead-of-a-chat. The current chapter is demographic and economic: the 2020s-super-aging-and-the-4-plus-million-UK-85-plus-projection-and-the-workforce-crunch-because-no-country-has-trained-enough-geriatricians-and-the-geriatrician-is-therefore-distributing-a-model-not-doing-a-caseload-and-the-integration-agenda (the Better Care Fund and the ICS and the nursing-home-CHC-and-the-Getting-It-Right-First-Time-for-older-people-and-the-hospital-at-home-and-virtual-ward evidence which in the 2020s became a proper trial literature). The history's tone is defensive and proud in equal measure: geriatric medicine spent decades being the specialty other specialties sent their problems to and has spent the last two proving that its methods - multidimensional assessment, function as an outcome, the team over the tumour-board model of one organ - are the preview of what all medicine will have to become for everyone, everywhere, soon. The Marjory Warren insight remains the newest thing in the field: many of the incurably dependent had been made so by the system that held them.' - [[physiology/integrative-physiology]], [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]].**
:::

::: summary
- Ageing narrows physiological margins; the geriatric syndromes (falls, delirium, incontinence, pressure injury) are multi-system margin-loss, so single-organ reasoning misreads them.
- Frailty is measurable (phenotype, deficit accumulation, eFI) and useful for prediction and referral - and harmful when used as a label without support or a licence to withhold treatment.
- Sarcopenia and malnutrition spiral fast; mobilisation, protein, and the preventable causes of reduced intake are treatment, and the bed itself is a clinical exposure.
- Comprehensive geriatric assessment on an A&E-restricted ward is one of medicine's best-evidenced service interventions - its elements are portable to every ward that admits older people.
- Decline is a diagnosis to investigate: weight loss and 'failure to thrive' deserve a medicines-and-depression-infection-nutrition-mouth audit before a narrative.
- Planning ahead (capacity, resuscitation, place of care) is part of active geriatric care, best done early, in the patient's own words, on paper the whole system can see.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Grade the frailty {level=1}
Using the clinical (electronic) Frailty Scale, classify: (a) robust and active 79-year-old; (b) slow, uses a frame, manages stairs with rest, no help with washing; (c) requires help with all personal care, continence impaired, mobile with assistance; (d) bed-bound and continuously sick. What does each grade change in a surgical admission?
::: solution
**The-grades-and-their-use ([[#def-frailty]]): (a) very fit-to-well (~1-2), (b) mildly frail (~4: 'vulnerable' - not dependent but slowed), (c) moderately to severely frail (~6-7), (d) severely frail-to-terminally-ill (~8-9). On a surgical admission the grades change the conversation and the peri-operative plan: fit patients get standard pathways; the mildly frail need the geriatric co-management and the goals talk before the emergency not during it, the falls-screen and the medicines review and the delirium-prevention-bundle as default not as a favour; the moderately-severely frail need 'what are the outcomes of this operation in people like me' answered in function-terms-with-the procedure-specific frailty data-for-the-specific-procedure-because-'survival' is the wrong currency at this grade and 'will I be in a nursing home afterwards' may be the decision-relevant risk; and at grade 8-9 the question becomes whether the operation is the right treatment at all versus optimal-conservative-management-with-clear-reassessment-triggers-which-is-not-withholding-care-it-is-matching-it. The honest caveat that belongs in every use of the scale: the grade describes the patient's current trajectory, not her worth or her ceiling - the eFI's own authors say the tool is for flagging people for assessment and support and prediction, not for deciding who deserves treatment - and a 9 is a person in front of you with a specific problem, which remains treatable on its own merits.**

::: exercise Defend the geriatric medicine case {level=3}
A treasury paper proposes closing 'low-value' geriatric assessment services and funding acute capacity instead, noting the specialty 'treats the dying slowly and the frail expensively'. Write the rebuttal on evidence, economics and equity, and concede what is true.
::: solution

the rebuttal's evidence - 'assessment-unit and orthogeriatric and stroke-unit admission-restriction and proactive co-management all have randomised or strong observational outcome data on death and institutionalisation and length of stay, and the economics follow - the delirium superimposed on dementia and the post-fall-deconditioning and the 'failure to thrive' readmissions are among the most expensive, lowest-satisfaction episodes in acute medicine, and the alternatives to geriatric co-management are not shorter stays, they are longer ones elsewhere with worse outcomes;
