The large bowel fails in three clinical voices: **the acute abdomen of the appendix and diverticulum, the chronic relapse of inflammatory bowel disease, and the functional noise of the irritable bowel.** Knowing which voice you are answering — and how to prove it — is the craft of this chapter; the same calprotectin, scope and CT separate all three.

This chapter covers appendicitis and diverticular disease, inflammatory bowel disease, coeliac disease and polyposis syndromes, and the functional bowel disorders including constipation and incontinence.


::: definition Appendicitis {#def-appendix}
**Pathophysiology (luminal obstruction — faecolith, lymphoid hyperplasia after infection, occasionally seed or tumour — then mucus distension, bacterial invasion, transmural inflammation; progression from visceral periumbilical pain (midgut afferents — [[anatomy/abdomen]]'s referred-pain dermatomes) to somatic right iliac fossa pain with peritoneal irritation; the caecal position variants (pelvic and retrocaecal and subhepatic — the atypical presentations that delay diagnosis, and pregnancy's upward displacement — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]]). Diagnosis: the history-and-examination pattern (migrating pain, anorexia, low-grade fever, tenderness-with-peritonism, rebound-and-cough signs — [[clinical-skills/physical-examination]]), the score systems (Alvarado and the AIR and pediatricAppendicitis scores — [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]]), the bloodwork (neutrophilia-and-CRP — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]) and imaging (graded compression ultrasound first in children and young adults — radiation discipline [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]] and [[public-health/environmental-occupational]]'s-radiation-burden; CT with its high accuracy for the adult equivocal case; MRI in pregnancy; the diagnostic-laparoscopy bonus of gynaecological diagnosis — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/menstrual-pelvic-disorders]]). The differentials that must not be missed: ectopic pregnancy and ovarian torsion and PID and gastroenteritis and mesenteric adenitis and Meckel's and Crohn's and caecal cancer and renal colic ([[renal/urinary-tract]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/early-pregnancy]], [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]]'s own Crohn's-ileitis-row). Management: appendicectomy (laparoscopic standard, wound-protective extraction — the SSI-and-port-site-metastasis-adjacent hygiene [[surgery/wounds-surgical-infection]] and [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]]) with perioperative antibiotic single-dose rules ([[microbiology/antimicrobials]]); the antibiotics-first trials (APPAC and CODA — non-operative management of uncomplicated appendicitis with a substantial recurrence-and-appendicolith-failure curve — [[#def-appendix]], [[epidemiology-ebm/randomised-trials]]) and shared-decision framing ([[clinical-skills/communication]]); the perforated case (resuscitation and broad-spectrum cover and source control; the phlegmon-or-abscess pathway of radiological drainage plus interval- Versus immediate appendicectomy debate [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]-and-[[surgery/acute-abdomen]]); the older adult (the caecal-cancer-masquerade rule: interval imaging or colonoscopy after non-operative management of right-sided inflammation — [[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]]); and the mucinous-neoplasm trap (do not morcellate a suspicious appendix; the pseudomyxoma-peritonei route — [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]]-and-[[pathology/cancer-diagnosis]]).**
:::

::: definition Diverticular disease {#def-diverticular}
**Pathophysiology (acquired pseudodiverticula — mucosal herniation at the vasa recta penetration points of the circular muscle — of the sigmoid in the Western world; the segmenting-motility-and-high-pressure-and-low-fibre-and-connective-tissue and constipating-drug hypotheses — [[gastrointestinal/digestion-absorption]]'s motility and [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]]'s opioid-calcium-clonidine list; the right-sided-and-colonic-and-Asian-pattern and the genetic-and-steroid-and-immunosuppression modifiers (the transplant population is over-represented — [[immunology/transplant-tumour-immunology]])). Presentation spectrum: asymptomatic diverticulosis (the incidental CT and colonoscopy finding whose counselling must avoid therapeutic-orthodoxy panic — fibre, and the myth-busting of nuts-seeds-popcorn); symptomatic uncomplicated disease (left-sided pain and bloating and disordered bowel — the IBS-overlap and the rifaximin-and-mesalazine-claims-with-negative-trials honesty — [[epidemiology-ebm/systematic-reviews]]); acute diverticulitis (pain-then-fever-and-CRP, the inverted-appendicitis of the left iliac fossa); and complicated disease (the Hinchey framework: pericolic abscess, pelvic or generalised purulent-or-faeculent peritonitis, fistula — colovesical with pneumaturia-and-faecaluria and the recurrent-UTI loop ([[renal/urinary-tract]]), colo-vaginal, colo-colo — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/menstrual-pelvic-disorders]]-adjacent; stricture with obstruction mimicking cancer — the resection-specimen rule ([[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]]); free perforation with faeculent peritonitis [[surgery/acute-abdomen]]); the diverticular bleed (the painless massive lower-GI haemorrhage — see [[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]]). Diagnosis: CT-with-contrast as the workhorse (the pericolic-stranding-and-abscess-and-free-air reading — [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]), the cancer-exclusion colonoscopy after an episode at 6-8 weeks (the interval-cancer-rate argument — [[public-health/screening]]), and the outpatient-versus-inpatient decision on severity-and-comorbidity-and-immunosuppression. Management: the uncomplicated-mild episode increasingly managed without antibiotics (observational evidence and the selective-antibiotics movement — [[infectious-diseases/stewardship-outbreaks]]-adjacent-stewardship of diverticulitis, and the clear-return-rules documentation [[clinical-skills/records-handover]]); the abscess pathway (antibiotics-and-percutaneous-drainage — [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]); the peritonitis pathways (laparoscopic lavage-for-purulent-peritonitis trial-disappointments, Hartmann's procedure versus primary anastomosis with diverting ileostomy in selected patients — the reversal-morbidity-and-never-reversed arithmetic [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]]; the damage-control logic in the frail immunosuppressed [[emergency-critical-care/shock-fluids]]-and-[[immunology/transplant-tumour-immunology]]); the elective-surgery decision (episode-number-is-not-the-indication — the shift from "count the attacks" to symptom-burden-and-complication-and-immunosuppression judgement — [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]'s stoma-and-quality-of-life counselling); and SCAD (segmental colitis associated with diverticulosis — the intersigmoid-mucosal-sparing inflammation that is neither IBD nor cancer but sits between — [[pathology/diagnostic-pathology]]).**
:::

::: definition Hereditary polyposis and cancer syndromes {#def-polyposis}
**FAP (adenomatous-polyposis coli — near-total penetrance from hundreds-thousands of colorectal adenomas under an APC germline hit with somatic second hits; [[genetics/cancer-genetics]]'s two-hit logic): the teenage-onset bleeding-and-diarrhoea, the extracolonic ledger (duodenal-and-ampullary adenomas as the modern mortality driver, gastric polyps, thyroid, desmoids and the post-colectomy mesenteric lesion, CHRPE and osteomas — the Gardner overlap; [[endocrine/thyroid]], [[musculoskeletal/bones-joints]]-no), the annual-or-biennial surveillance architecture from childhood (upper endoscopy by Spigelman staging and colonoscopy/sigmoidoscopy intervals — [[public-health/screening]]), and the surgical decision (prophylactic colectomy or proctocolectomy with pouch-versus-ileorectal options and the pregnancy-and-desmoid cautions — [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/contraception-fertility]]); chemoprevention trials (the NSAID-and-sulindac-celecoxib Polyprevention story — [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]). Attenuated FAP and the MUTYH-recessive cousin (later onset, fewer polyps, extra colon-cancer-risk and base-excision-repair biology — [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]]). Peutz-Jeghers (the hamartomatous polyps with arborising smooth muscle, the mucocutaneous pigmentation — [[musculoskeletal/skin]] — and the intussusception-and-bleeding in youth [[paediatrics/acute-childhood-illness]], plus the breast-and-gynaecological-and-pancreatic-and-testicular cancer risks — [[surgery/breast-endocrine-surgery]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/gynaecological-cancer]], [[gastrointestinal/pancreas-biliary]], [[surgery/vascular-urological-surgery]]); juvenile-polyposis-and-SMAD4-BMPC (the multiple juvenile polyps, the tiny-nose-and-telangiectasia overlap with HHT — [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]-and-[[blood/haemostasis-bleeding]] — and the cancer risk with surveillance); Cowden/PTEN (the trichilemmomas-and-macrocephaly-and-thyroid-and-breast-and-endometrial clustering — [[endocrine/thyroid]], [[surgery/breast-endocrine-surgery]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/gynaecological-cancer]]); and the Lynch syndrome chapter-staple (the mismatch-repair germline mutation with microsatellite-unstable tumours, the early-onset right-sided predominance, the endometrial-and-ovarian-and-urothelial-and-gastric-and-sebaceous risks — [[renal/urinary-tract]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/gynaecological-cancer]], [[musculoskeletal/skin]]-Muir-Torre; the tumour-MSI-and-MMR-IHC screening algorithm with BRAF-to-exclude-sporadic — [[pathology/cancer-diagnosis]], [[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]]; the colonoscopic-surveillance-from-20s-25s-and-the-aspirin-CAPP2-offer-and-the-risk-reducing-hysterectomy-bilateral-salpingo-oophorectomy discussion — [[public-health/screening]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]]; and the immune-checkpoint-therapy implication of MSI — [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]]). The clinic architecture (the family-history-taking rules and pedigree drawing — [[clinical-skills/history-taking]]; the genetic-testing-cascade and predictive testing in children with the age-appropriate-surveillance-start consensus — [[genetics/genetic-testing]]; and the primary-care register and uptake-inequity check — [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]]).**
:::

## Inflammatory bowel disease

::: definition Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis {#def-ibd}
**Aetiology (the four-legged stool: susceptibility genes — over 200 loci, NOD2 and IL23R and autophagy and barrier pathways for Crohn's, epithelial-and-IL-cytokine for UC — [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]]; the microbiota (dysbiosis, reduced diversity, the proteobacteria bloom — [[microbiology/microbiota]]); environment (the hygiene-and-infection-and-antibiotic-and-appendicectomy-and-smoking-paradox: smoking worsens Crohn's but appears protective in UC — a truth never to be used as advice, [[psychiatry/substance-use]]-and-[[public-health/prevention-promotion]]; diet-and-emulsifier-and-westernisation hypotheses — [[public-health/global-health]]); and immunity (broken tolerance with divergent Th17/Th1-type and Th2-type architectures — [[immunology/tolerance-regulation]] and [[immunology/adaptive-responses]]). Classification: UC limited to colon with continuous mucosal inflammation from the anal verge (proctitis/left-sided/extensive — the E1-E3 extent map) versus Crohn's anywhere from mouth to anus, transmural, segmental, with the Montreal age-behaviour-location coding; perianal Crohn's as its own disease face; the IBD-unclassified corner ([[pathology/diagnostic-pathology]]). Manifestations beyond the gut: the erythema-nodosum-pyoderma-arthropathy-uveitis-PSC-and-thrombosis-and-osteoporosis-and-fatigue set ([[musculoskeletal/skin]], [[musculoskeletal/bones-joints]], [[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]], [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]], [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]'s PSC-cholangiography, [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]] and [[endocrine/bone-calcium]]); note the parallel-versus-sequential logic of extra-intestinal activity (some run with luminal disease — erythema, uveitis, peripheral arthritis; some do not — ankylosing spondylitis, PSC). Complications: the stricturing-and-penetrating Crohn's phenotype (fistulae entero-cutaneous-entero-vesical-recto-vaginal — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/menstrual-pelvic-disorders]]-adjacent; abscess), toxic megacolon and fulminant colitis ([[surgery/acute-abdomen]]'s differential list), GI bleeding ([[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]]), the colorectal-cancer-risk-and-surveillance programme ([[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]] and [[public-health/screening]]), venous thromboembolism (active-IBD as a major transient risk factor — prophylaxis rules [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]), malnutrition-and-growth-failure (paediatric priorities and the exclusive-enteral-nutrition induction — [[paediatrics/growth-development]], [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]), and the primary-sclerosing-cholangitis-and-cholangiocarcinoma-overlap ([[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]] and [[gastrointestinal/pancreas-biliary]]). Investigations: the faecal-calprotectin-and-lactoferrin triage (the functional-versus-organic divider and its PPI-and-NSAID false-positives — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]], [[#def-functional]]); CRP-and-albumin-and-platelets-and-iron-studies-and-B12 (the disease-and-nutrition ledger — [[blood/anaemia]], [[gastrointestinal/digestion-absorption]]); infection exclusion at every flare (C difficile and enteric pathogens and CMV in the refractory colon — testing per [[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]], [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]]); endoscopy with map biopsies (the Crohn's-ileal-and-UC-continuous reading and the dysplasia-surveillance protocol — [[pathology/diagnostic-pathology]]); cross-sectional imaging (MR-enterography for Crohn's small bowel with the capsule-retention caution before strictures are excluded — [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]; the transperineal-and-pelvic-MRI for perianal disease — [[anatomy/pelvis-perineum]]); and bone-and-vitamin-D-and-vaccination-status baselines ([[endocrine/bone-calcium]], [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]). Treatment architecture (the treat-to-target era: symptoms plus calprotectin plus endoscopic-and-transmural healing — the STRIDE consensus logic): 5-ASA oral-plus-topical (the combination-superiority and mesalazine-dose-and-mesalamine-nephrotoxicity-monitoring [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]); corticosteroids for induction never maintenance (the IV-hydrocortisone acute-severe protocol and budesonide's first-pass-profile — [[endocrine/adrenal]]-and-[[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]]); immunomodulators (thiopurines with TPMT-and-NUDT15 pharmacogenetics and the lymphoma-and-skin-cancer caveats — [[pharmacology/patient-variation]], [[musculoskeletal/skin-cancer]]; methotrexate with folate and the hepatotoxic-fibrosis vigilance [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]); biologics and small molecules (anti-TNF and the infliximab-origin-rabbit-antisera story — [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]]; anti-integrin vedolizumab's gut-selectivity — [[immunology/adaptive-responses]]'s lymphocyte-trafficking lesson; anti-IL-12/23 and anti-IL-23 and the JAK-inhibitor and S1P-receptor-modulator era with their VTE-and-infection-and-zoster-safety frames — [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]]); therapeutic drug monitoring and immunogenicity (the anti-drug-antibody arithmetic and combination-therapy rationales — [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]], [[immunology/adaptive-responses]]); the acute severe ulcerative colitis protocol (Truelove-Witts criteria, IV steroids, day-3 decision point by stool-frequency-and-CRP — the Oxford score — with infliximab-or-ciclosporin rescue and surgery for failures — [[#ex-asuc]]; VTE prophylaxis and abdominal imaging and the steroid-continuation nuance — [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]], [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]); surgery: UC (panproctocolectomy with ileal-pouch-anal-anastomosis — the J-pouch and its functions, failures and pouchitis (see [[#ex-asuc]]; incontinence and sepsis and pouch-failure pathways [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]]-and-[[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]]-own-continence-physiology [[gastrointestinal/digestion-absorption]]'s motility section) versus end-ileostomy) versus Crohn's (conservation philosophy: strictureplasty, limited resection, the high anastomotic-and-endoscopic-recurrence rates mitigated by post-operative prophylaxis strategies — [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]]); the perianal algorithm (examination-under-anaesthesia, seton drainage, anti-TNF-first medical therapy, and the rare diversion); and the special situations (pregnancy: conceive-in-remission, most drugs compatible, methotrexate-teratogenic stop-rule and the small biologic-transfer-note — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]], [[pharmacology/patient-variation]]; vaccination before-and-during immunosuppression with live-vaccine-avoidance — [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]; infection and flare discrimination at every admission; and transition-of-care from paediatrics — [[paediatrics/adolescent-health]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]]).**
:::

::: example The day-3 decision {#ex-asuc}
A 26-year-old with extensive ulcerative colitis (8 bloody stools/day, HR 104, temp 38.1, Hb 96, CRP 98) is admitted, given IV hydrocortisone, and stool studies including C difficile are negative; by day 3: 7 bloody stools, CRP 82, albumin 24.
::: solution
**Acute severe UC failing IV steroids (the Truelove-Witts definition and the Oxford day-3 criterion — CRP-and-stool-count persistence predicts colectomy — [[#def-ibd]]): the rescue decision now, not "one more day": infliximab (with the TB-and-hepatitis screening practice ([[infectious-diseases/tuberculosis]], [[public-health/communicable-disease-control]]); anti-TNF mechanism ([[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]])) or calcineurin-inhibitor (ciclosporin/tacrolimus with the drug-level-and-magnesium-and-neurotoxicity monitoring — [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]], [[renal/potassium-calcium-phosphate]]) or surgery — the choice by local expertise and patient values ([[clinical-skills/communication]]). The running-care list: **VTE prophylaxis (the active-IBD hypercoagulability and the rectal-bleed-vs-anticoagulant moral arithmetic — [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]); nutrition-and-fluids-and-electrolytes (and the transfusion-threshold — [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]], [[blood/transfusion]], [[renal/water-sodium]]); abdominal-distension surveillance for toxic megacolon (imaging and surgical re-review rules — [[surgery/acute-abdomen]], [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]); avoidance of anticholinergics-and-opioids-and-NIL-dogma (the colon's motility pharmacology — [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[gastrointestinal/digestion-absorption]]'s motility section); CMV-biopsy-for-steroid-refractory-disease ([[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]]-own-refractory rule and [[microbiology/viruses]]'s-episode-management); the colectomy-conversation-with-a-colorectal-surgeon-present-from-day-1 (the laparoscopic subtotal colectomy with end-ileostomy as the emergency operation and the J-pouch-later pathway — [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]], [[anatomy/pelvis-perineum]]); and the psychological-and-fertility-and-return-to-work framing for a 26-year-old (the IBD nurses and the transition-and-adherence programme — [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]).**
:::

## Chronic diarrhoea, coeliac and the functional mind-gut

::: definition Coeliac disease {#def-coeliac}
**Immunology (gliadin peptides deamidated by tissue transglutaminase presented on HLA-DQ2 or DQ8 — the strongest common-HLA disease association — driving an intraepithelial-lymphocyte-destructive enteropathy; [[immunology/antigen-recognition]], [[immunology/autoimmunity]] and the [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]-vs-[[genetics/common-disease-genetics]] lesson it teaches). Epidemiology and the iceberg (1-3 % seroprevalence, most undiagnosed; associations — Down-and-Turn-and-Williams syndromes, type-1 diabetes, autoimmune thyroid, IgA deficiency — [[genetics/genome-chromosomes]], [[endocrine/diabetes]], [[endocrine/thyroid]], [[immunology/immunodeficiency]]); presentation (childhood failure-to-thrive-and-diarrhoeal classic; adult iron-deficiency-anaemia, fatigue, dental-enamel, osteoporosis, itchy-dermatitis-herpetiformis, unexplained transaminitis, infertility, hyposplenism [[blood/anaemia]], [[endocrine/bone-calcium]], [[musculoskeletal/skin]], [[gastrointestinal/liver-function]]'s-transaminitis-row, [[obstetrics-gynaecology/early-pregnancy]]-adjacent-recurrent-loss [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]). Diagnosis (serology first while eating gluten: tTG-IgA with total IgA for the deficiency trap; then duodenal-biopsy confirmation with the Marsh architecture and the HLA-exclusion-niche and the biopsy-negative-serology-positive corner — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]], [[pathology/diagnostic-pathology]]); never-test-on-a-gluten-free-diet-and-never-start-the-diet-before-serology ([[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]-adjacent-testing-integrity). Management (dietitian-led strict gluten-free with the label-and-cross-contamination-and-holiday-and-canteen-education, the supplement-repletion phase, the bone-density-question, the pneumococcal-and-other-vaccines-for-hyposplenism [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]], the follow-up-serology-and-symptom review and the non-response algorithm — adherence-first, then refractory-coeliac-type-1-versus-type-2 with its EATL-lymphoma-association and its immunosuppression-or-clinical-trial management [[pathology/cancer-diagnosis]], [[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]]). The contested category: non-coeliac gluten sensitivity and the FODMAP-overlap explanation and the wheat-ATTI and label economics — keep the diagnosis of exclusion honest ([[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]]-adjacent-food-environment).**
:::

::: definition Functional bowel disorders and constipation {#def-functional}
**IBS (Rome IV: recurrent abdominal pain with altered bowel habit, symptom-based diagnosis with limited bloodwork-and-calprotectin-and-coeliac-and-parasite screening — the positive-diagnosis model, not the dustbin — [[#def-ibd]]-divider-and-[[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]]); pathophysiology as gut-brain dysregulation: visceral hypersensitivity and altered motility and mucosal immune activation and microbiome shifts and permeability and central pain-processing, with the early-life-adversity-and-anxiety-and-HPA-axis epidemiology ([[physiology/hormones]]'s stress axis, [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]], [[microbiology/microbiota]], [[immunology/innate-immunity]]); subtypes (diarrhoea-predominant — the bile-acid-malabsorption-and-B12-and-hyposplenism-and-hyperthyroid-and-laxative-abuse differentials [[gastrointestinal/digestion-absorption]]-and-[[endocrine/thyroid]]; constipation-predominant and mixed; pain-dominant with the antispasmodic-and-tricyclic logic). Red-flag exclusion (blood, weight-loss, nocturnal-symptoms, age-of-onset-over-50, family history, anaemia, calprotectin-rise — the urgent-pathway referrals of [[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]]), including the missed-cancer-and-ovarian-cancer-mimic audits ([[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/gynaecological-cancer]]). Management: the therapeutic-alliance-and-explanation-first (the gut-brain formulation that patients accept without feeling dismissed — [[clinical-skills/communication]]); diet-first trials with a dietitian (regular-meals-and-fat-and-caffeine-and-alcohol-and-FODMAP-reintroduction-discipline — [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]); symptom-targeted drugs (antispasmodics-and-peppermint-oil, osmotic-laxatives-for-constipation-not-for-diarrhoea, PEG-and-linaclotide-and-prucalopride for constipation [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]-and-[[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], loperamide-and-codeine-analogues for diarrhoea with the-IBD-caution, bile-acid-sequestrants when the SeHCAT-logic applies [[gastrointestinal/pancreas-biliary]], eluxadoline-with-its-pancreas-and-sphincter-of-Odder-caveats [[gastrointestinal/pancreas-biliary]]); neuromodulators and gut-brain therapies (low-dose-tricyclic-for-pain, the SSRI-DRI-evidence-honesty [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]-adjacent, gut-directed-hypnotherapy-and-CBT-with-NNT-worth-knowing — [[epidemiology-ebm/systematic-reviews]]); and the refractory-reconsider (re-audit the diagnosis, microscopic colitis, bile-acid, endometriosis, chronic-intestinal-pseudo-obstruction with its megacolon-mimic danger [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]], [[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]]). Microscopic colitis (the chronic-watery-diarrhoea-with-normal-colonoscopy diagnosis requiring biopsies; collagenous-vs-lymphocytic; the NSAID-PPI-and-SSRI associations [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]; budesonide-first — [[gastrointestinal/digestion-absorption]]'s diarrhoea taxonomy). Chronic constipation (slow-transit-vs-functional-defecation-disorder-vs-drug-induced-opioid-iron-verapamil-clonidine [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]-and-[[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]]-vs-outlet (the anorectal manometry and balloon-expulsion and defaecating-proctography work-up, biofeedback-first — [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]-adjacent-motility-clinic practice); the impaction-with-overflow-diarrhoea trap ([[geriatrics-palliative/falls]]-adjacent-delirium-presentations and the-clean-out-then-maintain rule [[clinical-skills/physical-examination]]); and surgical selection for the rare truly-slow-transit-colony with intact outlet — [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]]). Faecal incontinence (obstetric sphincter injury — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/labour-birth]], neurogenic — [[nervous-system/motor-systems]], rectal-prolapse [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]]-adjacent, and cognitive/frailty presentations — [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]]: the continence-assessment, anal-sphincter physiology in [[gastrointestinal/digestion-absorption]], and the ladder from diet-and-rigidics-and-perianal-skin-care to sacral-nerve-stimulation [[surgery/gastrointestinal-surgery]]; ask-the-question-delicacy — [[clinical-skills/communication]]).**

::: example The left iliac fossa fire {#ex-diverticulitis}
A 71-year-old on azathioprine after renal transplant has 4 days of left iliac fossa pain and fever; CT shows sigmoid diverticulitis with a 4.5 cm pericolic abscess and no free fluid; WBC 14, CRP 210, lactate 2.2, creatinine 140 (baseline 110).
::: solution

::: example The anaemia with a flat biopsy {#ex-coeliac}
A 47-year-old woman with fatigue and iron-deficiency anaemia (Hb 102, ferritin 11, despite oral iron "for years") has normal gastroscopy reporting "normal mucosa" with no biopsies taken; she asks about gluten.
::: solution

Repair: **tTG-IgA-plus-total-IgA now (she is still eating gluten — do not start a gluten-free diet before serology-and-biopsy — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]); if positive, repeat gastroscopy with correct biopsies; if already gluten-free, a supervised gluten-challenge or HLA-exclusion discussion; the differential sweep (H-pylori-and-atrophic-gastritis, menorrhagia — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/menstrual-pelvic-disorders]], NSAID-blood-loss, angiodysplasia — [[gastrointestinal/gi-bleeding-cancer]]); once confirmed: the dietitian-and-lymphoma-and-bone-and-vaccine-hyposplenism package ([[endocrine/bone-calcium]], [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]), the thyroid-and-diabetes-and-family-screening offers ([[endocrine/thyroid]], [[endocrine/diabetes]], [[public-health/screening]]), and the adherence-and-social-life-and-canteen-and-holiday coaching ([[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]).**

::: example The diarrhoea that is constipation {#ex-impaction}

::: history
**The appendectomy century (Reclus's 1889 "appendiceal massacre" polemic against needless surgery, reversed by McBurney's localisation and the two World Wars' surgical doctrine; the antibiotics-first trials of the 2010s swinging the pendulum back — [[#def-appendix]]); the diverticular-disease epidemic with low-fibre Western diets (Painter's 1960s surgical-field observations and pressure studies — [[#def-diverticular]]); the Crohn-Canada-1932 description and the decades-long regional-enteritis-versus-tuberculosis argument still live in high-TB-settings practice ([[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]-adjacent-differential [[public-health/global-health]]); the 5-ASA-sulfasalazine story (a rheumatology by-product turned colitis standard — [[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]]-adjacent); the thiopurine-to-biologic immunosuppression arc (the rabbit anti-TNF antisera of 1991 founding infliximab — [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]]'s antibody-chimerisation biology); the coeliac rediscovery (Dicke's Dutch-hunger-winter clinical observations, van de Kamer's fat-balance proof of gluten injury in 1950, and the tTG-autoantigen identification of 1997 that enabled serology — [[#def-coeliac]] and [[immunology/autoimmunity]]); the Rome criteria and the gut-brain reframing of functional bowel disease ([[#def-functional]] and [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]]); and the modern treat-to-target and pouch-era outcome registries ([[public-health/health-systems-economics]]-adjacent audit science).**

::: summary
- Appendicitis is scored and imaged with radiation discipline; antibiotics-first is a legitimate choice with a documented recurrence curve, and the over-50s need the caecal-cancer question answered.
- Diverticular disease spans asymptomatic-to-peritonitis; management is severity-matched, immunosuppression lowers every threshold, and elective-surgery decisions follow symptoms not episode counts.
- IBD is treated to target with a stepwise immune arsenal; acute severe colitis has a day-3 rescue-or-surgery decision, VTE prophylaxis and a surgeon in the room from day 1.
- Coeliac disease is serology-then-biopsy on a gluten-containing diet, dietitian-managed for life, and monitored for refractory disease and lymphoma.
- IBS is a positive gut-brain diagnosis with a focused red-flag screen; microscopic colitis, bile-acid diarrhoea and impaction-with-overflow are the mimics that get missed.
:::
