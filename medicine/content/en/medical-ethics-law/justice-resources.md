Distributive justice in clinical practice is the ethics most clinicians experience as failure: the polyclinic queue, the denied funding request, the triage trolley, the job that goes to the connected candidate, and the specialty that cannot recruit to the place that needs it. **This chapter supplies the moral machinery for those moments: the frameworks for deciding who receives scarce benefit (utilitarian aggregation and its QALY implementation, prioritarianism and sufficiency, egalitarianism and health-equity work, the luck-versus-choice and responsibility debates, and procedural-fairness theory's A-reasonable-revisions answer to substantive disagreement), the micro-allocation machinery (waiting lists and their rules, triage's separate ethics, the funding-appeal-and-individual-request systems and their accountability deficits), the macro layer (the technology-assessment-and-coverage decisions' ethics, the invisible rationing and its disclosure problem, and the 'should-clinicians-ration' question's two-horned answer), and the meso questions specific to medicine (the geographical-inequalities and postcode-code data, the priority-groups-and-vulnerable-populations ethics, the workforce's own justice claims, and the global-research-and-shortage-hiring extensions the public-health chapters leave open).**

## The frameworks

::: definition Theories of distribution {#def-theories}
**The four families clinicians actually argue with: consequentialist aggregation (maximise total benefit - the QALY's engineering form, the cost-effectiveness-threshold's ethics, and its two classic objections: the separateness-of-persons complaint (sums across persons can justify crushing one) and the levelness objection (totals ignore who is worst off - the 'save-the-slightly-sicker-cheaper-cohort-over-the-dying-minority' counterexample the priority-weighted-EQ-5D-and-programme-budgeting-and-its-equity-weights literature is built to answer), prioritarianism (weight the worse-off, the 'leviathan-of-priority-versus-leviathan-of-aggregation' arguments with their formal-tools - the priority-weights, the disability-weight-controversy-see-also), sufficientarianism (ensure everyone reaches an adequate floor - the 'basic-health-services-essential-medicines-lists' and the international-development-goal architecture, with its 'the-floor-leaves-the-top-unconstrained' criticism and its 'the-floor-is-what-people-actually-agree-on' defence), and egalitarianism (reduce avoidable health gaps - the health-equity-and-inverse-care-law tradition and its definitional labours: horizontal-versus-vertical-equity (equal-treatment-equal-need, unequal-treatment-unequal-need), the 'is-the-gap-unfair-or-just-different' distinction (the 'unhealthy-because-unjust-versus-biologically-varied' demarcation), and the 'outcome-versus-access-versus-financial-protection' equity dimensions the WHO-frameworks track). The responsibility-and-luck debates (the 'should-lifestyle-affect-claim' arguments - the smoking-and-liver-transplant-and-joint-replacement-and-'the-previously-mentioned-deterrent-tobacco-tax-versus-access-condition-theories' ethics, the responsibility-fit-views-versus-the-'luck-egalitarianism-is-cruel-to-the-vulnerable' critique (Anderson's and later relational-egalitarianism's answer: the question is not whether your choices caused your illness but whether you can stand as an equal), and the practical resolution: the clinical-eligibility-evidence-based-rules-'is-quit-smoking-or-weight-a-success-predictor' questions answered by outcome-data-with-the-moral-frame-set-sideways-plus-the-anti-deterrent-argument-'waiting-lists-punish-presents-not-past'). The procedural-reply-to-deep-disagreement: accountability-for-reasonableness-revision (the A4A-and-'reasons-relevant-and-practical-and-enforceable-or-revisable'-and-'correct-and-timely-and-public'-criteria-the-'fair-allocation-decisions'-framework from the transplant-and-priority-setting studies - the 'substance-unsettled-process-can-still-be-legitimate' solution that is this course's working answer to every 'you cannot agree on the theory so how do you decide' argument. The synthesis for practice: no clinician holds one family consistently (the empirical ethics surveys say so), so the professional demand is not theoretical purity but reason-giving in public that survives the four families' cross-examination, and knowing which family a given institution is actually running (the QALY-hospital, the floor-fund, the priority-waitlist) so the advocacy happens in the institution's own language.**
:::

## The machinery

::: definition Micro-allocation: queues, triage, appeals {#def-micro}
**Waiting-lists' ethics (the rule-design space: first-in-first-out as the moral default its defenders call respect and its critics call regressive, severity-at-entry-versus-progression-through-wait-lists-prioritisation (the 'waitlist-urgency-versus-chronicity' arguments - sickest-first punishes the steady and rewards deterioration, the 'listing-when-worst' clinical-behaviour distortion the severity-weighted systems document), the 'urgency-adjusted-and-outcome-adjusted'-listing scores' engineering (the MELD-and-EuroSCORE-and-'is-the-'expected-benefit'-list-a-utility-score-in-disguise' debates), the cancellation-and-delay-and-'did-the-patient-die-on-the-list' mortality-audit duty, and the private-pay-and-concierge-and-'two-track-medicine'-queue-jumping's ethics (the 'is-a-second-list-a-second-class-right' analysis and its transparency-not-prohibition default compromise). Triage's separate ethics (the disaster-and-pandemic-ventilator-matrix literature's settled core: maximise-lives-saved-with-exclusion-of-futile-cases-and-'no-first-come-and-no-privacy-visible-to-the-triage-officer-and-the-'standard-of-care-under-surge'-legal-shields-and-the-'do-not-put-your-own-name-in-the-allocation' separation-of-triage-from-treating-clinician rule, the prioritarian-versus-instrumental-value-of-health-care-workers' debates whose resolution the vaccination-priority-tables made explicit with their 'essential-workforce-after-residents'-and-'the-reciprocity-argument' rows, and the disability-communitys-justified-'triage-protocols-that-count-years-of-life-worth-living-are-discrimination' charge and the settled corrective: no-utility-scaling-by-disability-status-per-se (the UK-legal-and-UNCRPD-and-ADA-lines), clinical-eligibility-and-comorbidity-burden-assessment-only, with the 'the-evidence-is-better-than-the-protocols-admitted' implementation-reviews. The funding-request-and-appeal architecture (the individual-funding-request-'is-this-a-right-or-a-lottery' problem: the 'clinical-priority-versus-cost-effectiveness' two-question-forms, the 'appeal-that-lets-the-clinician-advocate' function, the 'Cinderella-appeals'-critique - the treatments with vocal foundations win, the silent diseases lose - and the programme-budgeting-and-population-fund-designs that try to make the trade-offs before the tears, plus the disclosure problem: the decision-makers' identities-published-or-anonymous-and-the-'named-patient-effect'-that-every-funding-system-fights-by-design-not-virtue). The rationing-by-inaction taxonomy the clinician should be able to audit in their own service: the waiting-list-without-investment, the 'no-commissioner-will-pay-therefore-nobody-refers' local-formulary-and-service-design, the staffing-limited-clinic-closure, and the referral-criteria-that-define-the-need-away - each with its different accountability fix (register, transparency, escalation-route-and-minimum-standards). The chapter's rule of thumb: allocation is invisible by default and that invisibility is the injustice; the just systems publish their rules, their waits, their deaths-on-the-list and their appeals-success-rates, because procedural fairness is the only theory every framework can share.**
:::

## The macro layer

::: definition Coverage, cost-effectiveness and the clinician's role {#def-macro}
**Health-technology-assessment's ethics (the HTA-and-coverage-without-decision rules: the 'is-the-cost-effectiveness-threshold-a-rationing-cape-or-an-accountable-line' debate, the ICER-and-NICE-and-German-and-French-and-Brazilian-and-Thai-and-Korean-agencies' 'is-it-science-or-is-it-a-policy-with-a-range' transparency problem, the 'severity-modifier-and-end-of-life-and-rare-disease' exceptions that are NICE's own registered prioritarian-concessions and their 'the-exception-proves-and-hollows-the-rule' arguments, the patient-and-public-involvement-in-appraisal-committees' 'who-represents-whom' critique, and the confidential-discounts-and-managed-access-agreements' 'value-based-pricing-negotiated-in-secret' legitimacy problem the transparency campaigns target); the essential-medicines-and-floor-for-all layer (the WHO-model-list's 'effectiveness-equity-feasibility-triple' ethics, the 'out-of-pocket-and-catastrophic-expenditure-and-impoverishment-from-illness' global burden literature that makes financial protection a health-system-correctness-question-not-a-welfare-afterthought, and the 'the-100-essential-interventions-versus-UHC-as-a-political-project' arguments from the Global-health chapter's own chapters). The 'should-the-clinician-ration' two-horned-dilemma: bedside (the patient-in-front-claim's fiduciary force - the clinician's advocacy duty, the 'no-cost-at-the-bedside' norms that the American-medicine-defends-and-British-medicine-quietly-breaks literature measures, and the reframe: the ethical clinician's costs are the system's to see, hence the guideline-funding-and-'does-my-service-owe-a-fair-share-of-the-global-budget' duties) versus population (the 'rationing-invisible-at-the-coal-face'-truth that every clinical guideline with an age-or-severity-cut-off IS rationing written by clinicians, the 'the-advocacy-paradox' - clinicians who never mention cost deserve the budgets they get, and the professionalism answer: cost-awareness as a duty of the population you were also hired by, exercised through the system's channels (formulary-and-guideline-and-commissioning-roles) rather than at the bedside's bedside-quiet-restrictions - the 'silent-bedside-rationing-is-the-worst-of-all-worlds' verdict from every systems study. The global-justice extensions: the shortage-country health-workforce migration ethics (the 'recruitment-from-source-countries-and-the-WHO-code-of-practice-on-international-recruitment-and-its-voluntary-tooth' literature, the 'brain-drain-and-circular-migration-and-remittance-economics' frames, and the 'is-a-recruitment-levy-that-funds-source-country-training-a-just-price-or-a-licence-to-take' positions), the research-priority justice (the '90-10-gap-and-disease-burden-versus-market-return' distribution of research and the priority-setting frameworks' 'disability-adjusted-life-years-for-research-purposes-and-its-critics' debate), and the pandemic-era lessons this course's chapters already carry (the 'vaccine-apartheid-and-the-COVAX-and-TRIPS-waiver-debates', the 'ventilator-triage-when-the-whole-hospital-is-triage'-population-ethics, and the 'essential-worker-and-risk-pay-and-who-protects-the-protectors' reciprocity-unfinished-business). The chapter's applied test for any allocation decision, borrowed from the procedural-fairness literature: publicity (could the rule survive its own press release), relevance (are the reasons tied to the aims patients share), revision (is there a route to argue it), and enforcement (does anyone have to answer) - the four questions that let a clinician challenge an allocation without pretending to hold a philosophy.**
:::

::: example The funding appeal {#ex-appeal}
Your patient with a rare neurological condition needs a drug at 90,000 per QALY your system will not fund; the same drug is funded across the border and for the trial's control-to-open-label arm. The family wants you to 'go to the panel'; the commissioner wants you to 'be realistic'.
::: solution
**Individual-funding-appeals-played-well([[#def-micro]], [[#def-macro]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]]): The honest frame first: the panel is not a court of morals but a procedural-fairness device (the A4A machinery), so the submission wins on its relevance: the evidence-graded-benefit-for-this-patient-versus-the-trial-population (the outcomes that matter to her, not the surrogate endpoints the company's submission leaned on), the 'why-she-is-not-the-average-of-the-appraisal' argument (the modifier-criteria the system's own rules already contain - severity, rarity, the carer-and-informal-costs-the-model-omitted, the work-and-dependence-consequences), the comparative-burden honesty (the border-variation and the trial-access asymmetry stated as the equity argument the panel can act on without embarrassment), and the practical-proposals-the-panel-has-not-thought-of (the managed-access-and-clinical-trial-and-collaborative-buying-routes-and-the-time-limited-trial-with-pre-agreed-stop-rules-with-a-named-clinical-lead - the 'what-would-make-this-decision-reversible' package that gets taken seriously). The family conversation: the appeal-supports-the-relationship rule (you do the submission and they see you doing it, the named-timescales, and the no-promising), the open-label-versus-funded-access distinction explained without jargon (the 'if-it-stops-what-happens-to-her'-plan-asked-before-the-signature), and the 'whatever-the-panel-decides-we-look-after-her-in-the-meantime' commitment that stops the fight becoming the treatment. The system-honesty close: the panel's decision documented with the reasons they gave, the 'similar-patient-next-month-will-need-this-again' escalation to the clinical-network-and-commissioning-group-without-waiting-for-the-next-appeal-wave - the individual-fight-that-generates-no-policy-is-the-injustice-with-a-good-lawyer.**
:::
:::

::: example The two lists {#ex-twolists}
Your unit runs one transplant list under national criteria; a private centre two hours away lists the same patients on identical criteria but with an added 'fitness-for-complex-surgery' review you do not run. Two of your long-waiting patients are offered transfer 'for a faster list'. The families call you for advice.
::: solution
**Two-lists-one-justice([[#def-micro]], [[#def-macro]]): The first duty is information: the actual waiting-time distributions by list and blood-group and sensitivity (the 'faster-list' claim's truth varies by immunology - the private-centre's-typical-cohort-effect: lower-sensitisation-patients-finish-faster-anywhere, and the 'are-they-filtering-my-harder-patients-toward-me' question that the aggregate outcome-and-list-mix data either raise or answer, which you should request from the network before you opine). The clinical substance: the 'fitness-review-difference' is either a genuine risk-stratification the national criteria delegate locally (in which case the private centre may be safer for some patients and you should say so) or a wealth-adjacent-filter (in which case the 'identical-criteria' marketing is dishonest and the regulator's list-integrity remit is the route). The patient-facing advice: neutrality-with-numbers ('here is what each list's wait looks like for someone like you, here are the surgical-volume-and-outcome figures both units publish, here is what transferring costs you in travel and family-support and continuity - and here is my honest read'), the 'you will not be looked at differently here whichever you choose' guarantee said early because it is always the unasked question, and the transfer-administration help (records-and-sharing-agreement-and-the-clinician-to-clinician-call) as your duty either way. The system-justice limb: the episode goes in the network's-equity-log (the 'does-secondary-sector-selection-empty-the-public-list-of-easy-cases' monitoring exists for exactly this pattern), because the just answer to two-tier questions is not resentment but data, publication, and criteria-honesty-enforcement.**
:::

::: example The clinical trial's geography {#ex-trialgeo}
A multinational trial of your country's most-prescribed class needs 40 sites; your deprived-city hospital has the patients and not the trial infrastructure, and the sponsor's 'feasibility' shortlist contains only university and private sites. The local research nurse asks you to help you both say no.
::: solution
**Participation-justice-in-research([[#def-theories]], [[medical-ethics-law/research-ethics]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]]): The ethics object: trial-site-selection-is-a-distribution-decision with downstream effects (the drug's evidence-base, label and guidance will be generated without the population that carries the disease-burden, and the access-negotiations-after-appraisal-will-cite-that-evidence - the 'evidence-extraction-without-inclusion' critique the trial-equity literature now formalises), and the sponsor's 'feasibility' category is usually a cost-function, not a law. The help you can actually give: the capability-not-exclusion counter-offer (the 'site-readiness-package': the city hospital's recruitment-advantage documented from its own audit, the infrastructure-gap itemised into what funding closes (the research-nurse-fTE-and-data-entry-and-audit-time the model already pays university sites), and the 'your-feasibility-list-is-our-equity-risk' letter through the chief-investigator-and-national-institute route), the conditions-under-which-no-is-right (a-site-that-could-not-run-the-protocol-safely-should-not-join-and-the-team-says-which-specific-gap-matters), and the structural escalation (the funder's-eligibility-rules-requiring-participation-plans-and-the-public-health-bodies'-research-equity-conditions-the-UK-and-Australian-and-US-institutes-now-write). The justice of the small compromise matters too: if the trial proceeds without you, the patients still deserve the information (the community-liaison-and-summary-results-in-plain-English-and-the-post-trial-access-position-the-sponsor-should-be-made-to-state), because the chapter's rule is that the excluded population should at least get the results they were never asked to generate.**

## Where this leads

The population measurement in [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]] and the economics in [[public-health/health-systems-economics]] and the global extensions in [[public-health/global-health]], the trial-and-drug-access layer in [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]], the research-priority questions in [[medical-ethics-law/research-ethics]], the pandemic triage practice in [[emergency-critical-care/major-incidents]], and the professional duties that make advocacy legitimate in [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]].

::: summary
- Allocation theory splits into aggregation, priority, sufficiency and equality families; no clinician or institution holds one consistently, which is why procedural fairness (publicity, relevance, revision, enforcement) is the shared ethic and the audit tool.
- Micro-allocation is a design discipline: queue rules, triage matrices and funding appeals each embed a theory, and their injustices are mostly invisibility - the just services publish rules, waits, lists and outcomes.
- Triage's settled corrections: maximise benefits under surge, never utility-scale by disability status, separate triage from treating teams, and protect the workforce's claims through reciprocity rows rather than hero-convention.
- Cost-effectiveness agencies are prioritarian hybrids whether or not they admit it; their legitimacy runs on transparency of thresholds, exceptions and discounts, and the clinician's role is system-channel advocacy, never silent bedside rationing.
- The profession's own distributions - training places, rotas, pay, credit, sickness, migration recruitment - are justice documents; a fairness audit that stops at patients is not a fairness audit.
- The chapter's practical test for any allocation you make or meet: could the rule survive its own press release, are its reasons tied to ends patients share, can it be argued with, and must anyone answer?
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Rank by what theory {level=1}
A single ICU bed, four candidates: a 28-year-old with myocarditis likely-to-recover, a 60-year-old post-cardiac-surgery who is a surgical colleague, a 45-year-old with metastatic cancer and pneumonia, and a 19-year-old with DKA and aspiration pneumonitis. Identify which allocation each theory gives and which the ward should use.
::: solution
**Theories-to-verdicts([[#def-theories]], [[#def-micro]], [[emergency-critical-care/critical-care]]): Aggregation-with-survival-benefit: the 28-year-old or the 19-year-old (high-probability, many-years cases) and the two are separated by the years-versus-probability weighting; prioritarian (worst-off-first): contested between the two salvageable (equal urgency) and the cancer patient if judged worst-off-but-unsalvageable-which-priority-should-not-waste-the-bed-on; sufficiency-floor: the bed goes where it prevents the absolute-deprivation-of-a-life-worth-living first, which collapses to the same two; egalitarian (save-the-younger-as-an-equal-opportunity-to-live-a-life-stage: the 19-then-28-year-olds, the 'fair-innings' argument the NICE-severity-modifier-debates know). The ward's answer under a surge/triage protocol: eligibility-and-benefit-first (the cancer patient is not a candidate for this bed's purpose; the colleague's surgery is not failing) and between the two salvageable young patients the protocol's documented tie-break (typically life-cycle/fair-innings favouring the teenager, with the 'no-occupation-no-social-value-no-queue-jumping' rules that exclude the colleague argument explicitly), decided by the designated triage officer with the treating teams protected and the whole thing reviewable. The teaching point: every theory ranks the same four people and the disagreement is narrow and principled, which is the argument for rules written before the bed is needed and for letting the protocol, not the loudest consultant, speak.**
:::
:::

::: exercise Audit the invisible rationing {level=2}
Your trust's diabetes service has: a 14-week waits for retinal screening against a 12-week standard, a 'photocoagulation only if adherent' local rule, no-podiatry-vacancy-since-March, and a 'no-new-insulin-pumps-without-trial-of-analogues-first' policy. Audit it as a justice document and write the four-item fix.
::: solution
**Rationing-register([[#def-micro]], [[#def-macro]], [[endocrine/diabetes-complications]]): The audit's lens (each item classified: capacity-underinvestment (the 14-weeks), conditionality-creep (the adherence rule - the 'is-this-comorbidity-of-poverty-or-a-clinical-contraindication' question, the 'adherence-definitions-and-the-DNA-penalty'-literature, and the equality-impact-assessment it skipped), vacancy-normalisation (the podiatry-post-nobody-will-fill-and-the-foot-amputation-inequalities-data-that-make-an-empty-post-a-mortality-decision), and a rationing-policy-in-guideline-clothing (the pumps-first-analogues rule - whose evidence-base-and-cost-rationale should be published with the rule, and whose appeals-route should exist). The four-item fix: (1) publish the waits, the vacancy and the shortfall with their outcome consequences (the 'register-of-unfunded-services' transparency mechanism the campaign bodies propose and individual trusts prove works by embarrassment), (2) replace the adherence rule with an barriers-assessment-and-support protocol (the adherence-as-a-team-job-not-an-eligibility-gate reframe and its documented-attendance-wins), (3) escalate the podiatry post through the workforce-and-safety-caseload route with the amputation-audit attached and a named review date, and (4) re-issue the pump policy with its rationale, its exceptions route and its next-review date - the four questions of procedural fairness (publicity, relevance, revision, enforcement) applied to a secret-rationing-typical-list. The meta-lesson: invisible rationing is the injustice, and the audit makes it visible in the institution's own documents.**
:::

::: exercise Argue the priority group {level=2}
During a vaccine surge, priority tables must choose between: residents of care homes, frontline care workers, people with severe mental illness, and an ethnically-enclaved manual-workforce district. Give each claim's moral language, the evidence each needs, and the rule you would write when supplies force a ranking.
::: solution
**Four-claims-one-trolley([[#def-theories]], [[#def-micro]], [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]): The claims: care-home residents - worst-off-and-exposure-inside-institutions (mortality evidence overwhelming); care workers - exposure-and-system-function-and-reciprocity (the 'who-protects-the-protectors' row with its 'essential-workforce-buffers-the-vulnerable-directly' version); severe mental illness - deprivation-and-comorbidity-and-a-care-system-that-cannot-isolate (the 'excess-mortality-and-congregate-settings' data the pandemic made citable); the manual-workforce district - transmission-dynamics-and-household-and-workplace-structure (the R-effective-and-outbreak-data argument plus the health-equity argument that its residents are the unmet-need inverse-care law lives on). The evidence each needs: mortality-risk, transmission-contribution, system-criticality, and avoidability - the four currencies and their different data sources (the 'modelling-vs-equity-vs-reciprocity'-priority frameworks). The written rule when forced: a composite of immediate-mortality-avoidance-and-transmission-and-system-function-with-an-explicit-equity-weight-and-no-silent-trade (e.g. residents-and-staff-of-care-institutions-and-frontline-health-and-care-first on combined mortality-and-function grounds; then highest-exposure-and-deprivation-districts-by-measured-reproduction-number-and-then-the-clinically-extreme-otherwise; and the mental-illness-cohort addressed through its institutional-and-household-exposure-route-where-real-with-its-own-reasoned-claim-not-a-hierarchy-of-deservingness). The process duties: the published-rationale-in-plain-English, the community-and-disability-advocacy-consultation-before-not-after, the 'no-profession-and-no-property-gets-a-row' honesty-check, and the re-review-trigger-when-supply-or-evidence-changes - because in a ranking-for-a-public-good-the-reasons-are-the-medicine.**

Draft (condensed) your department's contribution to a workforce-justice submission to Health Education: two sentences each on recruitment-fairness, rota-burden-distribution, parental-and-carer-leave-effects-on-progression, and international-graduate-equity, each with the metric you volunteer for the audit.

**A-fairness-submission-with-numbers([[#def-workforce]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]): Recruitment-fairness: 'we publish selection criteria and panel outcomes, audit interview-and-shortlisting by grade, ethnicity, disability, and international-medical-graduate status, and require the diversity-of-panel-and-decision-reasons-documentation rule'; metric: selection-yield-and-satisfaction-by-applicant-group-with-annual-variance-review. ::: history

The cost-effectiveness-institution era: the Alaska-Medicaid-priority-list-experiment (1992's 'rationing-documented-by-name-in-a-BlueBook' shock-and-its-public-hearings), the Oregon-Waiver-priority-lists-and-the-'which-is-worse-a-knee-or-a-life' ballot-wars, the NICE-2000-and-its-threshold-range-and-'is-it-a-rationing-agency-or-a-quality-of-care-inspectorate' founding argument, the WHO-choice-and-the-Botswana-and-Mexico-and-Thailand-and-Brazil-HTA-bodies'-global-south-adaptations, and the 'ICER-and-the-US-never-nationally-weights-a-QALY-insurance-markets-do-it-for-them' comparison that makes America the field's control group. The equity-measurement revolution (the Whitehall-and-Marmot-era social-determinants-findings giving the inverse-care-law-and 'inequalities-by-design' - the Black-report-and-'the-excess-mortality-decades'-England evidence-base, the 'health-equity-impact-assessment' instruments, the AHRQ-and-commonwealth-fund-disparities-monitoring in the American system, and the Marmot-review-II-'the-politics-of-health-inequality'-programmatic), the pandemic's forced-field-lecture (the 'triage-matrices-rehearsed-and-published-or-not-by-state', the 'young-vs-old-and-the-'fair-innings'-revival-in-public', the 'do-not-intubate-orders-and-the-disability-rights-litigation-that-followed-and-won' cases, the 'vaccine-nationalism-versus-_COVAX' global-distribution-crisis and the 'TRIPS-waiver-18-months-of-procedure-versus-18-months-of-deadlines' argument, and the 'essential-workers-and-risk-and-rewards-and-the-ethnicity-of-outcomes' findings that reopened the reciprocity and structural-determinants debates in the same breath). The workforce-justice-delayed-agenda (the 'European-Working-Time-Directive'-trainees-were-not-patients'-implementation-fair-fight', the 'duty-of-care-to-doctors-and-the-'sleep-deprivation-as-an-instrument'-ethics', the '60-hour-culture-and-'the-A CGME-common-duty-hours-Revision'-era-and-its-'did-it-move-burden-to-nurses'-literature', the pay-gap-and-citizenship-and-visa-and-'who-built-the-NHS-workforce'-postcolonial-histories-now-being-written, and the 'recruitment-code-of-practice-2020s-revision'-process). This chapter's current frontier: the 'AI-allocation-audits' (the 'algorithmic-bias-in-population-health-risk-scores'-case-study-and-the-'label-choice-as-policy'-finding), the 'climate-justice-and-coverage' questions, and the 'long-COVID-and-the-'disability-versus-decongestion'-lists'-politics', each a distribution argument in new machinery.** - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[public-health/global-health]].**

::: example The algorithm in the queue {#ex-algorithm}
Your region's new booking-triage tool prioritises 'predicted 12-month mortality' from a claims-data model; six months in, the deprived-city's waiting lists lengthen while its affluent suburb's shorten. The vendor says the model is unbiased; the data analyst says check the label.
::: solution
**Audit-by-label([[#def-micro]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]): The technical truth the analyst named: the model's fairness is only as good as its target - the 'predicted-mortality-versus-predicted-need-versus-predicted-spend' label choice is a policy dressed as a feature (the care-records-allocation-audit's finding that spend-based labels encode access-history, so deprivation-suppressed-utilisation becomes deprivation-suppressed-priority; the model can be 'unbiased' about spend and unjust about sickness). The diagnostic: the differential-effect-by-decile-and-ethnicity table (the model-versus-clinical-need benchmarked on a sample with outcomes-not-claims measured - the 'label-bias-audit' design), the reflow simulation (what priority looks like under need-based-versus-spend-based-versus-severity-labels), and the waiting-list-mortality-by-geography monitoring the deployment should never have lacked. The response: the pause-not-abandon stance (the tool stays off live triage pending re-label-and-revalidation - the 'proven-beneficial-before-individually-harmless' standard for allocation software, which is the reverse of drug licensure's precautionary asymmetry and should worry the board more than it does), the re-specification with the equity team and patient representatives choosing the target-label as a governance decision, the recalibration-with-monitoring-and-sunset-reviews, and the publication of all of it - the algorithmic-transparency-in-healthcare-frameworks' rules (what-it-does-what-data-whose-values-reviewed-when) exist exactly for this moment. The justice-limb for the board memo: an allocation tool that is technically accurate about a biased target is not a neutral instrument but regressive-policy-without-the-decency-of-a-debate, and the fix (need-based-labels-with-distributional-impact-published-before-golive) is cheap relative to the lists it will unlengthen.**

## Exercises

::: exercise Name the trade-off {level=1}
Your health board must fund one of: a hip replacement programme expansion (high QALYs per spend, elderly waiting 40 weeks), a rare-disease drug (180k per QALY, nine patients), or a community diabetes-prevention expansion (moderate effect, deprived populations, invisible outcomes). Give each option's moral case and the board's legitimate decision procedure.
::: solution
**Three-good-arguments([[#def-theories]], [[#def-macro]]): Hips: aggregation-plus-age-not-as-exclusion (the 'QALYs-and-the-fair-innings-question-whether-70-is-a-reason' debate stated honestly, the 40-week-suffering-and-mobility-and-falls-cascade case, and the 'visible-waiting-list-politically-protects-itself' honesty about why this always funds). Rare drug: the severity-and-rarity-exception (the 'rule-of-rescue-and-identifiable-lives-versus-statistical-lives' argument, the 'nine-patients-and-no-clinical-trial-equivalent-voice' equity-in-rarity defence, and the '180k-figure-and-the-managed-access-and-collaborative-procurement-answers-the-cost-half' rebuttal). Prevention: the prior-and-equity-case (the 'the-diabetes-that-never-happens-is-invisible-in-every-ledger' problem, the 'cost-effectiveness-versus-cost-saving-versus-health-gain-versus-equity' four-way scorecards, the inverse-care-law-alignment, and the 'prevention-funds-lose-because-benefits-are-out-year-and-out-budget' fiscal-design confession). The procedure that makes any of these legitimate: the published-decision-framework-before-the-choice (the board's weights stated: threshold-range, severity-modifier, equity-impact-requirement - the 'programme-budgeting-and-its-weights' method's governance transfer), the documented-reasons-for-this-time-and-what-would-flip-it, the appeal-and-revision-route (for the hips it is the patients, for the drug the company, for prevention the director of public health), and the publication of the trade by name - 'we chose X over Y and here is who waits'. The exam point: the wrong answers are 'fund all three with efficiency' (untrue), 'follow the loudest campaign' (arbitrary) and 'let the QALY-number decide alone' (a theory smuggled in as arithmetic).**

::: exercise Answer the journalist {level=1}
A reporter with a sympathetic patient outside asks why the NHS (or your system) 'won't pay for a drug that works'. Give the two-minute answer that is honest about rationing, fair to the patient, and not a press-release.
::: solution
**Rationing-out-loud([[#def-macro]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]): The structure: concede the fact first ('the honest answer is that every health system decides what it will not pay for - we just do some of it in waiting lists and some in committees; the choice is between rationing that happens in public and rationing that hides'), then the mechanism in plain words ('this drug's evidence and its price have to be compared with everything else the money could do - hip replacements, cancer drugs, chemotherapy-support, community nursing - and the committee found the value for money at this price was not there yet'), then the patient's reality (the 'what-we-will-do-for-her-today-and-in-the-appeal-and-trial-routes' specifics - the answer the reporter actually needs to print), then the accountability line ('and if you want to hear the committee's actual reasons and the price they were offered, they are published, and here is where'), then the values-not-expertise honesty ('these are genuinely hard choices about how much a year of life is worth when we cannot buy all of them; I would rather argue with you in public than pretend there is no choice'). The taboos: no blaming 'the bureaucrats', no implying the decision is clinical when it is economic-with-clinical-input (that deception costs trust twice), and no euphemism about 'not currently recommended' when 'we will not pay at this price' is the sentence. The follow-up duty: the patient's-own-team-letter-and-the-appeal-support-because-a-good-press-answer-that-ends-in-the-patient-feeling-selected-against-is-a-bad-answer.**

::: exercise Redress the postcode lottery {level=2}
Map your region's variation in three services (say: bariatric surgery, IUI-fertility, and spinal-cord-injury-rehabilitation). Explain each variation's mechanism (explicit policy, capacity, commissioning-design, advocacy-asymmetry) and design the equalisation instrument with the least new bureaucracy.
::: solution
**Variation-forensics([[#def-micro]], [[#def-macro]]): The mechanism diagnoses: bariatric-surgery (usually explicit commissioning criteria-variation - the NICE-versus-local-eligibility-drift and the 'bariatric-exclusion-lists' that are policy not science), IUI-fertility (the classic policy-lottery with the legal-challenge-history - 'the-CGT-and-NHS-England-mandated-minimum-cycles-and-the-commissioners-who-ignored-them-and-the-'policy-not-guideline'-legal-repair'), and spinal-cord-injury-rehabilitation (the capacity-and-commissioning-design problem - small-population-super-specialty-with-a-prototype-network-inequality-and-the-'price-of-the-unit-not-the-need-for-the-unit'-contracting-distortion). The instrument design (least-bureaucracy version): the national minimum-standards-with-publish-or-explain duty (the 'every-area-publishes-its-criteria-and-its-waits-and-its-outcomes-on-one-page' mechanism - publicity as enforcement, the least-expensive-equals-tool-ever-evaluated), a single cross-boundary-access-fund (the 'commissioner-of-residence-pays-without-a-fighting-appeal' rule for services under-set thresholds - converts local rationing into national funding without new bodies), the equity-impact-assessment-required-for-any-criteria-restriction (the 'show-me-the-deprivation-table-before-you-close-the-eligibility-door' gate), and the annual variation-report to the assembly-of-commissioners with a named improvement owner - the accountability-for-reasonableness-package-at-system-scale. The clinical-advocacy craft this chapter preaches: the specialty-societies' own variation-atlases (the Fertility-and-Surgery-and-Rehabilitation-colleges' audits prove clinicians document inequality better than departments do), and the 'write-the-criteria-as-if-your-own-mother-waits-in-the-district-that-dares-not-appeal' test that every commissioning-desk should be made to take aloud.**

::: exercise Defend the refusal {level=3}
A pharma company offers your hospital a compassionate-use-and-early-access programme for a device that saves limbs in a disease you treat weekly; the data are a single-arm series, the price will later anchor the national appraisal, and your ethics committee waves it through 'because patients benefit'. Write your dissenting memo: the justice analysis, the consent truth about 'compassionate' framing, and the counter-proposal that protects patients without pre-underwriting the price.
::: solution
**Compassion-justice-and-price-formation([[#def-macro]], [[medical-ethics-law/research-ethics]], [[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]]): The justice analysis: the programme's two distributions - who-glimpses-the-device (the well-publicised-hospital-and-referred-and-literate patients - a new postcode-lottery-axis) and who-pays-later (the national appraisal anchored to early-access-prices-negotiated-in-the-patients'-shadow, the 'reference-price-and-international-anchoring-and-'the-compassionate-cohort-becomes-the-launch-market' commercial mechanics the transparency literature documents); the single-arm-series truth (the 'because-we-cannot-know-who-would-have-kept-the-limb-anyway'-problem - the natural-history-and-concurrent-control-absence that makes the 'benefit' partly unmeasurable, which is why the programme is research-in-distributional-clothing). The consent correction: the 'compassionate-use' framing's truth-in-labeling duty (patients told: unproven-for-this-purpose, no-payment, exit-plan-if-harm-or-no-benefit, and 'your-participation-shapes-what-the-country-later-buys' - the three sentences the compassionate frame usually hides). The counter-proposal: accept-with-conditions-that-convert-generosity-into-evidence-and-price-discipline (the managed-access-agreement-with-confidential-price-and-outcomes-commitment-and-national-registry-inclusion-every-patient-every-site-not-just-yours-and-the-pre-registered-stopping-rules-and-the-public-report-and-the-appraisal-body-invited-as-a-partner-not-surprised-later - the 'NHS-Cancer-Drugs-Fund-and-England-and-Scotland-shared-guidance' mechanics as the template) and the distribution rule (the eligibility-criteria-published-nationally-and-rotating-site-access-so-the-lottery-at-least-draws-fairly). The memo's closing paragraph is the chapter in miniature: 'the question is not whether these patients should have a chance - they should - but whether we let a company use their desperation twice: once to generate the evidence that should have preceded the device and once to set the price the whole country will pay for it. We can say yes to the patients and no to the structure, and this is how.'**
