Below the filtration machinery lies the plumbing — and the plumbing has its own diseases: **infection ascending from the perineum, crystals growing where urine concentrates, obstruction quietly destroying both kidneys, and four distinct cancers of the tract's lining and parenchyma.** This chapter is also the applied-microbiology and antimicrobial-stewardship chapter of the kidneys ([[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]] and [[infectious-diseases/stewardship-outbreaks]]), and the urological-surgery chapter in miniature ([[surgery/vascular-urological-surgery]]).

This chapter covers urinary infection, stones, obstruction and retention, cystic kidney disease, and the urological cancers.

::: example The smelly urine memo {#ex-asb}
A care-home 84-year-old with dementia and a long-term catheter is referred for "dark, strong-smelling urine"; she is afebrile, alert at baseline, haemodynamically normal; urine dipstick: nitrite and leukocytes both strongly positive.
::: solution
**Asymptomatic bacteriuria in a catheterised patient — the do-not-treat index case ([[#def-uti]]): the malodour-and-colour-of-urine are not symptoms; the dysregulated baseline pyuria and nitrites of a colonised biofilm prove presence, not disease; and the treating-the-dipstick pathway buys resistance, C difficile and side-effects without benefit (the nursing-home-antibiotic-stewardship trials — [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]] and [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]]).** The right actions: **the hydration-and-catheter-and-bowel-and-perineal-care review (the blocked-and-encrusted-catheter and constipation-and-skin work — [[renal/urinary-tract]]'s own nursing-protocol), the observation-plan-and-family-reassurance documentation (the fever-or-function-change-when-to-review safety-net — [[clinical-skills/communication]]), the catheter-exchange-consideration-only-if-obstructed (the remove-don't-swap-if-unnecessary decision), and the care-home's standing-order audit (the 'smelly-urine-protocol' replacement — the antibiotic-misallocation KPI — [[public-health/health-systems-economics]] and [[infectious-diseases/stewardship-outbreaks]]). Where symptoms DO exist (fever, rigors, flank pain, delirium-with-other-cause-excluded), the pathway flips to the complicated-UTI algorithm — and the obstructed-septic branch to [[#ex-uro-sepsis]].**

::: summary
- UTI management is classification plus stewardship: treat symptomatic lower disease briefly, upper disease decisively with source control, and never treat asymptomatic bacteriuria outside pregnancy and pre-urological-surgery.
- An obstructed infected kidney is a drainage emergency measured in hours; stone disease otherwise runs on analgesia, expulsion and elective urology, with 24-hour urine metabolic prevention for repeaters.
- Obstruction hides: postrenal AKI, nocturia and "just old age" retention all demand a bladder scan and a prostate and drug review; decompress before the nephrons negotiate.
- Cysts are mostly benign ageing, except PKD — a systemic ciliopathy with aneurysm, liver and family implications and now a disease-modifying drug.
- The tract's cancers differ by lining: smoking-driven urothelial carcinoma (haematuria pathway), VHL-axis renal cell carcinoma (immunotherapy-targeted combinations), prostate cancer (risk-stratified surveillance) and testicular cancer (cured by platinum; bank sperm first).
:::
