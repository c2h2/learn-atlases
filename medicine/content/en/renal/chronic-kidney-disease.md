Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is the trajectory end of everything the previous six chapters began: **a progressive nephron-loss syndrome whose early stages are silent, whose complications touch every organ, whose cardiovascular mortality outruns its renal one, and whose final common pathway — dialysis or transplantation — is one of medicine's great (and unequal) technologies.** Attached here is the glomerular-disease atlas, because glomerulonephritis is both a cause of the trajectory and a set of acute presentations of its own.

This chapter covers CKD definition and progression, its systemic complications, dialysis, transplantation, and the glomerulonephritides.

## The trajectory

::: definition CKD: definition, causes and the progression engine {#def-ckd}
**CKD: structure or function abnormality of the kidney persisting ≥3 months, GFR <60 and/or increased albuminuria (the A1-A3 albuminuria bands of [[renal/filtration]]) with the G1-G5 × A1-A3 risk grid and cause-label directing the follow-up interval (the quarterly-to-annual grid) and referral triggers (the rapid-slope, the A3-albuminuria, the G4-onset, the haematuria-and-proteinuria, the resistant-hypertension and the hereditary-question lists — [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]'s own gateway to nephrology).** Aetiologies (population-graded): **the diabetes (the leading cause — its nephropathy story below and in [[endocrine/diabetes-complications]]), hypertension and vascular ([[cardiovascular/hypertension]]), the glomerulonephritides ([[#def-gn]]), obstructive uropathy and reflux ([[renal/urinary-tract]] and [[paediatrics/acute-childhood-illness]]'s vesicoureteral cohorts), cystic and genetic (the ADPKD — [[renal/urinary-tract]] — and the ciliopathies-and-Alport-and-mitochondrial of [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]] and [[paediatrics/congenital-genetic]]), the recurrent-pyelonephritic and analgesic ([[renal/urinary-tract]] and [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]), systemic disease ([[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]] and [[immunology/autoimmunity]]), and the undetermined (commonest in the elderly — the under-investigated small-kidney).** The progression engine (the "remnant-kidney" physiology of [[renal/filtration]]'s hyperfiltration): **nephron loss → adaptive afferent-relaxation and single-nephron hyperfiltration and proteinuria-selective size-shifting of the barrier ([[renal/filtration]]'s Kf-lesson and [[renal/tubular-function]]'s tubulointerstitial protein-handling) → podocyte stress, filtered-protein-induced tubular cytokine release ([[pathology/chronic-inflammation-repair]] and [[immunology/innate-immunity]]) and peritubular-capillary loss and interstitial fibrosis — a self-sustaining loop that explains why GFR decline accelerates after insult and why proteinuria, blood-pressure and the RAAS-and-SGLT2 axes are the progression levers.** Slowing it: **the blood-pressure targets (the treated-<130/80-era and the SPRINT-renal-subgroup and the 2017/2021-grids ([[cardiovascular/hypertension]]) with the postural-and-frailty caveat ([[geriatrics-palliative/falls]]); RAAS blockade-titrated-to-tolerated-dose with the potassium-and-creatinine-dip-acceptance rules ([[renal/potassium-calcium-phosphate]] and [[renal/filtration]]'s efferent-physiology); the SGLT2 inhibitors (the reduced-progression signal across diabetic-and-not and the expected initial-eGFR-dip-and-long-term-slope-benefit geometry ([[endocrine/diabetes-complications]]) — now the other pillar with the finerenone (the non-steroidal-MRA ([[cardiovascular/heart-failure]]-adjacent) and the GLP1-receptor-agonist kidney-outcome trials (the FLOW-era diabetes-kidney and retinopathy-caveat nuance — [[endocrine/diabetes]]); the lifestyle-and-risk bundle (salt-and-protein-amount-and-quality and smoking-cessation and vaccination and analgesic-avoidance ([[public-health/prevention-promotion]] and [[renal/filtration]]'s sick-day-rules); the anaemia-and-acidosis-and-mineral corrections below (each a slope-influencer); and the statin-and-cardiovascular-risk management (the [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]] and [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]-CKD-specific-lipid-therapy-continuation--versus-initiation-on-dialysis nuance).**
:::

## The atlas

::: definition Glomerulonephritis: the working atlas {#def-gn}
**Presentations: asymptomatic-urinary-abnormalities (the isolated-haematuria-and-mild-proteinuria pathway of [[renal/filtration]]'s urinalysis-chapter and [[renal/urinary-tract]]'s malignancy-rules), chronic-gradual-nephritic (the IgA-and-C3-and-others' slow-slide into [[#def-ckd]]), nephrotic ([[#ex-nephrotic]]'s route) and the nephritic-and-RPGN-emergency (the haematuria-and-proteinuria-and-hypertension-and-oliguria-and-creatinine-doubling-days-to-weeks — same-day-nephrology with the serology-biopsy-pulse-steroids-parity ([[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]'s red-cell-cast row)).** The common members (with [[immunology/hypersensitivity]]-and-[[immunology/autoimmunity]]-mechanism-tags): **IgA-nephropathy (the infection-synpharyngitic-haematuria and the galactose-deficient-IgA1-and-mucosal-immunity-pathogenesis ([[immunology/adaptive-responses]]) and the risk-stratified supportive-then-selective-immunosuppression strategy with the targeted-release-budesonide era-nuances) — the commonest-glomerulonephritis-worldwide with its ESRD-fraction; post-infectious (the low-complement-and-nephritic-child and the staph-IgA-dominant-adult-variant — [[respiratory/respiratory-infections]] and [[microbiology/bacteria]]); membranous (the anti-PLA2R-and-THSD7A-autoantibody-nephropathy and the secondary-forms (tumour-and-hepatitis-B-and-lupus-and-drugs ([[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]])) — [[#ex-nephrotic]]'s subject); minimal-change (the child's-nephrotic-default ([[paediatrics/acute-childhood-illness]]) and the adult-paraneoplastic-Hodgkin-association ([[blood/lymphoma-myeloma]]) — steroid-superresponsive-with-relapse-programmes); FSGS (the genetic-and-adaptive-hyperfiltration (obesity-and-solitary-kidney-and-reflux) and circulating-permeability-factor variants ([[renal/filtration]]'s-podocyte-biology) and its-transplant-recurrence ([[#def-tx]])); the lupus-nephritis (the class-by-class ISN-RPS-system and the mycophenolate-and-cyclophosphamide backbone with the voclosporin-and-obinutuzumab trial-era additions ([[immunology/autoimmunity]] and [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]]); the ANCA-vasculitides (the pauci-immune necrotising GN with pulmonary-capillaritis overlap — [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]] and [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]]) — the plasma-exchange-controversy-era and avacopan ([[immunology/innate-immunity]]-adjacent complement blockade) and the relapse-trial-tapering-rituximab-programmes); anti-GBM (the Goodpasture's — the pulmonary-haemorrhage-and-RPGN-and-plasma-exchange-emergency ([[immunology/hypersensitivity]]'s type-II) — [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]-adjacent-alveolar-bleed); C3-glomerulopathy-and-dense-deposit (the complement-dysregulation-rarities and the partial-lipodystrophy-associations ([[immunology/innate-immunity]] and the [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]]-of-orphan-drug-access)); IgA-vasculitis/HSP (the child's-purpura-and-abdominal-and-arthritis-and-renal-syndrome ([[paediatrics/acute-childhood-illness]] and [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]] and [[musculoskeletal/skin]]-purpura-read) — usually-self-limiting-with-serious-GI-and-renal-tracking); and the membranoproliferative-pattern (the hepatitis-C-cryoglobulinaemic and infection-associated and complement-mediated trimembers ([[infectious-diseases/hiv]]-adjacent-hepatitis-co-infection and the [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]]-cryoglobulinaemic-vasculitis) — biopsy-and-cause-treatment).** The unifying method: **the pattern-plus-serology-plus-biopsy-triple (light-and-immunofluorescence-and-electron ([[pathology/diagnostic-pathology]]) with the complement-and-autoantibody-and-infection-screens ([[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]) and the genetics-of-treatment-resistant-or-familial (the [[genetics/genomic-medicine]]-podocyte-gene-panels with their transplant-prognosis-payoff) and the risk-stratified-treatment (supportive-first-for-the-mild, immunosuppression-for-the-progressive-immune-subsets, and the clinical-trial-referral-culture).**
:::

::: history
**The dialysis-era began with Abel-and-Rowntree-and-Turnbull's 1913 animal haemodialysis and Kolff's 1943 war-time artificial kidney and the Alwall and Skeggs-and-Leonard refinements**; **the 1950s-60s chronic-haemodialysis-birth (the Scribner-and-Quiole Seattle shunt and the era's ethical-firsts — the first allocation-committee ("God committee") — [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]])**; **the peritoneal-dialysis lineage (the 1923 Ganter animal work, the Lawson-and-Keith intermittent era and the Popovich-and-Twardowski continuous ambulatory programmes)**; **the transplant-era (the 1902 Ullmann and Carrel vessel-technique prehistory, the 1936 Voronoy human attempt and the 1953 Medawar acquired-tolerance era and the 1954 Murray-and-Harrison identical-twin transplant, the 1958-60 azathioprine-era, the 1967-78 cyclosporine-revolution (the Calne-and-Starzl era), the 1984-89 tacrolimus and mycophenolate generations, and the modern desensitisation and tolerance-trial landscape ([[immunology/tolerance-regulation]]))**; **the glomerular-pathology-atlas (the 1950s electron-microscopy-era redivision of "chronic nephritis" into minimal-change-and-membranous-and-FSGS, the 1960s-70s post-infectious-complement and IgA-dominant (Berger's 1968) descriptions)**; **the anti-GBM era (the 1970s autoantibody characterisation and plasma-exchange trials)**; **the KDIGO-era (the 2012-GFR-and-albuminuria-grids, the 2021-and-onward-SGLT2-and-finerenone-and-GLP1-guideline-upheavals)**; and **the registries-demonstrating-the-outcomes (the USRDS-and-ERA-EDTA-and-UKRR-and-DOPPS) with their equity-findings ([[public-health/determinants-inequalities]])** complete this long chapter's archaeology.
:::

::: exercise Time the complications {level=2}
Month 2 post-transplant: which infections dominate? Month 8? Year 5? Give one non-infectious cause of graft- dysfunction per window.
::: solution
**Month ~1-3: the opportunist-prevention window (the surgical-wound-and-line infections and the reactivation-of-donor-or-recipient-herpes — CMV's classic window — the BK-virus nephropathy emerging, with the prophylaxis regimens doing their job ([[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]] and [[microbiology/viruses]]); month 3-6: the CMV-BK-and-Pneumocystis-if-prophylaxis-lapsed territory and the rejection-treatment-escalation-infections ([[immunology/transplant-tumour-immunology]]); beyond 6 months: the community-respiratory-and-HPV-and-EBV-and-skin-viral-tumour-long-tail (the [[musculoskeletal/skin-cancer]] and the post-transplant-lymphoproliferative-disorder ([[blood/lymphoma-myeloma]]-adjacent-EBV) and the virus-associated-Kaposi-and-cervical programmes ([[obstetrics-gynaecology/gynaecological-cancer]]-and-[[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]-adjacent).** Non-infectious graft causes per window: the early vascular-and-ureteric-surgical-complications (thrombosis-and-leak — [[surgery/vascular-urological-surgery]]), the middle rejection-and-CNI-toxicity-and-BK-triple ([[#ex-transplant]]), and the late chronic-allograft-injury-and-recurrent-primary-disease and malignancy-associated-nephropathy ([[#def-tx]]).**
:::
:::

::: summary
- CKD is nephron-loss physiology: hyperfiltration, proteinuria and tubulointerstitial fibrosis self-perpetuate the slope; blood pressure, RAAS blockade, SGLT2 inhibition, finerenone/GLP-1 layers and lifestyle measure the slope back.
- The complications are organ-wide — cardiovascular death dominates; anaemia, mineral-bone disorder, acidosis, hyperkalaemia, infection and drug toxicity each have their own protocol and their own trial history.
- Dialysis is a decision, not a switch: prepare at eGFR 15-20, preserve arm veins, choose modality with life-goals, and offer conservative management as a real pathway.
- Transplantation is the survival-optimal therapy; rejection and BK/CMV collide, biopsy decides, and the long follow-up is an immunology-and-oncology-and-pregnancy programme.
- The glomerular atlas (IgA, membranous, minimal-change, FSGS, lupus, ANCA, anti-GBM, C3) is pattern-plus-serology-plus-biopsy medicine; treat the mechanism, not the oedema.
:::

::: exercise Grid the patient {level=1}

**G3b-A2 (the moderate-risk cell of the grid, escalating toward high with the diabetes-cause and slope; the 6-12-month intervals typical, 6 if slope-steep — [[#def-ckd]]'s referral-and-frequency triggers); progression-risk statements: the A2-vs-A1 relative-slope and mortality gradient, and the [[renal/filtration]]'s hyperbola caution (small creatinine changes hide large GFR ones on this part of the curve) — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]].**
