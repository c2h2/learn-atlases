Three ions, one regulatory theme: **potassium sets the excitable-membrane threshold (and its extremes stop the heart and the diaphragm), calcium sets contractile and secretory excitability (and is guarded by the parathyroid–vitamin-D–FGF23 triangle of [[endocrine/bone-calcium]]), and phosphate pays for everything (ATP) while pairing with calcium through solubility.** The kidney is the final arbiter of all three, and the disturbances are among the commonest and most rapidly lethal of laboratory emergencies — read here with their physiology, their ECGs ([[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]]) and their hour-by-hour management.

This chapter covers potassium balance and its emergencies, calcium and phosphate regulation, and the dysregulated-ion syndromes.

## Potassium

::: definition Potassium homeostasis: distribution then excretion {#def-k}
**Total-body potassium (~50 mmol/kg) is ~98 % intracellular (the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase and the K⁺-leak conductances maintaining the high [K⁺]ᵢ against the [[physiology/membrane-potentials]] gradient — so serum K⁺ reports distribution as much as balance: the acutely-raised serum may coexist with total-body depletion (the diabetic-ketoacidosis and diuretic states) and the low serum may hide normal or increased body potassium (the alkalosis and refeeding shifts)).** Acute distribution is governed by **insulin and β₂-catecholamines (Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase upregulation — the basis of the emergency treatments of [[#ex-hyperk]] and the hypokalaemia of the post-hypoglycaemic and tocolytic states ([[endocrine/diabetes-complications]])), pH (mineral-acidosis and the chloride-depletion-alkalosis shifts move potassium in exchange for H⁺ handling ([[renal/acid-base-disorders]]), while organic acidoses barely shift it), osmolality (solvent drag — the hypertonic rise) and cell proliferation or lysis (the leucocytosis-and-haemolysis pseudo-hyperkalaemia and the refeeding-and-insulin-and-β-agonist "refeeding-hypokalaemia" of [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]).** Excretion: **~90 % renal, and the distal nephron is the regulated gate (proximal and loop reabsorption is bulk and relatively fixed — [[renal/tubular-function]]): principal-cell secretion follows (i) luminal-negative electrogenicity from ENaC sodium entry (aldosterone-tuned — [[renal/water-sodium]]'s axis and the Liddle-and-AME-and-licorice-and-apparent-mineralocorticoid-excess hypertensive-hypokalaemias), (ii) flow and distal sodium delivery (the diuretic and post-obstructive and polyuric-phase potassium wasting), (iii) the ROMK-and-BK channel conductances (_flow_-activated BK in the high-flow states; ROMK's recycling role and its potassium-permissiveness — [[renal/tubular-function]]'s ascending-limb physiology), and (iv) plasma potassium itself (a direct effect on the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase and aldosterone — the potassium-aldosterone feedback loop ([[endocrine/adrenal]])); intercalated-cell H⁺/K⁺-ATPase reabsorbs in depletion (the hypokalaemia-maintained paradoxical-aciduria of [[renal/acid-base-disorders]]); colonic secretion (the aldosterone-sensitive BK component — the CKD-colon adaptation of [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]).**
:::

::: definition Hyperkalaemia {#def-hyperk}
**Serum K⁺ >5.5 mmol/L (moderate 6.0-6.4, severe ≥6.5 or with ECG change — the thresholds that trigger the emergency pathway): first exclude pseudo-hyperkalaemia (the fist-and-tourniquet and haemolysed-specimen and thrombocytosis-released potassium — the repeat-free-flow-sample rule ([[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]])).** Aetiologies in three frames: **reduced excretion (the big one: AKI and CKD — [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]] and [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]; hypoaldosteronism states — the Addisonian crisis ([[endocrine/adrenal]] and [[endocrine/endocrine-emergencies]]), the type-4 (hyporeninemic-hypoaldosterone) tubulointerstitial-and-diabetic nephropathy ([[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]) and the drug-blockade (the ACEi-and-ARB-and-spironolactone-eplerenone-and-amiloride-triamterene list ([[cardiovascular/heart-failure]]) plus the NSAID-and-heparin-and-ciclosporin-tacrolimus-and-co-trimoxazole-pentamidine (the amiloride-mimetic trimethoprim) and the potassium-containing-substitutes-and-herbal-remedies ([[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]))); increased load (the transfusion-and-sucrose-and-ringers-injury-fluids ([[blood/transfusion]]) and potassium-penicillin and salt-substitute and iatrogenic-infusion-errors ([[clinical-skills/patient-safety]])); transcellular shift (exercise and the α-agonist-and-β-blocker-and-digoxin-overdose-and-suxamethonium-and-depolarising-paralysis states ([[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]] and [[surgery/anaesthesia-pain]]) and acidosis and rhabdomyolysis and tumour-lysis ([[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]]-adjacent oncology) and the neonatal-and-family-channelopathies ([[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]] — the hyperkalaemic-periodic-paralyses)).** Manifestations are neuromuscular (the ascending weakness and flaccid paralysis mimicking Guillain-Barré ([[nervous-system/neuromuscular]]) and the palpitations) and cardiac: **the ECG ladder (peaked-tall-narrow T waves → flattened-P and PR-lengthening → QRS-widening → sine-wave → arrest — [[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]]'s repolarisation-and-conduction physiology) — noting the ECG-lags-serum caveat and worse-in-hyponatraemia-and-hypocalcaemia-acidosis-cofactor rule.** Management on three clocks: **(1) membrane-stabilise NOW if ECG-change or K⁺ ≥6.5 (the IV calcium — gluconate-peripheral-versus-chloride-central and the digoxin-calcium-caution (and its evidence-archaeology — [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]]-adjacent digoxin-specific-Fab-for-the-toxicity) — repeated on persisting traces ([[emergency-critical-care/resuscitation]] and [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]])); (2) redistribute in minutes (the insulin-10-12-u-with-50-g-dextrose-IV — the glucose-monitoring-hourly-for-6-hours rule ([[endocrine/diabetes]]), the nebulised-salbutamol-high-dose-additive (4-8× the bronchodilator dose — [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]) ± the bicarbonate-only-if-acidotic and the lipid-dialysis-augmenting nuance; (3) remove for real (the loop-if-perfused-kidney, the potassium-binder chapter — the polystyrene-sulphonates' colonic-necrosis caution and legacy evidence, and the modern patiromer-and-sodium-zirconium-cyclosilicate trials (with the cirrhotic-and-hypertensive sodium-load caveat) ([[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]] and [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]]-adjacent) — and the haemodialysis-decision (the refractory-with-ECG-and-renal-failure-indication (the ARDS-renal and iRAI-time-to-clear numbers) — [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]'s dialysis-modalities, and the emergency-threshold arithmetic ([[renal/acute-kidney-injury]])). Prevention: the sick-day-rules and drug-reviews and diet-counselling of the at-risk (the [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]], [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]] and [[endocrine/adrenal]] follow-up templates).**
:::

::: example The peaked T waves {#ex-hyperk}
An anuric dialysis-patient-delayed (missed two sessions, started trimethoprim for cystitis, eating "low-salt" substitute) has K⁺ 7.4, wide QRS with peaked Ts; BP and calcium normal; on amlodipine, ramipril and calcium-acetate.
::: solution
**True symptomatic-risk severe hyperkalaemia (the triple-hit: no excretion plus the trimethoprim-ENaC-block plus the ACEi plus the potassium-substitute load — [[#def-hyperk]]'s frames in one patient).** The hour-0 bundle: **IV calcium (the membrane-antagonism — repeats on the ECG-trace as the guide), IV insulin-with-dextrose (the hourly-glucose-monitoring rule — [[endocrine/diabetes]]), the high-dose-nebulised-salbutamol addition (with [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]'s caveat) and bicarbonate only if acidotic ([[renal/acid-base-disorders]]); stop-and-document (trimethoprim-switch, ACEi-hold (reassess-and-restart-plan — [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]]'s re-challenge logic), salt-substitute confiscation and diet-education ([[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]])); get the dialysis moving (the emergent-haemodialysis-with-the-catheter-access-and-ECG-continuous-monitor and the potassium-not-the-potassium-level-guiding-endpoint ([[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]])); and the 24-hour tail (the rebound-monitoring through the shift-therapies'-wearing-off, the binder-role (the SPS-era caution versus the modern-binders evidence — [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]'s potassium program), the cystitis's own management ([[renal/urinary-tract]]) and the missed-sessions-root-cause (the transport-and-depression-and-fistula problems — [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]]-adjacent social-work ([[public-health/health-systems-economics]])).**
:::

::: definition Hypokalaemia {#def-hypok}
**Serum K⁺ <3.5 (severe <2.5 — the arrhythmia-and-respiratory-failure band): decide depletion-versus-shift first. Shifts: the β-agonist-and-insulin-and-theophylline-and-alkalosis (the ventilator-induced-renal-and-respiratory-alkalosis combination ([[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]] and [[renal/acid-base-disorders]])) and the thyrotoxic-periodic-paralysis and familial-hypokalaemic-periodic-paralysis channelopathies ([[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]], [[endocrine/thyroid]] and [[nervous-system/neuromuscular]]) — where total-body potassium is normal and re-distribution overshoot punishes aggressive replacement ([[#ex-thyrotoxic-pp]]). Depletion with renal or gut losses: **gut (vomiting-and-ng-suction (the chloride-depletion-alkalosis with secondary hyperaldosteronism — urine chloride low — [[renal/acid-base-disorders]]) and diarrhoea-and-laxative-and-villous-adenoma secretory potassium-and-bicarbonate losses ([[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]] — and the hidden-eating-and-laxative-and-diuretic-disorder differential ([[psychiatry/eating-personality-disorders]])); renal (the blood-pressure-and-acid-base taxonomy ([[#ex-hypok-workup]]): normotensive-acidotic — RTA-1-and-2 ([[renal/acid-base-disorders]]) and the diuretic-recovery; normotensive-alkalotic-with-hypertension-absent — Bartter-and-Gitelman-and-diuretic-use ([[renal/tubular-function]]); hypertensive-alkalotic-with-suppressed-renin — the primary-aldosteronism-screen-and-its-confirmation-aldosteronism ([[endocrine/adrenal]] and [[cardiovascular/hypertension]]) and Liddle-and-AME-and-licorice; high-renin — the renovascular and renin-secreting rarities ([[renal/urinary-tract]]-adjacent); plus the Mg-depletion-perpetuating-hypokalaemia (the ROMK-disinhibition lesson ([[renal/tubular-function]]) — the alcoholism-and-PPI-and-cisplatin-and-diuretic-magnesuries ([[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]] and [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]])) and the hypocalcaemia-hypomagnesaemia co-derangements ([[#def-ca]]).** Manifestations: **muscle (weakness-and-cramps-and-rhabdomyolysis ([[renal/acute-kidney-injury]])-and-ileus ([[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]])-and-respiratory-failure ([[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]-adjacent diaphragm weakness), the nephrogenic-diabetes-insipidus-and-concentrating-defect ([[renal/tubular-function]]), the ECG (flattened-T-and-U-waves-and-ST-depression-and-QT-prolongation-and-torsades-risk ([[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]]) — worse with hypomagnesaemia and the QT-drug-list ([[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]] and [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]'s antipsychotic-QT ledger)).** Management: **the rate-and-route discipline (the oral-first rule for the non-emergency — the liquid-and-tablet-slow-release trade-offs; the peripheral-vs-central IV limits and the ECG-monitor-and-cause-treat rule for the severe-with-ECG-or-paralysis; the Mg-repletion-first-or-together ([[#def-mg]]) principle; the potassium-sparing-agent (spironolactone-or-amiloride) chronic-strategies by mechanism ([[cardiovascular/heart-failure]] and [[endocrine/adrenal]])) and the cautionary tales: the refeeding-and-insulin-drip-and-β-agonist-overshoot settings where potassium plummets (the [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]] refeeding-protocol and the DKA-insulin-potassium-replacement rule ([[endocrine/diabetes-complications]]-[[endocrine/diabetes]]) — replacement-without-overshoot is the chapter's dosing art).**
:::

::: example The low potassium that would not correct {#ex-hypok-workup}
A 46-year-old woman with "refractory hypokalaemia" (K⁺ 2.7 despite 80 mmol/day oral) has BP 118/74, bicarbonate 31, magnesium 0.48 mmol/L (low), urine potassium 62 mmol/L and urine calcium low-normal; she takes omeprazole and bendroflumethiazide and denies other medication.
::: solution
**Renal-potassium-wasting-with-metabolic-alkalosis-and-hypomagnesaemia in a normotensive: the differential (the [[#def-hypok]] taxonomy): thiazide-action (the NCC-block — the Gitelman-phenocopy ([[renal/tubular-function]])), Gitelman-syndrome, surreptitious-loop-use, PPI-hypomagnesaemia (the TRPM6-and-paracellular-Mg-absorption lesion — [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]-and-[[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]), and the low-urine-calcium pointing away from a pure-loop-pattern and toward Gitelman-or-thiazide.** Work-up and moves: **repeat magnesium-and-potassium-and-bicarbonate-and-creatinine, check urine chloride (the >40 with active-diuretic-effect or vomiting-with-bicarbonuria trap and the metabolic-alkalosis urine-chloride algorithm ([[renal/acid-base-disorders]])), renin-aldosterone (the secondary-pattern expected, versus the suppressed-renin-aldosteronism-masquerade ([[endocrine/adrenal]])), spot urine calcium-to-creatinine ratio (the Gitelman-versus-Bartter discriminator — [[renal/tubular-function]]) and, in adults with unexplained phenotypes, the SLC12A3 (NCC) gene panel or research-panel ([[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]])).** Treatment: **replete magnesium first-or-together (the ROMK-disinhibition mechanism ([[#def-mg]]): potassium cannot be held while Mg is low; the oral-Mg-salts-versus-IV trade (the diarrhoea dose-limit) and the PPI-review-and-switch decision ([[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]])), stop-or-move-off the thiazide (the alternative-antihypertensive plan ([[cardiovascular/hypertension]])), the potassium-sparing (amiloride-or-spiro) add-on (both raise Mg retention as a bonus ([[renal/tubular-function]])), the hidden-diuretic conversation if the screen is positive ([[psychiatry/eating-personality-disorders]] and [[clinical-skills/communication]]), and the follow-up trajectory-chart ([[clinical-skills/records-handover]]).**
:::

## Calcium, magnesium and phosphate

::: definition Calcium: regulation, excess and deficit {#def-ca}
**Total serum calcium must be albumin-corrected (the +0.02 mmol/L per g/L rule) or the ionized fraction measured (the acidosis-albumin-binding and citrate-transfusion ([[blood/transfusion]]) and hyperglobulinaemic ([[blood/lymphoma-myeloma]]) distortions); the regulated-ionised calcium obeys the PTH-vitamin-D-[[endocrine/bone-calcium]] triangle acting on the three effectors — bone, gut and kidney (the distal-tubule calcium reabsorption ([[renal/tubular-function]]) and the FGF23-and-phosphate interface ([[#def-mg]])).** **Hypercalcaemia: the two-big-epidemiologies (the primary-hyperparathyroidism-of-outpatient-incidental-calcium ([[endocrine/bone-calcium]]'s surgery-criteria and the malignancy inpatient — the PTHrP-humoral (the squamous and renal and bladder and breast), the osteolytic-with-low-PTHrP (myeloma and breast — [[blood/lymphoma-myeloma]]) and the rare 1,25-D (lymphoma) and ectopic-PTH (tumour) and the lithium-and-thiazide-and-vitamin-A-and-milk-alkali-and-granulomatous (the sarcoid ([[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]'s calcium example) and thyrotoxic ([[endocrine/thyroid]]) and adrenal-crisis ([[endocrine/adrenal]]) additions — the calcium-of-endocrine-disease cluster).** Symptoms (the bones-stones-abdominal-groans-psychiatric-overtones-and-polyuria-Starling-oedema-risk reading — the nephrogenic-diabetes-insipidus and the pancreatitis ([[gastrointestinal/pancreas-biliary]]) and peptic ([[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]]) and short-QT-([[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]]) features) — treatment: **the saline-first-volume-repletion (the litres-per-day and the cardiac-[[cardiovascular/heart-failure]]-caution) then the loop-diuretic-with-caution-role-and-the-avoid-corticosteroid-mimicker-thiazides, the IV bisphosphonate-or-denosumab (the osteoclast-window and hypocalcaemia-delay and the [[endocrine/bone-calcium]] oncology-dose schedules — the dental-onset osteonecrosis ledger ([[musculoskeletal/bones-joints]]-adjacent)), the calcitonin-tachyphylaxis-bridge, the glucocorticoids for the 1,25-D-mediated lymphomatous, granulomatous and myeloma mechanisms ([[blood/lymphoma-myeloma]]), the dialysis-for-the-oliguric-or-severe ([[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]) and the cause-definitive treatment (the parathyroidectomy-and-tumour pathways — [[endocrine/bone-calcium]] and [[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]]). **Hypocalcaemia: the surgical-hypoparathyroidism (the transient-and-permanent-post-thyroid-and-parathyroid ([[endocrine/bone-calcium]] and [[surgery/breast-endocrine-surgery]]) and the hungry-bone-post-parathyroidectomy ([[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]-adjacent), the vitamin-D deficiency-and-malabsorption ([[gastrointestinal/digestion-absorption]]), the CKD-secondary-hyperparathyroidism ([[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]), the hypomagnesaemia-induced-PTH-resistance-and-secretion-failure ([[#def-mg]]) and the citrate-chelation and pancreatitis-and-rhabdomyolysis-deposition sinks ([[gastrointestinal/pancreas-biliary]] and [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]). Symptoms: perioral-and-digit tingling (care: not cardiac — [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]]'s hyperventilation-mimic), the carpopedal-spasm and Chvostek-and-Trousseau and laryngospasm-and-stridor and seizures ([[nervous-system/epilepsy]]-differential) and the long-QT ([[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]]); treatment: **IV-calcium-gluconate (the central-line-preference-and-ECG-monitoring-and-extravasation-necrosis caution — [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]) for the severe-and-ECG-and-laryngospasm, the oral-calcium-and-calcitriol (the PTH-absent need for the activated-vitamin-D ([[endocrine/bone-calcium]])) and the magnesium-repletion-first rule and the calcium-salt chemistry (the carbonate-versus-citrate-and-acid-suppression choice — [[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]]).**
:::

::: definition Magnesium and phosphate {#def-mg}
**Magnesium (the forgotten cation — [[physiology/membrane-potentials]]' channel-plugging co-factor and the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase-and-PTH-secretion-and-insulin-signalling co-factor — [[cell-biochemistry/proteins-enzymes]]): regulated by the TAL-paracellular-and-DCT-TRPM6 machinery ([[renal/tubular-function]]) — hence the renal-wasting phenotypes (the alcoholism-and-diuretics-and-cisplatin-and-ciclosporin-and-aminoglycoside-and-amphotericin-and-PPI-and-EGFR-inhibitor list — [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]) and the Gitelman-and-HNF1B-and-TRPM6-EGF-family rare-diseases; consequences of depletion: refractory hypokalaemia (the ROMK-unplugging — [[#ex-hypok-workup]]) and hypocalcaemia (the PTH-resistance-and-secretion-block — [[#def-ca]]), the neuromuscular-irritability-and-tremor-and-tetany-and-seizures ([[nervous-system/epilepsy]]-adjacent) and the ECG (the torsades-predisposition — [[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]] — hence the Mg-as-torsades-treatment-and-obstetric-eclampsia-therapy ([[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]]) and the neonatal-hypomagnesaemic-seizures); treatment: **the IV-or-oral-Mg-sul-fate (the diarrhoea-and-renal-dosing cautions) and the repletion-monitoring ([[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]) and the alcoholism-and-refeeding protocols ([[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]] and [[psychiatry/substance-use]]).** **Phosphate: the intracellular-anion-and-ATP-and-2,3-DPG-and-hydroxyapatite-mineral ([[cell-biochemistry/energy-metabolism]] and [[endocrine/bone-calcium]]); regulated by the PTH-and-FGF23(Klotho)-driven NaPi-IIa/IIc internalisation in the proximal tubule ([[renal/tubular-function]], [[endocrine/bone-calcium]] and [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]'s XLH-and-tumoral-osteomalacia-axis).** **Hypophosphataemia: the shift-causes (refeeding-carbohydrate-and-insulin ([[#ex-refeed]]) and the respiratory-alkalosis ([[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]]-weaning-attempt-hyperventilation) and the β-agonists and glucose-load) and the depletion-causes (the antacid-and-binder-phosphate-trapping and the hyperparathyroidism-and-FGF23-excess states); severe (<0.3 mmol/L) effects: the ATP-depletion muscle-and-respiratory-pump failure (weaning-failure and rhabdomyolysis ([[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]-adjacent) and haemolysis ([[blood/haemoglobin-disorders]]-adjacent ATP-defect) and the paradoxical-2,3-DPG-shift ([[blood/haemoglobin-disorders]]'s oxygen-dissociation physiology), leukocyte-dysfunction ([[immunology/innate-immunity]]), the osteomalacia and the rickets of the FGF23-driven hypophosphatemia disorders (the X-linked hypophosphataemia and tumour-induced variants) ([[endocrine/bone-calcium]] and [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]); treatment: the oral-first-because-IV-calcium-phosphate-precipitation-and-hypocalcaemia-and-arrest-caution (and the severe-with-symptoms-monitored-IV-protocol — [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]] and [[endocrine/bone-calcium]]). **Hyperphosphataemia: the CKD-retention-and-secondary-hyperparathyroidism-and-vascular-calcification axis ([[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]'s bone-mineral-and-cardiovascular section), the acute-rise-with-hypocalcaemia (the tumour-lysis ([[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]]-adjacent oncology) and rhabdomyolysis and vitamin-D-intoxication and the phosphate-enema-accident ([[clinical-skills/patient-safety]])) — treated by the binders-with-meals and the diet-phosphate-restriction (the additive-and-processing-food-reading lesson) and dialysis ([[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]).**
:::

::: example The refeeding collapse {#ex-refeed}
A 34-year-old with anorexia nervosa (BMI 13) is admitted and started on 1800 kcal/day feeding with dextrose-containing fluids; day 3 she is too weak to lift her head, weaning from spontaneous breathing is failing, K⁺ 3.0, phosphate 0.22, magnesium 0.5, calcium normal.
::: solution
**Refeeding syndrome ([[#def-mg]] and [[#def-mg]]'s most complete illustration): the carbohydrate-restore-insulin-surge drives phosphate-into-cells (the ATP-synthesis-hunger) with potassium and magnesium and water retention and the [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]-and-[[endocrine/diabetes-complications]]-shared-shift-physiology, in a patient whose total-body stores are already depleted — the severe-hypophosphataemia (<0.3) explains the respiratory-pump (the weaning-failure — [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]-adjacent diaphragmatic ATP-depletion ([[cell-biochemistry/energy-metabolism]])) and the neck-flexor weakness, the hypokalaemia-and-hypomagnesaemia the arrhythmia-risk ([[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]]) and the oedema the sodium-retention rebound ([[renal/water-sodium]]'s insulin-renal-axis).** Management: **pause-or-slow-the-feeding (the calories-back-up rule) while repleting IV-phosphate (the monitored-protocol and the calcium-phosphate-solubility and hypocalcaemia cautions ([[endocrine/bone-calcium]])), IV magnesium and potassium (the Mg-first ([[#def-mg]]) rule), stop the dextrose-fluids (the fuel-on-the-fire correction — [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]]), the daily-(twice-daily-in-the-risk-band) electrolytes for the first 7-10 refeeding-days, the cardiac-monitoring-and-thiamine-before-sugar ([[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]] and [[psychiatry/substance-use]]'s shared-alcohol-cohort lesson and [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]]'s Wernicke's (thiamine's own story sits in [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]])), and the psychiatry-shared-nutrition-rehabilitation plan with slow-start-and-monitor-feed-up protocols (the refeeding-guideline calorie-ramps and the nasogastric-versus-oral decisions ([[psychiatry/eating-personality-disorders]]) and the capacity-and-coercion-law questions when refeeding is life-saving ([[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]] and [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]])).**

::: example The sleepy Asian man with weak legs {#ex-thyrotoxic-pp}
A 28-year-old Chinese man is found unable to stand at 4 am; K⁺ 1.6, pulse 118 irregularly irregular, BP 148/70, warm peripheries and lid retraction; ECG: U waves and short runs of atrial fibrillation; he takes no drugs.
::: solution
**Thyrotoxic periodic paralysis (the [[#def-hypok]] shift-phenotype: thyroid-hormone-upregulated Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase plus the β-adrenergic-and-insulin (post-carbohydrate-load) drive plus (in the KCNJ18-Kir2.6-linked familial form — [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]] and [[endocrine/thyroid]]) the channel-sensitivity — the young-Asian-male predilection and the precipitant (large meal-and-rest-after-exercise-and-stress); the paralysis is proximal-and ascending with areflexia sparing respiration usually ([[nervous-system/motor-systems]]'s pattern-recognition) and the thyrotoxicosis-with-atrial-fibrillation ([[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]]) signs give the diagnosis)** — distinguished from the true-depletion states by the total-body-potassium-normality and the post-shift-rebound-hyperkalaemia risk. Management: **the non-selective β-blockade (the propranolol-high-dose — the mechanism-targeted therapy reversing the pump-drive — with the airway-caution ([[respiratory/asthma-copd]] and [[endocrine/thyroid]]'s thyroid-storm-package (the [[endocrine/endocrine-emergencies]]'s iodide-and-steroid-and-carbimazole regimen) — this patient's fibrillation-and-weakness is a storm-spectrum presentation requiring admission-monitoring)), cautious potassium replacement (the small-doses-with-frequent-monitoring-and-the-stop-when-rising rule — the overshoot-hyperkalaemia is the classic iatrogenic harm ([[#def-hyperk]])), the cardiac-monitoring for the hypokalaemia-and-thyrotoxic-arrhythmia synergy ([[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]]), the K-sparing spironolactone-or-amiloride-prophylaxis-between-definitive-therapy (the radioactive-iodine-versus-thionamide-versus-surgery decisions ([[endocrine/thyroid]]) and the avoidance-list (the big-carbohydrate-meals-and-sedatives-and-β-agonists) teaching ([[clinical-skills/communication]])).**

::: history
**Potassium-and-the-heart grew from Ringer's and the early-1900s saline-perfusion and the Katzung and the "potassium-arrest" physiology ([[physiology/membrane-potentials]]-adjacent), the intra-war insulin-shock-psychiatry's hypokalaemic deaths and the 1950s digitalis-potassium-interactions** ([[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]]'s digitalis era); **Bartter (1962) and Gitelman (1966) and their 1990s-cloning reunion ([[renal/tubular-function]])**; **the primary-aldosteronism renaissance (the Conn 1955 adenoma-description, the later renin-aldosterone-index screening-era (1990s-2000s) — [[endocrine/adrenal]])**; **the thyrotoxic-periodic-paralysis gene-era (the 1958 clinical-clan-studies and the 2010 KCNJ18 finding — [[endocrine/thyroid]])**; **calcium:** the **iPTH-radioimmunoassay of the 1970s** and **the PTHrP-purification of the 1987-88 era ([[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]])** and **the CaSR-cloning (the 1993 Brown-laboratory era — the familial-hypocalcemic-hypercalciuria-and-Bartter-III genetics [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]])**; **magnesium:** the **mid-century alcoholism-and-tetany pathology and the Shils-refeeding-and-magnesium-repletion trials** and **the 1995-2000 TRPM6/claudin-Mg-transport discoveries ([[renal/tubular-function]])**; **phosphate:** the **FGF23-series (the 2000-rickets-gene-candidate and the Klotho-co-factor (2003-06) — [[endocrine/bone-calcium]])** and **the refeeding-syndrome-campaign (the 1940s-after-famine descriptions, and the NICE-era refeeding-protocols — [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]])**; the **modern-potassium-binder era (the 2014-15 patiromer-and-SZC trials and the return-of-the-binders-to-guidelines)** completes the ion-medicine history.
:::

::: summary
- Serum potassium reports distribution plus balance: shifts (insulin, β-agonists, pH, osmolality, lysis) confuse the reading, and the distal nephron (ENaC electrogenicity, flow, ROMK/BK, aldosterone) decides excretion.
- Hyperkalaemia is managed on three clocks — calcium to stabilise, insulin-and-β-agonists to hide, diuretics-binders-dialysis to remove — with the ECG ladder and co-factor thresholds guiding urgency.
- Hypokalaemia work-up is a blood-pressure-and-acid-base-and-urine-chloride-and-calcium tree; magnesium must be repleted or potassium never corrects; shift-hypokalaemias (thyrotoxic periodic paralysis) are treated with β-blockade and restraint, not aggressive potassium.
- Calcium disorders are albumin-corrected, PTH-differentiated emergencies: saline-plus-osteoclast-inhibition for hypercalcaemia, IV-then-oral calcium with magnesium-and-vitamin-D logic for hypocalcaemia.
- Phosphate is the refeeding ion: severe hypophosphataemia paralyses the respiratory pump and haemolyzes; refeed slowly, watch day 3, replace phosphate orally when you can.
:::

::: exercise Prevent the rebound {level=2}
