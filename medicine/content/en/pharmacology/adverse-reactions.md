Adverse drug reactions are dose-independent or dose-dependent harm done by necessary pharmacology. **The epidemiology is humbling: reactions cause a significant minority of admissions, harm concentrates in the old, the very sick and the over-medicated, and most serious reactions are predictable from the drug's mechanism, the patient's genetics and organ function, or the interaction list.** ADRs divide into augmented pharmacological effects (type A, common, preventable), idiosyncratic reactions (type B, uncommon, mysterious until immunology or genetics explains them), and the long-latency categories - carcinogenesis, teratogenesis - covered in their own chapters. This chapter organises the chaos: classification, mechanisms, specific syndromes, detection systems, and the causality reasoning that turns a temporal coincidence into a diagnosis.

## Classification and mechanisms

::: definition Type A, type B and the alphabet extensions {#def-adr-classes}
**Type A (augmented): the pharmacology turned up too high - beta-blocker bradycardia, insulin hypoglycaemia, anticoagulant bleeding, antimuscarinic confusion - common, dose-related, reversible, and the target of nearly every preventable-harm programme (the dose-error, interaction, and organ-function-failure pathways covered in the previous chapters, with the narrow-index list as the ward's risk register) - [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]], [[pharmacology/patient-variation]]; type B (bizarre): dose-unrelated host responses - immunological (drug hypersensitivity syndromes, serum-sickness-like reactions, drug-induced lupus with its anti-histone agents and the biologics' own autoimmunity paradoxes), and non-immunological idiosyncrasy explained increasingly by toxic metabolite handling and mitochondrial and enzyme polymorphisms the halothane hepatitis as the first molecularly understood idiosyncratic reaction - oxidative metabolism to trifluoroacetylated protein adducts - and the frameworks of the 1990s toxicology school: the acquired-drug-metabolism hypothesis and the minor-pathway-to-reactive-intermediates idea that made bioactivation a routine drug-development question - [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], [[immunology/hypersensitivity]]; the extended alphabet used in bulletins - type C (chronic: inhaled corticosteroid growth and bone effects, tardive dyskinesia, analgesic nephropathy), type D (delayed: teratogenesis with its trimester-specific windows and the thalidomide legacy covered in embryology and obstetrics, carcinogenesis with its long latencies), type F (family: pharmacogenetic - the succinylcholine apnoea, the malignant-hyperthermia susceptibility, the G6PD and haemolysis list, the HLA-linked severe cutaneous reactions which are pharmacogenetics wearing an immunology coat), and the withdrawn type E (withdrawal: opioids, benzodiazepines, beta-blocker rebound, steroid crisis) whose phenomena are so clinically important they survive under other names (the discontinuation syndromes that ADR reporting systems miss because 'no drug' seems like no cause) - [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]], [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; and the vocabulary of harm that registries and labels use - side effect (expected, often trivial), adverse effect (harmful), adverse reaction (no reasonable alternative to the drug as cause), adverse event (anything timed to the drug), serious (death, hospitalisation, disability, congenital anomaly, needed intervention), sentinel and never-events - the language determining whether a signal is ever seen, because a system that only counts litigation-grade harm learns least - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
:::

## The big syndromes

::: definition Severe cutaneous and systemic hypersensitivity {#def-scs}
**The severe cutaneous adverse reactions are a clinical-pathological family with shared immunology and different faces: maculopapular exanthem is common, benign and self-limiting (the viral-versus-drug rash in children and the amoxicillin-in-infectious-mononucleosis classic); urticaria and angioedema are mast-cell diseases with the ACE-inhibitor bradykinin variant that does not itch, does not respond to antihistamines, and can present months into therapy; and the severe quartet - Stevens-Johnson syndrome and toxic epidermal necrolysis (keratinocyte apoptosis with detachment, mucositis and the SCORTEN severity score, drugs causing the overwhelming majority, with the culprit usually started in the preceding one-three months), DRESS with its eosinophilia, organ involvement, herpesvirus reactivation biology and delayed-and-fluctuating course, and acute generalised exanthematous pustulosis with its rapid fever-pustule-neutrophilia pattern after antibiotics - [[musculoskeletal/skin]]; the HLA pharmacogenetics that turned these from lottery to prediction - HLA-B*5701 and abacavir with the mandatory-testing policy that eliminated clinically relevant hypersensitivity, HLA-B*1502 and carbamazepine and lamotrigine-associated risk in Southeast Asian and Han Chinese populations, HLA-B*5801 and allopurinol with screening recommendations in at-risk groups, HLA-A*3101 and carbamazepine cutaneous reactions across broader ancestries, HLA-B*35:01 and the flucloxacillin jaundice signal and the 21-month-latency case-control oddity, plus the hapten, p-i (pharmacological interaction with immune receptors) and altered-repertoire models explaining how a small molecule re-educates T cells - [[pharmacology/patient-variation]], [[immunology/adaptive-responses]], [[genetics/genetic-testing]]; the other systemic syndromes - drug-induced lupus (the anti-histone hydrazi-nitrofurantoin-minocycline-hydralazine-procainamide landscape and anti-TNF lupus with its different antibody texture and the arthralgia-serositis presentation and the weeks-to-months resolution after stopping), serum sickness and serum-sickness-like reactions (the cefaclor and monoclonal chimeric-agent patterns with complement consumption and the type-III mechanics), drug reaction with eosinophilia's organ loop (the liver-kidney-lung-thyroid involvement and the steroid-steroid-withdrawal-rebound management debates), vasculitis (the ANCA patterns with propylthiouracil and levamisole-adulterated-cocaine mimicking pauci-immune disease), and the drug-fever diagnosis of exclusion with its relative bradycardia lore, its weekend-off patterns and its rechallenge confirmation ethics - [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]], [[immunology/autoimmunity]], [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]]; and the management spine shared by all of them - stop the culprit early (the survival data for early withdrawal in SJS-TEN and the list of the usual suspects by timeline), supportive burn-unit and intensive-care management, biopsy and immunofluorescence where the differential is unclear, systemic therapies with their contested evidence, the documentation of the reaction with severity and date so the future-prescribing is safe, the patient-held record and allergy-label discipline (the penicillin-label audit findings that most labels are incorrect yet rechallenge is rarely done, and the C. difficile and surgery and cephalosporin cross-reactivity percentages actually quoted right, with the allergy-clinic de-labelling service as antimicrobial stewardship) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[infectious-diseases/stewardship-outbreaks]].**
:::

## Seeing, proving and preventing

::: definition Detection and causality {#def-adr-causality}
**Pharmacovigilance runs on imperfect data: spontaneous reporting (the yellow-card and equivalents) generates signals with excellent sensitivity for the bizarre and terrible specificity for rates, because reporting is driven by novelty (the Weber effect - reports peak after launch), media and litigation; the denominators are prescriptions not patients; and the numerator is one doctor's surprise (the PRR and reporting-difference statistics with the proportional-reporting analysis traps, and the case-series that become the mechanism literature), while population-level confirmation comes from prescription-event monitoring, cohort and self-controlled designs using primary-care and hospital-linkage data (the case-centred designs suited to transient exposure and sudden outcomes) - [[epidemiology-ebm/observational-studies]], [[epidemiology-ebm/measuring-disease]]; the individual causality judgement uses structured reasoning - temporal plausibility (latency by reaction type: minutes for anaphylaxis, days-weeks for exanthem, one-three months for SJS-DRESS, months-years for tardive syndromes), dechallenge (stopping improves it), rechallenge (restarting repeats it - sometimes the evidence, usually the unethical option), exclusion of the disease itself (the rash-that-was-the-lymphoma and the fever-of-infection-versus-drug-fever arithmetic), and the validated scales (Naranjo and WHO-UMC categories) used honestly as communication tools not oracles; the scoring systems' known weaknesses (under-attribution when multiple drugs stop together, the the confounding-by-indication problem) all documented in the method literature - [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]]; the prevention stack is where medicine already knows the answers and often does not use them - electronic prescribing with interaction and dose-band alerts and forced-function double checks for the anticoagulant-insulin-cytotoxic trio, pharmacist-led medication review at transitions with the reconciliation gaps documented as harm vectors, the high-alert label system (LASA name confusion, tall-man lettering, the concentrate-versus-diluted vial stock clean-outs), allergy and reaction records with structured severity detail, and the deprescribing programmes whose benefit is measured in falls, delirium and admissions avoided (the anticholinergic-burden and PPI and benzodiazepine and the deprescribing-evidence families) - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]]; and the duty layer - reporting serious suspected reactions even when uncertain (the signal-detection maths needing imperfect numerators), candour after harm, the compensation schemes (the vaccines-damage-payment analogy) and the conflict-of-interest management that keeps post-marketing evidence trustworthy (the withdrawal-after-harm histories from diethylstilbestrol to rosiglitazone read as vigilance-system case studies, good and bad) - [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]], [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]].**
:::

::: example The antibiotic rash review {#ex-rash-review}
Day 8 of flucloxacillin for a staphylococcal bacteraemia: the patient is itchy with a truncal morbilliform rash, eosinophils 1.4, creatinine creeping from 70 to 105; the team wants to switch to teicoplanin 'because penicillins are banned now'.
::: solution
**Rash-on-beta-lactam reasoning ([[#def-scs]], [[#def-adr-causality]], [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]): phenotype first - a benign maculopapular exanthem versus early DRESS versus urticarial progression, with the mucosal and blistering and systemic-feature red flags checked explicitly, and the timeline (day 8 is classic for T-cell mediated exanthem and for the hepatitis-versus-nephritis windows; flucloxacillin's own cholestatic jaundice can present months later, a separate signal worth documenting) - [[musculoskeletal/skin]]; the organ question - eosinophilia plus rising creatinine suggests early interstitial nephritis (the urine and skin and systemic features, the stop-and-watch-with-steroids-for-severe-cases literature) more than coincidental sepsis-related kidney injury, which requires both the drug decision and the clinical trajectory read together - [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]; the switch debate - stopping a culprit with a confirmed serious reaction is right, but 'penicillins banned' is the wrong conclusion from a non-severe exanthem: the cross-reactivity figures with cephalosporins and carbapenems are low and side-chain determined rather than beta-lactam-ring determined (the R1-side-chain similarity rules and the graded challenge and test-dose protocols in IgE-mediated history, with the skin-prick and intradermal testing and the oral challenge pathway that de-labels the majority), while teicoplanin carries its own rash-and-eosinophilia-phenotype risk and loses the anti-staphylococcal certainty on which the bacteraemia cure depends - [[infectious-diseases/stewardship-outbreaks]]; and the record - what actually happened (description, severity, treatment, dates) written so the next team can re-challenge or avoid rationally, the allergy-clinic referral for the patients who want their penicillin back, and the reporting of the probable DRESS to the pharmacovigilance system - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[pharmacology/patient-variation]].**
:::
:::

::: example The statin myopathy maze {#ex-statin-myo}
A woman on atorvastatin 80mg has disabling myalgia with normal CK on two statins; her LDL is very high with strong family history; she demands 'no more statins ever'; the genetics clinic mentions SLCO1B1.
::: solution
**Type-A-myopathy-versus-nosology ([[#def-adr-classes]], [[#def-adr-causality]], [[cardiovascular/vascular-disease]]): the mechanism map - statin muscle symptoms span a spectrum from exertional myalgia through immune-mediated necrotising myopathy with anti-HMGCR antibodies (the rare, progressive, statin-triggered autoimmune type that continues after stopping and needs immunosuppression, the type not to miss) - [[nervous-system/neuromuscular]]; the pharmacokinetic contributors - SLCO1B1 variants impairing hepatic OATP1B1 uptake raising simvastatin acid exposure most of all (the SEARCH trial signal, the atorvastatin and pravastatin ranking difference and the the fibrate-myonolysis caution and the CYP3A4-inhibitor stack and the hypothyroidism-and-vitamin-D-and-exercise-and-inflammatory-disease confounders to exclude before blaming the statin) - [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], [[endocrine/thyroid]]; the rechallenge strategy that converts 'no statins ever' into data - the N-of-1 and open-planned-reintroduction evidence (the StatinWISE and the blinded rechallenge studies showing most statin muscle symptoms are reproducible on placebo - the nocebo effect as real physiology and as counselling tool) with intermittent or low-dose regimens (rosuvastatin or pravastatin at alternate-day dosing given half-lives, and the monitored uptitration), coenzyme-Q10's weak evidence honestly quoted, and the non-statin escalation the genetics-and-lipids team exists for (ezetimibe's myopathy-neutrality, the PCSK9 inhibitors' LDL-lowering power with adherence-and-cost-and-needle considerations and inclisiran's twice-yearly logic, with bempedoic-acid's prodrug design avoiding muscle as its headline and its tendon-and-gout caveats) - [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]]; and the causality bookkeeping - the temporal relationship, the dechallenge-and-rechallenge pattern, the N-of-1 documentation, the shared decision recording her preferences (the risk perception and the framed LDL numbers and the family-screening cascade she triggers by agreeing to be a proband) - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[genetics/genetic-testing]].**
:::

::: example The clozapine bloods debate {#ex-clozapine-adr}
A man on clozapine asks why he needs 'weekly needles forever'; his friend stopped his antipsychotic because 'the tablets rotted his heart'; the practice debate is whether monitoring is over- or under-done.
::: solution
**Monitoring-as-harm-prevention-economics ([[#def-adr-classes]], [[#def-adr-causality]], [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]): the agranulocytosis story - idiopathic neutrophil destruction in a small percentage, largely in the first months, the reason for the register system with deferral thresholds and haematology liaison, and the benign ethnic neutropenia problem where absolute-count rules excluded eligible patients for years until race-adjusted reference ranges and the BENEFIT data changed policy (an ADR-management system creating access inequity - the justice reading) - [[blood/anaemia]], [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]]; the myocarditis question - early transient eosinophilic-and-CRP-and-troponin rises, most in month one, usually self-limited, the syndrome that produced the black-box era with more deaths from untreated psychosis than from myocarditis (the risk-comparison honesty and the symptom-and-biomarker-protocol approach), plus the later metabolic syndrome, constipation-and-ileus (the gut-antimuscarinic burden with mortality cases behind it), sedation, sialorrhoea, and the seizure dose relationship - [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]]; the adherence logic - the register as a third party in the relationship, the missed-bloods chasing as a safety-and-liberty tension (the discontinuation cascades and the community orders), and the long-interval question answered by incidence curves and trust-building rather than bureaucracy; and the comparison the patient deserves - untreated illness harms (suicide, metabolic drift, functional decline) quantified alongside drug harms, with the metabolic monitoring for all antipsychotics done at the intervals the guidelines actually specify, not the ones the clinic forgets - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]].**

::: example The herbal hepatitis {#ex-herbal-hep}
A 45-year-old with autoimmune hepatitis on azathioprine buys a 'liver support' and 'immunity' combination online; six weeks later ALT is 15 times baseline; the differential includes flare, drug, virus and the supplement.
::: solution
**Hepatocellular-injury-detective-work ([[#def-adr-causality]], [[#def-adr-classes]], [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]): the causality frame - the RUCAM-style reasoning: latency (herbal hepatitis commonly one-three months, azathioprine idiosyncrasy variable), pattern (hepatocellular-versus-cholestatic-on-the-R-value, and the mixed patterns the supplements favour), dechallenge (improvement after stopping the supplement before blaming the prescription), and exclusion (viral serology including the reactivation risk she carries on immunosuppression, autoimmune activity markers and the flare-versus-drug distinction that sometimes needs biopsy, imaging for the vascular and biliary causes, and the muscle-and-coeliac quiet exclusions) - [[immunology/autoimmunity]], [[infectious-diseases/tropical-infections]]; the supplement forensics - ingredient uncertainty online (the under-labelling, the adulterants, the green-tea-extract and garcinia-and-usnic-acid and black-cohosh-and-chaparral literature, the pyrrolizidine-alkaloid veno-occlusive disease pattern, and the bodybuilding products' hidden steroids and their cholestasis) with the reporting-and-poison-information-centre pathway and the batch-retention advice (supplement ADRs are the vigilance system's blind spot, and the same products interact pharmacokinetically - St John's wort induction lowering azathioprine and everything else) - [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]; the management balance - stopping suspects with severity monitoring (the Hy's-law heuristic and its honest limitations, INR-and-bilirubin-rising transfer criteria and the transplant-centre conversation thresholds), steroid-decision logic if autoimmune flare remains possible with hepatology, and the azathioprine-reintroduction question answered by what the injury pattern said; and the repair of trust - not mocking the self-medication, explaining why 'natural' products bypass the testing her prescription drugs passed, agreeing a single purchase list checked with pharmacy, and reporting the suspected product so the next reader of the signal has data - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]].**
:::

## Where this leads

Immunological mechanisms in [[immunology/hypersensitivity]] and [[immunology/autoimmunity]], metabolic idiosyncrasy in [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], genetic prediction in [[pharmacology/patient-variation]] and [[genetics/genetic-testing]], teratogenesis in [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]] and carcinogenesis in [[pathology/cancer-biology]].

::: history
**Modern pharmacovigilance was bought with two disasters: the 1937 sulfanilamide tragedy, in which diethylene glycol as a solvent killed over a hundred people mostly children, producing the US Food, Drug and Cosmetic Act of 1938 and the first duty to show safety before marketing; and the thalidomide embryopathy of 1957-61, whose recognition owes to Lenz and to McBride's case-series alarm, and whose regulatory inheritance is the Kefauver-Harris amendments of 1962 requiring efficacy and reporting, the European licensing reforms, the birth of teratogen vigilance and the pregnancy-prevention programmes that drugs like isotretinoin and valproate still run (the REMS and Pregnancy Prevention Programme architecture) - [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]], [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]]; the systematic detection era followed - the Boston Collaborative Drug Surveillance Program of the 1960s-70s applying epidemiological methods to inpatient reactions (the JAMA-adjacent literature establishing rates and risk factors), prescription-event monitoring from the Medicines Control the Medicines Statistics Division in Cardiff from 1970 after benoxaprofen's lesson, and the general-practice-record databases and the linkage and self-controlled designs of the 1990s-2000s that made rare-event signal confirmation routine - [[epidemiology-ebm/observational-studies]]; the iatrogenesis reckoning - the 1975 Francis the 1970s 'white-coat medicine' essays, the 2000 Institute of Medicine's To Err is Human, and the growing harm-attribution audits finding that a large minority of hospital adverse events are drug-related and half are preventable, which is why anticoagulants, insulin and cytotoxics became the high-alert-trinity - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]; and the mechanistic and genetic turn - the halothane hepatitis work of the 1970s-80s (the the glycoprotein-adduct immunology), the acquired-drug-metabolism hypothesis debates, the HLA-B*5701-abacavir story of the late 1990s-2000s (the from-case-series-to-mandatory-blood-test trajectory in a decade), the severe-cutaneous-reaction registries (the EuroSCAR case-control studies defining drug-specific odds ratios and latency), and pharmacogenetic screening implementation for carbamazepine and allopurinol and thiopurines with their equity-of-ancestry arguments - [[pharmacology/patient-variation]], [[immunology/adaptive-responses]], [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]].**
:::

::: summary
- Most serious ADRs are type A - predictable, dose-related, preventable by the boring defences: reconciliation, alerting, double checks, monitoring, and review after every transition of care.
- Type B reactions are increasingly explicable: bioactivation to reactive intermediates, HLA-restricted T-cell stimulation, and genetic variants in metabolism and immunity.
- Latency is diagnostic: minutes for IgE, days to weeks for exanthems and DRESS, one to three months for SJS-TEN, months to years for tardive and autoimmune syndromes.
- Severe cutaneous reactions demand early culprit withdrawal, burn-unit-level care, precise documentation and reporting; allergy de-labelling is stewardship.
- Causality is a structured argument (temporality, dechallenge, rechallenge, alternatives excluded), not a feeling; scales help communication but do not create proof.
- Vigilance systems need imperfect reports: the duty to report serious suspected reactions is what gives the next generation their denominators.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Classify the reaction {level=1}
Type A, B, C, D or withdrawal: warfarin bleed at INR 6; abacavir fever-rash; inhaled steroid thinning skin; thalidomide limb defects; benzodiazepine seizures on stopping; methotrexate cirrhosis after years; chloramphenicol aplastic anaemia.
::: solution
**Classification-drill with the reasoning ([[#def-adr-classes]]): warfarin-bleed-high-INR - type A on the steep concentration-effect curve with the interaction-and-dosing audit (the bleeding-risk-factor stacking in the elderly and the anaemia-hypertension-prior-stroke list) - [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]; abacavir-fever-rash - type B immunological, HLA-B*5701-restricted, and the rechallenge-danger teaching with mandatory pre-testing (the 'F A B' first-week-fever-rash rule that made pre-screening eliminate the reaction) - [[infectious-diseases/hiv]]; inhaled-steroid-skin-thinning - type C chronic local-plus-systemic absorption with dose-and-device-and-spacer determinants (the growth-monitoring and bone-and-cataract programmes in high-dose asthma) - [[respiratory/asthma-copd]], [[paediatrics/growth-development]]; thalidomide - type D with the mechanism (the the cereblon-mediated angiogenesis and limb-bud pathways discovered decades later) and the modern leprosy-and-myeloma rehabilitation with strict controls; benzodiazepine-seizures - withdrawal (type E surviving under its own name) with the taper-and-slow-reduce doctrine and the GABA-A adaptation physiology - [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]], [[physiology/synapses]]; methotrexate-cirrhosis - type C with the cumulative-dose-and-alcohol-and-obesity-and-diabetes fibrosis risk and the monitoring-protocol history (the PIIINP-versus-biopsy-versus-transient-elastography evolution) - [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]], [[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]]; and chloramphenicol-aplastic-anaemia - the classic idiosyncratic reaction, dose-unrelated, with the two-mechanisms-lesson (the reversible dose-related mitochondrial-suppression versus the rare fatal aplasia, the reason for the blood-monitoring and the restricted-use policy) - [[blood/anaemia]].**
:::

::: exercise Read the signal {level=2}
Six months after a drug launch, reports of acute kidney injury triple. List the explanations, the studies that separate them, and the decision ladder from signal to action.
::: solution
**Signal-appraisal ([[#def-adr-causality]]): the explanations - real toxicity (unmasked by wider use), channeling and indication effects (the sickest patients prescribed first), the Weber effect and media amplification, protopathic bias (early disease manifestations attributed to the treatment started because of them - the loop-diuretic-bladder-cancer classic and the hormone-replacement-therapy-endometrial-bias case), and diagnostic-activity effects (more creatinine checks in new users) - [[epidemiology-ebm/bias-confounding]], [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]; the study ladder - descriptive first (who, when, dose, co-medications, the SJS-rule latency check), then population designs with the right comparators (new-user active-comparator designs, self-controlled case series for transient exposures, case-crossover for sudden outcomes, the confounding-by-indication handled with restriction and matching and instrumental-variable ingenuity), then mechanistic corroboration (the Naranjo-quality case series, rechallenge data if any exist, the biomarker or biopsy evidence) - [[epidemiology-ebm/observational-studies]]; and the decision ladder - signal-to-label (warnings, monitoring requirements), signal-to-restriction (risk-minimisation with the renal-dose-and-level rules), signal-to-suspension (the benefit-risk re-examination with the clinician and patient voices documented), and the honesty about uncertainty in the communication (the underreporting-versus-overreaction balance and the not-creating-a-new-drug-phobia from a bias artefact) - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**
:::

::: exercise Run the allergy clinic {level=2}
Three referrals: 'penicillin rash aged 6, never re-exposed'; 'contrast reaction after second exposure'; 'NSAID urticaria-angioedema after a dental dose.' What is each workup and what can each patient be told?
::: solution
**Allergy-clinic-decisions ([[#def-scs]], [[#def-adr-causality]]): the childhood penicillin label - most such labels are wrong or outlived (the viral-exanthem and the amoxicillin-mononucleosis classics), risk-stratify the history (urticaria-anaphylaxis timing to dose and severity versus vague rash), then skin testing and supervised oral challenge, which removes the label in the large majority and restores first-line antibiotics, with the cross-reactivity teaching on cephalosporins (the side-chain rules replacing the old percentage-fear) and the benefit framing for surgery, tuberculosis and endocarditis regimens this patient may need one day - [[microbiology/antimicrobials]], [[infectious-diseases/stewardship-outbreaks]]; the contrast 'allergy' - most reactions are anaphylactoid direct-mast-cell and osmolar effects not IgE, recurrence risk real but manageable with the agent switch and premedication protocols and observation settings, with the shellfish-iodine myths defused (the iodine-is-an-element argument) and the betablocker-onboard potentiation-of-anaphylaxis teaching and the radiocontrast-nephropathy-versus-allergy distinction - [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]; and the NSAID reaction - differentiate COX-1-inhibition-driven cross-reactive disease (the aspirin-exacerbated respiratory and cutaneous disease with nasal polyps and chronic urticaria where all strong COX-1 inhibitors are suspect but paracetamol-at-graded-dose and COX-2-selective agents are usually tolerated, with challenge-proven reintroduction protocols) from single-drug selective reactions where the rest of the class is fine, and the selective-COX-2 rechallenge and desensitisation pathways when antiplatelet therapy is needed after stents - [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]], [[immunology/hypersensitivity]].**
:::

::: exercise Design the preventable-harm campaign {level=3}
Your hospital wants to halve preventable adverse drug events. Design the intervention package using the evidence on what works, what backfires, and how to measure honestly.
::: solution
**Harm-reduction-design ([[#def-adr-causality]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]): the package - high-alert medicines with standardised concentrations, smart pumps with hard limits and the bypass-audit, independent double checks where they still add safety (paediatric weight-based dosing and the chemotherapy) versus where they have become theatre, pharmacist review at admission, discharge and transitions with the reconciliation gap counted, the anticoagulation and lithium and methotrexate services, the deprescribing clinics and the STOPP-START-style reviews with the falls-fracture-delirium-outcome framing - [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]; what-backfires - alert fatigue (the pop-up-to-action rates and the tiered alerting), blanket bans that push suboptimal therapy (the clindamycin-colitis trade), tick-box double checks performed performatively, and the documentation-only medication reviews; and the measurement - harm-on-monitoring-not-chart-review methods (the trigger-tool and global-triggered-complications-study lineage), the preventability adjudication panel, the prescribing-culture indicators as process measures, with a learning-review-system that feeds incident themes back into protocols, the candour and apology pathway, and the patient-and-carer-reported harm capture since the systems miss most outpatient harm; and the governance - board-level harm metrics with the never-event register, the equity read-outs (harm concentrated in the frail, the non-English-speaking, the multi-morbid), and the honest statement that reductions will show first in near-misses and process, with admissions lagging - [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**
