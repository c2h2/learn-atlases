Autonomic drugs are applied receptor physiology. **The autonomic nervous system runs the viscera through two main chemistries - acetylcholine at nicotinic and muscarinic receptors, noradrenaline at alpha and beta adrenoceptors - and almost every drug in this class is either mimicking, blocking, boosting or depleting one of those transactions.** Because the same transmitters act everywhere, these drugs have no subtlety: they work on the heart, gut, bladder, eye, airways, sweat glands and blood vessels simultaneously, and the therapeutic index is usually the art of choosing which receptor subtype to hit. This chapter maps the pharmacology onto the physiology already covered in [[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]].

## The cholinergic side

::: definition Muscarinic drugs and blockade {#def-muscarinic}
**Acetylcholine at muscarinic receptors (M1 glands and gut and CNS, M2 heart, M3 smooth muscle and endothelium and glands) produces the rest-and-digest program: bradycardia via M2-activated potassium channels in the node, bronchospasm and secretion via M3, gut contraction and bladder detrusor contraction, pupil constriction and accommodation for near vision, and sweat - cholinergically, though the sympathetic postganglionic fibres to sweat glands are unusually cholinergic, a fact that explains organophosphate poisoning's wet skin (the physiology recap in [[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]]). Direct muscarinic agonists are mostly physiological tools - bethanechole for bladder and salivary testing, pilocarpine for glaucoma history and dry mouth (its radiation-and-Sjogren salivary use and its sweat-and-diarrhoea limits) and muscarine as the toxin that proved the receptor - but the clinical traffic is in antagonists. The antimuscarinics atropine (the bradycardia drug, the organophosphate antidote, the premedication that outlived its evidence), hyoscine/scopolamine (motion sickness patches with the confusion trade in the old), ipratropium and tiotropium (the inhaled selectivity-by-deposition and kinetic-trapping story in asthma and COPD), oxybutynin, solifenacin, trospium and darifenacin (the overactive-bladder armamentarium and its cognitive and constipation and urinary-retention bills, with the M3 selectivity and blood-brain-barrier-poor trospium and the transdermal gel first-pass workaround), tolterodine and fesoterodine's active metabolite and renal logic, tropicamide and cyclopentolate and atropine drops for the eye exam and the amblyopia argument, the parenteral antimuscarinics for motion sickness and secretions, and the long list of accidental anticholinergic load - tricyclics, antihistamines, antipsychotics, bladder drugs and the cognitive decline association and the deprescribing cascades in older adults. The antidote poetry: physostigmine crossing the blood-brain barrier for central anticholinergic syndrome, versus neostigmine's peripheral quaternary rescue of myasthenic crisis distinction and the non-selectivity of acetylcholinesterase inhibition that makes every organ part of the treatment and the toxicity (SLUDGE as the cholinergic toxidrome against the anticholinergic 'hot as a hare, blind as a bat, dry as a bone, red as a beet, mad as a hatter' mnemonic - two toxidromes on display in emergency medicine) - [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]].**
:::

## The adrenergic side

::: definition Adrenergic drugs and blockade {#def-adrenergic}
**Noradrenaline at alpha-1 (vascular and sphincter contraction, mydriasis), alpha-2 (presynaptic inhibition, central sympathetic withdrawal, platelets, pancreas), beta-1 (heart rate, contractility, renin), beta-2 (bronchial and uterine relaxation, vasodilation, tremor, potassium uptake) with adrenaline as the non-selective hero of anaphylaxis given intramuscularly into the vastus lateralis where absorption is fastest and most reliable. Sympathomimetics organise by selectivity and route: the inhalational beta-2 agonists from salbutamol's relief to salmeterol and formoterol's kinetics - formoterol's fast-onset-slow-offset making it the maintenance-and-reliever, budesonide-formoterol-as-single-inhaler MART logic and the SMART asthma-monitoring and overuse-threshold evidence (the beta-2-receptor-desensitisation genetics ADRB1-ADRB2-Arg16 debate kept honest) and the COPD long-acting beta-agonist-plus-antimuscarinic pairs - [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]; the alpha drugs - phenylephrine for anaesthesia hypotension, nasal decongestion and the rebound-rhinitis addiction clinic midodrine for orthostatic hypotension with the droxidopa story and nasal-spray-withdrawal counselling; and noradrenaline as the vasopressor of septic shock with arterial-catheter-and-short-line pragmatism (the vasopressin-sparing-second-agent and the metaraminol and phenylephrine infusions with the extravasation phentolamine antidote); dopamine's dose-range myth kept for history and dobutamine's inotropy with tachyarrhythmia and the levosimendan-calcium-sensitiser niche (the shock-hypotension-vs-low-output distinction); beta-blockers subdivided by selectivity (the bisoprolol, atenolol, metoprolol beta-1 preference and its dose-dependent fiction in asthma, with carvedilol, labetalol and nebivolol's nitric-oxide mechanism), intrinsic sympathomimetic partial agonists like pindolol (the exercise-heart-rate paradox), lipid solubility deciding CNS effects and dialysis removal (the propranolol migraine-and-tremor-versus-atenolol's-renal-fate), alpha-blocking subtypes - prazosin's first-dose syncope and PTSD-nightmare use, tamsulosin and silodosin's uroselective alpha-1A relief of prostatic obstruction and the intraoperative floppy iris syndrome that every ophthalmologist asks the anaesthetist about, and the yohimbine historical alpha-2 probe - [[cardiovascular/hypertension]], [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]], [[renal/urinary-tract]], [[psychiatry/lifespan-psychiatry]]. Reserpine and guanethidine as depletion and uptake-1 blockers that taught receptor physiology by destroying it, and the modern uptake inhibitors' autonomic side effects (the cocaine and tricyclic sympathetic potentiation and the tyramine-hypertensive-crisis-with-MAO-inhibitors lesson and the the peripheral-sympatholytic-history) - [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]].**
:::

## Ganglia, junctions and the adrenal

::: definition Ganglionic, neuromuscular and adrenal drugs {#def-ganglion-NMJ}
**Nicotinic receptors do two very different jobs: neuronal (alpha-4-beta-2-rich) ganglionic and CNS receptors that padlock transmission when depolarised chronically, and muscle-type receptors at the neuromuscular junction that open cation channels endplate-by-endplate. Ganglionic blockers (hexamethonium, trimethaphan's historical aortic-dissection and hypertensive-crisis use) proved the sympathetic dominance of postural tone - their orthostatic collapse taught more physiology than any textbook; the clinical successors are central alpha-2 agonists (clonidine's rebound-hypertensive withdrawal with its dry-mouth sedation and its ADHD and opioid-and-alcohol-withdrawal symptom control and the dexmedetomidine-ICU-sedation with bradycardia-hypotension and the arousable-sedation concept) and the adrenal alpha-1 agonist route. Varenicline as the partial alpha-4-beta-2 agonist for smoking cessation, with cytisine's older analogue re-emerging and the CNS-versus-ganglionic selectivity explaining why smoking drugs do not cause orthostasis (the receptor-subtype-selectivity case study) - [[public-health/prevention-promotion]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]]. At the junction the pharmacology is surgical: suxamethonium depolarises (fasciculations, then phase II block, potassium release with burns and denervation and myotonias, malignant hyperthermia triggering at the ryanodine receptor with dantrolene as the treatment, and the pseudocholinesterase-variant prolonged apnoea), rocuronium and the rest compete (reversed by neostigmine-accompanied-by-glycopyrrolide or, for steroidal agents, encapsulated by sugammadex with its own anaphylaxis-rate-and-renal-clearance questions and the recurarisation edge cases), botulinum toxins cleave SNARE proteins to make chemical denervation with the local-versus-remote and antibody-mediated-failure and the iatrogenic-spread warnings - and the antidote logic for each - [[surgery/anaesthesia-pain]], [[nervous-system/neuromuscular]]. The adrenal medulla's programme (chromaffin cells as modified postganglionic neurons secreting adrenaline and noradrenaline and dopamine into blood) is pharmacology's benchmark for the whole system: phaeochromocytoma as a tumour making its own sympathomimetic infusion (the alpha-block-then-beta-block sequence doctrine, the propranolol-only hypertensive crisis and pulmonary oedema case reports, and the metanephrine biochemistry and clonidine-suppression-test physiology and the incidentaloma workup), while the paraganglioma genetics and MEN and VHL and NF1 associations connect to the receptor machinery in a one-hour tour of endocrine-renal-dermatology-ophthalmology-adjacency (the unopposed-alpha concepts also behind cocaine chest pain where beta-blockade timing remains contested and the ergot and triptan vascular warnings) - [[endocrine/adrenal]], [[genetics/cancer-genetics]], [[endocrine/endocrine-emergencies]].**
:::

::: example The anticholinergic burden audit {#ex-anticholinergic}
An 82-year-old living alone is on amitriptyline for sleep, solifenacin for bladder, chlorphenamine for itch, hyoscine butylbromide for cramps, and paroxetine; she is constipated, dry, confused at night and fell twice.
::: solution
**Deprescribing-the-sum-of-antagonisms ([[#def-muscarinic]], [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]]): the arithmetic - each drug's antimuscarinic score adds on the same receptors, so the patient experiences one overdose spread over five prescriptions (the cognitive-decline and fall associations at higher burden scores read honestly with their confounding, and the delirium-withdrawal risk in the hospital that repeats the same list on admission and never asks why) - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]; ## Where this leads

The physiology these drugs exploit is developed in [[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]], the receptor machinery in [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]], poisoning syndromes in [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]] and [[nervous-system/neuromuscular]], respiratory applications in [[respiratory/asthma-copd]].

::: history
**This class is where receptor science was invented: the 1920s work of Otto Loewi, whose Vagusstoff experiment - perfusing one frog heart with the fluid of another stimulated vagus nerve, slowing it chemically - proved chemical neurotransmission (the dream-to-notebook story and the 1936 Nobel with Henry Dale, whose naming of adrenaline-like and acetylcholine-like substances framed the whole field) - [[physiology/synapses]]; the structural programme that followed - Dale's and Barger's synthesis of simpler acetylcholine analogues and the atropine-versus-muscarine separation that created the words muscarinic and nicotinic, and Raymond Ahlquist's 1948 paper proposing alpha and beta adrenergic receptors, written as a pharmacologist's guess that outlived every molecular alternative by thirty years - [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]]; the therapeutic harvest - the beta-blocker story, where James Black's rational design from isoprenaline antagonism produced propranolol and then practised what he preached: the 1988 Nobel, and the earlier ergot-derived dichloroisoprenaline that revealed both the receptor and its arrhythmia - [[cardiovascular/hypertension]], [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]]; the anticholinergic ester era - atropine's antiquity, the Belladonna chemistry of the 19th century, the synthetic quaternary ammonium compounds of the 1940s-50s (methantheline, propantheline and their 'fewer CNS effects by design' logic as structure-based drug development before the phrase existed) and the organophosphate double-history (Schrader's insecticides and the sarin and soman legacy of the German wartime programmes, and the neostigmine and edrophonium and pralidoxime antidote science born of the same chemistry) - [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]], [[pathology/environmental-pathology]]; and the modern refinement - the alpha-1A uroselectivity cloning era, tiotropium's kinetic selectivity and its COPD mortality-claim arguments, the long-acting muscarinic-plus-beta pairs of inhaler triple therapy, and the sugammadex encapsulation as a direct-chemical-antidote design that rewrote neuromuscular monitoring - all of which are receptor-subtype selectivity sold as convenience - [[respiratory/asthma-copd]], [[surgery/anaesthesia-pain]].**
:::

::: summary
- Autonomic drugs act on the same two transmitters everywhere, so effects and side effects are organ-wide; selectivity is a matter of receptor subtype, route and kinetics.
- Antimuscarinic burden accumulates across prescriptions and predicts confusion, constipation, retention, falls and decline - deprescribe with the patient's goals, not the form's.
- Sympathomimetics are chosen by receptor profile and route; beta-2 agonist overuse is a marker of poor asthma control, and formoterol-containing regimens changed reliever policy.
- Beta-blockade is relative selectivity, dose-dependent; the perioperative continuation of established beta-blockers versus cautious initiation is one of medicine's best-replicated drug-management findings.
- The neuromuscular junction and autonomic ganglia each have dedicated drugs - depolarising versus competitive block, reversal by neostigmine or sugammadex, botulinum toxins as chemical denervation.
- Phaeochromocytoma, organophosphates and anticholinergic poisoning are the class's clinical examinations: two toxidromes, one receptor map.
:::

::: exercise Treat the poisoning toxidromes {level=2}
A patient presents wet, slow, small pupils and fasciculating; another is dry, fast, mydriatic, confused and hyperthermic. Name the syndromes, the mechanisms and the antidote logic.
::: solution
**Two-toxidromes ([[#def-muscarinic]], [[#def-ganglion-NMJ]]): the cholinergic crisis - organophosphate acetylcholinesterase inhibition (muscarinic excess - DUMBELS; nicotinic - weakness, fasciculations, paralysis; central - confusion, seizures, apnoea), with atropine titrated to dry secretions (the large and escalating doses and the pure-anti-muscarinic logic and the oxygen-and-suction-first), pralidoxime reactivating the enzyme before ageing with the agent-specific and time-specific window, benzodiazepines for seizures, and decontamination plus the the occupational-and-nerve-agent context and the atropine-infusion-duration-with-new-production-enzyme-aging and the intermediate syndrome and delayed neuropathy follow-up - [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]], [[pathology/environmental-pathology]]; and the anticholinergic delirium - blockade producing hyperthermia, tachycardia, dry skin and mucosa, urinary retention, mydriasis and agitation, distinguished from serotonin syndrome (clonus and diaphoresis and diarrhoea) and neuroleptic malignant syndrome (lead-pipe rigidity, hyperthermia, raised CK, autonomic instability and dopamine blockade, with bromocriptine and amantadine as the antidotes and dantrolene for hyperthermia) - [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[nervous-system/movement-disorders]]; the physostigmine-question - central reversal of anticholinergic delirium where the diagnosis is secure, with atropine at the bedside and the caution against TCA-induced conduction problems and the cholinesterase-inhibitor-availability-versus-supportive-benzodiazepine-cooling-catheter-reality - [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]].**

::: exercise Critique the beta-blocker controversy {level=3}
Appraise for and against: 'beta-blockers are over-prescribed in AF rate control, under-used in heart failure, and mis-prescribed as new high-dose perioperative therapy.'
::: solution
**Three-part-beta-blocker-appraisal ([[#def-adrenergic]], [[epidemiology-ebm/critical-appraisal]]): over-prescribed in AF rate control - the rhythm-versus-rate trials (the the RACE and AFFIRM-era findings of non-inferior rhythm strategy and the beta-blocker-versus-calcium-blocker rate-control effectiveness studies with the exercise and hypotension side effects and the treatment-versus-risk-factor debate with weight, alcohol, sleep-apnoea and thyroid contributors - [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]]); the under-use in heart failure - the implementation gap with discharge-uptitration programmes and nurse-led clinics and the reflex-triggered prescriptions and the reasons for inappropriate withholding (the bronchospasm-history audit, the 'too tired' misattribution and the ejection-fraction-specific evidence for carvedilol, bisoprolol, sustained-release metoprolol and nebivolol) - [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]], [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]; and the perioperative mis-prescribing - the POISE-1 finding of fewer reinfarctions with more strokes and deaths when started acutely at high dose, contrasted with the continuation data for established therapy, teaching the direction-of-change rule (continue if chronic, do not start naively on the day, titrate over weeks if elective) and the hypotension-bradycardia-as-harm-mediation reading with the NICE-dose-escalation-stopping rule and the preoperative assessment clinic implementation and the shared-decision framing; and the meta-point - a drug class can be simultaneously under-used where mortality evidence exists and over-used where symptom-control inertia drives prescribing, and both are prescribing-quality problems with different fixes - [[surgery/perioperative-care]], [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]].**
