::: summary
- Growth velocity, not a single centile, is the signal: plot, re-measure with technique, and treat a downward crossing of centiles as a symptom.
- Failure to thrive is triaged as intake, absorption or utilisation - most is feeding practice; the red-flag sweep includes cardiac, renal, endocrine and safeguarding causes.
- Developmental surveillance reads shared attention, play and regression: regression at any age is an urgent referral; delay needs a plan with a named review date.
- Precocious puberty splits central versus peripheral by history, hormones and bone age; exogenous hormone exposure is a common modern culprit worth asking about directly.
- Short stature with absent puberty at 12 in a girl demands Turner testing even without stigmata; isolated constitutional delay is a diagnosis of sensible exclusion.
- Growth and development data are public health infrastructure - screening programmes need funded downstream pathways and equity of uptake or they collapse ethically.
