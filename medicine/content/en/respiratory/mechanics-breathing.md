Ventilation is applied physics: a pressure gradient generated by muscle, opposed by elastance (lung plus chest wall), resistance (airway) and inertia (gas and tissue mass), delivering tidal volumes to a foam of ~300 million alveoli held open by surfactant and hung in a pleural space that is normally sub-atmospheric and normally empty. Every clinical sign in the respiratory system — the shape of the expiratory flow curve, the flattened diaphragm, the tracheal tug, the pulsus paradoxus, the helium mixture, the ventilator's plateau pressure — is one of these variables behaving under disease.

This chapter covers the volumes and their measurements, the forces and work of breathing, airway dynamics and flow limitation, and the sensory-motor loop we experience as breathlessness.

## Volumes, compliance and the resting equilibrium

::: definition The spirometric and plethysmographic inventory {#def-volumes}
The lung's volumes (the spirometer's addable compartments plus the two gas-dilution-derived residuals — helium dilution, nitrogen washout, body-plethysmography, and their disagreement as a clinical test: **the plethysmograph measures all compressible thoracic gas, so a helium–body-box gap quantifies gas trapping and bullous volume** — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]'s principle-vs-method example): **tidal volume ~500 mL, inspiratory reserve, expiratory reserve, and the residual volume that no exhalation empties**; their sums (**the vital capacity and inspiratory capacity, the functional residual capacity (FRC — the equilibrium of the lung's inward recoil against the chest wall's outward spring, set where their opposing pressure–volume curves cross, the physiological end-expiratory " resting" volume and the buffer of arterial gas composition — its increase in airways obstruction and age and emphysema and its decrease in obesity, kyphoscoliosis, ascites and interstitial disease — the [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]] restriction chapter and the closing-capacity relationship (FRC minus closing volume — the age at which they cross explains why basal atelectasis and V/Q mismatch creep in over 60, upright anaesthesia and the supine position lowering FRC below closing capacity — the perioperative atelectasis of [[surgery/perioperative-care]]).** The **compliance** concept (ΔV/ΔP, the pressure–volume curve's slope, with its **hysteresis — the inflation limb paying the surfactant-and-recruitment opening-cost, the deflation limb holding volumes at lower pressures, the area the energy dissipated as tissue and surface-work heat**) unifies the restriction–obstruction divide: **the stiff lung (fibrosis, oedema, surfactant deficiency, chest-wall and pleural disease) needs pressure for every millilitre and breathes shallow and fast (the Otis frequency optimum); the compliant-but- collapsing lung (emphysema's loss of elastic recoil and radial traction) breathes slowly and against flow limitation (below).** **Surfactant — the type-II-cell lecithin-and-protein layer lowering alveolar surface tension inversely with area, stabilising the Laplace relation (P = 2T/r would empty small alveoli into large without it), and failing (prematurity — [[#ex-rds]], the surfactant-inactivating milieus of oedema, aspiration and pneumonia — [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]'s ARDS biology — and the rare genetic surfactant-protein diseases of childhood).**
:::

::: example The premature breath {#ex-rds}
A 28-week, 1.1 kg baby grunts and retracts minutes after birth, cyanosed and tachypnoeic; the chest film shows fine reticulogranular shadowing with an air bronchogram.
::: solution
**Respiratory distress syndrome (hyaline-membrane disease) — surfactant deficiency staged as mechanical failure.** The physics chain: **insufficient type-II surfactant (the developmental curve — antenatal steroids accelerate the lecithin synthesis and the trials that proved it) → rising surface tension and falling compliance (the stiff lung — every breath at high cost, the small alveoli Laplace-unstable and collapsing) → atelectasis, V/Q mismatch and shunt ([[respiratory/gas-exchange]]'s hypoxaemia arithmetic), with the film's granularity the collapsed alveoli and airless lung around open airways (the air bronchogram's logic), and the grunt the infant's own positive-end-expiratory-pressure manoeuvre (glottic-brake expiration holding FRC above closing capacity — the same physics as the NICU's CPAP the baby is about to receive).** Management is the chapter's treatment list in miniature: **the antenatal-dexamethasone programme, thermal and fluid care (the preterm's surface-area thermodynamics), exogenous surfactant (the protein-B/C-containing preparations, the INSURE-and-LISA-era minimally-invasive delivery), CPAP-and-ventilation with the lung-protective low-tidal-volume-and-appropriate-PEEP strategy (the baro-and-volutrauma-and-atelectrauma arithmetic of [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]'s ARDS section — the same physics that made gentle ventilation a mortality claim), the patent-ductus-and-retinopathy-and-BPD follow-up ([[paediatrics/newborn]], [[cardiovascular/valve-congenital-disease]]'s preterm duct), and the bronchopulmonary-dysplasia trajectory's long-term mechanics (the airway-and-alveolar simplification and the airflow-limitation phenotype of adolescence).** The teaching: **a deficiency of one spoon of lipoprotein produces a textbook of respiratory mechanics.**
:::
:::

## Flow, resistance and the limits of expiration

::: definition Airways, driving pressure and dynamic compression {#def-flow}
Airway resistance concentrates in the **medium-sized generation airways (the 4th–8th — the series-parallel arithmetic where total cross-sectional area still lags the downstream exponential, Poiseuille's r⁴ dominating bronchomotor tone, mucosal oedema and secretions — the β₂-and-antimuscarinic pharmacology of [[respiratory/asthma-copd]] acting on this segment's smooth muscle)**; flow there is **neither purely laminar (Poiseuille: flow ∝ pressure, viscosity-dependent) nor fully turbulent (flow ∝ √pressure, density-dependent), and the mix explains the clinical physics: the wheeze (flow-limitation oscillation — the Starling-resistor and flutter-valve dynamics), the helium–oxygen mixture's benefit in severe obstruction and upper-airway narrowing (lower density, more laminar fraction — [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]]'s Heliox), and the silent-chest caveat (too little flow to wheeze).** **Maximal expiratory flow is effort-independent above ~70–80 % of vital capacity** — the **equal-pressure-point and waterfall (Starling-resistor) model**: downstream of the equal-pressure point the airway narrows as pleural pressure exceeds intraluminal pressure until flow saturates at a value set by **elastic recoil (the driving pressure) upstream of the compression site and the downstream resistance** — hence the mechanics of the **forced manoeuvres (FEV₁ and peak flow as recoil-and-calibre tests, the flow–volume loop's shape: the obstructive scooped expiratory limb with reduced and density-dependent peak and mid-flows, the restrictive tall narrow loop, the variable extrathoracic (inspiratory-flattening — vocal-cord and laryngeal) versus intrathoracic (expiratory-flattening — tracheal and bronchial) obstruction patterns the loop reads like a radiograph)**, **dynamic hyperinflation** (incomplete expiration when time constants and flow limitation cut the expiratory flow — air trapping raising FRC above the inspiratory-capacity boundary: the flattened diaphragm's disadvantage of [[#def-work]], the intrinsic/auto-PEEP the ventilator's end-expiratory occlusion reveals, and the dyspnoea of every COPD staircase — [[respiratory/asthma-copd]] and [[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]]), and the **cough and forced-expiration physiology (the glottic-pressurise-then-open sequence, the high-velocity dynamic-compression jet shearing mucus — why weak cough follows neuromuscular and abdominal-wall failure ([[nervous-system/neuromuscular]]'s bulbar and myopathic clearing problem), and the peak-cough-flow as the secretion-clearance threshold metric of the bronchiectatic and neuromuscular clinic).**
:::

::: example The wheeze and the paradox {#ex-asthma-mechanics}
A 19-year-old asthmatic is too breathless to speak in sentences; RR 32, pulse 122, pulsus paradoxus 28 mmHg, silent chest with poor chest excursions, PEFR unmeasurable, SpO₂ 88 % on air, and an ABG showing pH 7.34, PCO₂ 5.4 kPa.
::: solution
**Life-threatening acute asthma read through its mechanics** ([[respiratory/asthma-copd]] owns the drug ladder; this is the physics ward round): **widespread bronchoconstriction plus oedema plus mucus → severe flow limitation with gas trapping → FRC climbs toward total capacity (the patient's fixed-chest posturing, the poor excursions and silent chest — flow too low to wheeze), inspiratory capacity collapses (the breathlessness arithmetic of [[#def-work]]), respiratory muscles operate at disastrous length–tension disadvantage ([[physiology/muscle]]) and their oxygen consumption soars.** The **pulsus paradoxus** is the star sign: **the hugely negative inspiratory pleural pressure is transmitted to the thin cardiac chambers — augmented leftward septal bulging and pulmonary vascular pooling plus reduced left filling drop systolic pressure by >12 mmHg during inspiration (the ventricular interdependence of [[cardiovascular/cardiac-cycle]]'s tamponade discussion, borrowed here by the chest-pump mechanics — the same number that marks severe croup, pericardial tamponade and massive PE).** The **PCO₂ of 5.4 kPa in a hyperventilating teenager is the crisis reading: not "normal CO₂" but the loss of the compensation that hyperventilation implies — impending arrest**, and with the silent chest it mandates the intensive-care escalation (the oxygen, the nebulised salbutamol-and-ipratropium, the IV magnesium's smooth-muscle calcium antagonism, the steroids already given, the aminophylline/café debate, the ketamine-and-magnesium induction of the ventilator, the lung-protective permissive-hypercapnia strategy and the auto-PEEP-managing prolonged expiratory times of [[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]] — with the reminder that the pneumothorax and the dynamic-hyperinflation arrest are the mechanics' final two chapters).
:::
:::

::: definition Work, breathing pattern and breathlessness {#def-work}
The **work of breathing** decomposes into the **elastic work (the pressure–volume area — compliance and threshold loads), the resistive work (flow against airway and tissue resistance) and the inertial work (gas and tissue acceleration — usually negligible at rest)**; the total **oxygen cost (2–4 % of resting VO₂ normally, rising above failure levels in severe obstruction) sets the Otis frequency optimum (minimising elastic-plus-resistive work per minute — the shallow-fast pattern of stiff lungs and the deep-slow, pursed-lip, expiratory-brake pattern of obstructed ones: the patient's breathing pattern is a work-minimisation algorithm run by the brainstem)**. The **muscles**: diaphragm (the zone of apposition's bellows and costal-margin hooping — its EMG and its fatigue in COPD, and its pacing in the high-cord-injured — [[nervous-system/motor-systems]]), the inspiratory accessory ladder (sternomastoid-and-scalene for the neck, serratus-and-intercostals for the thorax — the clinical retraction pattern of infancy and the tracheal tug of upper-airway obstruction), the expiratory pump (abdominals and internal intercostals — active only in exercise, cough and speech, and the reason abdominal surgery and obesity and pregnancy ([[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-antenatal]]'s splinting diaphragm) cripple expiration), and the laryngeal brake (the glottic aperture as the variable resistor of expiration and phonation). **Breathlessness (dyspnoea) is a perception, not a variable: the mismatch between respiratory motor command and afferent feedback (the corollary-discharge comparison — [[physiology/membrane-potentials]]-adjacent sensorimotor physiology, the Hering–Breuer and irritant and chest-wall and muscle mechanoreceptors of [[respiratory/control-breathing-tests]]'s reflexes, and the affective-cortical modulation that makes identical mechanics feel different across anxiety, training and culture — [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]], [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]])**, clinically parsed into its dimensions (the **unsatisfied-air hunger of bronchoconstriction and acidosis, the work-and-effort of muscle and chest-wall load, the tightness of asthma's sensory idiom, and the chronic-air-hunger tilt of heart failure and interstitial disease — with the "acute-on-chronic breathlessness" phenotype (the advanced cardiopulmonary patient) demanding the opioid-and-fan-and-anxiolytic-and-breathing-control symptomatic toolkit of [[geriatrics-palliative/symptom-control]] alongside the cause**). The **ventilatory-perception link back to the chemoreflexes (CO₂'s air-hunger potency, hypoxia's insidiousness at altitude and in type-1 failure — the [[respiratory/gas-exchange]] and [[respiratory/control-breathing-tests]] chapters)** closes the loop between physics and experience.
:::

::: example The breathless smoker on stairs {#ex-copd-mechanics}
A 67-year-old with GOLD-3 COPD walks slowly, stoops over his stick, and on stairs performs a few breaths through pursed lips; at rest his FRC is 140 % predicted, inspiratory capacity 1.6 L, and his diaphragm sits flat on film.
::: solution
**Chronic flow limitation with dynamic hyperinflation — the mechanics self-managed.** Reading the signs as physics: **loss of elastic recoil and small-airway collapse (the scooped expiratory limb, the trapping — the helium/box gap of [[#def-volumes])) raise FRC toward TLC; the flattened, shortened diaphragm works down its length–tension curve (the zone of apposition lost, so inspiration now hooped by the rib-wall accessory muscles — the stoop and stick positioning: the patient's own biomechanical fix, fixing the shoulder girdle to recruit the accessory muscles as inspirers); and every breath taken before the last has emptied adds end-expiratory volume (intrinsic PEEP) — so the inspiratory threshold load rises and the first breath of exercise already starts near capacity (the dynamic hyperinflation whose limit is his walk speed).** **Pursed-lip breathing = applied Starling-resistor physiology: the lip brake raises airway pressure downstream of the equal-pressure point, splinting airways open against collapse, buying time-constant-matched expiration and CO₂ clearance with fewer, deeper breaths (the same physics, formalised, as the ventilator's extrinsic PEEP-matching and the CPAP circuits of the exacerbation unit).** Management's mechanical list: **pulmonary rehabilitation (the leg-training that lowers the ventilatory requirement per watt — the peripheral-muscle rather than lung adaptation — and the breathing-retraining and inspiratory-muscle-training evidence), the long-acting bronchodilators-first ladder (the hyperinflation-reducing volumes-and-dyspnoea benefits measured as inspiratory-capacity increases — [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]), the oxygen (only the survival-indicated hypoxaemic group — [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]) and the non-invasive-ventilation-home programme for the persistent hypercapnic phenotype, the lung-volume-reduction (surgical or endoscopic valve) and transplant arithmetic of the upper-lobe-predominant emphysema (the NETT and the Emphysema and Valve trials' physiology-targeted selection — the hyperinflated, heterogeneous and collateral-ventilation-poor), and the pursed-lip-and-pacing self-management already written in the stem ([[geriatrics-palliative/ageing-frailty]]'s breathlessness trajectory too).**
:::
:::

::: example The sudden pain and the absent breath sounds {#ex-pneumo}
A tall 22-year-old has sudden right chest pain and breathlessness after resting; the trachea is central, the right percussion note is hyper-resonant with absent breath sounds and tactile fremitus, the JVP is flat, and the pressure trace shows paradoxical inspiratory fall in systolic pressure of 14 mmHg.
::: solution
**Primary spontaneous pneumothorax — [[#def-volumes]]'s "normally-empty, normally-negative space" made clinical.** The mechanics: **air in the pleural uncouples the lung from the chest wall — the lung's elastic recoil collapses it to its own pressure–volume equilibrium (the volume loss and absent breath sounds and fremitus: no tissue contact, no sound transmission), the hyperresonance is the empty hemithorax's bell, and the negative intrapleural pressure's loss removes the transpulmonary-pressure pull on the veins and atria (the mild pulsus paradoxus — the ventricular-interdependence sign of [[#ex-asthma-mechanics]], here modest because the pleural pressure is atmospheric, not positive). The tension variety is the different physics — a one-way valve driving pleural pressure positive each breath: the mediastinal shift, the JVP rise, the obstructive shock of [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]]'s venous-return curve cut and the need for immediate decompression before the drain ([[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]], [[surgery/trauma]]'s needle-and-finger-and-drill Thoracostomy-Times-Two-Plus era).** Management by size-and-symptoms (the aspiration-versus-small-bore-catheter-versus-conservative trial family — the BTS-and-ACCP arithmetic), the recurrence statistics (the second-pneumothorax rate that triggers the surgery-and-pleurodesis decision — [[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]]'s pleural lists), the air-travel-and-diving prohibitions (Boyle's law with a trapped gas volume — the [[#def-volumes]] plethysmograph principle turned into safety advice: expand on ascent, hence never before radiographic resolution and the weeks-rule), and the underlying blebs and the Marfanoid and tall-thin habitus and the smoking-and-cannabis risks and the rare Birt-Hogg-Dube and LAM and endometriosis catamenial lists ([[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]] and [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]).
:::

::: quiz
Maximal expiratory flow becomes effort-independent because:
- [ ] Expiratory muscles fatigue at predictable rates
- [x] Downstream airways undergo dynamic compression at the equal-pressure point, so extra effort raises both driving and compressing pressures equally
- [ ] The diaphragm cannot contract during expiration
- [ ] Airway resistance is constant at all volumes
::: solution
Beyond the equal-pressure point the intraluminal pressure falls below pleural pressure, airways narrow, and **extra effort raises alveolar and compressing pressure together — flow saturates at the value set by elastic recoil upstream and downstream resistance (the waterfall/Starling-resistor model of [[#def-flow]])**. This is why forced manoeuvres standardise spirometry, why the scooped flow–volume loop betrays obstruction, why pursed lips (raising downstream pressure) splint flow open, and why hyperinflation and air trapping follow when expiratory flow limitation truncates emptying ([[#ex-copd-mechanics]]).
:::

::: history
**Galen's** bellows-and-larynx experiments and the century-long victory of muscle-driven inspiration over chest-shrink theories; **Hooke's and Lower's** bellows resuscitations and **Dalton's and Lawes's** expired-air chemistry (the oxygen-carbon-dioxide accounting); **Poiseuille's** tube-flow law and the airway's r⁴; **Laplace's** sphere law (1805) applied to alveoli, and the surfactant's discovery era — **von Neergaard's** 1929 surface-tension proposal, **Pattle's** 1954 and **Clements's** 1957 demonstrations of the low-surface-tension lung lining, and **Avery and Meade's** surfactant-loss-in-RDS hypothesis (the 1957 paper and the Fujiwara-and-Robertson replacement-therapy trials of the 1980s) — the physiology-to-neonatology pipeline; **Rahn and Fenn and the Otis equation and the school's** mechanics of ventilation (the work-and-frequency synthesis of the 1930s–50s and the ideal-alveolar-air arithmetic shared with [[respiratory/gas-exchange]]), **McIlroy and Hyatt and the flow–volume and equal-pressure-point era (the dynamic-compression and effort-independence revolution of the 1960s — the maximum expiratory flow–volume curve)**, and **Cournand and Richards's** catheter-era cardiopulmonary physiology (the V/Q framework that made these mechanics clinical — the Nobel-shared 1956 work) ; the **body-plethysmograph (the Dubois-Ferris-Cournand 1956 validation paper)**; and **the surfactant-protein genetics era (the SP-B and SP-C and ABCA3 and NKX2-1 descriptions — [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]] presenting as neonatal respiratory failure)**.
:::

::: summary
- Lung volumes and compliances are equilibrium and energy measurements; FRC sets the gas buffer and the muscle operating lengths, and the helium–plethysmograph gap quantifies trapping.
- Surfactant makes the Laplace-stable, low-pressure lung; its deficiency (prematurity, inactivation) converts surface physics into atelectasis, stiff lungs and shunt.
- Maximal expiratory flow is effort-independent: recoil drives it, dynamic compression limits it, and the flow–volume loop and FEV₁/FVC read it — wheeze, Heliox, pursed lips and auto-PEEP are all density-and-waterfall phenomena.
- Breathing pattern is work minimisation (Otis) executed by a muscle hierarchy whose disadvantage (hyperinflation, obesity, fatigue) is a major dyspnoea generator.
- Breathlessness is a perception from corollary discharge versus afferent feedback — mechanically driven, affectively modulated, and treatable on both axes.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Classify the trace {level=1}
FEV₁ 2.4 L, FVC 3.0 L, FEV₁/FVC 0.80, TLC 78 % predicted, DLCO normal. Classify and give two causes.
::: solution
**Restrictive pattern (the ratio normal-high with reduced FVC, and reduced TLC confirming true restriction rather than the poor-effort or obstruction-mimic); normal DLCO separates the chest-wall/neuromuscular/extrapulmonary causes (obesity, kyphoscoliosis, diaphragmatic paralysis, myasthenia — normal gas transfer per volume) from the interstitial causes where DLCO falls ([[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]'s first discriminator) — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]'s pattern-recognition flow.**
:::

::: exercise Two FRCs {level=1}
Helium-dilution FRC 2.1 L; body-plethysmographic FRC 4.4 L in a COPD patient. Explain the discrepancy and its clinical uses.
::: solution
Helium reaches only **ventilated** gas; the plethysmograph measures **all compressible thoracic gas** (Boyle's law on the sealed box) — the 2.3 L gap is **unventilated or slowly-ventilating trapped/bullous volume**, the quantitative signature of air trapping and emphysematous spaces. Uses: **staging emphysema and hyperinflation (with the RV/TLC and IC/TLC ratios — the inspiratory-capacity fraction predicting dyspnoea and mortality), the pre-lung-volume-reduction and transplant arithmetic, and the pneumothorax-and-bullae caution for barotrauma risk ([[#def-volumes]], [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]])**.
:::
:::

::: exercise Why fast-shallow, why slow-deep {level=2}
Using the work decomposition and the Otis argument, explain why interstitial fibrosis and asthma push opposite breathing patterns, and why each pattern fails at its extreme.
::: solution
**Fibrosis: elastic work dominates (stiff lung — large pressure per litre); each breath is expensive, so the system minimises tidal volume and buys minute-ventilation with frequency — shallow-fast — until frequency's own resistive-and-dead-space cost (the fixed anatomical dead space fraction rising) and muscle fatigue set the floor (the failure point: rapid-shallow breathing with rising PaCO₂ — [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]'s type 2 trajectory). Asthma: resistive work dominates; slow-deep reduces peak flow (less turbulence — the widget's square-root), lengthens expiratory time for time-constant emptying and adds lung-volume recoil (bronchodilation by stretch and the pursed-lip brake — [[#ex-copd-mechanics]]), until deep breaths at high volumes put the muscles at length-tension disadvantage and raise the intrinsic-PEEP threshold load — the failure point is silent-chest hyperinflation ([[#ex-asthma-mechanics]]).** The exam sentence: **the pattern is the optimum of the wrong-cost-minimisation, and the disease names which cost.**
:::
:::

::: exercise Read the loop {level=2}
Describe the flow–volume loop shapes of (a) fixed tracheal stenosis, (b) variable extrathoracic obstruction, (c) severe kyphoscoliosis, and one cause each.
::: solution
**(a) Both limbs flattened (fixed limitation regardless of transmural-pressure sign) — post-intubation/tracheostomy stenosis, a goitre's compression or a midline tumour ([[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]]); (b) inspiratory flattening with normal expiration (the extrathoracic airway narrows when pleural-to-atmospheric pressure reverses on inspiration — vocal-cord dysfunction/paradoxical movements (the FEVD mimicker of [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]'s "difficult asthma"), and the unilateral/recumbent patterns of the tracheomalacia and goitre varieties); (c) a small loop scaled down with preserved shape (restrictive — the chest-wall pump of severe kyphoscoliosis with the reduced-volumes/high-ratio signature of the [[#def-volumes]] classification).** The loop as a radiograph: [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]] and [[clinical-skills/physical-examination]]'s flow-limitation localisation, and the reason the flow–volume loop is part of every breathlessness work-up ([[respiratory/control-breathing-tests]]'s stridor chapters).
:::

::: exercise The chest that will not inflate {level=2}
A ventilated asthma patient's plateau pressure is 44 cmH₂O with auto-PEEP of 12 on end-expiratory hold. Separate the compliance, resistance and hyperinflation components of the high pressures, and the ventilator and patient manoeuvres each responds to.
::: solution
**Plateau pressure = set tidal volume / respiratory-system compliance + auto-PEEP + PEEP_set (the alveolar end-inspiratory pressure); the resistive component lives in the peak-minus-plateau difference (large in asthma's narrow tubes — the flow-slowing and heliox and bronchodilator targets). Here the auto-PEEP (the trapped-expiration pressure — the [[#ex-copd-mechanics]] intrinsic-PEEP measured by the occlusion manoeuvre) is a major fraction: the fix sequence is (1) buy expiratory time (lower rate, higher inspiratory-flow and therefore shorter inspiratory time — permissive hypercapnia's bargain), (2) deepen sedation and synchronise (the double-triggering that stacks breaths — [[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]]'s asynchrony list), (3) bronchodilate and recruit-tolerate (recruitment manoeuvres are the wrong drug — more trapping), and (4) accept the plateau permissively within the lung-protective negotiation (the barotrauma-pneumothorax arithmetic — the tension physiology of [[surgery/trauma]]'s chest-drain chapter), and (5) the extremis options (the short-circuit exhalation catheters and the neuromuscular blockade's chest-wall relaxation and, historically, the halogenated anaesthetics' bronchodilation, with the ECMO bridge's transfer criteria — the refractory-hypercapnia-ventilation failure of [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]).**
:::

::: exercise Quantify the grunt {level=2}
Explain the infant's grunt, head-nodding and seesaw respiration as mechanics signs, and state what each implies about the failing component.
::: solution
**Grunt = the partial glottic closure in expiration — the infant's voluntary/expires-brake PEEP preserving FRC above closing capacity (the compliance-and-atelectasis failure — [[#ex-rds]]); head-nodding = the accessory-muscle desperation when the diaphragm alone cannot overcome the elastic load (the scalenes and neck strap muscles nodding the head as the origin — muscle-power-versus-load failure); seesaw (thoraco-abdominal) breathing = the chest wall moving out as the abdomen moves in (or vice versa), marking diaphragmatic fatigue/flattening or upper-airway obstruction — the pump geometry failing (the infant's highly-compliant chest wall transmitting the negative pleural swings inward — the retractions of the intercostals and subcostals, the same mechanics read in croup and bronchiolitis — [[paediatrics/newborn]] and [[respiratory/respiratory-infections]]'s RDS/bronchiolitis chapters). Each sign is a work-of-breathing paragraph — and their combination is the admission/escalation criterion that the paediatric early-warning score encodes ([[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]]).**
:::

::: exercise Density, altitude and the helium question {level=2}
Heliox (70:30 helium:oxygen) improves flow in (a) severe asthma, (b) fixed tracheal stenosis, and (c) emphysema's small-airway disease — but unequally. Rank the benefit and justify from the widget's two flow laws and the density-viscosity physics.
::: solution
**Benefit ranking: (b) ≥ (a) ≫ (c). The physics: helium cuts gas density ~3× with little viscosity change; turbulent-and-transitional flow (the large-airway, high-velocity, post-stenotic regimes) is density-dependent (the square-root curve) so it benefits most; laminar small-airway flow (Poiseuille — viscosity-dependent) barely changes. (b) the fixed stenosis — pure high-velocity turbulent jet: maximal response; (a) severe asthma — mixed large-and-medium turbulent obstruction: real but modest benefit (and the trial-honest verdict: helpful in selected severe cases as a bridge, not routine) and the practical caveats (the FiO₂ floor of the 70:30 mix in the hypoxaemic patient — the [[respiratory/gas-exchange]] A-a arithmetic of Heliox's low-oxygen fractions, and the ventilator-circuit and capnography calibrations it disturbs — [[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]]); (c) emphysema's small-airway and collapsibility physiology — mostly laminar and effort-independent: negligible.** The deeper exam point: **which pressure–flow law dominates where, in each segment of the tree — the same reasoning explains why the salbutamol response is greatest where muscle and calibre allow and why the flow-volume-loop mid-flows respond to helium's density while the FEV₁'s effort-independent part does not.**
:::

**Measurement tiers: the resting battery (spirometry with bronchodilator reversibility and the loop-pattern read ([[#def-volumes]] and the fixed-versus-variable flattening of [[#def-flow]]), lung volumes and gas transfer (the restriction-versus-trapping-versus-transfer discriminators), the ABG and the exercise oximetry, the ECG/peptide/echo interface (the cardiac and pulmonary-vascular causes — [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]], [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]), and the escalation tiers (the cardiopulmonary exercise test with its ventilatory-efficiency and operating-capacity and anaerobic-threshold variables — the pattern-classification of the ventilatory-limit, circulatory-limit, deconditioning and dysfunctional-breathing phenotypes; the inspiratory-and-expiratory pressures and the sniff Nasal-PNI for the neuromuscular contribution — [[nervous-system/motor-systems]]; the diaphragm ultrasound and the sniff-fluoroscopy for paralysis); the imaging (HRCT and V/Q scanning and the CTPA/leg study tiers) as [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]] prescribes.** **Pattern recognition: place each patient on the mechanics axes (obstruction-versus-restriction-versus vascular-versus pump-versus-control-versus perception: the [[respiratory/control-breathing-tests]]-chapter hyperventilation and the breathlessness-anxiety loop ([[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]]) and the obesity-and-deconditioning continuum); the multi-mechanics majority (the COPD-cardiac-obese-anaemic elderly) need each fraction quantified ([[blood/anaemia]], [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]]) so each is treated at its own threshold.** **Disease-specific axis: the asthma-COPD and interstitial and pulmonary-vascular protocols of the sister chapters; the mechanical correctives (the weight, the respiratory muscle training, the ventilatory support and the diaphragm pacing of the high-quadriplegic, the chest-wall orthotic).** **Symptomatic axis: the breathlessness-as-perception toolkit (the fan, the positions, the breathing control and pursed-lip teaching of [[#ex-copd-mechanics]], the opioid-and-benzodiazepine evidence and the anxiety-and-affective modulation, the rehabilitation programme and the dyspnoea-of-the-last-days anticipatory prescribing ([[geriatrics-palliative/symptom-control]], [[geriatrics-palliative/end-of-life-care]]).** **Ethical safeguards: the chronic-breathlessness prognostic honesty and the goal-of-care documentation ([[medical-ethics-law/end-of-life-ethics]]), the oxygen-prescription stewardship (the benefit-free hypoxaemic-normal patient — the "oxygen as drug" prescribing discipline of [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]), the opioid fears-vs-symptom balance, and the referral-to-nowhere audit — the pathway's own spirometry.**
