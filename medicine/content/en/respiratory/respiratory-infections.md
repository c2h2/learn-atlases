Respiratory infection is the everyday end of pulmonary medicine — and a continuous demonstration of the host-defence ladder of [[immunology/innate-immunity]] failing at one of its rungs: the glottis and cough, the mucociliary escalator, the alveolar macrophage-and-neutrophil axis, and the adaptive arms of secretory IgA and T-cell immunity ([[immunology/adaptive-responses]]). The clinical discipline has four towers: **pneumonia (syndromic diagnosis, severity scoring and source control), the respiratory viruses (population immunity and the antiviral window), tuberculosis (the slow granuloma and its public-health apparatus), and the special-host infections (aspiration, bronchiectatic, splenectomised and immunocompromised lungs)** — the last cross-linked to the immunology and infectious-disease chapters.

This chapter covers host defence, community-acquired and hospital-acquired pneumonia, influenza and the other viruses, and tuberculosis in its primary, latent, reactivation and extrapulmonary forms.

## Host defence and its failures

::: definition The airway's defences and where infection enters {#def-defence}
The sterile lower tract is maintained by **the anatomical and behavioural barriers (the nasal filtration and humidification, the epiglottic-and-glottic closure, the cough reflex arc — vagal afferent to nucleus and spinal motor efferents — [[respiratory/mechanics-breathing]] and [[anatomy/neuroanatomy]]), the mucociliary escalator (the airway-surface-liquid bilayer and the ciliary beat — the mucociliary failure syndromes: smoking-and-anaesthetic-and-sedative paralysis, the primary ciliary dyskinesia and Kartagener's dynein-arms (the situs-inversus-and-sinusitis-and-bronchiectasis triad — [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]), cystic fibrosis's dehydrated gel (the CFTR chloride-and-bicarbonate channel's thick mucus — the [[gastrointestinal/pancreas-biliary]]-and-genetics chapter's infection magnet), and the post-infectious obliterative-bronchiolitis damage), the alveolar clean-up (the macrophage's first-hour neutrophil-recruitment programme — the complement-and-toll-like logic of [[immunology/innate-immunity]] — and the lymphatic egress of [[anatomy/thorax]]'s mediastinal nodes), and the adaptive arms (the secretory IgA and the Th1-and-Th17 programmes determining intracellular and extracellular competence — [[immunology/adaptive-responses]]).** Infection follows failure patterns: **aspiration (the suppressed gag of sedation, seizure, stroke and intoxication — the anaerobic-and-mixed dependents with the gravity-distribution of the right main bronchus and posterior segments — [[anatomy/thorax]]); the obstructed lung (post-stenotic pneumonia behind a carcinoma — the un-resolving-consolidation rule of [[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]]); impaired clearance (the bronchiectatic and cystic-fibrosis pseudomonal succession, the COPD's exacerbation-dysbiosis axis — [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]); immune modification (the asplenic encapsulated-organism sepsis (pneumococcus, Haemophilus, meningococcus, Capnocytophaga) with the vaccination-and-prophylaxis programme — [[blood/haematopoiesis]]-adjacent and [[immunology/immunodeficiency]]; the cell-mediated-immunity-depleted — Pneumocystis, TB reactivation, CMV and fungal pneumonias of HIV, transplant and biologic therapy — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]] and [[immunology/transplant-tumour-immunology]]; the neutropenic (the post-chemotherapy bacterial-and-fungal pneumonia with its blunted-film signs — [[blood/leukaemia]]); and the aspirated-and-inoculated special exposures (Legionella's water systems, the bird-and-soil organisms — the hypersensitivity-pneumonitis mimics of [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]).**
:::

## Pneumonia: syndrome, severity, source

::: definition Community-acquired pneumonia {#def-cap}
Pneumonia is **acute parenchymal infection diagnosed by the constellation of acute symptoms (cough, fever, sputum, pleuritic pain, confusion in the old), the consolidation findings (the bronchial breathing and dullness and reduced breath-sounds-with-crackles anatomy of [[clinical-skills/physical-examination]] — and their modest accuracy, making the chest film or CT the reference — the ultrasound's growing role for the effusion-and-consolidation bedside), and the radiological infiltrate (the lobar, bronchopneumonic, segmental and atypical-interstitial patterns — the pattern pointing at pathogen more in textbooks than in practice)** with the **severity-and-disposition layer (the CURB-65 components — Confusion, Urea >7, RR ≥30, low BP under 90/60, age ≥65 — scored to the home/ward/high-dependency-and-ICU thresholds with the modern additions of the chest-film extent and the plasma-bicarbonate and lactate, and the oxygenation and shock definitions that trigger the intensive-care referral — [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]] and [[infectious-diseases/fever-sepsis]]), the pathogen-epidemiology layer (pneumococcus dominating the typical; atypicals — Mycoplasma (the young-person cold-agglutinin haemolysis and rash and extra-pulmonary minor syndromes — [[blood/haemoglobin-disorders]]), Chlamydophila, Legionella (the water-systems, smoking-and-age-and-immunosuppression risk, the diarrhoea-and-hyponatraemia-and-really-unwell-plus-multilobar-plus-neurological-and-requiring-ICU phenotype with the urinary-antigen test and the macrolide-or-fluoroquinolone intracellular-penetration requirement — [[microbiology/bacteria]]); the viruses (influenza A-and-B with the secondary bacterial staph-and-pneumococcus complication, RSV, SARS-CoV-2 and the rhinovirus-and-parainfluenza background); the aspirational and post-obstructive and travel-and-animal exposures — the TB re-think for every "pneumonia that keeps coming back") and the **aetiology-tests layer (the blood cultures for the bacteraemic severe cases, the sputum Gram-and-culture when produced and valid, the urinary antigens for pneumococcus-and-Legionella, the viral PCR panels, the Pneumocystis-and-TB- targeted tests by host, and the modern metagenomic and 16S research tiers — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]] and [[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]]).** Treatment: **empirical protocol therapy by severity and local resistance (the β-lactam ± macrolide/doxycycline and the severe-case combinations and the respiratory fluoroquinolone's place and its tendon-and-aortic-and-neurological caveats — [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]), the early-antibiotics-and-the-sepsis-bundle timing and the de-escalation at culture return, the supportive bundle (the oxygen titration, the fluids and the VTE prophylaxis and the oral-intake-and-swallow re-check — [[surgery/perioperative-care]]-adjacent nursing science), the **complication surveillance: the parapneumonic effusion (the Light's-criteria exudate — protein-and-LDH-ratio — and the complicated/empyema discriminators of pH <7.2, low glucose, frank pus and positive microbiology mandating drainage — the chest-tube-and-fibrinolytic-and-surgical-decision and the loculation imaging — [[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]]'s pleural lists), the abscess and necrotising and cavitating pneumonias (the staph, Klebsiella, anaerobe and TB differentials), the persistent-fever-and-non-resolving-pneumonia work-up (the resistant pathogen, the obstruction, the alternative diagnoses — including organising-pneumonitis-and-vasculitis-and-malignancy — the follow-up-chest-film-at-6-weeks-and-CT-and-biopsy pathway and the >50-and-smoker cancer-exclusion rule)**; and the prevention programme (the childhood and adult pneumococcal-and-influenza vaccination, the smoking and alcohol and dental-and-swallow rehabilitation, and the post-pneumococcal-splenectomy-vaccine-antibiotic architecture — [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]).**
:::

::: example The uncle's confusing weekend {#ex-cap-empyema}
A 69-year-old has 3 days of fever, right-sided pleuritic pain and confusion; RR 32, pulse 118, BP 98/60, urea 11.4, Temp 38.9; the right base is stony-dull with bronchial breathing above; film shows a right lower-lobe consolidation with a moderate meniscus; pH 7.31, lactate 3.4; CURB-65 scores 4.
::: solution
**Severe community-acquired pneumonia with a parapneumonic effusion — admit to the high-dependency bed, treat the sepsis, and tap the fluid now.** The first hour: **the oxygen titrated, the fluid bolus with reassessment (the [[emergency-critical-care/shock-fluids]] Starling discipline — his pleural and alveolar fluid will thank you less than his kidneys), the blood cultures-then-empirical-β-lactam-plus-macrolide (the Legionella urinary antigen and the pneumococcal antigen and the viral panel sent), the severity re-check with the ICU referral criteria (the rising oxygen and lactate and the shock — [[infectious-diseases/fever-sepsis]]).** **The effusion: diagnostic ultrasound-guided tap** — **the Light's criteria (the pleural-fluid protein/serum-protein >0.5 or LDH ratio >0.6 or LDH >two-thirds upper-limit-serum confirm the exudative parapneumonic nature) and the drain-decision discriminators — pH <7.2 (the bacterial-and-neutrophil glycolysis — the most useful single number), glucose <~3 mmol/L, frank pus, positive Gram/culture, loculation: any mandates the intercostal chest drain, with the intrapleural tPA-and-DNase trial evidence for loculated collections and the thoracic-surgery (VATS) escalation for the organised peel ([[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]]'s empyema pathway); an uncomplicated, small, free-flowing sterile transudate-like parapneumonic effusion resolves with the antibiotics alone.** The follow-through: **the repeat imaging at 24–48 h if not improving, the 6-week follow-up film with the CT-and-bronchoscopy rule for the smoker's non-clearing segment (the endobronchial carcinoma exclusion — [[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]]), the vaccination-and-smoking-and-alcohol plan, and the "why now" medical audit (the aspiration history, the undisclosed immunosuppression and the TB reactivation question).**
:::
:::

::: definition Hospital-acquired, aspiration and device-associated infections {#def-hap}
**Hospital-acquired and ventilator-associated pneumonia** are different diseases: the **endotracheal-tube biofilm (the tube's guard ring aspirating colonised oropharyngeal-and-gastric secretions — the micro-aspiration-around-the-cuff mechanism), the ward-and-ICU ecology (the Staphylococcus-aureus-MRSA and the Enterobacterales-and-Pseudomonas-and-Acinetobacter resistance gradient — [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]'s stewardship target), the risk time-course (the early-versus-late-acquisition pathogen shift) and the diagnosis (the new infiltrate plus the purulent-tracheal-secretions-plus-fever-plus-oxygenation-decline-plus-culture-significance-thresholds debate — the bronchoscopic-versus-blind-sampling and the CPIS-score's decline, with the modern procalcitonin-and-clinical-predominance).** Prevention is the bundle that beats treatment (the head-up-30-degrees, the cuff-pressure-and-subglottic-suction tubes, the sedation-interruption-and-weaning and the early-mobilisation, the oral-care-and-chlorhexidine debate, the stress-ulcer-prophylaxis de-escalation above gastric-pH-and-pneumonia-trade-off, and the hand-hygiene-and-contact-isolation backbone — [[microbiology/infection-control]]), while treatment is the **local-antibiogram-driven empirical broad-to-narrow protocol with the 7-day-duration evidence and the culture-negative-syndrome stewardship ([[microbiology/antimicrobials]])**. Aspiration syndromes separate the **chemical pneumonitis (the abrupt acid-aspiration — the Mendelson obstetric description — supportive care, no routine antibiotics, the steroid-no-benefit finding) from the bacterial aspiration pneumonia (the indolent anaerobic-and-mixed picture of the dependent segments — the dental-hygiene and swallow assessment and the feeding-tube-and-positioning-and-medication-review — the antipsychotic-and-benzodiazepine-and-antiseizure-reduction list ([[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]] and [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]]))** and the **recurrent-aspiration-and-chronic-cough/bronchiectasis work-up (the laryngopharyngeal and the oesophageal and the neurodegenerative causes — [[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]], [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]])**.
:::

## Viruses and the granuloma

::: definition Influenza and the respiratory viruses {#def-viruses}
**Influenza** — the segmented negative-sense RNA virus (the haemagglutinin-and-neuraminidase subtype arithmetic, the **antigenic drift (the polymerase-error point mutations driving the annual vaccine reformulation) versus shift (the segment reassortment between human and animal genomes in the mixing-vessel host — the pandemic mechanism and the H1N1-2009 and avian-H5N1-and-H7N9 stories — [[microbiology/viruses]])**) clinically: the abrupt fever-and-myalgia-and-headache-then-cough syndrome, the aerosol-and-fomite spread from the presymptomatic window, and the complications that dominate mortality (the viral-pneumonitis itself, the secondary bacterial pneumococcal-and-staph (the necrotising PVL-positive strains) pneumonias — [[microbiology/bacteria]], the cardiac-and-neurological and the exacerbation-of-chronic-disease toll, and the elderly-and-comorbid-and-pregnant-and-unvaccinated risk groups — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-antenatal]]). Management: **the neuraminidase-inhibitor (oseltamivir and the zanamivir and the baloxavir-cap-protein-inhibitor — started early, given anyway to the admitted-and-severe — [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]) and the vaccination programme (the egg-or-cell-or-recombinant-manufacture, the trivalent-and-quadrivalent-composition and the annual-boost logic in the elderly-and-clinically-extremely-vulnerable-and-pregnant-and-healthcare-worker schedules — [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]).** RSV: the infant bronchiolitis and the elderly pneumonia — the maternal-vaccine-and-infant-monoclonal-prevention era ([[paediatrics/acute-childhood-illness]]). SARS-CoV-2: the anosmia-and-thrombotic-and-multisystem spectrum, the vaccinated-era severity shift, the long-COVID phenotype (the fatigue-clinic model spanning several specialties). Measles-and-varicella pneumonias and the adenovirus outbreaks live in the [[microbiology/viruses]] and [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]] chapters; the fungal and protozoan pneumonias (the Aspergillus angioinvasive halo-and-cavitary-and-air-crescent in the neutropenic — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]]; the endemic dimorphic histoplasma-and-coccidioides; Pneumocystis jirovecii's bilateral-perihilar-reticulonodular-hypoxic-PJP with the LDH and the desaturation and the TMP-SMX-and-steroid-if-hypoxic treatment and the HIV-and-transplant-prophylaxis rules — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]]) complete the special-host table.
:::

::: definition Tuberculosis: the granuloma's two acts {#def-tb}
**Mycobacterium tuberculosis** — the acid-and-alcohol-fast, slow-growing, cord-forming aerobe — is **inhaled into the alveoli, swallowed by macrophages and contained (or not) by the T1/IFN-γ granuloma ([[immunology/innate-immunity]] and [[pathology/chronic-inflammation-repair]]'s caseating-necrosis centre):** **primary TB** (the childhood-and-immune-naive illness — the Ghon focus plus hilar nodes = the primary complex, usually silent and self-contained; the children's progressive and miliary and meningeal risks and the close-contact-and-immunosuppressed escalations); then **latent TB infection (the viable-but-contained bacilli of a positive tuberculin-skin-test or interferon-gamma-release-assay without disease — the 5–10 % lifetime reactivation risk multiplied by immunosuppression, the treatment-strategy question of the contacts-and-before-biologics-and-endemic-migrants — the rifamycin-shortened regimens (4R, 3HR, 3HP) replacing the old 6–9-month isoniazid era — [[public-health/communicable-disease-control]])**; and **reactivation (post-primary) TB** — the **apical-and-posterior-segment cavitary disease (the high-oxygen-tension-and-lymph-drainage apical milieu hypothesis, the caseous cavity's enormous bacillary burden and the aerosol-generating cough = the epidemiological engine).** Clinical: the chronic cough-and-haemoptysis-and-fever-and-night-sweats-and-weight-loss-and-fatigue-and-malaise, and the **extrapulmonary list (the pleural — the lymphocyte-rich exudate; the miliary — the random miliary-shadow and the basal-meningitis-and-bone (Pott disease) and genitourinary and pericardial and adrenal — [[endocrine/adrenal]]-and-[[musculoskeletal/back-pain]]-and-[[renal/urinary-tract]] references)**; diagnosis: **the three-sputum-and-Xpert-MTB/RIF-and-line-probe-resistance-tests and culture with drug-susceptibility, the chest imaging and the histology's caseating-granulomas (the TB-vs-sarcoidosis-non-caseating discriminator — [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]), the IGRA/TST interpretation with the BCG-and-immunosuppression caveats ([[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]] for the BCG scar's tuberculin false-positives and its limited-pulmonary-efficacy reality)**; treatment: **the rifampicin-and-isoniazid-and-pyrazinamide-and-ethambutol intensive phase then the rifampicin-isoniazid continuation (the 2+4 standard and the longer regimens for cavitary-and-delayed-culture-conversion, the pyridoxine with isoniazid and the neuropathy and the hepatotoxin monitoring and the optic-ethambutol and the urate-pyrazinamide and the contact-lens-urine-tears-rifampicin colour and the CYP3A4-and-2C9-induction interactions (the oral-contraceptive and warfarin and antiretroviral and antiepileptic and methadone negotiations — [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/contraception-fertility]])), the drug-resistance ladder (the mono-and-poly and MDR (rifampicin+isoniazid) and pre-XDR and XDR — the BPaL-and-longer-oral-regimen-revolution with its bedaquiline-QT-linezolid-neuropathy-haematology monitoring — [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]), and the public-health machinery (the notifiable-disease reporting, the contact-tracing-and-screening, the infectious-period-and-airborne-precautions-and-N95-and-negative-pressure-room rules, the directly-observed-and-supported-adherence and the treatment-completion and the HIV-co-infection-and-ART-timing-and-immune-reconstitution-inflammatory-syndrome (IRIS) interface — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]] and [[public-health/communicable-disease-control]]).**
:::

::: example The night sweats and the apex {#ex-tb}
A 36-year-old arrived from a high-incidence country 4 years ago; 8 weeks of cough, drenching night sweats, 6 kg weight loss and blood-streaked sputum; the film shows right-upper-lobe cavitation; three routine sputum cultures at 48 hours are "no growth so far".
::: solution
**Cavitary pulmonary tuberculosis until the acid-fast stains and PCR say otherwise — and the routine cultures will not find it** ([[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]]'s media-and-time point: the Löwenstein-Jensen-and-liquid-culture weeks and the Ziehl-Neelsen-and-fluorochrome auramine stains and the Xpert MTB/RIF-and-Ultra nucleic-acid-test with its rifampicin-resistance-detection). The sequence: **the immediate airborne-isolation-and-N95-and-negative-pressure-room and occupational-health notification (the cough-suppressing-hygiene and the transport plan and the ward's vulnerable-contacts audit — the immunosuppressed-patients-and-staff-and-children-and-pregnant exposure list), the three-sputum AFB-and-NAAT (and the induced-sputum-or-bronchoscopy-BAL when expectoration fails), the HIV-and-diabetes-and-immunosuppressive-drug co-screen (the bidirectional TB-Immune-link — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]] and [[endocrine/diabetes]]), the chest-film-and-clinical-threshold-treatment decision (the treatment of the cavitary smear-positive without waiting — and the public-health-and-medical-officer notification on the same day in the notifiable-system), and the standard 2+4 with pyridoxine-and-liver-and-vision-and-urate monitoring and the susceptibility-guided revision, the adherence plan (the DOT-and-community-nurse-and-video-observed options) and the contact-programme's TST/IGRA-survey with the latent-infection-treatment offers ([[public-health/communicable-disease-control]]).** The counselling layer: **the infectious-period definition and the school-and-work return, the rifampicin's orange fluids and lens-and-contraception-and-warfarin-and-anticonvulsant interactions ([[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]]), the alcohol-and-paracetamol-and-hepatitis-B-and-C liver-risk audit, and the post-treatment relapse-versus-reinfection and residual-cavity-and-aspergilloma late risks ([[microbiology/fungi-parasites]]).**
:::

::: example The tour-guide's pneumonia {#ex-legionella}
A 66-year-old returns from a coach holiday with 4 days of high fever, dry cough, diarrhoea, confusion and a sodium of 126; multilobar consolidation on film; routine sputum shows neutrophils but no organisms on Gram stain.
::: solution
**Legionnaires' disease — the water-system atypical with the extra-pulmonary scorecard.** The clues stack: **the hotel-spa-and-misted-air-conditioning incubation (the aerosolised environmental source — person-to-person transmission essentially absent — [[microbiology/bacteria]]), the relative-bradycardia-and-GI-and-neurological features, the hyponatraemia (SIADH-adjacent and natriuretic-renal), the hepatic transaminase rise, the unrevealing-Gram-and-culture-sputum (the facultative intracellular organism needing cysteine-enriched buffered-charcoal media — the routine plate's silence — [[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]]) and the multilobar-severe-radiology-clinical-mismatch.** Diagnose: **the urinary antigen (serogroup-1 — the commonest but not all) plus the respiratory PCR-or-antigen-and-antibody-serology paired, and the stool-and-specific-media culture when available; the public-health-legionella-unit notification and the hotel-and-plumbing-water-sampling epidemiology follow the case ([[public-health/communicable-disease-control]]).** Treat: **the intracellular-penetrating agent — the azithromycin-or-respiratory-fluoroquinolone protocol (β-lactams fail — the no-ribosome-and-no-penetration logic, and the rifampicin-addition folklore versus its interactions), the severity-tiered support and the ICU threshold, and the source-counselling and the risk-factor audit (smoking, age, immunosuppression, steroids).** The differential of the same picture (the psittacosis-and-Q-fever bird-and-livestock exposures — [[microbiology/bacteria]]-adjacent zoonoses, the Mycoplasma and viral atypicals, and the organising-pneumonitis mimic) is sorted by the exposure history and the paired serologies/PCR ([[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]]).
:::
:::

::: example The asthmatic on inhaled steroids with a dry cough {#ex-pneumocystis}
A 41-year-old with newly-diagnosed HIV (CD4 count 90) has 3 weeks of worsening dry cough and exertional desaturation from 98 % to 89 %; afebrile-or-low-grade; LDH raised; the film shows bilateral perihilar shadowing; he takes inhaled budesonide-formoterol for asthma.
::: solution
**Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia (PJP) — the desaturating, dry, diffusely-shadowing pneumonia of T-cell depletion (the inhaled-steroid contributor to the local-immunity argument aside).** Confirm: **the induced-sputum-or-BAL PCR/immunofluorescence (the sensitivity gradient from sputum to bronchoalveolar lavage), the β-D-glucan adjunct, the A-a-gradient-and-hypoxia severity numbers, and the TB-and-CMV-and-bacterial co-pathogen screen (the co-infection rule — the sputum and blood and stool panel) — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]] and [[microbiology/fungi-parasites]].** Treat: **high-dose trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole (the folate-pathway double-block — [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]) with the steroid adjunct when hypoxic (the PaO₂-threshold-adjuvant-prednisone trial evidence — the early-worsening-inflammatory-lysis mitigation), the alternative regimens (atovaquone-and-dapsone-TMP-and-pentamidine-and-clindamycin-primaquine) for intolerance, and the G6PD-check-and-folate-and-rash-and-hyperkalaemia-and-hyponatraemia monitoring ([[blood/haemoglobin-disorders]]'s favism and [[renal/potassium-calcium-phosphate]]'s trimethoprim-potassium footnote).** Then: **the ART-timing decision (the within-two-weeks-for-most-opportunistic-infections-with-the-meningitis-and-CNS-vasculitis exception logic and the IRIS anticipation — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]]), the secondary-prophylaxis-until-CD4-recovery rule, the PJP-prophylaxis indications (the CD4-threshold, the corticosteroid-dose and the transplant-and-biologic-risk lists), and the household-and-sexual-partner-testing-and-mental-health-and-adherence programme ([[public-health/communicable-disease-control]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]])**.

- [x] Rapidly reduce the bacillary burden and prevent resistance selection in the large cavitary populations

The kill-curve's fast limb is the intensive phase's job — **the burden reduction that makes the patient non-infectious and the four-drug cover that prevents resistance in the huge, mutating cavitary population (the mutant-frequency-and-monotherapy arithmetic — [[microbiology/antimicrobials]])**; sterilisation of the persisters (the slow tail — the widget) is the rifampicin-isoniazid continuation phase's task, and relapse is what happens when that tail is undertreated ([[#def-tb]]).

::: history
**Laennec's** auscultation-and-tubercle synthesis (1819) and the **Villemin's** transmission experiments, **Koch's** 1882 bacillus and tuberculin detour and the **Calmette-and-Guerin** BCG (1921 preparation, 1927 first use — [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]]), **the Eaton's-agent (Mycoplasma) atypical-pneumonia work of the 1930s–40s**; **the pneumococcal typing and the Felton's** antiserum era and the **Austrian's** pneumococcal-pneumonia clinical natural-history studies (the pre-antibiotic mortality tables still quoted in editorials), the **1939–45 sulfonamide-and-penicillin and the 1944 streptomycin and 1952 isoniazid and 1960s rifampicin and ethambutol and pyrazinamide TB-drug cascade** that converted sanatoria-and-collapse-therapy (the **Monaldi and Alexander's** artificial-pneumothorax era) into pharmacology and public health; **the 1976 Legionella's Philadelphia-assembly discovery (the CDC's classic outbreak epidemiology — [[epidemiology-ebm/observational-studies]])**, the **1918-pandemic-influenza through the 1957-and-1968-shift-pandemics and the 2009 H1N1 and 2019-2025 SARS-CoV-2 eras (the genomic-surveillance-and-vaccine-platform revolution)**, **the 1981-PJP-cluster recognition that uncovered AIDS ([[immunology/immunodeficiency]] and [[epidemiology-ebm/measuring-disease]]'s syndrome-first-surveillance lesson)**, and the **WHO DOTS-to-Stop-TB-to-End-TB strategy evolution and the MDR/XDR and shorter-regimen and new-drug (the bedaquiline and pretomanid and delamanid) era** of the TB chapter's modern pages.

::: summary
- Lower-tract sterility is an active, multi-rung defence; each clinical pneumonia phenotype (aspiration, obstructed, bronchiectatic, asplenic, cell-depleted, neutropenic) localises a rung.
- Community pneumonia is managed as syndrome + severity (CURB-65 and oxygenation) + epidemiology (typical/atypical/viral/aspirated), with early protocol therapy, source control of empyema by pH-and-glucose-and-pus criteria, and non-resolution re-investigation including cancer exclusion.
- Hospital and ventilator-associated pneumonia is a biofilm-and-ecology disease — bundles prevent, local antibiograms prescribe, and 7-day courses with de-escalation steward.
- Influenza is the drift-and-shift virus: vaccinate the risk tiers, give neuraminidase inhibitors early, and expect the bacterial complications that drive mortality.
- Tuberculosis runs containment versus reactivation; control is the airborne-infection-and-notification-and-contact-and-latent-treatment apparatus, and cure is the four-drug sterilising clock that the two-phase kill-curve dictates.

::: exercise Score and place {level=1}
Compute CURB-65 for [[#ex-cap-empyema]]'s patient from the data given, and state the disposition the score mandates.
::: solution
**Confusion (1) + urea >7 (11.4 → 1) + RR ≥30 (32 → 1) + BP <90 systolic or <60 diastolic (98/60 — diastolic counts: <60 threshold — borderline; strict criterion is ≤60: 1) + age ≥65 (1) = 4–5.** **Score ≥3–5: severe pneumonia — admit (high-dependency/ICU-capable assessment), the oxygenation-and-lactate-and-shock triggers sending the ICU referral regardless of score — the score is a bedside triage of mortality risk (the trial-derived 5-year-then-ICU-usable bands), not a ceiling on clinical judgement ([[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]]'s rules-vs-judgement caution).**
:::

::: exercise Light it out {level=1}
Pleural fluid: protein 42 g/L (serum 61), LDH 900 (serum 210), pH 7.05, glucose 1.2, pus-like; Gram stain neutrophils, no organisms. Apply Light's criteria and the drain rule.
::: solution
**Protein ratio 0.69 and LDH ratio 4.3 — exudate (either suffices; parapneumonic context). Drain-mandating features: pH 7.05 (<7.2), glucose 1.2 (very low), and the purulent character — culture-negative does not matter (prior antibiotics).** → intercostal chest drain plus antibiotics, the intrapleural-tPA-plus-DNase consideration for loculation and the thoracic-surgery review for the failed-lung and organised peel ([[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]] and [[#ex-cap-empyema]]).

::: exercise The spleen-less chest {level=2}
A 28-year-old post-traffic-splenectomy presents with 6 hours of fever and rigors; what syndrome, what organisms, what immediate action, and what should have been in his discharge letter?
::: solution
**Overwhelming post-splenectomy infection (OPSI) — the encapsulated-organism (Streptococcus pneumoniae dominant, plus Haemophilus influenzae type b and Neisseria meningitidis, and Capnocytophaga after dog bites) fulminant sepsis with its hours-scale mortality, the missing spleen's loss of marginal-zone IgM and opsonisation and ciliary filtration ([[immunology/immunodeficiency]]'s asplenia page and [[blood/haematopoiesis]]'s splenic-function physiology — the Howell-Jolly bodies' clue — [[blood/anaemia]]-adjacent). Immediate: the sepsis-bundle with blood cultures and immediate broad antibiotics (the meningococcal-covering third-generation cephalosporin protocol and the Waterhouse-Friderichsen-and-adrenal-crisis co-assessment), the ICU threshold low ([[infectious-diseases/fever-sepsis]]).** The letter: **the pneumococcal (conjugate-then-polysaccharide schedules) and Hib-and-meningococcal-and-Haemophilus-and-annual-influenza vaccination timetable, the standby-and-or-preventive-antibiotic (the penicillin-or-macrolide daily-with-safety-net rules — the local protocols and the adherence realities), the fever-action-card ("any fever = antibiotics and same-day medical review"), and the travel-and-tick-and-dog-and-freshwater (the babesiosis and Capnocytophaga and malaria extra-vigilance) counselling ([[public-health/vaccination-programmes]], [[public-health/global-health]]).**
:::

::: exercise Test and interpret {level=2}
Give the tuberculin-skin-test and IGRA mechanics, their false positives and negatives, and the two clinical questions each answers.
::: solution
**TST: intradermal purified-protein-derivative → the 48–96-hour induration read (the booster-phenomenon repeat-reading rule); IGRA: the interferon-gamma release from T cells stimulated by ESAT-6/CFP-10 (and TB7.7) — antigens absent from BCG and most environmental mycobacteria (the single-sample specificity advantage).** **Positives: shared for true infection; TST additionally false-positive post-BCG-and-environmental-mycobacteria; negatives: anergy (the HIV-and-sarcoidosis-and-miliary-TB-and-extremes false-negatives, with the higher-induration thresholds for recent contacts and the immunosuppressed varying by guideline), the recent-infection window (the 8-week conversion lag) and the technical TST errors.** **The two questions: "is she infected?" (both; IGRA after BCG) and "does she need latent-TB treatment?" (positivity plus the risk-stratification — the contacts-and-immunosuppressed priority) — neither diagnoses active disease ([[#def-tb]]; the active-disease answer is microbiology — [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]] for BCG.**
:::

::: exercise The un-vaccinated infant's cough {level=2}
A 7-week-old has 4 days of coryza progressing to coughing paroxysms with post-tussive retching and inter-paroxysm colour loss, no fever, and a "whoop" is not heard; the parents are well. Diagnose, justify the danger signs, and give management, prophylaxis and reporting.
::: solution
**Bordetella pertussis in the young-infant danger window** (the apnoea-and-cyanosis-and-bradycardia phenotype without the classically-absent-in-infants whoop; the waning-maternal-antibody and pre-vaccination susceptibility — the first dose is at 8 weeks — [[paediatrics/immunisation]] schedules — and the "adults-cough-too" source the household represents). Danger: **the apnoeic spells, the feeding failure and the bradycardic desaturations, the lymphocytosis-and-hyperleukocytosis-and-pulmonary-hypertension-and-death of the malignant form — admit under the paediatric criteria.** Management: **the macrolide (the azithromycin-and-clarithromycin-eradication-reducing-transmission-more-than-symptom-modifying logic — the cough lasts its 6–10 weeks regardless — [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]), the apnoea-monitoring-and-suction-and-feeding-support and the lymphocytosis-management-and-steroid-avoidance ICU transfer for the malignant phenotype; the PCR-and-culture (the Bordetella-pertussis-specific media) and the viral co-infection panel; the household macrolide prophylaxis and the close-contact-registry and the notifiable-disease report; and the immunisation-review (the catch-up schedule and the pregnancy-maternal-vaccination-lessons and the grandfather's-acellular-pertussis-cocooning-policy debate — [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]).**
:::

::: exercise Rifampicin's ledger {level=1}
Name the four monitoring/safety obligations of rifampicin and the three most consequential interactions, each with its mechanism.
::: solution
**Obligations: the orange body-fluids-and-lens staining warning; the hepatotoxicity baseline-and-symptom-and-interval-chemistry discipline (and alcohol-and-hepatitis-virus risk stacking — [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]); the platelet-and-renal-and-flu-like rare reactions; and the CYP-induction-and-absorption counselling. Interactions (the nuclear-receptor PXR-and-CAR upregulation of CYP3A4/2C9 and P-glycoprotein and the oral-contraceptive-and-hormone failure, the warfarin-INR-plummeting requiring dose re-titration and the reverse upon stopping — [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]], the antiretroviral-and-anticonvulsant-and-methadone-and-antifungal-and-immunosuppressant (the ciclosporin-and-tacrolimus levels) and the corticosteroid-and-calcium-channel-blocker lists ([[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], [[immunology/transplant-tumour-immunology]]) — the "start-rifampicin-recheck-everything" rule and its 2-week-offset-and-graduated-withdrawal upon stopping (the induction-on-and-off hysteresis).**

::: exercise Random shadows {level=2}
An HIV-positive man has fever, weight loss, a miliary-pattern film, a sodium of 127 and a normal-ish auscultation. Differential, the two fastest useful tests, and the cortisol question.
::: solution
**Miliary (disseminated) tuberculosis — the random small-shadow distribution of haematogenous seeding (the chest-film pattern-distribution lesson of [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]] — versus the tree-in-bud and centrilobular distribution of endobronchial spread, and Pneumocystis (the perihilar ground-glass with disproportionate hypoxia and raised LDH) and the fungal, lymphomatous and sarcoid alternatives. Fastest: the blood-and-sputum-and-urine mycobacterial-culture-and-Xpert (the urine-LAM in the advanced-immunosuppression with the proteinuria-and-HIV-specific performance caveats) and the induced-sputum/BAL; the ophthalmic-choroidal-tubercles-and-the-lumbar-puncture (the basal-meningitis screen) and the CT-and-the-liver-and-node-biopsy tiers. Sodium 127: the SIADH-versus-adrenal question — miliary TB's adrenal destruction (the Addisonian-hyponatraemia-hyperkalaemia-and-hypotension screen — [[endocrine/adrenal]] and [[endocrine/endocrine-emergencies]]) — the 9 am cortisol and the cosyntropin-stimulation test and the empiric-cover-while-testing judgement in the shocked patient. Management sequence: the immediate ART-and-TB sequencing with the IRIS anticipation ([[immunology/immunodeficiency]]), the meningeal-and-pericardial-steroid evidence applications, and the public-health machinery of [[#ex-tb]] — with the Pneumocystis co-treatment-thought-experiment's TMP-SMX double-duty ([[immunology/immunodeficiency]]).**

::: exercise Design the respiratory-infection programme {level=3}
For a region with high TB incidence and winter pneumonia pressure, design the two-track programme: the viral-and-pneumococcal prevention-and-acute pathway, and the TB elimination trajectory — with the physiological and epidemiological logic per line.
::: solution
**Pneumonia track: the universal-and-risk-tier pneumococcal-and-influenza-and-RSV-and-COVID-vaccination architecture (the mucosal-and-systemic-immunity and herd arithmetic — [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]), the smoking-and-biomass-and-indoor-air policies ([[public-health/environmental-occupational]]), the aspiration-risk register (the post-stroke and psychotropic and care-home swallow-and-dental programmes — [[nervous-system/stroke]] and [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]]), the CAP pathway (the pulse-oximetry-and-CURB-65 triage at every front door including the urgent-care and out-of-hours with the community-antibiotic protocol and the oxygen-without-ventilation warning — [[respiratory/gas-exchange]]), the empyema-and-pleural-service (the ultrasound-first tapping culture-and-drain protocol and the VATS access — [[#ex-cap-empyema]]), the ICU-and-NIV capacity and the post-pneumonia follow-up-with-cancer-exclusion rule ([[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]]); and the hospital bundle (the VAP-prevention and the antimicrobial-stewardship-team-and-antibiogram-audit — [[microbiology/infection-control]], [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]). **TB track: the passive-case-finding upgrade (the symptomatic-presenters Xpert-first policy and the contact-and- household- and-high-risk-cohorts active screening with the symptom-and-radiology-and-IGRA triage — the symptomatic-and-cavitary-and-smear-positive-prevalence yield arithmetic — [[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]]'s prevalence-optimised sequencing), the infection-prevention cascade (the triage-and-airflow-and-N95-and-quick-diagnosis logic — the shortest transmission-window is the fastest test), the treatment programme (the fixed-dose-combination and susceptibility-testing and shorter-MDR-regimen access and the adherence-and-social-support (the transport-and-food-and-job-protection) and the latent-infection-treatment for the eligible contacts and the immunosuppressed-before-biologics — [[public-health/communicable-disease-control]]), the HIV-TB joint services (the bidirectional-screening and the ART-timing-and-IRIS protocols — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]]), and the elimination arithmetic (the notification-completeness audit — the invisible-treatment-failure surveillance, the annual-incidence-decline-targets-4-5-10-%-per-step modelling and the vaccine pipeline (the M72-and-adjacent candidates — [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]]), the whole built on the chapter's core physiology: transmission requires a coughing, cavitating, untreated human, and every programme line shortens one of those words.**
