Between the airways and the vessels sits the interstitium — and a vascular tree of its own. This chapter joins two clinicopathological worlds: **the interstitial lung diseases (alveolitis and fibrosis of the wall — restriction, low transfer factor and exercise desaturation as the physiology triad of the previous chapters) and the pulmonary circulation (acute thromboembolism and chronic hypertension of the right heart — dead-space and afterload physiology)**. The ILD craft is pattern-diagnosis (exposure history, serology, HRCT and biopsy read by a multidisciplinary team); the vascular craft is risk-stratification (the Wells-and-D-dimer gate and the shock-to-thrombolysis ladder and the pulmonary- hypertension-specific therapy pathways).

This chapter covers the fibrosing and inflammatory ILDs, sarcoidosis and pneumoconioses, and pulmonary embolism and pulmonary hypertension.

## The interstitium

::: definition The fibrosing cascade and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis {#def-ipf}
Chronic ILD is, in most cases, **repeated epithelial injury with dysregulated repair: the aged-and-susceptible alveolocyte (the telomere-biology and MUC5B-promoter and surfactant-gene risk architecture — [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]] and the cell chapter's senescence) undergoes abnormal activation and epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition, with fibroblast and myofibroblast foci laying matrix (the UIP fibroblastic focus), the scaffold honeycombing (the end-stage cystic remodelling of [[pathology/chronic-inflammation-repair]]), and the traction bronchiectasis, in contrast to the older "inflammation-first" model that still fits the cellular/hypersensitivity and connective-tissue patterns.** **Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF)** is the archetype: **the older man (ex-smoker and occupational-dust and gastro-oesophageal-reflux associations), the dry cough and breathlessness and Velcro basal crackles and clubbing, the restrictive gases with the disproportionately low DLCO and exercise desaturation ([[respiratory/mechanics-breathing]] and [[respiratory/gas-exchange]]'s measurements), and the HRCT usual-interstitial-pneumonia pattern (basal-and-subpleural reticulation and honeycombing with traction, minimal ground-glass) diagnosed without biopsy when the pattern is definite at the multidisciplinary conference (biopsy's surgical-histopathological UIP — heterogeneity and fibroblastic foci and honeycomb change) — with the acute-exacerbation and pulmonary-hypertension and lung-cancer and trap-population (the progressive-pulmonary-fibrosis phenotype beyond IPF) complications.** Management: **the antifibrotics (nintedanib — the multi-receptor-tyrosine-kinase (VEGF-and-FGF-and-PDG) blockade; pirfenidone — the pleiotropic anti-fibrotic; both slowing FVC decline (the widget's two lines) without cure, with the diarrhoea-and-liver-and-photosensitive-rash counselling and the drug-interaction and pregnancy caveats), the transplant referral (the timing-by-trajectory logic — FVC-drop-and-desaturation-and-hospitalisation markers — before the window closes — [[immunology/transplant-tumour-immunology]]), the supportive programme (vaccination and pulmonary rehabilitation and oxygen (ambulatory and long-term by the failure rules of [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]) and cough management and reflux-and-sleep-and-antidepressant adjuncts and the advance-planning conversation the trajectory demands — [[geriatrics-palliative/palliative-care]]), and the avoidance list (the empirical "steroid-and-immunosuppression for everything" era's harm in UIP, and the acute-exacerbation and infection vigilance).**
:::

::: definition The ILD differential: exposures, drugs, systemic disease, granulomas and cysts {#def-ild-list}
The "ILDS" work-up (history-first: **the birds-and-mould-and-hay (chronic hypersensitivity pneumonitis — the upper-and-mid-zone-dominant fibrosis with centrilobular nodules and the air-trapping-mosaic of small-airway disease — antigen-avoidance-as-treatment and the bird-keeper's fibrotic-progression trap — [[immunology/hypersensitivity]] type-III-and-IV), the workplace dusts (the coal-and-silica (the upper-zone nodules-and-eggshell-calcification and mycobacterial-susceptibility and the Caplan-nodules-with-rheumatoid-arthritis variant — [[public-health/environmental-occupational]]-and-[[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]]-adjacent), the asbestos (the basal fibrosis with pleural plaques-and-effusions-and-the-lung-cancer-multiplication-and-mesothelioma-latency arithmetic — [[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]] and [[pathology/environmental-pathology]]), the beryllium-and-chronic-granulomatous-mimic-of-sarcoid), the drugs (the amiodarone chronic-pneumonitis-and-organising patterns with the thyroid-and-liver-and-skin ledger — [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]-chapter's-own; nitrofurantoin acute-and-chronic; methotrexate pneumonitis and the rare MTX-associated lymphoproliferative; the bleomycin and busulfan and checkpoint-inhibitor and EGFR-inhibitor and TNF-blocker lists — [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]] and [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]]-adjacent oncology), and the systemic diseases (rheumatoid and systemic-sclerosis ILD — the antisynthetase-and-MDA5-myositis rapidly-progressive pattern with its dermatologic tells — [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]]; the ANCA-associated capillaritis and diffuse-alveolar-haemorrhage overlap — [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]]).** The granulomatous and cystic corners: **sarcoidosis (the bilateral hilar adenopathy and upper-zone micronodular-lymphatic distribution, the non-caseating granuloma opposite TB's caseating — [[respiratory/respiratory-infections]]'s discriminator — the oculo-glandular (the uveoparotid and lacrimo-salivary — [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]]), cardiac (the block-and-arrhythmia-and-sudden-death screen — [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]]), renal-and-hypercalcaemia (the granulocyte 1α-hydroxylase's vitamin-D autonomy — [[endocrine/bone-calcium]]) and neuro-sarcoidosis; the ACE level's limited discrimination; the "observe-steroids-for-symptomatic-organ-threatening-and-sparing-agents-and-the-chronic-progressives" ladder — most remit), Langerhans-cell histiocytosis (the smoker's upper-zone cysts-and-nodules and the pneumothorax and pituitary-diabetes-insipidus — [[endocrine/hypothalamus-pituitary]]), lymphangioleiomyomatosis (the childbearing-age chylothorax-and-pneumothorax-and-renal-angiomyolipases-and-TSC-tuberous-sclerosis association and the sirolimus therapy — [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]'s tumour-suppressor)), the organising-pneumonitis syndromes (the patchy migratory consolidation of cryptogenic organising pneumonia and the post-infectious-and-drug-and-connective-tissue and radiation patterns — steroid-responsive and relapsing), and the rare (the pulmonary alveolar proteinosis (GM-CSF autoimmunity — treated by whole-lung lavage), the familial-telomere syndromes' mucocutaneous-and-liver-and-bone-marrow — [[blood/haematopoiesis]]-adjacent — and the light-chain and amyloid deposition — [[blood/lymphoma-myeloma]]).** The diagnostic instrument-set: HRCT pattern-and-distribution reading, the BAL (cellular differentials — lymphocytic-versus-neutrophilic-and-eosinophilic-PJP-and-haemorrhage-haemosiderin-macrophages — [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]) and transbronchial-cryobiopsy-versus-surgical-biopsy risk-benefit, the serology panel (the RF-and-CCP-and-ENA-and-myositis-and-ANCA-with-clinical-rheumatology-judgement — the "idiopathic-NSIP-that-becomes-SSc" phenomenon), and the multidisciplinary-team pattern-plus-context verdict that defines modern ILD diagnosis — with the longitudinal physiology (the FVC and DLCO trajectory) as the treatment-response instrument ([[#def-ipf]]'s decline lines).**
:::

::: example The wrong-COPD patient {#ex-ipf}
A 69-year-old retired plumber has 2 years of "incompletely treated asthma-COPD" despite inhalers; dry cough and progressive dyspnoea, RR 22, SpO₂ 91 % walking (resting 95 %), bibasal end-inspiratory crackles, clubbing; spirometry FEV₁ 1.6 FVC 1.9 (ratio 0.84), DLCO 38 %.
::: solution
**Restrictive-and-poor-transfer physiology mislabelled as obstruction — the classic IPF delay (a median of years in every registry) — send the HRCT.** The expected pattern and the pathway: **basal-and-subpleural-predominant reticulation with honeycombing and traction bronchiectasis (the definite-UIP radiological diagnosis — no biopsy needed when the pattern is definite at MDT; the BAL and serology to exclude the mimics: the hypersensitivity-exposure re-history (the plumbing loft insulation and the damp rooms) and the connective-tissue serology screen and the drug list — [[#def-ild-list]]).** Once confirmed: **stop the inhaled-steroid-drift, vaccinate (influenza-and-pneumococcal-and-herpes-zoster and the RSV era), start the antifibrotic discussion (nintedanib-or-pirfenidone with the diarrhoea-and-rash-and-liver-monitoring and adherence-titration plans — the trajectory-slowing, life-horizon-extending evidence of [[#def-ipf]]), refer early for transplant assessment (the age-band and fitness judgement and the listing-trajectory logic) or the equally-valid palliative-care-shared pathway (the cough-and-breathlessness-symptom toolkit and the advance-care-planning and preferred-place-of-dying documentation — [[geriatrics-palliative/palliative-care]]), the pulmonary-rehabilitation-and-oxygen-and-flights-and-insurance practicalities, the lung-cancer-and-PAH-and-exacerbation surveillance, and the family's-telomere-and-MUC5B-counselling question (mostly sporadic; the familial 5-10 % and its implications — [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]]).** The teaching point beyond the case: **crackles-plus-clubbing-plus-normal-or-high-ratio-plus-low-DLCO = interstitium until proven otherwise; the DLCO is the discriminates-everything number of the pulmonary function laboratory ([[respiratory/gas-exchange]]'s interpretation algorithm).**

## The pulmonary circulation

::: definition Pulmonary embolism: dead space made clinical {#def-pe}
Pulmonary embolism is **the occlusion of pulmonary arteries (almost always embolic from the deep-leg-and-pelvic veins — [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]'s Virchow-triad-epidemiology and risk-factor table — the occasional in-situ, tumour, air, fat and amniotic varieties) producing three parallel injuries: the dead-space (ventilated-unperfused units — the Vd/Vt rise and the CO₂-inefficiency and the hypoxaemia of atelectasis-and-shunt-more-than-the-occluded-unit-itself ([[respiratory/gas-exchange]]'s arithmetic)), the pulmonary-vascular-resistance surge (the mechanical-obstruction plus the humoral serotonin-and-thromboxane vasoconstriction) loading the RV ([[cardiovascular/heart-failure]]'s RV-afterload chapter: the thin normal RV fails acutely at suprasystemic pressures it never evolved to face — the dilation, the septal-bow-leftward, the LV-underfilling and the obstructive-shock spiral of [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]]'s four-classification, and the coronary-perfusion-pressure-fall-and-RV-ischaemia vicious circle), and the parenchymal infarction minority (the pleuritic-pain-and-haemoptysis-and-effusion of the distal-small-embolus with collaterals intact).** Presentation spans silent-to-sudden-death (the dyspnoea-pleurisy-isolated-tachycardia-syncope-hypotension spectrum — the syncope as the saddle-RV-failure marker and the mimic-of-everything — the anxiety and the musculoskeletal and the "asthma" mislabels). Diagnosis: the **pretest-probability-and-D-dimer gate (the Wells-and-Geneva rules and the age-adjusted-D-dimer and the PERC-out rule for the very-low-risk — [[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]]'s tiered-testing exemplar), then CTPA (the filling-defect and the RV/LV ratio and the incidental findings) or the V/Q (the pregnancy-and-renal-and-contrast and the chronic-PH work-up preference — [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]'s dose-and-pedigree logic) or the leg-vein ultrasound in the pregnancy-and-calf-symptom settings; the chronic-symptom pathway is the V/Q-scan-to-catheter CTEPH hunt (the mismatched-perfusion-segments that CTPA misses — [[#def-ph]]).** **Risk-stratified management (the ESC-classic): high-risk (shock-or-arrest — the fluid-cautious resuscitation and the catecholamines and immediate reperfusion — systemic thrombolysis with its bleeding-and-recent-surgery-and-stroke caveat-list, and the catheter-embolectomy-thrombolysis-and-surgical-embolectomy-and-ECMO-bridge rescue tiers) → intermediate (the RV-dysfunction-and-troponin-positive "intermediate-high" monitored bed with the deterioration-rescue plan) → low-risk (the same-day-or-48-hour-discharge with the direct-oral-anticoagulant and the safety-net education).** Anticoagulation (the heparin-to-DOAC-on-rivaroxaban-or-apixaban-single-drug-start and the dabigatran-and-edoxaban-parenteral-lead-in, and the warfarin-LMWH-and-DOAC-versus-LMWH trials in the cancer-associated-thrombosis — with the gastrointestinal-tumour-bleeding caveat; the antiphospholipid-triple-positives' DOAC-avoidance — [[immunology/hypersensitivity]]-adjacent; the pregnancy-LMWH-and-no-DOAC-and-no-warfarin-trimesters rules — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]]; the HIT alternatives — [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]), the duration decision (the provoked-3-months-and-unprovoked-and-ongoing-risk-factor-extension-and-DOAC-dose-reduction-reassessment, the sex-and-recurrence-scoring refinements, the thrombophilia-testing-when-it-changes-management rule, and the IVC-filter-niches and the thrombectomy-and-embolectomy escalation — [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]] and [[surgery/vascular-urological-surgery]]), and the after-care (the CTEPH-suspecting-non-resolving-breathlessness-revisit — the 6-minute-walk-and-gases-and-echo-and-V/Q follow-up, the anticoagulation-for-the-cancer-and-APLA-and-recurrent-lifetimes, and the thrombosis-prevention-bundles-in-surgery-and-medicine-and-pregnancy-and-travel — [[surgery/perioperative-care]] and [[public-health/environmental-occupational]]'s long-haul advice).**
:::

::: definition Pulmonary hypertension: the five groups {#def-ph}
Pulmonary hypertension is **a haemodynamic definition (mean PAP >20 mmHg at rest on right-heart catheterisation — the echo's probability-estimate being a screen, not the definition) subdivided by PVR (≥2 Wood-unit the modern threshold) into pre-capillary (PCWP ≤15 — the arterial diseases) and post-capillary (PCWP >15 — the left-heart disease of [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]], the commonest group worldwide, with the combined-post-pret-capillary hybrid).** The clinical groups: **(1 — pulmonary arterial hypertension: the idiopathic-heritable (the BMPR2 and the ALK1-and-GDF2-and-SMAD9-BMP-signalling genetics — [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]'s incomplete-penetrance-and-second-hit) and the connective-tissue (systemic-sclerosis-dominant) and portal-and-congenital-shunt-and-HIV-and-drug-and-toxin (the aminorex-appetite-suppressant-history and the current-methamphetamine) and the persistent-pulmonary-hypertension-of-the-newborn and the pulmonary-veno-occlusive-disease (whose venous involvement makes arterial vasodilators dangerous)) — the young-female-presenting-dyspnoea-with-the-loud-P2-and-RV-heave-and-RV-failure-signs-and-the-ECG-and-echo-and-catheter-confirmation-and-vasoreactivity-testing-then-combination-therapy-into-the-endothelin (ambrisentan-and-bosentan-and-sitaxentan-the-teratogenicity-and-liver-caveats) and PDE5-and-sGC (sildenafil-tadalafil-and-riociguat — the nitric-oxide-pathway — [[cardiovascular/vascular-disease]]'s-own-cousin-pharmacology) and prostacyclin (epoprostenol-treprostinil-selexipag — the IV-pump-infection-and-flushing-jaw-pain-and-diarrhoea logistics) axes and the transplant-and-atrial-septostomy-endgame; (2 — the left-heart group); (3 — the lung-disease-and-hypoxia group (the COPD-severity-correlation-and-the-desaturating-ILD-disproportionate-phenotype-and-the-treprostinil-inhaled-trial-and-the-LTOT-treatment-of-the-hypoxic-driver — [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]-and-[[#def-ipf]]); (4 — chronic thromboembolic (CTEPH: the organised-and-fibrotic-occluded-and webs-and-bands and the mismatched-V-Q-scan-screen and the surgical pulmonary endarterectomy cure and the balloon-pulmonary-angioplasty and the riociguat-medical-therapy and the lifelong-anticoagulation — [[#def-pe]]'s chronic sequel); (5 — the miscellaneous (the haematological — myeloproliferative-and-haemoglobinopathies-and-splenectomy ([[blood/haemoglobin-disorders]]-and-[[blood/leukaemia]]) and systemic and rare disorders with multi-hit physiology).** The shared pathobiology — **the smooth-muscle constriction, remodelling and in-situ thrombosis of small pulmonary arteries (the endothelin-and-NO-and-prostacyclin-imbalance and the BMP-signaling-loss — [[pathology/haemodynamics]]-adjacent) ending in RV failure (the [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]]'s forgotten-ventricle chapter: the coronary-perfusion-and-interdependence-and-tricuspid-regurgitation spiral)** — unifies the groups and their monitoring (the 6-minute-walk-and-BNP-and-catheter-haemodynamics-and-REVEAL-style-risk-composites and the pregnancy-and-high-altitude-and-exercise-and-dehydration avoidances — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]]'s Eisenmenger-adjacent maternal-mortality figures).**
:::

::: example The post-flight breathlessness {#ex-pe}
A 32-year-old, 3 days after a long-haul flight and taking the combined oral contraceptive, has sudden pleuritic pain and dyspnoea; HR 108, RR 22, SpO₂ 94 %, BP 128/76; Wells score "PE likely" (4.5); D-dimer not sent.
::: solution
**Probable PE in a haemodynamically stable patient: go to CTPA (the positive-pretest skips the D-dimer entirely — the rule-out test's purpose is the unlikely group — [[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]])** with pregnancy-status-first-imagining-adjustment (the bilateral-leg-ultrasound-and-V-Q-versus-CTPA-dose-discussion of [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]] if she is early-pregnant — the [[#def-pe]] imaging trade-offs). Meanwhile: **the anticoagulation-does-not-wait-for-the-scan-when-probability-is-high-and-bleeding-risk-low (the DOAC-loading regimen or LMWH per the local protocol), then risk-stratify on CT result (the RV/LV ratio and the troponin and the ECG S1Q3T3-and-RV-strain and the sPESI — the intermediate-high gets the monitored bed and the rescue-thrombolysis plan written in the notes, [[#def-pe]]'s ladder).** The after-decision list: **the duration and provocation logic (the transient risk factors — flight and oestrogen — the 3-month standard and the oestrogen-contraception switch to a progestogen-only or non-hormonal method — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/contraception-fertility]] — and the thrombophilia-testing-only-if-it-changes-management with the antiphospholipid-and-DOAC-avoidance caveat), the CTEPH-aware follow-up (the persisting-dyspnoea-revisit pathway), the travel-and-surgery-and-pregnancy-future-prophylaxis counselling, and the recurrence-versus-bleeding conversation about extended-therapy if unprovoked-recurrence appears ([[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]).**

::: example The blocked heart and the hilar shadows {#ex-sarcoid}
A 34-year-old Afro-Caribbean woman attends with 3 weeks of bilateral painful red eyes and ankle swellings and cough; chest film shows bilateral hilar enlargement; routine biochemistry shows calcium 3.2; 2 days later she has a syncopal episode and the ECG shows complete heart block.
::: solution
**The oculo-glandular-and-erythema-nodosum-and-bilateral-hilar-adenopathy presentation (Lofgren's syndrome — usually self-limiting and no biopsy needed when the picture is classic — [[#def-ild-list]]) has just declared its dangerous face: cardiac sarcoidosis (the granulomatous and scar tissue at the septum and conduction system — the block-and-ventricular-tachycardia-and-sudden-death risk — [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]]).** The urgent sequence: **admission-and-telemetry, the cardiac MRI (and the FDG-PET patchy-uptake pattern — [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]), the tissue diagnosis (the mediastinal-node or endomyocardial biopsy decision), the high-dose glucocorticoids (± the steroid-sparing or anti-TNF for refractory disease), and the pacemaker/ICU planning (the lead-extraction-free strategy and the ICD discussion for the ventricular-arrhythmia and scar-burden phenotypes — [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]]); the simultaneous hypercalcaemia management (rehydration and steroid's granuloma-suppression and the vitamin-D-and-calcium-stop list — [[endocrine/bone-calcium]]), the ophthalmology uveitis co-management ([[nervous-system/sensory-systems]]), and the long follow-up (the relapse-and-chronic-progressives and the conduction-disease surveillance and the pregnancy-and-drug counselling).** The lesson: **sarcoidosis is a multi-organ disease whose mortality concentrates in the heart, the brain, the eyes and the advanced-fibrosis subgroup — the "observe" advice applies to the self-limiting majority, never to these four without specialist review.**
:::

::: example The clot that did not dissolve {#ex-cteph}
Two years after a proximal-PE and rivaroxaban (which she completed at 6 months), a 47-year-old has progressive exertional dyspnoea; NT-proBNP is rising, the echo shows RV hypertrophy and a PASP of 75, and the CTPA report notes "no acute embolism, subtle residual web in the right lower lobe artery".
::: solution
**Chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (group 4) until proven otherwise — the CTEPH reminder that the resolved-CT is not a recovered circulation: the organised-fibrotic occlusion and the secondary small-vessel disease ([[#def-ph]] group 4).** The work-up: **the V/Q scan (the sensitive screen — the mismatched-segmental defects that CT reporting under-calls — [[#def-pe]]'s chronic-pathway rule), the right-heart catheterisation (the pre-capillary confirmation and severity), the pulmonary angiography and CT-and-MRI mapping of the lesion level (the surgically-accessible-proximal versus the distal-small-vessel anatomy), and the exclude-the-mimics panel (the recurrent-VTE-and-thrombophilia-and-antiphospholipid hunt and the haematological and splenic and inflammatory work-up — [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]).** The treatment ladder: **the pulmonary endarterectomy (the potentially-curative surgery of the accessible disease — the specialist-centre volume-outcome relationship and the operability-conference judgement), the balloon pulmonary angioplasty for the inoperable-and-residual, the riociguat (the sGC-stimulator) medical therapy and the lifelong anticoagulation (the recurrence-protection rationale), and the transplant-endgame referral; plus the underlying-cause hunt for the unprovoked-index-PE and the IVC-filter-only-by-exception debate — the case as the [[#def-pe]] chapter's own safety-net made visible: every post-PE patient with persistent-or-new dyspnoea returns for perfusion imaging.**

::: quiz
A pregnant patient in her third trimester has a suspected PE after a negative leg-ultrasound. The preferred definitive scan is often V/Q rather than CTPA because:
- [ ] V/Q is more sensitive for central saddle emboli
- [x] V/Q (with its perfusion-only fallback) delivers a lower maternal breast dose and maintains good discrimination when the chest film is normal
- [ ] V/Q avoids fetal radiation entirely while CTPA cannot
- [ ] V/Q can also grade the RV dilatation
::: solution
**The dose-and-utility trade ([[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]] and [[#def-pe]]): with a normal chest X-ray the V/Q has excellent diagnostic performance, and the ventilation-plus-perfusion study's breast-tissue dose exceeds the perfusion-only (and the CTPA's own small fetal dose is unavoidable in both modalities). CTPA wins when the parenchyma is abnormal (the competing explanation), for the alternative diagnoses, and where the V/Q is non-diagnostic — the decision is made with the local protocol and the shared explanation.**

::: summary
- Chronic ILD is epithelial-injury-with-dysregulated-repair (or its inflammatory mimics); the triad of restriction + low DLCO + exercise desaturation with HRCT pattern and exposure-and-serology context, read at multidisciplinary conference, is the diagnostic method.
- IPF is the antifibrotic-treatable, transplant-trackable, palliation-needed phenotype; the FVC slope is the currency of every decision.
- The ILD differential is a list worth memorising (exposures, drugs, connective-tissue, granulomatous — sarcoidosis with its calcium and cardiac and ocular faces, and the cystic rare diseases) because each has a treatment or avoidance change.
- PE: pretest gate → imaging → risk stratification (shock = reperfusion; RV-and-troponin = monitored; low risk = early-discharge DOAC), with the duration-and-recurrence logic and the CTEPH-revisit safety net.
- Pulmonary hypertension is five diseases wearing one haemodynamic definition; group matters (the vasodilators help group 1 and harm groups 2-and-venous-disease), and the right ventricle is the prognosis.

::: exercise The calcium question {level=2}
A sarcoid patient has calcium 3.1 and creatinine 140 with polyuria. Explain the mechanism and its management, and name two other granulomatous causes.
::: solution
**The granuloma macrophages' 1α-hydroxylase converts 25-OH-D to 1,25-(OH)₂-D without the renal PTH-feedback control ([[endocrine/bone-calcium]]'s regulated-versus-autonomous-axis lesson) → increased intestinal calcium absorption → hypercalcaemia, hypercalciuria (the polyuria and nephrocalcinosis and the AKI — [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]), suppressed PTH. Management: the rehydration first, stop the vitamin-D-and-calcium supplements, glucocorticoids (the granuloma-suppression and the 1α-hydroxylase-downregulation), and the ketoconazole-and-hydroxychloroquine-second-line and the steroid-sparing; sun-avoidance counselling. Other causes: TB ([[respiratory/respiratory-infections]]) and fungal granulitides (histoplasma and coccidioides — [[microbiology/fungi-parasites]]) and beryllium-disease ([[pathology/environmental-pathology]]).**

::: exercise The fibrotic bird-keeper {level=2}
A 58-year-old budgerigar-breeder has 3 years of progressive dyspnoea; HRCT shows upper-dominant fibrosis with traction bronchiectasis, centrilobular nodules and marked mosaic attenuation on expiratory sequences. Why is the case both "too late" and "not too late", and what is the full plan?
::: solution
**Fibrotic chronic hypersensitivity pneumonitis (cHP): "too late" in that the remodelling-fibrosis (often UIP-or-fibrotic-nonspecific patterns) may be irreversible even with antigen removal, and the progression-behaviour (the progressive-pulmonary-fibrosis phenotype) can continue after exposure cessation; "not too late" because the bird must leave the house today (the continued-exposure fuel-and-severity argument), and because the inflammatory component, the small-airway disease and the progression all have levers.** The plan: **the formal antigen-eradication (the bird-and-other-antigen hunt including the damp and the down-duvets and the hood-and-air-purifier-counselling and occupational-health-and-housing implications — [[public-health/environmental-occupational]]), serology-and-precipitins-supporting-not-decisive, the MDT-diagnosis with BAL-lymphocytosis and biopsy-nuances ([[#def-ild-list]]), the steroid-or-steroid-sparing-trial-for-the-cellular-component (the chronic-immunosuppression-evidence-limits honesty) and the antifibrotic-consideration-for-progressive-phenotype (the progressive-fibrosing-ILD indications and the nintedanib slope-evidence), the trajectory-monitoring with the widget's serial-FVC discipline, the transplant-referral-window and the support as in [[#ex-ipf]].**

::: exercise Thrombolysis thresholds {level=2}
A massive-PE patient arrests in the resus bay; ROSC after 4 minutes on adrenaline. Give the thrombolysis decision, the regimen, the CPR caveats and the rescue alternatives.
::: solution

::: exercise Vasodilator logic {level=2}
A systemic-sclerosis woman (the diffuse-cutaneous, PAH-associated serology) has progressive dyspnoea; echo shows high-probability PH. Give the work-up, the vasoreactivity-test logic, and the three-drug-axis rationale.
::: solution
**Work-up: the right-heart-catheterisation confirmation (the mPAP >20 and PVR >2 and PCWP ≤15 pre-capillary definition — excluding the left-heart (group 2) and lung (group 3) and CTEPH (group 4 — the V/Q scan) mimics that change therapy — [[#def-ph]]'s group-first rule), with the aetiology bundle (the nailfold-capillaroscopy-and-serology of [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]], the HIV-and-liver-and-thyroid screen, and the family-and-drug history).** **Vasoreactivity testing (the inhaled-NO-or-IV-epoprostenol challenge at catheter: the acute-responders — a small minority, mostly idiopathic/heritable — may take high-dose calcium-channel-blockers; non-responders proceed to the advanced axes).** The three axes as receptor physiology ([[pharmacology/drug-receptor]]): **the endothelin-receptor-antagonists (the ET_A-and-ET_B blockade — ambrisentan-and-bosentan-and-sitaxentan with the teratogenicity-and-liver-and-anaemia programme), the nitric-oxide pathway (the PDE5-inhibition-sildenafil-tadalafil and the sGC-stimulation-riociguat — the cGMP amplification — [[cardiovascular/vascular-disease]]'s same-cascade drugs), the prostacyclin pathway (the IP-receptor agonists — epoprostenol IV-pump and treprostinil and the oral selexipag — the cAMP vasodilation-and-platelet-antagonism), with the risk-composite (functional class-and-6MWD-and-BNP-and-haemodynamics) driving the initial-combination and IV-prostacyclin-for-high-risk strategies, the transplant-and-septostomy-endgame and the pregnancy-avoidance-absolute-contraindication counselling ([[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]])).**
