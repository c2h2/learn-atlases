Asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease are the two chronic airway diseases read off the same spirometer from opposite ends: asthma — variable, inflammatory, usually reversible airflow limitation with hyper-responsiveness, a disease of T2 immunity and smooth muscle; COPD — progressive, largely fixed limitation from small-airway fibrosis and parenchymal destruction (emphysema), a disease of noxious-particle dose, innate inflammation and failed repair. They share the exacerbation-and-decline trajectory that hospital beds are made of, the controlled-oxygen and ventilatory-support physiology of the sister chapters, and the management philosophy that **stop the exposure, inhaled pharmacology up the ladder, rehabilitation, and the rescue protocols for the two emergencies (the acute severe asthma and the acute-on-chronic hypercapnic failure)**.

This chapter covers asthma immunology and management, COPD pathophysiology and its long-term programme, and the acute exacerbations.

## Asthma: the T2 airway

::: definition Asthma: mechanism, phenotypes and diagnosis {#def-asthma}
Asthma is **chronic airway inflammation with variable expiratory airflow limitation and hyper-responsiveness**: in the allergic (T2-high) phenotype **the epithelial alarmins (TSLP, IL-25, IL-33 — the damper of the epithelium's immune control) licence dendritic cells and ILC2s to drive Th2 cytokines — IL-4 (IgE class-switching — [[immunology/adaptive-responses]]), IL-5 (eosinophil maturation and survival — [[blood/haematopoiesis]]'s eosinophil colony biology) and IL-13 (goblet metaplasia, mucus hypersecretion, smooth-muscle hyperplasia and hyper-responsiveness) — with allergen cross-linking of mast-cell IgE (the type-I immediate and late reactions and the exercise, viral and aspirin amplifiers — [[immunology/hypersensitivity]])**. The effector physiology: **oedema, mucus plugging, and bronchial smooth-muscle contraction acting on calibre and on the flow limitation of [[respiratory/mechanics-breathing]] — the wheeze, the hyperinflation, the V/Q mismatch with its raised A–a gradient, the air trapping, and the early-morning and exercise- and cold- and allergen-triggered variations**; the remodelling: **basement-membrane thickening, angiogenesis and muscle hypertrophy — the fixed component of long-standing disease ([[pathology/chronic-inflammation-repair]])**. The **phenotypes**: allergic (early-onset, atopic, eosinophilic, FeNO-high, IgE-driven), non-allergic adult-onset (often neutrophilic or paucigranulocytic), aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease (the COX-shift and cysteinyl-leukotriene overproduction triad with nasal polyps — [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]), obesity-associated (mechanical and leptin-and-adipokine driven, T2-low), late-onset eosinophilic (often non-atopic), and the triggers-plus-occupational lists — with the the smoking-affected "asthma-COPD overlap" ([[#def-copd]]). **Diagnosis needs variable symptoms plus objective variability** (the fixed pre/post-bronchodilator FEV₁ improvement ≥12 % **and** ≥200 mL — the spirometric reversibility criterion; or diurnal peak-flow variability >10 % (20 % in the older criteria debates) over weeks; or a positive **mannitol/methacholine challenge** (the hyper-responsiveness surrogate, high negative predictive value — its exclusionary use — [[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]]); or the treatment-and-exacerbation trial design of primary care), **FeNO and blood eosinophils and total and specific IgE as the T2-biomarker panel that both supports the diagnosis and prescribes the biologic**, and **the differential discipline (the flow-loop flattening of vocal-cord dysfunction — [[respiratory/control-breathing-tests]]; heart failure's "cardiac asthma" — [[cardiovascular/heart-failure]]; bronchiectasis and ABPA (the Aspergillus-sensitised eosinophilic wrecking — [[immunology/hypersensitivity]] and [[microbiology/fungi-parasites]]); eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis; the cough-variant and the preserved-ratio (PRISm) patterns)**.
:::

::: definition Asthma management: the ladder, the reliever, and the biologics {#def-asthma-rx}
The modern framework (GINA's convergence): **inhaled corticosteroid is the disease-modifying core (the T2 inflammation suppressor whose under-use — and the standalone short-acting β₂-agonist habit — carries the preventable asthma deaths), delivered preferably as ICS-formoterol (the fast-β₂-agonist-as-bronchodilator-and-maintenance molecule permitting single-inhaler maintenance-and-reliever therapy — the SMART and SYGMA and MANDALA-era trials), with the ladder: as-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol (mild), → low-dose maintenance-and-reliever, → medium-dose, → add LAMA (the long-acting muscarinic antagonist — the antimuscarinic bronchodilation pharmacology of [[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]]), → high-dose, → the add-on biologics and the oral-steroid-minimisation strategy (tiotropium and azithromycin niches and the bronchial thermoplastics' minority role)**. **Biologics by biomarker (the phenotype-matching example of [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]]'s selectivity story): omalizumab (anti-IgE — the allergic, positive-skin-test, IgE-band phenotype and the chronic-urticaria co-indication — [[immunology/hypersensitivity]]), mepolizumab and benralizumab (anti-IL-5 and anti-IL-5Rα with eosinophil antibody-dependent killing — the eosinophilic phenotype), dupilumab (IL-4Rα — blocking both IL-4 and IL-13 with its eosinophil-transient and conjunctivitis and parasitic-cautions — [[immunology/immunodeficiency]]-adjacent helminth logic), tezepelumab (anti-TSLP — the alarmin-upstream, broad-biomarker strategy)**; the oral corticosteroid-burden arithmetic (the cumulative-dose harm ledger — [[endocrine/adrenal]]'s suppression and the bone, glucose, cataract and weight costs — [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]]) is what these drugs are priced against. The **non-pharmacological spine**: inhaler technique and adherence (the measured-inhaler audits' embarrassment — [[clinical-skills/communication]]), the written action plan and self-management (the exacerbation-window and the oral-steroid-burst rules), trigger and occupational control, smoking and weight and exercise (the EIB — exercise-induced bronchoconstriction — pre-treatment and the swimmer's observation), the aspirin desensitisation protocol in AERD, the **pregnancy continuity (ICS-and-formoterol continued — the control-better-than-the-steroid-risk arithmetic — [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]])**, vaccination and the **exacerbation protocol (the acute severe and life-threatening asthma — the severity classification, the controlled oxygen and the repeated-nebulisation-plus-ipratropium-plus-systemic-steroid-plus-IV-magnesium sequence and the IV-β₂-agonist and ventilation last-resort ladder — the mechanics of [[respiratory/mechanics-breathing]] and the ABG alarms of [[respiratory/control-breathing-tests]] and the critical-care transfer — [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]], [[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]])**.
:::

::: example The night-shift nurse's wheeze {#ex-asthma-dx}
A 34-year-old has 6 months of nocturnal cough and wheeze worse at the end of night shifts, using a borrowed salbutamol inhaler twice weekly; spirometry: FEV₁ 3.1 L (86 %), FVC 4.0, ratio 0.78, improving to FEV₁ 3.6 L after salbutamol; FeNO 58 ppb; eosinophils 0.5 ×10⁹/L.
::: solution
**New asthma, T2-high, occupational-featured — diagnosed properly rather than prescribed properly.** The arithmetic: **post-bronchodilator FEV₁ improvement of 0.5 L (16 %) exceeds the ≥12 %-and-200 mL reversibility criterion (the baseline ratio being normal — variability, not fixed obstruction, is asthma's signature — [[#def-asthma]] and the "can I have your inhaler?" prescribing trap of the borrowed reliever); the FeNO (raised — the IL-13-driven epithelial nitric oxide synthase induction) and the eosinophilia both predict steroid response, and the night-shift pattern demands the workplace exposure history (the clean-and-haircare-product and isocyanate and flour-and-lipase agents of occupational asthma — the serial peak-flow-at-work-and-away diary and the specialist referral with its compensation-and-removal-from-exposure consequences — [[public-health/environmental-occupational]]).** Management: **launch the ICS-containing regimen immediately (the low-dose ICS-formoterol as-needed-or-maintenance strategy by symptom burden, with the reliever-no-longer-alone rule), inhaler technique taught-and-checked with the spacer discussion, the written action plan (the peak-flow zones and the prednisolone-burst script and the urgent-care triggers), the occupational remediation pathway, the follow-up spirometry-and-symptom review at 4–6 weeks, and the adherence-prescribing simplifications (the single-inhaler logic and the once-daily preference).** The teaching: **asthma is diagnosed by variability plus T2 evidence — a normal ratio with 16 % reversibility is asthma, and a low FEV₁ that never varies is the wrong chapter ([[#def-copd]]).**
:::

## COPD: the progressive airway

::: definition COPD: exposure, innate inflammation, and the two phenotypes {#def-copd}
COPD is **persistent airflow limitation from airway and alveolar disease — the noxious-particle burden (tobacco dominating; biomass fuel, occupational dusts and the post-TB and post-infectious and alpha-1-antitrypsin-deficiency minorities — [[#ex-a1at]]) producing innate and adaptive inflammation — alveolar macrophages, neutrophils (the neutrophil elastase and matrix-metalloproteinase proteolysis), CD8 T cells and the oxidative-stress amplification — the anti-elastase shortfall (the protease-antiprotease hypothesis: SERPINA1's PiZZ codominant inheritance and its basal panlobular emphysema and liver-disease doublet — [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]), the failure of repair (the alveolar-septal loss and the capillary rarefaction), and the small-airway fibrosis and exudate (the obliterative bronchiolitis that is the fixed-limitation's seat)** — with the structural phenotypes: **emphysema (centrilobular-upper — smoking; paraseptal-and-bullous; panlobular-basal — alpha-1): the loss of elastic recoil (the [[respiratory/mechanics-breathing]] flow-limitation and dynamic hyperinflation mechanics, the DLCO fall — [[respiratory/gas-exchange]]'s diffusion exercise), and chronic bronchitis (the Reid-index gland hypertrophy and the mucus hypersecretion and the infective-exacerbation axis, the "blue bloater" physiology of the chronic bronchitic with the recurrent infective exacerbations and the shunt-level hypoxaemia and the hypercapnia tendency) with the overlap of the two dominating practice; plus the **emphysematous gas-exchange failure endgame (the destruction of the vascular bed → the V/Q scatter → the type-1 then type-2 failure and the cor pulmonale of [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]'s pulmonary-hypertension territory).** **Diagnosis and staging: the post-bronchodilator FEV₁/FVC <0.70 (the fixed-ratio convention with its age-underdiagnosis and overdiagnosis caveats — the lower-limit-of-normal alternative and the PRISm discussion — [[epidemiology-ebm/measuring-disease]]) with the GOLD spirometric grades 1–4 and the symptom-exacerbation ABCD(E)-style group assessment (the mMRC and CAT questionnaires and the exacerbation history driving the pharmacology), the DLCO-and-imaging sub-phenotyping (the CT emphysema distribution and the bullae and the bronchiectasis and the incidental-nodule surveillance of [[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]])** and the **exacerbation definition (the acute respiratory symptom change beyond daily variation requiring extra therapy — the infective (viral — rhino-and influenza-and-RSV — [[microbiology/viruses]]; bacterial — the Haemophilus-and-Moraxella-and-pneumococcus purulence axis — [[microbiology/bacteria]]) triggers, the dysbiotic-airway hypothesis, and the AECOPD's mortality-and-decline arithmetic: each exacerbation hurts and predicts the next).**
:::

::: example The pack-year builder {#ex-copd-stable}
A 63-year-old builder (45 pack-years) is breathless walking his usual site stairs (mMRC 2), with 250 mL sputum most mornings and no recent infections; post-bronchodilator spirometry: FEV₁ 1.9 L (58 %), FVC 3.2, ratio 0.59; SpO₂ 95 %; DLCO 68 % predicted.
::: solution
**COPD, GOLD grade 2/3 with symptoms, emphysema-plus-chronic-bronchitis mix (the DLCO reduction says destruction, not pure airway disease — the [[respiratory/gas-exchange]] and [[respiratory/mechanics-breathing]] chapters converging on the CT-visible phenotype).** The management programme, in mortality-then-symptom order: **smoking cessation (the single intervention that changes the decline slope of the widget — the varenicline-and-NRT-and-combination pharmacotherapy and the behavioural support, the relapse-managed re-offer rule and the other-drug audit ([[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]])); vaccination and infection strategy (the influenza and pneumococcal and the pertussis-and-COVID-19-policy schedule and the prompt-exacerbation action plan — [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]); pulmonary rehabilitation (the exercise-programme evidence — the symptom, quality-of-life, readmission, and even mortality benefits of the 6–8-week programmes; maintenance-by-community-exercise and the breathlessness-control techniques of [[respiratory/mechanics-breathing]]'s pursed-lip lesson and the [[geriatrics-palliative/ageing-frailty]]-adjacent services)); pharmacology: **long-acting bronchodilation first (LAMA or LABA, then the LAMA-LABA dual — the hyperinflation-reducing lung-function and exacerbation benefits and the antimuscarinic-and-β₂ receptor pharmacology — [[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]], [[pharmacology/drug-receptor]]), inhaled corticosteroid added only at the eosinophilic/exacerbating phenotype (the ≥2-exacerbations-or-hospitalisation-plus-blood-eosinophils-≥300 rule and the ≥100-with-exacerbations consideration, with the eosinophil-withdrawal-titration caution and the pneumonia trade-off debate — the WISPAS-and-SHAPE-era arguments) and roflumilast and long-term-macrolide and mucolytic niches for the chronic-bronchitic frequent-exacerbator); the oxygen (not yet — his saturations sit above the LTOT threshold — [[#ex-aecopd]]'s trial-derived rules) and the vaccine-and-pulmonary-rehab-plus-nutrition-plus-psychology whole-patient programme; and the follow-up rhythm (exacerbation count, spirometry, the α1-antitrypsin question if the phenotype or the age or the basal-dominance fits — [[#ex-a1at]])**. The teaching sentence: **COPD management is decline-slope management plus symptom-and-exacerbation management, and neither is the inhaler alone.**
:::

::: example The gas and the acid {#ex-aecopd}
A 71-year-old with GOLD-3 COPD is more wheezy and confused with purulent sputum after a week of a cold; RR 28, SpO₂ 84 % on air, a flappy tremor, ABG: pH 7.24, PaCO₂ 10.9 kPa, HCO₃ 36, PaO₂ 7.2; chest film clear.
::: solution
**Acute-on-chronic type-2 (hypercapnic) respiratory failure in an infective AECOPD — the ABG reads "acute-on-chronic" from the bicarbonate (the renal compensation's footprint — [[renal/acid-base-disorders]]) and the acidotic pH (the acute decompensation on top).** The first hours, in physiology order: **controlled oxygen (the venturi 24–28 % targeting 88–92 % — the [[respiratory/gas-exchange]] Haldane-and-V/Q-release reasoning behind the target range, the "oxygen without ventilation" trap and the 30-minute re-gas rule); nebulised β₂-agonist plus ipratropium (the dose-interval and continuous-nebulisation escalation), prednisolone 40 mg (the 5-day course and its glucose-and-culture-and-myopathy caveats — [[endocrine/adrenal]]'s suppression footnote) and doxycycline-and-amoxicillin-and-macrolide by purulence-and-local-policy (the infective trigger — [[microbiology/bacteria]]), and the search for the precipitants list (the non-adherence and the embolus and the failure and the opioid-and-sedative co-prescription — [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy])).** **Non-invasive ventilation now (the pH ≤7.26-and-PaCO₂-driven-acidosis-after ~1 hour of maximal medical therapy or first-line when pH <7.25 — the trial-programme's intubation-and-mortality reduction — the interface, the titration and the 2-hour-pH-recheck rule, the ceiling-mask-and-monitor-and-aspiration-and-facial-necrosis care details and the escalation-to-intubation criteria (the pH-6.9-ish floor debate, the GCS-and-protection questions, the do-not-intubate-with-NIV-ceiling pathway and its documentation) — [[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]], [[respiratory/respiratory-failure]]).** The aftermath: **the home-NIV-and-long-term-oxygen reassessment (the persistent-retainer phenotype and the readmission-reduction criteria and the OPTIMIS-and-home-ventilation-network era), the rehabilitation and the smoking-and-vaccine re-ask, the future-exacerbation plan (the rescue-pack prescribing debate), and the palliative-care shared pathway for the advanced-trajectory patient (the breathlessness-and-opioid and the hospital-at-home and the preferred-place-of-death conversations — [[geriatrics-palliative/palliative-care]])**.
:::

::: example The basal panlobular 38-year-old {#ex-a1at}
A never-heavy-smoker 38-year-old has FEV₁/FVC 0.52 with DLCO 45 %, basal-predominant panlobular emphysema on CT, and father-and-uncle with "breathlessness and liver trouble".
::: solution
**Alpha-1 antitrypsin (AAT) deficiency — the protease–antiprotease hypothesis made clinical and examinable.** The genetics: **SERPINA1's codominant Pi-system (PiZZ — the Glu366Lys (Z) misfolding polymer's hepatocyte retention: the liver disease of the misfolded protein's storage and the lung disease of the antiprotease shortfall, the uninhibited neutrophil elastase digesting the alveolar septa — the basal-panlobular pattern because the lower-zone's greater perfusion delivers more elastase load and less anti-elastase shield — [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]'s codominance-and-gain-of-toxic-function example).** The work-up: **serum AAT level (the phenotyping with the acute-phase-rise caveat) and genotype, and the liver assessment (the hepatitis-and-alcohol-cofactors and the histology globules — [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]).** Management: **the aggressive smoking-and-exposure abolition (the multiplicative elastase-load risk and cessation's outsized benefit on this trajectory), the full COPD pharmacology-and-rehabilitation programme, the augmentation therapy question (the weekly intravenous pooled-human-AAT for the established-deficiency-with-emphysema phenotype, the CT-density-and-spirometric-subset evidence, the lifelong-infusion logistics — the [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]]'s biologic-availability realities), the liver surveillance and transplant interfaces, the family cascade screening (the spirometry-and-levels for first-degree relatives and the reproductive-counselling option — [[genetics/genetic-testing]]) and the registry-and-genetics-centre referral.** The teaching: **the protease-antiprotease and oxidative-amplification model of emphysema ([[#def-copd]]) was inferred from these kindreds before it was generalized to smoking — a reverse-translational classic.**
:::

::: history
**Hippocrates's** 'asthma' (the short-breathed) and Galen's school; **Henry Salter's** 1860 pathological account of fatal asthma (the contracted muscle and mucus-plugged bronchi of [[#def-asthma]]) and **Trousseau's** and **Curschmann's** and **Charcot-Leyden's** sputum-and-crystal legacy; **Coca and Cooke's** 1920s atopy programme (the allergy-revolution's linkage of asthma, hay-fever and eczema — [[immunology/hypersensitivity]]); **the tissue-amine, SRS-A-and-prostaglandin pharmacology and the β-receptor-and-cAMP era** that produced the β₂-agonists ([[pharmacology/drug-receptor]]); **Brown's** microfine cromoglycate and **Beasley's** beclomethasone-and-budesonide inhaled-steroid programme (the risk-benefit revolution) and the GINA-ladder and single-inhaler era; and on the COPD side: **Baillie's and Matthews's and Laennec's** distillations, the post-war British 'blue bloater and pink puffer' bedside taxonomy, the **MRC and Fletcher-Peto natural-history studies (the 1977 Lancet paper)**, the **NOTT and MRC domiciliary-oxygen trials of 1980–81 (the LTOT mortality rule)**, the ATS-and-BTS-and-GOLD-formation era (the 2001 GOLD report and its group-assessment revisions) and the triple-therapy-and-biologics generation of 2019–2025; and **the alpha-1 connection — Laurell and Eriksson's 1963 electrophoretic discovery, Eriksson's Fagerberg-kindred studies, and the Carrell-school polymer-proteinopathy crystallography** — the molecular-medicine bridge between the genetics and respiratory chapters.
:::

::: summary
- Asthma is T2-driven, variable airway disease: diagnose with objective variability plus T2 biomarkers, treat with ICS-containing regimens (ICS-formoterol single-inhaler strategy), and escalate to biomarker-matched biologics, never to standalone β₂-agonists.
- The acute severe asthma protocol is severity classification plus oxygen plus repeated bronchodilation plus steroids plus magnesium, with the "normal CO₂" and silent-chest alarms marking the escalation to critical care.
- COPD is exposure-dose airway-and-parenchyma destruction (elastase–antielastase and repair failure): manage the slope (cessation), the symptoms (long-acting bronchodilators ± phenotype-selected ICS), the exacerbations (steroids, antibiotics, controlled oxygen) and the failure (NIV and oxygen by trial-derived numbers).
- LTOT and home and acute NIV are the few interventions that change COPD mortality — each a direct application of the gas-exchange and controller physiology of this book's respiratory chapters.
- The phenotype fringes — alpha-1, AERD, asthma-COPD overlap, the eosinophilic and the small-airway — are where mechanism-matched therapy and genetics referrals earn their place.

Pre: FEV₁ 2.1 L, FVC 3.4; post-salbutamol: FEV₁ 2.4 L, FVC 3.5. Compute the change and state whether it meets the criterion and what it does and does not prove.
::: solution
**+300 mL and +14.3 % — meets both limbs (≥200 mL and ≥12 %) = significant reversibility.** It supports asthma in the right symptom context; it does not prove asthma alone (atopy, some bronchiectasis and even overlap-COPD patients vary) and its absence never excludes asthma (the variable-function requires sampling — serial peak-flow diaries and challenge testing settle the doubtful cases — [[#def-asthma]] and [[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]]).

::: exercise Oxygen rules and their evidence {level=2}
State the long-term-oxygen-therapy criteria and the mortality evidence, and the acute-exacerbation target-range rule with its mechanism, reconciling "oxygen saves" and "oxygen harms".
::: solution
**Long-term: PaO₂ ≤7.3 kPa (or ≤8.0 with cor pulmonale, secondary polycythaemia or nocturnal desaturation), worn ≥15 h/day, on the NOTT and MRC trials' mortality and survival-with-oxygen-versus-intermittent evidence (the pulmonary-hypertension and haematocrit and survival variables), with the ambulatory-exertional-oxygen's symptom-only verdict and the smoker's safety counselling.** Acute: **target 88–92 % via venturi (the AIMS/BTS evidence — the hypercapnic patients' Haldane-effect-and-V/Q-release-and-drive physiology and the Aubier-and-O'Driscoll-era oxygen-physiology studies — [[respiratory/gas-exchange]]), because the flat top of the curve means excess oxygen buys nothing except CO₂ retention and absorption changes.** Reconciliation: **oxygen is a dose-titrated drug — hypoxaemic mortality falls with controlled long-term dosing, uncontrolled high-FiO₂ dosing in the CO₂-retainer raises morbidity (the [[respiratory/control-breathing-tests]] blunted-controller and the V/Q-redistribution logic), the same molecule on both sides of the dissociation curve ([[respiratory/gas-exchange]]'s widget) — the prescription must specify indication, target and review like any other drug ([[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]).**
