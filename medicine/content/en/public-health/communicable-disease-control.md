Communicable-disease control is epidemiology executed as public health law: the chain of infection (agent, reservoir, portal, route, susceptible host) is the clinical object, and breaking it is an organised activity of surveillance, case-finding, isolation, contact work, outbreak science and border-and-setting measures. **This chapter covers the chain and its vocabulary (reservoirs, carriers, R-naught versus R-effective, the elimination arithmetic), surveillance and notification (the notifiable-disease machinery, syndromic and genomic surveillance, indicator and threshold alerts), outbreak investigation (the hypotheses-to-control sequence, the epidemic curve as diagnostic, the R-number craft), and the population tool-kit (case isolation and contact tracing including digital systems, the infection-control-and-prevention hierarchy at scale, the border-and-travel measures and their evidence, quarantine's ethics, and the tuberculosis-and-HIV-and-measles-and-STI-and-antimicrobial-resistance programmes that show every tool's strengths and failures in one pathogen's clothing).**

## The chain

::: definition Reservoirs, transmission numbers and elimination arithmetic {#def-chain}
**The chain of infection is a clinical and public-health document at once: the agent's habitat (human-only infections like measles and smallpox make eradication thinkable, zoonotic reservoirs like leptospirosis and most arboviruses make it impossible - the reservoir decides the strategy), the carriage states (the carrier - typhoid Mary's food-handling law still on the books - the incubatory and convalescent shedders who populate the infectious-disease-timelines clinicians memorise, and the chronic-carrier occupations rules), and the portals and routes (contact, droplet, airborne, vehicle, vector, vertical, sexual - each route a different control arithmetic: the respiratory-route R-naught ranking that orders infectiousness, and the 'route determines the measure' rule that makes the same pathogen behave differently in a household, a ward and a clinic). The transmission numbers operationalise everything: the basic reproduction number sets the herd-immunity threshold, the effective reproduction number (R-effective, with its generation-time and serial-interval companions) tells whether incidence is growing, and the now-household epidemic-curve craft (the R-t-with-confidence-intervals reporting culture, its real-time-estimation pitfalls - the truncation and reporting-delay artefacts that made some 2020 dashboards misleading - and the growth-rate-to-doubling-time arithmetic that should be a clinician's second language). The elimination vocabulary with its arithmetic: incidence falling, control, elimination as a public-health problem (the 'reduced-to-agreed-low-level' definition used for the neglected-tropical-disease 'reduced-to-agreed-level' targets), elimination of transmission (regional, verification-programmes), eradication and the 'warm and cold' reserves, and each step's re-escalation risk (the imported-case mathematics of elimination: the same importation that caused nothing pre-elimination can restart epidemics after coverage decays). The population-immunity-versus-incidence relationship is not destiny: the measles-reinstatement episodes (the 2017-2019 European and US outbreaks in under-vaccinated communities and the WHO's Europe de-listing revocations) are the standing demonstration that elimination is a maintained state, not a trophy.**
:::

## The system

::: definition Surveillance, notification and outbreak investigation {#def-surveillance}
**Surveillance is the diagnosis of populations: the notifiable-disease systems (statutory diseases and organisms with clinician-and-laboratory duties, the 'on suspicion, telephone today' class - the food poisoning and the rash-fever and the botulism and cholera calls that precede any test result - with their 2010s-and-pandemic-era UK statutory modernisation and equivalent frameworks elsewhere), the laboratory-reporting backbone (the reference-laboratory networks, the sentinel GP-and-hospital pathways that measure what circulation looks like between alerts, and the 'test-with-order-and-result-codes' data quality that every pandemic post-mortem found wanting), the syndromic and indicator systems (the emergency-department-and-999-and-absence-and-waste-water streams with their sensitivity-specificity trade-offs and their threshold-and-cluster-detection algorithms), and the genomic era (the pathogen-genome-surveillance programmes for respiratory and foodborne and antimicrobial-resistance organisms, the lineage-naming and outbreak-attribution pipelines, and the data-sharing-and-benefit-sharing governance the sequencing-equity debates produced). Outbreak investigation keeps the classic sequence (verify the diagnosis and cases counts, define the case, find cases systematically, describe by person-place-time, generate and test hypotheses - analytical designs nested in emergencies from the cohort and case-control studies of the Salmonella picnic to the real-time-evaluability of mass exposures - institute control while investigating, communicate, and conclude with the prevention plan), and the epidemic curve is its physical sign: the point-source spike (the single meal, the contaminated batch), the continuous propagated rise (person-to-person generations), and the plateau-and-tail of vehicle persistence each imply different measures; the attack-rate arithmetic (the secondary-attack rates that quantify household and ward transmission and the vaccine-effectiveness-against-infection estimates that ride on them) and the 'who-is-exposed-and-where-do-they-gather' contact-registry work. The control levers during an outbreak run the chain backwards: the isolation-and-communicability-period rules (the disease-specific 'when can they leave isolation' guidance that hospitals and schools live by), the contact-tracing-and-prophylaxis-and-post-exposure-prophylaxis pathways (the meningococcal-ciprofloxacin-or-refampicin-and-whooping-cough-and-measles-PEDS-rules that make the public-health phone call a prescription), the closure-and-exclusion-powers (the food-handler-and-nursery-and-school exclusion regulations with their paid-leave dependencies - the equity of who can afford to isolate), and the mass-prophylaxis-and-emergency-vaccination-ring campaigns. The respiratory-infection-pandemic layer belongs here as a discipline: the pandemic-influenza-adjacent phase-plans and their 'severity-versus-transmissibility' framing, the non-pharmaceutical-interventions evidence (the school-closures-and-social-distancing meta-analyses and their honest effect-size debates), the healthcare-capacity-and-surge-ethics (the triage-protocol-and-difficult-but-legitimate decisions the major-incidents chapter owns), and the 'test-trace-isolate-support' integrated-system concept whose 'support' term - the paid-isolation and food-and-job protections - is what the international evaluations found decides whether tracing works at all.**
:::

## The toolkit

::: definition Control programmes, borders and the ethics of population measures {#def-toolkit}
**The vertical-programme archive teaches generalisable lessons: tuberculosis (the case-detection-and-DOTS history, the latent-infection-screening-and-treatment programmes and their 'who-to-screen' risk-group debates, the drug-resistance programs that turned adherence science into public health, and the universal-vaccine-era prospects), HIV (the test-and-treat-and-undetectable-equals-untransmittable strategy that made treatment prevention, the pre-exposure-prophylaxis era and its equity-of-access audit, the key-population-and-community-services model and the criminalisation-versus-public-health argument, and the '95-95-95' cascade as a programme's dashboard), the STIs (the contact-network tracing as the oldest control, the partner-notification-versus-confidentiality craft, the resistance-driven-reinfection-era and doxycycline-PEP trial debates, and the screening-programmes' asymptomatic-infection arithmetic), antimicrobial resistance (the One-Health-and-agriculture-consumption-and-human-prescribing-and-infection-control-and-travel-migration-spread framing, the antibiotic-stewardship-to-governance reviews, and the 'resistance-as-the-tragedy-of-the-commons' economics that connect this chapter's tools to the next), and the neglected and vector-borne programmes (the insecticide-treated-nets-and-indoor-spraying-and-Wolbachia-and-GeneDrive-era vector biology as public engineering, the mass-drug-administration strategies and their 'mop-up-versus-mouse-trapping' epidemiological argument, and the climate-shift range expansion turning old maps into predictions). The border-and-travel layer: the International Health Regulations architecture (the 'events of international concern' declarations, country core-capacity obligations and their audit gap), the port-and-entry measures and their evidence ceiling (the 'entry screening rarely stops a determined pandemic' finding balanced against its delay-value in specific settings), the travel-and-trade restrictions' proportionality rules under the IHR (the travel-ban-law and aviation-shutdown debates with their 'the first country to act is the last to be protected' timing literature), and the certificate-and-vaccination-requirement systems (the yellow-fever-ICC-regulations precedent and the pneumonia-era digital-verification arguments). The ethics are the toolkit's constitution: the Siracusa-and-Sirmanji principles tradition and the Nuffield-adjacent justification framework (necessity, proportionality, least restrictive means, due process, reciprocity - the state's obligations to make isolation possible: the paid-isolation-and-job-protection-and-poverty-prevention terms the pandemic's 'support' failures made unforgivable), the liberty-versus-protection cases (the quarantine orders and their appeals, the treatment-isolation orders for infectious disease in psychiatric-and-substance contexts, the 'dot on the hand' directly-observed-therapy ethics), the privacy-versus-protection data questions (the contact-tracing-apps' decentralised-versus-centralised privacy architecture debate, the 'health-data-for-public-health-statutory-basis' arguments and the function-creep guardrails), and the equity constitution the communicable-disease field invented first: 'he 'diseases of poverty carry the costs of their containment back to the poverty' observation, the 'one health' justice extensions, and the 'who pays for the elimination that the wealthy import' climate-and-migration-and-conflict-responsibility arguments now running through global health's chapters.**
:::

## Where this leads

The clinical medicine of the pathogens is in [[infectious-diseases/fever-sepsis]], [[infectious-diseases/tuberculosis]], [[infectious-diseases/hiv]] and the tropical chapter; the hospital-side infection control is in [[microbiology/infection-control]] and [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]]; stewardship and resistance programmes are in [[infectious-diseases/stewardship-outbreaks]]; immunisation delivery is in [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]; the environmental reservoirs and vector work is in [[public-health/environmental-occupational]]; and the ethical framework is in [[medical-ethics-law/confidentiality]] and [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]].

::: history
**Communicable-disease control is the oldest organised public health and its case archive. The sanitary era (the miasma-versus-contagion arguments, John Snow's 1849-54 cholera work and the broad-pump handle's removal-adjacent epidemiology, the German Pettenkofer counter-theory, the sanitation build-out, and Semmelweis and Nightingale and the hospital's own fever wards) established surveillance-by-registrar (the bills of mortality), statistical geography and the trial-of-the-intervention (Snow's pump-and-company-comparison as the first natural experiment of public health). The bacteriological era (the 1876-1898 Koch-Pasteur-Behring line) brought the notifiable-disease idea (the notifiable-diseases acts from 1889 in Britain), the notification-and-isolation machinery, the fever hospitals, the disinfection-stations, and the occupational-and-school-entry medical inspection; the tuberculosis sanatorium era and its 'find-the-case-and-the-sputum' mass-radiography campaigns were the template for vertical disease programmes, and the public-health-law literature's 'liberty-versus-protection' cases (the smallpox-vaccination-compulsion decisions, the quarantine litigation of Angel Island and Ellis Island, and the 'typhoid Mary' Mallory detention 1915-1938) wrote the field's jurisprudence before bioethics existed. The vaccine-and-antibiotic mid-century produced the golden control narrative: the diphtheria-toxin-antitoxin and Schick-test eras, the malaria-DDT-and-drainage-and-Treponema-penicillin-arsphenamine-and-the-'magic-bullet'-era optimism, the smallpox eradication's 1967-80 execution (the Fenner-Foege-Dau-adjacent strategy debate, the 'count-houses-versus-count-percentages' method and the last-case epidemiology), and the Expanded Programme on Immunisation of 1974; the same decade produced the field's two humilities: the 're-emergence' concept (the HIV-era term, the Ebola-and-Lassa-and-Hantavirus-and-antibiotic-resistance-and-relapsing-tuberculosis-and-AIDS-epidemiology of the 1980s-90s), and the recognition that zoonotic spillover is ecology, not exception. The outbreak-science canon matured through field epidemiology (the Epidemic-Intelligence-Service model exported from Atlanta's smallpox-measles-and-Ebola-in-Africa field investigations, the WHO's Field-Epidemiology-Training-Networks and 'he 'outbreaks are a signal of system failure and a chance to fix it' doctrine), and the international-rulebook layer came late: the 1969-2005 International Health Regulations revision after SARS (2003) and the H5N1-and-anthrax-and-polio-and-Ebola-and-MERS-and-COVID-emergencies-adjacent 'core-capacities' monitoring and the 'events-of-international-public-health-concern' declarations (2009-pandemic-through-the-mpox-and-emergency-committee-controversies-adjacent-reform-debates), the 'One Health' framing (the 2004-WHO-World-Bank-FAO-strategy and the antimicrobial-resistance-O'Neill-review-economics), and the 2020s pandemic's post-mortem literature on 'the first-72-days' surveillance failures (the 'PCR-capacity-and-data-and-variants-and-sequencing-inequity' and 'wastewater-era-arrives' findings), 'test-trace-isolate-support' systems (with the 'support' word's absence diagnosed as the reason they failed in some countries and not others), and 'digital-contact-tracing' (the privacy-architecture-research and the adoption-ceiling-adjacent-privacy-debate). The current chapter is the post-antibiotic-adjacent shadow: the 'resistance-surveillance-as-public-health-triage' era, the climate-and-migration-and-conflict-and-urbanisation-driven range expansions (the dengue-and-chikungunya-and-West-Nile-and-Leishmania-and-Vibrio-and-hantavirus-geographies-rewritten-on-screen), and the 'pathogen-genomics-as-routine-public-health-infrastructure' build-out which is what the smallpox workers' counted houses eventually became.** - [[public-health/global-health]], [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]], [[microbiology/infection-control]].**
:::

::: summary
- Control targets the chain of infection: reservoir biology decides strategy (human-only pathogens can be eradicated, zoonoses managed), route decides the measures, and the transmission numbers (R-naught, R-effective, serial interval, secondary attack rates) decide whether incidence grows - and are the clinician's epidemiological vocabulary.
- Surveillance is population diagnosis: statutory notification on suspicion for the dangerous few, laboratory and sentinel backbones, syndromic and wastewater early-warning, and genomic pipelines - all of which failed loudly and expensively enough in recent pandemics that detection speed is now the field's primary metric.
- Outbreak investigation is clinical method applied to populations: verify, define, find, describe by person-place-time, hypothesise and test while controlling, communicate, and leave a prevention plan; the epidemic curve's shape is a sign, and the attack rates are the laboratory results.
- The tools - isolation, contact tracing with post-exposure prophylaxis, exclusion and closure, ring vaccination, mass prophylaxis, vector and environmental measures - each require the support systems (paid isolation, job protection, supplies) that make compliance possible and the ethics of reciprocity real.
- Vertical programmes (tuberculosis, HIV, STIs, immunisable diseases, resistance) share lessons: equity of access is a biological variable, treatment is prevention, elimination is a maintained state, and success quietly erodes the vigilance that produced it.
- Population measures obey proportionality, necessity, least-restrictive-means and time limits with due process - the public-health-ethics constitution whose violation (coercive, unsupported, stigmatising control) predictably destroys the cooperation the measures depend on.
:::

::: example The clinic with the cough register {#ex-tb}
