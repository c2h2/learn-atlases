Inflammatory arthritis is the group where delay costs damage and damage costs independence: **rheumatoid arthritis and its relatives destroy joints in the first months, gout destroys kidneys and tendons silently for years, and both respond dramatically when treated early and aggressively - which makes this chapter largely about speed, pattern recognition and treat-to-target discipline.**

This chapter covers rheumatoid arthritis, the spondyloarthritis family, crystal arthropathies, and the reactive-and-infectious adjacencies.

## Service shape: why speed is the intervention

The outcome gradient in inflammatory arthritis is organisational as much as pharmacological: community-to-rheumatology referral intervals, early-arthritis-clinic throughput, ultrasound access, treat-to-target review discipline and escalation autonomy for nurses and therapists determine whether patients actually receive the evidence inside the therapeutic window [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]]. The Netherlands-and-UK-and-Australian early-arthritis cohort programmes showed that promise-and-protocol clinics beat expert-queue clinics on remission and work outcomes - and that the patients who slip through are disproportionately the deprived, the rural, the non-English-speaking, the socially anxious and those whose pain was attributed to obesity, age or mental health before anyone examined their joints [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]. Register-based surveillance of biologic safety, national joint registries for the surgical tail, and honest audit of referral-interval equity across deprivation gradients are the unglamorous, everyday machinery that keeps the whole field honest that keeps this chapter's optimism honest [[epidemiology-ebm/measuring-disease]], [[public-health/screening]].

Two design choices deserve emphasis for anyone building or rebuilding such a service. First, the gatekeeping question is not "does this clinic exist" but "what interval does a GP-triggered referral actually achieve, and what fraction of patients with inflammatory-pattern synovitis are seen inside it" - measured, published and acted upon, that single metric compresses the damage curve more than any new drug [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[epidemiology-ebm/observational-studies]]. Second, the monitoring workload that DMARD and biologic safety demands - bloods, blood-pressure checks, vaccination review, bone protection, skin checks, pregnancy planning, flu-status conversations - is precisely the workload that nurse-led and pharmacist-led medicines-adherence clinics deliver better than doctor-time scarcity allows, provided the escalation pathway to a rheumatologist for flares and toxicity is explicit, resourced and honoured [[pharmacology/patient-variation]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[public-health/prevention-promotion]].

::: summary
- Inflammatory synovitis is a time-critical diagnosis: prolonged morning stiffness, swelling of MCP/wrist/MTP, systemic inflammation - refer before radiographs change.
- Rheumatoid arthritis is treated to a target with a DMARD anchor, escalating on a clock; steroids bridge, never steer.
- Spondyloarthritis is a family - spine, entheses, skin, gut, eye - and the questions about psoriasis, IBD, uveitis and urethritis are part of the examination.
- Gout is a treatable urate-transport disease with an IL-1β-driven inflammatory face; aspirate when infection is possible, and treat-to-target urate when you lower.
- Every immunosuppressive prescription carries the infection, vaccination, pregnancy, malignancy and perioperative conversations with it, and those conversations belong to a named service, not to luck.
- Polymyalgia, late-onset and undifferentiated presentations are where older adults get steroid treadmills and missed diagnoses; a taper plan and a review date are safety equipment [[#def-other]].
