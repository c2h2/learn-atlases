Bacteriology is best learned as a catalogue of shapes, stains and strategies: **the Gram reaction, the oxygen preference and a handful of virulence tricks predict most of the clinical syndrome, the right specimen, the empiric drug and the isolation precautions.** This chapter walks the medically important genera in the order you meet them at the bedside, from the cocci that clog your emergency department to the anaerobes that punish a missed diagnosis.

This chapter covers bacterial structure and classification, the Gram-positive cocci and rods, the Gram-negative families, the atypicals and spirochaetes, and the antimicrobial implications that follow from each grouping.

## Structure and classification

::: definition The bacterial body plan {#def-structure}
**The envelope decides most things (the Gram-positive thick peptidoglycan with teichoic acids and no outer membrane, which is why they keep the crystal violet and why vancomycin and beta-lactams work so well on them, and the Gram-negative inner membrane, thin peptidoglycan and outer membrane with lipopolysaccharide, porins and the periplasmic space where beta-lactamases lurk, which is why their drug list is shorter and their endotoxin shock worse - [[immunology/innate-immunity]] for the TLR4-CD14-MD2 biology and [[microbiology/antimicrobials]] for permeability and efflux as resistance mechanisms); the surface weapons and what they cause (capsules that anti-phagocytose and define the encapsulated organisms - pneumococcus and Hib and meningococcus and the asplenic patient's nightmares - [[immunology/innate-immunity]], [[blood/haematopoiesis]]; protein A and M protein and IgA proteases and biofilms on devices and heart valves and joints - [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]] for the device biology, [[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]] for the prosthetic joint); the toxins as disease-course authors (exotoxins with their A-B architecture - tetanus and botulinum neurotoxins with opposite synapses, diphtheria and Shiga toxins halting ribosomes by different cuts, cholera toxin locking adenylate cyclase - and superantigens - TSST-1 and Staphylococcal enterotoxins and Streptococcal pyrogenic exotoxins cross-linking MHC-II outside the groove and causing the rash-and-shock of toxic shock - [[infectious-diseases/fever-sepsis]]; and the cell-wall-associated inflammation - lipid A endotoxin, muramyl peptides, the Gram-positive lipoteichoic acid and the reason CSF in bacterial meningitis injures the hippocampus even after the organism dies, which is why dexamethasone exists in the guidelines); and the classification shortcuts (shape and arrangement, oxygen and growth requirements, the oxidation-fermentation and coagulase and catalase and urease and indole tests now partly replaced by MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry - see [[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]] - and the 16S ribosomal RNA sequencing for the unculturable).**
:::

## Gram-positive organisms

::: definition Staphylococci and streptococci {#def-cocci}
**Staphylococcus aureus as the quintessential pathogen (the Coagulase-positive-and-protein A-and-clumping-factor virulence toolkit and the skin-and-soft-tissue dominion - folliculitis to necrotising fasciitis - [[musculoskeletal/skin]], [[surgery/wounds-surgical-infection]]; the deep-seated syndromes - bacteraemia with its metastatic seeding of bone, joint, valve and kidney and the "find the source" commandments (line, heart, bone, abscess) and the MRSA epidemiology of healthcare and community strains with PVL-associated necrotising pneumonia; and the toxin syndromes - food poisoning from preformed enterotoxin with its short incubation, scalded-skin exfoliative toxin in infants, and toxic shock with its tampon-and-wound history - [[immunology/innate-immunity]] for the neutrophil-dependence of Staphylococcal control and the hyper-IgE/STAT3-HIES eczema-and-cold-abscess association in [[immunology/immunodeficiency]]); the coagulase-negative staphylococci (the biofilm masters of prosthetic joints, valves, pacemakers and lines with their multiple-resistance habit and the interpretation rule that one positive blood culture of S. epidermidis usually means skin contamination unless the picture fits - a clinical-reasoning pearl that prevents both missed device infection and 10 days of unnecessary vancomycin - [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]], [[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]]); and the streptococci by Lancefield group (the Group A pyogenes - pharyngitis and its non-suppurative sequelae of rheumatic fever and post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis, and the suppurative cellulitis, necrotising fasciitis and puerperal sepsis, plus the molecular mimicry of rheumatic carditis ([[cardiovascular/valve-congenital-disease]]) and the puerperal history in [[obstetrics-gynaecology/labour-birth]]; the Group B agalactiae early-versus-late neonatal disease and the intrapartum screening-and-prophylaxis programme - [[paediatrics/newborn]], [[obstetrics-gynaecology/labour-birth]]; the viridans group with their dental biofilms and culture-negative endocarditis and neutropaenic-bacteraemia mucositis association - [[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]]; the enterococci with their bile-salt esculin positivity, their nosocomial urinary-and-biliary-and-surgical habit, their intrinsic cephalosporin resistance and their VRE and linezolid-resistant eras - covered with resistance in [[microbiology/antimicrobials]] and [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]]; and Streptococcus pneumoniae - the lancet-shaped encapsulated diplococcus causing otitis, sinusitis, community-acquired pneumonia with rust sputum and lobar consolidation, meningitis in the extremes of age, and overwhelming post-splenectomy infection, whose capsular-swelling quellung reaction and bile solubility and optochin sensitivity are classics and whose serotype-replacing PCV programmes are vaccination ecology in action - [[respiratory/respiratory-infections]], [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]).**
:::

::: definition Gram-positive rods and the spore-formers {#def-gprods}
**The anaerobic spore-formers that produce their disease with toxins (Clostridioides difficile with its antibiotic-disrupted microbiome and toxin A and B enterotoxic-and-cytotoxic colitis and the recurrence-prone relapsing course, covered fully in [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]] and [[microbiology/microbiota]]; tetanus with its retrograde tetanospasmin blocking inhibitory interneuron exocytosis and the trismus-and-risus-and-opisthotonus spasms, the dirty-wound and vaccination-status question, the immunoglobulin-and-metronidazole-and-benzodiazepine-and-ventilator management and the public-health shame of every case in a vaccine-eligible person - [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]; botulism with its descending paralysis from presynaptic SNARE cleavage at the cholinergic junction, the home-canned-and-honey-and-wound-and-baby forms, the bulbar-and-respiratory failure with preserved sensation and mentation and the antitoxin-timing-before-paralysis-completes; gas gangrene with alpha-toxin myonecrosis and crepitus and the surgical-debridement-dominates-everything rule - [[surgery/trauma]], [[musculoskeletal/bones-joints]] for the osteomyelitis adjacency); the aerobic club (Corynebacterium diphtheriae with its beta-phage-encoded toxin and pseudomembrane that can obstruct and its myocarditis-and-neuropathy sequelae and the toxoid-vaccine triumph and the imported-cases-reawakening question - [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]; Listeria monocytogenes as the food-borne intracellular parasite of pregnancy, the neonate, the over-65 and the cell-mediated-immunocompromised with its meningitis-and-rhombencephalitis and negative-Gram-stain-Campy-positivity trivia and its ampicillin-treatment and cephalosporin-intrinsic-resistance trap - [[obstetrics-gynaecology/pregnancy-complications]], [[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]], [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]; Bacillus anthracis with its inhalational mediastinal classic and the toxin edema-lethal-factor biology and the bioterror choreography - [[respiratory/respiratory-infections]]; and the environmental opportunist Nocardia, weakly acid-fast branching soil bacterium causing pulmonary, CNS and skin disease in transplant patients, treated with co-trimoxazole for months - [[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]], [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]] for the brain abscess).**
:::

## Gram-negative and atypicals

::: definition Gram-negative families {#def-gneg}
**The enterics (Escherichia coli as the workplace of molecular biology and the leader of urinary, bloodstream, neonatal-meningitis and diarrhoeal disease with its pathotypes from ETEC traveller's to EHEC O157 Shiga-toxin haemolytic-uraemic syndrome - [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]] - to EPEC and EAEC, and its ESBL-and-AmpC-and-colistin-resistance-carbapenemase epidemic - [[microbiology/antimicrobials]]; Klebsiella's capsule-heavy pneumonia-and-liver-abscess-and-device habit and its carbapenemase epicentre status; Enterobacter and Serratia with inducible AmpC; Salmonella Typhi with the enteric fever, the chronic gallbladder carriage and the Vi-polysaccharide vaccine, and non-typhoidal Salmonella's food-borne gastroenteritis with its HIV-and-malnutrition-associated invasive form - [[infectious-diseases/hiv]]; Shigella with its 10-organism infectious dose and bacillary dysentery and HUS adjacency; Proteus struvite stones; and Yersinia pseudoappendicitis - [[gastrointestinal/bowel-disease]]); the non-lactose oddballs (Pseudomonas aeruginosa with its pyocyanin pigment, its biofilm and its cystic-fibrosis airway adaptation - [[respiratory/asthma-copd]]: its hot-tub folliculitis, malignant otitis in diabetics, burn and neutropaenic sepsis and its intrinsic-resistance list; Acinetobacter and Stenotrophomonas as the ICU surface dwellers - [[emergency-critical-care/critical-care]], [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]]); Neisseria (meningitidis with its droplet-herd-dormancy, its petechial-sepsis-with-Waterhouse-Friderichsen and its complement-terminal-pathway-deficiency association - [[immunology/innate-immunity]], [[endocrine/adrenal]]; gonorrhoeae covered in [[infectious-diseases/sexually-transmitted-infections]]); Haemophilus influenzae type b and the non-typeable airway disease with the vaccine-success story - [[respiratory/asthma-copd]], [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]]; Bordetella pertussis with its tracheal-cilium-paralysing toxins and the 100-days cough and apnoea-in-infants lethality and the adolescent-mother immunity-waning and cocooning policy debate - [[paediatrics/immunisation]]; and Campylobacter (the Guillain-Barre and reactive-arthritis triggers - [[nervous-system/motor-systems]], [[musculoskeletal/inflammatory-arthritis]]) and Helicobacter pylori (covered in [[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]] with its acid-ecology and cancer attribution).**
:::

::: definition Mycobacteria, atypicals and spirochaetes {#def-odd}
**Mycobacterium tuberculosis and friends (the waxy mycolic acid envelope that refuses the Gram stain, grows for weeks on Lowenstein-Jensen and drives the whole TB clinical-and-public-health apparatus covered in [[infectious-diseases/tuberculosis]]; and the nontuberculous mycobacteria - MAC in advanced HIV and the Lady-Windermere bronchiectasis phenotype and the rapid-growers of wounds and catheters - [[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]], [[infectious-diseases/hiv]]); the obligate intracellular and cell-wall-less (Chlamydia trachomatis's elementary-body biphasic life and trachoma and LGV and neonatal-pneumonia and reactive-arthritis bookends with the pair-treatment public health in [[infectious-diseases/sexually-transmitted-infections]]; Chlamydophila pneumoniae's mild atypical pneumonia; Mycoplasma pneumoniae with its no-cell-wall biology (penicillin-proof), its cold-agglutinin haemolysis and rash and encephalitis associations and its community-outbreak ecology in schools - [[respiratory/respiratory-infections]], [[blood/anaemia]]; Rickettsia and Coxiella with their vasculotropic endothelial infection and the spotted-fever-and-Q-fever-endocarditis-association - [[infectious-diseases/tropical-infections]], [[cardiovascular/valve-congenital-disease]] for culture-negative endocarditis); and the spirochaetes (Treponema pallidum's dark-field classic and its century-defining staging - [[infectious-diseases/sexually-transmitted-infections]]; Borrelia burgdorferi and the Lyme multi-system march with its facial palsy, heart block, arthritis and the tick-infection-time logic - [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]], [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]]; and Leptospira's rat-urine floods and Weil disease jaundice-renal-pulmonary haemorrhage - [[infectious-diseases/tropical-infections]], [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]).**
:::

::: example The line, the valve and the third set {#ex-staph-bacteraemia}
A 57-year-old haemodialysis patient via tunnelled line has Staphylococcus aureus bacteraemia; day-3 blood cultures remain positive; echo is normal; she has back pain and a swollen knee.
::: solution
**Staphylococcus aureus bacteraemia - a protocol disease ([[#def-cocci]], [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]], [[cardiovascular/valve-congenital-disease]]): the rules for SAB exist for a reason - source control first (remove-or-exchange the line and culture the tip and search for undrained collections with a low threshold for CT and MRI of the spine for the back pain - the vertebral-osteomyelitis-and-epidural-abscess trap) and repeat cultures until clearance with the day-3-positivity defining complicated and prolonged therapy and the infectious-diseases consult, echocardiography in essentially every patient (TEE because TTE misses prosthetic and small vegetations) and the anti-staphylococcal beta-lactam (flucloxacillin-or-cloxacillin) versus vancomycin or daptomycin by susceptibility and the oral-step-down OVIVA-era-trial nuance for bone and joint infection - [[musculoskeletal/bones-joints]], [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]] for the dialysis dosing; the metastatic audit - the knee aspirate for septic arthritis (never a "reactive" assumption with positive blood cultures), the spine MRI, the skin and eye and urine survey and the spleen-kidney microabscess CT question; and the governance - the line-bundle re-audit (the dressing and hub and clamp-and-scrub-the-destination technique), the dialysis-unit transmission screening, the MRSA isolation policy application and the mortality-audit that SAB still carries 20% - the number that justifies the 14-day-to-6-week protocol rigidity and the follow-up for relapse - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]].**

::: summary
- Gram stain, oxygen requirement and a short virulence vocabulary predict the syndrome: capsules for the encapsulated trio in asplenic hosts, toxins for the clostridial syndromes, biofilms for devices.
- S. aureus bacteraemia is protocol medicine - source control, repeat cultures, echo, ID input and a fixed duration decided by clearance day.
- Meningococcal disease is a notification-and-contacts emergency: antibiotics to the minute, prophylaxis to the household within hours.
- Know the intrinsic-resistance traps: enterococcus and cephalosporins, Listeria and cephalosporins, Mycoplasma and all cell-wall drugs, Pseudomonas and half the shelf.
- Toxin-mediated disease (tetanus, botulism, diphtheria, toxic shock) progresses independently of bacterial load - antitoxin, vaccine status and supportive care outrank antibiotics.
- Killing Gram-negatives releases endotoxin; anticipate and support the first dose rather than misread it as treatment failure.
:::

::: exercise Separate the branching filaments {level=2}
