The vascular tree is the circulatory system's distribution engineering: elastic conduit, muscular throttle, exchange lattice and capacitance bed, each segment failing in its own clinical idiom — the artery occludes, the aorta dilutes and tears, the vein clots and refluxes, the capillary misregulates. The physics is the same throughout — Laplace's tension–radius relation, the pulse-wave and reflection arithmetic of compliance, Poiseuille and turbulence at stenoses — and the chapter's four clinical sciences (atherosclerosis-obstructive, aneurysmal-dissecting, inflammatory and venous-lymphatic) are that physics read segment by segment.

This chapter covers arterial wall biology and stiffening, peripheral arterial disease, aneurysm and dissection, the vasculitides and Raynaud phenomenon, and chronic venous disease.

## The wall and its loads

::: definition Artery wall, pulse waves and stiffening {#def-wall}
The **elastic artery (aorta and its major branches)** is a compound pressure vessel — intimal endothelium with its glycocalyx and mechano-sensing (the shear-mediated nitric oxide biology of [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]]), a media of elastin lamellae and smooth muscle (the vasa vasorum feeding its outer wall — the adventitial and inflammatory contributions of [[pathology/haemodynamics]]'s atherogenesis fields), and an adventitial collagen shell — whose function degrades with **age, pressure, glucose and smoking**: elastin fatigue and fragmentation, collagen cross-linking (the advanced-glycation chemistry of [[endocrine/diabetes]]), medial calcification ([[renal/potassium-calcium-phosphate]]'s calcium–phosphate product and Monckeberg's medial calcific sclerosis of the diabetic and renal tubular), and smooth-muscle phenotype switching ([[cell-biochemistry/signalling-cell-cycle]]'s contractile-to-synthetic transition). The haemodynamic consequence is **arterial stiffening**: **pulse-wave velocity rises (the Moens-Korteweg square-root law — the stiffer the wall the faster the wave), the systolic pressure wave and its reflected early return summate in the aorta rather than the periphery (the augmentation index rising — isolated systolic hypertension, the wide pulse pressure of [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]]'s exercise, the carotid–femoral pulse-wave velocity as the research-grade arterial-age measurement), left-ventricular afterload and coronary diastolic perfusion pressure both worsen (the saw-tooth of the central versus brachial pressure), and the microcirculation of brain and kidney loses its pulsatile-flow dampening (the white-matter and glomerular injury of the stiff artery — [[nervous-system/stroke]] and [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]'s shared soil).** **Endothelial dysfunction — the glycocalyx shedding and the NO-to-endothelin-1 and reactive-oxygen shift, the adhesion-molecule upregulation and the procoagulant surface (the tissue-factor and thrombomodulin biology of [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]])** is the earliest, systemic, partly reversible lesion: the common currency of smoking, lipids, glucose and pressure, and the reason the chapter's diseases co-occur and predict one another ([[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]]'s risk-factor arithmetic everywhere).
:::

## Obstruction: the peripheral arteries

::: definition Peripheral arterial disease: claudication to tissue loss {#def-pad}
Atherosclerosis's limb distribution follows the same [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]] process with a leg-specific anatomy (**the superficial femoral in the adductor canal and the popliteal and the tibial and peroneal run-off — [[anatomy/lower-limb]]'s surgical corridors, with diabetes's medial-change-and-tibial and foot-vessel predominance**). The clinical ladder (the Fontaine and Rutherford classifications) is **intermittent claudication — muscle pain provoked at a fixed work and relieved within minutes of rest (the flow-reserve arithmetic of [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]]'s widget, in calf, thigh or buttock by lesion level, with the sexual and erectile and the Leriche aorto-iliac syndrome and the pseudo-claudication differentials of [[musculoskeletal/back-pain]] and spinal stenosis and venous and neurogenic causes)** → **rest pain (the critical perfusion floor: the nocturnal, dependent-position, foot-and-toes pain relieved by hanging the limb — the perfusion-pressure and arteriolar-maximal-dilation physiology)** → **tissue loss (ulcer, gangrene: chronic limb-threatening ischaemia, the wound that cannot pay for its own repair — the [[surgery/wounds-surgical-infection]] healing-physiology in deficit, the diabetic foot's neuropathic and deformity and infection overlays — [[endocrine/diabetes]])**. Diagnosis is bedside-plus-simple-physics: **the pulse examination and the arterial bruits and the dependent rubor and hair-and-nail trophic changes, then the ankle–brachial index (the Doppler systolic-pressure ratio: ≤0.9 diagnostic, the 0.4–0.9 claudication band, <0.4 typically limb-threatening — and its calcified-vessel false-negatives in diabetes and renal disease, rescued by the toe pressures' compressibility-immunity and the TcPO₂ transcutaneous oxygen and the duplex-waveform and CT and MR and catheter angiography tiers — [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]'s resolution-versus-risk ladder)**; and the **acute limb threat (the embolic or thrombotic six-P presentation — pain, pallor, pulselessness, paraesthesia, paralysis, poikilothermia — a vascular emergency whose viability triage (sensory and motor loss and the Doppler signals) decides immediate heparin plus embolectomy or thrombolysis or primary amputation, the reperfusion injury (the compartment syndrome and the myoglobinuric [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]] and potassium loads) and the embolic source-hunt (the atrial fibrillation and the aneurysm and the paradoxical route — [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]], [[#def-aneurysm]]) following within the hour ([[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]])**. Management runs on three legs: **the risk-factor and antithrombotic pharmacotherapy that halves cardiovascular death (the statin and the smoking-cessation and the antiplatelet and, in selected patients, the low-dose-rivaroxaban-plus-aspirin vascular-dose strategy — the COMPASS arithmetic and the bleeding balance, [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]), the exercise prescription (the supervised programme — the collateral and muscle-metabolic and endothelial mechanism and the guideline's first-line status before revascularisation), and the revascularisation for lifestyle-limiting claudication failing conservative care and for all limb-threat (the angioplasty-and-stent versus surgical bypass decision — the lesion-length and runoff and conduit and durability trade of the endovascular-versus-open trial families, and the hybrid and day-case era's patient-specific answer; the sympathetic-blockade and prostanoid and spinal-cord-stimulation rescue tiers for the unfit, and the amputation-level philosophy — preserve the knee, plan the prosthesis, and the stump-care-and-phantom-pain follow-up of the [[surgery/orthopaedics]]-adjacent rehabilitation services).**
:::

::: example The window-shopper's calf {#ex-claudication}
A 63-year-old smoker gets a right-calf cramp after 100 metres, forced to stop after 20 minutes of window-shopping, relieved in 3 minutes; foot pulses absent, calf muscle-wasting, and the ABI is 0.62 on the right and 0.95 on the left.
::: solution
**Typical unilateral superficial-femoral/popliteal-district claudication (Rutherford category 2–3, Fontaine IIb).** The localisation is clinical: **calf = femoropopliteal or tibial disease, thigh-and-buttock = aorto-iliac, erectile-dysfunction-plus-buttock the Leriche pattern; the ABI of 0.62 confirms moderate obstruction and the asymmetry localises the lesion side ([[#def-pad]]'s pressure-ratio logic and the [[anatomy/lower-limb]] adductor-canal predilection — the artery's tethering at the canal's exit is why it occludes first).** Management, ordered by benefit: **smoking cessation (the single greatest effect on progression and amputation — the relapse-managed programme, not the advice alone), supervised exercise (the walk-to-pain-and-rest protocol and its collateral and metabolic mechanisms — guideline first-line, and the adherence structures that make it real), full secondary-prevention pharmacology (high-intensity statin and antiplatelet and blood-pressure and diabetes optimisation — the coexistent coronary and carotid and renal-artery disease that kills these patients — the shared-atheroma policy of [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]] and [[cardiovascular/hypertension]]), foot care and the diabetic-foot interface if the nerves are involved ([[endocrine/diabetes]]); and the angiogram-and-intervention only for the failure of the above with lifestyle-limiting symptoms (the lesion and patient choice between the angioplasty-and-stent and the femoropopliteal bypass — the vein-conduit-first durability rule when surgery is chosen).** The follow-up script includes the patient's own insight: **the calf is announcing a systemic disease — the five-year coronary and stroke event rates rival the leg's.**
:::
:::

## Aneurysm, dissection, and Laplace's arithmetic

::: definition The dilating and tearing aorta {#def-aneurysm}
An **aneurysm** is a sustained arterial dilatation (aorta: ≥1.5× normal diameter — the infrarenal abdominal's 3-cm baseline and the fusiform atherosclerotic pattern versus the thoracic's connective-tissue territories — the Marfan and Loeys and vascular-Ehlers-Danlos aortopathies and the bicuspid-and-coarctation associations of [[cardiovascular/valve-congenital-disease]] and [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]'s fibrillin collagens, and the mycotic and inflammatory and IgG4-related variants). The natural history is **Laplace growth: wall tension rises with radius (the widget's linear term), the lamellar and elastin media thins and inflames (the MMP and macrophage proteolysis of [[pathology/chronic-inflammation-repair]]), and rupture risk climbs steeply above ~5.5 cm — the size-threshold logic (with the shape, growth-rate, saccular-morphology and family-modifier refinements) that justifies ultrasound screening of the 65-year-old male population (the mortality-reduction trials) and the elective repair-then-surveillance programme (the open and endovascular (EVAR) operations' aneurysm-sac exclusion, their type-I-to-III endoleaks and sac-re-growth and graft-infection and renal-and-spinal-cord-ischaemia surveillance imaging schedules).** Rupture presents the classic triad (pain, hypotension, pulsatile mass — only a minority carry all three) and lives or dies on the minutes-to-theatre-or-rupture-suite decision. **Dissection** is a different mechanics lesson: **an intimal tear (the ascending aorta's and arch's greater shear, or the vasa-vasorum haemorrhage of the primary intramural haematoma variant) drives blood into a cleaved medial plane (the cystic-medial-necrosis substrate of the hypertensive and the young connective-tissue and bicuspid and pregnancy and cocaine and iatrogenic groups), creating a false channel with its own flow politics — the complications are the branch vessels' (the coronaries' ostia and the carotids and spinals and renals and mesenterics — the stroke and the paraplegia and the gut and limb ischaemias) and the aortic valve and pericardium (the acute AR and the tamponade of [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]]'s classification).** The **Stanford A-versus-B division (any ascending involvement = A) is the treatment algorithm's only anatomy that matters: A dissects into the operating theatre (the mortality climbs hourly and the root and arch and coronary ostia reconstructions are the surgery), while uncomplicated B is managed with anti-impulse therapy (the beta-blocker-first rule — reducing dP/dt and shear, not just the pressure, then vasodilator add-on; the complicated B — malperfusion, rupture, rapid dilatation — gets the thoracic endovascular stent-graft era), with the long-term imaging of both and the genetic and family screening of the young and the familial (the TGF-β and fibrillin and collagen panels — [[genetics/genomic-medicine]]).** Aortic syndromes' differential (the tearing migration of pain and the pulse-and-BP deficits and the mediastinal widening and the D-dimer's age-adjusted rule-out and the CT angiography arbiter — [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]]'s chest-pain pathway with [[cardiovascular/electrophysiology-ecg]]'s and [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]]'s rivals) is the chapter's time-critical discrimination.
:::

::: example The tearing back {#ex-dissection}
A 66-year-old hypertensive man collapses with instant maximal interscapular tearing pain; right arm BP 188/102, left arm 128/80; the ECG shows LV strain; the chest film a wide mediastinum; he is drowsy but moving all limbs.
::: solution
**Acute aortic dissection until the CT says otherwise — the interarm difference, the maximal-at-onset pain and the hypertension triad.** Immediate physiology-directed therapy: **reduce aortic wall shear — intravenous beta-blockade first (esmolol or labetalol's titratable heart-rate floor of <60 and the anti-impulse pressure target of systolic 100–120), and only add the vasodilator (the nitroprusside or nicardipine) once beta-blocked, because the reflex inotropy of an unblocked vasodilator raises dP/dt and propagates the tear (the same unopposed-inotropy logic as [[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]]'s phaeochromocytoma rule, and the [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]] afterload-versus-contractility distinction made lethal).** Then the anatomy decides: **CT angiography of the whole aorta (the tear, the true-and-false channels and the branch-vessel involvement) — ascending (Stanford A) to theatre within the hour (the ascending-haematoma and pericardial-effusion and aortic-regurgitation and coronary-ostia and carotid-malperfusion findings each raising the urgency; the drowsiness here demands the arch-vessel assessment and the stroke-vs-deep-sedation-versus-selective-perfusion arithmetic); descending (uncomplicated B) to the anti-impulse unit with serial imaging and the beta-blocker's lifelong continuation, and complicated B (the malperfusion, the rupture, the refractory pain and hypertension, the rapid dilatation) to thoracic stent-grafting.** The complications list reads like the anatomy seminar (the spinal-cord artery-of-Adamkiewicz paraplegia and the renal and mesenteric and limb malperfusion and the late aneurysmal false-channel dilatation demanding lifelong imaging), and the aetiological footnote: **the uncontrolled hypertension of the stem is the population's main driver — [[cardiovascular/hypertension]]'s prevention argument stated in millimetres of aortic wall.**
:::

::: example The incidental 5.8 {#ex-aaa}
A 71-year-old ex-smoker's ultrasound for aortic screening shows a 5.8 cm fusiform infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm; he is fit, walks daily, and takes atorvastatin and ramipril.
::: solution
**Elective repair referral — the widget's right-hand curve has crossed the threshold.** The counselling follows the two-curve arithmetic: **at 5.8 cm the annual rupture risk (several per cent and climbing steeply — the wall-tension/Laplace logic of [[#def-aneurysm]]) now exceeds the elective-repair mortality (the screened-population trials' single-digit elective figures and the ~1–5 %-range operative risk by anatomy and fitness), whereas below ~5 cm watchful waiting is the evidence-backed answer (the early-repair trials' no-benefit finding and the surveillance-protocol intervals by size band).** The decision inputs: **anatomy (the infrarenal neck length and angulation and the iliac access — the EVAR-eligibility checklist versus open repair's durability in the fit long-survivor, and the complex FEVAR/branched era for the juxtarenal), fitness and patient choice (the EVAR-1 and DREAM and OVER long-run results — the early survival benefit of endovascular repair attenuating as reintervention and late endoleak and sac-growth surveillance accumulate — [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]'s follow-up-imaging burden), the perioperative programme (the cardiac-risk assessment of [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]], the renal-contrast and the spinal-fluid and the pulmonary and smoking-cessation measures, the aortic-cross-clamp and cardiac preload-and-afterload physiology of [[cardiovascular/cardiac-cycle]] and [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]] in the open operation's fluid management — [[surgery/anaesthesia-pain]]), and the lifelong messages (the other-aneurysm and the PAD and the coronary coexistence and the family-history screening of first-degree male relatives — the genetic-plus-smoking aggregation of [[#def-aneurysm]] and [[genetics/common-disease-genetics]])**.
:::

## Inflammation and spasm of the vessel

::: definition Vasculitis and Raynaud {#def-vasculitis}
The **vasculitides** are vessel-wall inflammation classified by caliber and phenotype (the Chapel Hill nomenclature — large: **giant-cell arteritis and Takayasu**; medium: **polyarteritis-nodosa and the ANCA-associated granulomatoses with polyangiitis and eosinophilic and microscopic**; small: the immune-complex and ANCA small-vessel diseases and the IgA and cryoglobulinaemic and anti-GBM overlaps with the glomerulonephritides of [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]-adjacent chapters) — organised here as the **vascular presentations that outrank their rheumatological ones**: **giant-cell arteritis (the temporomandibular-jaw claudication and scalp tenderness and the raised CRP and ESR of the over-50s, the permanent-monitored visual-loss threat demanding immediate high-dose steroids before biopsy — the temporal-artery biopsy and the ultrasound halo and the FDG-aortitis imaging, the polymyalgia overlap and the steroid-sparing tocilizumab era — [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]] holds the full classification); Takayasu (the young-woman pulseless aortic arch — the claudication of limbs and the carotid and the renal-artery stenosis's secondary hypertension of the hypertension chapter ([[cardiovascular/hypertension]]), the vessel-wall MRI and angiographic staging and the steroid-plus-sparing-immunosuppression and the surgical-and-angioplasty timing in the quiescent disease); the ANCA-associated and polyarteritis (the mononeuritis-multiplex and the digital and mesenteric and renal microaneurysm and the medium-vessel aneurysms and the hepatitis-B association — [[microbiology/viruses]]-adjacent); and the vessel-adjacent mimics (the cholesterol-crystal and warfarin and catastrophic and infective and radiated and drug-vasculitis lists).** **Raynaud phenomenon** is the episodic digital-arteriolar vasospasm (the pallor–cyanosis–rubor triphasic colour story, cold and emotion triggers) divided by cause: **primary (symmetric, young, normal nailfold capillaries and autoantibody-negative, the calcium-blocker and warmth and dose-titration management and the reassurance track) versus secondary to the connective-tissue diseases (the scleroderma-spectrum and the mixed and the overlap — the abnormal nailfold capillary microscopy and the specific autoantibodies — [[musculoskeletal/connective-tissue-vasculitis]] and [[immunology/autoimmunity]], digital pitting and ulcers, and the scleroderma-renal-crisis ACE-inhibitor imperative that makes blood-pressure surveillance a organ-saving test — [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]'s renin-driven lesion); the drug and occupational lists (the beta-blocker and the ergot and the chemotherapeutic and the cocaine and the hand-arm-vibration white finger); and the urgent ischaemic forms (the cryoglobulinaemia and cold-agglutinin and cryofibrinogenaemia protein phenomena, the antiphospholipid thrombotic digital and the vasculitic-digit, where the digital-ischaemia protocol — warming, vasodilators (the nifedipine and the PDE5 and the prostacyclin and the sympathetic-blockade tiers), the antiplatelet-and-source treatment, and the botulinum-toxin and cervical-sympathectomy rescue ladder — applies ([[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]'s thrombotic digital differential)).** Buerger's disease (the thromboangiitis obliterans — young heavy smoker, distal-vessel and superficial-thrombophlebitis-and-arch-vessel segmental inflammatory thrombosis, the corkscrew collaterals and the amputation-and-abstinence-only progression story) closes the list: **the one vascular disease whose entire treatment is the smoking** ([[immunology/hypersensitivity]]'s type-III-adjacent territory for the immune-complex vasculitides).
:::

::: example The white finger {#ex-raynaud}
A 29-year-old office worker has painful blue-white fingers in supermarkets and after washing dishes; this year the fingertips have become slow-healing cracks; examination shows taut shiny finger-pad skin and nailfold capillary dropout; ANA is positive at 1:1280 with a centromere pattern.
::: solution
**Secondary Raynaud with early systemic-sclerosis-spectrum disease (the limited/centromere phenotype) and incipient digital ulcers.** The pathophysiology upgrades from spasm to structure: **years of cold-triggered α₂-adrenoceptor-and-serotonergic-and-endothelin-1 vasoconstriction with deficient NO and prostacyclin vasodilation ([[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]]'s endothelium at its smallest scale) plus the obliterative intimal and adventitial fibrosis of the scleroderma vessel — the nailfold-capillary dropout is the microscope of that remodelling, and the digital ulcer the endpoint.** Management runs both halves: **the vaso-spastic arm (the warmth-and-glove-and-habit architecture, the dihydropyridine calcium-blocker titrated (the headache and ankle-oedema costs — [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]]'s pre-capillary-dilator Starling oedema), the PDE5-inhibitor and prostanoid and endothelin-receptor-agent tiers for ulcers, the sympathetic-blockade and botulinum rescue), the thrombotic-and-inflammatory arm (the antiplatelet and the ulcer-avoidance trauma and infection plan — the [[surgery/wounds-surgical-infection]] healing biology) and the systemic surveillance that saves organs: the blood pressure and creatinine (the scleroderma-renal-crisis — the renin-malignant-hypertension physiology of [[cardiovascular/hypertension]] and the ACE-inhibitor-as-lifesaving-treatment rule with the pro-crisis steroid caution), the pulmonary hypertension and interstitial-lung annual review ([[respiratory/interstitial-vascular-disease]]), the cardiac and GI (the oesophageal dysmotility of [[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]]) and the autoantibody-specific trajectory counselling.** The teaching: **Raynaud is a symptom, not a diagnosis — the capillaroscopy and the antibodies and the skin decide whether it is nuisance or disease** ([[clinical-skills/physical-examination]]).
:::

## The capacitance system: veins and lymphatics

::: definition Chronic venous disease and the calf pump {#def-venous}
The leg veins are the circulation's **capacitance compartment and orthostatic challenge**: the deep system and its bicuspid valves, the calf muscle pump ([[physiology/integrative-physiology]]'s exercise venous physiology and the perforators' junction with the superficial system — the long and short saphenous territories of [[anatomy/lower-limb]] — and the ambulatory venous pressure, which standing-without-muscle-action lets rise to ~90 mmHg against the supine ~15). **Venous hypertension** — from valve failure (the primary varicose and reflux disease) or obstruction (the post-thrombotic sequela of [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]'s deep-vein clot with its valve destruction) or pump failure (the immobile, the plegic, the standing occupations) — is the CEAP-classified ladder from **spider and varicose veins and oedema and dermatitis and pigmentation (the haemosiderin of the extravasated red cells — [[blood/haemoglobin-disorders]]'s pigment chemistry at the ankle) and lipodermatosclerosis to the venous ulcer (the medial malleolus's fibrin-cuff and cytokine and mechanical-stretch wound biology — the [[surgery/wounds-surgical-infection]] chronic-wound chapter's archetype)**. Management follows the column physics: **compression (the graduated bandage and stocking — the pressure-gradient substitution whose adherence and arterial-contraindication check (the ABI before compression — [[#def-pad]]'s number doing double duty) define the practice), the endovenous ablation and foam-sclerotherapy and surgical ligation-and-stripping era for symptomatic superficial reflux, the ulcer's four-part programme (compression-plus-debridement-plus-infection-control-plus-grafting-skin-substitutes, and the four-layer and intermittent-pneumatic and five-layer-trial evidence), the post-thrombotic and iliac-compression (the May-Thurner) and IVC-filter-adjacent interventional era, and the lymphoedema continuum ([[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]]'s Starling-oedema differential: the protein-rich interstitium's non-pitting and Stemmer-sign and recurrent-cellulitis lymphatic-damage spiral, treated by the compression-and-exercise-and-skin-care lymphology programme, the microscopic and filarial and surgical-and-radiation causes, and the emerging physiologic lymphatic-surgery and liposuction techniques).**
:::

::: history
**Monckeberg's** medial calcification (1903) and **Marchand's** atheroma-coining (1904) named the substrate; **Virchow's** triad (the endothelium, the flow and the blood — 1856) framed thrombosis and embolism (the [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]] lineage); **John Hunter's** ligation-era aneurysm surgery and **Astley Cooper's** and **Travers's** arterial lectures, **Laennec's** clinical aneurysm diagnosis, and **the Halsted school's** vascular teaching; **Rendu, Osler and Weber's** telangiectasias and **Hutchinson's** and **Raynaud's** (the 1860s—1880s) the spasm-and-vessel-nosology chapters; **Buerger's** 1924 thromboangiitis obliterans in the war-smoker cohort; **the Eales and Takayasu (1908's "pulseless disease" literature) and the Horton's giant-cell arteritis naming (the 1930s–40s)**; **Gross and Hufnagel's** coarctation operations (1945—48), **the Crawford's and DeBakey's and Cooley's aortic-surgery era and the Stanford group's hypothermic-arrest technique for the arch**, **Linton's and Babcock's and Trendelenburg's** saphenous-era of venous surgery, and the **Kunlin's and Dubost's and Wertheimer's** arterial-reconstruction generation (the femoropopliteal bypass and the thromboendarterectomy) and **Dotter's** and **Gruntzig's** endovascular line reaching the aorta through **Juan Parin's first endovascular stent-graft (the 1990s)** and the **EVAR-1, DREAM and OVER trial era** that randomised open versus endovascular repair and defined the modern aneurysm practice; **Fazekas and the screening trials (the MASS and the UK's AMSTAT and the Euroscreen randomised-ultrasound programmes)** for [[public-health/screening]]'s aortic application; and the **Chapel Hill nomenclature conferences (the 1990s–2012 and 2022 revisions)** that made [[#def-vasculitis]] a readable classification rather than an eponym parade.
:::

::: summary
- Arterial stiffening (elastin fatigue, cross-linking, calcification) raises pulse-wave velocity and central systolic load and strips the microcirculation's pulsatility buffer — the shared substrate of stroke, dementia, renal injury and isolated systolic hypertension.
- Peripheral disease grades from claudication (flow reserve) through rest pain (perfusion floor) to tissue loss; ABI with toe-pressure rescue, then exercise-plus-pharmacotherapy first, angioplasty-and-bypass-when-indicated — and cardiovascular death is the main outcome.
- Aneurysms follow Laplace (tension ∝ radius) with non-linear rupture risk — the screening-and-electric-repair arithmetic; dissections split into A (theatre) and B (anti-impulse first, stent-graft if complicated).
- Vasculitis and Raynaud are vessel-wall inflammation and vessel-tone dysregulation with limb- and organ-threatening endings — colour-triphasic fingers demand nailfold capillaroscopy and antibodies, never a blanket reassurance.
- Chronic venous disease is column-pressure failure — compression, ablation of superficial reflux, and the four-part ulcer programme; lymphoedema is the Starling differential's fifth mechanism with its own care ladder.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Interpret the ABI {level=1}
A diabetic patient's ABI reads 1.28 bilaterally despite a classic claudication history and absent foot pulses. Explain and give the next two tests.
::: solution
**The falsely-high ABI of non-compressible, calcified tibial vessels (the Monckeberg and diabetic and renal medial calcification — the cuff cannot occlude a rigid tube, so the measured "systolic" over-reads; the >1.30 compressibility-limit and the 1.0–1.3 borderline band)** — exactly the false-negative predicted by [[#def-pad]]. Next: **toe pressures (the digital arteries resist medial calcification — an absolute toe pressure and its exercise drop and the toe–brachial index restore the physiology), and transcutaneous oxygen measurement (the tissue-perfusion proxy predicting healing) plus the duplex-waveform and the CT-or-MR angiography when intervention is being planned.** The lesson generalises to every test whose assumptions the disease violates ([[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]]).

List the acute-limb-ischaemia viability assessment in order of decision weight and the corresponding intervention.
::: solution
**Sensory loss (light touch — the earliest) → motor deficit and its degree → the arterial and venous Doppler signals → skin and muscle incompressibility (the rigor of the irreversible limb).** Mapping: **viable (no deficit, audible signals) → heparin, admit, urgent imaging and source work-up, revascularise electively or semi-urgently; marginally threatened (minimal sensory change) → immediate heparin plus same-day catheter-directed thrombolysis or thrombectomy/endovascular or surgical embolectomy by cause and duration; immediately threatened (sensory plus mild-motor loss) → emergency revascularisation (open embolectomy and bypass typically faster than lysis) with the fasciotomy-and-compartment and reperfusion-metabolic (the potassium, myoglobin and [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]]) precautions; irreversible (paralysed, insensate, fixed staining, inaudible venous Doppler) → primary amputation (revascularising dead muscle kills — the myoglobin-and-potassium avalanche) ([[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]], [[emergency-critical-care/shock-fluids]]).** The source hunt (AF and the aneurysm and the thrombosed graft and the paradoxical embolus and the malignancy) and the anticoagulation decision run in parallel ([[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]).

::: exercise Screen or not {level=2}
Summarise the arguments (trial evidence and bias types) for one-off ultrasound screening of 65-year-old men for abdominal aortic aneurysm, and the two criticisms the women-and-never-smokers equity debate raises.
::: solution
**For: the prevalence and Laplace-natural-history logic ([[#def-aneurysm]] and the widget), the three randomised trials and their meta-analysis showing significant abdominal-aortic-aneurysm-mortality reduction (the UK's MASS and the Danish and Western-Australia programmes) with the number-needed-to-screen falling in the modern smoking-shift era, the test's benignity (the ultrasound — no radiation, no contrast), and the elective-repair-versus-rupture-mortality gap (the several-percent elective versus tens-of-percent-rupture arithmetic that only screening exploits).** **Criticisms: (1) sex — women's aortas are smaller, aneurysms rupture at smaller diameters and the trial evidence (mostly male) does not extend, so a male-only programme is both a sensitivity gap and a guideline gap, and the size- indexed-threshold debate continues; (2) risk-factor equity and falling prevalence — the cohort's smoking gradient means deprivation concentrates the disease while universal age-based screening becomes low-yield in never-smokers (overdiagnosis and the anxiety-and-follow-up costs — [[public-health/screening]]'s Wilson-Jungner checklist and [[epidemiology-ebm/diagnostic-tests]]'s prevalence-and-likelihood-ratio arithmetic), inviting risk-factor-targeted programmes and the women-who-do-smoke question.**

::: exercise Anti-impulse arithmetic {level=2}
Define the blood-pressure and heart-rate targets in acute dissection and why dP/dt matters more than the absolute pressure; give the beta-blocker-first sequence and the dialysis-patient's wrinkle.
::: solution
**Targets: systolic 100–120 mmHg and heart rate <60 within minutes.** The physics: **aortic wall stress's damaging component is the rate of pressure rise and the pulse-pressure amplitude (dP/dt and the ejection impulse on an intimal flap), not the steady pressure alone — a low-rate, lower-impulse circulation propagates dissections less (the Moens-Korteweg-and-shear reasoning of [[#def-wall]]), which is why the pure vasodilator (the nitroprusside or dihydropyridine alone) can be harmful — its reflex tachycardia-and-inotropy raises the impulse; beta-blockade first (the titratable esmolol or intravenous labetalol, then oral continuation) and vasodilator second is the sequence ([[physiology/autonomic-nervous-system]]'s receptor logic and the [[cardiovascular/circulation-blood-pressure]] afterload-contractility pair).** The dialysis or anuric patient's wrinkle: **volume- and pressure-state interpretation changes (the dry-weight-versus-hypertension arithmetic — [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]]'s intradialytic hypertension and the beta-blockade's reduced-tolerance of the volume shifts), with ultrafiltration-capable teams planning the blood-pressure reduction, the same anti-impulse priority applying to the follow-up imaging and the connective-tissue screening.**

::: exercise Headache and the jaw {level=2}

::: exercise Buerger versus everything {level=2}
A 33-year-old heavy cigar smoker has right-foot rest pain, a history of superficial thrombophlebitis, an arch angiographic corkscrew, and upper-limb involvement; the ABI is low and the antinuclear and antineutrophil-cytoplasmic antibodies negative. Diagnose, justify from the negative findings, and give the only disease-modifying treatment plus its evidence-strength.
::: solution
**Thromboangiitis obliterans (Buerger disease)** — the segmental inflammatory-and-thrombotic distal-vessel disease of young tobacco users (the cigar and roll-your-own nicotine-and-additive exposure, the distal upper-and-lower involvement, the migratory superficial thrombophlebitis and the arch-collateral pattern). The negatives carry the diagnosis: **the negative autoimmune serology against the scleroderma and ANCA and antiphospholipid mimics of [[#def-vasculitis]] and [[blood/thrombosis-anticoagulation]]'s thrombotic digital differential; the age-and-smoking pattern against premature atherosclerosis (which would still need the lipids and the diabetes and the popliteal-and-up pattern — [[cardiovascular/coronary-artery-disease]]'s territory); and the Emboli-and-arch aneurysm sources excluded (the echo and the aorta of [[#def-aneurysm]]).** Disease-modifying therapy: **complete tobacco and nicotine cessation — the only intervention shown to halt progression (the relapse-and-amputation correlation; the evidence is observational-cohort-strong and trial-poor, which is precisely how the clinical urgency is conveyed), supported by the addiction programme ([[psychiatry/substance-use]])**; adjuncts (the prostanoid and sympathetic-blockade and epidural and iloprost and the botulinum and stem-cell-trial tiers for critical ischaemia, the wound and amputation-level care) treat the ischaemia, never the cause ([[surgery/vascular-urological-surgery]]'s operative limits).
