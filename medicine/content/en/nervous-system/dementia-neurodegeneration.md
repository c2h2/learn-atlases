Dementia and the neurodegenerative diseases are where neurology meets the rest of a life: **memory is the presenting complaint, but the diagnosis is made from the pattern of cognitive and behavioural change, the timeline, and the exclusions, and management is mostly about driving and falls and medicines and money and carers.** The neuroscience is unfashionably slow-moving; the clinical medicine is demanding and humane.

This chapter covers the aetiologies and their clinical signatures, the assessment workup, the behavioural and psychological symptoms, and the practical and ethical apparatus of capacity, disclosure and end of life.

## The dementias

::: definition Types and their signatures {#def-types}
**The syndromic taxonomy that guides everything (memory-first amnestic presentations versus the non-amnestic - language-first primary progressive aphasia variants, visuospatial-first posterior cortical atrophy, behaviour-first frontal-variant syndromes and the parkinsonian-cognitive combinations - each with its pathology's usual culprit, and the recognition that "dementia" is a syndrome with causes just like "coma"); Alzheimer disease (the insidious-gradual-progressive episodic-memory-and-visuospatial-and-language-cascade with its amyloid-then-tau-then-neurodegeneration biological sequence, the ATN biomarker framework now usable via CSF and amyloid-PET-and increasingly plasma assays with the prognostic-versus-diagnostic caveats that anti-soluble-agents bring to primary care conversations, the vascular-and-TDP-and-alpha-synuclein copathology that explains most "pure" cases being impure, and the genetics - the APOE-e4 risk-allele reality and the rare autosomal-dominant PSEN1-PSEN2-APP families whose testing is a genetics-and-neurology-and-insurance conversation - [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]], [[genetics/genetic-testing]], the visual-variant anatomy in [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]]); vascular cognitive impairment (the stepwise-or-insidious-executive-and-gait-predominant profile with the white-matter-and-infarct burden on imaging and the same risk-factor medicine as stroke - the pure-form-is-rare-and-mixed-is-normal reality and the do-not-attribute-every-white-matter-hyperintensity-to-age-or-to-dementia rule) - [[nervous-system/stroke]], [[cardiovascular/hypertension]]; Lewy body disease in its clinical-full-house version (the fluctuating cognition and recurrent-formed-visual-hallucinations and REM-sleep-behaviour-disorder and parkinsonism with the severe neuroleptic sensitivity and the autonomic-and-falls-and-sensitivity-to-anticholinergics profile, the dementia-fluctuation-that-looks-like-delirium-and-often-is-diagnosed-as-both, and the DLB-versus-Parkinson-disease-dementia timing convention) - [[nervous-system/movement-disorders]], [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]]; frontotemporal lobar degeneration (the behavioural variant with its early disinhibition-or-apathy-and-loss-of-sympathy-and-hyperorality-and-compulsive-behaviours-and-executive-spared-memory presentation that families describe as "a different person" while the memory clinic's MMSE looks unfair, the language-variant progressive aphasias with their nonfluent-and-semantic and logopenic distinctions, the FTD-motor-neurone-disease overlap with C9orf72 and the CBS-PSP-tau syndromes, and the presenile-carer-legal-guardianship-and-early-death consequences that make this the most socially catastrophic dementia) - [[nervous-system/motor-systems]], [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]]; the other must-not-miss causes (the treatable-list that deserves its own mnemonic discipline - hypothyroidism and B12-and-folate deficiency and the normal-pressure-hydrocephalus-selection-honesty and chronic subdural haematoma and neurosyphilis and HIV and the autoimmune encephalitides that present psychiatrically and the limbic encephalitis with its subacute-memory-presentation and the paraneoplastic context - [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]], [[endocrine/thyroid]], [[blood/anaemia]], [[infectious-diseases/hiv]], [[infectious-diseases/tuberculosis]]); and the rapidly progressive dementias chapter written small for a reason (the prion diseases with their myoclonus-and-ataxia-and-visual-and-akinetic-mutism progression and the 14-3-3-and-RT-QuIC-and-DWI-cortical-ribboning-and-14-3-3-era diagnostics, the CJD-and-variant-CJD history and the infective-control-and-death-certification-and-neuropathology-registry obligations that no clinician should meet for the first time at the bedside) - [[pathology/diagnostic-pathology]], [[microbiology/infection-control]].**
:::

## Assessment

::: definition The cognitive assessment {#def-assessment}
**The history that outranks every test (the informant - who knows the patient's function, what changed first, work-and-hobbies-and-money-and-cooking-and-medications-and-driving-and-mood-and-sleep-and-behaviour, the timeline in months-and-years and the fluctuations that separate Lewy body and delirium from Alzheimer and the "she has always been like that" personality question; the functional inventory across basic-and-instrumental-activities because cognition is a diagnosis of daily-life consequence and the driving-and-financial decisions that come before the formal diagnosis; the medication-and-substance review with its anticholinergic-and-benzodiazepine-and-opioid-and-alcohol-and-cannabis-and-sedative loads and the anticholinergic-scale habit - [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]], [[psychiatry/substance-use]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]); the examination (the cognitive screen tests with their proper humility - MMSE and ACE-III and MoCA with their education-and-language-and-floor-ceiling caveats and the fact that a normal bedside screen never excludes mild cognitive impairment or frontal-predominant disease, the frontal-behavioural observations during the consultation itself and the clock-and-fluency-and-naming-and-prehensile-reflex findings, and the full neurological and systemic examination that finds the vascular and parkinsonian and NPH and subdural and Wilson and deficiency signs) - [[clinical-skills/physical-examination]]; the investigations panel (the bloodwork - FBC and biochemistry and calcium and TFTs and B12-and-folate and glucose-and-HbA1c and lipids and B12-mimicking conditions and the HIV-and-syphilis-serology where indicated and the autoimmune screen when the picture demands - [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]]; the imaging - structural CT-or-MRI with visual assessment scales (the medial temporal atrophy and white-matter and infarct and hydrocephalus and subdural and tumour reads) and the amyloid-PET-and-CSF-biomarker-use-rules-and-where-biomarker-positivity-changes-management-versus-adds-noise; the neuropsychometry when the profile needs formal shape and the capacity assessment when a specific decision is live) - [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]]; the differential with depression done properly (the pseudodementia-versus-depression-in-dementia problem - the "I don't know" versus "I tried my best" clichés that are less reliable than teaching suggests - and the depression-as-prodrome-versus-reaction debate and the trial-of-antidepressant-with-cognitive-reassessment as the practical answer, plus the apathy-versus-depression distinction that changes prescriptions) - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; and the diagnosis-communication itself (the name-it-clearly duty with the diagnostic-uncertainty-honestly-stated and the no-longer-optional disclosure of the diagnosis to the patient first where capacity allows and the practical-information pack - driving and benefits and advance planning and carer support and the support-organisation numbers - handed over at the same visit because forgetting is the disease and the appointment is the last reliable hour for weeks) - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]].**
:::

## Living and ending with dementia

::: definition Symptoms, medicines and safety {#def-management}
**The pharmacological modest-set (cholinesterase inhibitors - donepezil and rivastigmine and galantamine - with their modest cognitive-and-global-scores benefits in Alzheimer and Lewy body disease, the gastrointestinal-and-bradycardia-and-syncope-and-vivid-dreams side effects, the slow-titration and the withdrawal-trial conversations, and their dementia-with-Lewy-bodies-hallucination-and-apathy usefulness beyond the leaflet; memantine in moderate-to-severe Alzheimer disease with its own small effect size and the combination-practice reality; and the new anti-amyloid antibody era - the lecanemab-and-donanemab class with their amyloid-and-plasma-tau-clearance and modest slowing in biomarker-positive early disease, the ARIA-radiological-surveillance-and-anticoagulation-caution-and-MRI-access equity problems, the infusion-and-infusion-reaction logistics and the genuine controversy about whether a 0.45-point-scale-slowing is what patients would call progress - [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]], [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]); the behavioural and psychological symptoms (BPSD) protocol that is the actual daily medicine - the unmet-need decoding (pain and constipation and urinary retention and infection and sensory deficit and medication side effects and boredom and a mismatch between task and ability), the environmental-and-routine-and-communication interventions with their evidence and their time cost, the music-and-activity-and-occupied-spaces evidence and the dementia-friendly-ward design lessons, and the antipsychotic decision that must be earned (the small absolute benefit for severe distress or risk against the doubled-mortality-in-elderly-dementia signal of the atypical trials, the Lewy-body-catastrophic-sensitivity contraindication and the QT-and-falls-and-stroke-and-sedation caveats, the lowest-dose-shortest-time-and-regular-review and the medication-review governance that checking it, and the ECT-and-other-options-for-refractory-cases honesty) - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[nervous-system/stroke]]; the risk medicine (the driving-notification duty and the on-road assessment and family-concerns pathway - [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]]; the falls and the fracture prevention and the medication-de-prescribing and the orthostatic-and-vision-and-footwear audit) - [[geriatrics-palliative/falls]], [[endocrine/bone-calcium]]; the safeguarding dimension (the financial and carer and institutional abuse and neglect that dementia creates demand for, the mental-capacity-and-best-interests-and-restraint-and-deprivation-of-liberty legal architecture, and the hospital-maltreatment risk of "difficult" dementia patients and the reasonable-adjustment duty - adult safeguarding in [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
:::

::: example The man who kept paying for a house he sold {#ex-fv}
A 61-year-old retired engineer is referred after his third "irrational" property purchase; he has become talkative, overfamiliar, eats sweets at all hours, stopped showering, and spent the holiday fund on cryptocurrency; memory for events is reasonable; MMSE 27/30.
::: solution
**Behavioural-variant-frontotemporal-dementia - the syndrome whose cognitive tests are normal and whose life is collapsing ([[#def-types]], [[#def-assessment]]): the pattern - early personality-and-behaviour change with relative episodic-memory sparing and executive failure and the apathetic-or-disinhibited poles and the hyperorality-and-compulsive-stereotypies and the loss-of-sympathy-that-looks-like-selfishness, and the "high-scoring MMSE" trap that misroutes FTD patients to psychiatry and finance disputes for years - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]] for the mania misdiagnosis and [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]]; the workup - the frontal-predominant-atrophy-on-MRI-or-hypometabolism-on-PET-and-the-neuropsychometry-executive-profile-and-the-genetic-counselling-where-early-onset-or-family-history-with-the-C9orf72-and-MAPT-and-GRN-and-progranulin-discussion-and-its-insurance-and-family-implications - [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]], [[genetics/genetic-testing]]; the treatments that are treatments - the SSRI and other symptomatic medications-for-disinhibition-and-compulsion-and-hyperorality with their partial benefit, the behavioural-and-structured-routine-and-removal-of-financial-autonomy-in-stages approach and the social-prescribing-and-adult-support-services and the capacity assessment for the specific decision not a global verdict - [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]]; and the family as patient - the young-carer-depression-and-divorce-and-financial-ruin-and-children's-school-impact pattern (this is the dementia of the working-age patient), the Lasting-Power-of-Attorney-and-Deputyship-and-bank-fraud alerts and the specialist-young-dementia-team-and-voluntary-organisation signposting and the forward-looking conversation about a decade of disease that arrives 20 years before anyone expected - [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**
:::

::: example The fluctuating lady on the medical ward {#ex-dlb-hospital}
An 82-year-old with dementia-with-Lewy-bodies is admitted with a fall; day 2 she is "sensible" in the morning and convinced nurses are burglars at night; a junior gives haloperidol for agitation; the next day she is rigid, sleepy and cannot swallow safely.
::: solution
**Lewy-body-neuroleptic-sensitivity and the iatrogenic ward spiral ([[#def-types]], [[#def-management]]): the syndrome explanation - the endogenous cognitive fluctuation that families and staff misread as delirium-on-delirium and the antipsychotic-sensitivity (the 2-3-fold-and-severe-reaction-in-DLB risk with its rigidity-and-sedation-and-dysphagia-and-autonomic-collapse and its mortality) that makes typical neuroleptics and most antiemetics (metoclopramide, prochlorperazine) near-contraindicated - [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[nervous-system/movement-disorders]]; the harm-limiting actions now - stop the offending drug and document the reaction prominently as a severe-sensitivity-reaction alert rather than "allergy", rehydrate and review the aspirational swallow and aspiration-pneumonia plan and remove the other deliriogenic load (the catheter and the physical restraint and the opioids and the anticholinergics and the night-time blood-pressure checks) - [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]], [[renal/urinary-tract]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]; the alternative agitation protocol - the unmet-need audit (pain and constipation and urine and thirst and hunger and fear and noise and the missing glasses and hearing aid), the reorientation-and-presence-and-1:1-or-family-sit strategy and the music-and-snack prescriptions, with the distress-severe-and-risk-only rescue of quetiapine-low-dose-or-clozapine-or-cholinesterase-inhibitor-optimisation per guideline and the honest risk discussion with the family, and the ECT consideration in refractory danger - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; and the system lesson - the dementia-flags-at-triage-and-the-reasonable-adjustments-duty-and-the dementia-nurse-specialist-and-OT-admission-bundles-and-the-family-communication-book-at-the-bedside-and-the-discharge-plan-that-includes-who-sleeps-where, because the preventable harm in this case was not a single injection but a hospital that treated behaviour as non-compliance - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]].**
:::

::: example The artist who could no longer pour tea {#ex-pca}
A 58-year-old graphic designer has 2 years of "visuospatial problems" - misjudging steps, losing the page, trouble with faces and colour combinations; her eyes were checked as normal; memory is intact; MMSE 28 with poor cube-copy and object assembly; MRI shows posterior-predominant atrophy.
::: solution
**Posterior-cortical-atrophy - the visual variant that visits four specialists before neurology ([[#def-types]], [[#def-assessment]]): the syndrome - progressive visuoperceptual-and-visuospatial-and-visuoversal dysfunction (the Balint features of simultanagnosia and optic ataxia and ocular apraxia, the Gerstmann elements, the prosopagnosia and the colour-and-depth complaints) with preserved early memory and insight until late, the ophthalmology-and-optometry-detour that averages years and the fact that most carry Alzheimer pathology (or occasionally Lewy or prion) so the biomarker conversation is worth having - [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]] for the cortical-visual anatomy, [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]; the differential - the ocular causes excluded properly (the cortical-visual-impairment versus-retinal-versus-lens distinction) and the reversible mimics and the prion-Heidenhain-spectrum when the course is rapid with myoclonus - and the driving and workplace implications handled early because spatial jobs end before the diagnosis label arrives - [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]]; the management - the cholinesterase-inhibitor-offer with its realistic expectations and the occupational-therapy visual-strategy training (the scanning techniques and the contrast-and-lighting-and-organisation adaptations) and the low-vision service which most patients wrongly assume is only for eye diseases - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]; and the trajectory talk - the typical progression toward broader Alzheimer-type deficits over years, the support for a still-professional patient (the Design-register-and-benefit-and-early-registration conversations and the identity work of losing a visual career), and the research-participation option for biomarker-positive atypical phenotypes - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]].**
:::

::: example The grandfather with "slow legs and bladder" {#ex-nph}
A 74-year-old has 2 years of magnetic-appearing short-strided gait (first to the chair, twice fallen), new urinary urgency and mild slowness of thought; CT shows enlarged ventricles out of proportion to sulcal prominence; he cycles 10 km weekly and asks about "the shunt operation".
::: solution
**Idiopathic-normal-pressure-hydrocephalus - the triad is a selection criterion, not a promise ([[#def-types]], [[#def-management]]): the phenotype weighting - the gait-first-and-magnetic-apraxic-parkinsonian-lower-body picture with the incontinence-and-cognition arriving later, the imaging (Evans index and DESH signs and the atrophy-exclusion that the "out of proportion to sulcal enlargement" phrase guards) and the tap-test-and-LP-volume-and-external-lumbar-drainage-invasiveness-versus-predictive-value trade-off with its honest sensitivity numbers - [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]], [[nervous-system/motor-systems]]; the candidate conversation - shunt-response predictors (the idiopathic and gait-predominant and shorter-duration profile he partly has) versus non-response (the dominance of atrophy and vascular burden and Alzheimer co-pathology found in most autopsy series), the shunt-programming-and-overdrainage-and-subdural-and-infection-and-revision-rate realities and the "we operate on the legs, the bladder follows sometimes and memory least" statement families remember, with the neurosurgical second-opinion route normalised - [[surgery/perioperative-care]]; the alternatives and optimisation - the vascular-risk treatment and the falls programme and the urology-without-anticholinergic-overload plan and the bone health and the review interval that watches for the Alzheimer or Lewy body disease that eventually appears in many shunted patients; and the honest trial design - the published randomised-trial disappointments and the ongoing-selection-criteria debates given as context for why his surgeon will speak in probabilities not guarantees, and the do-not-shunt-as-an-experimental-hail-Mary caution - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]].**
:::

A nursing-home daughter demands "the line that feeds him" for her mother's severe-end-stage dementia (non-verbal, recurrent aspiration, 5 kg weight loss); the family meeting is scheduled.

**Hand-feeding-versus-PEG-in-advanced-dementia ([[#def-management]], [[geriatrics-palliative/end-of-life-care]]): the evidence delivered with care - feeding tubes do not prevent aspiration (saliva and reflux are untube-able) nor prolong survival nor heal pressure ulcers nor improve function or comfort in advanced dementia in the aggregate evidence, while adding restraints-and-agitation-and-diarrhoea-and-tube-accident burden, and the careful oral feeding with hand-feeding-and-positioning-and-texture-modification-and-mouth-care is the comparator with its own honest aspiration trade-off, the "doing everything by hand" language that converts comfort-care into active care for families - [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]], [[respiratory/respiratory-infections]]; the goals conversation - what her mother would have wanted (the prior-values archaeology before the label "she'd fight for every day" sticks), the prognosis framing (recurrent pneumonia as the disease's endgame not a fixable accident) and the offer of the palliative-care-team's parallel input, and the option to revisit the decision at a defined date; the capacity-and-law layer if she remains unable to decide - best-interests and the second-opinion and advocacy and, where relevant, the court-of-protection route for genuine disagreement and the do-not-treat-as-neglect-versus-do-not-feefeed-as-cruelty double-bound named openly - [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]]; and the after-plan - whichever path is chosen, the mouth-care and social-and-pleasure-feeding (the "comfort feeds" reframe with the family's guilt managed rather than mocked) and the nursing-home staff support and the anticipatory prescriptions for the next febrile episode so the next 2 a.m. phone call is not a new crisis - [[geriatrics-palliative/symptom-control]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**

::: exercise Diagnose late in a young mind {level=2}
A 49-year-old man with Down syndrome has 18 months of increasing seizures, gait change and loss of self-care; his family says "he's just ageing"; baseline he had learning disability with a supported job.
::: solution

## Where this leads

The delirium interface in [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]]; capacity and safeguarding in [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]] and [[medical-ethics-law/justice-resources]]; the Lewy-body motor overlap in [[nervous-system/movement-disorders]]; mood disorders and pseudodementia in [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; frailty and polypharmacy in [[geriatrics-palliative/ageing-frailty]] and [[geriatrics-palliative/polypharmacy]].

::: history
**Dementia's history is a story about who was blamed and who was believed: the pre-modern "second childhood" and the asylum's drowsy wards and the legal traditions of the lunatic and the weak-minded that still shape capacity law - [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]]; Alois Alzheimer's 1906 patient Auguste D. - the young woman with memory loss and hallucinations, the plaques-and-tangles histology by the Bielschowsky silver stain, and the Oskar and the Paul Emil whose case-note rediscovery in 2009 corrected decades of "she was never examined properly" folklore about Kraepelin's naming; the mid-century therapeutic nihilism - the "senile dementia, incurable" admission line and the community-care-era scandals of neglected long-stay wards that produced both the anti-institutional movement and decades of underfunded alternatives - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]; the sociological and neuropsychological turn - the Gooddens and the behaviour-mapping in nursing homes, the BPSD concept's legitimation of symptoms that had been managed with sedation, and the Tomas Kilpatrick-era self-advocacy and the dementia-friends-and-dementia-friendly-communities movement that turned the diagnosis from a family secret into a public health identity; the biomarker era - the amyloid-cascade hypothesis from Hardy and Higgins and the genetic autopsy evidence, the CSF and PET and now plasma assays, the NIA-AA research framework separating biology from syndrome and the appropriate-use-criteria debates about what clinicians should do with an amyloid-positive person who functions normally, and the anti-amyloid-antibody results - the ARIA-riddled-approval-battles - that redefined what "treatment" is allowed to mean while leaving dementia care's essentials (carers, environments, medicines, meaning) untouched - [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]], [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]]; and the neurodegeneration-big-science era - the C9orf72-discovery-week at a bar that unified FTD and motor neurone disease genetics and the TDP-43 and tau and alpha-synuclein proteinopathy classification that has replaced the disease-by-syndrome atlas with a molecular one, and the reverse-transformed prion propagation concepts blurring the boundaries that the CJD story once made absolute - [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]], [[nervous-system/motor-systems]].**
:::

::: summary
- Dementia is a syndrome; the pattern of the first symptoms (memory, language, behaviour, vision, executive, parkinsonism) and the speed are the diagnosis, before any scan.
- Always hunt the treatable and reversible contributors - drugs, depression, alcohol, thyroid, B12, subdurals, hydrocephalus, HIV, syphilis, immune and prion diseases - and time your cognitive testing accordingly.
- Frontotemporal dementia spares the MMSE and destroys the household; behavioural presentations in a 60-year-old deserve brain imaging and genetic counselling, not antipsychotics.
- Lewy body disease punishes typical antipsychotics; the fluctuation is the disease and the hospital is the deliriogenic hazard.
- Cholinesterase inhibitors and memantine do modest work; anti-amyloid antibodies are early-disease, biomarker-gated, scan-heavy and equity-challenged; none of it replaces the BPSD, driving, falls and carer medicine.
- Say the diagnosis, plan the future while capacity exists, and write down who is responsible for the next decision.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Dementia or depression {level=1}
A 74-year-old widower presents with 4 months of poor memory, "I don't know" answers, weight loss, early-morning waking and self-neglect; no neurological signs; CT normal. Plan.
::: solution
