Neuroinflammation is the branch where immunology meets the brain: **a headache-and-confusion patient can be dying of an infection treatable with a drug on every ward shelf, and a "psychiatric breakdown" can be antibody-mediated encephalitis curable with plasma exchange - in both, the interval between suspicion and treatment is measured in hours and defines the outcome.** This chapter covers meningitis, encephalitis, multiple sclerosis and the other demyelinating and autoimmune syndromes, plus the neuro-sarcoidosis and vasculitis borders.

## Infections of the nervous system

::: definition Meningitis and encephalitis {#def-meningitis}
**The acute presentation triad of fever and headache and neck stiffness with its honest insensitivity (the elderly and the alcoholic and the immunosuppressed present with confusion and nothing else) and the rash arithmetic (the non-blanching purpura of meningococcaemia with its septic-shock management priority over the LP - antibiotics-then-imaging-then-LP sequencing rather than the other way round) and the photophobia and opisthotonus and papilloedema-that-should-stop-you-from-performing-LP-before-imaging rule and the focal-sign-and-seizure-and-reduced-GCS-and-immunocompromise-and-posterior-fossa-suspicion versions of the same rule - [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]], [[infectious-diseases/fever-sepsis]]; the empirical-hour protocol that saves lives and is among the most examined algorithms in medicine - blood cultures and immediate parenteral antibiotics (the ceftriaxone-or-meropenem-per-local-guidance plus amoxicillin-for-the-Listeria-risk-groups - the over-50s and the immunocompromised and pregnancy) and dexamethasone-before-or-with-first-dose for the pneumococcal-inflammation-damage with its trial-derived restrictions, aciclovir for anyone with encephalitic features (the consciousness-change-and-seizure-and-focal-temporal-signs-and-CSF-lymphocytes picture) with the do-not-stop-aciclovir-until-PCR-result-and-time-point logic because HSV PCR can be negative in the first 72 hours and the untreated mortality of temporal-lobe HSV encephalitis is a third to a half - [[infectious-diseases/hiv]], [[microbiology/antimicrobials]], [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]]; the CSF interpretation table worth memorising (the neutrophilic-low-glucose-high-protein bacterial versus lymphocytic-normal-glucose viral versus the lymphocytic-low-glucose differentials - TB with its basal-meningitis-and-cranial-nerve-and-infarct-and-hyponatraemia combo and the Xpert-and-large-volume-and-repeated-sampling-yield and the treat-on-suspicion-and-adjuvant-steroid evidence, and cryptococcal with its India-ink-and-antigen-and-high-pressure-management, and listerial and the carcinomatous and sarcoid and Behcet mimics) - [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]], [[endocrine/hypothalamus-pituitary]] for the SIADH and adipsia of basal disease, [[nervous-system/stroke]] for the TB-infarct overlap; the complications ward - the raised-ICP-and-hydrocephalus-and-venous-thrombosis-and-empyema-and-infarct monitoring, the seizures, the meningococcal purpura fulminans and adrenal haemorrhage (Waterhouse-Friderichsen) and limb-salvage-amputations and skin-grafting survivorship and the hearing that must be tested in every post-meningitis child within weeks - [[emergency-critical-care/critical-care]], [[paediatrics/acute-childhood-illness]], [[infectious-diseases/fever-sepsis]] for the meningococcal-services-and-chemoprophylaxis-for-contacts-and-the-vaccine-programmes (MenB and MenACWY and the outbreak-response ring-vaccination) - [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]], [[public-health/communicable-disease-control]]; and the encephalitis differential beyond HSV (the autoimmune causes covered below, and the infectious cast list - varicella and its cerebellitis and vasculopathy, enteroviral rhombencephalitis, EBV, influenza and paediatric acute-necrotising encephalopathy and its oseltamivir-adjacent debates, West Nile and the flavivirus geography, rabies with its hydrophobia and its impossible-to-survive-symptomatic reality and the bite-wound prophylaxis that is the only intervention - [[infectious-diseases/tropical-infections]], [[microbiology/viruses]], [[nervous-system/motor-systems]] for the brainstem syndromes).**

## Demyelination and autoimmunity

::: definition Multiple sclerosis {#def-ms}
**The definition by dissemination (lesions separated in space across periventricular-and-juxtacortical-and-infratentorial-and-spinal locations and in time via simultaneous asymptomatic enhancing-and-non-enhancing lesions or a new lesion on follow-up or CSF oligoclonal bands doing much of the temporal work in the current criteria, which is why CSF is now an early rather than last test) and the clinical openers - optic neuritis (the painful unilateral visual loss of the young woman with the colour-desaturation and the normal-fundus retrobulbar form and the MRI decision that converts "watchful" into "early disease-modifying therapy") - [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]], the brainstem syndromes of internuclear ophthalmoplegia (the adduction lag with contralateral nystagmus and the skew and the skew with see-saw nuance) and trigeminal neuralgia in the young and the paroxysmal-attack-with-bilateral-symptoms tell and the cerebellar and sensory and motor presentations and the partial cord syndromes with the Lhermitte and banding sensations and the bladder-and-bowel-and-sexual dysfunction that go unasked about and untreated - [[nervous-system/motor-systems]], [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]]; the phenotypes - clinically-isolated and relapsing-remitting (85%, the attack definition and the steroid-accelerates-recovery-not-changes-disability rule and the infection-and-heat pseudo-relapse distinction that prevents needless admissions and needless drug-switches) and the secondary-progressive transition (activity-versus-progression re-defined by the modern imaging era and the smouldering-lesion concept) and primary-progressive (the spinal cord with its insidious-paraparesis-and-bladder-priority and the trial-desert reputation) and the rare catastrophic forms (the Marburg variant, tumefactive lesions mimicking glioma, and the area postrema syndrome of intractable vomiting that localises and diagnoses simultaneously) - [[pathology/cancer-biology]] - keep the mimic list here brief and in the next definition; the treatment era that has rewritten counselling - the platform drugs (interferons and glatiramer with their injection burdens and modest effects, and the old oral options), and the immune-traffic-and-depletion strategies (natalizumab with its JC-virus-and-PML risk calculus, the index-and-antibody-duration-and-prior-use arithmetic that drives prescribing and the PET-verified blood-brain-barrier work it still does, anti-CD20 ocrelizumab-ofatumab-ublituximab and their hypogammaglobulinaemia-and-encapsulated-infection-and-vaccine-futility-and-HBV-reactivation screens and their now-approved primary-progressive niche, the S1P modulators fingolimod-and-modals with the bradycardia-first-dose-and_macular-oedema-and-VZV-and-lymphopenia-rebound questions, fumarates with flushing and lymphopenia and the PML-ghosts, cladribine and teriflunomide and alemtuzumab with its long secondary-autoimmunity surveillance years and its remarkable lymphocyte-reconstitution biology) - [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]], [[immunology/vaccines-immunotherapy]], [[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]], [[cardiovascular/arrhythmias]]; and the escalation philosophy (the early-high-efficacy trials supporting hit-hard-early in adverse-prognosis presentations versus the escalation tradition, with the real-world cohort data narrowing the argument, and the emerging BTK-inhibitor and remyelination pipelines and the EBV-as-causal-factor evidence that makes a vaccine and an autologous-T-cell strategy plausible futures) - [[immunology/adaptive-responses]], [[epidemiology-ebm/randomised-trials]]; the symptomatic-and-rehabilitation medicine that determines daily life - the fatigue (the multifactorial workup with depression and sleep and thyroid and anaemia and the exercise programme with the strongest evidence and the amantadine-modafinil-fineretide literature that disappoints), spasticity (the the baclofen and tizanidine and diazepam and cannabinoid oral list, then intrathecal baclofen and botulinum toxin for focal patterns and rhizotomy for selected cases - [[nervous-system/motor-systems]]), neuropathic pain and paroxysmal symptoms (the carbamazepine-for-trigeminal-neuralgia and the gabapentinoid-and-duloxetine-and-amitriptyline evidence hierarchies - [[nervous-system/headache-pain]]), the bladder (the UTI-versus-spastic-vs-flaccid workup and the intermittent-catheterisation and the intravesical onabotulinumtoxin and urology shared care - [[renal/urinary-tract]]), the walking (the fampridine-diplegia caution and the physiotherapy and orthotics and functional electrostimulation) and the mood-and-cognition-and-return-to-work agenda with the neuropsychology referral that patients request more often than clinicians offer - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[geriatrics-palliative/ageing-frailty]] - rehabilitation keeps with [[public-health/health-systems-economics]].**

**Neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder (the AQP4-IgG astrocytopathy with its bilateral-or-severe optic neuritis and the longitudinally-extensive-transverse-myelitis spanning three-plus segments and the area-postrema intractable-vomiting and the hiccups syndrome and the encephalitic forms, the do-not-treat-as-MS-drugs warning because several MS agents worsen NMOSD and the complement-inhibitor-and-CD20-and-IL6-receptor treatments that replaced the azathioprine-and-mycophenolate era, and the attack-avoidance priority since disability is stepwise) - [[nervous-system/motor-systems]], [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]], [[immunology/autoimmunity]]; autoimmune encephalitis (the subacute-limbic-presentation - weeks of psychiatric symptoms and memory loss and seizures and dysautonomia and movement disorder, with the antibody taxonomy - NMDAR with its young-female psychiatric-onset-and-orofacial-dyskinesias-and-dystonia-with-extreme-delta-brush-and-ovarian-teratoma-search-and-ICU-course, LGI1 with its faciobrachial dystonic seizures and hyponatraemia and the old-voltage-gated-channel-complex-antibody mislabelling, CASPR2 and the Morvan and peripheral-hyperexcitability overlap, GABA-B with its early refractory seizures and small-cell lung cancer link - [[respiratory/lung-cancer-pleura]], [[pathology/cancer-biology]], [[nervous-system/epilepsy]], [[nervous-system/motor-systems]] - the tumour-search-and-immunotherapy-ladder (steroids-and-immunoglobulin-and-plasma-exchange then rituximab-and-cyclophosphamide then the escalating agents) with the outcome driven by time-to-treatment and by the underlying cancer, and the "first-episode-psychosis clinic" patients who are actually medical inpatients as the never-again-miss priority) - [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]], [[clinical-skills/clinical-reasoning]]; and the immune-checkpoint-inhibitor era (the anti-CTLA4-and-PD1-and-LAG3-and-CTLA4 combination neurological adverse events - myositis-and-myasthenia-overlap with myocarditis (the troponin-checking rule) and Guillain-Barré-like and meningoencephalitis presentations, and the steroid-first-and-hold-the-immunotherapy-versus-cancer-trade-off decided with oncology daily) - [[pathology/cancer-biology]], [[immunology/tolerance-regulation]], [[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]].**

::: example The "psychiatric admission" with dyskinesias {#ex-nmdar}
A 23-year-old graduate is sectioned after three weeks of insomnia, paranoia, hyperreligiosity and new drug misuse; on the ward she develops orofacial dyskinesias, dysautonomia with tachycardia and fluctuating consciousness, and generalised seizures; MRI is near-normal; CSF shows lymphocytes and oligoclonal bands.
::: solution
**Anti-NMDA-receptor encephalitis - the psychiatric ward holding pattern that must be broken within hours, not weeks ([[#def-other]]): the pattern - young woman, psychiatric-first, orofacial-and-limb dyskinesias, autonomic instability, seizures, hypoventilation-ICU trajectory, the EEG extreme-delta-brush (descriptive, not universal), MRI often unrevealing and CSF inflammatory with antibody confirmation in CSF preferred over serum - [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]], [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]]; the parallel urgent pathway - the antibody tests sent, the pelvic imaging for ovarian teratoma with the transvaginal ultrasound and MRI and the whole-body screening for the rare male-and-other-tumour associations and the repeat-search-when-antibody-positive-but-scan-negative protocol, and the immunotherapy ladder started on clinical suspicion where delay predicts intensive-care months (steroids plus immunoglobulin or plasma exchange, then rituximab and cyclophosphamide, then the escalating options) - [[pathology/cancer-biology]], [[immunology/autoimmunity]]; the ICU and general management - the airway and ventilation for the hypoventilation and the dysautonomia and the rhabdomyolysis and the DVT and the sedation choices with the antipsychotic-sensitivity-catastrophe caution (neuroleptic malignant syndrome mimics in this population are a real trap) and the catatonia-and-seizure treatment and the communication to the family about why her "psychosis" is inflammatory - [[emergency-critical-care/critical-care]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; and the long recovery and the ethics - the months-long stepwise unblinking recovery sequence (autonomic then movement then psychiatric then cognitive), the neuropsychological rehabilitation and return-to-study planning, the relapse-rate-and-second-tumour surveillance counselling, the capacity and detention law interface when a medical illness presents psychiatrically and consent to lumbar puncture and immunotherapy is needed from a patient who cannot give it - [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]], [[clinical-skills/communication]].**

**Inflammatory neurology's history is a sequence of diseases being rescued from psychiatry and from fatalism: the multiple-sclerosis-recognitions - Charcot's 1868 plaques-with-nystagmus-and-intention-tremor-tetrad and his patients' itineraries, and the decades of "sclerosis multiplex" as a pathological curiosity before the twentieth-century relapsing-remitting clinical identity and the CSF-band and then evoked-potential era of paraclinical support - [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]]; the encephalitis lethargica epidemic of 1916-1924 (von Economo's sleep disease with its parkinsonism sequelae and the still-debated infectious trigger and the rivalry over the entity's existence) as the first modern mass "infectious-then-autoimmune-then-psychiatric" mystery; the post-infectious-demyleination-animal-models (the experimental-allergic-encephalomyelitis of Morgan and Rivers and the Freund-adjuvant era) that framed MS as immune-mediated and produced the the copaxone discovery from myelin-basic-protein competition experiments, bench-to-bed in one arc - [[immunology/adaptive-responses]]; the neuromyelitis-optica-separation-from-MS-devic-s-1894-description-vindicated-a-century-later-by-the-AQP4-antibody-of-2004-Lennon-Waters-Verkman-and-the-MOG-antibody-of-the-2010s - the antibody era that turned a hundred mislabelled MS patients into a treatable spectrum and made "never give natalizumab to NMOSD" a prescribing rule; the autoimmune-encephalitis revolution of the 2000s - the ovarian-teratoma-NMDAR discovery from young women in psychiatric beds, the rapid antibody taxonomy (LGI1, CASPR2, GABA receptors and the intracellular targets with their T-cell biology), the demonstration that psychiatric presentations can be synaptic-autoimmune and reversible, and the ethical re-examination of the decades spent in asylum wards; and the modern immunotherapy arc - the interferon-era beginnings from the observation that viral infection flared MS in paradox, the monoclonal-antibody programme that made relapse prevention a molecule-selection problem, and the EBV epidemiology and the 2018-type Harvard student cohort study turning a ubiquitous virus into a probable cause - with vaccine-and-antiviral-hopes the corollary - [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]], [[epidemiology-ebm/observational-studies]].**
:::

::: summary
- Fever with neck stiffness and any reduced consciousness or focal sign: cultures, empirical antimicrobials (with Listeria cover where relevant), then image, then tap - in that order, in minutes.
- Encephalitic features mean aciclovir until PCR says otherwise; a negative early HSV PCR in the first three days does not clear the patient.
- MS is a clinic-radiological diagnosis of dissemination in time and space; optic neuritis and longitudinally extensive myelitis demand NMO and MOG antibodies before any long-term label.
- Subacute psychiatric illness with dyskinesias, dysautonomia or seizures is autoimmune encephalitis until excluded; treatment delay costs months of recovery.
- Early high-efficacy disease-modifying therapy, exercise, and symptomatic and rehabilitation medicine determine the MS patient's decade, not the steroid course.
- Immune-checkpoint inhibitors, anti-thymocyte globulin and anti-CD20 agents convert ordinary patients into inflammatory-neurology patients - know the syndromes before the third dose.
:::

## Exercises

::: exercise Read the CSF {level=1}
Interpret: (a) neutrophils 900, protein 2.4, glucose 0.6 (serum 9); (b) lymphocytes 120, protein 0.7, glucose 3.2; (c) lymphocytes 180, protein 1.8, glucose 1.4, symptoms 5 weeks; (d) lymphocytes 40, protein 0.6, glucose 3.4, oligoclonal bands positive in CSF only.
::: solution
**Cells-protein-glucose-reading ([[#def-meningitis]], [[#def-ms]]): (a) acute bacterial meningitis - treat, do not wait for culture; (b) viral pattern with the HSV-question and the aciclovir-and-PCR-plan; (c) the subacute-lymphocytic-low-glucose list - TB and cryptococcal and listeria and the malignant-and-sarcoid-and- Behcet versions with the Xpert-and-antigen-and-cytology-and-repeated-large-volume-sampling workup and the treat-on-clinical-suspicion rule - [[infectious-diseases/tuberculosis]], [[infectious-diseases/hiv]]; and (d) the intrathecal-IgG-restricted-bands pattern supporting MS in the right clinical-radiological context, remembering bands are supportive not diagnostic and appear in other inflammatory conditions - [[clinical-skills/laboratory-tests]].**
:::

::: exercise Manage the pseudo-relapse {level=1}
An MS patient on ocrelizumab presents with "legs worse again" for 2 days; she is passing cloudy urine, temp 37.4, no new lesion symptoms other than increased spasticity; what is the plan and what is not?
::: solution
**Heat-and-infection-unmask-old-deficits ([[#def-ms]]): the treatment-of-the-trigger - the urine culture and symptom-weighted UTI decision (asymptomatic-bacteriuria-caveats apply to catheterised MS bladders too) and antipyretics-and-cooling and the medication-and-bowel-and-pressure check, with the honest explanation that the spinal cord is conducting worse in the heat and the deficit will return when the trigger clears - [[renal/urinary-tract]], [[infectious-diseases/healthcare-infections]]; the relapse test - a genuine relapse means new-or-worse neurological deficit lasting more than 24 hours without fever-and-infection-and-heat, so a febrile patient with worsening of an old deficit is treated as pseudo-relapse until proven otherwise, and the steroid-burst reflex is resisted; the escalation if it is real - the high-dose steroid protocol with the infection-caution and plasma-exchange-for-severe-steroid-refractory-attack and the MRI-activity-and-therapy-review conversation (is the drug working?), which is a relapse-on-treatment problem not a steroid problem - [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]]; and the documentation - the relapse-and-infection log that drives future therapy decisions, because "wobbly leg on a UTI" charted as "relapse" can cost a patient confidence in their disease control on paper - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**
:::

::: exercise Handle the natalizumab dilemma {level=2}
A 34-year-old on natalizumab for 3 years, JCV-antibody-positive index 2.6, wants to know her PML risk; she is relapse-free and frightened of switching.
::: solution
**Risk-numbers-and-personal-values ([[#def-ms]]): the arithmetic - the antibody-index-and-duration-and-prior-immunosuppression risk stratification (high-index-and-2-year-plus patients approaching the percent-scale versus the low-risk strata) and the annual-and-6-monthly-MRI surveillance strategy and the new-mental-and-visual-and-motor-symptom education with the attend-early message that the trials of intensified MRI were built on - [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]], the PML semiology of this chapter; the options ranked - continue-with-intensified-surveillance (the early-detection-better-outcome data) versus switching (the rebound risk of S1P-withdrawal-and-natalizumumab-discontinuation, the washout windows, and the alternative drug fits - fumarate or anti-CD20 or cladribine or the oral options with their own ladders) and the emerging extended-interval-dosing strategy that cut PML risk in observational cohorts and reframes the conversation from "stop-versus-switch" - [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]]; the communication - absolute numbers not relative risk, the "your risk is now the size of" comparisons and the invitation to bring someone to the next appointment and the written summary because the fear erases the middle third of any sentence; and the process - the shared-decision record, the named review and the safety-net instructions and the PML-suspicion pathway (admit, PCR, stop drug, consider plasma exchange and immune reconstitution and the IRIS steroid dilemma) so that she knows exactly what would happen if the worst news came - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]].**
:::

::: exercise Coordinate the encephalitis admission {level=2}
A 29-year-old with probable autoimmune encephalitis is in ICU ventilated, on empiric antimicrobials, with antibodies pending; the psychiatry team asks who owns the patient.
::: solution
**A-shared-bed-with-one-medical-record ([[#def-other]], [[emergency-critical-care/critical-care]]): the clinical spine - antimicrobials continued until infectious exclusion is genuinely complete (the PCR-and-culture-timing logic and the HIV-and-travel-and-immune-status workup) while immunotherapy starts on the clinical trajectory rather than the postcard result, and the tumour search completed with pelvic-and-testicular imaging and CT chest-abdomen-pelvis and the repeat-search protocol - [[infectious-diseases/hiv]], [[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]]; the ICU medicine - the dysautonomia-and-seizure-and-hypoventilation management, the antiseizure choices with their behavioural effects, the neuroleptic-minimisation-with-the-agitation-alternatives (the benzodiazepine-and-beta-blockade-and-dexmedetomidine toolkit) and the rhabdo-and-DVT-and-nutrition-and-eyes-and-tube routines, with the delirium-versus-disease-confusion that only serial examination and EEG sort - [[nervous-system/epilepsy]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]; the interface with psychiatry - the agreed formulation (medical illness with psychiatric manifestations), the joint ward rounds or the named-liaison arrangement, the section-versus-capacity-for-medical-treatment analysis and the second-opinion-and-advocacy routes, and the honest statement to the family that both services are treating the same brain; and the after-pathway - the step-down rehabilitation and the neuropsychiatry follow-up and the driving-and-work-and-vaccine-and-contraception (teratogen-timing) conversations, with the single named clinician who holds the long-term plan because these patients fall between services for years - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]].**

::: exercise Design the meningitis service {level=3}
Design the 24/7 pathway for suspected CNS infection in a region with a large paediatric and immunocompromised population: antimicrobial policy, diagnostics, public health, audit.
::: solution
**Hours-and-roles-written-down ([[#def-meningitis]], [[public-health/communicable-disease-control]]): the pre-hospital-and-triage layer - the purpuric-illness ambulance protocols (IM benzylpenicillin with the non-blanching-rash public messaging history) and the ED door-to-antibiotic target with the LP-never-delivers-antibiotics rule hard-wired and the sepsis-bundle parallel activation - [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]], [[infectious-diseases/fever-sepsis]]; the microbiology spine - the same-day PCR panels and the culture-with-antigen and the 24-hour Gram-stain access and the CSF-volume-discipline and the TB-and-crypto-and-listerial add-on pathways for the risk groups with the immunocompromised-host-specific panel (the Toxoplasma and CMV and PML and cryptococcal and TB combinations) - [[microbiology/laboratory-diagnosis]], [[infectious-diseases/immunocompromised-host]]; the imaging-and-procedure rules - who-gets-CT-before-LP defined explicitly and the MRI-with-contrast-when-encephalitis-or-abscess-or-empyema-considered and the tap-withdrawal-and-open-pressure measurement standardisation and the radiology-on-call access times written into the policy - [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]; and the public-health-and-audit loop - the immediate-notification-and-chemoprophylaxis-and-vaccine-offer pathways for meningococcal and Hib and pneumococcal contacts with the out-of-hours-colleague-system that makes it happen at 3 a.m., the death-within-7-days-and-suspected-CNS-infection review meeting (the audit that keeps the antibiotic clock honest), and the survivorship clinic with its audiology and neurology and psychology and plastics for the limb-loss patients - [[public-health/vaccination-programmes]], [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[geriatrics-palliative/symptom-control]].**
