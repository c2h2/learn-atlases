Movement disorders are neurology's most physical examination: **the diagnosis is watched before it is heard, the signs are positive (tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia, dyskinesia, dystonia, myoclonus, tics, ataxia, chorea) and each has a description so distinctive that a careful video on a phone outperforms most blood tests.** Parkinson's disease dominates the chapter numerically and therapeutically; the rest is a vocabulary of unwanted movement.

This chapter covers the Parkinsonian syndromes and their treatment ladder, tremor, dystonia and tics, chorea and ataxia and myoclonus, and the spasticity-and-rigidity-versus-weakness distinctions.

## Parkinsonism

::: definition Parkinson's disease and its mimics {#def-parkinson}
**The core syndrome - bradykinesia plus rest tremor or rigidity, asymmetric and progressive, with the supporting history of micrographia and button trouble and rolled-up socks and hypophonia and softening gait with reduced arm swing and freezing and the non-motor army (hyposmia with its smell-test diagnostics, REM-sleep-behaviour-disorder as the strongest prodromal marker - the synucleinopathy herald, constipation, erectile and urinary and sweating dysfunction, depression and anxiety and apathy preceding motor disease by years, sleep fragmentation, and the later cognitive and hallucinatory complications) - the pathology of dorsal motor nucleus and olfactory bulb to limbic to neocortical spread (Braak staging) with its therapeutic implications for how early is too late - [[nervous-system/sensory-systems]] for smell, [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]] for the prodromal depression, [[physiology/synapses]] for the dopaminergic anatomy; the examination language that carries diagnosis and follow-up (the MDS-UPDRS and Hoehn-Yahr staging, glabellar tap and pendulum and reactivation-manoeuvre nuance and the pull-test timing and the "turning en bloc" and the start-hesitation and the handwriting sample requested at every visit); the pathological mimics and how to separate them - drug-induced parkinsonism (dopamine-blocking antipsychotics and metoclopramide and the cinnarizine-and-flunarizine-over-the-counter trap in some countries, symmetric and with tremor often absent and the DAT-scan resolving the question - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]), vascular parkinsonism (lower-body and stepwise and imaging-visible), normal-pressure hydrocephalus (the wet-wobbly-dementia triad with its honest gait-first-selection criteria for shunting - [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]]), and the atypical parkinsonian syndromes whose red flags are examination-day information - multiple system atrophy (early autonomic failure with the lying-standing BP arithmetic and the ataxia-and-stridor-and-poor-levodopa-response combination, the gluteal-sign and hot-cross-bun MRI era and the ICU-of-orthostatic-hypotension-management), progressive supranuclear palsy (early falls, vertical-supranuclear-gaze-palsy with the square-wave-jerk and roundhouse-turning and applause-sign and the levodopa-resistant axial rigidity), corticobasal syndrome (asymmetric apraxia and alien-limb and cortical sensory loss and myoclonus), dementia-with-Lewy-bodies (the fluctuating cognition and formed visual hallucinations and REM-sleep-behaviour-disorder and severe neuroleptic-sensitivity that makes the haloperidol given for hallucinations in the general ward a never-event - [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]], [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]]) - each with their carer-prognosis-and-advance-care conversations held far earlier than in Parkinson's because they move faster and treat worse.**
:::

::: definition Levodopa and the motor-complication years {#def-treatment}
**The pharmacological ladder and its arithmetic - levodopa as the most effective drug with the honest counselling that starting it early neither burns out neurons nor hastens complications (the decade-long ELLD and PD-MED era questions), dosed with a decarboxylase inhibitor and titrated to function with the protein-meal-interaction and constipation-absorption and iron-and-metoclopramide-and-benserazide-carbidopa-levodopa-ratios-and-COMT-and-MAO-B-inhibitor extensions (entacapone-and-opicapone and rasagiline-selegiline-safinamide with their interactions - the serotonergic caution with tramadol-and-cyclizine-and-SSRIs that every admission drug-chart should re-derive from first principles - [[pharmacology/metabolism-excretion]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[gastrointestinal/oesophagus-stomach]] for the motility cascade); dopamine agonists (pramipexole-and-ropeginirole-and-rotigotine-with-their-impulse-control-disorder-arsenal - the gambling and pornography and shopping and punding that families disclose only when asked with the validated questionnaire frankness, plus the sedation-and-sleep-attacks-and-orthostasis-and-leg-oedema-and-aggression-rebound literature and the younger-onset rationale for their use and the ageing-brain reason to taper them off) - [[psychiatry/substance-use]]; the motor complications of disease duration and levodopa pulsatility - wearing-off (predictable end-of-dose deterioration answered by fractionating doses and long-acting formulations and the COMT-and-MAO-B extensions) and dyskinesia (peak-dose and diphasic patterns, dyskinetic-distressing-versus-non-disabling distinction the patient makes, amantadine-as-modest-agent with its livedo-and-ankle-oedema-and-hallucination limits) and the fluctuations that turn a morning routine into a spreadsheet, with the advanced options - apomorphine (the rescue injection for freezes and the continuous subcutaneous pump with its nodules-and-somnolence management and cardiac-monitoring start) and levodopa-carbidopa-intestinal-gel via jejunostomy (the PEG-J era with its tube-site-and-confusion issues) and bilateral subthalamic-and-pallidal deep-brain stimulation (the levodopa-responsive, no-early-instability-or-untreated-depression-or-cognitive-decline selection with the battery-and-surgery-and-talk-volume-and-swallowing trade-offs discussed as a package, the microlesion-effect euphoria and the post-operative programming years and the suicide-risk-vigilance literature taken seriously by selection criteria) - [[surgery/perioperative-care]], [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]; and the non-dopaminergic and rehabilitation spine without which the drugs disappoint - the physiotherapy for gait-and-freezing strategies (laser cues and rhythmic auditory stimulation with their genuine effect sizes) and occupational therapy for the kitchen and the bathroom and the handwriting, the speech-and-language therapy for hypophonia (LSVT-Loud logic) and for the swallowing study when chest-infection recurrences start, the exercise prescription as the only intervention with consistent disease-modification signals in animal models and superb quality-of-life evidence in humans, the bone-health-and-falls interface and the driving review and the apathy-depression-hallucination management with the quetiapine-and-clozapine-and-pimavanserin-neuroleptic-hierarchy and the agonist-reduction-first order for hallucinations - [[public-health/prevention-promotion]], [[geriatrics-palliative/falls]], [[cardiovascular/hypertension]] for the orthostatic management with fludrocortisone-and-midodrine-and-droxidopa-era-and-sleeping-head-up-and-compression-and-salt-with-heart-failure-caution, [[renal/water-sodium]] for the volume physiology.**
:::

## Other movements

::: definition Tremor, dystonia, tics and myoclonus {#def-others}
**Tremor - the phenomenology that names itself when you slow down (rest tremor of parkinsonism with its pill-rolling and reactivation; action tremors divided into the essential tremor with its bilateral upper-limb postural-and-kinetic pattern and head-and-voice involvement and often family history and slow progression, its alcohol responsiveness that patients discover before doctors ask, and its cerebellar-adjacent Purkinje pathology literature, treated when function demands with propranolol or primidone (with the start-low-go-slow sedation problem) and topiramate and the deep-brain-stimulation-and-focused-ultrasound-thalamotomy options for the disabled refractory patient including the permanent-dysarthria-and-gait trade-offs of lesioning - the cerebellar rows of [[nervous-system/motor-systems]]; plus the drug-and-metabolic list (beta-agonists and lithium and valproate and SSRIs and thyrotoxicosis and withdrawal states and the enhanced-physiologic-tremor-of-sleep-deprivation-and-caffeine) that makes tremor a drug-history diagnosis before a neurology one - [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[endocrine/thyroid]]); dystonia - sustained-or-intermittent patterned muscle contractions causing twisting and abnormal postures, subdivided by distribution (focal - the task-specific writer's and musician's cramp and oromandibular and cervical with its sensory trick and sprintz sign and the botulinum-toxin era that made it treatable, with the dose-dependent spread-and-weakness-and-dysphagia neck-injection cautions and the antibody-question; segmental and generalised including the DYT families and the childhood-onset leg-first generalisation and the dopa-responsive dystonia that must be excluded in every young patient because a small levodopa dose transforms it - [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]]); the causes list (isolated or symptomatic - Wilson disease with its hepatic and Kayser-Fleischer and psychiatric combinations and the ceruloplasmin-and-copper studies and the penicillamine-and-zinc therapy with its own neurology-worsening caution, making it the metabolic disease every young movement patient gets screened for - [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]], [[gastrointestinal/liver-disease]]; drug-induced tardive syndromes - the tardive dyskinesia of dopamine blockers with its risk arithmetic and the VMAT2-inhibitor treatment era and the do-not-increase-the-antipsychotic-to-hide-the-movements rule that worsens the trajectory - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; and the painful and positional variants and the stiffness-syndrome with its paroxysmal escalation); tics - suppressible-and-premonitory-and-repetitive with their Tourette syndrome (childhood onset, waxing-waning, often with ADHD and OCD that determine school and self-esteem far more than the movements, the alpha-agonist and antipsychotic and topiramate treatment tiers, the CBIT habit-reversal therapy whose evidence families rarely know exists, and the deep-brain-stimulation-last-resort era) with the functional-tic differential (adult-onset abrupt "Tik-Tok era" presentations examined for distractibility-and-entrainment, treated with the functional-idiopathic-phenomena explanation and not more antipsychotics) - [[psychiatry/anxiety-disorders]], [[nervous-system/motor-systems]] for the entrainment test; and myoclonus-and-chorea-and-others (myoclonus - cortical with its jerky-locked-EEG-potentials and action sensitivity and the epileptic-association treated with valproate-and-levetiracetam-and-clonazepam, spinal and peripheral and propriospinal variants, and the Lance-Adams post-hypoxic syndrome - [[nervous-system/epilepsy]], [[emergency-critical-care/resuscitation]]; chorea - the flowing non-repeating dance with its adult causes (Huntington disease with the CAG-repeat anticipation and genetic-counselling-and-testing protocol and tetrabenazine-deutetrabenazine-and-VMAT2-antisymptomatic-era and the gene-silencing trials and the psychiatric and cognitive and dementia phenotype that the movement often matters least - [[genetics/non-mendelian-inheritance]], [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]], [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]]; vascular, autoimmune with antiphospholipid and SLE, pregnancy (chorea gravidarum), metabolic with non-ketotic hyperglycaemia (that hemichorea-hemibasal-ganglia-T1-bright MRI sign), drug-induced and senile; the urgent infectious-and-Wilson-and-SLE differentials; and athetosis-and-ballismus and the benign-but-startling syndromes families film - restless legs with its iron-deficiency arithmetic and ferritin-targeted replacement and the dopamine-agonist-augmentation-era-lesson that reframed it as an augmentation-prone disorder treated with alpha-2-delta ligands now - [[blood/anaemia]], [[renal/chronic-kidney-disease]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]).**
:::

::: example The man who acts out his dreams {#ex-rbd}
A 62-year-old teacher's wife sends video of him punching and shouting "defend the fence!" several times a month; he recalls vivid defensive dreams; he has constipation, reduced smell and mild action tremor; examination is otherwise normal; he takes no drugs.
::: solution
**REM-sleep-behaviour-disorder as prodromal synucleinopathy - a diagnosis with a decade of consequences ([[#def-parkinson]]): the recognition - dream-enactment with retained muscle atonia loss, the safety risk of injury to bed-partner-and-self and the differential of nocturnal frontal-lobe epilepsy and confusable parasomnias and the drug causes (SSRIs and SNRIs and even withdrawal) that must be removed first - video-polysomnography where diagnosis is uncertain or mimics loom - [[nervous-system/epilepsy]], [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; the risk conversation - isolated RBD carries a high long-term conversion rate to Parkinson's and dementia-with-Lewy-bodies and multiple system atrophy, the smell-test and DAT-scan and autonomic-testing research biomarkers not yet ready to predict the individual, and the honest way to say "we cannot tell you when or which, but here is what we would watch and what you can tell us" - [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]; the immediate management - bedroom safety engineering (bed on the floor and sharps-and-furniture-and-window-and-pet logistics) and melatonin-or-clonazepam-with-the-falls-and-driving-and-cognitive-cautions and bed-partner support and sleep-clinic referral patterns - [[geriatrics-palliative/falls]], [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]]; and the longitudinal plan - annual review with the motor and cognitive and autonomic and hallucination screen, driving advice revisited when sedatives are added or daytime sleepiness appears, and the future trial-and-research-register conversation for a patient with a decade of actuarial uncertainty ahead - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**
:::

::: example The gambler on pramipexole {#ex-icd}
A 54-year-old with young-onset Parkinson's, well controlled on pramipexole 3 mg plus levodopa, has lost his savings and an affair began "since the tablets"; his wife says he is "not himself"; UPDRS motor score is excellent.
::: solution
**An impulse-control disorder - the drug side effect that must be stopped despite excellent motor control ([[#def-parkinson]], [[#def-treatment]]): the disclosure - the question set asked routinely (gambling, pornography, shopping, eating, punding, hypersexuality) with validated tools because shame silences patients and partners are the informants, and the framing that this is receptor-driven and not character, essential to keeping the family together through the taper - [[psychiatry/substance-use]], [[clinical-skills/communication]]; the taper - the agonist-dose-reduction plan that usually resolves the behaviour, with the dopamine-agonist-withdrawal-syndrome (anxiety, pain, diaphoresis, drug craving, dysphoria) mapped in advance and levodopa-and-COMT-and-MAO-B-and-dose-frequency adjustments to hold motor function through it, the apomorphine-and-intestinal-gel-and-DBS candidacy conversations for the motor cost and the do-not-stop-multiple-drugs-at-once-to-fix-one-problem caution - [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]]; the collateral - the financial-and-relationship-and-legal-and-employment consequences needing practical help now (GamCare-type services and the bank and the solicitor and the family-repair conversation), the depression-and-suicidality screen during withdrawal, and the GP-and-pharmacy alerts so a future prescriber does not restart an agonist casually - [[psychiatry/mood-disorders]], [[public-health/determinants-inequalities]]; and the consent-memory - the pre-agonist counselling record (was ICD risk explained at initiation?), the routine re-asking at every review thereafter, and the teaching-point for the team that a perfect motor score is not a good outcome when the treatment spends the patient's life in other currencies.**
:::

::: example The tongue that locked at the gastro clinic {#ex-aps}
A 21-year-old woman arrives to the emergency department with her tongue "clenched and pulled back", her neck twisted to the left and both eyes drawn upward with panic; symptoms began four hours after her first metoclopramide dose for gastroenteritis; afebrile, normal bloods, no rigidity elsewhere.
::: solution
**Acute dystonic reaction - the oculogyric crisis and torticollis and protruding-tongue presentation of a dopamine blocker in a young patient ([[#def-others]], [[#def-treatment]]): the treatment - an anticholinergic (benztropine or trihexyphenidyl) or an antihistamine with anticholinergic activity (promethazine or diphenhydramine) given parenterally with the dramatic-minutes response as diagnostic confirmation, the airway-and-saliva watch for severe lingual and laryngeal involvement with the anaesthetic-backup threshold, and the oral-continuation-for-a-few-days-plus-stop-the-offending-drug prescription with the metoclopramide-allergy-style entry worded as an adverse reaction rather than true allergy - [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]], [[emergency-critical-care/acute-assessment]]; the differential that must not be missed - tetanus (the immunisation history and the trismus progression and the autonomic instability and the wound), hypocalcaemia and seizures and functional neck patterns and the serotonin-and-neuroleptic-overlap-fever-rigidity syndrome that this patient does not have because she is afebrile with normal reflexes - [[infectious-diseases/tropical-infections]] - tetanus sits in [[microbiology/bacterial-pathogenesis]], [[nervous-system/epilepsy]]; the teaching that prevents recurrence - the young-female-and-dehydration-and-recent-dose risk pattern, the community prescription-audit point about metoclopramide-for-viral-gastroenteritis being a bad default and the ondansetron alternative and the written information about which drugs to avoid (antipsychotics and the antiemetics) with a wallet card, because she will meet these drugs again at another 2 a.m. vomiting episode - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]; and the documentation - the reaction recorded with drug-and-dose-and-time and the description the next clinician can recognise, plus the conversation that this is a pharmacological effect, not an allergy to "painkillers" broadly, so she is not anaesthetised with avoidant fear for unrelated drugs - [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**
:::

::: example The rigidity, fever and confusion {#ex-nms}
A 46-year-old with schizoaffective disorder on haloperidol decanoate plus oral risperidone is brought from the community team: temp 39.4, CK 4200, BP swinging, HR 122, profuse sweating, marked lead-pipe rigidity, and muteness with fluctuating consciousness; no focal signs; urine dark.
::: solution
**Neuroleptic malignant syndrome - a diagnosis of the syndrome and a treatment of the dopamine block ([[#def-others]], [[#def-treatment]], [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]): the recognition-and-exclusion sweep - infection-and-sepsis and the serotonin syndrome (clonus-and-hyperreflexia-and-diarrhoea versus lead-pipe-rigidity-and-slow-evolution) and the anticholinergic-delirium-and-malignant-hypertheria-and-stiff-person-and-encephalitis comparators, with the LP-and-CT-and-blood-and-urine work running alongside treatment rather than before it - [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]], [[emergency-critical-care/poisoning]], [[psychiatry/psychotic-disorders]]; the immediate management - all dopamine blockers stopped and recorded as the causal decision, ICU-level monitoring with aggressive cooling and IV fluids and the urine-pH-and-rhabdomyolysis-renal-protection protocol and the benzodiazepine-sedation for agitation-and-rigidity and the dopaminergic rescue (bromocriptine-and-amantadine protocols) and the dantrolene debate for severe rigidity-and-hyperthermia with its liver caution - [[emergency-critical-care/resuscitation]], [[renal/acute-kidney-injury]], [[geriatrics-palliative/symptom-control]] - cooling keeps with emergency; the psychiatric parallel track - what happens to her schizoaffective illness when antipsychotics are contraindicated (the ECT literature for NMS-with-underlying-psychosis and for the refractory case, the dose-reintroduction-when-resolved rules with the long-acting-decanoate-avoidance lesson and the clozapine question and the capacity-and-advance-planning for the relapse risk), with psychiatry and intensive care and neurology sharing the bed and the family told what the drug did in non-blaming language; and the system half - the depot-injection-consent-and-monitoring-cadence that should have flagged the polypharmacy escalation, the incident report and the alert entry that a future clinician reads before ordering the next dose, and the duty-of-candour conversation about causation in a syndrome whose early signs (the rigidity and the fever of day two) get charted and missed - [[clinical-skills/patient-safety]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]], [[medical-ethics-law/professionalism-regulation]].**

A 55-year-old woman describes an evening "need to move the legs" with crawling sensations, sleep fragmentation and ferritin 22; what is the plan, and which old reflex must you resist?

::: exercise Run the falls-and-freezing consultation {level=2}
A 71-year-old with 11-year Parkinson's falls at doorways and in hospital corridors, takes six doses of levodopa plus amantadine and pramipexole, and reports "the floor turns to ice" but also lightheadedness on standing.
::: solution
**Two-diseases-in-one-gait-problem ([[#def-parkinson]], [[#def-treatment]]): disentangle - orthostatic hypotension (the lying-standing series and the post-prandial and the drug contributors, with the agonist first to go) versus freezing of gait (the medication-wearing-off timing and the environmental triggers - doorways and turns and dual-tasking and the new-anxiety-of-hospital) and the hallucination-and-cognition check before any dose increases - [[clinical-skills/physical-examination]], [[geriatrics-palliative/delirium-dementia-care]]; the medication surgery - timed dose optimisation against the diary, amantadine review in the confusion-and-livedo-and-renal-function context, agonist reduction for the orthostasis-and-impulse-and-sedation load and the honest statement that no tablet fixes mid-thought freezing; then the advanced options (apomorphine rescue for the freezing episodes and intestinal-gel and STN stimulation candidacy) - [[pharmacology/dosing-principles]], [[renal/filtration]]; the cue-based rehabilitation - the physiotherapy with visual-and-auditory-cue strategies (laser cane and metronome and the step-over-a-line trick and the weight-transfer pause-and-count), the OT home audit for thresholds-and-cards-and-lighting-and-the-eliminate-the-front-door-test and the community falls-team and the hospital admission rules (stop the bed rails, ask for the physio first, keep the dopaminergic times exact - the "missed levodopa is a fall" handover line) - the community rehabilitation services of [[public-health/health-systems-economics]]; and the forward conversation - the falls-in-a-Parkinson's-admission trajectory (fracture and delirium and deconditioning and the subdural with the anticoagulation question) with the bone protection and the vision check and the driving review, and the goals-of-care update that freezing usually heralds - [[nervous-system/headache-pain]] - keep the head-injury context with [[nervous-system/stroke]], [[geriatrics-palliative/end-of-life-care]].**

::: exercise Refer the atypical {level=2}
A 64-year-old with 18 months of "Parkinson's" has had two unexplained falls, loud noisy breathing at night, postural hypotension of 40 mmHg on standing, urinary urgency and only slight levodopa benefit at 900 mg. What do you tell this patient and their family?

**Naming-multiple-system-atrophy-kindly-but-early ([[#def-parkinson]], [[#def-treatment]]): the diagnostic reasoning to share - the poor-levodopa-response-plus-early-autonomic-failure-plus-stridor-plus-early-falls pattern, with the MRI-and-DAT-and-cardiac-autonomic-and-urology-and-sleep-studies used to support rather than to delay the label, and the honest caveat that clinical certainty accumulates over time - [[clinical-skills/medical-imaging]]; the prognosis conversation that should not wait for a crisis - the faster trajectory than Parkinson's and the different destination (chair-and-bed dependence, swallowing and airway problems, the stridor-and-sudden-death question and the tracheostomy-and-NIV-not-usually-helpful honesty) stated in stages with the family present and the information written down - [[clinical-skills/communication]], [[emergency-critical-care/airway-ventilation]]]; the active management list that makes "there is nothing we can do" false - the orthostatic programme and the falls prevention and the CPAP-and-positioning-and-possible-stridor-intervention pathway and the urology-and-bowel-and-erectile-dysfunction care and the SLT swallowing plan and the nutrition and the physiotherapy-maintenance, plus the advance-care-planning done while cognition is intact including the QoL-ceilings and hospital-admission preferences - [[geriatrics-palliative/ageing-frailty]], [[gastrointestinal/clinical-nutrition]], [[medical-ethics-law/consent-capacity]]; and the referral-and-support spine - the specialist-movement-disorder-and-autonomic-service review, the support-organisation and carer-impact conversation and the driver-and-DVLA-style notification and the benefits-and-employment paperwork that families navigate at midnight without help, with the named-clinician follow-up that keeps them out of emergency departments - [[public-health/health-systems-economics]], [[clinical-skills/records-handover]].**

## Where this leads

The basal-ganglia physiology in [[physiology/membrane-potentials]] and [[physiology/synapses]]; the neuroanatomical circuits in [[anatomy/neuroanatomy]]; dementia interfaces in [[nervous-system/dementia-neurodegeneration]]; drug-induced movement disorders in [[pharmacology/adverse-reactions]] and [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]].

::: history
**Movement-disorder medicine begins with the great clinical descriptions and ends with the implant: James Parkinson's 1817 essay on the shaking palsy - the surgeon-apothecary whose essay described six cases with the accuracy that took the specialty a century to catch up with, and whose name was attached by the French school (Charcot's addition of "paralysis agitans" and his own students' eponymous corrections) - [[nervous-system/motor-systems]]; the decerebrate-rigidity-and-substantia-nigra-lesion experiments of the late nineteenth century (Wilson's and Levin's and the Hungarian school's) and the Konstantin Tretiakoff nigral-dopamine-neuron realisation that waited for the Swedish fluorescence-histochemistry of the Falek and Carlsson era to become neurochemistry, and the 1960s Hornykiewicz dopamine-deficiency quantification that made replacement therapy rational - [[physiology/synapses]], [[pharmacology/drug-development-prescribing]]; the levodopa revolution and Cotzias's oral dosing insight and the Nobel to Carlsson and the Greenard-Cotzias-era controversies, the surgical lesioning era (pallidotomy and thalamotomy for tremor) that the L-dopa era temporarily buried and the Laitinen reappraisal and the Benabid high-frequency-stimulation discovery of the 1990s revived into deep-brain stimulation - the rare therapy that returned after its rival's motor complications proved the point - see the surgical-context rows of [[nervous-system/stroke]]; the movement-disorder-sociology of antipsychotic-induced tardive dyskinesia - the 1960s recognition that converted a psychiatric tool into a liability and forced the VMAT2-inhibitor and the risk-counselling era - [[psychiatry/treatments-mental-health-law]]; and the modern biological arc - the synucleinopathy prodrome identification through REM-sleep-behaviour-disorder cohorts, the LRRK2-and-PINK1-and-GBA genetics that reclassified Parkinson's from one disease into a pathology with several genetic routes, and the gene-silencing and GLP-1 and alpha-synuclein-antibody trials whose results this edition of the book cannot prejudge - [[genetics/mendelian-inheritance]], [[nervous-system/neuroinflammation]].**
:::

::: summary
- Parkinsonism is bradykinesia plus tremor or rigidity; the diagnosis is clinical, asymmetry and good levodopa response support it, and early falls, gaze palsy, autonomic failure and early hallucinations argue against it.
- Treat the person's function with levodopa first, agonists cautiously (impulse-control disorders), and the motor complications with fractionation, adjuncts and, early for the right patients, devices.
- Non-motor disease - sleep, mood, cognition, autonomic failure, pain and apathy - usually determines quality of life and needs asking about by name.
- Essential tremor is action tremor with a family history; propranolol and primidone first, thalamic stimulation or ultrasound for the disabled refractory patient.
- Young-onset movement disorder: think Wilson, dopa-responsive dystonia and drug induction before idiopathic disease.
- REM-sleep-behaviour-disorder is a diagnosis with a prognosis; safety-proof the bedroom and monitor for phenoconversion without foretelling it.
:::
