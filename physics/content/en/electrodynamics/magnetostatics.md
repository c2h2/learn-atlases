::: example The loop, the centre {#ex-loop}

::: solution

$$

$$

and

$$
\mathbf{B}_{\text{centre}} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2R}\, \hat{\mathbf{z}},
$$

the on-axis value at the centre; the same integration at the on-axis point at the distance $z$ gives $B(z) = \frac{\mu_0 I R^2}{2 (R^2 + z^2)^{3/2}}$.
:::

::: exercise level=3
Derive the $\mathbf{B}$ of the long wire, $\mathbf{B} = \mu_0 I/2\pi r\, \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}$, from the Biot-Savart, the $d\mathbf{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \frac{I d\mathbf{l} \times \hat{\mathbf{r}}}{r^2}$, integrating over the wire.
hint="Set up the integral: wire along z, field point at (r, 0, 0); dl = dz ẑ; r̂ from (0,0,z') to (r,0,0); the z-component cancels by symmetry, the φ-component integrates to μ0I/2πr."
::: solution
The wire is along the $z$-axis, carrying $I\hat{\mathbf{z}}$; the field point is at the cylindrical radius $r$. The current element $I\, dz' \hat{\mathbf{z}}$, at the height $z'$, acts through the vector from the element to the field point, $\mathbf{R} = r\hat{\mathbf{r}} - z'\hat{\mathbf{z}}$, $R = \sqrt{r^2 + z'^2}$. The Biot-Savart:

$$
d\mathbf{B} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{4\pi}\, \frac{dz'\, \hat{\mathbf{z}} \times (r\hat{\mathbf{r}} - z'\hat{\mathbf{z}})}{(r^2 + z'^2)^{3/2}} = \frac{\mu_0 I\, r}{4\pi}\, \frac{dz'\, \hat{\mathbf{z}} \times \hat{\mathbf{r}}}{(r^2 + z'^2)^{3/2}},
$$

with $\hat{\mathbf{z}} \times \hat{\mathbf{z}} = 0$; in the right-handed cylindrical system $(\hat{\mathbf{r}}, \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}, \hat{\mathbf{z}})$, $\hat{\mathbf{z}} \times \hat{\mathbf{r}} = \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}$, so every element contributes in the $\hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}}$ direction, as the right-hand rule about the wire requires. The remaining integral, with the substitution $z' = r\tan\theta$, is

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{dz'}{(r^2 + z'^2)^{3/2}} = \frac{2}{r^2},
$$

and the total is

$$
B = \frac{\mu_0 I\, r}{4\pi} \cdot \frac{2}{r^2} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}, \qquad \mathbf{B} = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}\, \hat{\boldsymbol{\phi}},
$$

matching the Ampere-loop result of [[#eq-wire]]: the Biot-Savart integral and the Ampere symmetry loop are two methods for the same wire field, the element integral and the loop integral of the same content.
